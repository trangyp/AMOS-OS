## Purpose & Definition

H High Scale is the **human/societal-scale tier** of the AMOS cognitive matrix scales dimension. While the lifecycle operations (O00-O15) define what cognition does and the control planes (C01-C12) define how cognition is governed, the scales dimension defines at what level cognition operates. H High Scale addresses cognitive phenomena that emerge at the level of human interaction, societal governance, and collective intelligence — scales where individual cognitive cycles aggregate into patterns of cooperation, conflict, institution-building, and cultural evolution.

The high scale tier encompasses the AMOS Human Interaction Engine (HIE), the Governance Economy OS (TSS/TPE), and the Universe Total Canon (UTC) frameworks. It is where the cognitive matrix interfaces with human stakeholders, social systems, and civilizational-scale processes. The high scale translation rules define how lifecycle operations and control plane decisions are translated upward to human-meaningful actions and downward from societal goals to individual cognitive cycles.

## Cross-references to Scales

- **Lower scales:** H High Scale depends on mid-scale and low-scale cognitive cycles for its raw material. Individual cognitive cycles (O00-O15) at the low scale aggregate into patterns at the mid scale, which in turn aggregate into high-scale phenomena.
- **Translation:** The `TRANSLATION_RULES` sub-artifact defines bidirectional translation between scales — upward (emergence) and downward (decomposition).
- **Scale consistency:** High-scale phenomena must be consistent with their lower-scale constituents; scale betrayal is detected and flagged.

## AMOS Architectural Alignment

H High Scale is the highest tier in the `04_SCALES` dimension of the 25-plane MECE architecture. It interacts with all control planes at scale and provides the societal context for all lifecycle operations. The AMOS Human Interaction Engine (HIE) with 7 state layers, 9-step pipeline, 10 goals, and 8 strategy profiles operates at this scale.
