## Toroidal Go Potential Field Dynamics

The potential field model translates discrete Go stone placement into a continuous electrostatic-like influence landscape over the toroidal $19 \times 19$ lattice, enabling quantitative strategic reasoning beyond discrete liberty counting.

### Charge Assignment & Superposition
Each stone on the board is modeled as a point charge: Black stones carry $q_i = +1$ and White stones carry $q_i = -1$. The aggregate charge density $\rho(x, y) = \sum_i q_i \delta(x - x_i, y - y_i)$ encodes the full board configuration as a distribution on $\mathbb{T}^2_{19}$. The toroidal topology eliminates boundary artifacts — influence wraps around all edges, mirroring the periodic strategic structure of the AMOS cognitive matrix.

### Spectral Poisson Solver
The 2D discrete Poisson equation $\nabla^2 \Phi = -\rho$ is solved in the Fourier domain via the 2D DFT on $\mathbb{Z}_{19} \times \mathbb{Z}_{19}$. The Green's function in frequency space is:
$$\widehat{G}(k_x, k_y) = \frac{1}{4 - 2\cos(2\pi k_x / 19) - 2\cos(2\pi k_y / 19)}$$
with the zero-frequency mode set to zero (gauge freedom). The inverse DFT recovers the spatial potential $\Phi(x, y)$, yielding a smooth influence field whose sign and magnitude encode territorial dominance.

### Strategic Interpretation
- **Dominance regions**: $\Phi > 0.05$ indicates Black-controlled territory; $\Phi < -0.05$ indicates White-controlled territory.
- **Contest boundary**: $|\Phi| \le 0.05$ identifies neutral contested intersections where strategic initiative is undecided.
- **Influence gradient**: The field gradient $\nabla \Phi$ points in the direction of maximum territorial pressure, guiding move selection toward high-leverage intersections.
- **Liberty coupling**: The potential field naturally encodes liberty structure — isolated stones produce localized potential wells, while connected groups produce extended plateaus of influence.

### Toroidal Symmetry & D4 Invariance
