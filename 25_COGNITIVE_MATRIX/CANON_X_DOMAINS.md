# Absolute Structural Integrity Canon

## 0. Canon Status

`ABSOLUTE_STRUCTURAL_INTEGRITY_CANON.md` defines the AMOS canonical
constraint that no structure, conclusion, model, decision, execution,
or evolution may acquire stronger status than the integrity of the
structure supporting it.

The canon normalizes a recurrent source principle across AMOS:

```text
INTEGRITY
>
COMPLETENESS
>
FLUENCY
>
SPEED
>
TOKEN SAVINGS

At the structural level:

```text
A RESULT IS NOT VALID
MERELY BECAUSE
IT IS COMPLETE,
COHERENT-SOUNDING,
EXECUTABLE,
POPULAR,
REPEATED,
OR ADDRESSABLE.
```

A result is structurally admissible only when its load-bearing
relationships remain sufficiently intact for the claim class being
asserted.

**Trang Phan**

# 1. Canonical Law

The Absolute Structural Integrity law is:

```text
NO AMOS OBJECT
MAY CLAIM MORE VALIDITY,
AUTHORITY,
CERTAINTY,
SCOPE,
CAUSAL FORCE,
OR FINALITY
THAN ITS LOAD-BEARING STRUCTURE SUPPORTS.
```

Symbolically:

$$
V(X)
\leq
I_{\text{support}}(X)
$$

where:

* \(V(X)\) represents the admissible validity status of object \(X\);
* \(I_{\text{support}}(X)\) represents the integrity of its
  load-bearing premises, provenance, scope, regime, dependencies,
  authority, and validation state.

This equation is an **AMOS structural notation**.

It is not asserted as a physical equation.
