## Domain Scope

The **15_C05_MIND_BEHAVIOR** domain encompasses cognitive psychology, behavioral science, consciousness studies, learning theory, and psychometrics within the AMOS knowledge architecture. It provides the cognitive-behavioral grounding for all AMOS domains that model human and agent behavior, from individual decision-making to collective dynamics. This domain bridges neuroscience with social science, covering the full spectrum from neural correlates of consciousness to behavioral economics. It interfaces with the UBI neuroemotional domain for affective modeling and with the society-culture domain for collective behavior patterns.
