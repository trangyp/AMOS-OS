## Domain Scope

The **14_C04_BIO_NEURO** domain covers biological systems, neuroscience, genomics, immunology, cell biology, and bioelectricity within the AMOS knowledge architecture. It provides the biological grounding for all AMOS domains that model living systems, from molecular biology to neural circuit architecture. This domain encompasses both the structural biology of organisms and the functional neuroscience of cognition, bridging the gap between physical chemistry and emergent mental phenomena. It interfaces with the UBI framework domains for biological intelligence modeling and with the medical clinical domain for translational applications.
