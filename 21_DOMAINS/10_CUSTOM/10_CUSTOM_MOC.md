## Purpose

This MOC indexes the Custom domain layer — the extensible domain
specification framework for AMOS. Custom domains are user-defined or
specialized cognitive domains that extend beyond the canonical domain
set, providing a template-driven mechanism for adding new domain models,
interfaces, and provenance chains without altering the core canon. This
layer ensures that domain extension is governed, templated, and
provenance-traced rather than ad hoc.

## MECE Domain

**Domains dimension — Custom / Extension Domains.**

Within the MECE architecture the custom domain occupies the **extension**
slice of the domains layer. It is distinct from canonical domains (which
define core cognitive capabilities) and from strategy domains (which
define governance and economic systems). Custom domains provide the
templated interface for heritage intelligence, NeuroSyncAI, planetary
synchronization, and other specialized domains, ensuring that every
extension has a domain spec, interfaces, provenance, and a binding
contract.

## Epistemic Boundary

Custom domain artifacts are **DERIVED** from the AMOS corpus. A custom
domain spec defines how a specialized domain is structured; it does not
prove that the domain has been deployed or that its interfaces have been
executed. `TEMPLATED != INSTANTIATED`; `SPECIFIED != DEPLOYED`. Custom
domains remain models until implementation evidence is independently
established.
