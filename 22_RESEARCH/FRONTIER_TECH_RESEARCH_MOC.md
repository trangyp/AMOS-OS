## Admission rule

A frontier result may change domain architecture when:
1. source identity/version is bound;
2. claim type is explicit;
3. scope/regime is compatible;
4. the change repairs ownership, comparison, evidence typing or invalidation semantics;
5. it does not promote the source result to Canon;
6. competing evidence remains visible.
