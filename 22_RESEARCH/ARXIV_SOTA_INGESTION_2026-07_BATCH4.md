### 6.1 Convergent Themes

1. **Biological grounding → AMOS cognitive organism**: Multiple papers (Biological Motifs, Dynamic Neural Manifolds, Theater of Mind) confirm that biological metaphors improve agent reliability. AMOS's cognitive organism architecture (immune/circulation/metabolism/homeostasis) is validated by this trend.

5. **Heterogeneous compute is inevitable**: Three-layer cognitive architecture, quantum-classical hybrid neurons, in-memory processing, and neuromorphic vision all point to a future where AMOS must route workloads across diverse accelerators. The metabolic governor's tier system (T0-T5) provides the routing framework.
