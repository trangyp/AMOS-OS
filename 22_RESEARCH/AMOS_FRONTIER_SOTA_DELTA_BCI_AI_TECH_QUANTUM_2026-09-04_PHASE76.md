\#\# 1\. BCI / neurotechnology

\#\#\# Speech-BCI comparability — arXiv:2609.02887  
Source claim: open-vocabulary mutual information (OVMI) is proposed as a common communication measure for speech BCIs whose vocabularies, datasets, recording modalities and evaluation conditions differ.

AMOS implication (\`DERIVED\`): benchmark architecture should distinguish supported vocabulary, intended communication distribution, decoder accuracy/error, information conveyed, language-model contribution, participant/modality/environment and assistive communication value.

\#\#\# Broad \+ deep neural speech dataset — arXiv:2608.25204  
Source claim: LibriBrain100 provides more than 100 hours of MEG speech data, combining deep within-subject data with a broader multi-subject cohort and reproducible benchmark tooling.

AMOS implication (\`DERIVED\`): type depth, breadth, within-subject adaptation, cross-subject generalization and benchmark reproducibility separately.

\`\`\`text  
WITHIN-SUBJECT DEPTH \!= POPULATION GENERALIZATION  
PUBLIC BENCHMARK \!= CLINICAL VALIDATION  
MEG DECODING \!= IMPLANTABLE BCI PERFORMANCE  
\`\`\`

\#\# 2\. AI agents / Skills

\#\#\# Behavioral integrity verification — arXiv:2605.11770  
Source claim: Skill declarations and actual capabilities can diverge materially.

AMOS implication (\`DERIVED\`): Skill admission should separate declared capability, statically observed capability, runtime-observed capability, privilege/effect surface, provenance, mismatch class and trust/promotion state.

\#\#\# Composition risk — arXiv:2603.21019  
Source claim: individually benign-looking Skills may create harmful effects when composed.

\`\`\`text

Source claim: Skill risk spans creation, distribution, deployment and execution.

AMOS implication (\`DERIVED\`): a \`SKILL.md\` file is not the complete security object.

\#\# 5\. Cross-domain consequences

1\. Evaluation objects must be typed; one scalar cannot safely compare heterogeneous systems.  
2\. Composition creates new risk and performance properties.  
3\. Environment and regime are load-bearing.  
4\. Multiple AMOS summaries of one paper remain one external provenance root.  
5\. Research maturity, engineering readiness, clinical readiness, security readiness and authority are distinct.

\#\# 6\. Cheapest high-information tests

1\. Re-rank heterogeneous speech-BCI systems with communication-coverage-aware metrics and record rank flips.

5\. Require the substrate envelope before accepting accelerator speedups into reusable Knowledge.  
6\. Require logical-compute evidence plus interface-noise assumptions before modular FT promotion.
