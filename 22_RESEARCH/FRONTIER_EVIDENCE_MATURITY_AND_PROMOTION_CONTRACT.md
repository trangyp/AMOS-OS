# Frontier Evidence Maturity and Promotion Contract

## Purpose

Govern how BCI/neurotechnology, AI/agent systems, advanced compute/tech, and quantum research may alter AMOS architecture without confusing novelty with validity.

```text
RECENT != MATURE
PREPRINT != REPLICATION
BENCHMARK WIN != GENERAL ADVANTAGE
COMPONENT DEMO != DEPLOYED SYSTEM
RESEARCH ADMISSION != KNOWLEDGE PROMOTION != CANON PROMOTION != DEPLOYMENT AUTHORITY
```

## 1. MECE evidence axes

Every frontier claim is typed on separate axes:

1. **Evidence form** — theorem, analytic model, simulation, benchmark, component experiment, system experiment, field deployment, longitudinal deployment.
2. **Independence** — same-team repetition, external reproduction, multi-site replication, population replication.
3. **Environment** — synthetic, curated benchmark, lab, controlled user study, field, independent home/production use.
4. **Duration** — single run/session, repeated sessions, weeks/months, chronic/longitudinal.
5. **Scale** — sample/participants/devices/models/tasks/sites.
6. **Transfer** — same task/device/model, cross-task, cross-subject, cross-device, cross-regime.
7. **Safety/governance** — advisory only, bounded reversible effect, consequential effect, regulated/clinical effect.
8. **Resource accounting** — compute, memory, energy, bandwidth, latency, maintenance, human support, failure recovery.
9. **Baseline quality** — weak/incomparable, matched baseline, competitive contemporary baseline, independently reproduced baseline.
10. **Provenance/freshness** — source identity/version, ancestry, publication/revision date, invalidation triggers.

These axes are not collapsed into one maturity score.

## 2. Promotion states

```text
R0 DISCOVERED
R1 SOURCE_BOUND
R2 RESEARCH_SYNTHESIZED
R3 ARCHITECTURE_RELEVANT
R4 KNOWLEDGE_CANDIDATE
R5 VALIDATED_KNOWLEDGE
R6 DEPLOYMENT_EVIDENCE
R7 CANON_CANDIDATE # only if canon rules are independently satisfied
```

Promotion is monotone only while load-bearing premises remain valid. A new version, regime shift, replication failure, safety incident, or stronger counterevidence may demote or quarantine a claim.

## 3. Domain-specific minimums

### BCI / neurotechnology
Architecture-impact claims must bind participant/population, modality, task, independence, calibration burden, longitudinal duration, hardware/telemetry, adverse events, user agency, support dependence, and consent/privacy scope.

### AI / agentic systems
Bind model/runtime version, task distribution, tool/context dependencies, workflow topology, authority/effect surface, attack model, recovery, negative-transfer evidence, latency/cost/energy, and benchmark contamination risk.

### Advanced compute / tech
Bind model/workload, precision, hardware, memory hierarchy, software stack, batch/sequence/concurrency, power/thermal limits, measurement method, tail latency, throughput, and energy.

### Quantum
Bind physical modality, error/noise assumptions, QEC code/distance, decoder/gadget, logical metric, classical-control assumptions, communication/link noise where applicable, resource overhead, and classical baseline.

## 4. Frontier promotion gate

A claim may alter architecture when it repairs one of:
- ownership decomposition;
- evidence typing;
- state/schema requirements;
- lifecycle/recovery semantics;
- benchmark comparability;
- invalidation or promotion logic.

It may not be promoted merely because it is newer, from a prestigious venue, numerically superior on one benchmark, or repeated by descendant summaries.

## 5. Cross-domain invalidation rule

If one source supports claims in multiple frontier domains, record one provenance origin and multiple scoped dependents. Do not count the dependent notes as independent evidence.

## 6. Stop rule

Stop frontier expansion when new evidence no longer changes architecture, decision boundaries, maturity classification, or a live competing hypothesis. Preserve remaining papers as Research rather than inflating architecture.
