## 1. Philosophical & Mathematical Foundation

Fractal reasoning operates on the principle that complex dynamical systems exhibit self-similar structural patterns across scale dimensions ($L$: Foundation/Substrate $\rightarrow$ $M$: Relational/Coordination $\rightarrow$ $H$: Executive/Emergent).

A complex system does not collapse instantaneously nor recover miraculously:
- **Collapse adheres to a 10-stage sequential degradation** starting from Foundation ($L$) or Relational ($M$) erosion and terminating in Executive ($H$) dissolution.
- **Recovery adheres to a 12-stage sequential restoration** starting strictly from Foundation ($L$) energy and memory stabilization, propagating upward through Relational ($M$) coordination to Executive ($H$) governance.
