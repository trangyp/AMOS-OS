# 15 Homeostasis — Map of Content

## Purpose

The Homeostasis sub-plane governs the **biological integrity and self-regulation layer** of the AMOS Cognitive Organism. It defines how the cognitive system maintains stable operating conditions under perturbation — monitoring health vectors, regulating stress ratios, shedding load under overload, and preserving the biological substrate bindings that ground cognition in embodied intelligence. Homeostasis is not a passive equilibrium; it is an active, governed regulation loop that detects deviation from viable operating ranges and triggers corrective action before cascading failure occurs. The separability law requires: `HOMEOSTASIS != REPAIR`. Homeostasis maintains stable operating envelopes; repair addresses causal faults after they occur. Both are regulation surfaces but with distinct triggers, scopes, and mechanisms.

## MECE Domain

This sub-plane belongs to the **C — Cognitive Capability & Orchestration** MECE domain (plane `05_COGNITIVE_ORGANISM`). Within the Cognitive Organism's seven MECE organ clusters, Homeostasis occupies **Group G: Regulation & Self-Healing Assurance** alongside the Repair Engine. It provides the health-vector monitoring, stress-ratio computation, and load-shedding signals that the Cognitive Organism uses to decide when to reduce cognitive depth, defer non-critical operations, or escalate to repair. Homeostasis does not own effect authority — it signals and regulates, but consequential action still requires the full control-plane governance pipeline.

## Homeostatic Regulation Loop

The homeostasis regulation cycle operates as follows:

1. **Substrate monitoring** — The SI and BEI engines continuously monitor somatic and bioelectromagnetic signals for deviation from viable operating ranges.
2. **Health-vector computation** — `UBI_HOMEOSTASIS` aggregates all substrate channels into a multi-parameter health vector (stress ratio, resource availability, cognitive load, integrity status).
3. **Integrity check** — `ABSOLUTE_BIOLOGICAL_INTEGRITY` verifies that no inviolable constraint has been breached. If any absolute invariant is violated, the organism enters protected mode.
4. **Load shedding** — When the stress ratio exceeds the load-shedding threshold, the homeostasis controller signals the Cognitive Organism to reduce cognitive depth, defer non-critical operations, and prioritize regulation over performance.
5. **Escalation** — If homeostatic correction is insufficient and the organism cannot return to a viable envelope, the controller escalates to [[05_COGNITIVE_ORGANISM/16_REPAIR/16_REPAIR_MOC|16 Repair MOC]] for causal fault localization and recovery.

## Epistemic Boundary

Homeostasis artifacts are AMOS_MODEL with canonical status CONDITIONAL and implementation PARTIAL. The health-vector model, stress-ratio computation, and substrate bindings are structurally defined but do not by themselves prove that a deployed runtime instruments real biological signals or that the load-shedding directives are enforced by the execution layer. `HOMEOSTASIS != REPAIR` — homeostatic regulation maintains stable envelopes; it does not diagnose or repair causal faults. The UBI substrate bindings (BEI, SI, NBI, NEI) are theoretical models of biological intelligence channels; their empirical correspondence to actual neural or somatic processes is not established by the structural model alone. The absolute biological integrity constraints are normative invariants, not empirical guarantees that the system will detect every integrity violation before cascading failure.
