## Purpose

The Homeostasis sub-plane governs the AMOS cognitive organism's biological equilibrium maintenance systems. It encompasses the four non-compensatory biological intelligence domains (NBI, NEI, SI, BEI) that together maintain physiological, emotional, cognitive, and bioelectromagnetic stability. Homeostasis is the foundational layer that must be preserved before any higher-order cognition, reasoning, or execution can proceed.

> **Epistemic boundary:** `AMOS_MODEL` — biological intelligence models are architectural metaphors, not biological proof.
