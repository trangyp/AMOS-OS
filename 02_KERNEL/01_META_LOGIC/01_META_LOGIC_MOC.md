## 20. Formal-Proof Firewall

Logical language can look theorem-like without constituting a proof.

Therefore:

```text
EQUATION
!=
THEOREM

ASSERTION
!=
DERIVATION

DERIVATION
!=
FORMAL PROOF

FORMAL PROOF
!=
EMPIRICAL TRUTH
```

A formal theorem can be valid within an axiomatic system while saying nothing directly about empirical reality unless the model-world mapping is separately established.
