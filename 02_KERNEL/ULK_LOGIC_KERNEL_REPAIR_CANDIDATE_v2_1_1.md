## 3. ALU-01 — Classical propositional logic

### Syntax

Let `P` be a finite set of proposition atoms. Formulas are generated by

`phi ::= p | bottom | not phi | (phi and phi) | (phi or phi) | (phi -> phi)`

for `p in P`.

### Semantics

A valuation is a function `v: P -> {0,1}`. Satisfaction is defined recursively by the ordinary two-valued truth functions. In particular:

- `v |= not phi` iff `v` does not satisfy `phi`;
- `v |= phi and psi` iff both are satisfied;
- `v |= phi or psi` iff at least one is satisfied;
- `v |= phi -> psi` iff `phi` is false under `v` or `psi` is true under `v`.

Semantic entailment is

`Gamma |= phi`

iff every valuation satisfying all formulas in `Gamma` also satisfies `phi`.

### Invariants

- normalization must preserve truth under every admitted valuation;
- contradiction checking must be semantic or proof-rule grounded, never confidence-threshold grounded;
- unsupported non-classical AST nodes must fail closed or route elsewhere.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED` for the repository-bound classical propositional/Core-19 subset. This does not establish completeness for every syntax accepted elsewhere in AMOS.

## 4. ALU-02 — First-order term unification

### Term algebra

For signature `Sigma` and variables `V`, terms form the free term algebra `T(Sigma,V)`.
A substitution `sigma: V -> T(Sigma,V)` extends homomorphically to all terms.

A substitution unifies terms `s,t` iff

`sigma(s) = sigma(t)`.

A most-general unifier `mu`, when it exists, satisfies: for every unifier `sigma`, there exists substitution `theta` such that

`sigma = theta o mu`

on the variables relevant to the problem.

### Invariants

- occurs-check prevents cyclic substitutions such as `x -> f(x)` in the admitted finite first-order term model;
- symbol arity and function-symbol identity are preserved;
- unification success is not theorem proving;
- quantifier semantics, model finding, and full first-order proof search require distinct machinery.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for finite first-order **term unification with occurs-check** only.

## 5. ALU-03 — Temporal logic / LTL

### Infinite-trace LTL reference semantics

For an infinite trace `pi = s_0,s_1,...` over atomic propositions and position `i`:

- `pi,i |= X phi` iff `pi,i+1 |= phi`;
- `pi,i |= F phi` iff there exists `j >= i` with `pi,j |= phi`;
- `pi,i |= G phi` iff for every `j >= i`, `pi,j |= phi`;
- `pi,i |= phi U psi` iff there exists `j >= i` such that `pi,j |= psi` and `pi,k |= phi` for every `i <= k < j`.

### Finite-trace executable projection

The current internal checker evaluates `ATOM/NOT/AND/OR/IMPLIES/X/F/G/U` over an explicit finite trace. Its `X` operator uses strong-next semantics: `X phi` is false at the final state.

Finite-trace evaluation is not identical to infinite-trace LTL model checking.

### Invariants

- trace finiteness/infinity is an explicit semantic coordinate;
- temporal ordering does not imply causality;
- model-check success does not imply future prediction;
- malformed or unsupported operators fail closed.

### External adapter

Spot is admitted as a research adapter candidate for future-time LTL/PSL and omega-automata operations. It is not currently bound in the active execution environment and must remain `UNAVAILABLE_HOLD` until executable/version/license and evidence gates pass.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for the stated finite-trace fragment.

## 6. ALU-04 — Epistemic/modal logic

### Kripke semantics

A finite Kripke model is `M = (W,R,V)` where:

- `W` is a finite set of worlds;
- `R subseteq W x W` is the accessibility relation for the selected modality/agent;
- `V` assigns proposition atoms to worlds.

For world `w`:

- `M,w |= box phi` iff `M,u |= phi` for every `u` with `w R u`;
- `M,w |= diamond phi` iff `M,u |= phi` for some `u` with `w R u`.

Epistemic readings such as knowledge require additional frame assumptions appropriate to the selected epistemic logic. A generic `box` result is not automatically human knowledge.

### Invariants

- accessibility relations are explicit and agent/modality scoped;
- frame properties such as reflexivity, transitivity, symmetry, seriality, or Euclideanness are assumptions to validate, not silently infer;
- modal satisfaction is not confidence scoring.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for finite propositional Kripke semantics over the repository-supported modal operators. No claim of dynamic epistemic logic, common knowledge, probabilistic epistemics, or first-order modal completeness.

## 7. ALU-05 — Non-monotonic logic / Dung argumentation

### Abstract argumentation framework

A finite Dung argumentation framework is `AF = (A,R)` with arguments `A` and attack relation `R subseteq A x A`.

A set `S subseteq A` is conflict-free iff no `a,b in S` satisfy `a R b`.

`S` defends argument `a` iff for every `b` attacking `a`, there exists `c in S` attacking `b`.

The characteristic function is

`F_AF(S) = { a in A : S defends a }`.

The grounded extension is the least fixed point of `F_AF` on the finite powerset lattice. Admissible and preferred semantics remain separately typed.

### Invariants

- attack is a formal relation, not empirical causation or hostility;
- extension semantics are never mixed silently;
- enumeration bounds must be explicit for potentially expensive preferred-extension search;
- contradiction tolerance here does not license arbitrary inconsistency elsewhere.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for finite conflict-free/defence/grounded/admissibility and bounded preferred-extension operations.

## 8. ALU-06 — Dependent type theory

### Judgement form

The core judgement is

`Gamma |- t : A`

meaning term `t` has type `A` under context `Gamma`.

The bounded internal calculus contains predicative universes, dependent Pi types, lambda abstraction/application, natural numbers, beta normalization, and definitional equality using de Bruijn indices.

A dependent function type has the form

`Pi (x : A), B(x)`.

Beta reduction satisfies

`(lambda x => t) u  -->  t[x := u]`

under capture-avoiding substitution.

### Invariants

- contexts and de Bruijn indices remain well-scoped;
- universe levels are nonnegative and predicative within the admitted fragment;
- definitional equality is not propositional equality unless the formal system explicitly supplies that bridge;
- type checking does not grant runtime/effect authority.

### External proof adapter

Lean 4 is admitted as a candidate proof-checking adapter. In the active environment it is unavailable and remains `UNAVAILABLE_HOLD`. A future binding requires exact Lean version, source hash, dependency lock, kernel-check exit status, and theorem/assumption identity.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for the repository's small dependent Pi calculus; not full Calculus of Constructions and not Lean/Coq equivalence.

## 9. ALU-07 — Quantum logic

### Formal reference semantics

For the standard Hilbert-space quantum-logic interpretation, propositions may be represented by closed subspaces or orthogonal projection operators on a Hilbert space `H`.

For a projection `P`:

- complement is represented by `I-P`;
- conjunction corresponds to intersection of proposition subspaces when defined in the chosen representation;
- disjunction corresponds to the closed span of proposition subspaces;
- the resulting lattice is generally orthomodular and need not be distributive.

For a normalized finite-dimensional state vector `psi`, Born probability for projection `P` is

`Pr(P | psi) = <psi, P psi>`

when `P` is a valid orthogonal projection and the inner product convention is fixed.

### Invariants

- vectors/operators carry explicit dimension and scalar-field types;
- density/state normalization and projection/unitarity conditions are checked before using formulas that require them;
- numerical tolerance is explicit;
- numerical linear algebra is not quantum-hardware execution;
- formal quantum logic is not evidence of quantum advantage or a physical state-preparation result.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for the current finite-dimensional classical numerical reference checker over its enumerated operator surfaces only. Exact current source authority for the monolithic upstream `unified_brain.py` remains revision-bound and must be revalidated when that source changes.

## 10. ALU-08 — Categorical/topos logic

### Category laws

A small category `C` has objects, morphisms, identity morphisms, and partially defined composition. Whenever compositions are well typed:

- associativity: `(h o g) o f = h o (g o f)`;
- identities: `id_B o f = f = f o id_A` for `f: A -> B`.

### Heyting semantics

For a Heyting algebra `(H, <=, meet, join, =>, 0, 1)`, implication satisfies the adjunction condition

`a meet b <= c` iff `a <= (b => c)`.

This gives intuitionistic propositional semantics for the admitted finite algebraic lane.

### Topos boundary

A category is not an elementary topos merely because it satisfies category laws or has a Heyting algebra. Topos claims require the relevant categorical structure, including finite limits, Cartesian closure, and a subobject classifier, with all typing and universal properties checked.

### Current executable boundary

`EXECUTABLE_BOUNDED_CANDIDATE_REBOUND` for finite small-category law validation plus finite Heyting-algebra semantics. Elementary-topos status is **not established** by the current checker.

## 11. Cross-fragment routing invariant

Let `F` be the set of the eight fragment identities and `route(x)` the fragment selected for a formal request `x`. A valid route requires:

1. exactly one primary fragment identity or an explicit multi-fragment composition object;
2. syntax supported by that fragment;
3. assumptions/frame/theory declared where they change semantics;
4. a matching executable binding before claiming execution;
5. preservation of `UNKNOWN/GAP` where no sound route/binding exists.

Cross-fragment composition must not coerce one fragment's semantics into another. In particular:

- SAT does not mean theoremhood outside the encoded theory;
- reachability does not mean entailment;
- modal accessibility does not mean causal influence;
- temporal precedence does not mean causation;
- a type inhabitant does not establish empirical truth;
- a numerical quantum calculation does not establish hardware execution;
- category-law satisfaction does not establish topos structure.
