## 1. Purpose & Empirical Grounding Law

`K_REALITY` acts as the physical and empirical grounding filter for all higher-order cognitive layers ($L_1 \dots L_6$). It establishes that no symbolic formulation, mathematical abstraction, or synthetic reasoning construct can override verified physical conservation laws, thermodynamic energy constraints, or direct empirical sensor observations.

## 2. The Four Substrate Slices

`K_REALITY` manages four invariant substrate slices at Layer 0:

1. **Reality Gate Slice:** Enforces conservation of mass-energy, causal non-retroactivity, and locality bounds.
2. **Reasoning Graph Slice:** Validates that graph topologies represent physically feasible causal dependencies.
3. **Memory Graph Slice:** Preserves immutable ground sensor traces and hardware execution receipts.
4. **Interface Coupling Slice:** Manages real-world I/O transducers, API connectors, and execution side-effects.

## 3. Empirical Grounding Invariants

- **$\text{MODEL} \neq \text{OBSERVATION}$:** A high-confidence model output is an inference, never an empirical observation.
- **$\text{CANONICAL} \neq \text{EMPIRICAL\_TRUTH}$:** Mathematical elegance or canonical symmetry does not substitute for empirical measurement.
- **Sensor Falsifiability:** Every empirical claim must specify a concrete physical measurement capable of falsifying it.
