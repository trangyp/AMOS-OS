# Heritage Intelligence

`HERITAGE_INTELLIGENCE.md` is the canonical Knowledge Plane reference artifact for **Heritage Intelligence** within `11_KNOWLEDGE/06_DOMAIN_KNOWLEDGE`.

It captures deep historical, structural, and civilizational patterns, providing a long-horizon intelligence model that audits decision fidelity against systemic entropy, institutional forgetting, and ungrounded optimization drift.

## 1. Core Functions

1. **Civilizational Provenance:** Grounding operational choices in historical survival invariants rather than transient market or algorithmic incentives.
1. **Trust & Drift Verification:** Continuous monitoring of institutional trust decay and authority-capability divergence (`CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY`).
1. **Pre-Mortem Failure Basin Analysis:** Identifying collapse pathways before irreversible resource allocation.
