## 1. Status of Modern Quantum Theory

Under the Trang Framework's own criteria:

> Modern quantum theory may remain incomplete in an ontological sense.

The distinction must be precise:

> **Incomplete does not mean incorrect.**

Modern quantum theory is exceptionally strong in predictive accuracy, mathematical structure, empirical verification, and practical effectiveness. The Trang Framework does not deny that record.

The proposed incompleteness concerns questions such as:

- a complete ontology;
- explicit boundary-locking mechanisms;
- formation and persistence of identity;
- transition from possibility to stable form;
- cross-scale integrity;
- system memory;
- foundational repair;
- emergence of classical reality.

In short, the Trang Framework proposes that quantum theory describes behavior extremely well while leaving open a broader question:

> What conditions allow a form or pattern to exist, stabilize, preserve identity, and persist?

**Class:** `MODEL / OPEN_QUESTION`

Modern quantum theory is especially strong in:

## 3. Ontological Questions

The Trang Framework focuses on unresolved or interpretation-dependent questions such as:
