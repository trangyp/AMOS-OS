Guiding principles to TCJ governance
Delivery empowerment at the Squad level:
TCJ Leadership is responsible for defining target (the “what”) while squad has full autonomy to realize
said target (the “how”)
Shared outcomes between TCJ and bank:
No overlaps in efforts and decision rights
Simple governance structure with clear guidance from TCJ and bank:
Establish formal governance channels to ensure collaboration and provide mechanisms to raise and
resolve issues swiftly. Regular cadence for each forum.
All units work towards same delivery targets:
Squads (delivery units) will deliverables every 12 weeks
