# Unified Biological Intelligence™ (UBI)

`UNIFIED_BIOLOGICAL_INTELLIGENCE.md` is the canonical Knowledge Plane reference artifact for **Unified Biological Intelligence™ (UBI)** within `11_KNOWLEDGE/05_FRAMEWORKS`.

It establishes the 4 non-compensatory biological domains of intelligence:

1. **NBI — Neurobiological Intelligence™:** Cortical networks, reasoning, perception, working memory.
1. **NEI — Neuroemotional Intelligence™:** Limbic & autonomic affect regulation, empathy, interpersonal accuracy.
1. **SI — Somatic Intelligence™:** Fascial architecture, posture, proprioception, biomechanical stability.
1. **BEI — Bioelectromagnetic Intelligence™:** Neural/cardiac field synchrony, oscillatory timing, systemic coherence.

## 1. Biological Effectiveness & Non-Compensatory Laws

$$i = \left( \text{NBI} \cdot \text{NEI} \cdot \text{SI} \cdot \text{BEI} \right)^{1/4}$$

$$e = i^2$$

Because $e = i^2$, cognitive over-optimization cannot compensate for somatic breakdown or emotional dysregulation. Systemic effectiveness requires multi-domain biological alignment.
