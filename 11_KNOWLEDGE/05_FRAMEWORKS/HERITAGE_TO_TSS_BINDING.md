# Heritage-to-TSS Binding

`HERITAGE_TO_TSS_BINDING.md` is the canonical Knowledge Plane reference artifact specifying the operational coupling between **Heritage Decision Intelligence™** and **The Trang System™ (TSS)** within `11_KNOWLEDGE/05_FRAMEWORKS`.

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. Structural Coupling

```text
TSS SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS (Ω, H, F, S, Cycle C1–C7)
                     │
                     ▼
HERITAGE DECISION ENVELOPE:
├── Emergence (C1): Establish Genesis Invariants & Trust Basins
├── Expansion (C2): Reserve-Scaled Growth & Reversibility Locks
├── Overreach (C3): Pre-emptive Pruning & Complexity Auditing
├── Fragmentation (C4): Cross-Silo Mediation & Asset Ring-Fencing
├── Crisis (C5): Emergency Triage & Fallback Execution
├── Collapse (C6): Seed Archive Preservation & Orderly Wind-Down
└── Reset (C7): Reconstruction Charters & Iterative Roll-Forward
```

______________________________________________________________________

## 2. Key Binding Principles

1. **Cycle-Paced Action:** Strategic action timing must be synchronized with the system's position in $C_1 \dots C_7$. Expanding aggressively in $C_3/C_4$ triggers catastrophic collapse.
1. **Reversibility Guarantee:** No high-stakes decision is committed without a pre-computed rollback basin.
1. **Authority vs Capability Enforcement:** High computational capability or resource dominance does not grant unilateral authority without verifiable governance receipts.
