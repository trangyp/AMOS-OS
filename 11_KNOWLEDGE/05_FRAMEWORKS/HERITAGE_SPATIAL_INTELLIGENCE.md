It models geographic, spatial, architectural, and territorial organization, connecting physical topography and settlement geometry to structural resilience and governance.

---

# 1. Spatial Principles

1. **Topographical Symbiosis:** Aligning infrastructure networks with natural terrain, watersheds, and physical transport barriers.
2. **Defensive Depth & Perimeter Buffers:** Nested boundaries preventing localized territorial breaches from collapsing heartland logistics.
3. **Decentralized Node Clusters:** Polycentric urban and economic hubs that can maintain autonomous function if central nodes are severed.
