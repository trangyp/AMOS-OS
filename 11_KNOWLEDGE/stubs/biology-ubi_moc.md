## 2. Core Pillars of Biological Intelligence (MECE Taxonomy)

* **Endogenous Bioelectric Code:** Resting membrane voltage potential distributions ($V_{mem}$) across non-neural somatic cell networks functioning as morphogenetic pre-patterns and anatomical memory.

* **Cellular Decision-Making:** Single-celled and non-neural biological agency, memory retention, and habituation (e.g., *Physarum polycephalum* network optimization, ciliates).

### 2.2 Molecular Machinery & Metabolic Substance Graphs
* **Stochastic Nanomachines:** Diffusion-controlled reaction kinetics, fluctuation-dominated transport, and molecular motors operating under low Reynolds number regimes ($\text{Re} \ll 1$).
* **Substance Graphs & Metabolic Topology:** Simple-graph mathematical representations of cellular metabolic networks proving scale-free, small-world resilience against enzymatic deletions.
* **Self-Assembly Thermodynamics:** Electrostatic and hydrophobic free-energy minimization governing spherical viral capsid assembly and protein loop-closure folding principles.

* **Photosynthetic Coherence:** Exciton energy transfer across Fenna-Matthews-Olson (FMO) pigment-protein complexes exhibiting vibronically assisted quantum coherence at physiological temperatures.
* **Enzymatic Proton Tunneling:** Temperature-dependent kinetic isotope effects confirming non-classical wavepacket tunneling across hydrogen-bonding activation barriers.

### 2.4 Prebiotic Synthesis, Astro-Exobiology & Genetic Codes
