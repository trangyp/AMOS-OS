### 2.1 Sensor Topology & Electrophysiological Capture
* **Transdural Telemetry (Shi et al. 2026):** Minimally invasive sub-cranial sensor arrays seated across the dural boundary, eliminating parenchymal tissue damage while achieving high-density micro-electrocorticography ($\mu$ECoG) signal-to-noise ratios ($>30$ dB).
* **Intracranial Penetrating MEAs:** High-channel intracortical microelectrode arrays (Blackrock NeuroPort, Utah array, Neuralink N1 1024-channel threads) capturing single-unit action potentials and local field potentials (LFP).
* **Endovascular Stentrode Arrays:** Transvascular jugular-catheterized electrode stents seated in the superior sagittal sinus adjacent to primary motor cortex, enabling long-term chronic telemetry without open craniotomy.
* **Non-Invasive High-Density EEG & fNIRS:** Wearable 128/256-channel electroencephalography coupled with functional near-infrared spectroscopy capturing hemodynamic and electrophysiological cortical dynamics.
