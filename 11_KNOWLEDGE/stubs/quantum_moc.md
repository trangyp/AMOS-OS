## 3. Epistemic Invariants & Anti-Overclaim Firewalls

1. **`NISQ != FAULT_TOLERANT`:** No NISQ demonstration (variational circuits, uncorrected qubits) shall be promoted to fault-tolerant universal quantum computing without verified logical error suppression below the fault-tolerance threshold.
2. **`PROOF != EMPIRICAL_ADVANTAGE`:** Theoretical polynomial or sublinear speedups do not establish runtime superiority over highly optimized classical parallel baselines (GPU/TPU clusters) until I/O state-preparation overhead ($\mathcal{O}(N)$ QRAM bottleneck) is accounted for.
3. **`ANALOGY != IDENTITY`:** Formal analogies between quantum equations (e.g., Schrödinger equation applied to price distributions or neural waves) and physical quantum hardware do not imply that the underlying financial or biological substrate is quantum-coherent.
4. **`DECOHERENCE_FIREWALL`:** Macroscopic cognitive processes in AMOS must be represented classically or semi-classically unless protected by biologically plausible, empirically verified topological decoherence shields.
