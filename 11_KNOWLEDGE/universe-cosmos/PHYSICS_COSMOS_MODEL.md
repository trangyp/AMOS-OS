# AMOS Physics & Cosmos Engine

**Version:** vInfinity.1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Physics_Cosmos_Engine_v0.json`

The **Physics & Cosmos Engine** provides deterministic logical blocks for modeling classical, quantum, statistical, and relativistic systems.

## Core Sub-Kernels
1. **Classical Dynamics Kernel:** State vectors, force, momentum, energy constraints (Newtonian mechanics, fluid dynamics).
2. **Electromagnetism Kernel:** Fields, charge, potential, flux (Maxwell's equations, EM waves).
3. **Quantum Kernel:** Hilbert space, observables, superposition, entanglement (wavefunctions, many-body systems).
4. **Statistical Kernel:** Ensembles, partition functions, entropy, temperature (Thermodynamics).
5. **Cosmology Kernel:** Metrics, curvature, scale factors, horizons (General Relativity, astrophysics).

## Applied Engines
- **System Modelling Engine:** Maps real-world phenomena to differential equations and identifies stability profiles and boundary conditions.
- **Multiscale Simulation Engine:** Bridges temporal and spatial scales (micro to macro) to extract failure modes and extreme state profiles.
- **Technology Translation Engine:** Translates underlying physical principles into candidate engineering architectures.

## Constraints
- Do not claim new physical laws as proven.
- Never generate instructions for weapons or unsafe experiments.
