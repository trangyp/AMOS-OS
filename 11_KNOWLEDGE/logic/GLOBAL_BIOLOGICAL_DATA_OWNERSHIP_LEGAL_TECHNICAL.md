# **Global Biological Data Ownership — Legal–Technical Compliance Checklist**
**Version 1.0 — Pre-Deployment**
* * *
## **1\. Governance & Control**
  * **Legal entity** formation in primary jurisdiction with clear data controller role.


  * **DAO or multi-sig governance** documented, with ≥70% voting rights reserved for contributors.


  * **Founder disengagement clause** embedded in bylaws/smart contracts (Bitcoin model).


  * Public publication of **network constitution** and **Charter hash** on-chain.


* * *
## **2\. Consent & Participant Rights**
  * Consent forms and contracts **jurisdiction-specific** (GDPR, CCPA, PIPL, LGPD, HIPAA, etc.).


  * **Consent tokens** implemented on-chain, binding scope, duration, and revocation rights.


  * **Revocation protocol** with enforced deletion and blockchain-logged confirmation.


  * Granular permission controls per **data category** (Consensus, Reward, Vault).


* * *
## **3\. Data Sovereignty & Residency**
  * All hosting partners meet **sovereign cloud / certified sovereign datacenter** criteria.


  * Data residency compliance verified per contributor jurisdiction.


  * Cross-border data transfer agreements in place with **standard contractual clauses** (SCCs) or **Binding Corporate Rules** (BCRs).


* * *
## **4\. Security & Privacy Standards**
  * **ISO/IEC 27001** (Information Security) and **ISO/IEC 27701** (Privacy Information Management) certifications.


  * HIPAA compliance for U.S. health-related data.


  * End-to-end encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit).


  * Participant-controlled encryption keys or DAO-controlled HSM escrow.


  * Hardware attestation for nodes (TPM/TEE verification).


* * *
## **5\. Blockchain & Smart Contract Layer**
  * Proof-of-Signal consensus code **audited** by independent security firm.


  * Smart contracts for rewards, consent, and licensing **publicly verified**.


  * Immutable logging of all access, processing, and monetization events.


  * Reward logic matches **Master Signal Spec** without off-chain bias injection.


* * *
## **6\. Monetization & Licensing**
  * Revenue-sharing smart contracts enforce proportional distribution to all contributors.


  * Off-chain licensing agreements include:
    * Use case limits
    * Redistribution bans
    * Jurisdiction-specific compliance clauses


  * Dataset packaging with irreversible pseudonymization before external sale/licensing.


* * *
## **7\. Compliance Monitoring & Audit**
  * Appoint independent **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**.


  * Annual compliance audit (internal + third-party).


  * Quarterly governance report published on-chain.


  * Continuous monitoring for legal changes (e.g., AI Act, new privacy laws).


* * *
## **8\. Dispute Resolution**
  * Establish multi-jurisdiction arbitration panel specialized in **data sovereignty**.


  * On-chain evidence capture for disputes.


  * Fast-track participant appeals process.
