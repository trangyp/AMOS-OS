### **3\. Functional Limits of Biometric Systems**
  * Biometrics capture **who** but not **how** a person functions.


  * Cannot detect nervous system regulation, emotional integrity, or systemic drift.


  * No use in psychological safety, ethical prediction, or trauma-informed systems.


  * Vulnerable to spoofing, data theft, and passive misuse.
