# Typed Tensor Contracts

`T_R = T[claim, evidence_class, domain, HML_scale, time, regime, observer, provenance, confidence, consequence, governance]`

## Fractal tensor
`T_F = T[object, HML_scale, recursion_depth, pattern_class, boundary, entropy_proxy, lacunarity_proxy, mutation_state, selection_state, time, regime, provenance]`

## Evidence tensor
`T_E = T[evidence_id, source_id, source_type, ancestry, timestamp, version, scope, regime, measurement, quality, independence, revocation_state]`

## Claim tensor
`T_C = T[claim_id, text, class, premises, evidence_refs, scope, regime, freshness, causal_level, competing_set, falsifiers, confidence_ceiling]`

## Governance tensor
`T_G = T[action, capability, authority, consequence_radius, reversibility, approval, rollback, evidence_threshold, mutation_class]`

## Memory tensor
`T_M = T[item_id, content_class, state, provenance, dependencies, freshness, contradiction_state, retention_class, revalidation_epoch]`

## Compatibility invariant
Tensor composition is prohibited until shared axes are semantically compatible.
Same-name axes do not prove same meaning.
