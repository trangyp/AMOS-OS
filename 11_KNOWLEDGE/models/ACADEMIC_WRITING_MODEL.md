# AMOS Academic Writing Engine

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Academic_Writing_Kernal_Engine_v0.json`

The **Academic Writing Engine** provides an end-to-end framework for generating, structuring, and translating academic knowledge. It enforces rigorous logical boundaries and formatting constraints.

## The 9-Layer Academic Stack

1. **L1: Problem Formulation** (Questions & Hypotheses)
2. **L2: Background & Literature** (Concept mapping, gap detection)
3. **L3: Theoretical Framework** (Constructs & relationships)
4. **L4: Methodology Design** (Sampling, power, analysis plan)
5. **L5: Data & Results** (Effect sizes, uncertainty reporting)
6. **L6: Discussion & Implications** (Interpretation, theory update)
7. **L7: Full Paper Composition** (IMRAD, Citations, Formatting)
8. **L8: Cross-Domain Translation** (Policy briefs, industry application)
9. **L9: Review & Critique** (Internal peer review, rebuttal generation)

## Core Principles

- **Structural Integrity:** No claim without support; no section without function.
- **Readability & Precision:** Jargon is minimized or defined; gradual complexity ramp.
- **Argument as System:** A paper is not just text, but an explicit claim graph linking methods to evidence.
- **Ethics & Integrity:** Strict avoidance of fabrication; uncertainty is flagged and assumptions stated.
