# AMOS Society & Culture Engine

**Version:** vInfinity.1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Society_Culture_Engine_v0.json`

The **Society & Culture Engine** models the evolution of institutions, cultural norms, demographics, and media information propagation.

## Core Sub-Kernels
1. **Institutional Kernel:** Models roles, rules, resource flows, enforcement, and legitimacy across states, markets, and civil society.
2. **Cultural Norms Kernel:** Models values, rituals, taboos, symbols, scripts, and status signals.
3. **Demographic Kernel:** Tracks cohorts, fertility, mortality, migration, and urbanization.
4. **Media Information Kernel:** Models channels, messages, amplifiers, filters, and network nodes.

## Applied Engines
- **System Map Engine:** Maps power flows and conflict hotspots.
- **Cultural Dynamics Engine:** Separates short-term noise from long-term value shifts, detecting fragility points.
- **Impact Assessment Engine:** Analyzes distributional impacts on vulnerable groups and social cohesion.

## Constraints
- Avoid prescriptive cultural judgments.
- Do not generate targeted manipulation strategies.
- Maintain neutrality and flag sensitive demographic topics.
