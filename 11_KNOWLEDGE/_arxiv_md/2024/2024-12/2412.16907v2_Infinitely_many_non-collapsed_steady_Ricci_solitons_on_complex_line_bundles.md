ON COMPLEX LINE BUNDLES
HANCI CHI
Abstract. We construct a continuous 3-parameter family of non-shrinking Ricci soli-
tons on complex line bundles O(k) over CP2m+1, where the base space is not necessarily
K¨ahler–Einstein. Each O(k) with k ∈[3, 2m+1] admits at least one asymptotically con-
ical (AC) Ricci-flat metric in this family. For each O(k) with k ≥3, the family includes
infinitely many asymptotically paraboloidal (AP) steady Ricci solitons.
1. Introduction
Ricci solitons are candidates for blow-up singularities of the Ricci flow. Specifically, a
shrinking Ricci soliton is a candidate for a Type I singularity while a steady Ricci soliton
is a candidate for a Type II singularity. From Perelman’s no-collapsing theorem [Per02],
Type II singularities must be non-collapsed.
Complex line bundles O(k) over CPn provide a rich source of examples for gradient Ricci
solitons. The principal orbit of O(k) is the lens space S2n+1/Zk, a circle bundle over CPn.
The integer k classifies the circle bundle, corresponding to a multiple of the indivisible
element in H2(CPn, Z). This setup has led to numerous examples of Ricci solitons with
various asymptotic behaviors. The B´erard-Bergery metric on O(n + 1) is an AC Ricci-flat
metric; see [BB82]. By [Cao96], it is known that O(n + 1) admits a 1-parameter family of
steady K¨ahler–Ricci solitons. These solitons have collapsed volume growth. In particular,
they are asymptotically cigar-paraboloidal (ACP). Expanding K¨ahler–Ricci solitons on
O(k) with k > n + 1 are found in [FIK03].
The U(n + 1)-invariant ansatz is further
generalized to complex line bundles over products of Fano manifolds in [DW11]. Recently,
new steady Ricci solitons were found on O(k) independently by [Win23], [Sto24] and
[App17]. It was proved in [App17] that each O(k) with k > n + 1 admits a non-collapsed
AP steady Ricci soliton.
In this article, we examine complex line bundles O(k) over CP2m+1, where the symmetry
condition is relaxed from U(2m+2)-invariance to Sp(m+1)Sp(1)-invariance, allowing the
base space to be squashed. Specifically, the associated group triple is:

In the special case where k = 1, this construction yields the quaternionic Hopf fibration.
Since the principal orbit is locally isometric to S4m+3, the cohomogeneity one dynamical
system on O(k) is the same for different values of k. The same dynamical system also
appeared in the author’s earlier work [Chi26], where a continuous 1-parameter family of
non-collapsed steady Ricci solitons were constructed on Euclidean spaces. However, the
geometric interpretation of the solutions is different in the present setting. Here we study
complex line bundles O(k) →CP2m+1, where the global existence of complete metrics
depends crucially on the integer parameter k. This leads to new families of complete Ricci
solitons.
For k ∈{1, 2}, we construct a compact invariant set to obtain the following theorem.
Theorem 1.1. For k ∈{1, 2}, there exists a continuous 3-parameter family of complete
Ricci solitons {ξ(k, θ, s4, s5) | θ ∈(0, π), s4 ≥0, s5 ≥0} on O(k). In particular,
(1) each ξ(k, θ, s4, s5) with s4, s5 > 0 is an AC non-Einstein expanding Ricci soliton;
(2) each ξ(k, θ, 0, s5) with s5 > 0 is an asymptotically hyperbolic (AH) Einstein metric;
(3) each ξ(k, θ, 0, 0) is an asymptotically locally conical (ALC) Ricci-flat metric. The
base of the asymptotic cone is the non-K¨ahler CP2m+1/Zk;
(4) each ξ(k, θ, s4, 0) with s4 > 0 is an ACP steady Ricci soliton. The base of the
asymptotic paraboloid is the non-K¨ahler CP2m+1/Zk.
The parameter s5 controls the generalized mean curvature of the principal orbit. As
the generalized mean curvature is freely scaled under homothetic change if ϵ = 0, the
parameter s5 is not needed and we set s5 = 0 for the steady case. The parameter s4 is
related to the second derivative of the potential function, and the Ricci soliton is Einstein
if s4 = 0. The parameter θ controls how CPm+1 is squashed as a twistor space over HPm.
Solutions with θ = 0 admit U(2m + 2)-symmetry and we have the Fubini–Study CPm+1.
The limiting case where θ = π blows up the base space HPm. The cohomogeneity one
steady soliton equation of this limiting case is equivalent to that of a complex line bundle
over an irreducible CP1, whose principal orbit is S3/Zk.
If k ≥3, our techniques for proving Theorem 1.1 become obsolete. Nevertheless, we
generalize [App17, Theorem 5.1] and obtain the following existence theorems.
Theorem 1.2. For each k ≥3 and θ ∈(0, π), there exists a βk,θ ≥0 such that
{ξ(k, θ, s4, s5) | s4 ≥βk,θ, s5 > 0} is a continuous 2-parameter family of complete ex-
panding Ricci solitons. In particular,
(1) each ξ(k, θ, s4, s5) with s4 > βk,θ is an AC non-Einstein Ricci soliton;
(2) each ξ(k, θ, βk,θ, s5) is an AH negative Einstein metric if βk,θ = 0, an AC non-
Einstein Ricci soliton if βk,θ > 0.
Theorem 1.3. For each k ≥3 and θ ∈(0, π), there exists an αk,θ ≥0 such that
{ξ(k, θ, s4, 0) | s4 ≥αk,θ} is a continuous 1-parameter family of complete steady Ricci
solitons. In particular,

Compared to Theorem 1.1, the asymptotics for steady solitons in Theorem 1.3 are less
settled, as they depend on the value of αk,θ. The boundary cases where θ ∈{0, π}, however,
are better understood in the previous examples. Specifically, the metrics ξ(2m + 2, 0, 0, 0)
and ξ(2, π, 0, 0) are AC Ricci-flat metrics, with the asymptotic cones’ bases being the
standard orbifolds S4m+3/Z2m+2 and S3/Z2, respectively; see [BB82]. The existence of
αk,0 and αk,π are established in [App17, Theorem 5.1]. In particular, we have αk,0 > 0 if
k ≥2m + 3, and αk,π > 0 if k ≥3. Thanks to these results, we can further obtain the
following existence theorem for some non-collapsed steady Ricci solitons.
Theorem 1.4. On each O(k) with 3 ≤k ≤2m+1, there exists at least one AC Ricci-flat
metric, whose base of the cone is the Jensen S4m+3/Zk. On each O(k) with k ≥3, there
exist infinitely many AP steady Ricci solitons whose base of the paraboloid is the Jensen
S4m+3/Zk.
