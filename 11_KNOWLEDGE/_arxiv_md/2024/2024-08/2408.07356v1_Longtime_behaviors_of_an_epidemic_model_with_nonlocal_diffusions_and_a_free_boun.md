Abstract.We propose a nonlocal epidemic model whose spatial domain evolves over
time and is represented by [0, h(t)] with h(t) standing for the spreading front of epi-
demic. It is assumed that the agents can cross the ﬁxed boundary x = 0, but they
will die immediately if they do it, which implies that the area (−∞, 0) is a hostile en-
vironment for the agents. We ﬁrst show that this model is well posed, then prove that
the longtime behaviors are governed by a spreading-vanishing dichotomy and ﬁnally
give some criteria determining spreading and vanishing. Particularly, we obtain the
asymptotical behaviors of the principal eigenvalue of a cooperative system with non-
local diﬀusions without assuming the related nonlocal operator is self-adjoint, and the
steady state problem of such cooperative system on half space [0, ∞) is studied in detail.
Keywords: Nonlocal diﬀusion; epidemic model; free boundary; principal eigenvalue;
spreading and vanishing.
