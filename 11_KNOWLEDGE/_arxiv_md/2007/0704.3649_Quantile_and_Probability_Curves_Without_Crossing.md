  # 3. BOOTSTRAP CAPSULE
  # ============================================================

  bootstrap_capsule:

    class: DERIVED

    text: >
      The paper solves quantile-crossing and related monotonicity violations
      by replacing an estimated curve Qhat with its monotone rearrangement
      Qhat*, obtained by treating Qhat(U|x), U~Uniform(0,1), as a random
      variable and taking its quantile function. Rearrangement preserves the
      value distribution of the original curve while sorting it into increasing
      order. The paper proves that Qhat* is no farther from the true monotone
      target than Qhat in Lp norm, and is strictly closer under specified
      crossing conditions. It derives Hadamard derivatives of the
      pre-rearrangement and rearrangement operators, functional limit laws,
      asymptotic equivalence in the correctly monotone population case,
      valid bootstrap inference, and limit theory for smoothed and integrated
      functionals. In an empirical IV-quantile application and a matching
      Monte Carlo design, rearrangement removes crossings and often improves
      estimation error relative to both the original and isotonic estimators.
