# Quantile and Probability Curves Without Crossing

**arXiv ID:** 0704.3649
**Year:** 2007
**Domain:** Statistics / Quantile Regression

## Summary

This paper addresses the crossing problem in quantile regression — where estimated quantile curves cross each other, violating the monotonicity property. The authors propose methods to ensure non-crossing quantile and probability curves.
