## 33. AMOS COMPRESSION CAPSULE

**Paper:** Crépieux et al., arXiv:0704.1427v1.

**Question:** How does Coulomb interaction modify photo-assisted shot noise?

**FQH result:** fractional quasiparticle transport produces predicted
evenly spaced singularities in the noise derivative, with spacing tied to
(\\nu e).

**CNT result:** Luttinger-liquid interactions smooth the conventional
photo-assisted staircase; finite nanotube size progressively restores
step-like behavior.

**Shared mechanism:** AC voltage generates Bessel-weighted photon
sidebands (\\omega_0+p\\omega).

**Critical validity boundary:** the treatment is model- and
regime-dependent; perturbative divergences outside its validity domain
must not be interpreted physically.
