  bootstrap_capsule:
    class: DERIVED

    text: >
      The paper models how AC-driven photo-assisted shot noise changes in
      Coulomb-interacting mesoscopic conductors. In a fractional quantum Hall
      system, interactions and fractional quasiparticle charge transform the
      noninteracting staircase into evenly spaced singular features whose
      spacing is tied to the filling factor/effective charge. In an interacting
      carbon nanotube injected from an STM tip, Coulomb interactions smooth the
      differential-noise staircase, while finite-size/contact effects can restore
      more step-like behavior when the wavepacket scale becomes comparable to or
      exceeds the nanotube length.
