      The paper models dephasing of a quantum-dot level caused by charge
      fluctuations from a nearby fractional-quantum-Hall quantum point contact.
      In weak backscattering, quasiparticle tunneling produces a non-equilibrium
      dephasing rate proportional to zero-frequency backscattering-current noise,
      with anomalous Luttinger-liquid bias scaling. Strong backscattering is

      the dephasing amplitude.
