  # 3. BOOTSTRAP CAPSULE

  bootstrap_capsule:

    class: DERIVED

    text: >
      The paper measures current noise in quantum point contacts versus
      source-drain bias, gate voltage, and in-plane magnetic field. Johnson
      noise is used to calibrate electron temperature and gain. Finite-bias
      excess noise probes electron partitioning. At zero field, the noise factor
      shows an asymmetric suppression associated with the 0.7 conductance
      structure; with increasing in-plane field this evolves smoothly into the
      symmetric double-dome signature expected for spin-resolved transport.
      A phenomenological model in which spin splitting grows with one-dimensional
      electron density reproduces much of the conductance and noise behavior
      using parameters extracted from conductance. A second device exhibits
      finite plateau noise consistent with bias-dependent electron heating,
      modeled through Wiedemann-Franz thermal transport. The source therefore
      separates three load-bearing contributions: equilibrium Johnson noise,
      partition shot noise, and device-specific thermal heating noise.
