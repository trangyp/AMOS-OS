  bootstrap_capsule:

    class: DERIVED

    text: >
      The paper generalizes a Vanishing Conjecture previously formulated for
      Laplacians and Hessian-nilpotent polynomials to differential operators
      with constant coefficients via the notion of Λ-nilpotency:
      Λ^m P^m = 0 for all m≥1. Its central theorem states that the Jacobian
      Conjecture is equivalent to VC/HVC for all second-order homogeneous
      constant-coefficient differential operators. Rank and linear-change-of-
      variable invariance are the main transport mechanism. The paper then
      transfers known HN/JC results into the Λ-nilpotent setting, studies
      higher-order operators and Laurent-polynomial reformulations, and develops
      a formal bridge between sequences Λ^m P^m and classical orthogonal
      polynomials, culminating in isotropy results for homogeneous
      full-rank Δ_A-nilpotent polynomials.
