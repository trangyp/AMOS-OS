Abstract
We show that a certain action which gives rise to the pure spin
connection formulation of gravity by CDJ can be consistently derived
from the action for the instanton representation of Plebanski gravity.
This is an illustrative example of when certain symmetries of the basic
ﬁelds commute with the symmetries of the equations of motion.
