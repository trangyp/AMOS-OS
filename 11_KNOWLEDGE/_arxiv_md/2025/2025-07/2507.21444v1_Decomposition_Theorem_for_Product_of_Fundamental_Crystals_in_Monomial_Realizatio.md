Decomposition Theorem for Product of Fundamental Crystals in
Monomial Realization of type Cn
Manal Alshuqayr1 and Toshiki Nakashima2
Abstract
We consider a product of fundamental crystals of type Cn in monomial realization,
where the product means a natural product of Laurent monomials, not a tensor product.
Then we shall show that the product still holds a crystal structure and describe how it is
decomposed into irreducible crystals, which is, in general, diﬀerent from the decomposition
rule for the tensor product of the fundamental crystals.
1. Introduction
