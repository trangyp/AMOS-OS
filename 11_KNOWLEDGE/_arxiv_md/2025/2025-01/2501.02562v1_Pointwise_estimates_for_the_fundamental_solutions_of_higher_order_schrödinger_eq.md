In recent years, there has been a surge of interest in the dispersive estimates for higher
order Schr¨odinger operators H = (−∆)m + V. Local dispersive estimates have been es-
tablished by Feng, Soﬀer, and Yao [12], as well as Feng, Soﬀer, Wu, and Yao [13].
For fourth-order Schr¨odinger equations, Erdoˇgan, Green and Toprak [9] derived global
dispersive estimates for three-dimensional cases, with Green and Toprak [18] later ex-
tending these results to four-dimensional scenarios. Subsequent work by Soﬀer, Wu, and
Yao [40], along with Li, Soﬀer, and Yao [28], demonstrated dispersive bounds in 1-D and
2-D settings, respectively. Moreover, Erdoˇgan, Goldberg, and Green [8] established dis-
persive estimates for scaling-critical potentials under the condition 2m < n < 4m and
m > 1. For n > 2m, Erdoˇgan and Green [10,11] obtained dispersive estimates by exam-
ining the Lp boundedness of wave operators. Most recently, pointwise estimates for the
fundamental solution of e−itH in odd dimensions were proved in [5].
We brieﬂy outline the strategy employed in the proof of Theorem 1.1. Inspired by the
work on Schr¨odinger operators, our starting point is to represent the propagator via the
Stone’s formula. The peculiarity of the rank one perturbation is the so-called Aronszajn-
Krein formula (see (2.2)). As usual, we treat the high and low energy parts respectively.
One of the key ingredients throughout the proof is to take suitable space-time decompo-
sitions when estimating various oscillatory integrals appeared in both the high and low
energy regimes. This strategy ultimately leads to a simpliﬁcation of the problem to the
analysis of certain 1-dim oscillatory integrals, as detailed in Lemmas 2.7 and 2.6.
