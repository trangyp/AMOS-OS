3. Capped Volatility Process and VIX Option Pricing

prices FV (T) in (4) and VIX call option prices CV (K, T) in (5) are inﬁnite. On the other hand the

practical usefulness of the SABR model with β < 1 for pricing these products.

prices. The predictions for this case have been discussed in [17] and [29].
