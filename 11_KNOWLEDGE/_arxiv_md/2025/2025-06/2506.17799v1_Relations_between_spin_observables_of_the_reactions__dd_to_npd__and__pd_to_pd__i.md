Relations between spin observables of the reactions
dd →npd and pd →pd in impulse approximation
Yu.N. Uzikova,b,c1
a V.P. Dzhelepov Laboratory of Nuclear Problems, Joint Institute for Nuclear
Researches, Dubna, 141980 Russia
b M.V. Lomonosov State University, Physics Faculty, Moscow, 119991 Russia
c Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University),
141701, Dolgoprudnyi, Moscow region, Russia
We have shown that the vector and tensor analyzing powers Ay, Ayy and also
double spin correlation coeﬃcients Cy,y, Cyy,y of the reaction dd →n + p + d
in impulse approximation are linearly connected to corresponding observables of
the pd- elastic scattering. Obtained relations are necessary for motivation of the
polarization experiments for the ﬁrst phase of the SPD NICA project and for
analysis of expected data.
PACS: 13.88.+e, 13.75.-n, 13.85.Hd, 03.65.Nk
Introduction
Spin observables of pd-elastic scattering are reasonable well described by
spin dependent Glauber theory [1–4] using existing experimental data on spin
amplitudes of elastic pp- and pn-scattering. This means, that experimental
data on spin observables of the pd-elastic scattering being compared with cor-
responding calculations on the basis of spin-dependent Glauber theory can be
used as an eﬀective test of spin amplitudes of pN-elastic scattering [5]. Such
kind of test is important at energies of SPD NICA collider √sNN = 5 −20
GeV, where data on spin pN amplitudes are very noncomplete and existing
phenomenological model parametrizations for pN-sccatering [6–8] need a ver-
iﬁcation. Up to now, Glauber theory of elastic dd-scattering is widely used for
description of unpolarized diﬀerential cross section [9], but spin-dependent
nucleon-nucleon elastic scattering amplitudes are not yet included into such
kind of formalism. Therefore, the spin observables of dd-elastic scattering
cannot be used for testing spin pN-amplitudes. Since only symmetric collid-
ing mode of dd-collision is planned to be realised at SPD NICA, we analyse
here the relations between spin obervables in the dd →n + p + d reaction in
the impulse approximation, assuming the mechanism of the pole diagram in
Fig. (1), and corresponding observables in pd →pd scattering. We consider
vector and tenzor analyzing powers Ay, Ayy and also spin correlation coeﬃ-
cients Cy,y, Cyy,y of the reaction dd →n+p+d and as a result, we show that
these observables are connected with similar observables of the pd- elastic
scattering by linear relations.

Summary
The main results of this work are given in Eqs. (11),(13),(14) for vector
and tenzor analyzing powers and in Eqs.
(26), (33) for double spin cor-
relations. The obtained results are restricted by the pole diagram for the
transition amplitutde of the reaction dd →npd and S-wave approximation
for the deuteron wave function. Both this approximations are expected to
be valid at small internal relative momenta q < 0.15GeV/c between nucleons
in the deuteron. At higher momenta q > 0.2GeV/c the contribution of the
D-wave increases and has to be taken into accout. Furthermore, at momenta
q > 0.2GeV/c, the contribution of rescatterings of ﬁnal proton and deuteron
on the ﬁnal neutron is increasing because the relative distances between nu-
cleons in the deuteron d1 become smaller. These eﬀects will be considered in
a separate publication.
