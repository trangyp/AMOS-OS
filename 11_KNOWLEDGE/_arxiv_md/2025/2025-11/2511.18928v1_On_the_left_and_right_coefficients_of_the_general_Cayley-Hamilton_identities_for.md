1. INTRODUCTION AND BASIC DEFINITIONS
The natural symmetrization of the determinant and the adjoint matrix can serve
as a starting point of a symmetric variant of the classical determinant theory for
square matrices over an arbitrary ring. The most important feature of this approach
is that it works for square matrices over an arbitrary ring and especially over a Lie
nilpotent ring.
The Grassmann (exterior) algebra
E = K ⟨v1, v2, ..., vi, ... | vivj + vjvi = 0 for all 1 ≤i ≤j⟩
(over a field K) generated by the infinite sequence of anticommutative indetermi-
nates (vi)i≥1 is our basic example, which is Lie nilpotent of index 2.
The natural Z2-grading is E = E0 ⊕E1 with E0 being the K-subspace (subalgebra)
generated by the monomials of even length and E1 being the K-subspace generated
by the monomials of odd length. Any element g ∈E can be uniquely written as
g = g0 + g1, where g0 ∈E0 is the even and g1 ∈E1 is the odd component of g.
Notice that g0 ∈E0 = Z(E) is central and (g1)2 = 0.
