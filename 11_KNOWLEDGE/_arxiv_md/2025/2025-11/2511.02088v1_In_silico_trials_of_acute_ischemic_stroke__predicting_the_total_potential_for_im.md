1 Introduc*on 
Acute ischaemic stroke (AIS) occurs due to an occlusion, by a thrombus, of an artery supplying blood to the brain. The occlusion

blood perfusion within the aﬀected region and its surrounds. Detailed analysis of the limita*ons of these models have been 
discussed in previous publica*ons [24–29]. 
Finally, we do not explicitly model any adverse events, such as haemorrhage or thrombus fragmenta*on. Though these
