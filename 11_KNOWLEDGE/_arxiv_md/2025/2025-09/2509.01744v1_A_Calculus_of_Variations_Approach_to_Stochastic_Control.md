A Calculus of Variations Approach to Stochastic Control
Matthew Lorig ∗
This version: September 3, 2025
Abstract
We use classical tools from calculus of variations to formally derive necessary conditions for a Markov control to
be optimal in a standard finite time horizon stochastic control problem. As an example, we solve the well-known
Merton portfolio optimization problem.
Keywords: Calculus of variations, stochastic optimal control, functional derivative
