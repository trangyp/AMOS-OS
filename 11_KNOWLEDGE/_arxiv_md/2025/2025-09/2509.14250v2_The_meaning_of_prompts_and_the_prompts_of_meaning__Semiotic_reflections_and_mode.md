In prompting, indexicality serves an important function because it creates the basis for coherent and

Indexical prompts establish meaning by creating a direct connection to specific elements within the

maintaining conversational coherence and enables complex, multi-turn interactions that build upon 
previous exchanges. The indexical function extends beyond a simple deictic reference to encompass more

prompting thus enable the construction of extended dialogues where meaning emerge, not just from
