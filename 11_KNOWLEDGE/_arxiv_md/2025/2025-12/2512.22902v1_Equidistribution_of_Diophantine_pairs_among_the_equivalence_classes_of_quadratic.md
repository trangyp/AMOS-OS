GORAN DRAˇZI´C, MATIJA KAZALICKI, AND RUDI MRAZOVI´C
Abstract. For a fixed integer n, we say that {a, c} ⊂Z\{0} is a D(n)-pair if ac+n is a perfect
square. In this short note we prove that D(n)-pairs are asymptotically equidistributed (via their
associated quadratic forms) among proper SL2(Z)-equivalence classes of binary quadratic forms
of discriminant 4n with fixed content. As a consequence, we obtain a more streamlined and
simpler proof of Badesa’s asymptotic formula for the number of D(n)-pairs.
1. Introduction
Let R be a ring and n ∈R. A D(n)-m-tuple in R is a set of m distinct nonzero elements
such that the product of any two of them plus n is a perfect square. When R = Z (or Q) and
n = 1, such sets are called (rational) Diophantine m-tuples, and they have been studied since
antiquity.
