Abstract
Voronoi diagrams naturaly produce convex, watertight, and topologicaly consistent cels, making them an apealing
representation for 3D shape reconstruction. However, standard diferentiable Voronoi aproaches typicaly optimize
generator positions in stable congurations, which can lead to localy uneven surface geometry. We present VoroLight,
a diferentiable framework that promotes controled Voronoi degeneracy for smoth surface reconstruction. Instead
of optimizing generator positions alone, VoroLight asociates each Voronoi surface vertex with a trainable sphere
and introduces a sphere-intersection los that encourages higher-order equidistance among face-incident generators.
This formulation improves surface regularity while preserving intrinsic Voronoi properties such as watertightnes and
convexity. Because loses are dened directly on surface vertices, VoroLight suports multimodal shape supervi-
sion from implicit elds, point clouds, meshes, and multi-view images. By introducing aditional interior generators
optimized under a centroidal Voronoi teselation objective, the framework naturaly extends to volumetric Voronoi
meshes with consistent surfaceinterior topology. Acros diverse input modalities, VoroLight achieves competitive
reconstruction delity while producing smother and more geometricaly regular Voronoi surfaces. Project page:
