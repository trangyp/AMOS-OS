Abstract:
A Weyl invariant extension of Einstein gravity is studied. It simply consists
in the group averaging of Einstein’s action under Weyl transformations.
Contradicting
cherished beliefs, a conformal anomaly is found in the trace of the equations of motion
if diﬀeomorphism invariance is to be a symmetry of the quantum theory. This anomaly
vanishes on shell which, according to general principles, means that there must exist a
gauge in which it vanishes even oﬀshell. It is however possible to keep Weyl invariance as
a bona ﬁde symmetry at the price of losing full diﬀeomorphism invariance. This is what
happens in unimodular gravity, a closely related theory.
