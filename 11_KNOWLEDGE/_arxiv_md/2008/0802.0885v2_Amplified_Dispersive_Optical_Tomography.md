**arXiv ID:** 0802.0885v2
**Year:** 2008
**Domain:** Optics / Optical Coherence Tomography

## Summary

This paper presents amplified dispersive optical tomography, a technique combining Raman amplification with dispersive Fourier transform for optical coherence tomography applications.
