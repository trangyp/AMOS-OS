ASYMPTOTIC ANALYSIS FOR APPROXIMATE HARMONIC MAPS FROM
DEGENERATING CYLINDERS AND APPLICATIONS TO MINIMAL SURFACES
JIAYU LI, LEI LIU, AND MIAOMIAO ZHU
Abstract. We investigate the blow-up analysis and quantitative behavior for a sequence of maps
{un}∞
n=1 from degenerating tori (T 2, gn) or from degenerating cylinders (S 1 × [0, π], gn) with free
boundary conditions un(S 1 × {0, π}) ⊂K to a compact Riemannian manifold (N, h) satisfying
E(un) + ∥τ(un, gn)∥L2 ≤Λ < ∞,
where τ(un, gn) is the tension field of un, K ⊂N is a smooth submanifold. We establish generalized

some geodesics on N or some geodesic-like curves on K where some length formulas are given.
This partially confirms a conjecture by Ding-Li-Liu [4] in the sense of approximate sequence case.
Moreover, we study an evolution system to seek minimal cylinders in a compact Riemannian
manifold with free boundary and with arbitrary codimensions. By studying the convergence of the
flow at infinity, we obtain some existence results of minimal cylinders with free boundary. Compared
with the closed case in, an interesting new phenomenon here is that the neck may converges to a
geodesic-like curve on K.

In dimension two, i.e. (M, g) is a surface, harmonic maps have important applications to minimal
surface. It is well known that a conformal harmonic map is in fact a minimal surface. Thus it
provides a way to obtain minimal surfaces (with arbitrary codimension) in a give Riemannian
manifold by finding conformal harmonic maps.
From the perspective of variational methods, this was achieved by Schoen-Yau [18], Sacks-
Uhlenbeck [16], Sacks-Uhlenbeck [17] for closed surfaces. The idea is that for each conformal
structure on the domain surface, one produces an energy minimizing map which is a harmonic
map, and then minimize over all conformal structures to produce a branched minimal surface.
Later, this idea was used to produce minimal surfaces with free boundary, see Fraser [6] for the
case of minimal disks and Chen-Fraser-Pang [1] for high genus cases.
