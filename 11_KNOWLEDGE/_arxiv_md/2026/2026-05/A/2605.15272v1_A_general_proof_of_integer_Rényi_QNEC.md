aInstituto Balseiro, Centro Atómico Bariloche, S.C. de Bariloche, 8400, Río Negro, Argentina

Abstract: The Rényi quantum null energy condition conjectures that the second null
shape variation of the sandwiched Rényi divergence (SRD) of an excited state relative to
the vacuum is non-negative in local Poincaré-invariant quantum field theory, giving a one-
parameter generalization of the quantum null energy condition (QNEC). We prove Rényi
QNEC for all integer Rényi parameters n ≥2 for von Neumann algebras carrying a half-
sided modular inclusion structure. The only assumption on the excited state is finiteness
of its SRD relative to the vacuum. Concretely, for any σ-finite von Neumann algebra with
such an inclusion, we prove log-convexity, under the associated null-translation semigroup,
of the Kosaki Ln norm of any normal positive functional with finite Ln norm.

Introduction
QNEC was first conjectured as a non-gravitational limit of the quantum focusing conjec-
ture in semi-classical gravity [1], and has since been proven in free QFTs [2, 3], holographic
QFTs with AdS duals [4], and general local Poincaré-invariant QFTs [5]. Moreover, math-
ematically rigorous proofs of the QNEC have been provided using von Neumann algebraic
techniques [6, 7].
A rather elegant, albeit non-rigorous, information theoretic argument for the QNEC
was presented in [8] by Wall. The argument exploits the fact that local Poincaré-invariant
QFTs obey a global energy condition, namely the averaged null energy condition (ANEC),
which has been proven under various conditions [9–13]. Schematically, the ANEC is:

global energy condition, the knowledge of all information in a given region places a lower
bound on the energy in the complementary region. The argument relies on an estimate for
the minimum energy in the complementary region given all information in a region, the
invariance of this estimate under local unitaries, and the fact that the estimate must satisfy
the data processing inequality for quantum channels. This reasoning leads to the QNEC,
which in a 1 + 1-dimensional QFT takes the form

The von Neumann entanglement entropy and the one-point function of the energy-
momentum tensor are ultraviolet (UV) divergent in QFT, so neither side of (1.2) is in-
dividually well-defined; only their difference is finite. Ceyhan and Faulkner [6], and later
Hollands and Longo [7], make Wall’s argument mathematically rigorous for arbitrary QFTs
that admit the structure of half-sided modular inclusions (HSMI) [14–18], assuming that
the state Ψ has finite averaged null energy. Relative entropy in QFT is UV-finite, and
[6] rigorously prove the QNEC as the non-negativity of the second null derivative of the

Our interest in QNEC stems, among other things, from the fact that it is not merely a
formal consistency condition on QFTs, and can lead to non-trivial quantum thermodynamic
bounds on quantities such as entropy production in dynamical processes. These bounds have
been found in quenched holographic QFTs [19–21], and general 1+1-dimensional conformal
field theories [22] (see also [23]). QNEC has also been applied to prove renormalization group

which is a one-parameter generalization of the relative entropy.

Main theorem
Our main result, Theorem 5.5, is a proof of the RQNEC for integer α ≥2 in general
Poincaré-invariant QFTs that possess the structure of half sided modular inclusions (HSMI),
with the single additional hypothesis that the SRD of the excited state relative to the QFT
vacuum is finite. Stated differently, with integer α ≥2, for Kosaki Lα spaces constructed
with a reference normal faithful state on any (σ-finite) von Neumann algebra, our main
result Theorem 5.4 is the log-convexity under HSMI of the Kosaki Lα norm of any normal
positive functional in the predual that has finite Lα norm. These terms are all defined in
Sections 2 and 3. A key point of our proof is that no differentiability or generator-domain
assumptions are imposed on the excited state.
The finiteness of SRD (equivalently Lα
norms) is the minimal condition under which the RQNEC (equivalently log-convexity) is
well defined.

1.3
Outline
The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Sec. 2 we discuss some preliminaries on
Haagerup’s non-commutative Lp spaces and define the SRD. This is followed by Sec. 3
where we describe the half-sided modular inclusions, null translations and the statement of
the Rényi QNEC conjecture. Sec. 4 constructs the contractive semigroup extension of null
translations on Lp spaces and proves the Ward identity. Then in Sec. 5, we obtain the null
derivative formulas and prove RQNEC under a regularity assumption, and then relax the
assumption and complete the proof for general excited states. Sec. 6 discusses related open
problems and future directions.
Appendix A compiles standard definitions and results on topologies, integrals and one-
parameter semigroups on Banach spaces, and also includes useful continuity properties in
Haagerup Lp spaces. In Appendix B we provide a detailed summary of the proof and a
table of notation.

6
Discussion and future directions
We have proven the integer RQNEC (Conjecture 3.6) for arbitrary von Neumann algebras
carrying a half-sided modular inclusion structure, under the single additional hypothesis
that the SRD of the excited state with respect to the vacuum is finite on the larger algebra
MA. In particular, no form-domain assumption Ψ ∈Dom(P 1/2) is required, in contrast to
the proofs of QNEC [6, 7]. Whether QNEC itself can be proven without such a form-domain
assumption remains an interesting open problem.
An immediate target for future work is to prove RQNEC for general real Rényi param-
eter α > 1. We expect that the proof technique used here can be adapted to prove this
statement, and we hope to present the details in a future publication.
In the remainder of this section, we discuss several directions for future research.

Further generalizations of Rényi QNEC
A family of divergences known as optimized quantum f-divergences has been studied for
finite-dimensional systems [55]. These divergences satisfy the data processing inequality,
and the SRD is one example of this family. It would be interesting to study these divergences
in the von Neumann algebra setting for general QFTs, and prove convexity statements sim-
ilar to RQNEC. It is also natural to conjecture the α-z Rényi QNEC for the α-z Rényi
divergence [56], a two-parameter generalization of the relative entropy. It has been formu-
lated in terms of Haagerup Lp spaces [33, 57, 58], and investigating its log-convexity under
null translations will also be interesting.
