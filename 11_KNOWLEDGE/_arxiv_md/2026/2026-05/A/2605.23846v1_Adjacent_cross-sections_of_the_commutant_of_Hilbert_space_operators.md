A recent approach to (ISP) in an arbitrary (inﬁnite dimensional) Banach
space X was initiated in [APTT], and culminated in [Tc] proving that every
operator T on X has an almost invariant half-space. We recall that M is a
half-space in X if M and X/M are inﬁnite dimensional. Furthermore, M is
almost invariant for T if M is invariant for a ﬁnite rank perturbation of T . The
technique of this approach was reﬁned in the Hilbert space setting in [JKP],
which led to a structure theorem in the paper [HP].
