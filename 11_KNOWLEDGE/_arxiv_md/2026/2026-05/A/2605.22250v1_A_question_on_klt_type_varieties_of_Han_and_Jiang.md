A QUESTION ON KLT TYPE VARIETIES OF HAN AND JIANG
JIHAO LIU
Abstract. We prove that being of klt type is not an open condition in flat families of varieties.
This answers a question of Han and Jiang. The construction in this paper substantially uses
generative AI: the general idea for the counterexample was suggested by ChatGPT Pro 5.5, and
the explicit example was found and proved by the Rethlas system.
1. Introduction
Han and Jiang asked [HJ26, Problem 4.6] whether being of klt type is an open condition in a
flat family of varieties. We answer their question by using Singh’s determinantal family [Sin99].
Theorem 1.1. Being of klt type is not an open condition in flat families of varieties. As a
consequence, [HJ26, Problem 4.6] has a negative answer.

Let P ⊂R be a prime of height 1. If s /∈P, then RP is a height-one localization of the
normal ring R[s, s−1]. If E /∈P, then RP is a height-one localization of
RE ≃R[E, E−1].
In both cases RP is regular. Finally, s, E cannot both lie in P, because
R/sR ≃S[E]
and E is a nonzerodivisor on S[E], so (s, E) is an R-regular sequence. Hence R satisfies R1.
Since R is Cohen–Macaulay, Serre’s criterion implies that R is normal.
The morphism π is flat. The map C[T] →R is injective because it admits the retraction
R →C[T],
A, B, C, D 7→0.
Since R is a domain, it is torsion-free over the PID C[T], hence flat. By base change,
R = R ⊗C[T] C[s, E]
is flat over C[s, E], and therefore over C[s].
The closed fibers are identified as follows. For s = 0,
R/sR ≃R[E]/(T) ≃S[E],
so X0 ≃Spec S × A1
E, already shown to be of klt type. If a ∈C∗, then the change of variables
T = aE gives
R/(s −a) ≃C[A, B, C, D, E]/IaE ≃R.
Thus Xa ≃Spec R for every a ̸= 0. In particular all closed fibers are normal.
It remains to see that the nonzero fibers are not of klt type. Let
RZ = Z[A, B, C, D, T]/IT,Z,
Rp = RZ ⊗Z Fp,
where IT,Z is generated by the same three 2 × 2 minors over Z. By [Sin99, Proposition 4.5], for
m = 5 and n = 2, the ring Rp is not F-pure for every prime p ∤10.
Suppose that Spec R were of klt type.
Then there exists a Q-divisor ∆≥0 such that
(Spec R, ∆) is klt.
By [Tak04, Corollary 3.4], after spreading out, for closed points µ in a
dense open subset of Spec A0, the corresponding reduction is strongly F-regular as a pair; in
particular, the underlying ring is F-pure. This is not possible because the image in Spec Z of a
dense open subset of Spec A0 contains a dense set of primes, but Rp is not F-pure for any prime
p ∤10.
Consequently, X0 is of klt type, while Xs is not of klt type for every s ∈C∗. Since every
Zariski-open neighborhood of 0 in A1
C contains a nonzero closed point, the klt-type fiber locus
is not open. This proves the theorem.
□
Remark 1.2. Junpeng Jiao informed that our result is more or less well-known by experts
(although the author cannot find any direct reference, particularly after [HJ26]), and the more
natural question to ask is whether klt is a constructible condition or not. As a counterpart, it
is interesting to ask whether being Fano type is a constructible condition or not.
Remark 1.3. The counterexample in this paper was obtained with the assistance of generative
AI. The general idea for the construction was suggested by ChatGPT Pro 5.5; the explicit exam-
ple was then found and proved by the Rethlas system. See [Ju+26] for a detailed introduction
to the Rethlas system.
Due to the limitation of generative AI, it is possible that we have missed some related refer-
ences in the literature, and we welcome any comments from experts.
