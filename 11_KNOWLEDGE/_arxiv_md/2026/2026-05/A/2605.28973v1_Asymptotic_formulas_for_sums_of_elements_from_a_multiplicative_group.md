Abstract. Let K be a number field, k ≥2 an integer, (K∗)k the
k-fold direct product of K∗with coordinatewise multiplication,
and Γ a finitely generated subgroup of rank r of (K∗)k. Further,
let H(α) denote the absolute exponential height of an algebraic
number α. Fix non-zero elements a1, . . . , ak ∈K. We give as-
ymptotic formulas for the number of x = (x1, . . . , xk) ∈Γ with
H(a1x1 +· · ·+akxk) ≤X as X →∞such that no non-empty sub-
sum of a1x1 + · · · + akxk vanishes. By the same method of proof,
we obtain an asymptotic formula as X →∞for the number of
non-negative integers n with H(un) ≤X, where {un} is a linear
recurrence sequence.
