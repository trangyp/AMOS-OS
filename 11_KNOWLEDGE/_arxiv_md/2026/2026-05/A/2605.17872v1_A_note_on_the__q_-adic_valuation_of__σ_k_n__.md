A note on the q-adic valuation of σk(n)
Olivier Bordellès
Abstract
In this note, we obtain an exact formula for the q-adic valuation of σk(n) where q is an odd prime,
allowing us to derive an explicit upper bound which is asymptotically better than the previous bound
obtained by Zhao when n is large and k Ê q −2. The key parts are played by the LTE lemma and the
use of cyclotomic polynomials.
