A GEOMETRIC CORRESPONDENCE FOR REPARAMETERIZATIONS OF
GEODESIC FLOWS
STEPHEN CANTRELL, D´IDAC MART´INEZ-GRANADO, AND EDUARDO REYES
Abstract. For any non-elementary, torsion-free hyperbolic group, we provide a correspon-
dence between the left-invariant Gromov-hyperbolic metrics on the group that are quasi-
isometric to a word metric, and continuous reparameterizations of the associated Mineyev’s
flow space. From this correspondence, we produce the first examples of continuous reparam-
eterizations of geodesic flows on negatively curved manifolds with all periodic orbits having
integer lengths. For surface and free groups, this also yields isometric actions on Gromov-
hyperbolic spaces on which loxodromic elements are precisely the non-simple elements. Key
ingredients in our proof are an analysis of the geometry of Mineyev’s flow space (such as the
metric-Anosov property recently proven by Dilsavor), and the density of Green metrics in
the moduli space of (symmetric) metrics on the group.
We further establish continuity of the Bowen–Margulis–Sullivan geodesic current map on
the moduli space of metrics, as well as a Bowen-type description of these currents as limits of
sums of appropriately normalized atomic geodesic currents. For surface groups, we apply this
continuity result to show that the Bowen–Margulis–Sullivan map restricts to a topological
embedding on Hitchin components (up to contragradient involution) when equipped with
their Hilbert lengths.

1. Introduction
Let M be a closed, negatively curved Riemannian manifold with fundamental group Γ. Write
g = (gt)t∈R for the geodesic flow on the unit tangent bundle T1M. In this work we construct a
correspondence between the following objects:
• Reparameterizations of the geodesic flow, i.e. continuous flows on T1M whose oriented
flow lines coincide with those of g.
• Left-invariant pseudometrics1 on Γ which are Gromov-hyperbolic and quasi-isometric
to a word metric.
We consider these objects up to natural equivalence relations (coming from scaling conditions
and flow conjugacy) and write Par(T1M, g) for the collection of (classes of) reparameteriza-
tions and H++
Γ
for the collection of (classes of) pseudometrics. Our first main result, stated as
Theorem A below, establishes a bridge between these spaces. Consequently, we uncover new
phenomena and examples, both in the setting of reparameterizations and that of pseudomet-
rics. Before stating this result, we discuss a motivating example from the theory of Anosov
representations.

The H¨older regularity of such reparameterizations allows for the implementation of tools
from thermodynamic formalism. Using such techniques it is possible to deduce results of vari-
ous flavours for Anosov representations. These include counting asymptotics [Sam14; Sam15],
existence of pressure metrics and analyticity of Hausdorff dimensions of limit sets [BCLS15;
BCLS18], entropy rigidity [PS17], and mixing results [Sam15; CS23; CS24; DMS25].
The space Par(T1M, g) is defined as the space of continuous reparameterizations of the flow
g, up to weak conjugacy and rescaling (see Theorem 5.3 for the precise notions). Since weak
conjugacy implies conjugacy among H¨older reparameterizations, the works [BCLS15; CDPW25]
imply that many subspaces of Anosov representations (such as Hitchin components) embed into
Par(T1M, g). When M is a closed hyperbolic surface, this allows us to interpret Par(T1M, g)
as the “highest Teichm¨uller space”, a perspective discussed by Tholozan [Tho19] and based on
ideas by Sullivan [Sul93].
Other than Tholozan’s work in dimension 2, not much is known about the geometry of
Par(T1M, g) for general M, especially beyond the H¨older setting. For instance, it is unclear
what functions can be the period function for a continuous reparameterization, whereas Livˇsic’s
theorem implies that the period of a H¨older reparameterization Ψ is non-arithmetic. That is,
no a ∈R satisfies LΨ[g] ∈aZ for all g ∈Γ (see also Theorem 1.1).

1.2. Bowen–Margulis–Sullivan geodesic currents. The relation between reparameteriza-
tions and pseudometrics also holds for their associated spaces of measures. Suppose Γ is a
non-elementary torsion-free hyperbolic group Γ, with associated Mineyev’s flow space (UΓ, g).

Green metrics on hyperbolic groups. These metrics were introduced by Blach`ere and Brofferio

given by µ is transient, the Green metric dµ on Γ is given by
dµ(g, h) := −log P(∃k s.t. gZk = h).
Note that dµ is not necessarily symmetric. For Γ a non-elementary hyperbolic group, we have
that dµ belongs to H++
Γ
as long as µ has superexponential moment and dµ ∈DΓ if in addition µ is
symmetric [BHM11; Gou15; GGPY21]. These results are based on the work of Ancona [Anc88].

spheres, we construct sequences of Green metrics whose induced metric structures converge to
