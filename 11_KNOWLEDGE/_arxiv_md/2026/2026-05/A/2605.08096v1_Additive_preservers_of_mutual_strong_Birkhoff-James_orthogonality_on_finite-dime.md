BOJAN KUZMA AND SRDJAN STEFANOVIĆ
Abstract. We describe additive surjections on direct sum of matrix algebras
that preserve singularity in one direction. As an application, we classify addi-
tive surjections on finite-dimensional C∗-algebras that preserve mutual strong
Birkhoff–James orthogonality in one direction.
1. Introduction, preliminaries and statement of the main results
In a normed space X we lack an inner product; therefore, a notion of orthogo-
nality is required that coincides with the standard definition
x ⊥y ⇔⟨x, y⟩= 0
whenever X is an inner product space (throughout this paper, the scalar field is
assumed to be the field of complex numbers C, unless explicitly mentioned other-
wise). One of the most studied notions is the Birkhoff–James orthogonality, which
has been investigated for more than 90 years (see the pioneering works of Birkhoff [2]
and James [7], [6], [5]).
We say that two vectors x, y ∈X are Birkhoff–James (BJ for short) orthogonal,
and write x ⊥BJ y, if
∥x + λy∥≥∥x∥,
for all λ ∈C.
In [17], Arambašić and Rajić naturally extended the above definition to (right)
Hilbert C∗-modules over a C∗-algebra A. Namely, for two elements x, y ∈X we
say that x is strongly BJ orthogonal to y, and write x ⊥S
BJ y, if
∥x + yc∥≥∥x∥
for all c ∈A.
Then, in [15], Arambašić et al. defined the mutual version of the relation previously
considered. Namely, for two elements x, y ∈X, we say that x and y are mutual
strong BJ orthogonal, denoted by x ⊥⊥S
BJ y, if x ⊥S
BJ y and y ⊥S
BJ x.
In this paper, we will consider a C∗-algebra A, regarded as a right Hilbert C∗-
module over itself. So a ∈A is strong BJ orthogonal to b ∈A if for any c ∈A there
holds
(1.1)
∥a + bc∥≥∥a∥.

In this paper, we study additive preservers. They have been studied for many
years (see, for example, the papers [21] and [9]). Recently, there was a renewed
interest in additive preservers of the orthogonality relation — see the paper of
Wójcik [24, Section 4], where he proved that the additive preserver of BJ orthog-
onality on real normed space is automatically linear and hence, from the famous
result by Oman [20, Lemma 3.3, page 38], it is a scalar multiple of an isome-
try. We remark in passing that Blanco and Turnšek [3] extended this result by
classifying bijections preserving BJ orthogonality in both directions on projective
spaces induced by reflexive smooth spaces. Also, in two papers by Li, Liu, and Per-
alta (see [11] and [12]), Wójcik’s result was extended to complex normed spaces.
In [11, Proposition 2.2] it was shown that an additive map Φ, which preserves the
BJ orthogonality between two complex normed spaces X, Y , with dim X ≥2 and
admitting a conjugation (i.e., there exists a conjugate-linear and involutive isome-
try on X) is automatically real-linear; if, in addition, Φ is surjective and preserves
BJ orthogonality in both directions, then it is automatically continuous. This re-
sult was extended in [12, Lemma 2.14] where it was shown that Φ admits a unique
real-linear extension to a BJ preserver on completions of X and Y provided that,
besides the previous assumptions on X, we also have that X∗is strictly convex.
