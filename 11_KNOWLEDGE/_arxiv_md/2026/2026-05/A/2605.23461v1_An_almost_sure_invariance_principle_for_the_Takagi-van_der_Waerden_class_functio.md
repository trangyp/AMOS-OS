AN ALMOST SURE INVARIANCE PRINCIPLE FOR THE
TAKAGI–VAN DER WAERDEN CLASS FUNCTIONS
YUZABURO NAKANO
Abstract. The Takagi–van der Waerden functions are a well-known
class of continuous but nowhere differentiable functions. In this paper,
we study their weighted versions, the Takagi–van der Waerden class
functions fr,a(x), from a probabilistic point of view.
We prove that
the local modulus of continuity of fr,a(x) is described by a standard
Brownian motion under some regularity assumptions on the weights,
as an application of a strong approximation for elephant random walks
remembering the very recent past with variable step length.
