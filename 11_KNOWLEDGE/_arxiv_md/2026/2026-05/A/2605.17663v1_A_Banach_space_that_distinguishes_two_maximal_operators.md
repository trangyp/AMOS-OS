Abstract. Maz’ya and Shaposhnikova introduced a non-classical maximal operator M⋄as

The problem is closely related to, but apparently distinct from, several well-developed
lines of work. The first related body of work concerns the Fefferman–Stein sharp function
and its good λ inequalities. The classical theory gives estimates of Mf in terms of f♯and
is central in weighted inequalities and singular integrals [19, 20, 15, 22]. A closely related
comparison problem for variants of the sharp maximal function was studied by Martell, who
introduced sharp maximal functions associated with approximations of the identity on spaces

smaller than the classical sharp maximal function while not being comparable to it in general

terms of boundedness properties of M [30], and related Fefferman–Stein equivalences and
maximal operator criteria have continued to be studied [31, 42, 32].
