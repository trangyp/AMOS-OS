A FAMILY OF 2 × 2 TETRABLOCK CONTRACTIONS WITH
NON-COMMUTING FUNDAMENTAL OPERATORS
MAINAK BHOWMIK
Abstract. In this note, we provide a family of 2 × 2 tetrablock contractions that have tetra-
block isometric dilation, but the corresponding fundamental operators do not commute. This
answers a question raised by Bhattacharyya [Indiana Univ. Math. J. 2014] about the necessity
of commuting fundamental operators for a tetrablock contraction to have rational dilation in
a negative direction.
1. Introduction & Background

In a landmark work [1], Agler established a nice connection between complex geometry and
operator theory. More precisely, he used the Carath´eodory distance between two points z and
w in a bounded domain Ωin Cd to compute the largest angle between the eigenfunctions of a
commuting d-tuple T of 2 × 2 matrices having joint spectrum in {z, w} and satisfying (1.1)

Theorem 1.2 (Agler [1]). Let K be a compact pseudoconvex subset of Cd. A unital contractive

necessary. In the next section, we provide a family of 2 × 2 tetrablock contractions that have
tetrablock unitary dilations by Corollary 1.3, but the corresponding fundamental operators do
not commute. En route, we show how the Schwarz-type lemma for the polydisk can be used in
proving a commuting triple of 2 × 2 upper triangular matrices to be a tetrablock contraction
- a difficult task, in general.

3. Concluding Remarks
Although the tetrablock contractions described in Theorem 2.3 have tetrablock unitary
dilation, we do not have the explicit tetrablock isometries/unitaries that dilate them. The
non-commutativity of the fundamental operators implies that the dilation tuple is not of the
form (1.4) in Theorem 1.4.
In [5], a more general construction of the dilation tuple has been provided, which encom-
passes a larger collection of tetrablock contractions by relaxing the condition [F1, F ∗

of Theorem 1.4. However, the commutativity of F1 and F2 remains necessary to have such
a dilation tuple.
In this case, the dilation space is not the minimal dilation space of T3.

Till now, these are the known explicit constructions for the tetrablock isometric dilation of
tetrablock contractions satisfying several conditions, including the commutativity of the funda-
mental operators. Therefore, an explicit construction of the tetrablock isometric dilations for
the 2 × 2 tetrablock contractions produced in the previous section remains unknown because
of non-commuting fundamental operators. Finding dilation tuples for these tetrablock con-
tractions is worth exploring, and it might bring new ideas to tackle the long-standing rational
dilation problem for the tetrablock.
