The Newtonian quadrupolar tidal tensor accurately describes the dominant Earth–Moon tide.
In its principal frame, this tensor is diagonal and produces the familiar 90◦stretching–squeezing
pattern. A 45◦projection of this field is possible, but it is not an independent cross-tidal residual.
We formulate a Halilsoy-inspired residual extension of the local lunar tidal tensor. The key idea
is that Halilsoy’s cross-polarized cylindrical gravitational waves contain an off-diagonal tidal sector
capable of rotating a local eigenframe. By importing this mechanism as a residual ansatz, we define
an alpha-dependent coefficient χH(α, t, ρ) that measures a possible off-diagonal correction to the
diagonal Newtonian principal-frame tensor.
This residual does not break the 90◦orthogonality of the principal axes. Instead, it rotates the
whole eigenframe and produces a 45◦-type signature through a sin(2β) acceleration channel. Its
scale is ∆aH

The construction is not a replacement for standard tidal theory and does not model the Earth–
Moon system as a Halilsoy spacetime. It provides a mathematically explicit and observationally
testable residual framework: the Newtonian tensor explains the dominant lunar tide, while the
Halilsoy-inspired sector identifies a possible alpha-dependent off-diagonal cross channel.

Interpretation and limitations
It is important to state the boundary of the model.
The Earth–Moon system is not being modeled as a
Halilsoy spacetime.
This distinction is essential be-
cause ordinary terrestrial tides are accurately described,
at leading order, by Newtonian and post-Newtonian
tidal theory, while Halilsoy waves belong to the class of
exact cylindrically symmetric gravitational-wave space-
times [5, 6, 13, 16]. The present construction uses Halil-
soy geometry only to motivate the form of an off-diagonal
tidal residual, not to replace the physical source model
of the lunar tide.
The ordinary lunar tide is not claimed to be caused by
a Halilsoy standing wave. The Halilsoy solution is used
as an exact relativistic example showing that off-diagonal
tidal sectors can arise naturally and rotate tidal eigen-
frames. The effective tensor EM,H is therefore a residual
ansatz, not a full spacetime solution for the Earth–Moon
system.
