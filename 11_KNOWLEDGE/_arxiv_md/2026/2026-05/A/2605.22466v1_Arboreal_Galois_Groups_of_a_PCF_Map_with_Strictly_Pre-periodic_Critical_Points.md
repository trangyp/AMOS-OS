ABSTRACT. We study the arithmetic and geometric iterated monodromy groups associated to the postcritically finite
(PCF) quadratic rational function f(x) =
2
(x−1)2 defined over a number field k, whose critical points are both strictly
pre-periodic. We give explicit recursive descriptions of the topological generators of the geometric iterated monodromy
group of f and show that the arithmetic iterated monodromy group has Hausdorff dimension zero. We describe an
explicit criterion to determine the values a ∈k for which the associated arboreal Galois group achieves its maximum
possible size. In particular, we show that maximality of the arboreal Galois group can already be verified at level four,
which is computationally accessible. Finally, we determine the intersection of the constant field of the arithmetic iterated
monodromy group with k(µ2∞), providing the first full study of a PCF quadratic map with non-abelian constant field.

Let f : P1

monodromy group Garith(f) - which can be thought of as the arboreal Galois group for a transcendental base point.
A central question in arithmetic dynamics is: for which a ∈k does Ga(f) achieve this maximum, i.e. equal to
Garith(f)?
These groups were first studied by Odoni in the 1980s [17–19] for arithmetical and dynamical purposes, and
have since been studied extensively [2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 13, 16, 22]. A particularly interesting setting is when f is post-
critically finite (PCF), i.e., when the critical points of f have finite orbits. In this case, the tower of extensions
(given by the splitting fields of f n(x) −a for n ≥1) has finitely many ramified primes. It is known that for
PCF maps, the arboreal Galois groups have infinite index in Aut(T), see for example [15, Theorem 3.1]. Most
explicit descriptions of the associated Galois groups have been obtained for PCF polynomials [1,21] . The rational
function case remains largely open, with only recent work beginning to address it [3,10,11,20].
