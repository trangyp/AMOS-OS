It should be obvious from eq.(31) that α does not affect the 4D propa-
gator. Another observation is that one could choose different values of αs
for each column of the Domain Wall matrix, such as 1, α1, α2, α3, . . . , , αLs.
It turned out, in the test that were performed, to be numerically optimal to
choose all αs equal.
One could look at this in a perhaps much simpler way, by dividing the
Domain Wall Matrix with α, which would replace c+ and c−with c+
α and
c−
α . This again emphasises, that α almost directly scales the quark masses
and hence that this method might be especially useful for when they are
small.
It is important to note that, if even-odd preconditioning were to be
applied, it would have to be done from the left in order not to cancel out
the effect of α.
