A NOTE ON AZUMAYA ALGEBRAS AND ONE-FORMS
SIQING ZHANG
Abstract. The crystalline differential operators on a smooth variety X give rise to a
non-split Azumaya algebra over the cotangent bundle of the Frobenius twist X′. In some
cases, this Azumaya algebra splits when restricted to finite covers of X′. In this short
note, we show that, whenever X has a non-closed global one-form, there is a degree one
cover of X′ on which the Azumaya algebra does not split, answering a question of Sasha
Petrov.
1. The Question
Throughout k is an algebraically closed field of characteristic p > 0. Let X be a smooth
proper variety over k, and let X′ be its Frobenius twist. By Bezrukavnikov–Mirkovic–
Rumynin [1, Theorem 2.2.3], the sheaf of crystalline differential operators on X defines an
Azumaya algebra AX on the cotangent bundle T ∗X′.
In many useful cases, especially in the characteristic-p geometric Langlands and non-
abelian Hodge Theory for curves [2, 5, 3], and for abelian varieties [6], this Azumaya
algebra splits when restricted to spectral varieties; compare also the p-adic analogue [7].
Sasha Petrov asked the following question:
Question 1. [10, Question 7.3(1)] Does there exist a smooth proper variety X together
with a smooth subvariety Z ⊂T ∗X′, finite over X′, such that AX|Z does not split?
In this note, we answer this question affirmatively in every positive characteristic.

Theorem 4. Let X/k be a smooth proper connected variety. If not every global one-form
on X is closed, then there is a smooth subvariety Z ⊂T ∗X′, finite over X′, such that AX|Z
does not split. Moreover, the finite morphism Z →X′ is an isomorphism.
Proof. Combine Lemma 2 and Proposition 3, and take Z to be Γω for some ω such that
obX′(ω) ̸= 0.
□
2.1. The Examples.
2.1.1. Surface examples.
The existence of smooth projective surfaces with non-closed global one-forms goes back
at least to Mumford [9, Corollary on p.341]. The author would like to thank Sasha Petrov
for pointing out this example.
In [12, Corollary 3.4 and the paragraph following it], Takeda constructs some generalized
Raynaud surfaces that also have non-closed global one-forms. [12] is written under the
assumption that p > 2, but one can check that these examples still hold when p = 2, see
e.g. [13, §5] for more details.
2.1.2. Higher-dimensional examples.
For higher-dimensional examples, we can take X to be any of the surfaces above and
take X × Pn
k. Indeed, if η ∈H0(X, Ω1
X/k) is non-closed, then so is the pullback of η to
X × Pn
k.
Remark 5. If the Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence degenerates at E1, then we must
have that H0(X, Z1
X/k) = H0(X, Ω1
X/k). Therefore, by Deligne-Illusie [4, Theorem 2.1 and
Corollary 2.5], all examples above do not lift to the ring of second Witt vectors W2(k).
