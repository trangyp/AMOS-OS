HUISHI YU
Abstract. We construct a Poincar´e sheaf on the compactified Prym variety associated with
an ´etale double cover of integral curves with planar singularities, and prove that the associated
Fourier–Mukai transform is an autoequivalence of its derived category.
As an application, we prove the motivic decomposition conjecture of Corti–Hanamura for
the Laza–Sacc`a–Voisin fibration, and construct a multiplicative motivic perverse filtration
lifting the cohomological one.

0. Introduction
Let A be an abelian variety over an algebraically closed field k, and let ˆA be its dual. The
Poincar´e line bundle P on A × ˆA is the universal family of algebraically trivial line bundles
on A. In the seminal work [42], Mukai proved that the Fourier–Mukai functor with kernel P
is an equivalence between the bounded derived categories Db(A) and Db( ˆA).
In particular, this applies when A is either the Jacobian of a smooth curve or the Prym
variety of a finite cover of smooth curves, and it is natural to extend the theory to curves with
singularities. In [10], Arinkin extended the Fourier–Mukai transform to compactified Jacobians
of integral projective curves with planar singularities. Melo–Rapagnetta–Viviani [40, 41] later
generalized this result to fine compactified Jacobians of reduced projective curves with planar

0.2. Applications. In [38], Maulik–Shen–Yin studied the dualizable abelian fibrations that
satisfy the Fourier vanishing condition (FV). They proved that such fibrations have many
good properties, and their main application is that the relative compactified Jacobian JC is a
dualizable abelian fibration that satisfies the Fourier vanishing condition (FV), provided that
the curves are of planar singularities and the total space JC is nonsingular.
In this paper, we give other examples of dualizable abelian fibrations that satisfy (FV).
Theorem 0.5 (Theorem 6.3). Let C →B be a projective flat family of integral curves with
planar singularities over an irreducible base B. Let π : eC →C be an ´etale double cover such
that the geometric fibers of eC →B are integral. If the total space of the relative compactified
Prym variety P is nonsingular, then P →B is a self-dualizable abelian fibration satisfying
(FV).
Theorem 0.5 can be applied to the compactification of relative intermediate Jacobian fibra-
tion associated with a general cubic 4-fold constructed by Laza–Sacc`a–Voisin. For a general
cubic 4-fold X, we denote by U ⊂B = (P5)∨the locus of smooth hyperplane sections of
X, and JU →U the associated intermediate Jacobian fibration. Laza–Sacc`a–Voisin [35] con-
structed a smooth projective compactification J of JU, which is a hyper-K¨ahler variety of
OG10 type and admits a Lagrangian fibration J →B that extends JU →U. We call J →B
an LSV fibration. Since J arises as the descent of a nonsingular relative compactified Prym
variety, Theorem 0.5, together with a descent argument, yields the following.
Theorem 0.6 (Theorem 6.5). The LSV fibration J →B is a self-dualizable abelian fibration
satisfying (FV).
