Abstract
Given a polarised maximal degeneration of compact Calabi-Yau man-
ifolds, assuming there exists a canonical basis of the section ring for the
polarisation line bundle, satisfying the valuative independence condition,
we will prove the metric SYZ conjecture.

The metric SYZ conjecture is currently known for Abelian varieties, cer-
tain K3 surfaces, and a large class of hypersurface examples inside toric Fano
manifolds [19, 22, 24, 20, 1, 29, 14]. A more general approach is based on non-
archimedean (NA) pluripotential theory [5, 6, 23].
Some key features are as
follows (see also Section 2 for some quick recap):
1. Given the polarised meromorphic degeneration family of compact CY
manifolds π ∶X →D∗, we can take the base change to a degeneration
family over the formal disc XK →Spec(K) where K = C((t)), and then
canonically associate a NA object called the Berkovich space Xan
K . There
is a hybrid topology on X ⊔Xan
K , so that Xan
K can be viewed as a tropical
limit of Xt as t →0.
2. The essential skeleton Sk(X) is canonically embedded as a subset of Xan
K .
There is a canonical Lebesgue measure µ0 supported on Sk(X) ⊂Xan
K ,
which is the natural weak limit of normalised CY measures µt on Xt in
the hybrid topology. The central result of NA pluripotential theory due to
Boucksom-Favre-Jonsson [5] is the solution of the NA Calabi conjecture.
In this setting, it provides a unique (up to constant) continuous psh poten-
tial ϕ0 on (Xan
K ,c1(L)), which solves the non-archimedean Monge-Amp`ere
(NA MA) equation
MA(ϕ0) = (Ln)µ0,
and is called the NA CY potential. It is recently proved [27] that whenever
1 ≤dimSk(X) ≤n, up to suitable normalisation, the K¨ahler potential of
(Xt,ωt,Ωt) converges in the C0-hybrid topology to the NA CY potential
ϕ0.
3. A choice of SNC model π ∶X →D for the degeneration family π ∶X →D∗,
induces a retraction map rX from Xan
K
to the dual complex ∆X . The
essential skeleton Sk(X) can be viewed as a subset of ∆X .
Definition 1.2. An SNC model satisfies the weak comparison property for
the NA CY potential ϕ0, if there is an open subset U ⊂Sk(X) with full
Lebesgue measure µ0(U) = 1, such that ϕ0 factors through the retraction
map rX ∶Xan
K →∆X over the open subset U ⊂Sk(X) ⊂∆X .
Remark 1.3. The name ‘comparison property’ comes from a result of C.
Vilsmeier [32], which allows one to compare the NA MA equation with the
real Monge-Amp`ere equation when this property is satisfied. The weak
comparison property is preserved under smooth blow ups and blow downs,
hence if it holds for one SNC model, then it will hold for all SNC models
(cf. Remark 2.10). As a small caveat, Def. 1.2 is similar but not identical
to the comparison property in [23], and we will give some exposition in
Section 2.7.
The arguments in [23] essentially proved the following:
Theorem 1.4. Let π ∶X →D∗be a polarised maximal degeneration of
compact CY manifolds. Suppose the weak comparison property is verified
for some semistable SNC model, then the metric SYZ conjecture holds for
the degeneration family π ∶X →D∗.

The search for canonical basis is an active topic in algebraic geometry.
1. In the Gross-Siebert programme [15, 16, 17, 18], given a polarised maximal
degeneration of compact CY manifolds, one can build a graded ring from
punctured Gromov-Witten invariants, and define the mirror CY family
via the relative Proj construction of this graded ring. Consequently, the
mirror family carries a polarisation line bundle, whose section ring has a
canonical basis called theta functions. This is a vast generalisation for the
classical theta functions on abelian varieties.
2. It is unknown in general whether the Gross-Siebert theta functions sat-
isfies the valuative independence condition for polarised maximal degen-
eration of compact CY manifolds. However, in an analogous context for
cluster varieties, the Gross-Siebert construction produces a distinguished
basis of theta functions for the coordinate ring on certain open Calabi-Yau
manifolds, and the very recent work of Cheung-Magee-Mandel-Muller [11]
verified the valuative independence condition.
3. H. Blum and Y. Liu informed the author that they can prove the following
in full generality (not necessarily assuming maximal degeneration).
Theorem 1.6. [2] Let (XK,L) →Spec(K) be any polarised degeneration
of smooth projective CY varieties over the formal punctured disc, where L
is relatively ample. Then there exists some integer m0 ≥1, such that for
any l ≥0, there exists a K-basis {θl
α} ⊂H0(XK,m0lL) satisfying valuative
independence.
Remark 1.7. This relies on deep techniques from birational geometry
concerning finite generation of section rings, and construction of multi-
parameter test configurations. Blum-Liu’s general result also applies to
projective log canonical Calabi-Yau pairs over Spec(K).

Remark 4.4. If one wishes to directly study the real Monge-Amp`ere equation
on Sk(X), one would face the following basic difficulty. The structure of Sk(X)
as a simplicial complex depends on the choice of the model X, and Sk(X) has

the real Monge-Amp`ere equation is not invariantly defined. The affine structure
(here the subset U) is determined by the solution φ, rather than given a priori.

is an interesting question for the NA CY potential ϕ0, whether there is some
Hausdorff codimension two subset of Sk(X), such that its complement admits
some affine structure, wherein ϕ0 solves the real MA equation.

can canonically identify By using 3.4. This identification is compatible with

Calabi-Yau manifolds. This would be a very appealing metric version of mirror
symmetry.
