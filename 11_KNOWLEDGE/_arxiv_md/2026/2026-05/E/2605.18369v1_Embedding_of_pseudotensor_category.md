1. Introduction
The notion of vertex algebra is a rigorous mathematical definition of the chiral
part of a 2-dimensional quantum field theory, which was formulated by R.Borcherds
in [8]. Conformal algebras provide an axiomatic description of the ”singular” part
of the vertex algebras and regard the vertex algebra as a high-weight module [12].
Using the language of pseudotensor categories, a natural generalization of confor-
mal algebras was introduced in [1], which is called pseudoalgebras. In [1], a full
classification of semisimple finite Lie pseudoalgebras was obtained. Classification of

completed in [2, 3, 4]. Unital associative, Leibniz, graded left-symmetric, Jordan,
Zinbiel pseudoalgebras were studied in [19, 20, 21, 13, 23].
An algebra of a certain type is usually defined by generating operations and
relations.
The notion of operad is an algebraic device which encodes a type of
algebras. Instead of studying the properties of a particular algebra, we focus on
the universal operations that can be performed on the elements of any algebra of
a given type. The information contained in an operad consists in these operations
and all the ways of composing them. The operadic point of view has several advan-
tages, such as applying many results known for classical types of algebras to other
types of algebras and simplifying both the statements and the proofs. The operad
theory also plays an important role in the development of deformation theory and
quantum field theory. The original definition is due to Peter May, and was given
in [17], where an operad is regarded as a collection of operations equipped with a
notion of composition and subject to various conditions. In the encyclopedic book
[16] written by J.L.Loday and B.Vallette, it provides a comprehensive account of
algebraic operad theory, containing introduction to algebraic operads, a conceptual
treatment of Koszul duality and applications to homotopical algebra.
The use of operadic viewpoint to study pseudoalgebra quickly emerged after the
concept of pseudoalgebra was proposed. We can construct the following one-to-one
correspondence
P H-pseudoalgebra structure on M ↔HomOp(P, EndM)
where H is a Hopf algebra, M is a H-module, P is a algebraic operad and EndM
has some modifications defined by EndM(n) = HomH⊗n(M ⊠n, H⊗n ⊗H M) for
n ≥0. However, the further research on the operadic morphism from P to EndM
seems to be difficult.
We have no specific methods to explain clearly how this
operadic morphism is mapped. The main obstruction is that we are not yet able
to construct the free P H-pseudoalgebra structure on a fixed H-module. Hence,
the various kinds of tools in the operad theory can not be applied directly to the
study of pseudoalgebra.
When considering chiral algebra(vertex algebra) and Lie∗algebra(Lie conformal
algebra), there are many works arising by using operadic methods. The pioneering
work is dual to A. Beilinson and V. Drinfeld in their book [7], where they give
chiral algebra and chiral homology in a taste of algebraic geometry via pseudotensor
category and D-module language. The chiral Koszul duality between chiral algebra
and factorization algebras are studied in [11]. In [5, 6], the authors translate the
construction of the chiral opeard to the purely algebraic language of vertex algebras
and give rise to vertex algebra cohomology. The article [18] study the algebraic
operad of SUSY vertex algebra. There is also research on quantum field theory by
colored operad; see [10] and the references therein.
In the book [7], it is proved that any pseudotensor category M can be realized as
a full pseudotensor subcategory of a tensor(=symmetric monoidal) category M⊗.
Then they give a specific construction of M⊗, denoted by M(XS), when pseu-
dotensor category M = M(X) is the category of right D-modules on a smooth
curve X.
In this paper, we realize the above embedding functor and the tensor cate-
gory in a purely algebraic setting when the pseudotensor category is the category
M(H) of left H-modules. We construct two tensor categories, which are called

Let P be a symmetric operad over k, M be a pseudotensor k-category, and

Since there are no (pseudo)tensor products on pseudotensor category when the
pseudotensor structure is not representable, we can not define a P-pseudoalgebra
