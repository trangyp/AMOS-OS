EKEDAHL-OORT STRATA UNDER NATURAL EMBEDDINGS OF ORTHOGONAL AND
UNITARY SHIMURA VARIETIES
QIJUN YAN AND CHAO ZHANG
Abstract. In this paper, we study the behavior of Ekedahl-Oort strata under natural embeddings between
the good reductions modulo 𝑝of GSpin Shimura varieties and Rapoport-Smithling-Zhang unitary Shimura
varieties, a prototypical setting for the construction of special cycles in the Kudla program. In each case,
we determine the EO stratum containing the image of a given EO stratum under the embedding. We also
compute discrete invariants of these Shimura varieties, including their 𝑝-ranks and 𝑎-numbers; in the
GSpin case, these are obtained via the Kuga-Satake embedding.
Introduction
0.1. Background and main goal of the paper. Let A𝑔,𝑁,F𝑝be the moduli space over F𝑝of principally
polarized abelian varieties of dimension 𝑔with symplectic level 𝑁-structure, where 𝑝is a fixed odd
prime and 𝑁≥3 is an integer prime to 𝑝. As a characteristic-𝑝phenomenon, A𝑔,𝑁,F𝑝admits several
natural stratifications, including the Newton stratification, the Ekedahl–Oort (EO) stratification, the
𝑎-number stratification, and the 𝑝-rank stratification.
These stratifications are defined by different
invariants of abelian varieties in characteristic 𝑝, and some are finer than others. For instance, the EO
stratification refines both the 𝑝-rank stratification and the 𝑎-number stratification, while the Newton
stratification is finer than the 𝑝-rank stratification. Several of these stratifications extend to the good
reductions modulo 𝑝of more general Shimura varieties.
The EO stratification is the main focus of this paper, although some of our results also concern related
strata. In the Siegel case, the EO stratum of a point is determined by the isomorphism class of the
associated principally polarized truncated BT-1 [21]. More precisely, two geometric points of A𝑔,𝑁,F𝑝
lie in the same EO stratum if and only if their 𝑝-torsion group schemes are isomorphic as principally
polarized BT-1’s. This stratification has been generalized to broader classes of Shimura varieties; see
[33] for the PEL case, [38] for the Hodge type case, and [29] for the abelian type case.
Now let Sh𝐻,F𝑝−→Sh𝐺,F𝑝be a morphism of geometric mod 𝑝Shimura varieties arising from a
morphism of Shimura data with good reduction at 𝑝(see §1.1). It is known that the image of an EO
stratum of Sh𝐻,F𝑝is contained in a single EO stratum of Sh𝐺,F𝑝; we call the latter the image EO stratum.
Once explicit parametrizations of EO strata on both sides are available, it is natural to ask which EO
stratum of Sh𝐺,F𝑝contains the image of a given EO stratum of Sh𝐻,F𝑝.
In this paper, we study this question for certain natural embeddings between GSpin Shimura va-
rieties and Rapoport–Smithling–Zhang (RSZ) unitary Shimura varieties. These embeddings arise in
a prototypical setting for the construction of special cycles in the Kudla program; see, for example,
[10, 12, 11]. In the cases considered here, the EO stratifications admit relatively simple parametrizations.
For instance, for the embedding
Shg
GU(𝑛,1),F𝑝−→Shg
GU(𝑛+1,1),F𝑝,
the EO strata on the source are parametrized by {0, 1, . . . , 𝑛}, while those on the target are parametrized
by {0, 1, . . . , 𝑛+ 1}. Our goal is to determine, for each EO stratum on the source, its image EO stratum;
see Theorem A and Theorem B below.

0.3. Structure of the paper. Sections 1 and 2 provide general preliminaries on Shimura varieties of
Hodge and abelian type, with particular emphasis on the orthogonal and GSpin cases, as well as on the
𝐺-zip stacks that serve as period domains for the zip period maps defining the EO strata.
The orthogonal case is developed in §§ 3–11. Sections 3–5 supply the zip-theoretic and combinatorial
preparation, especially the normalization of the relevant reductive pairs and the choice of frames for
the zip group data attached to the 𝐺-zip stacks whose underlying topological spaces parametrize the
EO strata of ShGSpin(𝑛,2),F𝑝. In § 6, we establish the order-preserving bijection between 𝜇𝑊and the
underlying topological space of the corresponding normalized zip stack, and apply the general criteria
of [22] in our special orthogonal setting to determine when two framed zip orbits coincide. We then
prove Theorem A in § 7 and compute the 𝑎-numbers of the EO strata of ShGSpin(𝑛,2),F𝑝; § 8 treats
the analogous question for the zero-dimensional Kuga–Satake embedding, while Sections 9 and 10
are devoted to the Newton stratification of ShGSpin(𝑛,2),F𝑝and the computation of the corresponding
𝑝-ranks. Finally, § 11 presents examples of Theorem A in small ranks, together with an illustrative
diagram.
The unitary case is developed in §§ 12–13.8. In § 12, we introduce the RSZ unitary Shimura varieties,
their moduli interpretations, and the natural embeddings between them, and in § 13 we formulate and
prove Theorem B. The 𝑝-ranks and 𝑎-numbers of EO strata for unitary Shimura varieties are computed
in §§ 13.6 and 13.7, and § 13.8 concludes this part with an illustrative diagram for Theorem B.
