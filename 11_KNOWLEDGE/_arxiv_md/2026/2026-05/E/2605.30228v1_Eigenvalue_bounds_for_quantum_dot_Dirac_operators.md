EIGENVALUE BOUNDS FOR QUANTUM DOT DIRAC OPERATORS
JOAQUIM DURAN
Abstract. We exploit the connection between quantum dot Dirac operators and ∂-Robin
Laplacians.
First, we ﬁnd a graphical relation between their smallest positive eigenvalues,
which allows us to deduce a recipe for translating bounds (from above and below) from one to
the other. As an application, we provide new upper and lower bounds for the eigenvalues of
the quantum dot Dirac operators, which depend only on geometric quantities of the underlying
domain. In particular, we obtain some Faber-Krahn type inequalities for convex thin domains.
