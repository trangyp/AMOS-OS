Abstract
Although irregular vectors for the Virasoro algebra are widely used in modern math-
ematical physics, a rigorous existence and uniqueness theorem in arbitrary rank has not
been available in the literature. In this paper, we develop an algebraic framework, based
on Virasoro differential operators on the parameter space, which gives such a theorem for
arbitrary integer and half-integer ranks. A key ingredient is the construction of a canoni-
cal operator L∗from the coefficient matrix of the vector-field part of a truncated Virasoro
realization. This operator closes the recursive system by isolating the derivative with re-
spect to the highest irregular parameter. Using this mechanism, we prove the existence
and uniqueness of formal irregular vectors of arbitrary integer rank. We then construct
the truncated Virasoro vector fields required in the half-integer rank setting and prove the
existence and uniqueness of the corresponding half-integer rank formal irregular vectors.
These results provide an algebraic foundation for the rigorous construction of irregular
conformal blocks built from higher-rank irregular vectors.

Introduction
Irregular conformal blocks and irregular vectors of the Virasoro algebra have played an impor-
tant role in the interaction between two-dimensional conformal field theory, gauge theory, and
the theory of isomonodromic deformations. They first appeared in the study of the AGT corre-
spondence [1] and its degenerations [7], where special vectors in Virasoro Verma modules were
used to describe conformal blocks associated with asymptotically free gauge theories. Since
then, irregular conformal blocks have appeared in a wide range of problems, including strong-
coupling expansions of partition functions in Argyres-Douglas theories [8], Painlevé and quan-
tum Painlevé equations [5, 9, 15, 17, 18], confluent Heun equations [4, 14, 16], black-hole per-
turbation theory [2, 3], and exact WKB analysis [12, 13].
There are, however, two different kinds of objects that are often both called irregular vec-
tors. The first type, introduced by Gaiotto [7], has regular singular behavior with respect to the
relevant deformation parameters. These vectors, often called Gaiotto vectors, are characterized
by Whittaker-type conditions for the Virasoro algebra and have been studied extensively in con-
nection with the degenerations of the AGT correspondence [6, 10]. The second type, which is
the subject of the present paper, has genuinely irregular singular behavior. In the higher-rank
case, the corresponding formal vectors contain essential singularities in the highest irregular
parameter; for example, exponential factors of the form

This distinction is important: the latter objects are not obtained by a straightforward Whittaker
construction and require a more delicate recursive analysis.
Irregular vectors with genuinely irregular singular behavior were studied by Gaiotto and
Teschner [8] in connection with Argyres-Douglas theories. They wrote down the expected Vira-
soro constraints for general rank and gave explicit descriptions in the rank two and rank three
cases, revealing recursive structures that are expected to persist in general. A general form of
the defining relations for integer-rank irregular vectors was later written down by Nishinaka
and Uetoko [19]. These works provided the physical and algebraic motivation for the general
higher-rank theory, but a mathematical proof of the existence and uniqueness of the corre-
sponding formal vectors was not available.
Another approach to irregular conformal blocks is provided by irregular vertex operators.
In [17, 18], such operators were introduced through suitable intertwining relations involving
irregular Verma modules and irregular vectors, and they are used to construct irregular confor-
mal blocks relevant to Painlevé equations [17], quantum Painlevé equations [5], and confluent
Heun equations [14, 16]. The recursive construction of these operators already contains part of
the mechanism used in the present paper.
Half-integer ranks have appeared in the theory of irregular conformal blocks since the early
works on Gaiotto vectors [7] and irregular vertex operators [18]. In the setting of genuinely ir-
regular vectors, the rank 5/2 case was proposed in [20]. A significant conjectural framework
for arbitrary half-integer ranks was later proposed by Hamachika, Nakanishi, Nishinaka, and
Tanigawa [11], motivated by confluent limits from integer-rank equations and supported by
extensive finite-rank computations. A related approach was developed in [12], where an ex-
plicit differential realization is given using half-integer-indexed parameters.
The present paper adopts the parameter-space viewpoint of [20]: the half-integer-rank
vector is regarded as a vector in an integer-rank irregular Verma module, with parameters
c0,c1,...,cr−1,Λ2r−1. In this setting, however, a general construction of the Virasoro vector
fields, as well as an existence and uniqueness theorem for the corresponding formal vectors,
was still missing.

The purpose of this paper is to provide such a construction. We prove the existence and
uniqueness of irregular vectors of arbitrary integer and half-integer ranks. The key idea is to
isolate the derivative with respect to the highest irregular parameter: cr in the integer-rank
case and Λ2r−1 in the half-integer-rank case.
In the half-integer-rank case, we take a route different from the explicit formula approach.
Rather than seeking closed formulae for the full differential realization, we construct only the
vector-field part needed for the recursive system. More precisely, we prove the existence and
uniqueness of the relevant truncated Virasoro vector fields from their commutation relations
with the scalar higher modes. This gives a general intrinsic construction valid for arbitrary half-
integer rank.
To this end, we construct a canonical operator L∗from the coefficient matrix of the rele-
vant vector fields. The operator L∗eliminates the intermediate deformation derivatives and
produces a closed recursive system for the coefficients of the irregular vector. This places the
integer and half-integer-rank constructions in a common framework.
A related observation appears in the recent work [12]. In the rank 5/2 situation related to
the Painlevé I tau function, they identify a Virasoro generator corresponding to differentiation
with respect to the natural deformation parameter and use it to compute the expansion un-
der the assumption that the corresponding irregular vector exists. This is close in spirit to the
role of L∗in the present paper. However, in that setting, there is essentially a single natural
deformation parameter, whereas in higher rank, one has to eliminate several intermediate de-
formation derivatives and isolate the derivative with respect to the highest irregular parameter.
In our construction, L∗is incorporated into the recursive construction itself. This gives a closed
recursive system and proves the existence of the resulting formal vector and the deformation
equation associated with L∗.
The existence and uniqueness theorem is formulated for normalized formal vectors in com-
pleted irregular Verma modules. The lower-mode deformation equations belong to the full de-
formation theory of the associated conformal blocks, but they are not required for the recursive
construction of the vector itself.
These results give a rigorous foundation for irregular conformal blocks built from higher-
rank irregular vectors. They also clarify the algebraic deformation structure underlying these
formal objects.
The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review the Virasoro algebra, irregular
Verma modules, and the construction of rank one irregular (Gaiotto) vectors. We also recall a
determinant formula from our previous work that will be used later. Section 3 is devoted to the
construction and uniqueness theorem for irregular vectors of arbitrary integer rank. In partic-
ular, we introduce the canonical operator L∗and derive the corresponding recursive system. In
Section 4, we construct the truncated Virasoro vector fields associated with half-integer-rank
singularities and prove their commutation relations. Finally, we establish the existence and
uniqueness theorem for irregular vectors of arbitrary half-integer rank.

Discussions
The recursive systems constructed in this paper are expected to be part of a stronger differential
structure involving the full lower Virasoro differential system. More precisely, after a suitable
scalar gauge transformation, the irregular vectors are expected to satisfy
Dm|I (r)〉= 0,
0 ≤m ≤r −1.
Equivalently, one expects that an appropriate scalar multiple of the irregular vector satisfies
the full lower differential constraints. Differential systems of this kind, in which the lower Vira-
soro constraints are combined with a descendant expansion to determine the irregular vector,
have already appeared in the physics literature for low-rank examples: in particular, for integer
ranks 2,3 and for half-integer ranks 3/2,5/2 [8, 11, 12, 20]. In these examples, the D0-constraint
can be verified by analyzing the Laurent weight structure of the recursive coefficients; the rank
two case is discussed in Appendix B. The remaining constraints Dm|I (r)〉= 0 for m > 0 seem
to require a finer compatibility between the recursive coefficients and the lower deformation
vector fields. This suggests that the full lower system should be understood as a flatness or
gauge-trivialization problem for the lower deformation directions.
It would be interesting to compare explicitly the truncated Virasoro vector fields constructed
here with the differential realizations appearing in the physics literature. In particular, Hamachika–
Nakanishi–Nishinaka–Tanigawa proposed conjectural formulae for the half-integer-rank case,
while Iorgov–Iwaki–Lisovyy–Zhuravlov provided an explicit differential realization with a proof,
using half-integer-indexed parameters. Since our construction is characterized by the commu-
tation relations with the scalar higher modes, one expects to recover the corresponding vector-
field parts of these differential realizations after translating between the half-integer-indexed
parameters and the parameter-space convention used here. The scalar zero-th order terms,
on the other hand, are gauge dependent and should be compared only after a choice of scalar
gauge.
