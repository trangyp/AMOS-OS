Abstract
In Ehrhart theory, the well-konwn sign pattern problem asks: given a positive integer
d ≥3 and integers 1 ≤i1 < · · · < ik ≤d −2, does there exist a d-dimensional integral
polytope P such that in its Ehrhart polynomial i(P, t) the coefficients of ti1, . . . , tik are
negative, while the remaining coefficients are positive? This problem was proposed by
Hibi, Higashitani, Tsuchiya, and Yoshida. In this paper, we first construct a class of
simplices Sd(m) whose Ehrhart polynomial has leading coefficient m and all remaining
coefficients fixed positive constants. Then, using the Cartesian product of Sd(m) and the
Reeve tetrahedron, we obtain the first complete solution to the sign pattern problem.
Finally, in attacking the sign pattern problem, we provide a fast algorithm for computing
the h∗-polynomial of a class of simplices ∆(0, q).

The study of Ehrhart positivity has attracted considerable attention in recent years. Nu-
merous families of polytopes have been established as Ehrhart positive, including hypersim-
plices [18], minimal matroids [19], rank-two matroids [21], cross-polytopes [30, Section 2],

polytopes [31, 32], matroid polytopes [20], and smooth polytopes [13]. For a comprehensive
introduction to Ehrhart positivity, we refer the reader to Liu’s survey [30].
Inspired by the study on Reeve tetrahedra and Ehrhart positivity, Hibi, Higashitani,
Tsuchiya, and Yoshida [28] investigated possible sign patterns of coefficients in Ehrhart poly-
nomials and proposed the following question:
Open Problem 1.2. [28, Question 4.1]; [30, Question 4.2.1]; [22, Conjecture 4.3] Given a
positive integer d ≥3 and integers 1 ≤i1 < · · · < iq ≤d −2, does there exist a d-dimensional
integral polytope P such that the coefficients of ti1, . . . , tiq in i(P, t) are negative, while the
remaining coefficients are positive?

7
Concluding Remarks
One of the important contributions of this paper is the resolution of the well-known sign
pattern problem of coefficients in Ehrhart polynomials. This work is based on the authors’s
previous paper [33]; in particular, we will need Theorem 1.3 from that paper. By Theorem
1.3, it suffices to verify the existence of certain specific sign patterns.
In [33], we resolve the Ehrhart coefficient sign pattern problem for dimensions d ≤9. In the
early stage of this research, we used Maple to construct the integral polytopes corresponding
to all sign patterns of dimension 10 ≤d ≤20 (Under Theorem 1.3). The relevant program
is available at the following address: https://github.com/TygerLiu/TygerLiu.github.io/
tree/main/Procedure/Sign-pattern-problem.
In attacking the sign pattern problem, we construct a family of simplices Sd(m). These
simplices possess desirable properties, including reverse isoperimetric phenomenon discussed
in the text. In future work, we will further explore the applications of the family of simplices
Sd(m) in Ehrhart theory.
