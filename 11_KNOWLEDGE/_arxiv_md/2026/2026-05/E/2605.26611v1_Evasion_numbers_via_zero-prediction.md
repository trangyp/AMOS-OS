TAKASHI YAMAZOE
Abstract. Cruz Chapital, Goto, Hayashi and the author showed
that the game-theoretic variants sI
game∗and sI
game∗∗of the splitting
number s are consistently different, although the corresponding two
games differ only in a minor case. This result suggests that even if

relational system of evasion and prediction and for a variation of
it. We show that such a difference occurs for the standard one, but
not for the variation.

forces e ≥λ by cf(µ) = λ and e0 ≥κ by bookkeeping (and µ<κ = µ).
Since we are performing a finite support iteration, λ many Cohen reals
are cofinally added and they witness non(M) ≤λ. Since e ≤non(M)
holds in ZFC, we have ⊩P λ ≤e ≤non(M) ≤λ and hence ⊩P e = λ.
Lemma 3.14 implies ⊩P e0 ≤κ. Therefore, ⊩P e0 = κ and e = λ.
