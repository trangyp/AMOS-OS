ANDREA BIDOIA
Abstract. Caffarelli’s contraction theorem and the analogous Laplacian result in [8, 9]
are two examples of how log-Hessian bounds on probability densities yield estimates on the
derivative of the corresponding Brenier map with optimal dimensional dependence. The main
goal of this paper is to extend such phenomenon to a broader class of convex estimates such
as norms.
