Abstract
Let Ng be a closed, connected, nonorientable surface of genus g. We prove that for
g ≥13, the mapping class group Mod(Ng) can be generated by exactly two elements.
This improves the previously known bound of g ≥19.

Introduction
Let Ng be a closed, connected, nonorientable surface of genus g. The mapping class group
Mod(Ng) is the group of isotopy classes of self-diffeomorphisms of Ng. Finding minimal
generating sets for mapping class groups is a long-standing problem in geometric group theory
and low-dimensional topology.
Because Mod(Ng) is non-abelian, every generating set must contain at least two elements.
Szepietowski [Sze06] proved that Mod(Ng) is generated by three elements for all g ≥3. Later,
Altun¨oz, Pamuk, and Yıldız [APY21] answered a question posed in [Far06, Problem 3.1(a)]
by showing that Mod(Ng) can be generated by exactly two elements for g ≥19, one of which
is a periodic rotation of order g.
The genus bound g ≥19 in [APY21] is essentially a geometric limitation: to isolate the
necessary Dehn twists via the lantern relation, the surface must be large enough so that the
supports of the shifted generators do not accidentally overlap. In this note we lower the genus
bound by choosing a different second generator and employing a more efficient sequence of
commutators.
