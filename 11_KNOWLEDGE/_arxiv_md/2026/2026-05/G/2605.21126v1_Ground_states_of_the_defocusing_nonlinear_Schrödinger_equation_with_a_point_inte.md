Abstract
This paper is concerned with ground states of the defocusing non-
linear Schr¨odinger equation with a point interaction,
i∂tψ = −∆αψ + ψ|ψ|p−2
in
R × RN,
where −∆α denotes the Laplacian of point interaction centered at
the origin with inverse s-wave scattering length −2(N −1)πα and we
suppose that either (i) N = 2, α ∈R and p > 2 or (ii) N = 3,
α < 0 and 2 < p < 3. At sufficiently small masses, (i) we prove that
this equation admits ground states, (ii) we obtain some qualitative
properties of ground states and (iii) we obtain some results relating
ground states with critical points of the associated action functional.

Before recalling the precise definition of −∆α, we anticipate that −∆α
is an L2-self-adjoint operator which models the action of −∆together with
a potential concentrated at the origin in spatial dimensions N ∈{1, 2, 3}.
Even though the definition of −∆α was proposed in the 1960s (see [1]), the
study of nonlinear problems with a point interaction is much more recent.
To contextualize, let us recall a few papers concerned with the focusing
NLSE with a point interaction,
i∂tψ = −∆αψ −ψ|ψ|p−2
in
R × RN.
(1.4)

On one hand, this equation is relatively well understood when N = 1, in
which case the energy space associated with −∆α is the usual Sobolev space
H1(R). To cite a few papers, [2, 3, 4] contain results about the orbital stabil-
ity/instability of the unique positive solution; [5] investigated the asymptotic
stability; [6] studied the strong instability and [7] contains results about the
global dynamics below ground states (see also [8, 9, 10] for related results).
On the other hand, the study of (1.4) in higher dimensions N ∈{2, 3} started
receiving increasing attention only in the last four years. More precisely, [11]
proved the existence of action ground states and studied conditions for their
orbital stability/instability in the case N = 2; [12, 13] proved the existence
and obtained qualitative properties of ground states and clarified the relation
between the notions of ground states and action mininizers and [14] proved
the uniqueness and nondegeneracy of the positive action ground state. Fur-
thermore, more general semilinear problems with a point interaction in higher
dimension N ∈{2, 3} were already considered in [15, 16, 17, 18, 19].
Shifting the attention to the defocusing NLSE, let us highlight the differ-
ence introduced by the presence of a point interaction. On one hand, it is
well known that the defocusing NLSE
i∂tψ = −∆ψ + ψ|ψ|p−2
in
R × RN
does not admit nontrivial standing waves with finite energy (see [20, Exercise
6.4]). In fact, this result is a direct consequence of the fact that −∆is a
positive-definite operator. On the other hand, it is no longer clear whether a
similar result holds for (1.1) when either (1.2) or (1.3) is satisfied, in which
case the Laplacian of point interaction −∆α has a negative eigenvalue.
Problem (1.1) has received comparatively less attention than (1.4). A
few results were already obtained for this equation in the case N = 1: [21]
proved the existence and orbital stability of action ground states and [22]
studied the asymptotic behavior of small solutions. In higher dimension, [23]
proved the existence of standing waves in the case N = 3 and, very recently,
[24] showed that there exists a unique positive standing wave which is radial
and radially decreasing; furthermore, this preprint obtained the explicit form
of the set of nontrivial standing waves for a fixed frequency ω ∈[0, |ℓα|[ in
dimensions N ∈{2, 3}.
To finish the brief literature review, we cite a few papers with results
which are applicable to both the focusing and defocusing cases: [25] inves-
tigated the asymptotic stability in the case N = 1; [26] studied the well-
posedness of the associated Cauchy problem; [27] obtained conditions for
failure of scattering and [28] explained the relation between standing waves
of the NLSE in the punctured Euclidean space RN \ {0} with solutions to
the NLSE with a point interaction.
