FIXED POINT RESULTS FOR ASYMPTOTICALLY H¨OLDER
NONEXPANSIVE TYPE MAPPINGS
C. S. BARROSO AND C. S. R. DA SILVA
Abstract. In this work, we extend Goebel-Kirk fixed point theorems to the
setting of mappings of asymptotically H¨older-nonexpansive type. By providing
several non-trivial examples, we show that this new framework strictly contains
its classical counterparts. Furthermore, we prove that if a Banach space contains
an isomorphic copy of either c0 or ℓ1, then the fixed point property (FPP) for this
class of mappings fails. Finally, we show that every infinite-dimensional Banach
space contains a compact convex set K admitting a fixed-point free, affine self-
mapping T which is of asymptotically H¨older-nonexpansive type and possesses
no continuous iterates.
1. Introduction
Fixed point theorems for nonlinear operators constitute a fundamental pillar of
modern functional analysis, providing powerful tools for problems arising naturally
in various branches of mathematics and engineering. Central to this framework is
the fixed point property (FPP), which asserts that every mapping from a specific
class on a closed, bounded, and convex set K has a fixed point. This property has
undergone significant evolution, particularly regarding metric behaviors that reflect
the underlying geometry of the space. The modern theory traces back to 1965,
when Browder, G¨ohde, and Kirk independently proved that nonexpansive mappings
T : K →K on a nonempty, closed, bounded, and convex subset of a uniformly
convex Banach space have a fixed point. This seminal result expanded the field
beyond Banach’s contraction principle, prompting an extensive body of research
into the connection between geometric properties and the FPP (see [10, 18]).
In 1972, Goebel and Kirk [9] introduced asymptotically nonexpansive mappings
and established their FPP. Recall that T is said to be asymptotically nonexpansive
if the Lipschitz constants of its iterates converge to 1 as n →∞. That is,
∥T n(x) −T n(y)∥≤(1 + kn)∥x −y∥,
∀x, y ∈K, n ∈N,
where (κn) ⊂(0, 1) is such that kn →0. Specifically, they proved that if K is a
nonempty, closed, bounded, and convex subset of a uniformly convex space, then
any such mapping T has a fixed point. As highlighted in [19], the interest in this
class of mappings lies in their ability to test the limits of nonexpansive theory.

1.2. Results. The study of α-H¨older continuous mappings was initiated by Kirk in
1998 [13] through minimal displacement estimates. Recent developments were done
in [2, 3]. Within this framework, however, the existence of fixed points becomes
a central question.
In this paper, we address this problem by allowing H¨older
exponents to oscillate and asymptotically converge to 1.
This approach enables
us to move beyond displacement bounds and establish the FPP. Specifically, we
provide the following theorems:
Theorem 1.2. Let X be a Banach space such that ϵ0(X) < 1, and let K ⊂X be a
nonempty, closed, bounded, and convex subset. If T : K →K is of asymptotically
H¨older nonexpansive type, then there exists x ∈K such that T nx →x.
As a consequence, it follows that if some iterate T N is continuous then the limit
of (T nx) is a fixed point of T. In particular, we have the following:
Corollary 1.1. Let X and K be as in Theorem 1.2. Then every asymptotically
H¨older nonexpansive mapping T : K →K possesses a fixed point.
While these results significantly generalize [12, Theorem 1], the extent to which
other classical results (such as those in [4, 6, 21]) can be extended to this framework
remains a subject for future investigation. For the present, it is worth noting that
a compact version of Theorem 1.2 follows directly from the arguments in [12]:
Theorem 1.3. Let K be a nonempty, compact, and convex subset of a Banach
space X. Suppose T : K →K is of asymptotically H¨older nonexpansive type and
has a continuous iterate T N for some N ∈N. Then T has a fixed point in K.
The sharpness of these results is evident in the requirement ϵ0(X) < 1.
As
shown below, the FPP fails for the present class of mappings in spaces containing
isomorphic copies of c0 or ℓ1.
Theorem 1.4. Let X be a Banach space containing an isomorphic copy of c0.
Then there exists a bounded, closed, and convex subset K such that, for any
increasing sequence αn →1, there exists a fixed-point free asymptotically (αn)-
H¨older-nonexpansive mapping T : K →K.
Theorem 1.5. Let X be a Banach space containing an isomorphic copy of ℓ1. Then
there exists a bounded, closed, and convex subset K such that, for any increasing
sequence αn →1 in (0, 1), there exists an affine, fixed-point free mapping T : K →K
that is uniformly asymptotically (αn)-H¨older 1-Lipschitz.
The following result highlights the relevance of Theorem 1.3.
Theorem 1.6. Let X be an infinite dimensional Banach space. Then there exists a
compact convex set K ⊂X and a fixed-point free affine mapping T : K →K which
is of asymptotically H¨older nonexpansive type and has no continuous iterations T n.
