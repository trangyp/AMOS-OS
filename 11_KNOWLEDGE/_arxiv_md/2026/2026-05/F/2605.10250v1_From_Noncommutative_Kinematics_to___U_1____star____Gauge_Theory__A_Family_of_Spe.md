Abstract
We construct a spectral-triple framework for a noncommutative planar system associated
with a fixed nondegenerate irreducible unitary sector of the kinematical symmetry group
GNC, labelled by central parameters (ℏ0, ϑ0, B0) with ℏ0, ϑ0, B0 ̸= 0 and ℏ0 −ϑ0B0 ̸= 0. For
the corresponding two-parameter family (r, s) of unitarily equivalent concrete realizations of
this irreducible representation, we construct even spectral triples (Sℏ0,ϑ0,B0, H, Dr,s
ℏ0,ϑ0,B0).

ℏ0,ϑ0,B0 are isospectral, with spectrum {±√n : n ∈N0}, and have compact

Introduction
Noncommutative geometry offers a rich framework for unifying fundamental interactions and
for exploring the quantum structure of spacetime [9, 10, 21]. One of its simplest yet physically
compelling arenas is noncommutative quantum mechanics (NCQM) on the plane, which emerges
naturally from string theory in a background magnetic field and serves as a laboratory for noncom-
mutative gauge theories [11,17,31,32]. The kinematics of a system moving on a noncommutative
plane is encoded in the seven-dimensional nilpotent Lie group GNC, a triple central extension
of the translation group R4 [5, 7]. Its unitary irreducible representations are labelled by three
real parameters (ℏ, ϑ, Bin), where ϑ measures the noncommutativity of the position coordinates
and Bin appears as a background internal magnetic field in the commutator of the kinetic mo-
menta [4,6]. Related deformation-quantization approaches to NCQM and supersymmetric mod-
els on the noncommutative plane have provided important star-product frameworks for studying
such systems [23, 24]. A recent clarificatory note examined the status of these NCQM sectors
and showed that a generic sector with nonvanishing ℏ, ϑ, and Bin is kinematically inequivalent to
ordinary quantum mechanics [8]. The central character (ℏ, ϑ, Bin) fixes the unitary equivalence
class of the representation, and sectors with different Bin belong to distinct irreducible sectors
of GNC. Thus NCQM is not a mere reparametrization of quantum mechanics but a distinct
kinematical theory with its own symmetry group and representation theory.
In the present work we restrict attention to the nondegenerate sector defined by
ℏ̸= 0,
ϑ ̸= 0,
Bin ̸= 0,
ℏ−ϑBin ̸= 0.
For such a sector the noncentral generators admit a two-parameter family of unitarily equivalent
presentations, denoted by (r, s), first given in [6] (see Eq. (2.8) there). These parameters are
kinematical presentation parameters: they label different concrete presentations of the same
irreducible unitary representation and do not change the underlying kinematical sector. To pass
from this general discussion to the spectral-triple construction, we fix once and for all a coadjoint
orbit of GNC corresponding, via the orbit method, to a chosen irreducible unitary representation
in the above nondegenerate sector [4,6]. Equivalently, we fix a triple
(ℏ, ϑ, Bin) = (ℏ0, ϑ0, B0)
and work throughout with the associated fiber algebra and kinematical data. This point should
be contrasted with the earlier analysis in [3], which was carried out over the sector (ℏ0, ϑ0, 0)
with vanishing internal magnetic component. The present paper instead works over the genuinely
nondegenerate sector (ℏ0, ϑ0, B0), with ℏ0 ̸= 0, ϑ0 ̸= 0, B0 ̸= 0, and ℏ0 −ϑ0B0 ̸= 0. This fixed
nondegenerate irreducible unitary sector of GNC provides the kinematical background for the
entire paper.

sector (ℏ0, ϑ0, B0) and keeps the kinematical presentation data separate from the star-product
deformation data. The resulting theory is therefore organized by a fixed nondegenerate GNC-
sector, its associated effective Moyal-side involutive algebra, and localized bounded perturbations
associated with the external U(1)⋆ϑeff ,ϱ gauge sector.
