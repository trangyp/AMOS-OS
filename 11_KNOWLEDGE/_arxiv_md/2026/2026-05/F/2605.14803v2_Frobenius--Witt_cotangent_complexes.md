FROBENIUS–WITT COTANGENT COMPLEXES
KANAU SHIMADA
Abstract. We introduce the notion of the Frobenius–Witt cotangent complex, which can be
considered as a derived variant of the module of Frobenius–Witt differentials defined by T.
Saito in [Sai22]. This new object also can be seen as an arithmetic variant of the notion of
cotangent complex. We explain the suitability of these two viewpoints through a series of propo-
sitions. Furthermore, we establish a relationship between Frobenius–Witt cotangent complexes
and the regularity of noetherian local rings, which can be considered as a derived variant of
Saito’s regularity criterion ([Sai22, Theorem 3.4]). This proof relies heavily on computations of
Frobenius–Witt cotangent complexes in the case of perfectoid rings.

1. Introduction
Around 1970, M. Andr´e and D. Quillen introduced the notion of the cotangent complex,
which is a derived analogue of the module of K¨ahler differentials. On the other hand, in [Sai22],
T. Saito introduced the notion of the module of Frobenius–Witt differentials FΩA associated
with a commutative ring A as an arithmetic analogue of the module of K¨ahler differentials ΩA,
and proved that they are closely related to the regularity of commutative rings.
In this paper, we introduce the notion of Frobenius–Witt cotangent complex FLA as a derived
version of the module of Frobenius–Witt differentials FΩA. The main idea is to follow the
definition of cotangent complexes in [Lur18, Section 25.3]. Accordingly, we first define a derived
version of the notion of the Frobenius–Witt derivation, and then introduce Frobenius–Witt
cotangent complexes as universal objects that corepresent the spaces of them.

Using this theorem, we prove a derived variant of Saito’s regularity criterion ([Sai22, Theorem

Theorem 1.3 (Corollary 6.14). Let R be a p-torsionfree noetherian local ring of residue char-

(1) R is regular,
(2) FLR is a free R/p-module of rank dim R + logp[k : kp],

Our proof of (1) ⇒(2) uses essentially only homological algebra, apart from the ring-theoretic
relationship between perfectoid rings and regular rings, and in particular provides a conceptual
alternative proof of [Sai22, Theorem 3.4].
In the final section, under the assumption that the field with one element exists, we formulate
a conjecture concerning the existence of a refinement of the Frobenius–Witt cotangent complex.

3. Preliminaries on category theory
In this subsection, we recall some basic facts about the notion of animation. We also prove
some preliminary results in order to introduce an animated version of Frobenius–Witt deriva-
tions.
Definition 3.1. Let C be a category. An object X ∈C is called compact projective if and only
if the functor HomC (X, −) : C →Set commutes with all small filtered colimits and all reflexive
coequalizers.
Definition 3.2. Let C be a category. A set S consisting of objects of C is called a set of compact
projective generators of C if it consists of compact projective objects of C and generates C under
colimits.
Definition 3.3. Let C be a category, S be a set of compact projective generators of C and
C0 be the full subcategory of C generated by S under all finite colimits. Then we define the
animation Ani(C ) of C by the ∞-category:
Ani(C ) := FunQ(C op
0 , S),
where the right-hand side is the full subcategory of the ∞-category Fun(C op
0 , S) consisting of
functors preserving all finite products.
Remark 3.4. Let C be as in Definition 3.3.
• The ∞-category Ani(C ) depends only on the category C , not on the choice of S.
([Mao24, Remark A.20]).
• The ∞-category Ani(C ) admits all sifted colimits. ([Lur09, Proposition 5.5.8.10]).
• Let π0 : Ani(C ) →Ani(C ) be the functor of ∞-categories induced by the truncation
functor π0 : S →S of spaces. Then its essential image is canonically equivalent to the
category C . In this way, we can regard the category C as a full subcategory of Ani(C ).
([Lur09, Remark 5.5.8.26])
• Each object of C0 is compact projective in Ani(C ) and they generate Ani(C ) under
sifted colimits. ([Lur09, Proposition 5.5.8.22]).
