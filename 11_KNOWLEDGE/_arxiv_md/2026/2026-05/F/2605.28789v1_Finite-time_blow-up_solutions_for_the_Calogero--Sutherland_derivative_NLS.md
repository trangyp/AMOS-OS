Strategy of proofs. However, the existing arguments for proving finite-time blow-up
for (CM) do not seem to offer any insight into a possible construction of blow-up
solutions for (CS). To overcome this, we develop an alternative approach which
strongly rests on the explicit formula found for (CS). In this regard, let us mention
that the use of explicit formulae for integrable PDEs with Lax pairs on Hardy
spaces (e.g. the Benjamin–Ono equation, the cubic Szeg˝o equation, CS, CM, and
the half-wave maps equation) have turned out to be extremely powerful, yielding
results on scaling-critical global well-posedness, weak turbulence, zero-dispersion
limits, classification of solitary waves, and soliton resolution; see [Bad24, Bad25,
Che25, Gas23, GGM26, Ger24, GG15, GL24, GL25, GL26, GP23, GP24, HK24,
KLV25, Mae26].
In the approach taken below, we construct a family of smooth finite-time blow-
up solutions within a subclass of finite-gap potentials. More precisely, we identify a
resonant algebraic condition on the coefficients, and this resonance produces finite-
time blow-up solutions. Furthermore, the explicit formula allows us to compute the
complete dynamics of the finite-time blow-up. In the complementary non-resonant
case, we prove global existence and uniform-in-time Sobolev bounds within the
same finite-gap family. Thus the finite-dimensional class considered here exhibits a
sharp resonance/non-resonance dichotomy. To the best of our knowledge, this gives
the first construction of finite-time blow-up solutions for the focusing Calogero–
Sutherland DNLS equation. A distinctive feature of the construction is that the
blow-up mechanism is obtained directly from the explicit formula. In our companion
