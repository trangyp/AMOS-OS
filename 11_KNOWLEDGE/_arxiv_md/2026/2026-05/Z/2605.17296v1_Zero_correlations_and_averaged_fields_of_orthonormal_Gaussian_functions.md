a zero of pn+2 between two zeros of pn+1, and this suggests a positive correlation between
the zeros of pn and pn+2. This motivates a weaker notion adapted to planar zero sets. We

of fn+1 and fn and positive pair correlations in the short-range limit between the zeros of
fn and fn+2. (This notion will be made precise in Section 2 in terms of normalized pair
correlations.)
