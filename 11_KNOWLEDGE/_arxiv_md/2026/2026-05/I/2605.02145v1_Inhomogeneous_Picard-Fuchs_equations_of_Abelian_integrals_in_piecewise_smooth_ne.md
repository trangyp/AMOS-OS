1. Introduction and main results
It is well known that the algebraic structure of Abelian integrals and the associated
Picard-Fuchs equations play an important role in the analysis of limit cycle bifurcations
in (smooth) polynomial near-Hamiltonian systems. The algebraic structure of Abelian
integrals provides an efficient way to simplify Abelian integrals as linear combinations
of a finite number of generating functions. By the Picard-Fuchs equations of generating
functions we can study the qualitative property of Abelian integrals to estimate the
number of zeros of some Melnikov functions, see for example [1, 12, 13, 19] and references
therein.
Recently, the method of Picard-Fuchs equations has been applied in the study of
limit cycles for some piecewise smooth near-Hamiltonian systems.
