QI DING AND LEI ZHANG
Abstract. In this paper, we characterize all eigenfunctions corresponding to nonpositive eigen-
values of the Jacobi operator of the link M of the Lawson–Osserman cone C in R7. In particular,
we prove that C is integrable, i.e., all Jacobi fields on C of homogeneous degree 1 and 0, are gen-
erated by rotations and translations in R7. As applications, we prove that M is rigid as minimal
submanifolds in S6, and derive the optimal decay order for minimal submanifolds in R7 asymptotic
to C at infinity.

1. Introduction
Let C0 be a regular minimal cone in Euclidean space Rm+1, and Σ be the link of C0. Then Σ is
minimal in the unit sphere Sm ⊂Rm+1. Let JΣ denote the Jacobi operator of Σ in Sm. A smooth
normal vector Y on Σ is called a Jacobi field on Σ if JΣY = 0 (see Simons [29]). Allard–Almgren
[2] introduced a concept for the regular C0 as follows:
(†)
every Jacobi field on Σ arises as the variational vector field of some 1-parameter family of
minimal submanifolds in Sm,
which is referred to as an integrablility condition. Allard–Almgren [2] proved the celebrated unique-
ness theorem of tangent cones for minimal submanifolds provided one of tangent cones has multi-
plicity one and satisfies the condition (†). However, (†) does not hold for all regular minimal cones
pointed out by Allard–Almgren [2, page 216]. In [23], Simon established the uniqueness theorem
for the non-integrable case, where he developed a famous method, called Simon–Łojasiewicz in-
equality nowadays. Nevertheless, the condition (†) also acts a key role in the study of the structure
of minimal submanifolds with non-isolated singularities. Simon [26] proved rectifiability and local
finiteness of measure of the singular sets for minimal submanifolds close to cylindrical minimal
cones of the form C0 × Rk, where C0 satisfies (†).
In [27], Simon proposed an integrability condition stronger than (†) on the regular minimal cone
C, which contains that:

Here, 1-homogeneous Jacobi fields on C correspond to Jacobi fields on the link of C, and 0-

The integrability condition (‡) imposed on minimal cones plays an essential role in the study of
the uniqueness of minimal hypersurfaces converging to these cones or their products with Euclidean
factors. For the codimension 1 case, all the quadratic minimal cones
Cp,q := {(x, y) ∈Rp+1 × Rq+1 : q|x|2 = p|y|2}
satisfy the integrability condition (‡) proved by Allard–Almgren in [2, Chapter 6] (see also Simon–
Solomon [28, Proposition 2.7]). Making full use of (‡), Simon–Solomon [28] established the unique-
ness of complete minimal hypersurfaces asymptotic to an area-minimizing Cp,q (see also Mazet
[20]). Furthermore, Edelen–Spolaor [7] resolved the uniqueness of minimal hypersurfaces in the
unit ball close to an area-minimizing Cp,q and their result also implies the Bernstein type result
in [28]. For cylindrical cones, Simon proved the uniqueness of multiplicity one tangent cones of
the form C × R for area-minimizing hypersurfaces under the integrability condition on C in [27],
where both C3,3 and C2,4 do not satisfy the condition. Nevertheless, Székelyhidi [32] can resolve
the uniqueness of the cylindrical tangent cones C3,3 × R for area-minimizing hypersurfaces in R9.
Recently, Firester–Tsiamis–Wang [9] proved the remaining case of C2,4 × R by adopting the strat-
egy of Székelyhidi [32]. See Collins–Li [4] for the Lagrangian case under a Lagrangian integrability
condition.
For infinitesimal variations without Lagrangian or complex structure, the Jacobi operator of the
link of a minimal cone of high codimension is an elliptic system of second-order. Let M be an
n-dimensional closed minimal submanifold immersed in Sn+q. Simons [29] proved that the space
of Jacobi fields on M has dimension ⩾q(n + 1) with the equality achieved only if M is the totally
geodesic submanifold Sn ⊂Sn+q. In general, the precise computation of eigenvalues and eigenspaces
of Jacobi operators for minimal submanifolds of high codimensions is highly nontrivial except the
products of standard spheres.

Theorem 1.1. All Jacobi fields of the link M of the Lawson–Osserman cone in R7 are Killing
Jacobi fields, and the cone is integrable, i.e., (‡) holds. In particular, the first eigenvalue of the
Jacobi operator of M is −15

group SO(7). Theorem 1.1 tells us that the eigenfunction space with eigenvalue −3 of the Jacobi
operator of M consists of normal projection of coordinate functions. Furthermore, Theorem 1.1
indicates that Morse index of M is 8 and the space of Jacobi fields has dimension 17. Morse index
is an important object in the research of minimal submanifolds, and is useful in the proof of the
Willmore conjecture solved by Marques–Neves [19]. We refer the readers to Urbano’s book [35] for
more details.

the operator eL in (5.6).
As an application of Theorem 1.1, we prove a rigidity result as below.
Theorem 1.3. The link M of the Lawson–Osserman cone C is rigid as minimal submanifolds in
S6. Namely, any embedded minimal submanifold in S6 which is suﬀiciently C3-close to M must
coincide with M itself up to isometry.
The above C3-close condition is not essential, which can be weakened with the help of Allard’s
regularity theorem. For completeness, we give the proof in §6 by following a standard approach.
We refer Colding–Minicozzi [14], Evans–Lotay–Schulze [8], Schulze [22], Sun–Zhu [31] for rigidity
of round cylinders and products of spheres of high codimension.
Remark 1.4. The following problem was collected by Yau in [37, P9, Problem 31]: is there a
nontrivial continuous family of compact, embedded codimension one minimal hypersurface in Sn?
Theorem 1.3 yields a negative answer for the link of the Lawson–Osserman cone in the higher-
codimensional setting.
At last, we use Theorem 1.1 to study the asymptotic behavior of minimal submanifolds converging
to the Lawson–Osserman cone C at infinity, which is given in §7. In particular, we derive decay
rates for minimal submanifolds (with boundary) converging to C as below.
