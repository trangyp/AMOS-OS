INFINITE-EXPONENT PARTITION RELATIONS
ON HIGHER ANALOGUES OF THE REAL LINE
LYRA A. GARDINER, JONATHAN SCHILHAN, AND THILO WEINERT
We present a number of results concerning infinite-exponent partition relations
(IEPRs) on linear orders of the form ⟨α2, <lex⟩for α an ordinal, generalising the
setting of the real line (i.e. the case α = ω), working throughout in ZF. As a
particular consequence of our results, we obtain a full classification of the relation
⟨α2, <lex⟩! (τ)τ for τ countable.
1. Introduction
1.1. Background and main results. This paper is a sequel to the first author’s
[Ga25], in which infinite-exponent partition relations on arbitrary linear orders were
introduced and a number of results were proved for the setting of the real number
line ⟨R, <⟩. In this paper we expand our scope to “higher analogues of the reals”,
by which we mean linear orders of the form ⟨α2, <lex⟩for α an arbitrary infinite
ordinal, proving a number of results in this setting by combining ideas from that
paper with ideas from the third author’s [LSW17]. The majority of the results in
this paper were obtained during a visit by the first and second authors to the third
author in Udine in March 2024, and a subsequent visit by the first author to the
second author in Vienna in September 2025.
Our base theory throughout this paper is ZF without the Axiom of Choice,
as IEPRs on linear orders are either false or trivial in a certain sense under AC;
see [Ga25, §1.3] for a discussion of this. Our main results are the following; they
are all stated in terms of the arrow notation for partition relations, defined in §1.2.
