INFINITESIMAL AUTOMORPHISMS AND OBSTRUCTION THEORY
ON THE MODULI OF L-VALUED G-HIGGS BUNDLES
SANGHYEON LEE AND SANG-BUM YOO
Abstract. For an arbitrary reductive group G, we compute the infinitesimal auto-
morphisms of L-valued principal G-Higgs bundles over a compact K¨ahler manifold X,
extending known results for Ω1
X-valued G-Higgs bundles.
Using this computation, when G is semisimple and X is a smooth projective variety,
we show that the moduli stack of stable L-valued G-Higgs bundles is a Deligne-Mumford
(DM) stack.
Furthermore, when X is a smooth projective surface and L “ KX, we construct a
symmetric perfect obstruction theory on this stable locus. We expect this will provide a
foundation for defining Vafa-Witten invariants for reductive groups G.

1. Introduction
Let X be a compact connected smooth K¨ahler manifold, equipped with a fixed K¨ahler
form ω. For a reductive linear algebraic group G, the Hitchin-Kobayashi type correspon-
dence for the stability of principal G-bundles on X, and G-bundles with additional data
has been studied for a long time.
In [4], B. Anchouche and I. Biswas proved that a holomorphic principal G-bundle EG
has an Einstein-Hermitian connection if and only if EG is polystable. (See [4, Definition
3.5] for the definition of polystability.) As an intermediate step, they also proved that if
EG is stable, then the adjoint vector bundle adpEGq :“ EG ˆG g is polystable where g is
the Lie algebra of the Lie group G.
Furthermore, in [5], I. Biswas and G. Schumacher proved that an Ω1
X-valued G-Higgs
sheaf admits a hermitian Yang-Mills-Higgs connection if and only if it is polystable. 1
