Abstract
A 2024 paper of Sun, Ding and Wang introduced a second class of constacyclic codes over finite fields, denoted C(q, m, r, ℓ),
with length (qm −1)/r, where r | (q −1) and the defining monomials have total q-ary degree congruent to r −1 modulo r. In the
non-projective intermediate range 2 < r < q −1 the paper gave a sharp-looking upper bound and a BCH-type lower bound, and
left the minimum distance open. We prove that the upper bound is the exact minimum distance for every admissible intermediate
parameter. More precisely, if ℓ= (q −1)a + b < (q −1)m −1, 0 ≤b ≤q −2, and b ≡r −1 (mod r), then, for every prime
power q, every divisor r of q −1 with 2 < r < q −1, and every m ≥2,

We determined the minimum distance of the intermediate constacyclic codes C(q, m, r, ℓ) introduced by Sun, Ding and
Wang for every prime power q, every m ≥2, and every divisor r | (q −1) with 2 < r < q −1. The exact value is
