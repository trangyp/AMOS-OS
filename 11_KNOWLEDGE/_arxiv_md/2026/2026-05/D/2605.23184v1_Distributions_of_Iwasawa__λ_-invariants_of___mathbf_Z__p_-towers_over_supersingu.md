Abstract
A graph-theoretic analogue of Iwasawa theory, initiated by Gonet and Vallières, has attracted
considerable interest in the study of Iwasawa invariants. On the other hand, for a pair of prime
numbers (𝑟, ℓ), one obtains a graph, called the supersingular ℓ-isogeny graph (SIG), whose ad-
jacency matrix has eigenvalues given by the ℓ-th Fourier coefficients of the weight 2 Eisenstein
series and newforms of level 𝑟. In this paper, we fix prime numbers 𝑟and 𝑝, and let ℓvary over
infinitely many primes. We then investigate the distribution of the Iwasawa 𝜆-invariants of the
constant 𝐙𝑝-towers over the SIGs, thereby revealing connections among graph theory, Iwasawa
theory, elliptic curves, and the Galois representations attached to newforms. At the end of this
paper, we propose a conjecture concerning the Galois orbits of newforms.

In [6], Dion, Lei, Ray, and Vallières investigated the distributions of Iwasawa invariants, and
in particular, they showed that Iwasawa 𝜆-invariants are always odd if 𝑝≠2. Kataoka [15]

invariants are (𝜇, 𝜆). On the other hand, Lei and Müller [18], [19], [20], [21] study 𝐙𝑝-towers
of certain directed graphs called isogeny graphs, in particular supersingular ℓ-isogeny graphs,
abbreviated as ℓ-SIGs. Isogeny graphs may be regarded as graph-theoretic analogues of modu-
lar curves over finite fields, and ℓ-SIGs are graph-theoretic analogues of the supersingular loci of
modular curves. Indeed, the vertices of an ℓ-SIG are isomorphism classes of supersingular elliptic
curves over the algebraic closure ̅̅̅̅𝐅𝑟of a finite field of characteristic 𝑟(≠ℓ) and the edges cor-
respond to ℓ-isogenies. In particular, in [20], they study 𝐙𝑝-towers consisting of supersingular
connected components of isogeny graphs.
In this paper, we treat SIGs of level 1. The history of studies on such SIGs is long, and it is
known that the eigenvalues of the adjacency matrix of an ℓ-SIG of level 1 coincide with the Fourier
coefficients of weight-2 Eisenstein series and newforms [8], [9], [12]. Each SIG is determined
by the choice of a pair of prime numbers 𝑟, ℓ, and these 𝑓𝑖are determined only by the choice of 𝑟.

call the double supersingular isogeny graph. Given a double supersingular isogeny graph 𝑋, a
special 𝐙𝑝-tower, called a constant 𝐙𝑝-tower,
