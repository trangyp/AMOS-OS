Abstract
We propose an RG-improved effective-field-theory framework for the deeply bound dibaryon d∗(2380), a ∆∆bound
state in the (J, I) = (3, 0) 7S3 channel. Its binding momentum γ ≃320 MeV gives γ/mπ ≃2.3, indicating the need
to re-organize the short-range dynamics beyond a formal pionless EFT. We match the large-Nc-constrained pionless
contact potential to a meson-exchange-saturated contact interaction in which the σ, ρ, ω dynamics are integrated out at
the hadronic scale mV , yielding the controlled expansion parameter γ/mV ≃0.42. Normalizing the contact coupling to
the deuteron and substituting the phenomenological CD-Bonn couplings gives B∆∆≃96 MeV. The ≃14% discrepancy
from Bexp = 84 MeV is of the natural size of O(1/N 2
c ) ≃11% corrections to the NN potential, confirming compatibility
with a controlled EFT expansion organized around the finite-range hadronic scale. As a result, the observed d∗(2380)
pole emerges from the virtual state to bound state by using the EFT re-organization in this work.
Keywords:
dibaryons, effective field theory, large-Nc QCD, meson-exchange saturation, d∗(2380)
1. Introduction
The d∗(2380) dibaryon was discovered by the WASA-
at-COSY collaboration [1, 2] and confirmed through in-
dependent channels [3, 4, 5, 6]. With quantum numbers
JP (I) = 3+(0), it lies B∆∆≃84 MeV below the ∆∆
threshold, consistent with a compact hexaquark [7, 8, 9],
a molecular ∆∆bound state [10, 11], a mixed configu-
ration [12, 13], or the Dyson–Xuong D03 member of the
SU(6) dibaryon classification [14].
Lattice-QCD stud-
ies find attraction in this channel [15, 16], and recent
symmetry-based analyses connect d∗(2380) to entangle-
ment suppression and approximate spin-flavor SU(6) con-
straints in decuplet-baryon scattering [17, 18, 19].
The binding momentum γ = √M∆B∆∆≃322 MeV
gives γ/mπ ≃2.3 > 1, so a formal pionless EFT [20, 21, 22]
breaks down. For comparison, the deuteron has γd/mπ ≃
0.33 [23, 24]. We propose that this breakdown signals not a
failure of EFT itself, but the need to re-organize the match-
ing scale. Once the short-distance contact LEC is deter-
mined by meson-exchange saturation [25, 26, 27, 28, 29]
at Λ ∼mV , the expansion parameter becomes γ/mV ≃
0.42, within the convergence domain of a nonperturbative
hadronic EFT [24, 30, 31]. Large-Nc contracted spin-flavor
symmetry [32, 33, 34, 35] further constrains the ∆∆con-
tact interaction, providing a factor-of-five enhancement of

d∗(2380) can be understood as a consequence of EFT re-
