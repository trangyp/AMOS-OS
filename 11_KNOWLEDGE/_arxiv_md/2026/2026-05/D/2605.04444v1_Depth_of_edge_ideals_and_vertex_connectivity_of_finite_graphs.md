Abstract. Let G be a finite graph on [n] := {1, . . . , n} and κ(G) its vertex
connectivity. Let S = K[x1, . . . , xn] denote the polynomial ring in n variables
over a field K and I(Gc) the edge ideal of the complementary graph Gc of G. It
is a classical result that depth S/I(Gc) ≤κ(G) + 1. We give a sharp lower bound
of depth S/I(Gc) in terms of n and κ(G).
Furthermore, a sharp lower bound
of depth S/I(Gc)2 as well as that of depth S/I(Gc)(2) in terms of n and κ(G) is
given.
Introduction
Every graph to be studied is simple and finite. Let G be a graph on the vertex
set V (G) = {x1, . . . , xn} and E(G) the set of edges of G. For W ⊂V (G), G −W
stands for the induced subgraph G|V (G)\W of G on V (G)\W. If G is not a complete
graph, then let κ(G) denote the smallest cardinality of W ⊂V (G) for which G−W
is disconnected.
We call κ(G) the vertex connectivity of G.
In particular, G is
connected if and only if κ(G) > 0. If T is a tree on [n], then κ(G) = 1. If Cn is
a cycle of length n, then κ(Cn) = 2. We employ the convention that the vertex
connectivity of the complete graph on [n] is n −1.
A clique of G is a subset C ⊂V (G) for which {xi, xj} ∈E(G) for all xi, xj ∈C
with xi ̸= xj. The clique complex of G is the simplicial complex on V (G) which
consists of all cliques of G. Let S = K[x1, . . . , xn] denote the polynomial ring in n
variables over a field K. The Stanley–Reisner ideal of ∆(G) is the ideal
I∆(G) = (xixj : 1 ≤i < j ≤n, {xi, xj} ̸∈E(G))
of S and the Stanley–Reisner ring of ∆(G) is the quotient ring K[∆(G)] = S/I∆(G).
The edge ideal of G is the ideal
I(G) = (xixj : 1 ≤i < j ≤n, {xi, xj} ∈E(G)) ⊂S.
One has I∆(G) = I(Gc), where Gc is the complementary graph ([3, p. 153]) of G.
It is observed in [11] that Hocster’s formula [3, Theorem 8.1.1] guarantees that
depth S/I(Gc) ≤κ(G) + 1.

One has
