DISCREPANCY OF DETERMINANTAL POINT PROCESSES ON
COMPACT, CONNECTED TWO-POINT HOMOGENEOUS SPACES
CARLOS BELTRÁN, UJUÉ ETAYO, GIACOMO GIGANTE, PEDRO R. LÓPEZ-GÓMEZ,
AND RYAN W. MATZKE
Abstract. We study the L∞discrepancy of point sets generated by deter-
minantal point processes on all compact, connected two-point homogeneous
spaces, namely spheres and projective spaces. Using concentration inequalities
and variance estimates for the number of points in metric balls, we derive
general upper bounds for the discrepancy of homogeneous determinantal point
processes. In the particular case of the harmonic ensemble, we show that the
discrepancy of N points is O((N1−1/D)1/2 log N) with high probability, where
D denotes the real dimension of the manifold. For the projective ensemble on
CPd, we obtain the sharper bound O((N1−1/D log N)1/2). These results extend
previously known discrepancy estimates for determinantal point processes on
the sphere to all compact, connected two-point homogeneous spaces.

The random point configurations yielding the quasi-optimal discrepancy estimates
in the cases mentioned above are essentially obtained by selecting one point uni-
formly in each element of an area regular partition of X. A more general approach
is to estimate the discrepancy of a random collection of points drawn from a deter-
minantal point process (dpp). This has been done in [1] for the spherical ensemble
on the 2-dimensional sphere, matching the bound (N 1/2 log N)1/2, and in [6] for the
harmonic ensemble on the D-dimensional sphere, yielding the slightly worse bound
(N 1−1/D)1/2 log N. In [7], the authors studied the expected L2 discrepancy of the
projective ensemble on the complex projective spaces CPd, and the harmonic en-
semble for spheres Sd, finding upper bounds (N 1−1/2d)1/2 and (N 1−1/2d)1/2√log N,
respectively. The first bound is optimal, but the second has an extra factor of
√log N, similar to the findings for the L∞discrepancy of the harmonic ensemble in
Theorem 1.5 below.
The main goal of this paper is to extend the techniques used to study the
discrepancy of dpps on spheres to the remaining compact, connected two-point
homogeneous spaces.

1.2.1. The harmonic ensemble on compact, connected two-point homogeneous spaces.
The harmonic ensemble on a compact Riemannian manifold M is the dpp generated
by the reproducing kernel of the finite-dimensional subspace of L2(M) spanned
by the eigenfunctions of the Laplace–Beltrami operator on M corresponding to
eigenvalues up to a certain threshold. It was first introduced in the case of the sphere
Sd in [6] and later generalized to all the compact, connected two-point homogeneous
spaces in [2].
