Abstract. We improve the decoupling exponent for functions with spectrum in-
side AD-regular collections of arcs on the parabola. We achieve this by incorporat-
ing recent Szemer´edi–Trotter-type estimates into the bootstrapping argument from
[BD15]. As an application, our results complement, and in some cases improve
earlier results [CDPG+22] for arithmetic Cantor sets.
