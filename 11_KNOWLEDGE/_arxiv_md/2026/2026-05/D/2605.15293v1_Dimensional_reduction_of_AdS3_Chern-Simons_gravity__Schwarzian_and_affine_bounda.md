Abstract: We study a symmetry-reduced sector of AdS3/Z2 gravity formulated as an
SO(2, 2) Chern–Simons theory on a 3D-manifold with toroidal boundary. The reduction is
implemented by requiring a globally defined symmetry and restricting to the sector in which
the gauge connection is invariant along the symmetry flow. The resulting theory reduces to
a two-dimensional BF-like model together with an induced one-dimensional boundary action.
We show that the reduced theory admits two inequivalent boundary sectors, originated by two
different boundary conditions for the parent 3d theory at the level of the variational principle.
On the boundary subspace Aτ = Φ, the universal one-dimensional action reproduces the
standard Drinfel’d–Sokolov reduction in JT gravity which captures the Schwarzian boundary
dynamics. On the generalized boundary Aτ = λ′Φ + u−1∂τu, the same action instead yields
a deformed Schwarzian functional with affine residual symmetry, naturally associated with
a non-extremal or Rindler-type regime. We further show how the so(2, 2) algebra of the
3D Chern–Simons model naturally leads to current-dressed Kac–Moody extensions of both
sectors.
Keywords: Chern-Simons, AdS3 gravity, JT Gravity, Schwarzian action, Virasoro-Kac-
Moody coadjoint orbits.

Introduction
The Jackiw–Teitelboim (JT) gravity/Sachdev–Ye–Kitaev (SYK) correspondence provides
one of the simplest settings in which a gravitational bulk description gives rise to an effective
one-dimensional boundary dynamics, which is governed by the Schwarzian action [1–8]. In
the standard gauge-theoretic formulation, JT gravity can be described as an SL(2, R) BF
theory, and the Schwarzian mode arises after imposing suitable asymptotic conditions and
performing a Drinfel’d–Sokolov reduction [9–14]. This framework has been generalized in
several directions, including extensions with additional current sectors and deformations
relevant to non-extremal or near-horizon regimes [15–20]. The purpose of this paper is to
show that different one-dimensional boundary theories can be understood within a common
three-dimensional framework. More precisely, we consider AdS3/Z2 gravity formulated as
an SO(2, 2) Chern–Simons theory on a three-manifold with toroidal boundary [21–24], and
we study a symmetry-reduced sector obtained by selecting a globally defined vector field and
restricting to connections invariant along its flow. The resulting theory reduces naturally
to a two-dimensional BF-like model together with an induced one-dimensional boundary
action. Our main point is that the reduced theory may accommodate two inequivalent
boundary models, already at the level of the variational principle. The first one is obtained
by restricting the boundary configuration space to the condition
Aτ = Φ,
(1.1)
with Φ the dilaton field, leading to the standard Drinfel’d–Sokolov reduction and to
Schwarzian dynamics [7, 13, 14]. The second one is obtained by allowing instead gen-
eralized boundary data of the form
Aτ = λ′Φ + u−1∂τu,
(1.2)
giving rise to a deformed Schwarzian action with affine rather than projective residual
symmetry [20]. In this way, the ordinary Schwarzian and its deformed affine counterpart
arise as two different boundary realizations of the same symmetry-reduced AdS3 Chern–
Simons system.
A second point concerns the role of the additional chiral SL(2, R) sector. The distinction
between the two sectors is fixed by the admissible boundary data of the gravitational sector
and is therefore independent of whether additional current degrees of freedom are present.
By contrast, a Kac–Moody extension appears when the second chiral sector is not treated as
a decoupled copy, but is reorganized after reduction as an internal loop-valued sector acted
upon by the boundary reparametrization mode of the first. This leads naturally to current-
dressed extensions of both the Schwarzian and the deformed affine sectors [16–19, 25–27].
The Kac-Moody extension of the Schwarzian sector derived from the dimensional reduction
of AdS3 can thus provide a gravitational dual for SYK-like models with global symmetries
[17].

of AdS3 gravity and specify the global setting relevant for the reduction. In section 3 we

lation as a three-dimensional Chern-Simons theory based on the gauge group SO(2, 2) for
Λ < 0, ISO(2, 1) for Λ = 0, or SO(3, 1) for Λ > 0, with Λ the cosmological constant. [21–24].

8.3
Unified interpretation
The discussion above separates two logically independent ingredients of the reduced boundary
theory. First, the admissible boundary data of the gravitational sector determine the nature
of the reparametrization mode: projective in the Drinfel’d–Sokolov sector, affine in the
deformed sector. Second, the interpretation of the second chiral sector determines whether
the resulting one-dimensional theory is purely gravitational or current-dressed. If the second
sector is left independent, one obtains a decoupled boundary system; if it is reorganized as
an internal current sector acted upon by the first, one obtains a Kac–Moody extension. The
symmetry-reduced three-dimensional theory therefore gives rise to the Schwarzian theory,
the deformed affine Schwarzian theory, and their corresponding current-dressed extensions.

9
Conclusion
In this paper we studied a symmetry-reduced sector of three-dimensional AdS3/Z2 gravity
formulated as an SO(2, 2) Chern–Simons theory on a manifold with toroidal boundary.
Imposing invariance along the non-contractible cycle, we showed that the theory reduces
to a two-dimensional BF-like system together with an induced one-dimensional boundary
action. The main result is that the reduced theory admits two inequivalent boundary sectors,
selected already at the level of the variational principle. On the boundary subspace
Aτ = Φ,
(9.1)
the universal one-dimensional action reduces to the quadratic form underlying the Drinfel’d–
Sokolov reduction, and hence to the Schwarzian theory. On the generalized boundary
space
Aτ = λ′Φ + u−1∂τu,
(9.2)
the same universal boundary action instead gives a deformed Schwarzian functional with
affine residual symmetry. In this sense, the Schwarzian and deformed Schwarzian theories
arise as two distinct boundary realizations of the same symmetry-reduced Chern–Simons
system. A second result is that the distinction between the two sectors is logically independent
of the appearance of a Kac–Moody sector. The former is fixed by the choice of admissible
boundary data for the gravitational chiral sector. The latter depends instead on how the
second chiral SL(2, R) sector is interpreted after reduction: either as a decoupled copy,
or as an internal loop-valued sector acted upon by the reparametrization mode of the
first. This leads naturally to current-dressed extensions of both the Schwarzian and the
affine/deformed sectors. Moreover, the Kac-Moody extension of the Schwarzian sector
successfully reproduces the low energy description of charged SYK-like models with internal
SL(2, R) charge, providing then a well defined gravitational dual theory. Our analysis is
purely at the level of the reduced gravitational and gauge-theoretic description. In particular,
while the current-dressed affine sector may be relevant for non-extremal or Rindler-like
generalizations of symmetry-enhanced Schwarzian systems, we do not claim a microscopic
boundary realization here. Establishing such a realization remains an open problem.
