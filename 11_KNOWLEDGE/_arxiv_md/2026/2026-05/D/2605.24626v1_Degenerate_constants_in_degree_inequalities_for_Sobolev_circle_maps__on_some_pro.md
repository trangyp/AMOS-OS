DEGENERATE CONSTANTS IN DEGREE INEQUALITIES FOR SOBOLEV
CIRCLE MAPS: ON SOME PROBLEMS POSED BY BREZIS
XU’AN DOU AND ZEYU JIN
Abstract. We sharpen the constants in two degree inequalities for circle-valued Sobolev maps in
degenerate regimes, as p →1+ or δ →0+. The two proofs use the same power trick together with
elementary estimates. The results answer two open problems posed by Brezis [3, Section 5]. The
proofs are obtained by generative AI and are verified by the authors.

The proofs use a power identity of the degree: if f ∈VMO(S1; S1) and k ≥1 is an integer, then
fk ∈VMO(S1; S1) and
(1.2)
deg(fk) = k deg f.
This is a basic property of the degree and follows from e.g. deg fg = deg f + deg g for two VMO
maps f, g, see [4, Theorem 12.1]. It can also be viewed as a functorial identity, since f k = Pk ◦f,
where Pk : S1 →S1, Pk(z) = zk and deg fk = deg(Pk) deg f = k deg f.
Both proofs use the same power trick. In the proof of Theorem 1.3 we apply Theorem 1.1 to the
powers fk and average over k with weights chosen so that the endpoint factor p −1 appears. In the
proof of Theorem 1.4 we choose a single power n ≃δ−1; the map fn converts the threshold δ into
the fixed threshold
√
3, and deg(fn) = n deg f yields the extra factor δ.
The proofs of Theorems 1.3 and 1.4 were obtained by the generative AI system Rethlas [6]1 and
were subsequently checked and verified by the authors.
The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section 2 proves Theorem 1.3. Section 3
proves Theorem 1.4.
