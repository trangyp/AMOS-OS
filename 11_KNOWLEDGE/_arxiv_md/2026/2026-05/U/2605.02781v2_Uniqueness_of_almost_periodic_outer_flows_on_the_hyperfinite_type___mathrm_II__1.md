UNIQUENESS OF ALMOST PERIODIC OUTER FLOWS
ON THE HYPERFINITE TYPE II1 FACTOR
CYRIL HOUDAYER AND AMINE MARRAKCHI
Abstract. We show that any almost periodic outer flow α : R ↷R on
the hyperfinite type II1 factor with Connes’spectrum Γ(α) = R satisfies
the Rokhlin property and thus is unique up to cocycle conjugacy. The
proof relies on a key cocycle perturbation result for type III amenable
equivalence relations. As a byproduct of our methods, we also show that
every almost periodic factor of type III1 with separable predual has an
extremal almost periodic faithful normal state.
1. Introduction and statement of the main results
1.1. Uniqueness of almost periodic outer flows on R. By the funda-
mental work of Connes [Co75b], there is a unique amenable type II1 factor
with separable predual: it is isomorphic to the unique hyperfinite type II1
factor R of Murray–von Neumann [MvN43].
Moreover, Connes [Co75a]
showed that there is a unique automorphism θ ∈Aut(R) up to outer con-
jugacy when the corresponding action Z ↷R is outer, i.e. θn /∈Inn(R) for
every n ∈Z \ {0}.
A next natural step is to classify continuous flows R ↷R on the hy-
perfinite type II1 factor. This problem, which has been pushed forth by
Takesaki since the early 1980s, is still open. In order to state the classifica-
tion problem for flows R ↷R on the hyperfinite type II1 factor, we follow
Kawahigashi’s beautiful exposition on Popa’s W∗-news blog [Po25].
A natural replacement of the outer conjugacy for a single automorphism
is the cocycle conjugacy for a flow. However, it is not straightforward to
define the right notion of “outerness” for a flow. We should keep in mind
that there is a certain similarity between the classification of flows on the
hyperfinite type II1 factor up to cocycle conjugacy and the classification of
amenable type III factors and their modular automorphism group. In that
respect, an important invariant for the cocycle conjugacy class of a flow
α : R ↷R is Connes’ spectrum Γ(α), which is a closed subgroup of R. The
condition Γ(α) = R is equivalent to the factoriality of the crossed product
von Neumann algebra R ⋊α R. However, unlike the analogous situation of
the modular automorphism group σφ of a type III1 factor M, the condition
Γ(α) = R does not necessarily imply that α is outer, i.e. that αt /∈Inn(R)
for every t ∈R\{0}. A stronger assumption that one can make on α is strict
outerness which means that R′ ∩(R ⋊α R) = C1. If α is strictly outer, then

We emphasize the fact that Theorem A holds for arbitrary almost periodic
flows α : R ↷R with no extra assumptions on the fixed-point subalgebra
Rα ⊂R. Prior to our work, the uniqueness of almost periodic flows on the
hyperfinite type II1 factor up to cocycle conjugacy was only known in some
very specific cases such as minimal almost periodic flows (i.e. (Rα)′∩R = C1)
[MT12, Theorem 6.12]. In [Ka88b], an interesting example of an ergodic
almost periodic flow (i.e. Rα = C1) is also treated.
We also point out that Theorem A holds more generally for almost pe-
riodic actions of arbitrary second countable locally compact abelian groups
on the hyperfinite II1 factor (see Theorem 7.1 below).
The proof of Theorem A divides into two independent steps. First, we
prove the theorem in the case where the flow α is prime, i.e. when the
fixed point algebra Rα is a factor. We exploit the structure of prime almost
periodic flows on the hyperfinite II1 factor [Jo82, OPT79] to show by hand
that α : R ↷R has the Rokhlin property. Our argument is inspired by
[MT12, Theorem 6.12] and [Ki95, Proposition 2.5].
The second step, which is the key novelty of our paper, is a reduction
from the general case to the prime case by a cocycle perturbation argument.
In fact, we prove that every almost periodic flow α such that Γ(α) = R is
cocycle conjugate to an almost periodic flow that is prime.
Theorem B. Let M be a factor with separable predual and α : R ↷M an
almost periodic flow such that Γ(α) = R. Then α is cocycle conjugate to an
almost periodic flow β : R ↷M such that Mα ⊂Mβ and Mβ is a factor.
More precisely, we have βt = Ad(vt) ◦αt for some continuous homomor-
phism v : R →U(Z(Mα)).
Quite unexpectedly, in order to prove Theorem B, one has to deal with
type III equivalence relations, even if one is only interested in the case where
M is a II1 factor. Indeed, the almost periodicity of the flow α : R ↷M
implies that Mα is stably normalized by the eigenvectors of α and this
induces an amenable equivalence relation R on the center Z(Mα).
The
morphism v : R →U(Z(Mα)) of Theorem B, is obtained by studying the
cohomology of R and it turns out that this equivalence relation can be
of arbitrary type (possibly type III) even when M is a II1 factor.
This
phenomenon was already observed in [BHV15].

1.3. Extremal almost periodic weights. Let M be a factor with sepa-
rable predual and denote by P(M) the set of all faithful normal semifinite
weights on M. Following [Co72, Co74], we say that φ ∈P(M) is almost
periodic if the modular automorphism group σφ : R ↷M is almost periodic.
Almost periodic weights are of the utmost importance in the classification
theory of type III factors. Indeed, Connes [Co74] showed that when M is
a type III factor, to any almost periodic weight φ ∈P(M), one can asso-
ciate a canonical discrete decomposition of the form M = N ⋊θ Γ, where
N is a type II∞von Neumann algebra and θ : Γ ↷N is a trace-scaling
action for some countable subgroup Γ < R∗
+. Using the above discrete de-
composition, the classification problem for type III factors reduces to the
classification of type II∞von Neumann algebras and their outer automor-
phisms. In [Co72, Co75a, Co75b], Connes applied this approach with great
success to the structure and the classification of factors of type IIIλ with
λ ∈[0, 1).

almost periodic weight. We then simply say that M is an almost periodic
