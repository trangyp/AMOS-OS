ULTRAPOWERS OF SPECTRAL SUBSPACES
HIROSHI ANDO AND ISAAC GOLDBRING
Abstract. We prove, for any W∗-probability space (M, φ) where M is a type
III1 factor, any nontrivial, proper closed F ⊆R, and any nonprincipal ultrafilter
U on N, that the ultrapower M(σφ, F)U of the spectral subspace M(σφ, F) is
a proper subset of the spectral subspace MU(σφU, F). We discuss the model-
theoretic implications of this result.
1. Introduction
The main result of this paper is in response to the recent paper [4] of Arulseelan,
Hart, Sinclair, and the second author detailing a model-theoretic framework for
W∗-probability spaces. Let us begin with some context.
By a W∗-probability space we mean a pair (M, φ) with M a von Neumann al-
gebra and φ a faithful, normal state on M. A model theoretic treatment of such
pairs in the case that φ is a trace was given in [7]; a tracial von Neumann alge-
bra (M, φ) is viewed as a many-sorted structure whose sorts are given by op-
erator norm bounded balls equipped with the metric stemming from the norm

continuous.

In [4], the authors wished to have honest multiplication as a primitive in the
language and thus abandoned the use of operator norm bounded balls in favor
of balls given by totally bounded elements. Here, an element a ∈M is said
to be K-totally bounded (with respect to φ) if both left and right multiplica-
tion by both a and a∗extend to bounded maps on L2(M, φ) of operator norm at
most K while a is said to be totally bounded if it is K-totally bounded for some
K > 0. The totally bounded elements are strongly dense in M, and thus using
the K-totally bounded elements as sorts (as K ranges over N) ended up yield-
ing a model-theoretic framework for W∗-probability algebras that allowed for
multiplication in the language. It is worth pointing out that the proof that this
treatment actually works was quite nontrivial and used a number of technical
ideas from Tomita-Takesaki theory as well as some ideas of Kadison.
In this paper, we suggest (and ultimately refute) an alternate proposal for a
model-theoretic treatment of W∗-probability spaces, this time using spectral
subspaces as sorts. The precise definition of the spectral subspace M(σφ, F)
will be given in the next section, but suffice it to say that these spaces play a cru-
cial role in the study of type III factors and the collection S
a>0 M(σφ, [−a, a]) is
strongly dense in M, whence at first glance it seems somewhat feasible that these
sets could be used as sorts in a model-theoretic description of W∗-probability
spaces.
A minimum requirement for an assignment (M, φ) 7→S(M, φ) to be consid-
ered as a sort is that it is a definable set, which, operator-algebraically, means
that S(Q

tion with ultraproducts does not generally hold in the case of spectral subspaces.
In fact, we show something stronger:
Theorem (Theorem 4.1). For any σ-finite type III1 factor M, any faithful normal
state φ on M, any nonprincipal ultrafilter U on N, and any nontrivial, proper closed
subset F ⊊R, we have that MU(σφU, F) ̸= M(σφ, F)U.
Here, M(σφ, F)U is the set of all x ∈MU which is represented by a bounded
sequence (xn)n such that xn ∈M(σφ, F) for every n ∈N. In particular,
M(σφ, R)U = MU and M(σφ, ∅)U = {0}.
In other words, except for the trivial situations F = ∅, R, the spectral subspace
M(σφ, F) is not even a definable subset of (M, φ) (let alone a definable set rel-
ative to the ambient theory of W∗-probability spaces). We note that part of the
main result of [4] is that taking the set of K-totally bounded elements (for a
fixed K) does commute with ultraproducts. In some sense, our result validates
the choice of totally bounded elements as choice of sorts.

There is a connection between spectral subspaces and totally bounded elements.
Indeed, in our recent preprint [1], we showed that if x ∈M(σφ, [−a, a]) is a con-
traction, then x is totally ea/2-bounded. It is easy to see that the converse may not
hold even for a type I factor. Our result shows that despite the close relationship
and similarity between these two classes of operators, totally bounded elements
are preferable for the model theoretic approach to von Neumann algebra theory.
Although taking spectral subspaces does not commute with ultraproducts (or
even ultrapowers), we show that there is always a containment in one direction:
Theorem (Proposition 3.1). For any family (Mi, φi)i∈I of W∗-probability spaces and
any ultrafilter U on I, one has that Q

Q

Model-theoretically, this implies that the spectral subspaces M(σφ, F) form a
zeroset relative to the theory of W∗-probability spaces.
To keep this note relatively short, we assume that the reader is familiar with ba-
sic facts about W∗-probability spaces and continuous model theory; they may
consult our preprint [1] for more information. We do however review the nec-
essary material on spectral subspaces in the next section.
