The theory developed here adds seven pieces beyond the basic resolution of the open problem. First, the admissible-
weight framework includes exact monotone power weights and approximate weights satisfying 𝑤′∕𝑤≥𝑞∕𝑥−𝜌.
Second, the shifted family covers 𝐼𝜈+𝑛and classifies the endpoint choices 𝐼𝜈+𝑛+𝑘. Third, lower endpoint bounds give
two-sided estimates for power weights and for weights with reversed one-sided comparison. Fourth, the sharp quotient
is characterized by endpoint expansions and the stationary equation (68), with a one-parameter optimized constructive
constant. Fifth, both constructive and sharp constants satisfy useful parameter comparison theorems. Sixth, positive
power mixtures and monotone regularly varying amplitudes are covered without changing the endpoint scale. Seventh,
the resulting estimates recover and strengthen the Gaunt-type upper bounds by covering the full natural range 0 < 𝛾< 1.
