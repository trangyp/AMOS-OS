WEAK SOLUTIONS OF THE GENERALIZED MONGE-AMP`ERE
EQUATION AND THE SUPERCRITICAL DEFORMED
HERMITIAN-YANG-MILLS EQUATION: BOUNDARY CASES
REI MURAKAMI
Abstract. We prove the existence and uniqueness of weak solutions for the
generalized Monge-Amp`ere equation and the supercritical deformed Hermitian-
Yang-Mills equation in cohomology classes lying on the boundary of the solv-
able region. Moreover, we prove that the associated geometric flows converge
to the weak solutions in the sense of currents. The proof combines viscosity-
theoretic and pluripotential-theoretic techniques.
1. Introduction
Partial differential equations play a central role in K¨ahler geometry. A basic
example is the complex Monge–Amp`ere (MA) equation, which governs the Ricci
curvature and underlies the theory of K¨ahler–Einstein metrics. Other important
examples include the J-equation, introduced in [Don99, Chen00] in connection with
constant scalar curvature K¨ahler metrics, and the deformed Hermitian–Yang–Mills
(dHYM) equation (also called the Leung–Yau–Zaslow equation), which arises in
mirror symmetry [LYZ01].
Recent progress shows that the solvability of a broad class of fully nonlinear ellip-
tic equations in K¨ahler geometry is characterized by the existence of a C-subsolution,
which is a positivity condition on differential forms [SW08, FLM11, DK17, Sun17,
Sz´e18, GS24, CJY20, Pin22, Lin23a, Lin23b, FYZ25]. Moreover, this condition ad-
mits a numerical characterization in terms of the positivity of intersection numbers
[Chen21, Song20, DP21, CLT24, FM24], similarly to the Demailly-P˘aun character-
ization of K¨ahlerness [DP04].
Given this characterization, it is natural to ask what happens when the positivity
conditions fail. In [DMS26], the authors conjectured the existence and uniqueness
of a canonical weak solution for the J-equation and the dHYM equation even in the
absence of positivity conditions [DMS26, Conjectures 1.5 and 1.12]. Furthermore,
in dimension two, they confirmed this conjecture using the weak solution theory of
the MA equation [BEGZ10].
In this paper, we confirm the conjecture in the boundary case (see Assumptions
1.4 and 1.13) in a slightly weaker form (see Remrak 1.9 (iv)), by extending the
pluripotential framework of [BEGZ10]. The corresponding existence and uniqueness
result for the J-equation (with ω and χ K¨ahler forms, unlike in Setup 1.1) was
announced in [DMS26]. To the best of the author’s knowledge, a detailed proof has
not yet appeared in the literature.
Motivated by [DP21, FM24], we consider the generalized Monge-Amp`ere (gMA)
equation (1.8), an equation that generalizes both the MA equation and the J-
equation. Our setting is as follows.

Although we expect our strategy to extend to more general complex Hessian
equations, there are several difficulties. For example, the general inverse σk equa-
tion was studied in [Lin23a, Lin23b]. It takes the form (1.8) with possibly negative
coefficients ci, and in particular unifies the gMA equation and the supercritical
dHYM equation. In [Lin23a], the author proved that these equations have con-
vex sublevel sets under the strict Υ-stability condition. However, it is not known
whether a property analogous to (3.13) holds. Such a property would imply the
existence of weak solutions via the convolution argument. The uniqueness problem
appears substantially more difficult, since our uniqueness argument relies on the
convexity of the operator.

plurisubharmonicity might be a difficulty as well.
