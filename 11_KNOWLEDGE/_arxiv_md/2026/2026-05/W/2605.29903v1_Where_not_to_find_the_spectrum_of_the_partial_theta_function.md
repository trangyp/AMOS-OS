Abstract. The spectrum of Ramanujan’s partial theta function θ(q, x) :=
P∞
j=0 qj(j+1)/2xj, q ∈D1 (the unit disk centered at the origin), x ∈C, is the

moduli of the zeros of θ are separated by the negative half-integer powers of |q|.

called spectral numbers or spectral values. Recently, the role played by θ in the

was revealed in [25], [9] and [22], based on the classical works of Hardy, Petrovitch
and Hutchinson (see [7], [26] and [8]). The relationship with the spectrum of θ was
mentioned in [22]. This stimulated the study of the analytic properties of θ, see
[14]–[21].
