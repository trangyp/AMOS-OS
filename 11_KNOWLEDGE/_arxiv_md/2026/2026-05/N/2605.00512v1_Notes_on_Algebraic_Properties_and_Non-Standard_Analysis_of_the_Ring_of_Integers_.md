Abstract
We summarise known algebraic and model theoretic results on the ring A of
integers modulo infinitely large primes for number theorists, and share topics in
transcendental number theory with algebraists and model theorists. In particular, we
extend transcendence criteria by Anzawa–Funakura and Matsusaka–Seki in order to
show application of non-standard analysis to the study of transcendence.

In number theory, the ring A plays a role of the base ring in the study of finite multiple
zeta values, which are finite analogues of multiple zeta values introduced by D. Zagier.
Starting from [KZ], there have been various studies on specific elements by number the-
orists, but not so many about the algebraic structure of A other than the well-known fact
that A is a Q-algebra are mentioned in those works.
On the other hand, groups and rings over the form Q /
L
and the residue fields of such
rings at their maximal ideals have ever been deeply studied in algebra and model theory
from various other motivations, e.g. studies of slender groups, Fuchs-44-groups, ultra-
powers of Banach spaces and Banach algebras, P-points in βN \ N, and so on. Although
algebraists and model theorists have ever revealed several properties of A , it seems that
they do not connect those results to number theoretic objects.
The aim of this survey article is to bridge those distinct branches of mathematics in order
to accelerate the study of A . In particular, we summarise known algebraic and model
theoretic results on the ring A for number theorists, and topics in transcendental number
theory with algebraists and model theorists. In order to show application of non-standard
analysis to the study of transcendence, we extend transcendence criteria [MS26] Lemma
2.1 and Lemma 2.3. Although the extensions are not so significant compared to the
novelty of [MS26] Lemma 2.1 and Lemma 2.3, we expect that they help the reader to
recognise how to extend other arguments in similar ways.
We briefly explain contents of this paper. In §1, we introduce convention for this paper.
Especially, we recall an ultrafilter and an ultraproduct for the reader who is not familiar
with non-standard analysis. In §2, we summarise known algebraic and model theoretic
results on the ring A . In §3, we study C-rational points of A , and introduce several
notions on transcendence of elements of A . In §4, we give extensions of transcendence
criteria [MS26] Lemma 2.1 and Lemma 2.3.
