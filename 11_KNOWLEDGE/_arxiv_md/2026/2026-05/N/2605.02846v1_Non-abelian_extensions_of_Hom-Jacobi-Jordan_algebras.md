NON-ABELIAN EXTENSIONS OF HOM-JACOBI-JORDAN ALGEBRAS
NEJIB SAADAOUI
:
Abstract. This paper develops a cohomology theory for Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebras using
and applies it to classify non-abelian extensions. The main result establishes that equivalence
classes of split extensions of a Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra J by V are in bijection with the
second cohomology group H2(J, V ), generalizing classical results from Lie and Leibniz algebra
theory. We characterize extensions explicitly through 2-cocycles (ρ, θ) satisfying compatibility
conditions, and provide complete classifications of low-dimensional cases.
Keywords. Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra, representation, extension, classification, solvable.

1. Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebras
In this section, we recall some basic facts about Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebras and extend
the notions and results about solvability and nilpotency of Hom-Lie algebras to the case of
Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebras.
Definition 1.1. [17] An algebra J over a field K is a Jordan algebra if it satisfies the following
conditions for any x, y ∈J:
x ◦y = y ◦x,
(1.1)
x2 ◦(y ◦x) = (x2 ◦y) ◦x.
(1.2)
Definition 1.2. [12] A Hom-Jordan algebra is a triple (V, ◦, α) consisting of a vector space V , a
commutative multiplication ◦: V × V →V , and a linear map α: V →V satisfying the following
identity for any x, y ∈V :
α2(x) ◦(y ◦(x ◦x)) = (α(x) ◦y) ◦α(x ◦x).
Definition 1.3. [7] A Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra is a triple (J, [·, ·], α) consisting of a vector
space J, a symmetric bilinear map (bracket) [·, ·]: J × J →J, and a linear map α: J →J
satisfying
α([x, y]) = [α(x), α(y)],
(1.3)
[α(x), [y, z]] + [α(y), [z, x]] + [α(z), [x, y]] = 0,
(1.4)
for all x, y, z ∈J. We recover Jacobi-Jordan algebras when the linear map α is the identity map.
Definition 1.4. A Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra (J, [·, ·], α) is called:
(1) a zero Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra if the bilinear map [·, ·] vanishes for all elements in
J;
(2) a regular Hom-Jacobi-Jordan algebra if α is an algebra automorphism. Otherwise, we
call J nonregular.
