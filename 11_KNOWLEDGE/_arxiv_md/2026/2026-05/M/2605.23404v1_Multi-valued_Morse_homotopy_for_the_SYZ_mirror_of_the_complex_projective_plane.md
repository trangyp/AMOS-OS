Morse homotopy was first introduced by Fukaya [Fuk93] as a Morse theoretical model of
the Fukaya category. In [FO97], Fukaya-Oh proved that the category of Morse homotopy

on a closed manifold B consisting of functions is A∞-equivalent to the Fukaya category
of the cotangent bundle T ∗B consisting of Lagrangian sections.
Kontsevich proposed a categorical formulation of mirror symmetry in [Kon95], which is
called homological mirror symmetry (HMS) conjecture. On the other hand, Strominger-
Yau-Zaslow proposed a geometric formulation of mirror symmetry in [SYZ96], which
is called SYZ conjecture. Kontsevich-Soibelman proposed a framework to prove HMS
conjecture based on the SYZ picture and Fukaya-Oh’s results for Morse homotopy. In this
line, Futaki-Kajiura introduced the category Mo(P) of Morse homotopy on a manifold P
with boundary. The category Mo(P) is one of the generalizations of the weighted Fukaya-
Oh category proposed by Kontsevich-Soibelman in [KS01]. Futaki-Kajiura and the first
author proved homological mirror symmetry for some toric manifolds by using Mo(P)
in [FK21, FK22, Nak24a, Nak24b]. Nishida generalized this framework to toric orbifolds
and proved homological mirror symmetry for weighted projective spaces in [Nis25].
In terms of the SYZ conjecture and HMS conjecture, the mirrors of holomorphic vector
bundles are considered to be Lagrangian multi-sections. In a recent work by Oh and the
second author [OS24], they constructed Lagrangian multi-sections that are mirror to toric
vector bundles over a toric surface. In particular, the second author discussed a relation-
ship between toric vector bundles, Lagrangian multi-sections, and spectral networks in
[Sue24].
In this paper, we propose a definition of the category Momult(P) of multi-valued Morse
homotopy consisting of multi-valued functions associated to Lagrangian multi-sections,
which is a generalization of Mo(P) proposed by Futaki-Kajiura in [FK21].
In order
to treat multi-valued functions, we introduce a notion of a jagged gradient tree that is
gradient tree modified by interactions from spectral networks subordinate to Lagrangian
multi-sections proposed by the second author in [Sue24]. Such modifications to gradient
trajectories is inspired by the notion of a tropical Morse tree proposed by Gross-Siebert
in [GS12]. We then compare Momult(P) with a dg category DGvect(CP 2) consisting of
holomorphic toric vector bundles over CP 2.
1.1. Main results. Let P be the moment polytope of the complex projective space
CP 2. We construct the category Momult(P) of multi-valued Morse homotopy on P as one
of the generalizations of the category Mo(P) of weighted Morse homotopy proposed in

associated to Lagrangian multi-sections satisfied some asymptotic condition. The space of
morphisms is the Z-graded vector space spanned by the critical points of the multi-valued
functions associated to the Lagrangian multi-sections. An A∞-structure is defined as the
modified A∞-structure of Mo(P) by interactions of spectral networks subordinated to the

(tropical) Lagrangian multi-sections and spectral networks.
Definition 2.1. Let Σ be a complete fan in NR. An r-fold tropical Lagrangian multi-
section over Σ is a data Ltrop := (Ltrop, ΣLtrop, µ, p, φtrop), where
(1) (Ltrop, ΣLtrop) is a connected cone complex weighted by µ : Ltrop →Z>0,
(2) p : (Ltrop, ΣLtrop) →(NR, Σ) is a branched covering map of a cone complex such
that P

The branched covering map p : (Ltrop, ΣLtrop) →(NR, Σ) maps each cone σ in ΣLtrop
homeomorphically on to a cone p(σ) in Σ. We can view φtrop as a multi-valued piecewise
linear function on NR. In particular, a support function associated to the holomorphic
line bundle over the toric variety XΣ is a tropical Lagrangian section (see e.g. [CLS11]).
