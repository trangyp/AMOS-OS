The Lonely Runner Conjecture states that if k +1 runners start at the
same point on a unit-length circular track and run with distinct constant

The Lonely Runner Conjecture is a classical problem in Diophantine approxima-

The Lonely Runner Conjecture has several equivalent formulations and has

announced the eleven-, twelve-, and thirteen-runner cases [12].
