Measurement of Born Cross Sections for e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−at √s = 3.51 −4.95 GeV
and Observation of ψ(3770) →K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−
BESIII Collaboration∗
Using 44.55 fb−1 of e+e−collision data collected by the BESIII detector at the BEPCII collider,
we report the first measurement of the Born cross sections for the e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction
at fifty-six center-of-mass energies between 3.51 and 4.95 GeV. A fit to the dressed cross sections
reveals the first observation of the ψ(3770) →K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−process, with a statistical significance of
6.0σ including systematic uncertainties. This result represents the first observation of charmless
three-body baryonic decay of a vector charmonium state above the open-charm threshold.
No
significant signals for other charmonium(-like) states i.e., ψ(4040), ψ(4160), Y (4230), Y (4360),
ψ(4415), Y (4500), Y (4660) or Y (4710) are observed, and the upper limits for the product of the
branching fraction and the electronic partial width at the 90% confidence level for each assumed
charmonium(-like) state are provided. Additionally, the ratios of Born cross sections between this
work and the previous measurements of e+e−→K0
S ¯Ξ+Σ−and K−¯Ξ+Σ0 are provided, which can
be used to validate theoretical predictions related to isospin symmetry.
The study of charmonium(-like) states produced in
e+e−annihilation and decaying into baryonic final states
is a key element to test quantum chromodynamics-
related theory. The potential model [1] predicts six vec-
tor charmonium states in the energy region from 3.7 to
4.7 GeV, identified as the 1D, 3S, 2D, 4S, 3D, and
5S states [2].
In the past decades, an abundance of
charmonium(-like) vector states has been observed with
masses above the open-charm threshold at e+e−colliders.
Three conventional charmonium states, i.e., ψ(4040),
ψ(4160), and ψ(4415) [3], have been observed in open-
charm final states. Five non-conventional charmonium-
like states, i.e., Y (4230), Y (4360), Y (4500), Y (4660),
and Y (4710), have been observed in hidden-charm fi-
nal states via initial state radiation (ISR) processes at
BaBar [4–7] and Belle [8–12] experiments, or by direct
production processes at CLEO-c [13] and BESIII [14–
18]. These non-conventional states cannot be classified
as resonances consisting solely of a pure c¯c quark pair.
The overpopulation of structures and the discrepancies
between potential model predictions and experimental
measurements suggest that some of these structures may
be candidates for exotic states [19, 20]. To explain their
nature, many hypotheses including hybrid states [21],
multi-quark states [22], and molecular states [23] have
been proposed.
However, no definitive conclusion has
been drawn. As suggested in Refs. [24, 25], the obser-
vation of baryonic decay modes may favor an interpre-
tation of some charmonium-like states as predominantly
conventional charmonium. Still, the coexistence of such
decays with non-conventional features reflects our lim-
ited understanding of the strong interaction in the non-
perturbative regime, calling for further experimental in-
put.
The measurement of the exclusive Born cross sec-
tions for the e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−process at center-of-
mass (c.m.) energies above the open-charm threshold of-
fers the opportunity to search for the baryonic charm-
less decays of the vector charmonium(-like) states. Al-
though experimental studies in this energy region have
been performed by the BESIII experiments [26–35], only
some evidence for ψ(3770) →Λ¯Λ, ψ(3770) →Ξ−¯Ξ+ and
ψ(4160) →K−¯Ξ+Λ [29, 31, 34] processes have been re-
ported. No significant signals of baryonic decays for other
vector charmonium(-like) states have been found.
In this Letter, we present a measurement of the Born
cross sections for the reaction e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−, in the
range of c.m. energy (√s) from 3.51 to 4.95 GeV [36, 37].
Unless otherwise noted, the charge-conjugate process
e+e−→K−¯Ξ0Σ+ is included by default.
This mea-
surement is based on e+e−collision data correspond-
ing to a total integrated luminosity of 44.55 fb−1 [38–
42] collected by BESIII detector [43] at the BEPCII col-
lider [44]. Potential resonances, i.e., ψ(3770), ψ(4040),
ψ(4160), Y (4230), Y (4360), ψ(4415), Y (4500), Y (4660)
and Y (4710) are studied by fitting the dressed cross sec-
tions of the e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction. The ψ(3770) →
K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−process is observed for the first time with a
significance of 6.0σ including systematic uncertainties.
In addition, the ratios of Born cross sections of the
e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction to those of the two isospin
partner processes e+e−→K0
S ¯Ξ+Σ−and K−¯Ξ+Σ0 are
provided.
The BESIII geometric description and the detector re-
sponse are modeled with a geant4-based software pack-
age [45].
The subsequent decays for Ξ0 →Λπ0 and
π0 →γγ are processed via the evtgen generator [46, 47]
according to the branching fractions provided by the Par-
ticle Data Group (PDG) [48]. The detection efficiency
is determined by Monte Carlo (MC) simulations with a
sample of 105 events at each c.m. energy point, with a
phase-space model by the kkmc generator [49] includ-
ing effects of the beam energy spread and ISR effect.
To estimate the background, an inclusive MC sample is
generated at a c.m. energy of 3.773 GeV. The inclusive
MC sample includes the production of D ¯D pairs (includ-

Candidates for the process e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−are se-
lected via a partial-reconstruction technique. For each
event, only the information of K+ and Ξ0 is measured,
where Ξ0 is reconstructed via the Λπ0 mode with the
subsequent decays Λ →pπ−and π0 →γγ, while the
information of ¯Σ−is inferred from the recoiling system
against the reconstructed K+Ξ0 system.
Charged tracks are reconstructed in the multi-layer
drift chamber (MDC) within the angular region | cos θ| <
0.93, where θ is the polar angle with respect to the z-axis
in the laboratory system, which is the MDC symmetry
axis.
At least two positive and one negative charged
tracks are required to be reconstructed in the MDC.
Particle identification (PID) for charged tracks combines
measurements of the specific ionization energy loss in the
MDC (dE/dx) and the flight time in the time-of-flight
(TOF) detector to form likelihoods L(h) (h = p, K, π)
for each hadron h hypothesis. Tracks are identified as
protons when the proton hypothesis has the greatest like-
lihood (L(p) > L(K) and L(p) > L(π)), while charged
pions are identified by requiring that L(π) > L(p) and
L(π) > L(K), and the charged kaons are identified by
requiring that L(K) > L(π) and L(K) > L(p). Events
with at least one proton, one positive kaon, and one neg-
ative pion are kept for further analysis.
Photons are reconstructed from isolated showers in
the electromagnetic calorimeter (EMC). The energy de-
posited in the nearby TOF counter is included to im-
prove the reconstruction efficiency and energy resolu-
tion. The energies of photons are required to be greater
than 25 MeV in the EMC barrel region (| cos θ| < 0.8),
and greater than 50 MeV in the EMC end cap (0.86 <
| cos θ| < 0.92). Furthermore, to suppress electronic noise
and energy deposits unrelated to the collision events, the
difference between the EMC time and the event start
time is required to be within 0 < t < 700 ns. To elimi-
nate showers from charged tracks, the opening angle be-
tween the position of each shower in the EMC and any
charged track must be greater than 10 degrees. Events
with at least two photons are kept for further analysis.

The systematic uncertainties of the Born cross section
measurement include contributions from the following
sources: integrated luminosity, kaon tracking and PID,
Ξ0 reconstruction, fitting method, branching fractions
of intermediate states, and the assumed line shape of
the resonance fitting. The luminosity at each c.m. en-
ergy point has been measured via Bhabha scattering
events, with a systematic uncertainty of 1.0% [38] below
4.0 GeV, 0.7% [39] from 4.0 to 4.6 GeV, and 0.6% [40]
above 4.6 GeV. By using a control sample of J/ψ →
K∗(892)0K0
S with the method from Ref.[54], both the
systematic uncertainties of kaon tracking and PID are
estimated to be 1.0%.
The uncertainty due to the Ξ0
reconstruction is evaluated to be 4.5% by incorporating
the uncertainties of tracking and PID of the proton and
pion, the Λ/Ξ0 reconstruction, the decay length require-
ment of Λ, and the requirements of the Λ and Ξ0 mass
windows using a control sample of ψ(3686) →Ξ0¯Ξ0 [55].

decays are negligible.
The uncertainty of the input line shape includes two
parts: the parameterization and the model of the line
shape, which are evaluated using the method described
in Ref. [35].
The first component arises from the sta-
tistical uncertainty of the input line shape of the cross
sections.
The uncertainty is estimated by varying the
central values of the parameters according to their er-
ror matrix. Then, a Gaussian function is used to fit the
ϵ·(1+δ) distribution. The second source stems from the
influence of different resonances. For the ψ(3770), since it
is included in the nominal line shape, this uncertainty is
estimated by varying the mass and width of the ψ(3770)
by 1σ according to the PDG values [48]. For other reso-
nances, the uncertainty is quantified by incorporating the
additional states in the ISR calculation. The total sys-
tematic uncertainty is calculated to be 1.0% by adding
the three contributions in quadrature.

In summary, using e+e−collision data collected by
the BESIII detector at the BEPCII collider correspond-
ing to an integrated luminosity of 44.55 fb−1, the Born
cross sections for the e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction are
measured at fifty-six c.m. energies ranging from 3.51
to 4.95 GeV. A fit to the dressed cross sections of the
e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction is performed under the as-
sumption of a charmonium(-like) resonance i.e. ψ(3770),
ψ(4040), ψ(4160), Y (4230), Y (4360), ψ(4415), Y (4500),
Y (4660) or Y (4710) and a continuum contribution. The
decay of ψ(3770) →K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−is observed for the first
time with a significance of 6.0σ, including the system-
atic uncertainty.
No significant signal for any other
charmonium(-like) resonances is found. The ΓeeB values
and the upper limits at the 90% confidence level for these
charmonium(-like) states decaying into the K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−fi-
nal state are provided. In addition, the ratios of Born
cross sections of the e+e−→K+Ξ0 ¯Σ−reaction to those
of the isospin partner processes e+e−→K0
S ¯Ξ+Σ−and
e+e−→K−¯Ξ+Σ0 are presented in Fig. 3.
The mea-
sured values are in a good agreement with the scenario
of isospin conservation. The obtained results are crucial
for studying the vector charmonium(-like) states decay-
ing into charmless baryonic final states and for further
investigation of the nature of charmonium(-like) states
above the open-charm threshold.
