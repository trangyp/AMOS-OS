MUBS FROM BENT FUNCTIONS
WILLIAM M. KANTOR
Abstract. This note contains a simple construction of complete sets of MUBs,
using bent functions to write the new basis vectors as explicit linear combina-
tions of the standard basis.
1. Introduction
This note contains a short, elementary construction of complete sets of mutually
unbiased bases (MUBs) in CN, where N = pn for a prime p.
This sequel to
[Ka] uses an idea presented there without the connection to bent functions being
noticed. Preferring brevity we refer to [Wo, Ka] for background and some references
concerning complete sets of MUBs.
Our point of view is different from the groups/geometries approaches to MUBs
[WoF, Wo, CCKS, Ka]. However, this has not led to the construction of any new
complete sets of MUBs.

4. Remarks
Examples 2.2 and 3.4 are the most familiar complete sets of MUBs [WoF]. Others
are in [Ka, Ex. 2.5, 2.7, 3.5, 3.7].
For p > 2 all known mubent sets of functions V →Zp consist of quadratic
functions, except for sets obtained using [CM] (see [Ka, Ex. 3.7]).
All known mubent sets of functions V →Z4 consist of quadratic functions.
It is not clear how to use Theorems 2.1 or 3.1 to deal with the unitary equivalence
of complete sets of MUBs in CN. When ℶconsists of quadratic functions the groups
and geometries approach in [CCKS, Ka] uses Heisenberg groups to turn unitary
equivalence into a question about isomorphisms of associated finite affine planes.
There are many known pairwise inequivalent complete sets of MUBs for any p and
suitable N. When p > 2 the number of known complete sets is O(N), whereas
when p = 2 that number is not bounded above by any polynomial in N.
