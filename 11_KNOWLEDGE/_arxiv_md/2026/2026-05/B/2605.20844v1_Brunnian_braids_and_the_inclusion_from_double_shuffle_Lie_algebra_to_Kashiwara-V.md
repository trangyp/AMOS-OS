Abstract. It is proved by L. Schneps that the double shuffle Lie algebra dmr0 injects to the
Kashiwara-Vergne Lie algebra krv2 in [24, 25]. We show that dmr0 with the infinitesimal hexagon

We generalize the inclusion in two directions, one using lower central series of brunnian Lie
algebras and the other is to establish explicit links between the pentagon equation map, the stuffle
coproduct, the divergence map and the necklace cobracket.

1. Introduction and main results
For a Riemann surface M, Brunn(M) is the n strand brunnian braids group over M, it consists
of the braids in the pure braid group PBn(M) that becomes trivial braid by removing any of its
strand. It was called the indecompoable braids by Levinson [18]. The Brunian groups of disk D
and sphere S2 fit into a exact sequence relating the higher homotopy groups of sphere [7] and some
Brunnian groups has surprising pseudo-Anosov properties [26]. In this note, we use the Brunnian
braids to study the inclusion from the double shuffle Lie algebra to Kashiwara-Vergne Lie algebra.
The pure braid group PBn(D) over the disk D gives rise to the category of parenthesized
braid PaB in [5], similarly there is the notion of parenthesized Brunnian braid.
