BEYOND ENDOSCOPY FOR GL2 OVER Q WITH RAMIFICATION 4: CONTRIBUTION OF
NON-ELLIPTIC PARTS
YUHAO CHENG
Abstract. We continue our work on GL2 over Q in the ramified setting for Beyond Endoscopy. We establish
asymptotic formulas for each term of the trace formula when summing over n < X, using arbitrary smooth
test functions at the places in S = {∞, q1, . . . , qr} where 2 ∈S, for the standard representation, up to an error of
o(X). This yields an identity depending on a parameter X, leading to certain identities that can be regarded as
a limit form of the trace formula for GL2 over Q. On the spectral side, we employ the contour shift method and
the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma. On the geometric side, both the identity part and the unipotent part contribute
o(X). The elliptic part was reduced to the hyperbolic part in the previous paper. Finally, using hyperbolic
Poisson summation, we relate the hyperbolic part back to the spectral side and determine its contribution.

1. Introduction
1.1. General Philosophy. The Beyond Endoscopy program was proposed by Langlands near 2000 [Lan04].
Roughly speaking, it is a two-step process. The first one is to determine whether a representation π of
a quasi-split algebraic group G comes from a representation σ of a "smaller" group H via the L-group
morphism ϕ: LH →LG. Moreover, we want to find the smallest H (which we call primitive). The second
step is to compare these data for different groups.
