A Drinfeld modular form is a rigid analytic function f : ΩÑ C8 satisfying
a transformation condition and regularity at cusps similar to those for elliptic
modular forms.
Though the definitions of elliptic and Drinfeld modular
forms are parallel, arithmetic of Drinfeld modular forms is sometimes very
different from the elliptic case.
It has been realized that Drinfeld modular forms of level Γ1pmq behave
rather strangely. For example, for a positive integer M, the space of elliptic
modular forms of level Γ1pMq is written as the direct sum of the spaces of
level Γ0pMq with nebentypus. On the other hand, such a decomposition is
not possible in general for the space of Drinfeld modular forms of level Γ1pmq,
since representations of pA{mAqˆ over C8 are not necessarily semisimple.
