NIKOLAI L. POLIAKOV
Ultrapowers and unions of chains are classic tools of model theory (see [1]). Combining these
two constructions leads to new concepts, the most well-known of which is the operation of the
ultralimit (or limiting ultrapower) of ordinal rank α. In the sense of [2], an ultralimit is the union
of an increasing chain of successive ultrapowers and their unions with respect to a system of
natural (diagonal, at non-limit steps) embeddings. In this paper, we modify this notion by using a
different (no less natural) system of embeddings. The resulting construction, the skewed ultralimit,
has useful applications in the theory of ultrafilters. Using it, [3] obtained a generalization of
Blass’s theorem on the model-theoretic characterization of the Rudin–Keisler order (see [4]). In
the present paper, we use skew ultralimits to give a precise description of the Rudin–Keisler
(pre)order on the equivalence class of a Ramsey ultrafilter with respect to the Comfort preorder.

Classical objects of theory of ultrafiltars are various (pre)orders on βX.
For all u, v ∈βX, u is Rudin–Keisler less than v, denoted by u ≤RK v, iff there exists
f : ω →ω such that ef(v) = u. Equivalence classes τ(u), u ∈βX, with respect to the relation
≈RK := ≤RK ∩≤−1
RK are called ultrafilter types. Therefore, ≤RK generates the Rudin–Keisler order
on the set of types of ultrafilters. A non-principal ultrafilter u ∈βX is called (RK-)minimal if
v ≤RK u implies v ≈RK u for any non-principal ultrafilter v ∈βX. Minimal ultrafilters have
numerous alternative characterizations, see [6] and [7]. In particular, they are known as Ramsey
ultrafilters.
The Comfort pre-order is defined as follows (for simplicity, we restrict ourselves to the case of
ultrafilters on ω). For u, v ∈βω
(i) a space Y is u-compact iff ef(u) ∈Y for any f : ω →Y ;
(ii) u is Comfort less than v, denoted by u ≤C v, iff any v-compact space is u-compact.
