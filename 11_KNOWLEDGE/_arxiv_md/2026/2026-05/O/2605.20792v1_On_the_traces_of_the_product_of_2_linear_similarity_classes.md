ON THE TRACES OF THE PRODUCT OF TWO LINEAR
SIMILARITY CLASSES
KLAUS NIELSEN
Abstract. It is shown that the product of two nonscalar conjugacy classes of
the special linear group SL(n, K) contains matrices of arbitrary trace if n ≥4
and K is an abitrary field or n = 3 and K is finite.
1. Introduction
Let Ω, Ψ be nonscalar similarity classes of GL(n, K). In [1], Adan-Bante and
Harris have shown that the set tr ΩΨ = {tr ωψ; ω ∈Ω, ψ ∈Ψ} of traces of ΩΨ
satisfies | tr ΩΨ| ≥q −1 if K = GF(q) is a finite field. They also proved that
| tr ΩΨ| ≥q
2 if Ωand Ψ are conjugacy classes of SL(n, q).
It follows that the product of 2 nontrivial conjugacy classes of PSL(n, q) contains
at least q conjugacy classes. This verifies a conjecture of Arad and Herzog in a
special case. Arad and Herzog [2] conjectured that in a finite simple group G the
product of 2 nontrivial conjugacy classes of G is never a conjugacy class.
Let M(n, K) denote the set of n × n matrices over the field K. In this note, we
want to improve the results of Adan-Bante and Harris and show
Theorem 1.1. Let n ≥3. Let Ω, Ψ be similarity classes of M(n, K). Assume that
Ωand Ψ are nonscalar. Then tr(ΩΨ) = K.
Theorem 1.2. Let n ≥3. If K is infinite let n ≥4. Let Ω, Ψ be conjugacy classes
of SL(n, K). Assume that Ωand Ψ are nonscalar. Then tr ΩΨ = K.
