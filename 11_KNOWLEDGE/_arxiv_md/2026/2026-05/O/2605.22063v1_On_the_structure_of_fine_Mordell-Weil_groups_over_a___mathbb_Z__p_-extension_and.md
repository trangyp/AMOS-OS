Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the structure of the fine Mordell-Weil groups over the intermediate subextensions

Introduction
In this paper, we always let p denote an odd prime. Let F be a number field and A an abelian variety over
F. The (p-primary) fine Selmer group of A is a much studied object in Iwasawa theory (for instances, see
[1, 8, 9, 17]) and plays a central role in both the statement and proof of the Iwasawa main conjecture (see
[4, 5, 13]). Analogous to the usual Selmer group, the fine Selmer group R(A/F) fits into the following
short exact sequence
0 −→M(A/F) −→R(A/F) −→Ж(A/Fn) −→0,
where M(A/F) and Ж(A/F) denote the fine Mordell-Weil group and fine Tate-Shafarevich group, re-
spectively, as defined by Wuthrich [18]. These groups can be thought as the “fine” counterpart of the
usual Mordell-Weil group and Tate-Shafarevich group. In recent years, these fine objects have garnered
significant research attention, as evidenced in the works [3, 7, 10, 11, 14].
The goal of the paper is study the structure of the fine Mordell-Weil groups over the intermediate
subextensions of a given Zp-extension F∞of F. For each n, we let Fn be the unique subextension of F∞/F
such that |Fn : F| = pn. The fine Mordell-Weil group M(A/Fn) has a natural Zp[Gal(Fn/F)]-module
structure. Denote by Γ the Galois group Gal(F∞/F) and write Λ for ZpJΓK. Via the canonical surjection
Λ ↠Zp[Gal(Fn/F)], we may view M(A/Fn) as a Λ-module structure. Recall that a homomorphism
M −→N of finitely generated torsion Λ-modules is said to be a pseudo-isomorphism if its kernel and
cokernel are finite. We denote this relation by M ∼N. Finally, let Φj denote the pj-th cyclotomic
polynomial. We are now in a position to state our main result.
