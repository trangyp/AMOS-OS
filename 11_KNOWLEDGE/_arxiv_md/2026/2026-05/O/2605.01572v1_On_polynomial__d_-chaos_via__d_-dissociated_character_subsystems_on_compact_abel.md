The proofs of Theorems 1 and 2 are given in Section 3. The main tool in the
proof is the application of analogs of Riesz products, along with Theorems C
and D. We note that the method of proof, which relies on comparing arbitrary
subsystems of character systems with “model” systems such as the classical
Rademacher system and the Rademacher d-chaos, is well known (see, e.g., [2,
Chap. 7], [20, Chap. VII]). This technique enables one to establish results on the
behavior of polynomials with respect to special subsystems in function spaces,
provided that the corresponding estimates are already known for the “model”
case. In the present paper, we use p-adic analogs of the Rademacher chaos as
the “model” systems.
In Section 1, we define d-dissociated systems and provide examples. Section
2 is devoted to the construction of Riesz product-type measures. In Section
4, as a consequence of our results and known theorems on the discretization
of integral norms, we establish conditions on the number of points required to
discretize the Lq-norm in our case.
