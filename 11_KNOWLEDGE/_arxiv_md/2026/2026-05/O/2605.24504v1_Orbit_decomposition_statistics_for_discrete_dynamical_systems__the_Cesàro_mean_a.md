ORBIT DECOMPOSITION STATISTICS FOR DISCRETE DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS:
THE CESÀRO MEAN AND A LARGE DEVIATION PRINCIPLE
GUNTHER CORNELISSEN AND SUN WOO PARK
ABSTRACT. If a self-map σ: X →X has a dynamical zeta function with nonzero radius of convergence
1/Λ and the Cesàro mean B of #Fix(σk)/Λk exists and is positive, we show a large deviation principle for
the number of prime orbits occurring in the decomposition of a general orbit of length ⩽X (an element of the
free abelian monoid generated by the prime orbits or, equivalently, a prime orbit of a finite multiset in X ) with
speed B log X and universal rate function equal to that of the Poisson distribution with unit mean. We also
show a large deviation principle for more general strongly additive functions. The proof uses asymptotic results
on the total number of general orbits, as well as a weak analogue of Mertens’s second theorem, that may be of
independent interest. The theory applies, for example, to endomorphisms of algebraic groups over finite fields,
additive cellular automata, and automorphisms of some solenoids.
1. Introduction
We consider a discrete dynamical system (X , σ) given by a set X and iteration of a self-map σ: X →
X . Just like the positive integers under multiplication are the free multiplicative abelian monoid gener-
ated by the primes, prime (i.e., finite closed) orbits of a point under iteration of σ span a free additive
monoid, whose elements, that we call general orbits, correspond bijectively to orbits of finite multisets in
X ; the cardinality of a prime orbit, its length, extends additively to general orbits. Thus, it makes sense
to study analogues for such dynamical systems of statistical properties of prime decomposition, such as the
expected number of prime factors of an integer (Hardy–Ramanujan [14], 1917), convergence to the normal
distribution (Erd˝os–Kac [10], 1940), speed of convergence (Rényi–Turán [23], 1958) and Large Deviation
Principles (Mehrdad–Zhu [21], 2016).
Some such generalisations exist in the setting of normed abelian monoids with axiomatic assumptions
on the corresponding prime counting function akin the prime number theorem (a research area known as
‘abstract analytic number theory’ [17] or the theory of ‘Beurling primes’ [3]), see, e.g., [20] for Erd˝os–Kac
and [15] for Large Deviations. This theory is not applicable to most of the discrete dynamical systems we
will consider.
Results like the Central Limit Theorem (of which the Erd˝os–Kac theorem is a number-theoretical ana-
logue) and Large Deviation Principles (LPD’s for short, see, e.g. [9]) take place on different scales, and there
is no reason to expect that their generalisations to other contexts should depend on the same assumptions or
should be treated in historically chronological order. In this paper, we focus solely on deducing LPD’s for

(i) the associated Cesàro mean B is algebraic (and then, belongs to Qab) if the zeta function of the
