ON A MULTIPHASE VECTORIAL BERNOULLI FREE BOUNDARY
PROBLEM
GIOVANNI SICLARI AND BOZHIDAR VELICHKOV
Abstract. We study the regularity of minimizers of a multiphase vectorial Bernoulli free
boundary problem. This problem consists in a minimization problem for the Bernoulli func-
tional over families of Sobolev functions with disjoint supports and non trivial grouping. We
prove that minimizers exist, are locally Lipschitz continuous, and that their free boundaries do
not contain points where three or more phases meet. Our main regularity result establishes that
the free boundary is locally a C1,η graph near two-phase and branching points for some η > 0.
Keywords. Vectorial free boundary, partition problem.
MSC classification. 35R35
1. Introduction
Let D be an open bounded set in Rd. In this paper, we are interested in the regularity of
minimizers of a multiphase vectorial Bernoulli free boundary problem in D. Roughly speaking,
the terms ”multiphase,” ”vectorial,” and ”Bernoulli” describe a partition problem for the Dirichlet
energy involving a measure term and non-trivial grouping, that is, we consider families of Sobolev
functions possessing mutually disjoint supports across different families. However, the supports
can have non empty intersection within a given family.

1.2. A short review of the literature. Problem 1.2 combines two well-studied classes of free
boundary problems: partition problems and vectorial Bernoulli free boundary problems. Indeed,
similar to a partition problem, we have n disjoint phases but our functional consist of the Dirichlet
energy with a measure term. In recent years, both classes of free boundary problems have been
subject of intense investigation by the mathematical community.
On the one hand, concerning partition problems for the Dirichlet energy without measure terms,
basic properties as Lipschitzianity can be establish with very different theories. We mention [22, 27]
for harmonic maps and [13, 15, 17] for limit of competition-diffusion models. Furthermore, there
are strong connections with (spectral) shape optimization problems, see [3, 10, 12, 14, 16] and the
theory of symmetric 2-valued harmonic functions as pointed out for example in [37]. Regularity of
the free boundary had been investigated in the literature by means of the Almgren monotonicity
formula as in [22, 42], an approach made possible by the absence of measure terms, which allow to
classify points in the free boundary according to their Almgren frequency. We refer for example
to [23, 36, 40]. Finally, see the detailed recent survey [37] for further information on the subject.
On the other hand, for the vectorial Bernoulli free boundary problem basic properties as Lip-
schitzianity and non-degeneracy of minimizers, interior density estimates and local finiteness of
the perimeter of the free boundary have been investigated in [5, 11, 28, 29, 30, 33, 34, 44, 45].
Finer results, as the regularity of the free boundary at one phase points, has been established with
different techniques in several papers see [11, 20, 28, 29, 31, 33, 34, 41]. Singular cones has also
been studied, see [9, 21, 24, 48] while the regularity at point of density one of the free boundary
is still subject of current investigation, see [34, 39] for a characterization of the blow-ups limits at
such points. Finally, we mention the comprehensive survey [43] for more details.
Furthermore, several shape optimization results are related to the problem we are dealing with.
We refer in particular to the minimization of functionals with eigenvalues instead of the Dirichlet
energy and a measure term. More precisely, let Ωbe open and λi(Ω) the number i Dirichlet
eigenvalues of Ωfor the Laplacian. Regularity of minimizers of the functional λ1(Ω) + Λ|Ω| was
investigated in [4], of λ2(Ω)+Λ|Ω| in [29, 35] and of Pn
i=1 λi(Ω)+Λ|Ω| in [33], see in particular [33,
Lemma 2.4]. We also mention that as corollary of the analysis of two-phase and branching points
for the two phase Bernoulli functional in [18], a free boundary regularity result was obtained for
the functional Pk

problem was introduced in [7] and can be seen, in particular, as a scalar version of our problem
for eigenvalues, that is, with trivial grouping, all the support are disjoint.
1.3. Our main results. Roughly speaking, in this paper, we show that any minimizer U of (1.2)
is locally Lipschitz continuous in D and that the free boundary ∂ΩU is the union of two C1 graphs
in a neighborhood of two-phase and branching points whose definition we now recall.
Definition 1.1. We say that x ∈D is a two-phase point if x ∈∂ΩUj ∩∂ΩUj′ for some j ̸= j′ and
there exists r > 0 such that the Br(x) ∩{|U| = 0} = ∅. We say that x ∈D is a branching point if
x ∈∂ΩUj ∩∂ΩUj′ for some j ̸= j′ and Br(x) ∩{|U| = 0} ̸= ∅for any r > 0.

one-phase singular cones approaching a branching point. Thus, we first show that the blow-ups
with variable centers at two-phase or branching points are flat, and we use the improvement of
flatness for minimizers of the vectorial Bernoulli free boundary problem (see [20]) to transfer this
information to the one-phase points near a branching point.

In Section 2 we establish basic properties of minimizers

and conclude the proof of Theorem 1.2.
