ON THE FACE MAP OF THE ADMISSIBLE SET WITH IWAHORI
LEVEL
QINGCHAO YU
Abstract. To each face F of the coweight polytope Pµ, we associate a subset Adm(µ)F
of the µ-admissible set Adm(µ), which we refer to as a face of Adm(µ). This gives rise to
a face decomposition of Adm(µ). As an application, we give a complete description of the
fibers of the face map |∆|f defined by Pappas-Rapoport [16] and prove that the face map
is surjective.
Introduction
Let (Φ, X∗, Φ∨, X∗, ∆0) be a reduced based root datum. Let W0 be the Weyl group. Let
˜W = X∗⋊W0 = {tλz | λ ∈X∗, z ∈W0} be the extended affine Weyl group. Let µ be
a (not necessarily minuscule) dominant coweight. The coweight polytope Pµ is defined as
the convex hull of W0(µ), the W0-orbit of µ. Let F(Pµ) be the poset of faces of Pµ, where
F ⩽F′ if F ⊆F′.
Define the µ-admissible set as
Adm(µ) = {w ∈˜W | w ⩽tµ′ for some µ′ ∈W0(µ)}.
The notion of the admissible set arose from the study of local models of Shimura varieties
and moduli of G-Shtukas. We refer to [17] and the references there for the early history
of the subject, and [15], [19], [7], and [1] for more recent developments. The local models
stratify into affine Schubert varieties parametrized by the admissible sets (see [15, Theorem
9.3]). The admissible sets also serve as the support of the Bernstein functions in the center
of the affine Hecke algebras (see [4, Corollary 5.7]).
The combinatorics of Adm(µ) has been studied intensively. Haines-He [6] prove that
Adm(µ) is equal to the intersection of combinatorial obtuse cones, which indicates that
Adm(µ) should be regarded as the alcove-theoretic analogue of the coweight polytope Pµ.
Mili´cevi´c-Viehmann [14], He-Yu [9], Schremmer [20] use the quantum Bruhat graph to study
Adm(µ) and give a nice description of Adm(µ).
Very recently, in [16], Pappas-Rapoport defined a morphism called the divisor map ∆on
the quotient stack of the local model, which induces a map
|∆| : Adm(µ) −→F(Pµ).

Pappas-Rapoport defined the face map |∆|f as follows. For any w ∈Adm(µ), define
Λ(w) = {µ′ ∈W0(µ) | w ⩽tµ′} and define |∆|f(w) as the smallest face of Pµ containing
Λ(w). Then it is clear that |∆|(w) ⊃|∆|f(w) for any w ∈Adm(µ). They conjectured that
the face map |∆|f is surjective and that the map |∆| coincides with the map |∆|f (cf. [16,
Conjecture 4.1.6, Remark 4.1.7]).
The goal of this paper is to give an explicit description of the fibers of the face map |∆|f
and prove that |∆|f is surjective.

certain Levi subgroup. We refer to §2 and §3 for the exact definition. Moreover, we define
the notions of the interior, the boundary, and the center of the face Adm(µ)F (see §3).
These notions play important roles in analyzing the face structure of Adm(µ) and the face
map.
