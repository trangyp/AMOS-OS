ON THE HARRIS–VIEHMANN CONJECTURE FOR HODGE–NEWTON
REDUCIBLE LOCAL SHIMURA DATA OF ABELIAN TYPE
SANDRA NAIR, XINYU ZHOU
Abstract. We address a new case of the Harris–Viehmann conjecture, which establishes a par-
abolic induction formula on the cohomology groups associated to non-basic local Shimura data.
It follows that all supercuspidal representations on a Shimura variety are concentrated along the
basic locus, making the conjecture relevant to the Langlands program. Historically, many cases of
the Harris–Viehmann conjecture have been approached with the additional condition of Hodge–
Newton reducibility on the underlying local Shimura datum. Building on previous work by Man-
tovan (EL/PEL case) and Hong (Hodge case), we extend the proof of the conjecture to unramified
non-basic local Shimura data of abelian type under the assumption of Hodge–Newton reducibility.
We leverage Shen’s construction of Rapoport–Zink spaces of abelian type at the hyperspecial level.

Langlands correspondence. Following this, Rapoport and Viehmann conjectured the existence of
local Shimura varieties in [RV14], of which (the generic fibers of) Rapoport–Zink spaces are a special

The Harris–Viehmann conjecture concerns the cohomology of local Shimura varieties with under-
lying non-basic local Shimura data. Originally conjectured by Harris in [Har01], later modified by
Viehmann in [RV14], and then further amended by Bertoloni Meli in [Mel22], the conjecture gives a
parabolic inductive formula for the ℓ-adic cohomology of such local Shimura varieties. In particular,
it asserts that all the supercuspidal representations are concentrated along the basic locus.

1.2. History of results on the Harris–Viehmann conjecture:
(1) Boyer proved the Harris–Viehmann conjecture for Drinfeld modular varieties in [Boy99].
(2) Mantovan proved the existence of the canonical Hodge–Newton filtration for p-divisible
groups with additional structures in [Man08]. She passed to their Rapoport–Zink spaces of
PEL type under the assumption that the Newton polygon coincides with the Hodge polygon
up to or from the nontrivial break contact point, to prove the Harris–Viehmann conjecture.
(3) Shen followed up on this in [She14] by considering the more general case when the Newton
polygon admits a nontrivial contact point with the Hodge polygon, and assuming that this


## Page 4


4
contact point is a break point of the Newton polygon. This also established the Harris–
Viehmann conjecture for Rapoport–Zink spaces of PEL type.
(4) Hansen gave another proof of the conjecture in [Han21] for G = GLn under the Hodge–
Newton reducibility assumption. He used the theory of local Shimura varieties developed
by Scholze in [Sch18].
(5) Gaisin and Imai proved the conjecture (assuming Hodge–Newton reducibility) for a gener-
alization of the diamond of a non-basic Rapoport–Zink space at infinite level in [GI25].
(6) Hamann and Imai proved the conjecture under a mild Hodge–Newton reducibility condition
for a general local Shimura variety, by studying the moduli stack of parabolic bundles on
the Fargues–Fontaine curve in [HI25].
Our main goal is to establish the Harris–Viehmann conjecture for unramified local Shimura data
of abelian type, which are Hodge–Newton reducible with respect to fixed Levi and parabolic sub-
groups. We work with Rapoport–Zink spaces arising from unramified local Shimura data of abelian
type as constructed in [She20].
1.3. Road map for the paper. In Section 3, we review some background, including relevant defi-
nitions. In Section 4, we recall the construction of Rapoport–Zink spaces of Hodge type in [Kim18],
and summarize the Mantovan–Hong method, which proves the Harris–Viehmann conjecture for
Hodge–Newton reducible unramified local Shimura data of Hodge type. In Section 5, we recount
the construction of Rapoport–Zink spaces of abelian type in [She20], as well as some geometric
properties that follow.
We take a detour to address the Weil descent datum for local Shimura
varieties of abelian type in Section 6, for completeness. Section 7-Section 9 relates the geometry of
Rapoport–Zink spaces of abelian type with Hodge–Newton reducibility, setting up for studying their
cohomologies in Section 10. We put the results together to finish the proof of the Harris–Viehmann
conjecture for Hodge–Newton reducible unramified local Shimura data of abelian type in Section 10.
