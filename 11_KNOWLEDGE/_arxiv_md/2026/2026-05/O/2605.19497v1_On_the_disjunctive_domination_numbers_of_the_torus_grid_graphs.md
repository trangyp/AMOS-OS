Abstract
Let Γ = (V, E) be a graph. A disjunctive dominating set in Γ is a set S ⊆V such
that every vertex not in S is adjacent to a vertex of S, or has at least two vertices in
S at distance 2 from it. The minimum cardinality of a disjunctive dominating set in
Γ is called the disjunctive domination number of Γ. In this paper, we give bounds for
the disjunctive domination numbers of the torus grid graphs Cm□Cn, and determine
the disjunctive domination numbers of C3□Cn and C4□Cn.
Keywords: Disjunctive domination, Torus grid graphs

Introduction
Consider a simple graph Γ with vertex set V (Γ) and edge set E(Γ).
The distance
between two vertices u and v in Γ is denoted by d(u, v). For a vertex v ∈V (Γ), the set
of vertices at distance i from v is Γi(v) = {u ∈V (Γ) | d(u, v) = i}. For simplicity, we
use Γ(v) := Γ1(v). Let Pn and Cn denote a path graph and a cycle graph on n vertices,
respectively. The Cartesian product G□H of two graphs G and H is a graph with vertex
set V (G) × V (H), where two vertices (g, h) and (g′, h′) are adjacent if and only if either
g = g′ and hh′ ∈E(H), or h = h′ and gg′ ∈E(G). The graphs Cm□Cn are called torus
grid graphs. Set [k] := {0, . . . , k −1}.
Following Goddard et al. [1], a set S ⊆V (Γ) is called a disjunctive dominating set
(abbreviated DJCD-set) of Γ if for every vertex v ∈V (Γ) \ S, either v is adjacent to a
vertex in S, or there exist two vertices s1, s2 ∈S such that d(s1, v) = d(s2, v) = 2. In this
case, we say v is DJC-dominated by S. The minimum cardinality of a DJCD-set in Γ is
called the disjunctive domination number and is denoted by γd
2(Γ). Since its introduction,
it has been studied in [2–10].
