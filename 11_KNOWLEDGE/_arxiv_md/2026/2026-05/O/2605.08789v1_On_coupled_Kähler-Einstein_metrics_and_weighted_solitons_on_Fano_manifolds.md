Abstract. We consider coupled K¨ahler-Einstein metrics and weighted soli-
tons on Fano manifolds. These are natural generalizations of K¨ahler-Einstein
metrics. As in the case of K¨ahler-Einstein metrics, the existence is known to
be equivalent to algebraic conditions which generalize the K-polystability. In
this survey, we outline recent developments for these two cases.

For λ = −1 or λ = 1 the existence problem is an extension for the problem for

Further, Hultgren [42] proved the existence of coupled K¨ahler-Ricci solitons on
toric Fano manifolds (Theorem 3.9 below), and showed that there is a toric Fano
manifold such that the classical Futaki invariant does not vanish but for certain
choice of decomposition of the anticanonical class the barycenter lies at the origin
and thus, for such a choice, there exists couple K¨ahler-Einstein metrics though
there is no K¨ahler-Einstein metric, see Example 3.8 below. Hence, coupled K¨ahler-
Einstein metrics can be more flexible canonical K¨ahler metrics on Fano manifolds.

uncoupled (i.e. k = 1) K¨ahler-Einstein metrics on Fano manifolds. Here is a brief
summary of some of the well-known results in the uncoupled case.
