precisely when p,q lie on a horizontal line, the singularity is again under control in our case.

The proof of Theorem 1.3, which can be found in Section 2, is based on a modification of

Fässler and Orponen [9] recently performed a comprehensive study of 1-dimensional ker-

Fässler and Orponen in [9] proved that any smooth 1-dimensional kernel in H which is odd
