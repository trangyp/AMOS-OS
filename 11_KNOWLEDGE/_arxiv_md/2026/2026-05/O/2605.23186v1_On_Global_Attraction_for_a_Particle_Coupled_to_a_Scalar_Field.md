Abstract
We study the question of global attraction, in the energy norm, for finite energy
solutions to classical particle coupled to a scalar wave field. The attraction could take
place to either the set of the stationary solutions in the case of a confining potential or to
the soliton manifold in the case of zero potential.
By energy conservation argument we establish that in both cases there is no global
attraction.

The asymptotic behavior of solutions to (1.1) is by now well-known [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]. Either
the set of the stationary solutions or the soliton manifold is the set of attraction of solutions, in
