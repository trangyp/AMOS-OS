Abstract. Let (X, ω) be a compact n-dimensional K¨ahler orbifold, the sta-
bilizer groups of which are abelian and have rank at most two. Let E be an
orbi-ample vector bundle of rank 2 over X and let H be a Hermitian metric on
E such that the curvature form of det H is −2π√−1ω. We show that a certain
weighted sum of Bergman kernels for Symi E ⊗det(E)k+j as i and j vary over
a finite set admit an asymptotic expansion. This extends a similar result for
cyclic K¨ahler orbifolds [15].

In the case when (X, ω) is a compact K¨ahler manifold, the works of Tian [20],

2. Orbifolds
In this section, we recall necessary definitions and facts about orbifolds and
orbi-bundles. We refer the readers to [1], [3], [13], and [18] for more details.
Definition 2.1. Let X be a topological space. An orbifold chart of dimension
n ≥0 on X is a triple (U, G, φ) where U is a connected open subset of Rn, G is
a finite subgroup of diffeomorphisms of U, and φ : U →X is an open map that
induces a homeomorphism U/G →φ(U).
Let (U, G, φ) and (U ′, G′, φ′) be orbifold charts. An embedding λ : (U ′, G′, φ′) →
(U, G, φ) between orbifold charts is a smooth embedding λ : U ′ →U such that
φ ◦λ = φ′.
We say that an orbifold chart (U, G, φ) is a holomorphic chart of complex dimen-
sion n if U is a subset of Cn and G is contained in the group of biholomorphisms
of U. Similarly, we say that an embedding λ : (U ′, G′, φ′) →(U, G, φ) between
orbifold charts is a holomorphic embedding between orbifold charts if λ : U′ →U
is holomorphic.
We recall some notation regarding group actions on a manifold. Let M be a
manifold and denote the group of diffeomorphisms of M by Diff(M). Let G be a
finite subgroup of Diff(M). The isotropy group of a subset S ⊆M is
GS = {g ∈G : g · S = S}.
If S = {x} only has one element, then we will write Gx for GS. A subset S ⊆M is
called G-stable if it is connected and, for any g ∈G, either g ·S = S or g ·S ∩S = ∅.
The following proposition lists basic properties of embeddings between orbifold
charts. The proof of the proposition can be found in [13].
Proposition 2.2.
(1) For any embedding λ : (U ′, G′, φ′) →(U, G, φ) between
orbifold charts on X, the image λ(U ′) is a G-stable open subset of U, and
there is a unique isomorphism λ : G′ →Gλ(U ′) for which λ(g′ · ex) =
λ(g′) · λ(ex).
(2) The composition of embeddings between orbifold charts is an embedding
between orbifold charts.
(3) For any orbifold chart (U, G, φ), any diffeomorphism g ∈G is an embedding
of (U, G, φ) into itself and g(h) = ghg−1.
(4) If λ, µ : (U ′, G′, φ′) →(U, G, φ) are two embeddings between the same orb-
ifold charts, there exists a unique g ∈G with λ = g ◦µ.
