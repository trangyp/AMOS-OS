Abstract
We investigate a question posed by Chen [5]: if X and Y are paracom-
pact submetrizable spaces and f ∶X →Y is a perfect map, can X and
Y be submetrized by metrics ρ and d respectively such that f remains
perfect with respect to the induced topologies?
1
Introduction
A space X is submetrizable if X has a weaker metrizable topology, or, equiva-
lently, if X can be mapped onto a metrizable topological space by a continuous
one-to-one map.
In [5] Chen proved that if ⟨X,τX⟩is a paracompact σ-space and f ∶⟨X,τX⟩→
⟨Y,τY ⟩is a perfect map, then both ⟨X,τX⟩and ⟨Y,τY ⟩can be submetrized by
metric ρ and d respectively such that f ∶⟨X,τρ⟩→⟨Y,τd⟩is a perfect map. He
also posed the following question:
Question 1. Let both ⟨X,τX⟩and ⟨Y,τY ⟩be paracompact submetrizable spaces
and f ∶⟨X,τX⟩→⟨Y,τY ⟩be a perfect map. Then can ⟨X,τX⟩and ⟨Y,τY ⟩be
submetrized by metric ρ and d respectively such that f ∶⟨X,τρ⟩→⟨Y,τd⟩?
We give a negative answer to this question and show that the answer is
positive if we additionally assume that ⟨Y,τY ⟩is perfectly normal.
