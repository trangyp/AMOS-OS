Probing hidden-charm pentaquarks from the πN →J/ψN reaction
Samson Clymton,1, ∗Sun-Young Ryu,2, † Jung-Keun Ahn,3, ‡ and Hyun-Chul Kim4, 5, 6, §

We investigate the dynamical generation of hidden-charm pentaquark resonances in the πN →
J/ψN reaction utilizing an off-shell coupled-channel formalism. Motivated by the absence of pen-
taquark signals in J/ψ photoproduction, we evaluate rescattering effects with two-body kernel am-
plitudes constructed from effective Lagrangians that explicitly incorporate t-channel meson and u-
channel baryon exchanges. We demonstrate that the u-channel Λc exchange, of which an analogous
contribution is absent in the photoproduction kernel, greatly enhances the rescattering contributions
through the ¯D(∗)Σc intermediate states. Consequently, the ¯D(∗)Σ(∗)
c
channels yield contributions of
comparable magnitude to the ¯D(∗)Λc channels, directly leading to prominent pentaquark signals.
The partial-wave analysis reveals that the Pc¯c(4312) and Pc¯c(4457) states emerge as clear peak struc-
tures with JP = 1/2−and 3/2−, respectively. In contrast, the Pc¯c(4380) and JP = 5/2−states are
strongly suppressed because the Λc exchange does not provide the required tensor interactions. The
result for the total cross section reaches the microbarn level at the peak positions.
I.

Pc¯c(4440) : mPc¯c = (4440+4

−2.8) MeV.
These findings have stimulated extensive theoretical research into heavy pentaquark states (see recent reviews [4–10]
and references therein). In contrast, the GlueX Collaboration conducted J/ψ photoproduction experiments to search
for these hidden-charm pentaquark states but found no signals of the Pc¯c structures [11]. Consequently, since these
states have yet to be independently confirmed by other experiments, the Particle Data Group (PDG) classifies them
as “one-star (∗)” baryons [12].
Meanwhile, the LHCb Collaboration also observed two hidden-charm pentaquark states with strangeness S = −1:
the Pc¯cs(4338) with mPc¯cs = (4338.2 ± 0.8) MeV/c2 and ΓPc¯cs→J/ψ Λ = (7.0 ± 1.8) MeV [13], and the Pc¯cs(4459) with
mPc¯cs = (4458.8+6.0
−3.1) MeV/c2 and an unknown width [14]. Subsequently, the Belle Collaboration reported evidence
supporting the existence of the Pc¯cs(4459): MPc¯cs = (4471.7±4.8±0.6) MeV/c2 and Γ = (21.9±13.1±2.7) MeV [15].
However, the measured mass is approximately 13 MeV/c2 higher than the LHCb value. This discrepancy leaves it
unclear whether the Pc¯cs(4472) observed by Belle is identical to the Pc¯cs(4459). Theoretical work by Clymton et
al. [16] suggests that the Pc¯cs(4472) should be treated as a distinct state. This situation reinforces the critical need for
independent experimental confirmation to accurately determine the properties of newly observed particles. Therefore,
verifying the four Pc¯c hidden-charm pentaquarks through alternative experiments is essential.
Because the Japan Proton Accelerator Research Complex (J-PARC) provides pion beams with momenta up to
20 GeV/c, it serves as an ideal facility for experiments involving charmed hadron production [17, 18]. Recently,
Ryu et al.
proposed measuring the πN →J/ψN reaction at J-PARC [19].
This process requires a threshold
momentum of 8.2 GeV/c, well within the the capabilities of the facility.

In the present work, we investigate the emergence of hidden-charm pentaquark resonances in the πN →J/ψN

the dynamical generation of these resonances, we have intentionally excluded explicit s-channel pole diagrams from
the kernel amplitudes.
In a previous study [25] on the production mechanism of hidden-charm pentaquarks, it was demonstrated that six
such states are dynamically generated, four of which correspond to the findings reported by the LHCb Collaboration.
Based on an analysis of their coupling strengths to relevant channels, these four states can be interpreted as ¯DΣc(1/2−),
¯D∗Σc(1/2−), ¯DΣ∗
c(3/2−), and ¯D∗Σc(3/2−) molecular configurations, respectively, arranged in ascending order of
their masses. By analyzing the coupled amplitudes in the complex energy plane, the pole positions of these states
clearly emerge on the second Riemann sheet, confirming their nature as well-defined resonances. In addition to these
negative-parity molecular states, corresponding positive-parity states were also identified. Thus, the coupled transition
amplitudes derived in Ref. [25] can be utilized as input to investigate the γN →J/ψN and πN →J/ψN reactions. It
has already been shown that incorporating these amplitudes into the rescattering equation for J/ψ photoproduction
off the nucleon suppresses the pentaquark signals, yielding results consistent with the GlueX data. Therefore, it is
of significant interest to examine the πN →J/ψN process by including these amplitudes as rescattering effects. In
this work, we will demonstrate that two hidden-charm pentaquark states indeed manifest as resonances within this
reaction.
The present paper is organized as follows: In Sec. II, we formulate the πN →J/ψN reaction within the off-shell
coupled-channel framework, treating the transition amplitudes developed in Ref. [25] as rescattering effects.
We
also construct the kernel amplitudes for this reaction based on an effective Lagrangian, explicitly incorporating both
meson (t-channel) and baryon (u-channel) exchanges. Subsequently, we explain the numerical method for solving the
coupled rescattering equation. Section III presents the numerical results for the πN →J/ψN reaction. Here, we
analyze the total cross sections for the open-charm processes, specifically πN →¯D( ¯D∗)Yc(Y ∗

findings and their implications for future experiments.

in Fig. 4, incorporating the Λc exchange greatly amplifies the rescattering effects for both channels. This dramatic

from the initial πN channel. This specific comparison is restricted to these two intermediate states because they
couple most strongly to the observed Pc¯c states. In contrast, the corresponding kernel in J/ψ photoproduction lacks
an analogous contribution; this weak ¯D(∗)Σc coupling precisely suppresses the hidden-charm pentaquark signals in
the γN →J/ψN reaction.

IV.
SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS
In this work, we investigated the dynamical generation of hidden-charm pentaquark resonances in the πN →
J/ψN reaction. Motivated by the absence of Pc¯c signals in J/ψ photoproduction, we employed an off-shell coupled-
channel formalism to evaluate the rescattering effects. We constructed the two-body kernel amplitudes using effective
Lagrangians, explicitly incorporating t-channel meson and u-channel baryon exchanges. We demonstrated that the u-
channel Λc exchange plays a crucial role in the pion-induced reaction, providing a mechanism that has no counterpart
in the photoproduction process. This Λc exchange greatly amplifies the rescattering contributions through the ¯D(∗)Σc
intermediate states. Consequently, the ¯D(∗)Σ(∗)
c
channels yield contributions of comparable magnitude to the ¯D(∗)Λc

channels, directly leading to prominent pentaquark signals. The partial-wave analysis revealed that the Pc¯c(4312) and
Pc¯c(4457) states emerge as clear peak structures with JP = 1/2−and 3/2−, respectively. In contrast, the Pc¯c(4440)
signal is obscured by the dominant Pc¯c(4457), while the Pc¯c(4380) and the JP = 5/2−states are strongly suppressed
because the Λc exchange do not provide tensor interactions required to produce them. Finally, the calculated total
cross section for the π−p →J/ψn reaction reaches the microbarn level at the peak positions. Coupled with the
pronounced two-peak structure, this large signal-to-background ratio establishes the π−p →J/ψn reaction as an
effective probe for the experimental search and definitive identification of hidden-charm pentaquark states.
