PROXY SMALLNESS MEETS t-STRUCTURES
MICHAL HRBEK, PAT LANK, GIOVANNA LE GROS, AND SERGIO PAVON
Abstract. We introduce a notion of proxy smallness for t-structures on triangulated
categories associated to a Noetherian scheme. Specifically, the theory is developed in
the presence of tensor actions. Consequently, our results yield a new characterization
of schemes that are locally complete intersections in terms of t-structures, as well as a
topological classification of preaisles on the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves.

1. Introduction
1.1. What is known. Proxy smallness, introduced in [DGI06b, DGI06a], provides a
flexible weakening of compactness in triangulated categories. It has found applications in
commutative algebra and homotopy theory. More recently, it has been related to structural
properties of derived categories; see e.g. [BIS25].
A key application for this notion comes from [Pol19, Theorem 5.2]. Loc. cit. showed
that a Noetherian local ring R is a complete intersection if, and only if, every object of
Db
coh(R) is proxy small. This was extended in [Let21, Corollary 5.7] to Noetherian rings
which are locally complete intersections. Thus, proxy smallness detects singularities.
However, proxy smallness does not behave well under globalization: naive global
analogues fail to capture the correct geometry when passing from local to global settings.
To address this, [BILP22] introduced a tensor refinement. Roughly speaking, an object
E ∈Db
coh(X ) is said to be ⊗-proxy small if it generates, via tensor operations, a perfect
complex with the same support. They show that a Noetherian scheme X is locally a
complete intersection if, and only if, every object of Db
coh(X ) is ⊗-proxy small.

1.2. What we do. The goal of this paper is to show that proxy smallness admits a natural
refinement when viewed through the lens of t-structures and tensor actions.
1.2.1. Overview. Tensor actions between triangulated categories associated to a scheme
arise naturally. For instance, in Krause’s recollement relating Dqc(X ) and the homotopy
category of injectives K (Inj(X )) [Kra05]. In this setting, we introduce a compatibility
notion between tensor structures and t-structures. See §2.3 and §2.4. Our approach
proceeds in three steps.
First, we study ⊗-aisles in Dqc(X ) generated by pseudocoherent complexes, showing
that they are compactly generated (see Theorem 3.5). This extends [ATJLS10, Theorem
3.10] from the affine case to arbitrary Noetherian schemes. The result is of independent
interest and provides a key technical input.
Second, we introduce a notion of proxy smallness adapted to t-structures. See §4. After
developing its basic properties and comparing it with the classical notion, we characterize
t-proxy smallness in terms of recollements Theorem 4.7, yielding a t-structural analogue
of [BIS25, Theorem 4.3]. Continuing the comparison between these two notions, we then
discuss some examples and show that the collection of t-proxy small objects is strictly
smaller than the collection of proxy small objects Example 4.11.
Third, we incorporate tensor actions into this framework. We introduce a notion of
t-⊗-proxy small objects (see Definition 5.1) and study subcategories that are stable under
both the tensor action and the operations defining preaisles.

4. t-proxy smallness
This section introduces a notion of proxy smallness for t-structures. Using recollements,
we characterize it in Theorem 4.7. Let T be a triangulated category admitting coproducts,
and denote by Tc the subcategory of compacts. Recall the following definition, which is
originally due to [DGI06a], however we follow the formulation of [BIS25].
Definition 4.1. A skeletally small subcategory S ⊆T is proxy small if ⟨S⟩= ⟨⟨S⟩∩Tc⟩.

necessarily finitely) the objects of S.
