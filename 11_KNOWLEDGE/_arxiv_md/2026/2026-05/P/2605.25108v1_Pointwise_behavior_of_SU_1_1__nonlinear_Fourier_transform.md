POINTWISE BEHAVIOR OF SU(1,1) NONLINEAR FOURIER TRANSFORM
SERGEY A. DENISOV
Abstract. We show that SUp1, 1q NLFT can diverge pointwise for square-summable coefficients.
As a consequence, we prove that the classical pointwise asymptotics of polynomials orthogonal on
the unit circle can fail for measures in the Szegő class. We also discuss some special cases when the
pointwise convergence holds.
