COMPOSITION-DIFFERENTIATION OPERATORS ON
WEIGHTED DIRICHLET SPACES
ANIRBAN SEN, SOMDATTA BARIK AND KALLOL PAUL
Abstract. We characterize bounded, compact, and Hilbert–Schmidt com-
position–differentiation operators on weighted Dirichlet spaces. The essential
norm is estimated via the asymptotic behavior of a function that involves the
generalized Nevanlinna counting function of the inducing map.
Norm esti-
mates for particular inducing maps are given, and examples are provided to
demonstrate the applicability of the results.
1. Introduction
Let H(D) be the space of complex-valued holomorphic functions on the open
unit disk D = {z ∈C : |z| < 1}. For φ ∈H(D), with φ(D) ⊆D, the composition
operator Cφ on H(D) is defined by
Cφf = f ◦φ, f ∈H(D).
The properties of composition operators have been extensively studied over the
past decades, see [8, 13, 14, 15] for further details. In recent developments, a
new form of this operator has emerged as an object of study. Let D denote the
differentiation operator, which is generally unbounded on the spaces of analytic
functions. We consider the composition-differentiation operator given by
Dφf = CφDf = f ′ ◦φ, f ∈H(D).
It is known that Cφ is bounded on various analytic function spaces, whereas
Dφ can be unbounded in the same setting. In [5], the authors defined the op-
erator Dφ and examined its boundedness and compactness between weighted
Bergman spaces via Carleson-type measures. Following this, the operator Dφ has
been studied extensively on Hardy, Bergman, and Dirichlet spaces, see [2, 4, 7].
However, the bounded and compact composition–differentiation operators on
weighted Dirichlet spaces have yet to be fully characterized.
The goal of this article is to analyze composition–differentiation operators act-
ing on weighted Dirichlet spaces Dα for α ∈(0, 1) and to obtain elegant charac-
terizations. More precisely, we begin by establishing a function-theoretic charac-
terization of the boundedness of Dφ on weighted Dirichlet spaces in terms of the
generalized Nevanlinna counting function Nφ,α, stated as follows.
