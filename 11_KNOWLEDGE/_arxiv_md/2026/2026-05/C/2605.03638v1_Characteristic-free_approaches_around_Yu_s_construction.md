CHARACTERISTIC-FREE APPROACHES AROUND YU’S
CONSTRUCTION
YUTA TAKAYA
Abstract. We give a direct characteristic-free construction of twisted Heisenberg-Weil
representations when there are no symmetric and ramified roots. As a consequence, we show
that twisted Yu’s construction naturally extends to residual characteristic 2. Moreover, we
give a geometric realization of such twisted Heisenberg-Weil representations via the Deligne-
Lusztig construction for Heisenberg group schemes. As an application, we give an explicit
description of positive-depth parahoric Deligne-Lusztig induction in the generic case.

Introduction
0.1. Background. In the smooth representation theory of p-adic groups, Yu’s construction
[Yu01] is one of the most general and wide-ranging constructions of supercuspidal repre-
sentations to date. It is the main source of Kaletha’s local Langlands correspondence for
regular supercuspidal representations ([Kal19], [Kal21]). However, there are several technical
subtleties in the construction, arising essentially from the use of symplectic Heisenberg-Weil
representations developed in [Gér77]. One is the exceptional behavior at p = 2, which is
studied in detail in [FS25], and the other is the need to incorporate a quadratic twist as in
[FKS23] for the construction of stable L-packets. We refer to this twisted version as twisted
Yu’s construction. This quadratic twist is intrinsic also from the geometric viewpoint: it
has been observed (e.g. [CO25a], [CO25b], [Nie24], [IN26]) that twisted Yu’s construction is
innately realized in the cohomology of positive-depth Deligne-Lusztig varieties.
In this paper, we aim to give direct characteristic-free constructions of twisted Heisenberg-
Weil representations from the representation-theoretic and geometric point of view. As a
consequence, we show that twisted Yu’s construction naturally extends to p = 2 and provide
an explicit description of positive-depth parahoric Deligne-Lusztig induction in the generic
case.

The structure of the paper. In Section 1, we prove Proposition 2 by introducing an
axiomatic framework for decomposition of symplectic representations. In Section 2, we con-
struct the twisted Heisenberg-Weil representation κx,r,ϕ as a tensor product of polarized or
unitary Heisenberg-Weil representations. In Section 3, we show that twisted Yu’s construc-
tion works in residual characteristic 2. In Section 4, we compute the cohomology of certain
Artin-Schreier sheaves, and we use this result to show that κx,r,ϕ is realized in the cohomol-
ogy of WI,r in Section 5. In Section 6, we apply the computation in Section 5 to the explicit
description of positive-depth Deligne-Lusztig induction in the generic case.
