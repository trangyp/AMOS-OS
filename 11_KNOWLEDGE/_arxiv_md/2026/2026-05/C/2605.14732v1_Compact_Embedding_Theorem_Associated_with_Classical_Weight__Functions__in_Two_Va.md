Abstract
For a classical weight function ρ defined on a simply connected open subset Ωof R2 (either bounded or
unbounded) with piecewise C1 boundary, we prove density and compact embedding of a matrix-weighted Sobolev
space in the weighted Lebesgue space L2(Ω, ρ). As an application, we investigate via a variational method,
eigenvalue problem of a degenerate Helmholtz operator on triangle.
Keywords: Matrix-weighted sobolev spaces, compact embedding, generalized Helmholtz operator.
2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 46E35, 35J70, 33C50.

Introduction
Compact embeddings of Sobolev spaces are essential in the analysis of partial differential equations (cf. [9]). For
degenerate partial differential equations this issue is fundamental and has attracted considerable attention for decades
(cf. [13, 19, 6, 20]). Motivated by potential applications of the space of polynomials, P, to the theory of weighted
Sobolev spaces and second-order degenerate partial differential equations, we follow the definitions [1, Sections

space L2 associated with ρ, and provide an application.
