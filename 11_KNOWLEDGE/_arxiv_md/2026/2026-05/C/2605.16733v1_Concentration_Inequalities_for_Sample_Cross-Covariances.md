Concentration Inequalities for Sample Cross-Covariances
Jiaheng Chen and Daniel Sanz-Alonso
University of Chicago
Abstract
This paper establishes sharp dimension-free concentration and expectation bounds for the
deviation of a sample cross-covariance matrix from its mean. For sub-Gaussian random vectors,
we prove a high-probability operator-norm bound governed by the effective ranks of the two
marginal covariance matrices. In the Gaussian case, we prove a matching expectation lower
bound, allowing arbitrary correlation between the two random vectors.
