Abstract. In this paper, we present a hypersequent calculus for bimodal logic GR,
where the two modalities represent the arithmetic provability predicates of G¨odel and
Rosser, respectively. We prove the cut-elimination theorem for the calculus.
§1
Introduction
According to G¨odel’s first incompleteness theorem, there exists an arithmetical
sentence A such that neither A nor ¬A is provable in a formal arithmetic system,
e.g., Peano Arithmetic, PA. In the construction of such a sentence A, a unary
predicate referring to provability is used, and G¨odel assumed the condition
of what is called ω-consistency of the system in the theorem. Rosser offered
another provability predicate to construct such a sentence so that the condition
of the theorem is weakened to just the consistency of the system. While not
all of the so-called derivability conditions are satisfied, as Kreisel [7] proved,
the consistency assertion on the system itself, constructed from Rosser’s prov-
ability predicate, is actually provable in the system, that is, G¨odel’s second
incompleteness theorem does not hold with the Rosser’s provability predicate.
The study of the G¨odelean provability predicate via modal logic has been
a major field of research in mathematical logic since Solovay’s landmark work
[17] established the axiomatic system of modal logic GL with its completeness
theorems.
