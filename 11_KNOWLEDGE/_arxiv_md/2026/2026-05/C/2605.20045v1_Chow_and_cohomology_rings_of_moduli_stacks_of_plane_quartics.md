CHOW AND COHOMOLOGY RINGS OF MODULI STACKS OF PLANE
QUARTICS
KENNETH ASCHER AND DONGGUN LEE
Abstract. This paper studies the Chow and cohomology rings of the Hacking moduli stack
PH of plane quartics. We construct a smooth proper Deligne–Mumford stack resolving the
Calabi–Yau wall crossing between the KSBA and K-moduli compactifications for plane quar-
tics via stack-theoretic weighted blowups. Its coarse moduli space is, up to normalization,
the fiber product of the natural diagram relating the KSBA, K-moduli, and boundary polar-
ized Calabi–Yau compactifications. From this, we compute the Poincar´e polynomial of PH,
show that the cycle class map is an isomorphism with rational coefficients, and determine
generators and relations for its Chow ring in terms of tautological classes. Analogous results
are established for the GIT and K-moduli stacks.

understanding of their topology and intersection theory are extremely rare.
