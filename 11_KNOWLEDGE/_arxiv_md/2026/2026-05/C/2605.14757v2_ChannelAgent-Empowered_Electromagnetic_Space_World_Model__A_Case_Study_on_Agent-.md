AbstractAs sixth-generation (6G) wireles networks evolve
toward increasingly heterogeneous scenarios, tasks, and service

I. INTRODUCTION
As sixth-generation (6G) wireles systems continue to
evolve, future networks are expected to operate in increasingly
diverse and dynamic scenarios. Emerging paradigms such
as integrated sensing and comunication, articial inteli-
gence (AI)-native air interface, and ubiquitous conectivity
are continuously expanding the functional scope of wireles
systems [1][3]. Meanwhile, multi-band coexistence, complex
deployment environments, and diverse key performance in-
dicator (KPI) requirements pose heterogeneous chalenges in
scenarios, tasks, and performance objectives. Linking electro-
magnetic (EM) space with comunication performance, wire-
les chanels are closely coupled with propagation conditions
and service requirements. Therefore, proactive sensing, under-
standing, and adaptation of chanel states and comunication
tasks become critical for future wireles systems.

To met this demand, recent advances in AI have introduced
powerful data-driven tols into wireles comunications.
From task-specic neural network predictors [4] to emerg-
ing large models [5], AI-based methods have demonstrated
promising performance in a wide range of comunication
tasks, including chanel prediction [6], beam management
[7], radio map construction [8], and resource alocation [9].
However, most existing aproaches stil function primarily
as algorithmic tols under predened task pipelines: they
mainly learn pasive input-output mapings for specic tasks,
lacking the autonomous decision-making capability to proac-
tively adapt to changing environments and dynamic service
requirements.
This limitation sugests that future wireles systems should
move beyond isolated predictive models toward an au-
tonomous and closed-lop wireles inteligence paradigm [10].
In this context, AI agents ofer a promising direction. An
AI agent can be regarded as an autonomous computational
entity that perceives its environment, performs reasoning and
decision-making, and continuously interacts with the environ-
ment through actions to acomplish task objectives [1], [12].
Compared with conventional AI models that mainly provide
predictive capabilities, an agent further emphasizes task inter-
pretation, decision-making, tol orchestration, and fedback-
driven adaptation, thereby transforming model capability into
task-oriented action capability. This property is particularly
important for future wireles systems, where comunication
decisions must adapt to task objectives, environmental dynam-
ics, and propagation conditions.
Motivated by this insight, this paper introduces the concept
of AI agents into the chanel domain and rst proposes a
ChanelAgent-empowered EM space world model for multi-
dimensional comunication tasks in the wireles EM world.
Rather than treating comunication tasks as predened predic-
tion problems under xed input-output mapings, the proposed
model is designed as a closed-lop architecture consisting
of multi-modal sensing, ChanelAgent with inteligent brain
core and knowledge and tol suport, and execution with
fedback update, moving wireles systems beyond pasive
algorithm invocation toward a more inteligent paradigm with

Rather than folowing a pasive ting pipeline for predened
tasks, the proposed model forms a task-oriented closed-lop
architecture that enables wireles systems to sense the
wireles EM space, generate decisions, execute actions, and
rene subsequent behavior through fedback.
A. Multi-modal Sensing
Serving as the sensory interface of the proposed model,
multi-modal sensing is responsible for acquiring external state
information relevant to the curent comunication task and
forming an initial representation of the wireles EM space.
Unlike conventional systems that mainly rely on local link
measurements, this component suports a multi-dimensional
understanding of the wireles EM space, jointly characterizing
chanel conditions, environmental context, user requirements,
and service-related constraints, thereby establishing a more
holistic sensing of the curent scenario. Since comunication
tasks and their KPI priorities may vary acros scenarios, the
sensing information should jointly characterize environmen-
tal conditions and task constraints. Acordingly, multi-modal
sensing provides the input basis for subsequent proceses.
B. Inteligent Brain Core
As the cognitive center within ChanelAgent, the inteligent
brain core is responsible for transforming sensing inputs
into executable comunication decisions for the curent task.
Rather than functioning as a single, black-box model, it
undertakes the core functions of task interpretation, problem
formulation, and decision generation, enabling the system
to infer task objectives from sensing inputs and organize
subsequent actions acordingly.
Specicaly, this core contains thre closely coupled stages.
During task interpretation, the system identies the curent
task objective by jointly considering the scenario state, service
requirements, and performance constraints, and determines the
KPIs to be prioritized. Subsequently, in reasoning and plan-
ning, it formulates the problem acording to the environment
state and task requirements, and converts the overal objective
into an executable task chain. Finaly, within the decision
engine, the system determines the task strategy and identies
the tols required for subsequent execution.
Through this proces, ChanelAgent does not simply ex-
ecute a xed algorithm for a given input, but autonomously
performs understanding, problem formulation, and decision-
making around the task objective. For example, when a vehicle
turns at an intersection, the system can dynamicaly determine
whether propagation state re-evaluation, beam reselection, or
chanel prediction update is required acording to environ-
mental changes, link conditions, and service demands.
C. Knowledge and Tol Suport
Knowledge and tol suport provides the esential prior
grounding and execution suport for ChanelAgent within the
proposed model. It complements online sensing and reasoning
by suplying both reusable domain knowledge and calable
functional modules.
The wireles knowledge base mainly stores comunication-
related prior knowledge, such as wireles environment knowl-
edge (WEK) [13], protocol rules, mapings betwen KPIs
and task objectives, historical decision experience, and ex-
pert rules. Such knowledge provides important references for
task interpretation and reasoning, alowing the core decision
proces to go beyond instantaneous inputs and perform more
informed problem formulation and objective decomposition.
The tol pol, on the other hand, provides the executable
modules required for task realization, such as neural predic-
tion backbones, ray-tracing simulators, optimization modules,
and parameter adjustment tols. These tols do not operate
independently. Instead, they are invoked on demand under the
cordination of the decision engine to suport the execution
of diferent comunication tasks.
D. Execution with Fedback Update
Execution with fedback update is responsible for imple-
menting the generated decisions in the wireles EM space and
rening the proposed model acording to execution outcomes.
The selected strategy is caried out through the required tols
to acomplish the coresponding comunication task, thereby
translating the outputs of ChanelAgent into efective actions
in the wireles EM space. The resulting performance is then
evaluated and fed back to ChanelAgent while also being used
to rene the knowledge and tol suport. Through this proces,
the proposed model continuously improves future decisions

V. CONCLUSIONS
To adres the increasing heterogeneity in future 6G sys-
tems, this paper proposes a ChanelAgent-empowered EM
space world model, where ChanelAgent serves as the inteli-
gent core for closed-lop sensing, reasoning, decision-making,
and fedback update. An agent-driven chanel generation case
study is instantiated through path los prediction, in which a
hybrid feature selection mechanism combines reinforcement-
learning-inspired policy adaptation and evolutionary search.
Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method adap-
tively derives task-suitable feature subsets and improves path
los prediction performance acros diferent tasks, providing
an initial validation of the proposed model. Future work wil
focus on extending the model to more complex comunication
tasks and dynamic environments, and enhancing its online
decision-making and continual learning capabilities.
