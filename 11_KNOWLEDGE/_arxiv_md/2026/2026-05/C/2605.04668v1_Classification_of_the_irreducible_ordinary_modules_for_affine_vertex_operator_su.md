Introduction
Admissible representations were first studied by Kac and Wakimoto to explore mod-
ular invariant representations and the modular invariance property of normalized (su-
per)characters of admissible modules for the affine Lie (super)algebras associated to any
simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra or osp(1|2n) has also been proved in [KW88]. Fur-
thermore, they classified the admissible weights for all affine Lie algebras in [KW89]. Let
g be a basic classical Lie superalgebra, bg be the corresponding affine Lie superalgebra,
and Lbg(k, 0) be the simple quotient of the universal affine vertex operator superalgebra
of bg at the level k. A vertex operator superalgebra Lbg(k, 0) is called admissible if it is
isomorphic to an admissible representation as a bg-module, and k is called an admissible
level. In [AM95], Adamovi´c and Milas proved that for the admissible level k, g = sl2,
a bg-module L(λ) is a Lbg(k, 0)-module if and only if λ is an admissible weight of level
k, and they presented a conjecture for arbitrary affine Lie algebras. This conjecture was
proved by Arakawa in [Ar16], that is, Arakawa proved that Lbg(k, 0) at the admissible
level k is rational in the category O for any simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra g and
the representations of Lbg(k, 0) are equivalent to admissible representations of bg of level
k whose integral root system is isometric to that of L(kΛ0). This result was extended
to the only anisotropic basic Lie superalgebra g = osp(1|2n) by Gorelik and Serganova
in [GS22]. Moreover, an interesting bijection between admissible representations for the
affine Lie algebra bg at boundary admissible levels and C× fixed points in homogeneous
elliptic affine Springer fibres for the Langlands dual affine Lie algebra bg∨has been con-
structed by Shan, Xie and Yan in [SXY24]. On the other hand, Creutzig, Huang and
Yang proved that the ordinary module category of Lbg(k, 0) is a braided tensor category
for any simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra g at the admissible level k in [CHY18]. By
using the theory of vertex superalgebra extensions, Creutzig, Genra and Linshaw classi-
fied all the irreducible ordinary modules for L
