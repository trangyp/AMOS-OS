CONVERGENCE OF LORENTZIAN SPACES AND
CURVATURE BOUNDS FOR GENERALIZED CONES
CHRISTIAN KETTERER
Abstract. The goal of this article is twofold. We introduce a notion
of convergence for Lorentzian pre-length spaces, ℓ-convergence, that ex-
tends previous convergence notions in this context. We show that time-
like curvature and timelike curvature-dimension bounds are stable un-
der (measured) ℓ-convergence.
Then, we show that ℓ-convergence is
well adapted for generalized Lorentzian cones: a sequence of generalized
cones −Ii ×fi Xi converges in ℓsense if the base Ii and the fiber Xi
converge in GH sense and the functions fi converge uniformly. We use
this to show sharp timelike curvature and timelike curvature-dimension
bounds for such cones. Finally, we obtain a pre-compactness theorem
for ℓ-convergence in the class of smooth generalized cones that have a
uniform lower bound on the full Ricci (or Riemann) curvature tensor.

A. ℓ-convergence. We will introduce a new notion of convergence for the
class of Lorentzian pre-length spaces (Section 4). The main idea roughly is
to say that a sequence of signed time separation functions ℓi associated to
Lorentzian pre-length spaces Yi converges if the ℓis converge uniformly as
functions on the sequence of product spaces Yi × Yi (Definition 4.5). For
this we require that distance functions di that induce the topology on Yi
can be chosen such that the sequence (Yi, di) converges in a GH-type sense
(see Definition 4.3). We call this ℓ-convergence (see Definition 4.8), and
measured ℓ-convergence if there are also weakly converging measures in-
volved. For the precise definition we assume the existence of coverings and
a condition we call uniformly non-totally imprisoning (Definition 4.4). In
Section 5 we show that timelike curvature and timelike curvature-dimension
bounds are stable under (measured) ℓ-convergence (Theorem 5.6, Theorem
5.1, Theorem 5.5). The notion of ℓ-convergence includes the convergence
type that was used to show stability of the curvature-dimension condition
in [19] (see also [11]). Moreover, it covers uniform convergence of a sequence
of Lorentzian spacetimes w.r.t. a fixed Riemannian metric on the underly-
ing manifold (Theorem 6.7). Most importantly, we show that a sequence
of generalized cones converges in ℓ-sense if the sequence of bases and the
sequence of fibers converge in GH sense and the sequence of warping func-
tions f converges uniformly (Theorem 6.4). In fact, ℓ-convergence is directly
motivated by the desire to define a convergence for Lorentzian generalized
cones that has this property.
B. Cuvature of generalized cones. The second goal of the paper is to
employ ℓ-convergence for the study of timelike curvature and curvature-
dimension bounds of generalized Lorentzian cones (Section 7). We extend
the previously mentioned theorem by Alexander-Graf-Kunzinger-S¨amann to
the case of non-smooth FK-concave functions.
