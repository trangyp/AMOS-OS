p-ADIC ASYMPTOTIC SUBALGEBRA ENUMERATION
TOMAS REUNBROUCK
Abstract. We introduce the notion of p-adic asymptotics, or p-asymptotics, to the con-
text of finite-index subgroup and subalgebra enumeration. For finitely generated groups
and finite-dimensional algebras, we connect these asymptotics with the poles of their as-
sociated local zeta functions. Our two main results establish the smallest real pole for
local zeta functions associated with residually nilpotent algebras, as well as its simplicity
and residue whenever this algebra is graded. We thereby provide proof to parts of two
conjectures raised by Rossmann [14] and give a precise description of the p-asymptotic
behaviour inside these algebras.
