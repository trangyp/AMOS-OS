HADAMARD PRODUCT OF CONVEX FUNCTIONS AND JACKSON
OPERATOR
KRZYSZTOF PIEJKO1, JANUSZ SOK´O L2,∗, KATARZYNA TRA¸ BKA-WIE¸C LAW3
Abstract. In this paper we consider some properties of Jackson’s difference operator for convex
univalent functions in |z| < 1 with complex parameter q as a Hadamard product of two power
series.
Jackson in 1908 introduced for a real q, q ∈[0, 1), the difference operator dqf(z) for an
analytic function f in the unit disc |z| < 1 in the complex plane. Thanks to this operator, many
mathematicians have extended the theory of functions in q-theory. The q-theory has found many
applications in theory of hypergeometric series, special functions, combinatorics, number theory, fluid
mechanics, quantum mechanics and physics.
