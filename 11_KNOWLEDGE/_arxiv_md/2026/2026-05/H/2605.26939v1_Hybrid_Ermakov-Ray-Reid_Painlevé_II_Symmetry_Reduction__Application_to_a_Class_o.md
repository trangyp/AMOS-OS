Abstract
Here, a class of nonlinear moving boundary problems for a novel extension of a two-component
mKdV system is shown to admit exact solution via application of a hybrid Ermakov-Ray-Reid
/ Painlev´e II symmetry ansatz.The mKdV system has its genesis in a reduction of a coupled
nonlinear NLS system incorporating deBroglie - Bohm potential terms.

Introduction
Ermakov-type systems with genesis in the classical work of [4] have diverse physical applications in
both physics and nonlinear continuum mechanics [33]. The original Ermakov equation together with
its admitted nonlinear superposition principle has application notably in the analysis of boundary
value problems associated with the large amplitude oscillation of thin-walled cylinders composed
of hyperelastic Mooney-Rivlin material subject to a range of boundary loadings [14, 43]. In ad-
dition, the classical Ermakov equation occurs in the analysis of physical pulsrodon phenomena in
oceanographic warm-core eddy evolution theory [15]. The canonical nonlinear superposition prin-
ciple admitted by this Ermakov equation can be derived via Lie group invariance considerations
as in the hydrodynamic context of [13]. Nonlinear two-component coupled systems of Ermakov-
Ray-Reid type were originally introduced in [11, 12] with extension to 2+1-dimensional Ermakov
systems in [16]. Ermakov-Ray-Reid systems have diverse physical applications such as in nonlinear
optics [20,24], magnetogasdynamics [21], rotating shallow water hydrodynamics [22], elasticity [25],
2+1- dimensional Madelung hydrodynamic system analysis [23] and many-body theory [34].
In [17], multicomponent Ermakov-type systems were derived via symmetry reduction of a multi-
layer hydrodynamic system. Application was subsequently made to the analysis of non autonomous
many-body theory [34].
Hybrid Ermakov-Painlev´e II systems were originally derived in [26] in the context of wave packet
representations admitted by multi-dimensional coupled nonlinear Schrodinger systems incorpora-
tions de Broglie-Bohm potential. Therein, the canonical base Ermakov-Painlev´e II euation was
derived in the analysis of transverse wave propagation in a generalised Mooney-Rivlin hyperelas-
tic material. It has later been derived in a range of physical applications, such as cold plasma
physics [32], Korteweg capillarity theory [31] and in the analysis of Dirichlet boundary value prob-
lems for the classical Nernst-Planck electrolysis system [1].
Integrable structure underlying Ermakov-Painlev´e II systems has been detailed in [29]. Therein,
an admitted invariant was applied in an algorithmic solution procedure involving a Painlev´e II

II was used therein. An important link between the canonical single component Ermakov-Painlev´e
II equation and the integrable Painlev´e XXXIV equation was established.
Moving boundary problems of Stefan-type which admit exact solution via Painlev´e II symmetry
reduction was conducted for the canonical solitonic Dym equation (qv [45]) and its reciprocal
associates in [27]. The physical motivation resided in the classical Saffman-Taylor model. The
latter describes the percolation of a liquid into a porous medium or Hele-Shaw cell [7]. In [44],
an integrable extension of the Dym equation was shown to be embedded in a novel geometric
solitonic system descriptive of torsion evolution asssociated with the spatial binormal motion of
inextensible curves. A reciprocal transformation [8] was applied to established equivalence of this
torsion system to the m2KdV solitonic equation originally derived in [6] and later, independently
in [2]. In terms of physical applications, the extended Dym equation occurs, importantly in the
description of unidirectional dispersive shallow water propagation with novel associated peaked
solitonic phenomena [3]. In [30] certain moving boundary problems of Stefan-type for the extended
Dym equation were shown to admit exact solution via Painlev´e symmetry reduction.
Iterated
action of the B¨acklund transformation for Painlev´e II was applied to extend the range of such
solvable moving boundary problems. In addition, applications of reciprocal-type transformations
to determine associated solvable moving boundary problems was detailed.
Moving boundary problems in 1+1- dimensional soliton theory which are exactly solvable via
Painlev´e II symmetry reduction have been analysed in [28,36–39]. In [41], a novel extension of the
solitonic mKdV equation has recently been shown to be amenable to Ermakov-Painlev´e II symetry
reduction and application thereby made to solve a class of associated moving boundary problem.
Here, an extended mKdV coupled two-component system is shown to admit novel symmetry
reduction to a hybrid Ermakov-Ray-Reid/Painlev´e II system as originally set down in [29].
A
class of moving boundary problems for the coupled mKdV-type system is shown thereby is admit
exact solution. This class involves a region bounded by a pair of moving curves. Moving boundary
problems for two-component coupled systems with this characteristic occur in modern soliton theory
in connection with the resonant nonlinear Schr¨odinger equation [5]. The latter and the resonant
Davey-Stewartaon system have physical applications notably in capillarity theory [18,19] and cold
plasma physics [9].
