LOWER PATH REGULARITY IN ALL DIMENSIONS
MICHAEL HINZ1, JONAS M. TÖLLE2, AND LAURI VIITASAARI3
Abstract. We prove precise almost sure lower path regularity results for a wide class of
stochastic processes in all space dimensions d ≥1. Examples include Gaussian processes, in
particular, fractional Brownian motions with Hurst index H ∈(0,1), Rosenblatt processes,
and solutions to stochastic diﬀerential equations driven by fractional Brownian motions with
Hurst index H ∈( 1
4,1), all in arbitrary dimensions d ≥1. Our key tool is a new continuity
result for Riesz potentials of occupation measures, which we use as substitutes for local
times.

1. Introduction
The almost sure continuity of sample paths is a key research topic in the area of stochastic
processes, widely addressed in the literature and with numerous applications. For example,
the Hölder continuity of suﬃciently high order may be used to deﬁne pathwise integrals in
the Young [66] or rough path sense, see [26]. A classical result on the almost sure sample
path continuity is the famous Kolmogorov-Chentsov criterion, see for instance [39, Theorem
3.23] or [42, Theorems 1.4.1 and 1.4.4], which allows to translate moment conditions into
pathwise upper Hölder regularity. For Gaussian processes, this criterion is known to be also

To obtain almost sure lower path regularity bounds is a much more challenging task. A
prototype result in the classical case of Brownian motion is [15, Theorem 1.6.1] by Csörgő
and Révész. A well-known way to obtain almost sure lower path regularity is through local
times [6, 7, 31] which are, roughly speaking, deﬁned as the Radon-Nikodym derivatives of
the occupation measures with respect to the Lebesgue measure [6, 7, 31]. Upper regularity
results for local times were obtained in [41] and [53] in the classical case of Brownian motion.
For a fairly general class of Rd-valued Gaussian random ﬁelds, similar results were shown
in [65, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2]. They translate naturally into lower path regularity results
for the random ﬁeld itself [65, Theorem 3.1]. In the special case of the Rd-valued fractional
Brownian motion with Hurst index H ∈(0,1) the mere existence of local times requires
the dimensional condition Hd < 1 to be satisﬁed.
In [40], the scope of such results was
broadened to include also non-Gaussian examples, in particular the Rosenblatt process [59].
This process is deﬁned for H ∈(1
2,1), and since the existence of its local times again needs
Hd < 1, one can only consider the case d = 1. Inspired by studies of Besov regularity of
local times [10, 9], the existence and regularity of local times for a wide class of stochastic
processes X = (Xt)t∈[0,T] was investigated further in [60]. These results apply to Rd-valued
fractional Brownian motions with Hd < 1, to the Rosenblatt process in dimension d = 1, and
to solutions of stochastic diﬀerential equations driven by the Rd-valued fractional Brownian
motion with H ∈(1
4, 1
d).
In the present article, we revisit the class of processes X = (Xt)t∈[0,T] studied in [60], that
is, we consider hypercontractive Hölder continuous processes whose increments have char-
acteristic functions satisfying certain scaling and decay properties, see Assumption 1. We
now provide results on almost sure lower path regularity for all space dimensions d ≥1. The
new key idea is to replace local times by α-Riesz potentials of occupation measures. Unlike
local times, these potentials exist in Rd for any d ≥1, so that the severe dimensional restric-
tion incurred with the existence of local times becomes obsolete. Our ﬁrst main statement
is Theorem 2.1, in which we prove natural upper regularity results for α-Riesz potentials
of occupation measures; they parallel [65, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2].
In fact, Theorem 2.1
is formulated in a way that includes the known local time case, cf. [60, Theorem 2.1], as
the “limit case” α = 0, provided that the dimensional condition for the existence of local
times is met. In our second main statement, Theorem 2.3, we give the desired almost sure
lower path regularity results for the process X = (Xt)t∈[0,T] itself. They match the known

Recently there has been a growing interest in occupation measures and their regularity
properties, partially motivated by well-known applications in stochastic analysis, such as
regularization by noise [13, 25, 27, 28, 33, 58] and existence results for pathwise integrals
with discontinuous integrands [29, 30, 35, 36, 38, 37]. The present article continues our series
of papers [35, 36, 38, 37]; they are all linked to the idea of viewing potentials of occupation
measures as less precise, “smeared” variants of local times. This idea is supported by the
straightforward observation that local times, if they exist, can be represented as limits of
rescaled Riesz potentials of occupation measures, see Lemma 4.1 below. A similar kind of
result holds even if local times do not exist, but only so-called average densities [5, 21, 22, 67]
exist. We take the opportunity to state and prove this observation in the auxiliary Lemma
4.7.
