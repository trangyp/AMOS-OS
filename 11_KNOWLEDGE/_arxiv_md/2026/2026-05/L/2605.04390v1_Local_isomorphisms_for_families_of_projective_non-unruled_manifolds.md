LOCAL ISOMORPHISMS FOR FAMILIES OF PROJECTIVE
NON-UNRULED MANIFOLDS
MU-LIN LI
Abstract. Let π : X →S and π : Y →S be two smooth families of projective
non-uniruled manifolds over a Riemann surface S (probably non-compact).
Suppose these two families are pointwise isomorphic.
We prove that there
exists an open dense subset U ⊂S such that the two restricted families are
locally isomorphic over U. This partially answers Wehler’s question on locally
isomorphic of families of compact complex manifolds.
1. introduction
Let π : X →S be a proper holomorphic submersion between complex mani-
folds X and S. Therefore all the fibers of π are compact complex manifolds of
equal dimension. Such a proper submersion is called a smooth family of compact
complex manifolds. Let π : X →S and π′ : Y →S be two families of compact
complex manifolds over a complex manifold S. We say these families are pointwise
isomorphic if there exists a bi-holomorphism fs : Ys = π′−1(s) →Xs := π−1(s) for
every point s ∈S.
We say the two families π and π′ are locally isomorphic at s if there exists an
open neighborhood Us ⊂S and a bi-holomorphism gUs : YUs →XUs such that
s ∈Us and π′
Us = πUs ◦gUs. Here πUs : XUs = π−1(Us) →Us is the restriction of
π, and π′
Us : YUs = π′−1(Us) →Us is the restriction of π′.
In 1977, Wehler posed the following question.
Question 1.1. Let π : X →S and π′ : Y →S be two smooth families of compact
complex manifolds over a complex manifold S. Suppose that these two families are
pointwise isomorphic. If the function
s →dim H0(Ys, TYs)
is constant on S, are these two families locally isomorphic at every point of S?
Wehler [12] proved that it is true for families of complex tori and families of
compact manifolds with negative curved holomorphic curvature. Meersseman [8]
attempted to prove it for arbitrary families of compact manifolds, but a counterex-
ample constructed by Kirschner [5] revealed gaps in his proof.
This led to the
proposal of the following weakened version of the question.

Conjecture 1.2. Let π : X →S and π′ : Y →S be two smooth families of compact
complex manifolds over a complex manifold S. Suppose that these two families are
pointwise isomorphic. If the function
s →dim H0(Ys, TYs)
is constant on S, then there exists an open dense subset U ⊂S such that the
restricted families πU : XU = π−1(U) →U and π′
U : YU = π′−1(U) →U are locally
isomorphic at every point s ∈U.
In this paper, we focus on families of projective non-uniruled manifolds. By using
the coarse moduli space of polarized Kähler manifolds constructed by Fujiki [3] and
Schumacher [10], we establish the following theorem.
Theorem 1.3. Let π : X →S and π′ : Y →S be two smooth families of projective
non-uniruled manifolds over a Riemann surface S. Suppose the two families are
pointwise isomorphic. Then there exists an open dense subset U ⊂S such that the
restricted families πU : XU = π−1(U) →U and π′
U : YU = π′−1(U) →U are locally
isomorphic at every point s ∈U.
The main idea of the proof is as following. For two smooth families of projective
non-uniruled manifolds π : X →∆and π′ : Y →∆, we first construct two new
families of polarized projective non-uniruled manifolds (πU : XU →U, α) and (π′
U :
YU →U, β). These two families induce two holomorphic maps f, f ′ : U →M
satisfying f(U) = f ′(U), where M is the coarse moduli of polarized projective non-
uniruled manifolds. Then by using the fact that the moduli functor of polarized
projective non-uniruled manifolds is locally complete, we finish the proof.

Definition 2.7. Let (f : X →S, ωX ) be a polarized family of compact Kähler man-

family for polarized non-uniruled manifolds.
Proposition 2.8.
[3, Proposition 8] For any polarized compact Kähler manifold
(X, ωX) such that Aut0(X) is a complex torus its local modular family (f : X →S, ωX )
with (X0, ωX0) = (X, ωX) exists. Moreover it is locally complete at every point in a
neighborhood of 0.
