LOCAL NEWFORMS FOR GENERIC REPRESENTATIONS OF p-ADIC
SO2n+1: REDUCTION
YAO CHENG
WITH AN APPENDIX BY CHI-HENG LO
Abstract.
We prove that if the space of newforms is non-zero for every irreducible generic
supercuspidal representation of SO2n+1 then it is also non-zero for all irreducible generic
representations of SO2n+1.
1. Introduction
This paper is a sequel to [Che25], in which we proved the uniqueness part of the newform
conjecture for the p-adic group SO2n+1 proposed by Gross ([Gro15]) and verified the expected
arithmetic properties of newforms, conditional on their existence. The aim of the present
paper is to reduce the existence part of the conjecture to the case of generic supercuspidal
representations.
In the literature, the newform conjecture for generic supercuspidal representations was
treated in the PhD thesis of Tsai ([Tsa13]); however, it seems that Tsai’s proofs contain some
issues (see §6.9). In the present paper, we do not attempt to resolve these issues. Therefore,
it may be safer to say that the existence part of the conjecture remains conditional.
1.1. Main results. To state the conjecture and our results, let F be a finite extension of
Qp, and let SO2n+1(F) denote the split odd special orthogonal group defined over F.
In
[Gro15] (see also [Tsa13] and [Che25]), Gross defined a family {Kn,m}m≥0 of open compact
subgroups of SO2n+1(F), generalizing the families introduced by Casselman ([Cas73]) for n = 1
and Roberts–Schmidt ([RS07]) for n = 2 to arbitrary n. He also defined a family {Jn,m}m≥0
of open compact subgroups of SO2n+1(F) such that Kn,0 = Jn,0 and, for each m ≥1, Kn,m is a
normal subgroup of Jn,m of index 2.
Let Un(F) be the maximal unipotent subgroup of SO2n+1(F), which consists of upper
triangular matrices. Fix an unramified additive character ψ of F, and define a non-degenerate
character ψUn of Un(F) by

(1) the subspaces satisfy πKn,m = 0 for 0 ≤m < cπ and dimC πKn,cπ = 1;
(2) the action of Jn,cπ/Kn,cπ on πKn,cπ is given by the scalar επ;

This conjecture holds when n = 1 and 2, due to the works of Casselman ([Cas73]) and
Roberts–Schmidt ([RS07]), respectively. It also holds when π is unramified. In the prequel to
the present paper, we obtain the following results:
Theorem 1.1 ([Che25]). Let π be an irreducible generic representation of SO2n+1(F). Then
(1) the subspaces satisfy πKn,m = 0 for 0 ≤m < cπ and dimC πKn,cπ ≤1;
(2) if πKn,cπ ≠0, then the action of Jn,cπ/Kn,cπ on πKn,cπ is given by the scalar επ;
(3) if π is tempered and πKn,cπ ≠0, then the natural pairing of the one-dimensional spaces
HomKn,cπ (1,π) ×HomUn(F) (π,ψUn) Ð→C,
is non-degenerate.
In view of Theorem 1.1, it remains to verify the existence part of the conjecture, namely,
to prove that πKn,cπ ≠0. Following the prequel paper, πKn,cπ is referred to as the space of
newforms of π. The aim of the present paper is to further reduce the existence part of the
conjecture to the case of generic supercuspidal representations. More precisely, we obtain the
following result in this paper.
Theorem 1.2. If the space of newforms is non-zero for every irreducible generic supercuspidal
representation, then it is also non-zero for all irreducible generic representations.
By Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2, to settle the newform conjecture, it suffices to prove:
●that the space of newforms is non-zero for every irreducible generic supercuspidal
representation;
●that the natural pairing appearing in the third assertion of the newform conjecture is
non-degenerate for non-tempered generic representations.
As mentioned, Tsai’s proofs for supercuspidal representations seem to contain some issues;
however, we believe that her strategy is essentially correct. We hope to settle this conjecture
for supercuspidal representations, either by following Tsai’s approach or by providing a new
proof. For the assertion concerning the non-degeneracy of the natural pairing, on the other
hand, we currently have no approach to proving it.
