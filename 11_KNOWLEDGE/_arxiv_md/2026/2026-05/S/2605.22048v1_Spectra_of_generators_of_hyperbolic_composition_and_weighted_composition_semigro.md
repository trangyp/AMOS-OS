SPECTRA OF GENERATORS OF HYPERBOLIC COMPOSITION
AND WEIGHTED COMPOSITION SEMIGROUPS
YONG-XIN GAO AND ZE-HUA ZHOU
Abstract. In this paper, we provide complete characterizations for the spec-
trum, essential spectrum, and point spectrum of the generators of weighted
composition C0-semigroups induced by hyperbolic semiflows on Bergman spaces.
We give an explicit example showing that the spectral properties can be influ-
enced by the behavior of the semigroup near non-fixed self-contact points.
1. Introduction
A (one-parameter) semigroup of bounded operators on a Banach space X is a
semigroup homomorphism t 7→Tt from the additive semigroup [0, +∞) into the
algebra of bounded operators on X, that is, T0 = IdX and Ts+t = TsTt for all
s, t ≥0. A semigroup (Tt)t≥0 is called strongly continuous (or a C0-semigroup) if
the orbit map t 7→Ttx is continuous from [0, +∞) into X for each x ∈X.
For any C0-semigroup (Tt), its generator A : D(A) ⊂X →X is defined by

The spectra of single weighted composition operators on various types of Banach
spaces of analytic functions has been extensively studied in numerous papers. The
most complete descriptions are usually obtained when the operator is compact or
invertible., see [7, 12, 13, 14] for instance. Yet it is not very clear when a weighted
composition operator uCφ can be embedded into a C0-semigroup. However, when
such an embedding exists, the semigroup structure offers another perspectives for
computing the spectrum of uCφ. And in this context the spectrum of the generator
of such a weighted composition semigroup plays a crucial role.
In the very recent papers [1, 17], the authors investigate the spectra of the gen-
erator of (utCφt)t∈R when it forms a C0-group. Note that a semigroup (utCφt)t≥0
extends to a group precisely when each operator utCφt is invertible for t > 0, and
this forces every φt to be an automorphisms of the disk D. The authors in [1] de-
termine the spectrum of the generator of such a C0-groups in the case that (φt) is a
flow of hyperbolic automorphisms. These result generalize known descriptions for
the spectra of invertible weighted composition operators to wider classes of analytic
function spaces.
Given these developments, it is natural and of significant interest to ask about
the spectral properties of a general C0-semigroup (utCφt)t≥0 that does not extend
to a group. This is exactly the main purpose of the present paper. In this setting,
the (φt) no longer consists of automorphisms, hence a finer analysis is evidently
required.
This paper contains two main results.
First, Theorem 3.1 gives a complete
description of the spectrum of the generator of a hyperbolic weighted composition
semigroup under the broadest possible hypotheses. In addition, we also characterize
the essential spectrum of such generators, which is presented in Theorem 3.3.
As a special case, we obtain a full spectral characterization of the generators
of (unweighted) composition semigroups. The eigenvalues of such generators have
been studied in many literatures. In [2, 3] the author gives a complete description of
the eigenvalues of the generator of a hyperbolic composition semigroup. However, a
complete picture of the whole spectrum of the generator of a hyperbolic composition
semigroup has been lacking, and our Corollary 3.4 fills this gap.
The paper is organized as follows. We first introduce the settings and fix our
notations in Section 2. Then in Section 3 we state our main results, provide ex-
planatory remarks, and compare our results with those previously known in the
literatures. Section 4 discusses the properties of semiflows and semicocycles that
induced the weighted composition semigroups. In Section 5, we give the spectral
radii of the weighted composition operators in the semigroups. And Section 6 de-
termines the eigenvalues of the generators. Section 7 contains complete proofs of
our main results Theorem 3.1 and Theorem 3.3. Finally, in Section 8 we give some
further remarks and questions.

4. Semiflows and semicocycles
In this section, we conduct a detailed investigation of the behavior of semiflows
and semicocycles near the boundary, particularly in the vicinity of the fixed points
of semiflows.
Since discussing the properties of semiflows directly on the disk D is sometimes
inconvenient, we occasionally employ alternative models. For a hyperbolic semiflow
(φt), there exists a univalent map h on D such that
h ◦φt(z) = h(z) + t
for all z ∈D and t ≥0. For convenience, we may assume h(0) = 0, then with this
normalization h is unique. Set Ω= h(D). Then the pair (Ω, h) is called a canonical
model for (φt), and h is referred to as the Koenigs function of (φt). According to
Theorem 9.3.5 in [4], the domain Ωis contained in a horizontal strip of width π/α0.
The following theorem shows that our requirement on the analyticity of (φt) at
its self-contact points, which is imposed in Section 2, is equivalent to the condition
that φt is analytic at the self-contact points for all t > 0. Moreover, one can see
that this condition is intimately related to the properties of the Koenigs function
h near the fixed points of (φt).

semigroup of weighted composition operators does not satisfy the SMT, nor even
WSMT.
Turning to eigenvalues, the spectral mapping theorem holds for the point spectra
of all C0-semigroups, see, e.g., p.277 of [10]. Consequently,
σp(utCφt)\{0} = etσp(A).
Since 0 /∈σp(utCφt) for any t ≥0, we immediately obtain a description of the point
spectra of the operators utCφt as a corollary of Theorem 6.1.
Corollary 8.3. Let (utCφt) is a C0-semigroup on Ap, where (φt) is a hyperbolic
semiflow. Supose (φt) is analytic at its self-contact points, and (ut) is continuous
at these points.
(i) If γ1 < γ0, then for any t ≥0,
σp(utCφt) = {λ ∈C : eγ1t ≤|λ| ≤eγ0t},
and
σp(utCφt) ⊃{λ ∈C : eγ1t < |λ| < eγ0t}.
(ii) If γ1 > γ0, then for any t > 0, we have σp(utCφt) = ∅.
