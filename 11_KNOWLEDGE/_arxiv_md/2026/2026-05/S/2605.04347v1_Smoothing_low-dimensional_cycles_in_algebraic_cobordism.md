SMOOTHING LOW-DIMENSIONAL CYCLES IN ALGEBRAIC COBORDISM
CHUHAO HUANG
Abstract. We show that every cycle in the degree d algebraic cobordism group ΩdpXq of a
smooth projective variety X over a field of characteristic 0 is smoothable when 2d ă dimpXq, that
is, it can be written as a linear combination of cycles represented by smooth closed subvarieties
of X. This generalizes a result of Kollár and Voisin from Chow groups to algebraic cobordism
groups.
1. Introduction
Throughout this paper, we work over a fixed field k of characteristic 0.
1.1. Smoothability of algebraic cycles. The question of smoothability of algebraic cycles was
first asked over k “ C and in the context of singular cohomology by Borel and Haefliger in [BH61,
Section 6.17]. The question can be stated as follows. Let X be a smooth complex projective variety
of dimension n. We define H˚pX, Zqalg to be the subgroup of H˚pX, Zq generated by fundamental
classes of subvarieties of X, and define H˚pX, Zqsm similarly with the additional requirement that
the subvarieties be smooth. Classes in H˚pX, Zqsm are called smoothable. The question is whether
we have H˚pX, Zqalg “ H˚pX, Zqsm or not.
It was soon realized that this equality does not hold in general. The first negative result was ob-
tained by Hartshorne, Rees and Thomas in [HRT74]. They show that the second Chern class c2pTq
of the tautological bundle T over the complex Grassmannian Gp3, nq is never smoothable for n ě 6.
Benoist and Debarre prove in [Deb95, BD23] that for a very general complex Jacobian pX, θq of

Among all the counter-examples mentioned above, the dimension of the cycle is always at least
half of the dimension of the ambient variety. When the dimension of the cycle is relatively small
compared to the dimension of the variety, there are positive results. Hironaka proves in [Hir68]

In a recent break through [KV24], Kollár and Voisin give a complete answer to this question by
generalizing Hironaka’s result to all d ě 0.
Theorem 1.1 ([KV24, Theorem 1.2]). Let X be a smooth projective variety over k of dimension n.
Then, for any d such that 2d ă n, every cycle in CHdpXq is smoothable.
The goal of this article is to prove an analogous result in algebraic cobordism. Our main result
is as follows.
Theorem 1.2 (Theorem 4.5). Let X be a smooth projective variety over k of dimension n. Then
classes in the degree d algebraic cobordism group ΩdpXq are smoothable when 2d ă n.

In algebraic geometry, we can make an analogy with the topological setting by viewing the Chow

of Kollár and Voisin, namely Theorem 1.1, by incorporating information on Chern numbers of
algebraic cycles.
