STABILITY CONDITIONS AND INFINITESIMAL DEFORMATION OF
CURVES
KOTARO KAWATANI
Abstract. Let X be an infinitesimal deformation of a smooth projective curve X0
over a field.
We study stability conditions under such deformations and show that
the derived push-forward functor associated with the inclusion X0 →X induces an
isomorphism between the space of stability conditions on X and that on X0. This yields
a direct comparison between the deformed and undeformed settings. As an application,
we prove that the autoequivalence group Aut Db(X) naturally acts on Db(X0), providing
a link between derived symmetries and the deformation structure.
1. Introduction
Since the 2000s, the study of triangulated categories has developed rapidly. One of the
key invariants in this area is the space of stability conditions Stab D on a triangulated
category D introduced by Bridgeland [Bri07]. A notable feature of this invariant is that
it carries the structure of a complex manifold, although it may be empty.
As one might expect, it is very difficult to give an explicit description of Stab D. For
instance, when D = Db(X) is the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves on a
scheme X, the space Stab D is well understood only in special cases. If X is a smooth
projective curve over a field k, the space Stab D was described by Okada [Oka06] (genus
g = 0), Bridgeland [Bri07] (g = 1), and Macr´ı [Mac07] (g > 1). The case of an irreducible
singular curve X of arithmetic genus 1 was first studied by Burban and Krauß [BKl06].
More general cases have been studied in Karube [Kar24] and Liu [Liu25].
If X is a
Noetherian affine scheme of positive dimension, then Stab D is empty by [KM23].
We note that, in the existing literature, mainly reduced schemes have been considered.
Therefore, in this paper, we turn our attention to the non-reduced case, focusing in
particular on infinitesimal deformations.
Before discussing the main case of interest, we first consider a toy model of infinitesimal
deformations. Namely, let X be the affine scheme Spec R of an Artinian local k-algebra
(R, m) with the residue field k. We note that Spec R is an infinitesimal deformation of
the point Spec k.
Proposition 1.1 (=Proposition 3.10). Let (R, m) be an Artinian local ring with the
residue field k = R/m. Then the space Stab Db(Spec R) is non-empty and isomorphic to
Stab Db(Spec k).
Proposition 1.1 suggests that the space of stability conditions is invariant under infini-
tesimal deformations of a point. In view of this, the case of curves is a natural next step
to consider. Motivated by this observation, we state our main result as follows.
