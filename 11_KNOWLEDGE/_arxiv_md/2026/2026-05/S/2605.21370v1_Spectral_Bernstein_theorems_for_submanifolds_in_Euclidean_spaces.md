Spectral Bernstein theorems for submanifolds in
Euclidean spaces
Yuxin Dong∗, Hezi Lin†, Wei Zhang
In this paper, we consider the essential spectrum of submanifolds in Eu-
clidean spaces under various geometric hypotheses. Our results involve extrinsic
conditions such as finite total mean curvature, the convergence of the gradi-
ent of the extrinsic distance, and the extrinsic volume growth or the pinching
curvature. In particular, we prove that the essential spectrum of a complete
non-compact submanifold Mn in a Euclidean space is [0, +∞) provided the
second fundamental form A of Mn satisfies ∥A∥Lp < ∞, p > n.

Introduction
The Laplace operator −△on a complete non-compact manifold (M, g) is essen-
tially self-adjoint on the domain C∞
0 (M), and thus it admits a unique self-adjoint
extension to L2(M). The spectrum of −△consists of all λ ≥0 such that △+ λI

Sometimes we denote it by σ(M), which can be
decomposed as
σ(M) = σp(M) ∪σess(M),
where σp(M) is the point spectrum, formed by eigenvalues with finite multiplicity, and
σess(M) is the essential spectrum, formed by accumulation points of the spectrum and
by the eigenvalues with infinite multiplicity. Thus the essential spectrum is a closed
subset of [0, +∞). The spectrum of −△on a complete non-compact manifold M has
been proved to be closely related to the geometric and topological properties of M.
Since the Euclidean space Rm has only essential spectrum consisting of the whole
half line [0, +∞), we may naturally consider which extrinsic conditions ensure a sub-
manifold of Rm having a trivial spectrum, that is, which submanifold has the same
spectrum as Rm.
