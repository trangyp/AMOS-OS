SIMPLE PROOFS FOR THE EXISTENCE OF SMOOTH
SOLUTIONS TO A REACTION-DIFFUSION SYSTEM
MODELING REVERSIBLE CHEMISTRY
HECTOR BOUTON(1), LAURENT DESVILLETTES(2), AND HELGE DIETERT(3)
Abstract. We present in this work a very short proof for the existence,
uniqueness and smoothness in dimensions d ≤3 of the system of reaction
diffusion ∂tai −di ∆ai = (−1)i (a1 a3 −a2 a4), where ai ≥0 model the con-
centrations of chemical species undergoing a chemical reaction and diffusing
(each with its diffusion rate di > 0) in a bounded container.
Classification AMS 2010: 35A01, 35A09, 35K57, 35Q92.
Keywords: Chemical reaction-diffusion systems; Entropy method, Strong solutions to
systems of PDEs

This system models, according to the mass action law, the evolution of 4 chemical

they are confined (whence the homogeneous Neumann boundary condition), and
undergo the reversible reaction A1 + A3 ⇌A2 + A4 (for presentation, we assume
that the reaction rates are k = l = 1). We focus on dimensions d ≤3, which are
the most interesting for applications.
This system has attracted a lot of attention in the past years. Weak solutions in
all dimension were known to exist at least since the 2000s (cf. [DFPV07], and in a
more general context [Fis15]), while strong (unique) solutions were built in [CDF14]
in dimension 1 and 2. In higher dimension, such strong solutions were provided in
the papers [Sou18], [CGV19] and [FMT20] (cf. also the pioneering work of [Kan90]).
In this work, we intend to provide a complete self-contained proof of existence to
strong solutions to system (10), (11) in dimension d ≤3, which is very short. It uses
heavily the entropy structure of the equation, together with some ideas coming from
our previous work [BDD25], including an interpolation inequality that we present
here in detail using only elementary computations. Note that this entropy structure
