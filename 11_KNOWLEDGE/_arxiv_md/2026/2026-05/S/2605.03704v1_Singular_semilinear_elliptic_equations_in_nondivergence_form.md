Studies of singular semilinear equations of the form similar to (1.1) were initiated over half
a century ago with the works of Fulks and Maybee [22], Stuart [40], and Crandall, Rabinowitz,
and Tartar [12]. Since then, the topic has been extensively investigated to cover broader cases
of P, f, and γ, as well as various generalizations or perturbations of (1.1). We refer to the
recent paper [39] for an exposition of the existing results.
Our problem can be considered as a multidimensional counterpart of the Emden–Fowler
equation, originally an ODE of the form u
′′(x) = Cxβuα, where α, β ∈R. This equation and its
prominent generalizations, Lane–Emden–Fowler type equations, have been applied in science,
electrical engineering, astrophysics, and the analysis of non-Newtonian fluids [11, 23, 35, 43].
More recently, Boccardo and Orsina [6] made a substantial contribution to this class of
equations by investigating the existence of solutions to (1.1) for operators P in divergence
form, i.e. when Pu = div (A∇u), and with nonnegative f ∈L1(Ω).
Elliptic operators in nondivergence form are of considerable interest in the theory of stochastic
processes, as they form an important subclass of generators of diffusion processes, see e.g. [3,
Section 6.7.3]. Stochastic analysis largely facilitates the construction and estimates of integral
kernels, e.g. the Green function or the Poisson kernel; we refer to [9, 44] in this context.
Our first main result is the following theorem on the existence of solutions to (1.1) with the
same range of f and γ as in [6], but for P in nondivergence form. For the symbols and notions
used in the formulation, see Section 2.
