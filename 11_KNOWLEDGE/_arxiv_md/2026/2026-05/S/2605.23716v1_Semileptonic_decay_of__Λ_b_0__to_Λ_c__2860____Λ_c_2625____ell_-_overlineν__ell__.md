Hui-Hui Duan∗, Jia-Bao Feng, Feng-Mei Liu, and Qin Chang†
School of Physics, Henan Normal University, Xinxiang 453007, Henan, People’s Republic of China
Abstract
In this work, we calculate the transition form factors for the weak decays Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ and Λ0
b →
Λc(2625)+ using QCD light-cone sum rules, and compute the branching fractions of the corresponding semilep-
tonic decays Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ℓ−¯νℓand Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ℓ−¯νℓ.
Our predicted branching fraction for Λ0
b →
Λc(2625)+ℓ−¯νℓis consistent with experimental data and other theoretical predictions, validating the reliability
of our method. On this basis, we also present the branching fraction of Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ℓ−¯νℓ. These results
may serve as a theoretical reference for future experimental measurements of this decay channel.

Introduction
In our previous work, we calculated the weak decay form factors and semileptonic decay branching fractions of
Λb →Λcℓνℓwithin the framework of QCD light-cone sum rules, using the light-cone distribution amplitudes of the
initial-state Λb baryon [1, 2]. The obtained results were found to be in good agreement with experimental data
and other theoretical approaches. In that work, we considered the transition from the ground-state Λb baryon
to the ground-state Λc baryon, corresponding to the spin-parity quantum numbers
1
2
+ →
1
2
+.
Subsequently,
within the same framework, we computed the 1
2
+ →1
2
−process associated with Λb →Λc(2595) and obtained
results consistent with other theoretical predictions. Therefore, in the present work, we aim to further investigate
processes with higher quantum numbers for the final-state baryon, namely
1
2
+ →
3
2
±, corresponding to Λ0
b →
Λc(2860)+/Λc(2625)+.
In this paper, we plan to compute the weak decay form factors and the corresponding semileptonic decay branch-
ing fractions for the Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ and Λc(2625)+ transition processes. The decay channel Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ℓ−νℓ
was first measured by the CDF Collaboration in 2008 [3]. According to the latest update from the Particle Data
Group (PDG), the branching fraction is Br(Λ0

−0.5% [4]. On the theoretical side, calcu-
lations of the Λ0

theory [7, 8], heavy quark spin symmetry [9], QCD light-cone sum rules [10], light-front quark model [11], and
covariant confined quark model [12, 13], as well as studies of new physics effects in this decay channel [14, 15]. The
above experimental and theoretical works provide rich material for our study of weak decays of initial spin- 1
2 heavy
baryons into final spin- 3
2 baryons. However, these investigations have so far mainly focused on the transition from
an initial state with spin-parity 1
2
+ to a final state with spin-parity 3
2
−, while the 1
2
+ →3
2
+ process has received
less attention.
In this work, we will employ QCD light-cone sum rules as the theoretical tool to calculate the weak decay form
factors for Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ and Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+, and then evaluate the semileptonic decay branching fractions of
Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ℓ−νℓand Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ℓ−νℓ. The spin-parity quantum numbers of Λc(2625)+ and Λc(2860)+
are 3
2
−and 3
2

The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. In Sec. 2, we present the basic scheme for calculating
the semileptonic decay branching fractions. In Sec. 3, we compute the weak decay form factors using the QCD
light-cone sum rule method. Numerical analyses are given in Sec. 4. Finally, Sec. 5 contains our summary and
conclusion.

5
Conclusion
Within the framework of QCD light-cone sum rules, we calculate the form factors for the transitions Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+
and Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+. The obtained form factors are then extrapolated to the physical region of the semileptonic
decays Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ℓ−νℓand Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ℓ−νℓ, and we compute their differential decay widths and
branching fractions, as shown in Table 4.
In the calculation of the form factors using QCD light-cone sum rules, we take into account both positive-
and negative-parity states of the final-state spin-3/2 baryon, separate the Lorentz structures associated with the
final-state spin-1/2 baryon, and consider only those of the spin-3/2 state. Our calculated branching fraction for
the semileptonic decay Λ0
b →Λc(2625)+ℓ−νℓis found to be in good agreement with experimental measurements
and other theoretical calculations. Furthermore, we present theoretical predictions for the branching fraction of
Λ0
b →Λc(2860)+ℓ−νℓ, which can serve as a theoretical reference for experimental searches and analyses of this
decay channel.
