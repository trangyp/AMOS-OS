Sharp Estimates for Conjugate Functions with Applications to
Trigonometric Polynomials
Silouanos Brazitikos
Abstract
We prove a sharp estimate for conjugate functions using a harmonic majorant in
a half-strip.
As an application, we remove the logarithmic loss from a theorem of
Papadopoulos on minima of trigonometric polynomials and obtain the optimal order
in the corresponding inequality.
