4. Concluding remarks
In this paper, we obtained two families of the special solutions of the q-Heun
equation which are written as finite summations of the q-hypergeometric functions
by using the q-integral transformation. One is described in Theorem 3.7, where the
parameters satisfy the condition that β is a positive integer and the singularity x = 0
of the q-difference equation is apparent. By replacing the variable x to 1/x, we have
similar solutions to the q-Heun equation of which the parameters satisfy α1 −α2 ∈Z
and the singularity x = ∞is apparent. Another family of the special solutions is
described in Theorem 3.3, where the parameters satisfy the condition that l2 −h2 is a
positive integer and the accessory parameter E satisfies a certain relation. Although
the relation for E is described explicitly, direct interpretation from the structure of
the q-difference equation is not obtained. It might be related to some analogue of
apparent singularities of differential equations other than the points x = 0, ∞, and
we hope to develop a theory in this direction.
