SPECTRAL OBSTRUCTIONS TO CONTRACTING TRANSPORT MAPS
ON CURVED SPACES
SHREY ARYAN
Abstract. Caffarelli’s contraction theorem states that the Brenier optimal transport map from
the standard Gaussian measure to a more log-concave probability measure is 1-Lipschitz. Owing
to its many applications in analysis, probability, and geometry, the problem of extending this
theorem to curved spaces has appeared repeatedly in the literature, going back to Villani [V+09].
More recently, Milman [Mil18] formulated precise conjectures in this direction. In this work, we
construct counterexamples to these conjectures.
1. Introduction
In [Caf00], Caffarelli proved the following theorem:
Theorem 1.1 (cf. Theorem 11 [Caf00]). Let d ≥1, and let γd denote the standard Gaussian
measure on Rd. If µ(dx) = γd(dx) and ν(dx) = e−W(x)γd(dx) are two probability measures
where W is a convex function, then there exists a convex function ϕ : Rd →R such that T = ∇ϕ
is 1-Lipschitz, and transports µ to ν, i.e. T#µ = ν.
The Gaussian space (Rd, |·|, γd) can be regarded as a weighted manifold with synthetic dimension

Although the conjectures above are false in general, several positive generalizations of Caffarelli’s
theorem are known. These include results on infinite-dimensional spaces [F¨U04,MS24], results

Kim and Milman [KM12] used the heat flow to construct Lipschitz transport maps and gave an
alternative proof of Caffarelli’s theorem. Entropic optimal transport has also led to new proofs
and extensions [CP23,FGP20].
