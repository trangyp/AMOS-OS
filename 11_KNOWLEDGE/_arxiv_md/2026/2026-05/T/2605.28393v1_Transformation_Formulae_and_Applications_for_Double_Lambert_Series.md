Abstract. In this paper, we study a class of double Lambert series and establish several
identities and transformation relations for them. These formulae provide useful tools for
reducing certain double Lambert series to single Lambert series. As applications, we derive
identities related to recent conjectures of Andrews, Dixit, Schultz, and Yee, and of Amde-
berhan, Andrews, and Ballantine. We also propose a new proof of a result of Amdeberhan,
Andrews, and Ballantine.
