The formal degree conjecture for groups over local function fields
Anantha Krishna B
Department of Mathematics, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, India
ananthakb@iisc.ac.in
Abstract
In this article, we will prove that the formal degree conjecture is compatible with the Deligne-
Kazhdan correspondence for quasi-split groups, assuming that the local Langlands correspondence is
compatible with the Deligne-Kazhdan correspondence. Consequently, we establish the formal degree
conjecture for GLn over local function fields of characteristic p > 0, and for Sp2n, split SO2n, SO2n+1,
and GSp4 over local function fields of characteristic p > 2.

Theorem 1.1 (Loose version). Let F be a local field with char(F) = p > 0. Then the formal degree
conjecture holds for discrete series representations of following split groups defined over F
• GLn,
• GSp4(p > 2),

For an exact version of the above theorem, see Theorem 9.1. To prove this, we first prove that the
formal degrees of discrete series representations are equal under Kazhdan’s isomorphism whenever the
fields are sufficiently close (Theorem 5.1). We also match the component groups and adjoint gamma
