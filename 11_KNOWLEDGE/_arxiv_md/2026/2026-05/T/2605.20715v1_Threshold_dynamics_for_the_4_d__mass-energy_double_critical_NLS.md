ALEX H. ARDILA, ZUYU MA, JASON MURPHY, AND JIQIANG ZHENG
Abstract. We consider the 4d mass-energy double critical NLS
(i∂t + ∆)u = −|u|2u + |u|u.
In [5,16], the authors established a scattering/blowup dichotomy for solutions
satisfying the energy constraint E(u0) < Ec(W), where W is the energy-critical
NLS ground state and Ec is the energy for the underlying cubic NLS. We prove
that the scattering/blowup dichotomy persists even at the energy threshold
E(u0) = Ec(W).

1.1. Strategy of the proof, part I. The proof of Theorem 1.2 proceeds by
contradiction. In particular, if Theorem 1.2 fails then there exists a non-scattering
solution u satisfying the energy constraints (1.4). Without loss of generality, we
may assume that u fails to scatter forward in time. Using the double-track profile
decomposition from [16] and the sub-threshold scattering result Theorem 1.1, we
can prove that the solution u must be precompact in ˙H1 modulo a time-dependent
frequency scale λ(t) ≥1 and spatial center x(t). We can additionally prove that u
is forward global.
To complete the proof, we would like to show that such a solution cannot
exist. In general, this requires an analysis of the compactness parameters λ(t)
and x(t). For example, in the radial setting one has x(t) ≡0. This simplifies the
problem considerably, as it allows for the direct use of a virial-type estimate (cf. [3]).

general. Nonetheless, there is one situation in which we can effectively control x(t),
