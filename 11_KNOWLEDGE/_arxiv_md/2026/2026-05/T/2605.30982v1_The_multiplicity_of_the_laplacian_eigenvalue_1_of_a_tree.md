Introduction
In this paper, we consider a simple undirected connected graph G. Let V (G) and E(G) denote the vertex set
and edge set of G, respectively. For V1 ⊆V (G) and E1 ⊆E(G), G −V1 denotes the induced subgraph of G
on V (G) \ V1, and G −E1 denotes the graph obtained from G by deleting the edges in E1. For v ∈V (G), let
dv(G) denote the degree of v in G. When no confusion arises, we simply write dv or d(v). In a graph G, d(u, v)
denotes the length of a shortest path between u and v. The diameter of G is d(G) = max{d(u, v) | u, v ∈V }.

In 1985, Faria [2] proved that for any graph G, mG(1) ≥p(G) −q(G), where p(G) denotes the number
of pendant vertices of G and q(G) denotes the number of quasi-pendant vertices of G. Andrada et al. [1] gave
a partial characterization of graphs satisfying mG(1) = p(G) −q(G). Later, Grone et al. [10] proved that for
any tree T, mT (1) ≤p(T) −1. Gupta [3] showed that mT (1) = p(T) −1 if and only if d(u1, u2) ≡2
(mod 3) for any two pendant vertices u1, u2 of T. Subsequently, in 2026, Wong et al. [11] gave a complete
characterization of graphs satisfying mT (λ) = p(T) −1. Guo et al. [9] showed that if T is a tree with n vertices,
then mT (1) ∈S = {0, 1, . . . , n −5, n −4, n −2} and for every k ∈S, there exists a tree T with n vertices
such that mT (1) = k. In 2025, Wang et al. [7] showed that if T is a tree with n vertices and β′(T) ≥2,
then mT (1) ≤n −2β′(T) −1, where β′(T) is the induced matching number of T, and they gave a complete
characterization of extremal graphs. Li et al. [4] proved that mG(1) ≤2c(G) + P(G), where c(G) denotes the
dimension of the cycle space of G, and equality holds if and only if G = Cn with 6 | n.
A graph G is said to be reduced if p(G) = q(G). In 2025, Tian et al. [6] proved that if a tree T is reduced,
then mT (1) ≤n−2
4 , and they gave a complete characterization of extremal graphs, as stated in Theorem 1.2 below.
Subsequently, in 2026, Tian [6] further investigated this problem and proved that if T is reduced and contains
no pendant P3, then mT (1) ≤n−6
4 , and equality holds if and only if T ∼= G1 (see Fig. 1). In this paper, we
continue the study of Tian’s results. We prove that if T is reduced, contains no pendant P3, and T ̸∼= G1, then
mT (1) ≤n−7
