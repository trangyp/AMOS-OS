THE INFLATION FUNCTOR
IN PLURIPOTENTIAL HOMOLOGICAL ALGEBRA
PEDRO MAGALHÃES AND ANNA SOPENA-GILBOY
Abstract. We introduce a functor from cochain complexes to bicomplexes, called infla-
tion functor, which sends quasi-isomorphisms to the class of pluripotential weak equiv-
alences.
We show this functor is part of a Quillen adjunction.
Its right adjoint is a
well-known construction in complex geometry, which gives a sheaf-theoretic presentation
of Bott-Chern and Aeppli cohomologies. The inflation functor plays a key role in pluripo-
tential Koszul duality theory for operads and allows us to construct the ∞-category of
bicomplexes in the pluripotential sense.

Various recent developments in complex geometry are related to the consideration of a

Kähler and non-Kähler settings. This is the subject of several recent works [AT15], [TT17],

The category BiCok of bicomplexes over a field k admits a model structure with pluripo-

of a further structural development of pluripotential homotopy theory. This is a functor

from cochain complexes to bicomplexes, which sends quasi-isomorphisms to pluripotential

By Theorem 1.21 of [Ste25a], the class of pluripotential weak equivalences coincides with
the class of homotopy equivalences. Moreover, there is a cofibrantly generated model struc-
ture on BiCok whose weak equivalences are the pluripotential weak equivalences. The cofi-
brations are the injective morphisms, and the fibrations are the surjective morphisms. We
will refer to this as the pluripotential model structure. The tensor product and internal Hom
endow BiCok with the structure of a symmetric monoidal model category, and hence BiCok
is a BiCok-enriched model category.
Lemma 2.6. Consider on BiCok the pluripotential model structure and on Chk the pro-
jective model structure.
The functor B: BiCok →Chk preserves weak equivalences and
fibrations.
Proof. Corollary 1.25 of [Ste25a] states that if f : A →B is a pluripotential weak equivalence,
then H(f) is an isomorphism for any additive functor H : BiCok →V, where V is an additive
category and H(P) = 0 for every acyclic bicomplex P; that is, HA(P) = HBC(P) = 0 (see
Definition 1.23 in [Ste25a]).
By [Ste25b], the functor H∗◦B, where H∗denotes taking cohomology of a cochain
complex, satisfies this condition. It follows that B preserves weak equivalences. Moreover,
in both model categories BiCok and Chk, fibrations are simply (bi)degree-wise surjections.
