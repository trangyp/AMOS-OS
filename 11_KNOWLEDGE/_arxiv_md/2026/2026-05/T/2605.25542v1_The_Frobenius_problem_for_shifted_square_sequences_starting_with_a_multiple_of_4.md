Abstract
The greatest integer that does not belong to a numerical semigroup S is called
the Frobenius number of S, and finding the Frobenius number is called the
Frobenius problem. In this paper, we solve the Frobenius problem for shifted
square sequences starting with a multiple of 4.
1. Introduction
Let N be the set of nonnegative integers.
To begin, we introduce a
numerical semigroup and submonoid generated by a nonempty subset.
Definition 1.1. A numerical semigroup is a subset S of N that is closed
under addition and contains 0, such that N\S is finite.
