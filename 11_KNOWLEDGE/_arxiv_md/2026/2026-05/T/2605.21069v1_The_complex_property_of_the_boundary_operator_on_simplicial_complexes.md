THE COMPLEX PROPERTY OF THE BOUNDARY OPERATOR
ON SIMPLICIAL COMPLEXES
PHILIPP BARTMANN, MATTHIAS KELLER
Abstract. We study the complex property ∂∂= 0 of the boundary operator
∂on a weighted, infinite, and possibly non-locally finite simplicial complex.
We give a characterization of this property in ℓ2 in terms of the recurrence of
the links of simplices. The complex property is essential to ensure that Hodge
Laplacians ∆H indeed act as δ∂+ ∂δ and to decompose ∆H into a direct sum
of operators acting on k-forms. Furthermore, it allows us to define relative
cohomology classes, show a respective weak Hodge decomposition, and prove
the existence of harmonic Dirichlet eigenforms. We also discuss a transience
property for simplicial complexes, that was introduced by Parzanchevski and
Rosenthal.
1. Introduction
The boundary and coboundary operators ∂and δ are fundamental objects in the
theory of simplicial complexes. The axiomatic complex property of the coboundary
operator says
δδ = 0.
The boundary operator ∂is defined as the formal adjoint of δ and therefore it is
natural to ask if ∂∂= 0 holds as well. This is clearly true for finite simplicial
complexes, but may fail in the infinite setting. Indeed, it was already observed by
Parzanchevski and Rosenthal in [14, Section 3.1] that there may be 1-forms ω such
that
∂∂ω(∅) ̸= 0
whenever the set of vertices is infinite.
They also mention that such an ω can
even be chosen in ℓ2 when the 1-skeleton gives rise to a transient graph such as
3-dimensional Euclidean lattices or regular trees.
This phenomenon was further explored in [3, Example 2.5], where it was shown
that ∂∂̸= 0 can also occur for forms of higher degree whenever the simplicial com-
plex is not locally finite. Clearly, to give meaning to the objects in non-locally
finite settings, one has to consider weights which satisfy a local summability con-
dition similar to the case of graphs, [17, 10]. Strengthening the local summability
condition, it was shown in [3, Lemma 3.2] that ∂∂= 0 holds on ℓ2.
In this note, we explore this phenomenon systematically and characterize the
complex property of the boundary operator ∂, i.e., when
∂∂= 0
holds on ℓ2. We give a characterization in terms of the recurrence of the graphs
arising from links of simplices.
In this sense, we draw a surprising connection
between a probabilistic property of links and a topological property of the simplicial
complex.
The underlying idea is a localization technique in spirit of Garlands
