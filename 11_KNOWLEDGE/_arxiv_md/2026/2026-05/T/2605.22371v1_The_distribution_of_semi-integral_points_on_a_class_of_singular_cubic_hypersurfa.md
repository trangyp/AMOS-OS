Recently, the distribution of semi-integral points defined by conditions on intersection multiplicities with
divisors on integral models has been extensively studied. The distribution of integral points defined by con-
ditions on intersection multiplicities has also been studied in [CLT12a, CLT12b, TBT13, Cho24]. Campana
points are among the most extensively studied classes of semi-integral points. Campana points are among the
most extensively studied classes of semi-integral points. They were introduced by F. Campana [Cam05, Def-
inition 4.1.1] and D. Abramovich [Abr09, Definition 2.4.17], and the Manin’s conjecture for Campana points
is formulated in [PSTVA21, Conjecture 1.1] and [CLTBT26, Conjecture 8.3]. These studies are based on the
notion of Campana orbifolds developed in [Cam04, Cam05, Cam11, Cam15]. The distribution of Campana
points has been studied in [BVV12,VV12,BY21,PSTVA21,Shu22,Str22,Xia22,BBK+24,PS24,CLTBT26].
Related counting problems for geometric Darmon points have been studied in [SS24, AP25].
Geometric
Darmon points were introduced in [MNS24, Definition 2.10] based on ideas arising from H. Darmon’s work
[Dar97] on the generalized Fermat equation. Weak Campana points are defined by more delicate conditions,
and their distribution is studied in [Abr09,AVA18,Str22].
A further generalization of these notions is given by M-points, introduced in [Moe24, Definition 3.12] and
[Moe26a, Definition 3.12]. A conjecture on the distribution of M-points on smooth varieties is formulated
in [Moe26a, Conjecture 1.4].
In [Moe26b, Theorem 3.3], the distribution of M-points on smooth Fano
toric varieties is studied, and the result is shown to agree with [Moe26a, Conjecture 1.4]. As explained in
[Moe26a, § 3.3], by choosing the parameter M appropriately, M-points recover several previously studied
classes of semi-integral points, including Campana points and weak Campana points.

that the asymptotic formula in Theorem 1.1 agrees with [Moe26a, Conjecture 1.4] on the a-invariant and
the b-invariant. In § 4, we prove Theorem 1.1 by refining the method of [LWZ19].
