ABSTRACT
The Large Vector Multiple (LVM) is the relevant gauge multiplet for gauging isometries
acting on both the chiral and the twisted chiral fields in a p2, 2q sigma model. Here we show
that a recently proposed new gauge multiplet is a constrained or partially dualized version
of the LVM. Gauging using this multiplet results in a p2, 2q βγ system interacting with a
sigma model.

Introduction
Geometric quotients may often be interpreted via the introduction of gauge fields in a physics
setting. The simplest example is the symplectic reduction, cf. [1] for a discussion in the con-
text of mechanics.
When the manifold in question admits additional structure (such as
K¨ahler, hyper-K¨ahler, etc.), this is most naturally formulated in the language of supersym-
metry [2, 3] (see [4, §15.4] and [5] for a pedagogical introduction). For example, N “ p2, 2q
supersymmetric sigma models in 2D involving only chiral and anti-chiral fields naturally
correspond to K¨ahler target spaces [6]. However, they do not exhaust all models with this
amount of supersymmetry: one can as well add so-called twisted chiral [7] and semichiral mul-
tiplets [8]. From a geometric standpoint, this means entering the realm of generalized K¨ahler
geometry [9, 10, 11] (the dictionary between the differential geometric and supersymmetric
settings was conclusively set up in [12]; see also the reviews [5, 13]). Important examples
of such geometries are provided by supersymmetric WZNW models [14, 15, 16], as well as
deformations of certain integrable sigma models [17, 18, 19]. In order to facilitate quotients
in this generalized setup new types of vector multiplets were proposed in [20] (including the
non-Abelian versions in [21]). The multiplet relevant for the present paper is the so-called
Large Vector Multiplet (LVM) that is used to gauge symmetries acting simultaneously on
chiral and twisted chiral fields.
Recently, another multiplet has been introduced [19], which leads to some new interesting
quotient constructions. Here we show that this gauge multiplet has three natural interpre-
tations: it is a constrained Large Vector Multiplet (LVM) [20] which can equivalently be
viewed as a partial dual to the LVM (in the sense explained below) and which can further be
interpreted in terms of a particular coupling of a general βγ system to a sigma model [22].
We also describe the relation of the duality proposed in [19] to the usual LVM duality [23].

Summary and outlook
The Large Vector Multiplet that gauges chiral and twisted chiral fields was introduced
in 2007. As is clear from our discussion its uses have still not been fully explored. Above
we discussed partial dualizing or constraining it along with field redefinitions. There are
other similar constructions possible as well as applications, e.g., to quotients. Some novel
applications will be given in the upcoming publication [28], and there are important issues
regarding moment maps in the context of Generalized K¨ahler Geometry (GKG) to be sorted
out. The gauge multiplets for T-duality in GKG are well known [29] as is the gauging using
the LVM. The moment maps needed for a general quotient construction are discussed in the
mathematical literature [30],[31],[32]. Their full implementation in superspace has not been
sufficiently explored at the level of sigma models in general. It is also interesting to elucidate
the role of the restricted LVM for these constructions. Apart from that, there are important
issues regarding the allowed Fayet-Iliopoulos terms, presently under study.
As another example of the use of the partially gauged LVM, it was found in [19] that
in certain cases, where gauging is obstructed in N “ p2, 2q superspace for theories of chiral
fields alone [33], the obstruction may be lifted by introducing auxiliary twisted chiral fields
and moving to the generalized K¨ahler setup instead. It would be useful to develop this
approach in full generality and clarify its implications for geometry.
