THETA FUNCTIONS AND TRANSFORMATIONS OF BILATERAL
BASIC HYPERGEOMETRIC SERIES
NIAN HONG ZHOU
Abstract. We establish new transformation formulas involving theta functions and certain
bilateral basic hypergeometric series.
From these formulas, we construct companion q-
series for a class of q-series such that the asymptotic expansion of their quotient admits a
simple closed form. This allows us to prove several conjectures of McIntosh on asymptotic
transformations of q-series. Moreover, our results extend some identities of Ramanujan and
McIntosh.
