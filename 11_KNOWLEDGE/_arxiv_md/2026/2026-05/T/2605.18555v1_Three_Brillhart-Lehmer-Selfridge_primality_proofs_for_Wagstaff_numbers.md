The F > N1/3 threshold with the discriminant check is BLS’s central
improvement over the classical F > N1/2: instead of needing half the bit-size
of N to be factored, we need only a third. The quantity reported in the
margin column of Table 1 is M := ⌊log2 F 3⌋−⌊log2 N⌋, the integer bit-count
by which F 3 exceeds N in bit-length; M ≥1 already implies F 3 > N. For
the three exponents below, M ∈{46, 3261, 2860}, so the threshold is met
with considerable slack.
