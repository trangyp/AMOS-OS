linear groups over Euclidean number rings

Abstract
For R a Euclidean number ring, let Γnppq be the level-p principal congruence subgroup of
SLnpRq. Borel–Serre showed that the cohomology of Γnppq vanishes above a degree ν that is
quadratic in n. Let K be the fraction field of R, and TnpKq the Tits building of SLnpKq. For
R “ Z, Lee–Szczarba asked when HνpΓnppqq is isomorphic to rHn´2pTnpKq{Γnppqq, which was
answered by Miller–Patzt–Putman. We study a generalized version of Lee–Szczarba’s question.
We prove that for a prime p in a Euclidean number ring R with fraction field K, that a natural
map HνpΓnppqq Ñ rHn´2pTnpKq{Γnppqq is always surjective, and give a sufficent set of conditions
on p P R that guarantee when this map is an isomorphism.

Introduction
The cohomology of arithmetic groups plays a fundamental role in algebraic K-theory and num-
ber theory. Examples of arithmetic groups include special linear groups over a number ring and
their finite-index subgroups. An important class of finite-index subgroups are principal congruence
subgroups, i.e. the kernel ΓnpJq of the map SLnpRq Ñ SLnpR{Jq that reduces coefficients mod J,
where J Ă R is an ideal such that R{J is finite. In this paper, we study the high degree cohomology
of ΓnpJq when R is a Euclidean domain and J “ ppq for p P R a prime.

Steinberg Modules.
In the case of SLnpRq for R a number ring, the dualizing module D has
the following description. For K a field, let TnpKq be the Tits building for SLnpKq, that is, the
simplicial complex whose k-simplices are flags

The Solomon-Tits theorem [Sol69, Bro89] says that TnpKq is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of
spheres of dimension pn ´ 2q. The Steinberg module for SLnpKq, denoted StnpKq, is rHn´2pTnpKqq.
The action of SLnpKq (and its subgroups) on TnpKq descends to an action on StnpKq.
When

finite-index subgroup Γ Ă SLnpRq is StnpKq.
The cohomology groups we are interested in are thus isomorphic to pStnpKqbQqΓnppq, where K
is the field of fractions of a Euclidean number ring R, and p P R is prime. The bQ is unnecessary
if Γnppq Ă SLnpRq is torsion-free.
