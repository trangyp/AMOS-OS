Lemma A.7. Let A and B be two commutative F-algebras. Let ψ : B →A be
an F-algebra morphism, and let V be a finite-dimensional F-vector subspace
of B. Assume that the restriction ψ |V is injective. Let λ be a partition. Then,

Proof. Pick a basis (v1, v2, . . . , vn) of the F-vector space V. Then, the n-tuple
(ψ (v1) , ψ (v2) , . . . , ψ (vn)) is a basis of ψ (V) (since the restriction ψ |V is injec-

We are in one of the following two cases:
Case 1: We have ℓ(λ) > n.
Case 2: We have ℓ(λ) ≤n.

n = dim V. Thus, (4) shows that Sλ (V) = 0. But λ also has length ℓ(λ) > n =
dim (ψ (V)), and therefore (4) shows that Sλ (ψ (V)) = 0. Hence, Lemma A.7
holds in Case 1 (since both Sλ (V) and Sλ (ψ (V)) are 0).

(since (ψ (v1) , ψ (v2) , . . . , ψ (vn)) is a basis of ψ (V)).
