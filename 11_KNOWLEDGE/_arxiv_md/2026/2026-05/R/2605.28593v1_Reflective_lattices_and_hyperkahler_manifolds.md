Ekaterina Amerik1, Andrey Soldatenkov2, Misha Verbitsky3
Abstract: Using the results of Nikulin and Vinberg on the groups of
isometries generated by reflections, we construct a subvariety called the
Nikulin–Vinberg locus in the moduli space of polarized hyperk¨ahler man-
ifolds. It is obtained as a finite union of components of higher Noether–
Lefschetz loci which parameterize manifolds with certain special N´eron–
Severi lattices.
The Nikulin–Vinberg locus is the closure of the set of
hyperk¨ahler manifolds with Picard number ě 3 which have finite groups
of birational automorphisms. Using this construction and a refinement of
an argument by Oguiso, we show that any non-trivial family of projective
deformations of a hyperk¨ahler manifold with b2pMq ě 6 has a dense set
of fibers which have an infinite group of birational automorphisms.

1.2
Moduli spaces of polarized hyperk¨ahler manifolds
By a hyperk¨ahler (also known as irreducible holomorphically symplectic or IHS)
manifold M we will mean a compact simply connected complex 2n-dimensional
manifold that admits a Riemannian metric of holonomy Sppnq, see [Bea], [Ma]
and [Ve] for the definitions and the foundations of the theory of such manifolds.
Let Λ “ H2pM, Zq be the second cohomology of a hyperk¨ahler manifold M
equipped with the Beauville–Bogomolov–Fujiki (BBF) form q. The latter has
signature p3, b2pMq ´ 3q, and we will assume that it is rescaled to be integral
and primitive. All deformations of M that admit K¨ahler metrics will also be
hyperk¨ahler manifolds with the same Λ and q.
From now on we fix a deformation class of hyperk¨ahler manifolds and denote
by M one of the manifolds in this deformation class. The moduli theory of the
deformations of M is governed by the period domains attached to Λ. Let us
recall their definitions.
Given a primitive sublattice L Ă Λ of signature p1, kq, its orthogonal com-
plement LK in Λ has signature p2, b2pMq ´ kq. Let

M. For a very general (that is, lying outside of a countable union of proper
subvarieties) point of DL we have equality L “ NSpMq. We will call DL the
period domain of L-polarized hyperk¨ahler manifolds. Note that the condition
on the signature of L guarantees that all L-polarized hyperk¨ahler manifolds are
projective. In particular, if L “ Zh for some primitive h P Λ with qphq ą 0,
we will write Dh instead of DL and call it the period domain of h-polarized
hyperk¨ahler manifolds. We will denote the orthogonal complement hK in Λ by
