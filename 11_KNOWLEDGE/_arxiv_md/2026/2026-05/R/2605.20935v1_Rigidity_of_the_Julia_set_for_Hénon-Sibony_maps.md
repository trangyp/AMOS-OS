Abstract. Let f and g be two H´enon–Sibony maps of Ck. We show that if they have
the same forward Julia set, then they share a common iterate, thereby extending Lamy’s
results from dimension 2.
Keywords. H´enon–Sibony maps, Julia sets, Green currents
Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 37A25, 37A50, 37F80
1. Introduction
The classification of polynomial automorphisms of C2 of Friedland and Milnor [FM89]
establishes complex generalized H´enon maps as the paradigmatic examples of polynomial
automorphisms with positive entropy. In higher dimensions, however, the structure of
the group of polynomial automorphisms of Ck is significantly more complex, and no such
central family exists. Sibony [Sib99] proposed regular polynomial automorphisms as a
natural generalization of H´enon maps. He demonstrated that their rich dynamics can be
developed (e.g., [Sib99, DS09]) in a manner analogous to that of H´enon maps, which have
been extensively studied over the last thirty years—most notably by Bedford, Lyubich,
and Smillie, as well as Fornæss and Sibony (e.g., [BS91, BLS93, FS92]). We shall refer to
these as H´enon–Sibony maps. Such a map f (resp. f−1) is a polynomial automorphism
of Ck of degree d > 1 (resp. δ > 1) whose extension to Pk possesses an indeterminacy set

In dimension 1, Beardon [Bea90, Bea92] proved that polynomials sharing the same Julia
set must share an iterate (i.e., fn = gm for some n, m ∈N∗), up to an affine symmetry of
the Julia set. In our setting, two H´enon–Sibony maps that share an iterate automatically
have the same Julia sets, the same Green functions, and the same Green measure. This
naturally raises the question of whether the converse holds, namely to what extent these
dynamically defined objects rigidly determine the map.
In dimension 2, this question was addressed by Lamy in [Lam01], where he shows that
if two H´enon maps share the same forward Julia set, then they must share an iterate. As a
consequence, Dujardin and Favre [DF17] proved that the same conclusion already follows
from the equality of the Green measure. Since then, this rigidity phenomenon has been
revisited and strengthened in several directions, notably by Bera, Pal, and Verma [BPV20,
Ber21]. In higher dimension, related questions have also been investigated by Bera and
Pal for polynomial shift-like maps [BP22].
Both Lamy’s and Bera, Pal, and Verma’s proofs rely on Jung’s theorem [Jun42], ei-
ther directly or through the lens of Bass-Serre theory [Ser77] and the Friedland–Milnor
classification [FM89]. In the two-dimensional case, Bera, Pal, and Verma also rely on
the B¨ottcher coordinate introduced by Hubbard and Oberste-Vorth [HOV94]. However,
in higher dimensions, a simple classification of automorphisms is unavailable, even for
H´enon–Sibony maps; furthermore, the Green function fails to be pluriharmonic on the
basin of attraction of I−

maps, providing a novel proof for the classical H´enon case. Our main result is as follows.
