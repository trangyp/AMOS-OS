Introduction
The Stone-ˇCech Compactification of the discrete space N denoted by βN
is the space of all the ultrafilters on N. Collection of the sets of the form
bA = {u ∈βN : A ∈u} forms a basis for a topology on βN . Each n ∈N
is identified with the ultrafilter un = {A ⊂N : x ∈A}. The usual addition
and multiplication on N can be extended to βN; with these operations,βN
forms a compact Hausdorff right topological semigroup and the study of βN
as a compact Hausdorff right topological semigroup has proven to be a fer-
tile ground for translating classical problems of elementary number theory
in the realm of topological algebra. Central to this program is the extension
