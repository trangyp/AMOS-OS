1. Introduction
Shifted quantum affine algebras were introduced by Finkelberg and Tsymbaliuk in their
study of quantized K-theoretic Coulomb branches of three-dimensional N = 4 supersymmet-
ric quiver gauge theories [FT19]. Since their introduction, shifted quantum affine algebras
have appeared in a number of related contexts, such as representation theory, Hall algebras,
supersymmetric gauge theory, and cluster algebra theory.
In particular, various aspects
of their representation theory, as well as their connections with Hall algebras and cluster
algebras, have been investigated in [HZ25, Pin25, GS25, Bou23, GHL24].
Shifted quantum affine algebras form a family of variants of quantum loop algebras in
Drinfeld’s current realization [FT19]. More precisely, if g is a finite-dimensional simple Lie
algebra and µ is an integral coweight, the shifted quantum affine algebra Uµ
q (ˆg) is obtained
from the Drinfeld presentation of the quantum loop algebra Uq(Lg) by allowing the Drinfeld
currents to have prescribed orders of zero or pole determined by the integers αi(µ). Thus
the shift changes the asymptotic behavior of the Drinfeld currents ϕ±
i (z) and leads to a
representation theory which is close to, but genuinely different from, the usual representation
theory of quantum affine algebras [Her23].
The representation theory of shifted quantum affine algebras was developed by Hernandez
in [Her23]. In that work, the category Oµ of representations of Uµ
q (ˆg) were introduced, simple
objects were classified by rational ℓ-weights of prescribed degrees, and a fusion product was
constructed by means of a deformed Drinfeld coproduct. This gives a ring structure on the
Grothendieck group of the shifted category Osh = L
µ Oµ .
The purpose of the present paper is to develop representations theory for shifted twisted
quantum affine algebras. Let g be a simply-laced simple Lie algebra of type A2n (n ≥2),
A2n−1 (n ≥3), Dn+1 (n ≥2), or E6, and let σ be a non-trivial Dynkin diagram automorphism
of order M.
We say that g is of type (Xm, M) if σ has order M.
The corresponding
twisted affine Lie algebra is denoted by ˆgσ.
The twisted quantum affine algebra Uq(ˆgσ)
was introduced independently by Drinfeld and Jimbo [Dri88, Jim85]. Its finite-dimensional
simple representations were classified by Chari and Pressley in terms of Drinfeld polynomials
[CP98]. The Drinfeld new realization of twisted quantum affine algebras was studied in
[Dri88, Dam12, Dam15, Jin98, JM99]. The theory of q-characters and Kirillov–Reshetikhin
modules in the twisted case was developed by Hernandez [Her10], and the category Obσ for
twisted quantum affine Borel algebras was studied by Wang [Wan23]. These works show that
many structures from the untwisted theory have twisted counterparts, although the diagram
automorphism produces additional features in the current relations, the Serre relations, and
the form of the ℓ-weights.
