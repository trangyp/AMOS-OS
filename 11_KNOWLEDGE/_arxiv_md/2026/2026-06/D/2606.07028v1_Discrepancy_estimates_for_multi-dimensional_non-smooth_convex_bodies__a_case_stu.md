is due to Bruna, Nagel, and Wainger [9]. It provides decay estimates for c

particular, they do not yield matching lower bounds for c

decay rates in a distinguished set of directions and in its complement, and we show that this
anisotropic behavior leads to matching bounds for the discrepancy.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2 we establish our main abstract discrepancy
estimates, proving Theorem 1.2, Theorem 1.4, and Theorem 1.5. In Section 3 we test these
results on a particular domain, which we call the barrel, emphasizing both their effectiveness and
limitations. More precisely, we state suitable estimates for the Fourier transform of the barrel
which allow us to apply the abstract results of Section 2 and derive the corresponding discrepancy
bounds. Section 4 contains several auxiliary results on oscillatory integrals and Fresnel-type
functions, which are used in Section 5 to derive asymptotic formulas for the Fourier transform
of the barrel. These asymptotics are then combined in Section 6 to prove the Fourier estimates
stated in Section 3.
Throughout the paper, we will use the notation A ≲B when there exists a constant c > 0
independent of the relevant parameters, such that A ⩽cB. We will write A ≈B when A ≲B
and B ≲A. Throughout the paper, constants will be denoted by c. As usual, the value of c may
change from line to line.
