EFFECTIVE QUASIANALYTIC REMEZ INEQUALITIES
ON TAME SETS
ARMIN RAINER
Abstract. We establish a Remez inequality for functions in quasianalytic
Denjoy–Carleman classes CM on a large family of fat compact sets K ⊆Rn
with tame geometry, including all fat compact sets definable in the o-minimal
expansion of R by restricted CM functions.
The inequality generalizes the
classical Remez inequality for polynomials and its quasianalytic versions on
convex bodies, replacing the polynomial degree by the Bang degree, an integer
associated with the weight M and the size of the function.
The constants
depend explicitly on the Bang degree and the geometry of K.
We derive
a range of quantitative consequences: estimates for the volume of sublevel
sets, comparison of Lp-norms, effective inequalities of  Lojasiewicz, Harnack,
and Markov type with explicit constants, and decay estimates for oscillatory
integrals.

The Remez inequality has been extended to various classes of functions (e.g. an-
alytic [Bru99b], plurisubharmonic [Bru99a], and holomorphic [Bru08]), generally,
in the univariate or convex multivariate case. Still in the convex setting, [Yom11]
showed that, in the Remez inequality for polynomials, the Lebesgue measure can
be replaced by a certain geometric invariant which may be nonzero for discrete or
even finite sets E; see also [BY15].
In this paper, we focus on quasianalytic Denjoy–Carleman classes CM, that is,
classes of smooth functions whose sequence of derivatives is bounded by a given
positive sequence M = (Mj)j≥0 with suitable properties.
Nazarov, Sodin, and Volberg [NSV04] proved a univariate Remez inequality for
functions f of class CM, where the so-called Bang degree replaces the polynomial
degree. The Bang degree is a nonnegative integer which can be computed from the
sequence M and which depends on the size of f as a parameter. This number was
introduced by Bang [Ban53] and named Bang degree by [NSV04]. A multivariate
Remez inequality for functions f of class CM on convex bodies K ⊆Rn was obtained
in [Rai23], where also effective bounds on the zero sets were proved.
The main goal of this paper is to establish a Remez inequality for functions f of
class CM on a large family of compact sets K ⊆Rn with “tame geometry”. In
addition, we develop several analytic and geometric consequences of this Remez
inequality. The family of admissible sets K (the same as the one used by [Pie15])
includes all fat compact sets that are definable in RCM , the o-minimal expansion
of the real ordered field by all restricted functions of class CM (assuming that

We recall the definition of an o-minimal structure over the real ordered field and

Definition 3.1 (O-minimal structure over the real ordered field). A structure over
the real ordered field (R, +, ·, <) is a sequence S = (Sn)n≥1 of collections of subsets
Sn of Rn, such that for all n, n′ ≥1:
(1) Sn is a Boolean algebra with respect to the usual set-theoretic operations;
(2) Sn contains all semialgebraic subsets of Rn;
(3) if X ∈Sn and X′ ∈Sn′, then X × X′ ∈Sn+n′;
(4) if n ≥n′ and X ∈Sn, then π(X) ∈Sn′, where π : Rn →Rn′ is the
projection on the first n′ coordinates.
