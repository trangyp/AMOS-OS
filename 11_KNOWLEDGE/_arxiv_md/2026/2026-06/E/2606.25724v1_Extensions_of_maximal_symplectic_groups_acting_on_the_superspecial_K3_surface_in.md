EXTENSIONS OF MAXIMAL SYMPLECTIC GROUPS ACTING ON
THE SUPERSPECIAL K3 SURFACE IN CHARACTERISTIC p
SOPHIE E. FRIESEN
Abstract. We classify all possible finite groups acting faithfully on the superspecial
K3 surface which contain the maximal symplectic groups, as classified by Ohashi and
Sch¨utt.
1. Introduction
Automorphism groups of K3 surfaces have been an active area of research for the
last decades. Starting with foundational work by Nikulin [Nik79], a central question
was the following:
Question. Let G be a finite group. Is there a K3 surface X over an algebraically
closed field k such that G embeds into Aut(X)?
In order to study finite groups of automorphisms of K3 surfaces, it is helpful to first
focus on symplectic automorphisms, i.e. automorphisms that leave a nowhere vanishing
two-form invariant. Let X be a K3 surface. We denote the group of symplectic auto-
morphisms as Auts(X). The symplectic automorphisms contained in G ⊂Aut(X) form
a normal subgroup, denoted by Gs. A finite symplectic group Gs ⊂Auts(X) is called
maximal if there is no finite subgroup G′
s such that Gs ⊊G′
s ⊊Auts(X). A finite group
G whose symplectic subgroup is Gs is called an extension of Gs and [G : Gs] is called
the non-symplectic index of G (cf. [Jan19]). An extension is non-trivial if [G : Gs] ≥2.
Over the complex numbers, Mukai [Muk88] classifies all the possible finite groups which
can occur as Gs. This has been extended to a classification of the finite groups with sat-
urated symplectic subgroups by Brandhorst, Hashimoto and Hofmann [BH21][BH23].
These classifications need not hold over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic
p due to the existence of supersingular K3 surfaces, as has been noted by Dolgachev
and Keum [DK09]. Indeed, the symplectic automorphism group of these K3 surfaces
can contain finite subgroups that are not realized over the complex numbers, see for
instance [OS24, Theorem 1.1]. The following is the main result of this paper.
Theorem 1.1. Let X be a K3 surface over an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p > 11. Assume that G ⊂Aut(X) is a finite group such that Gs does not appear over
the complex numbers (i.e. cannot be found in the classification of Mukai [Muk88]). Then
G = Gs.
This result is in contrast to the complex case, where each maximal group has an action
on some algebraic complex singular K3 surface that possesses a non-trivial extension
[BH21]. The proof of Theorem 1.1 will be discussed in Section 4.3. An important input
for this proof will be Theorem 1.2(i) as well as the results of [OS24].

3. Actions on supersingular K3 surfaces
3.1. Known results in positive characteristic. Let X be a supersingular K3 sur-
face of Artin invariant σ in characteristic p > 0. The following result by Nygaard gave
a condition on the non-symplectic index of the full automorphism group.
Theorem 3.1 ([Nyg80]). Let X be a supersingular K3 surface of Artin invariant σ
and Aut(X) the automorphism group of X. Then the non-symplectic index [Aut(X) :
Auts(X)] divides pσ + 1.
This result was further improved by Jang in [Jan19] for p ≥5 by giving a description
