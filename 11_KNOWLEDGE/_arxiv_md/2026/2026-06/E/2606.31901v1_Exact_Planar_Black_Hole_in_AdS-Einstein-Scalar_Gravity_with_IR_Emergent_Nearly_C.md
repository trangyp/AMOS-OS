Exact Planar Black Hole in AdS-Einstein-Scalar
Gravity with IR Emergent Nearly Conformal Fluid
Sangheon Yun
IndigoWave, Center for Quantum Spacetime, Sogang University,
35 Baekbeom-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul 04107, KOREA
Abstract
We study an exact analytic solution describing a static plane-
symmetric hairy black brane in four-dimensional Einstein gravity min-
imally coupled to a neutral scalar, arising as a consistent truncation of
the type IIA supergravity whose low-energy limit captures the strongly
coupled thermal dynamics of the ABJM theory. The solution is char-
acterized by two independent parameters. We perform the thermo-
dynamic description by treating the scalar hair parameter as an inde-
pendent variable, deriving the generalized first law and verifying the
Euler relation. The UV boundary theory is a three-dimensional QFT
at finite temperature deformed by a marginally relevant scalar opera-
tor with logarithmic RG flow. The boundary theory exhibits explicit
scale-symmetry breaking at high energies but recovers the behavior of
a conformal fluid in the infrared thermal limit.
Keywords: scalar-hairy black brane; two-parameter thermodynamics; log-
arithmic holographic RG flow; nearly conformal holographic hydrodynamics.

Introduction
The gauge/gravity duality [1, 2, 3] provides a powerful framework for study-
ing strongly coupled field theories through their dual gravitational descrip-
tions.
The compactification of higher-dimensional supergravities down to
four dimensions further allows one to investigate the full effects of the strongly
coupled regime through the resulting low-energy effective theory. The four-
dimensional low-energy effective action was obtained in [4] from the eleven-
dimensional supergravity and also in [5] from the type IIA supergravity to
study the strongly coupled thermal dynamics of the ABJM theory [6], yield-
ing a theory with several scalar and gauge fields. In this note, we focus on a
minimal truncation of that action: Einstein gravity minimally coupled to a
single neutral scalar ϕ+. Our solution may therefore be viewed as a minimal
holographic model of thermal ABJM dynamics, in which the most relevant
thermal mode under the neutral truncation condenses.
An exact static planar hairy black brane solution of this system was
obtained in [7] and its thermodynamics analyzed in [8], where conformal
invariance was also observed to be restored in the infrared. The potential and
exact solution of [7, 8] were derived in a purely gravitational context, without
reference to any string or M-theory embedding; in particular, no connection
to the ABJM theory was made in those works.1 The identification of this
model as a consistent truncation of type IIA supergravity on AdS4×CP3 dual
to ABJM theory was first established in [5]. The present paper is therefore
the first to combine these two lines of work: recognizing the solution of [7] as
a thermal solution within the ABJM-dual gravity theory, and exploiting that
origin to extend the thermodynamic analysis. The present paper extends the
analysis of [8] in three respects, all stemming from the ABJM origin of the
theory.
First, the hair parameter α is given a genuine thermodynamic status,
rather than being treated as a fixed label on the solution. In [8], α was elim-
inated in favor of m from the outset, so that every thermodynamic quantity
was expressed through the single parameter m; this hides the fact that m and
α are varied independently by independent physical processes, namely chang-
ing the horizon size and changing the scalar condensate. Once α is restored
as an independent variable, its conjugate quantity turns out to depend on α

These three thermodynamic results have a direct counterpart on the hy-

One of the most fruitful applications of
gauge/gravity duality is holographic hydrodynamics [14, 15], in which the col-
lective low-energy excitations of a strongly coupled gauge theory are treated
as a relativistic fluid living on the boundary of an asymptotically AdS space-
time. In the simplest cases, such as N = 4 super-Yang-Mills theory, the
boundary theory possesses exact scale symmetry, and the dual fluid is au-
tomatically conformal: its bulk viscosity vanishes and its equation of state
is fixed by tracelessness of the stress tensor. However, physical systems of-
ten deviate from perfect scale invariance. In many realistic field theories,
scale symmetry is explicitly broken by the presence of dimensionful cou-
plings or the logarithmic running of coupling constants. Understanding how
these non-conformal UV features evolve into IR hydrodynamic properties re-
mains a central question [16]. In this paper, we investigate this transition
by analyzing our exact four-dimensional planar black hole solution within an
AdS-Einstein-scalar system. Our solution exhibits a non-conformal UV limit,
driven by a neutral scalar field ϕ+ that runs logarithmically. Remarkably, we
find that as the theory flows toward the IR, the thermal state represented
by the black hole horizon screens the non-conformal UV physics: despite the
broken conformal symmetry in the fundamental action, the resulting holo-
graphic hydrodynamics manifests a perfectly conformal equation of state.
The mechanism for this IR conformality is precisely the one identified in our
second point above: ϕ+(rh) depends on Ct but not on S, so that ζ = 0 and
c2
s = 1/2 hold regardless of how much scalar hair the brane carries. The two
characterizations are complementary rather than contradictory: the theory
is only nearly conformal in that its UV is genuinely deformed by the running
scalar and the full RG trajectory is non-conformal, yet the IR thermal sector
is exactly conformal, in the sense that every hydrodynamic transport ob-
servable (c2
s, ζ, η/s) and the equation of state take precisely their conformal
values.

Discussion
In this paper, we have studied an exact static planar hairy black brane in
Einstein gravity minimally coupled to a neutral scalar, arising as a consis-
tent truncation of the type IIA supergravity. The original four-dimensional
effective action [4] was derived to study the thermal dynamics of the ABJM
theory [5], and our solution provides a minimal holographic model of thermal
ABJM dynamics in which the most relevant thermal mode under the neutral
truncation condenses.
Although the exact solution was obtained previously in [7, 8], the present
work makes three new contributions, all rooted in the ABJM origin of the
theory.
We treat α as a second independent thermodynamic variable, yielding
the generalized first law dM = T dS + µ dCt with a thermodynamic central
charge Ct in direct analogy with the ABJM central charge. The Euler rela-
tion M = TS+µCt and the Smarr relation 3M = 2TS are then both satisfied
identically, a consistency absent from the single-parameter description of [8].
This two-parameter framework is analogous in spirit to the holographic CFT
thermodynamics recently developed in [12, 13, 28, 29], where the central
charge plays the role of a second thermodynamic variable alongside entropy,
though our Ct has a distinct physical origin rooted in the ABJM scalar trun-
cation rather than the variation of Newton’s constant.
We identify l Ct as the thermodynamic volume of the system via v = l Ct
and P = −µ/l > 0 (eq. (19)), the unique choice for which v depends on
the scalar hair alone rather than on the horizon size, since l is the only
dimensionful coupling in the bulk action. This shows that the generalized
15


## Page 17


first law dM = T dS + µ dCt is equivalent to dM = T dS −P dv, with
the equation of state P = ε/2 holding identically as a property of the two-
parameter structure itself.
We explain the emergent IR conformality observed in [8]: the indepen-
dence ∂ϕ+(rh)/∂S|Ct = 0 forces ζ = 0 via the Eling–Oz formula [9], while
the Smarr relation forces c2
s = 1/2 [10]. Both results hold for all α and are
invisible in single-parameter analyses. As shown in Section 4, both values are
reproduced by an independent route – the caloric equation of state S ∝T 2
together with the universal KSS viscosity bound – giving two structurally un-
related derivations of the same IR conformal fluid. The non-conformal UV
to conformal IR transition observed here also provides a concrete example of
the more general phenomenon studied in [25, 30].
As a further consistency check, we have numerically computed quasinor-
mal mode frequencies [30, 31] for the scalar δϕ+, tensor hxy, vector htx and
hty, and scalar-gravity coupled perturbations across a wide range of momenta
k and hair parameters α. In all cases we find Im(ω) < 0, confirming the ab-
sence of dynamical instabilities. A comprehensive quasinormal mode analysis
is left for future work.
In the limit α →∞at fixed m > 0, T →0 while S diverges; the diverging
scalar field signals the need for stringy corrections in this regime. The solu-
tion admits an extremal limit m →0, α →∞with m2/3eα/2 fixed, achieving
T →0 with finite residual entropy S0, suggesting a holographic dual with
macroscopic ground-state degeneracy and possibly indicating topological or-
der or a quantum critical phase.
Our boundary system may be viewed as an effective low-energy descrip-
tion of thermal ABJM theory in which only the most relevant neutral thermal
mode survives. The deformation parameter α controls the scalar condensate;
α = 0 corresponds to the undeformed ABJM thermal plasma (ϕ+ = 0, pure
AdS4-Schwarzschild), while α > 0 introduces a running scalar that breaks
conformal invariance in the UV while leaving all conformal hydrodynamic
observables (c2
s, ζ/η, η/s) unchanged—a concrete realization of UV-broken
but IR-restored conformality in the ABJM context. The precise identifica-
tion of the dual operator Oϕ within the full ABJM Kaluza–Klein spectrum
[4, 5] remains an interesting open problem.
