EXPLICIT BOUNDS FOR DICKMAN’S FUNCTION
ANDREAS WEINGARTNER
Abstract. We establish numerically explicit upper and lower bounds
for Dickman’s function ρ(u), resulting in estimates with a relative error
of less than 0.005/u2 for all u ≥5. This allows for an approximate eval-
uation of ρ(u) without the need to solve the delay differential equation
numerically.

prime factor does not exceed n1/u [3].
