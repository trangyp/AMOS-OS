Equivariant compactifications, trivial embeddability and finite
type
Alexandru Chirvasitu
Abstract
We characterize finite-type G-principal U-equivariant bundles on normal U-spaces for com-
pact Lie groups U and G, in several ways, including (a) their extensibility across the U-
equivariant compactification βUX and (b) their becoming finite-type upon extending the struc-
ture group along at least one U-equivariant compact-Lie-group embedding G ≤K. This general-
izes non-equivariant results of Phillips and the author’s characterizing finite-type matrix-algebra
bundles, upon specializing G to projective unitary groups.
When the U-action on X has virtually abelian isotropy, matrix-algebra equivariant bundles
are also finite-type precisely when, locally over a finite open U-cover, they are tensor factors of
trivial matrix bundles. In a K-theoretic offshoot we prove that for U-actions with finite isotropy
groups on compact Hausdorff spaces X equivariant vector bundles E →X are factors of trivial
bundles K-theoretically: there is a class a ∈KU(X) with [E]a the class of a bundle induced
by a U-representation (which furthermore can be chosen so as to restrict to isotropy groups to
multiples of the regular representations). This generalizes a result of Donovan and Karoubi.
Key words: C∗bundle; equivariant bundle; equivariant compactification; finite type; local object;
locally trivial; principal bundle; structure group

Introduction
The paper is concerned with U-equivariant bundles (or bundlesU, following the language of [9,
Definition 0.1]) in the sense of [36, SSI.8, I.9]: bundles E −→X over a base space, either G-principal
for a topological (virtually exclusively compact Lie) group G or vector/algebra/C∗-algebra/etc.,
with all maps and structures involved equivariant with respect to U-actions by the topological
(again always compact Lie) group U. The notion is thus broader than, say, [25, §1] (where the
action U ⟳G is trivial) and narrower than [26, pp.265-266] (or [31, §VII.1]).
Recall [36, Definition I.9.1] that finite-type bundles are those trivializable over finite open covers
(by invariant open subsets if working equivariantly). Theorem 0.1 is in large part motivated by

Trivial embeddability and neutral actions
In addition to the (essentially two) alternative characterizations of the finite-type property touched
upon by Theorem 0.1, involving respectively extensibility along compactifications and finite type
after structure-group extension, [32, Proposition 2.9] also equates finite type, for matrix bundles
A −→→X, with unital embeddability into trivial matrix bundles. This has [32, Lemma 2.7] as an
auxiliary, building on [14, Lemma 12] in the manner sketched in Remark 1.4(2).
With a view towards extending such results equivariantly, we record here how trivializability
of equivariant bundles by tensoring is not only sufficient, but also necessary for the algebra-bundle

Isolating the properties marked out by Theorem 2.1 will provide streamlined language.
Definition 2.2
(1) An action α : U ⟳X of a compact (virtually always assumed Lie) group
on a normal space is neutral if all finite-type bundlesU on X can be neutralized by tensoring with
other (non-zero) such. Equivalently (Theorem 0.1(a) ⇔(c)), the induced action U ⟳βUX on the
universal equivariant compactification is neutral.
(2) α is locally neutral if X admits a finite cover by U-invariant open sets over which the action
is neutral in the sense of (1).
The notions and language effectively quantify neutrality universally over bundles, having fixed
the underlying action. One can also quantify over actions.
(3) U itself is (locally) neutral if all of its actions on normal (equivalently, compact Hausdorff)
spaces are. More generally, U is (locally) X-neutral for a class X of spaces if its actions on spaces in
X are all (respectively locally) neutral. The same language applies if X is a class of actions rather
than spaces.
