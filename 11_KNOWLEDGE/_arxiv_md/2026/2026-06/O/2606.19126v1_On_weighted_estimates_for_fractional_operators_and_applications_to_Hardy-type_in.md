properties of Muckenhoupt weights. In Section 3 we identify the largest rearrangement-
invariant space Dα so that the fractional maximal operator Mα : Dα →L∞(Rn) is
bounded. This boundedness property is crucial to the proof of Theorem 1.2. In Section 4
we prove our main results Theorems 1.1 and 1.2. In Section 5 we construct examples
of weight pairs (u, v) satisfying (1.4) where u takes the form u = M(g)Θ(α)q/nvq/p with

some extensions of the classical Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg inequalities (see Theorem 6.5
and Corollary 6.6).
