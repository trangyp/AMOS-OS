Abstract. Myroshnychenko, Tatarko, and Yaskin constructed a body K in Rn,
n ≥5, with the property that there is exactly one hyperplane H passing through
c(K), the centroid of K, such that the centroid of K ∩H coincides with c(K). This
construction provided answers to questions of Gr¨unbaum and Loewner for n ≥5,
which are still open in dimensions 3 and 4. We study analogues of these questions
in the settings of hyperbolic space Hn and s-concave functions on Rn.
