ON CHAIN RULE AND RENORMALIZATION
ROMAN V. DRIBAS AND NIKOLAY A. GUSEV
Abstract. We discuss the relationship between the chain rule for the divergence operator and the renor-
malization property for weak solutions of the continuity equation. We construct an example of bounded
divergence-free vector field on the plane, which demonstrates that in general the first property is not sufficient
for the second one.
Keywords: renormalized solutions, continuity equation, chain rule property, disintegration theorem

1. Introduction
Renormalization property was introduced by R.J. DiPerna and P.-L. Lions [DL89] in connection with
the problem of uniqueness of weak solutions of the continuity (or transport) equation with non-smooth
vector field (in the context of hyperbolic conservation laws a very close concept was introduced earlier by
S.N. Kruzkov [Kru70]). Sufficient conditions, under which the renormalization property holds, were obtained
in [DL89, Amb04, ABC14, BB19] and many other papers. (Note that uniqueness of weak solutions can also
be studied by methods which do not involve the renormalization property, see e.g. [CLR03, ABC14, Pan08,
Pan15, Pan18, Pan25].) Using the same techniques, under certain assumptions on the vector field v and
on the scalar field ρ: Rd →R for any β ∈C1(R) with β(0) = 0 and β′ ∈L∞(R) one can characterize the
distribution div(β(ρ)v) in terms of the distributions div(ρv) and div v. For instance, in the smooth setting
we have
div(β(ρ)v) = (β(ρ) −ρβ′(ρ)) div v + β′(ρ) div(ρv).

1.3. Main result. For d = 2 the homogeneous chain rule property holds for any bounded measurable
compactly supported divergence-free vector field v: R2 →R2 by [BG16, Theorem 7.1], see also [GK24,
Theorem 1.4] for a generalization of this result. But the homogeneous renormalization property is equivalent
to the weak Sard property, and in [ABC13, Section 4] there was constructed an example of Lipschitz function
which does not have the weak Sard property. Hence the homogeneous chain rule property is always strictly
weaker than the homogeneous renormalization property.
In this work we prove that even non-homogeneous chain rule property is not sufficient for the homogeneous
renormalization property:
Theorem 1.7. Let d = 2. Then there exists a compactly supported vector field v: R2 →R2 such that v has
the non-homogeneous chain rule property, but does not have the homogeneous renormalization property.
In order to obtain the desired vector field v in Section 3 we construct its stream function f in such a
way that f does not have the weak Sard property. By Theorem 1.5 it follows that v does not have the
homogeneous renormalization property.
Our construction of f is similar to the one introduced in [ABC14, Section 4], but in our modification we
ensure that the disintegration of the Lebesgue measure (restricted to the critical set of f) under f is such
that the non-homogeneous chain rule property holds, unlike in the original example from [ABC14]. The
proof of the non-homogeneous chain rule property is given in Section 4, see also Lemma 2.5.
