ON STRONG ALGEBRABILITY AND SPACEABILITY OF
CONTINUOUS FUNCTIONS AND FRACTAL DIMENSIONS
JIA LIU1 AND SAISAI SHI2
Abstract. In this paper, we investigate the strong algebrability and (α, β)-
lineability/spaceability of continuous functions with prescribed fractal dimen-
sions. For 1 < s < r < t ≤2, we define
Hs[0, 1] = {f ∈C[0, 1] : dimHGf([0, 1]) = s},
Br[0, 1] = {f ∈C[0, 1] : dimBGf([0, 1]) = r}
and
Bt[0, 1] = {f ∈C[0, 1] : dimBGf([0, 1]) = t}.
We prove that Hs[0, 1] ∩Br[0, 1] ∩Bt[0, 1] is both strongly c-algebrable and
spaceable. This complements recent findings of Bonilla et al. [24], Esser et al.
[36], and Liu et al. [52].
We prove that for any 1 < s ≤t ≤2, Hs[0, 1] ∩Bt[0, 1] is (p, c)-spaceable
for p = 1, 2. We also prove that Hs[0, 1] ∩Bt[0, 1] is (n, m + n)-lineable for
any m, n ∈N, thus complementing the recent work of Liu et al. [50].
Keywords. Lineability, Spaceability, Algebrability, Hausdorff dimension, Lower
box dimension, Upper box dimension, Continuous functions

1. Introduction
In the present paper, we investigate the algebraic genericity of continuous func-
tions, with a focus on their behavior with respect to fractal dimensional prop-
erties.
Lineability, spaceability and algebrability, three key notions for charac-
terizing algebraic genericity, were originally coined by V. I. Gurariy and they
first appeared in [7, 8, 16, 47, 58].
Since those concepts were put forward, re-
search in this field has quickly attracted the attention of numerous scholars. The
properties of lineability, spaceability and algebrability are investigated in several
contexts with interesting applications in many fields such as Real and Complex
Analysis [2, 6, 7, 18, 19, 34, 58], Series Theory [1, 5, 13, 21, 15], Linear Dynam-
ics [20], Operator Theory [26, 27, 54], Classical Banach Sequence Space Theory
[11, 12, 25, 19, 29, 48], Measure and Probability Theory [14, 32, 41, 56], Set The-
ory [43], ODE [11] as well as Fractal Geometry [24, 28, 50, 52] and Multi-Fractal
Analysis [35]. With further research in recent years, a series of related stronger
concepts have been successively introduced, such as strong algebrability [12], (α, β)-
lineability/spaceability [40] and pointwise lineability/spaceability [55], etc.

Over the last thirty years, numerous authors have extensively studied the patho-
logical real-valued functions [4, 6, 7, 8, 22, 23, 24, 30, 31, 34, 42, 44, 45]. Early
breakthroughs include Gurariy’s proof that the set of continuous nowhere differ-
entiable functions on [0, 1] is lineable [46].
Fonf, Gurariy and Kadets [42] later
established their spaceability in C[0, 1], while Enflo et al. [34] proved that for every
infinite dimensional closed subspace X of C[0, 1], the set of functions in X having
infinitely many zeros in [0, 1] is spaceable in X. The vast literature on this subject
has been built during the last two decades; for a more detailed and comprehensive
treatment, we refer to the survey paper [23] or the monograph [4].
In the study of algebraic genericity for the fractal dimensions of graphs of con-
tinuous functions, Bonilla et al. [24] proved that for a given s ∈(1, 2], the set
of continuous functions whose graph has both Hausdorff and box dimensions s is
maximal-dense-lineable in C[0, 1] and that the set of functions whose graph has
Hausdorff dimension s is spaceable. Recently, by addressing two open questions
raised by Bonilla et al. [24, Question 2.1 and Question 3.4], Liu et al. [52] proved
that given s ∈(1, 2], the set of functions f ∈C[0, 1] whose graph has both box and
Hausdorff dimensions s everywhere in [0, 1] is maximal-dense-lineable and dense-
algebrable. They raised the following question [52, Question 5].
Question 1.1. Let dim be one of dimH, dimB and dimB. Let α, β be two cardinal
numbers with 1 < α < β. Given s ∈(1, 2], is it possible to obtian the pointwise
spaceability or (α, β)-lineability/spaceability of the set of functions f ∈C[0, 1] with
dimGf([0, 1]) = s?
Very recently, Liu et al. [50] partially answered the above question. They proved
that given s ∈(1, 2], the set of continuous functions whose graph has Hausdorff
dimension s is (p, c)-spaceable for p = 1, 2 and (n, n + m)-lineable for any m, n ∈
N. They also proved that the set of continuous functions whose graph has upper
box dimension s is (α, c)-spaceable if and only if α < ℵ0. Besides, they briefly
discussed the (α, β)-lineability of functions whose graph has both Hausdorff and
box dimension s. Notably, C. Esser et al. [36] proved that the set of functions
f ∈C[0, 1] with dimHGf([0, 1]) = s ∈(1, 2] is strongly c-algebrable.
