ON BOUNDS OF EXTENSION DEGREES FOR SIMILARITY OF
INTEGRAL MATRICES OVER NUMBER FIELDS
MINGQIANG FENG AND ZIYANG ZHU
Abstract. It is well-known that if n × n integral matrices A and B of a number field K
are similar over all completions of the ring of integers of K, then A and B are similar over
the ring of integers of a finite extension of K. We prove that there is no uniform bound of
the degree of extension of K valid for all n × n matrices. On the other hand, we provide a
upper bound of the degree of extension of K for a given separable characteristic polynomial.
1.
Introduction
It is a classical problem to study the similarity of integral matrices of a number field K. In
particular, Guralnick [Gu80, Theorem 7] proved the following theorem
Theorem 1 (Guralnick). Let M, N ∈Matn(OK) where K is a number field K and OK is the
ring of integers of K. If M and N are similar over each local completion OKp for all finite
places, then there is a finite extension L/K such that M and N are similar over OL.
It should be pointed out that the proof given in [Gu80] is neither constructive nor effective.
It is natural to ask whether there is a uniform bound of the degree of extension L/K for all
such matrices in Matn(OK). More precisely, if M and N are similar over each local completion
OKp for all finite places p, we define
CK(M, N) := min{[L : K] : L/K is a finite extension such that M, N are similar over OL}.
Question 2. Whether CK(M, N) can be bounded when M and N run over Matn(OK) such
that M and N are similar over each local completion OKp for all finite places p?
In this paper, we first give a negative answer to this question (see Theorem 9).
Theorem 3. There is a sequence of matrices Mn and Nn in Matn(Z) where Mn and Nn are
similar over Zp for all primes p such that
lim
n→∞CQ(Mn, Nn) = ∞.
On the other hand, we prove that there is an effective upper bound when the characteristic
polynomial of M and N is a fixed separable polynomial over K (see Theorem 16).
Theorem 4. Let χ(t) be a monic separable polynomial over OK, then there is an effective
constant C = C(f, n, K) depending only on f, n and K such that
max{CK(M, N) : M and N are similar over OKp for all places p with ch(M) = χ} ≤C,
where ch(M) is the characteristic polynomial of M.

INTRODUCTION
Notation and terminology are standard if not explained. For n×n matrices M, N ∈Matn(R)
over a commutative ring R, we say that M and N are similar over R if there exists U ∈GLn(R)
such that UM = NU. Let K be a number field and OK be the ring of integers of K. For any
finite place p of K, the completion of K with respect to p is denoted by Kp and OKp is the
integral ring of Kp. We write OKp := Kp if p is infinite.
The paper is organized as follows. In §2, we provide a lower bound for CK(M, N) and show
this lower bound can be arbitrarily large by explicit constructing certain required matrices over
Z. In §3, we give an upper bound for CK(M, N) if the characteristic polynomial is fixed and
separable. The proof of this result relies on the work of Wei and Xu [WX12, WX13] and some
techniques from class field theory.

The Upper Bounds
Although Theorem 9 claims that CK(·, ·) admits no uniform upper bound, CK(·, ·) does
possess a uniform upper bound (Theorem 16) when the characteristic polynomial is fixed and
assumed to be separable. To determine this bound, we employ a criterion for the existence of
integral points (Corollary 12); the conditions imposed by this criterion on the id`ele class group
allow us to construct the required field extensions via the class field theory. As an application,
we conclude this section with Example 18, which completes the discussion on the upper bound
initiated in Remark 10.
