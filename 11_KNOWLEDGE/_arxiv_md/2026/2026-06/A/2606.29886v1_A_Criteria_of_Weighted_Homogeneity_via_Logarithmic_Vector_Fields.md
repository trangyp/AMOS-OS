JIHAO LIU AND XIPING ZHANG
Abstract. Recently in [6] the authors proposed a conjecture that the homogeneity of an isolated
hypersurface germ can be detected by the existence of non-degenerate holomorphic logarithmic
vector fields. In this paper we prove this conjecture affirmatively.
1. Introduction
The study of Singular varieties have gained significant importance in modern algebraic geome-
try. One of the most fundamental prototypes of singularities is the isolated weighted homogeneous
hypersurface singularities. Such singularities are relatively simple, yet they contain rich geometric
information, and they have been studied extensively across various branches of mathematics.

Recently in [6] da Silva Machado and Seade discussed the lower bound of GSV indices of holo-

Conjecture 1.1. Let D be the isolated hypersurface singularity as above. The following statements
are equivalent.
(i) (D, 0) is weighted homogeneous.
(ii) There exists a holomorphic vector field tangent to D with an isolated singularity at 0 and is
transverse to the link spheres of D.
(iii) There exists a holomorphic vector field tangent to D with a non-degenerate isolated singu-

conjecture completely.

Corollary 3.3. Conjecture 1.1 is true.
Proof. It remains to prove that, if ˜ν ∈DerW,0(−log D) has an isolated singularity at 0 and is

germ (D, 0) in the sense of [14, Definition 2.1]. By [14, Theorem A] the hypersurface D has be to

Remark 3.4. In fact in the proof of [14, Theorem 3.3] the author states that all the eigenvalues of
the Jacobian matrix DF(0) have length < 1. Therefore the eigenvalues of the linear part of ˜ν have
negative real parts and ˜ν is non-degenerate. Thus on an isolated hypersurface germ, if a holomorphic
