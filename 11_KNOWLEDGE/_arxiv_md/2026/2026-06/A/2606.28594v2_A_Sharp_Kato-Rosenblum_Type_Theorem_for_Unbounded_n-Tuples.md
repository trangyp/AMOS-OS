A Sharp Kato-Rosenblum Type Theorem for Unbounded
n-Tuples
Rishabh Bhutani and Dan Virgil Voiculescu
Abstract
We prove a generalization for commuting n-tuples of unbounded self-adjoint operators and the
Lorentz (n,1) ideal, n ≥3, of the Kato-Rosenblum theorem. The result is derived from earlier work for
bounded operators [8]. Also, a very weak result for n = 2 unbounded operators and other additional
results are obtained.

As already noted in [8], unlike the case n = 1, all wave operators are equal for n ≥3 and this extends
to generalizations. In the case n = 2, with the exception of some weak existence results, it is not known
whether strong wave operators, if they exist, are all equal (see the recent discussion in [9]). Difficulties
with n = 2 were already noted in [10].
Along similar lines to the way Theorem 1 is derived, we also derive a very weak result for n = 2
(Theorem 3).
In addition to the introduction and references, there are three more sections. In section 2, we have
collected various preliminaries in particular about the quasicentral modulus, singular subspace from
normed ideal perturbation point of view ([7], [8]), and Theorems 1.5 and 2.3 of [8]. In section 3, we carry
out the proof of Theorem 1 by constructing algebras of operators to which Theorem 1.5 of [8] is applied.
Section 4 is about additional results. On one hand is the very weak result for n = 2 unbounded operators,
while on another hand are consequences of the n ≥3 strong result like a two spaces version.
