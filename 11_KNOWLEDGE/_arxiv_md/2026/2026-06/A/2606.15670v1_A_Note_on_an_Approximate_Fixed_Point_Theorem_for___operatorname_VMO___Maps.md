Remark 1.4. We make the following comments on Theorem 1.2.
(1) Brezis asked in Open Problem 5.10 of [1] whether an approximate fixed point statement

unit ball B, since the VMO maps are equivalent up to a set of zero measures.
(2) Lanconelli and Uguzzoni proved a statement equivalent to Theorem 1.2; see Proposition 12
of [4]. Their proof is based on the VMO degree theory developed in that paper. Here we
give a direct proof in the Euclidean ball, using only local averages, a ray-exit construction,
and the classical no-retraction form of Brouwer’s theorem.
(3) The proof below is self-contained apart from the classical Brouwer’s fixed point theorem,
and it does not use VMO degree theory.
