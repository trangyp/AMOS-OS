A REMARK ON RATIONAL QUARTIC CURVES IN PRIME FANO THREEFOLDS
OF DEGREE 22
KIRYONG CHUNG AND JEONG-SEOP KIM
ABSTRACT. In this short note, using the Sarkisov link between a prime Fano threefold V22
of degree 22 and the quintic del Pezzo threefold V5, we prove that the Hilbert scheme of
rational quartic curves in V22 admits a generically 2-to-1 rational map onto the projective
space P4.
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1. Previous works. The geometry of moduli spaces of rational curves in projective va-
rieties has been extensively studied from various perspectives, including birational ge-
ometry and enumerative geometry. Among these directions, the construction of com-
pactifications and the investigation of their geometric invariants play a fundamental role
in understanding the global structure of these moduli spaces. In [CK11, CHK12, CM17,
CHL18, Chu23], the authors focused on the birational relation among several compact-
ifications of moduli spaces of rational curves. Through a systematic study of various
compactifications, the authors established explicit birational relations by extending the
moduli-theoretic birational maps. On the other hand, in [CKK25a], by finding torus-
invariant quartic curves in the Mukai–Umemura threefold ([MU83]), the authors proved
the smoothness of the corresponding Hilbert scheme. These results subsequently enabled
the application of various localization techniques to compute Donaldson–Thomas (DT)
type invariants and verify several important conjectures. For detailed descriptions of con-
jectures and their proof, see [CMT18, CT21, KP08] and [CLW21, CW26]. Also, we obtained
global descriptions of Hilbert schemes of rational curves for the quadric threefold and the
quintic del Pezzo threefold ([CHY23] and [CKK25b]). Once such geometric descriptions
are established, localization formulas ([GP99]) can be canonically applied to compute the
associated DT-type invariants. On the other hand, for prime Fano threefolds V22 of degree
22, including the Mukai–Umemura variety, the use of exceptional collections (and their

1.2. Result. In this short article, we propose a geometric property of the Hilbert scheme
of rational quartic curves in V22 inspired by so-called correlators in quantum cohomology
([BM04, Section 2.2]). Let H4(X) be the Hilbert scheme of curves C in a smooth projective
variety X with Hilbert polynomial χ(OC(m)) = 4m + 1. Unless otherwise stated, we
henceforth assume that H4(X) is irreducible to simplify the discussion. Let V22 99K V5
be the Sarkisov link with centers: a line L ⊂V22 and a rational quintic curve Γ ⊂V5. For
the definition and geometric property of the Sarkisov link, see Section 2.2 and [KPS18].
It is well known that the strict transform C of a general rational quartic curve C ⊂V22,
not meeting the line L, intersects Γ in four points (cf. [TZ12, Section 1.3]). We prove that
such correspondence provides a covering structure of the Hilbert scheme H4(V22) over a
projective space P4.
Theorem 1.1 (= Theorem 3.5). Let H4(V22) be the Hilbert scheme of rational quartic curves in
V22. Then there exists a 2-to-1 rational map
Φ : H4(V22) 99K Sym4Γ(∼= P4)
which associates a general rational quartic curve to four points in a rational quintic curve Γ ⊂V5.
The outline of the proof of Theorem 1.1 is as follows. For four general points in V5,
there exist three rational quartic curves (Lemma 3.2). One of them is contained in the
strict transform of the flopping center of the Sarkisov link, and thus the inverse image of
Φ consists of two rational quartic curves in V22. Moreover, by analyzing the exceptional
locus of a residual map, we can show that the rational map Φ in Theorem 1.1 is branched
over a hypersurface in P4. For a geometric meaning of the branch locus of the map Φ, see
the proof of Theorem 3.5.

2.1. Rational quartic curves in V5. Let V5 be the quintic del Pezzo threefold. In [CKK25b],
by extending the result of [FGP19, Proposition 4.11], it is proved that the Hilbert scheme
H4(V5) of rational quartic curves in V5 is isomorphic to a Grassmannian bundle over the
Hilbert scheme H1(V5) of lines in V5. More precisely, a rational quartic curve in V5 arises
as the residual curve of a linear section containing a line in V5. In detail, let l be a line in
V5, and let Λ be a 4-dimensional linear subspace of P6 containing l. Then Λ∩V5 = l ∪C for
a rational quartic curve C in V5. Moreover, such a residual line l is uniquely determined
by C. Indeed, if C admitted two distinct residual lines l1 and l2, then the linear span Λ of
C in P6 would contain both l1 and l2. Consequently,
Λ ∩V5 ⊇C ∪l1 ∪l2
would have degree at least 6, contradicting the fact that deg(V5 ∩Λ) = 5.
Note that the family of 4-dimensional linear subspaces containing a fixed line in P6 is
isomorphic to Gr(3, 5), and H1(V5) ∼= P2. Hence H4(V5) is isomorphic to a Gr(3, 5)-bundle
over P2 ([CKK25b, Theorem 1.1]). A key technical step in the proof of this claim was
to exclude the possibility that a non-Cohen–Macaulay curve C with Hilbert polynomial
χ(OC(m)) = 4m+1 is supported on an elliptic quartic curve ([CKK25b, Section 4.2]). This
was achieved by studying the geometry of surfaces arising as hyperplane sections of the
quintic del Pezzo variety V5.
Proposition 2.1 ([FN89, Corollary 2.1], [Ili94, 1.2.1]). There exists a curve Σ and a surface B
containing Σ on V5 which satisfy the following.
Σ = {x ∈V5 | ∃only one line ℓin V5 such that x ∈ℓ},
B \ Σ = {x ∈V5 | ∃exactly two lines ℓin V5 such that x ∈ℓ}
V5 \ B = {x ∈V5 | ∃exactly three lines ℓin V5 such that x ∈ℓ}
Moreover, B ∈| −KV5| = |2H∗| where H∗= OV5(1).

3. RATIONAL QUARTIC CURVES IN V22
The Sarkisov link between V5 and V22 induces a relationship between certain families
of rational curves in these varieties. We first study the geometry of the quintic del Pezzo
threefold V5 arising from its Pl¨ucker embedding V5 ⊂P6. In this section, we will repeat-
edly denote by p1, p2, p3, and p4 four points of V5, which are also points of a rational quintic
curve Γ in V5. They correspond to a point of the fourth symmetric product of the curve Γ,
and they span a linear subspace in P6. We denote by
D = p1 + p2 + p3 + p4 ∈Sym4Γ
and
ΛD = ⟨p1, p2, p3, p4⟩⊆P6.
We say that the four points p1, p2, p3, and p4 are general if D ∈Sym4Γ is general and their
linear span ΛD has dimension 3.
3.1. Lemmas. We begin with several lemmas needed for the proof of Theorem 1.1 (=
Theorem 3.5).
Lemma 3.1. Let Γ be a rational quintic curve in V5, and p1, p2, p3, and p4 be four general points
of Γ in V5. Then the linear subspace ΛD spanned by these four points does not contain any line in
V5.

Lemma 3.2. Let p1, p2, p3, and p4 be four general points of the curve Γ in V5. Then there exist
exactly three rational quartic curves in V5 passing through these four points.
Proof. Let ΛD be the 3-dimensional linear subspace of P6 spanned by p1, p2, p3, and p4.
Then ΛD intersects V5 in five points. We denote by p5 the fifth point of V5 ∩ΛD other than
p1, p2, p3, and p4, and define the residual map
ρ : Sym4Γ 99K V5; p1 + p2 + p3 + p4 7→p5.
Let ℓbe a line in V5 passing through the point p5. By Lemma 3.1, ΛD does not contain ℓ.
Let ΛD,ℓ= ⟨ΛD, ℓ⟩be the 4-dimensional linear subspace of P6 spanned by ΛD and ℓ. Then
the intersection V5 ∩ΛD,ℓis a reducible curve of degree 5 of the form ℓ∪C, and hence C
is a curve of degree 4. By adjunction, the curve ℓ∪C has arithmetic genus 1. Since ℓis
a secant line to C, it follows that C is a rational quartic curve. Note that there are three
distinct choices for the line ℓ= ℓj. Consequently, there exist three distinct rational quartic
curves C = Cj, each associated with a different line ℓj for j = 1, 2, 3.
□
Remark 3.3. The result in Lemma 3.2 coincides with the correlator computed in Section 2.2

Lemma 3.4. Let p1, p2, p3, and p4 be four points of the curve Γ in general position. Then there
exists a unique rational quartic curve contained in the hyperplane section A (Notation 2.2) passing

Proof. We prove the lemma case by case according to the classification of the hyperplane
section A of V5 given in [Pro92, Section 2].

3.2. Proof of Theorem 1.1. Let V22 be a prime Fano threefold of degree 22. Assume that
the Hilbert scheme H4(V22) is irreducible.
Theorem 3.5. Under the above assumption, the Hilbert scheme H4(V22) is birational to a double
cover of P4(∼= Sym4Γ) branched over a hypersurface.
Proof. We denote by H4(V5, Γ) the Hilbert scheme of rational quartic curves C in V5 such
that the 0-dimensional subscheme C ∩Γ has length 4. By Lemma 3.2, there exists a gener-
ically 3-to-1 rational map
H4(V5, Γ) 99K Sym4Γ ∼= P4.
Let [C] ∈H4(V5, Γ), and assume that C admits a strict transform C on V22 under the
Sarkisov link. Then
C.H = C.(3H∗−2Γ) = 3 · 4 −2 · 4 = 4,
so C is a rational quartic curve in V22. Note that C admits a strict transform on V22 if and
only if C ̸⊆A. Let us define
SA = {[C] ∈H4(V5, Γ) | C ⊆A},
which parametrizes the rational quartic curves C in V5 such that C ∩Γ is a 0-dimensional
subscheme of length 4 and the curve C is contracted by the Sarkisov link to V22. Then SA
maps birationally onto Sym4Γ by Lemma 3.4. Consequently, the map
H4(V22) 99K H4(V5, Γ) \ SA
is birational, and hence we obtain a generically 2-to-1 rational map
Φ : H4(V22) 99K Sym4Γ ∼= P4.
