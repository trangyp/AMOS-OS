A coproduct obstruction for derived unramified
cohomology
David Kumallagov
Abstract
Let k be a perfect field of exponential characteristic p, and let R be a
commutative Z[1/p]-algebra. We prove that the first derived unramified
functor
F 7−→R1
nr,RF
from homotopy invariant Nisnevich sheaves with transfers of R-modules to
birational sheaves commutes with arbitrary small direct sums. This gives a
positive answer, after inverting the exponential characteristic, to a question
of Kahn and Sujatha; on smooth projective varieties no inversion is needed.
We also describe an obstruction to this for the functor R2
nr,R in categorical
terms, which includes the familiar Griffiths group obstruction. As applications
of the motivic nature of the functors Rq
nr, we prove torsion-order bounds and
a correspondence-detection statement for surfaces.
Keywords:
derived unramified cohomology, birational motives, triangulated
categories, homotopy invariant sheaves with transfers, Griffiths groups,
torsion order
