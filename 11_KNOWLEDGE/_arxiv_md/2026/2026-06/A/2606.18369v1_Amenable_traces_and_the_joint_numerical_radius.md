AMENABLE TRACES AND THE JOINT NUMERICAL RADIUS
VERN I. PAULSEN, MIZANUR RAHAMAN, AND EBRAHIM SAMEI
Abstract. We provide necessary and sufficient characterizations of the existence of an
amenable trace on a C∗-algebra in terms of the joint free numerical radius of tuples of
unitaries, isometries, and partial isometries in the algebra. We apply these results to obtain
new obstructions to various lifting properties.
1. Introduction and Preliminaries
The main goal of this paper is to give a number of characterizations of when a unital
C*-algebra enjoys an amenable trace in terms of the joint free numerical radius which was
introduced in [11].
Definition 1.1. Let A ⊆B(H) be a unital C∗-algebras, and let a1, . . . , an ∈A. The free
joint numerical radius wcb(a1, . . . , an) (also referred as the cb-numerical radius) is defined by
wcb(a1, . . . , an) = sup{w(U1 ⊗a1 + · · · + Un ⊗an)},
where the supremum is taken over every k, every choice of n contractions U1, . . . , Un ∈Mk.
By considering compressions, it is not hard to see that the quantity remains unchanged
if we allow U1, . . . , Un ∈B(K) for an arbitrary Hilbert space and the tensor product is the
spatial tensor or if we restrict to n-tuples of unitaries instead of contractions.
This quantity was introduced in [11] where it was used to give an extension of a classical
result of Ando’s that characterized operators T with w(T) ≤1/2 and then used to give
a new characterization of C*-algebras with the weak expectation property(WEP). Those
ideas were further extended in [12,13] to give some new tensor product and order theoretic
characterizations of WEP. Most recently the joint free numerical radius was used in [9] to
give new proofs of some exactness and lifting properties for group C*-algebras. We further
extend some of those ideas in section 4.
Among the results that we prove is that a unital C*-algebra has an amenable trace if
and only if for any generating n-tuple a1, ..., an in the algebra, consisting of isometries or
co-isometries, one has wcb(a1, . . . , an) = n. We also give necessary and sufficient conditions
for a C*-algebra generated by partial isometries to have an amenable trace.
Amenable traces are important in C∗-algebra theory because they capture finite-dimensional
approximation of tracial states and link C∗-algebraic structure with von Neumann algebraic
approximation phenomena such as Rω-embeddability ([5]). They are also relevant to quan-
tum information theory through the study of quantum correlations, Tsirelson-type problems,
and nonlocal games ([15]), themes that played a decisive role in the negative resolution of
the Connes Embedding Problem via the breakthrough result MIP∗= RE ([14]).
We begin by recalling some key facts about amenable traces.
