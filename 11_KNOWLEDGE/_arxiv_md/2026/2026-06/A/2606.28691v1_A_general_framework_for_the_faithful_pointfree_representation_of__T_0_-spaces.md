A general framework for the faithful pointfree representation of
T0-spaces
Rui Prezado∗and Anna Laura Suarez†
Abstract
We introduce a general framework for studying natural contravariant adjunctions that refine
the adjunction between frames and spaces so that the fixpoints are T0-spaces.
Our objects of study are spatializable Frm-concrete categories, or SFC-categories. These con-
sist of a faithful functor O : C →Frm equipped with an object 2C ∈C, satisfying compatibility
conditions that ensure that (2C, S) forms a dualizing object in the sense of Porst and Tholen,
where S denotes the Sierpi´nski space.
Three important instances of pointfree T0 spaces present in the literature fit into this frame-
work: strictly zero-dimensional biframes, MT-algebras, and Raney extensions.
We show SFC-categories are assembled in an ordered category – a category enriched in
preordered sets – whose morphisms are suitable functors which preserve certain initial liftings.
SFC-categories induce natural dual adjunctions, and morphisms between them will respectively
induce suitable morphisms between these adjunctions.
Motivated by the characterization of sober spaces as maximal objects in the fibers of Ω:
Top →Frmop, and of TD-spaces as the minimal ones, due to Banaschewski and Pultr, we study
initial and terminal objects of fibers for an arbitrary SFC-category. We prove that the natural
adjunction for fiber-initials has exactly the sober spaces as fixpoints, while for fiber-terminals
contains at most TD-spaces, recovering their results of in a much more general setting.

Introduction
The classical adjunction between frames and topological spaces,
Ω: Topop ⇄Frm : pt,
restricts to a duality between the category of sober spaces and the category of spatial frames, but
it does not capture all T0 spaces. In particular, the unit of the adjunction for a T0 space X is a
subspace embedding σX : X →ptΩX, which is a homeomorphism if and only if X is sober.
The theories of Raney extensions ([36]), of strictly zero-dimensional biframes ([29]), and of MT-
algebras ([10]), all provide pointfree faithful descriptions of the category of T0-spaces, by refining
the natural adjunction Ω⊣pt.
The aim of this paper is to provide a general framework for the pointfree and faithful treatment
of T0 spaces, by studying refinements of the classical adjunction Ω⊣pt. To do so, we introduce
a notion of “spatializable Frm-concrete category”, or SFC-category for short. This consists of a
quadruple (O, C, 2C, ιC) where O : C →Frm is a faithful functor, 2C ∈C, and ιC : O2C ∼= 2 an
isomorphism, and such that compatibility conditions are verified which ensure that the pair (2C, S)
forms a dualizing object in the sense of Porst and Tholen ([38]). The dualizing object induces a
natural dual adjunction ΩC ⊣ptC, as shown below:

We describe the notion of adjunction in the ordered category of Frm-concrete categories [1,
34], and using this as a building block for our setting, we confirm that SFC-functors will induce
morphisms between the induced natural adjunctions. We study the notion of adjunction for PFC-
categories, and show that right adjoints are morphisms in SFC.

We move on to study SFC-categories where O has a left FC-adjoint, i. e. those for which the
free object in C over a frame exists. In agreement with the classical characterization of sober spaces,
seen as objects of the Frm-concrete category Ω: Top0op →Frm, as well as the definitions of sober
Raney extension and sober MT-algebras from [36] and [10], we define an object C ∈C to be sober
if every point p : OC →2 of its underlying frame is realized as Op for some point p : C →2C (i.
e. C ”has enough points”). We describe a pointfree notion of sobrification for pointfree T0-spaces,
and find that when the category C has pullbacks sober objects are coreflective in C.
Next, we consider the SFC-categories for which all T0-spaces are fixpoints. We recall the charac-
terizations, due to Banaschewski and Pultr, of sober spaces as the minimal elements and TD spaces
as the maximal ones for the Frm-concrete category Ω: Top0op, respectively.

Outline
In Section 1 we recall the necessary background on categories and frames. We introduce the notion
of Frm-concrete category in a 2-categorical framework, and show that in this setting hom-categories
are a preorder. We then introduce the notion of fiber as an instance of such a preorder poset. We
also review the Bruns–Lakser completion, the Funayama embedding, and several characterizations
of the TD-axiom and its connection with essentiality in categories of lattices.
Section 2 introduces and explores our general framework. In Subsection 2.1, we illustrate the
main motivating examples. In Subsection 2.2, we introduce our main objects of study, spatializable

Frm-concrete categories, or SFC-categories. We show that every SFC-category (O, C, 2C, ιC) admits
a dualizing object, of which one half is the Sierpi´nski space. The result by Porst and Tholen that
dualizing objects induce natural adjunctions, adapted to FC-categories, shows that there is a natural
adjunction ptC : C ⇆TopC : ΩC, which we call Dual(O, C, 2C, ιC). We show some preliminaries
examples (Examples 2.4, 2.3, 2.5) for categories of spaces.
The adjunction ptC : C ⇆TopC : ΩC is idempotent (Theorem 2.6), and O determines a map in
RADJ to the natural adjunction for frames and spaces (Proposition 2.4).
In Subsection 2.3, we define morphisms between SFC-categories and obtain the category SFC;
then we extend the assignment Dual(−) to a functor Dual : SFC →RADJ to the categories of
adjunctions with right maps. We also define the notion of adjunction between objects of SFC,
which we call FC-adjunctions, whose right adjoints preserve cartesian lifts (Theorem 2.17), as a
result we show that coreflectors coming from SFC-adjunctions are morphisms in SFC (Proposition
2.20).
In Subsection 2.4, we develop a pointfree notion of sobrification. We restrict ourselves to SFC-
categories where O is a right FC-adjoint. We express the sober coreflection of an object of C as a
pullback (Proposition 2.25), and deduce that when C has all pullbacks sober objects are coreflective
in C (Corollary 2.26). We also recover a generalization in this setting of the result in point-set
topology stating that the sobrification of a subspace of a sober space is the intersection of all sober
subspaces containing it (Theorem 2.29).
In Subsection 2.5, we turn our attention to application of these results in concrete settings,
and show that Skula, MT, and Raney all provide instances of SFC-categories, thus making their
dual adjunctions with Top a corollary of the result by Porst and Tholen adapted to FC-categories
(Theorems 2.30, 2.31, and 2.32). We show that the natural transformation ker : S →Filt naturally
defines a functor SS : Skula →Raney, and that this coincides with a functor introduced in [37].
We slightly strengthen a result from [12] and show that there is a functor SM : MT →Raney. We
show that the functors SS : Skula →Raney and SM : MT →Raney are morphisms in SFC,
and show that the map is RADJ they induce is also a left map of adjunctions (Propositions 2.36
and 2.38).
Section 3 focuses on the subcategories of initial and terminal objects of fibers. In Subsection
3.1, we slightly strengthen the characterization of Banaschewski and Pultr in [8] of sober spaces
as the fiber-maximal, and TD-spaces as fiber-minimal objects of Ω: Top0op →Frm (Theorem
3.7), and prove that the former coincide with fiber-initials and the latter with fiber-terminals. We
introduce total SFC-categories, those with all T0-spaces as fixpoints and such that the fiber-initials
and the fiber-terminals induce their own natural adjunction. Letting II : CI →C be the inclusion of
fiber-initial objects, and IT : CT →C that of terminal-ones, we show that for a total SFC-category
(O, C, 2C, ιC), the natural adjunction Dual(OIT , CT , 2C, ιC) is a subobject of Dual(O, C, 2C), and that
Dual(OII, CI, 2C, ιC) is a quotient (Theorems 3.10 and 3.15).W e show that sober spaces coincide
with the fixpoints of Dual(OII, CI, 2C, , ιC), and that the fixpoints of Dual(OIT , CT , 2C, ιC) are TD
spaces, although we do not have the reverse inclusion (Theorem 3.18).

and MT-algebras (Theorems 3.25, 3.30, 3.39). We study fiber-terminal objects in the category of
MT-algebras; we show that there exist fibers without terminal objects (Example 3.4) and prove that
in MT, too, spectra of terminal objects coincide with the TD-spaces (Proposition 3.36). Finally,
we obtain a duality for TD-spaces as a result of a dual adjunction with fiber-terminal MT-algebras
(Theorem 3.39).

Dual adjunctions
We provide some background and well-known results on the theory of concrete dual adjunctions.
There are numerous references in the literature which study duality theory from such an abstract
perspective, among which we mention [16, 38, 15, 21].
We fix a faithful functor U : C →S. Given morphisms f : U(D) →U(C) in S and p: D →C in
C, we say that f lifts to p if U(p) = f. We highlight that lifts are necessarily unique.
A cartesian lifting1 (with respect to U) of a family of morphisms {fi : X →U(C) | i ∈I}2
in S is a pair (X, θ) consisting of an object X in C and an isomorphism θ: U(X) ∼= X such
that any morphism h: U(D) →U(X) lifts to a morphism D →X if and only if each morphism
fiθh: U(D) →U(C) lifts to a morphism D →C.
We say a family of morphisms {pi : D →C | i ∈I} in C is cartesian (with respect to U) if
(D, idU(D)) is a cartesian lifting (with respect to U) of the family {U(pi): U(D) →U(C) | i ∈I}.
Remark 1.1. Given (X, θ) a cartesian lifting w.r.t. U of a family {fi : X →U(C) | i ∈I}, we have
that fiθ lifts for all i ∈I, since the identity on U(X) lifts; we denote the lift of fiθ by fi. We note
that the family {fi | i ∈I} is cartesian w.r.t U, and we say this is the lifted family of the given
cartesian lift.
