Introduction
In 1964, Gell-Mann suggested that multiquark states beyond the minimal valence quark con-
stituents q¯q and qqq might exist [1]. Latter, the ﬁve-quark baryons with the quark constituents
qqqq¯q were developed [2], and the name pentaquark was introduced by Lipkin [3]. A diquark can
eﬀectively serve as an antiquark, thus two diquarks and one antiquark can attract each other by
the same means as three antiquarks do in an ordinary antibaryon.
Although some pentaquark candidates were claimed in the experiments before 2015, none of
them survived scrutiny of additional experimental data, for example, a candidate for a K+n
resonance called Θ+(1540), whose minimal quark constituents would be uudd¯s, was claimed in 2003
[4, 5, 6], a candidate for a D∗±p∓resonance called Θ0
c(3099), whose minimal quark constituents
would be uudd¯c, was claimed in 2004 [7].
In 2015, the LHCb collaboration observed two exotic structures Pc(4380) and Pc(4450) with pre-
ferred spin-parity JP = 3
2
−and 5
2
+ respectively in the J/ψp mass spectrum in the exclusive process
Λ0
b →J/ψpK−[8], and stimulated deep interest in the spectroscopy of hidden-charm pentaquark
states and opened a new era for deeper understanding the basic subjects, such as conﬁnement,
dynamically symmetry breaking, etc. In 2019, the LHCb collaboration re-investigated the same
process but a data sample with an order of magnitude larger than previously one, conﬁrmed the
old structure Pc(4450), which is made up of two narrow superimposed structures Pc(4440) and
Pc(4457), and observed a new structure Pc(4312) [9]. If these Pc states are genuine resonances, not
kinematical eﬀects, such as threshold cusps, triangle singularities, coupled channel eﬀects, etc, the
minimal valence quark constituents should be uudc¯c, irrespective of the diquark-diquark-antiquark
type, diquark-triquark type or meson-baryon type [10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15].
In 2020, the LHCb collaboration investigated the exclusive process Ξ−
b →J/ψK−Λ, and dis-
covered a hidden-charm structure Pcs(4459) with strangeness for the ﬁrst time in the J/ψΛ mass
spectrum with a signiﬁcance of 3.1σ [16].
In 2021, the LHCb collaboration investigated the exclusive process B0
s →J/ψp¯p, and discovered
a hidden-charm structure Pc(4337) without strangeness in the J/ψp and J/ψ¯p systems with a
signiﬁcance approximately 3.1 to 3.7σ according to the possible assumed spin-parity [17]. In 2022,
the LHCb collaboration investigated the exclusive process B−→J/ψΛ¯p and discovered a new
hidden-charm structure Pcs(4338) with strangeness in the J/ψΛ mass spectrum with the favored
spin-parity JP = 1
2

investigate the exotic P states due to additional quark-antiquark pairs have to be created from the
QCD vacuum, and the corresponding branching fractions are expected to be smaller.
