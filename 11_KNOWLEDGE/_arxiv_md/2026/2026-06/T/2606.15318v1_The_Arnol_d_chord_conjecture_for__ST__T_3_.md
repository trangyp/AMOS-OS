THE ARNOL’D CHORD CONJECTURE FOR ST ∗T 3
KAINAN GUO, ZHENGYI ZHOU
Abstract. We prove the Arnol’d chord conjecture for ST ∗T 3, ST ∗(S1 × S2), ST ∗S3 as well as arbitrary
connected sums among them.
1. Introduction
In [4], Arnol’d stated the following chord conjecture.
Conjecture 1.1 (Arnol’d). For every closed contact manifold (Y, ξ) and any contact form α, every closed
Legendrian Λ ⊂Y carries a Reeb chord.
In comparison to its cousin—the Weinstein conjecture—we only know a handful of contact manifolds for
which the chord conjecture holds. Notably, it holds for all contact 3-manifolds by Hutchings–Taubes [15],
for the contact boundary of displaceable Liouville domains by Mohnke [22], which was generalized to the
contact boundary of any Liouville domain with vanishing symplectic cohomology by the second author [31].
There are also cases in which the chord conjecture has been established for certain classes of Legendrian
submanifolds in some contact manifolds, e.g. for some Legendrian spheres from the surgery perspective by
Cieliebak [9], and for conormals of submanifolds in cosphere bundles by Broˇci´c, Cant, and Shelukhin [6].
In this note, we add several classes of contact manifolds for which the chord conjecture holds for all closed
Legendrian submanifolds.
Theorem 1.2. The chord conjecture holds for ST ∗T 3.

Theorem 1.3. The chord conjecture holds for the following pairs (Y, Λ):
(1) Y = ∂V , where V is a Liouville domain that embeds symplectically into a Liouville domain W with

Theorem 1.4. The chord conjecture holds for the following contact 5-manifolds:
(1) ST ∗S3 and ST ∗(S1 × S2).

2.2. Viterbo isomorphism. In [30], Viterbo proved that for an oriented closed manifold Q, the symplectic
cohomology of T ∗Q is isomorphic to the homology of the free loop space LQ, using generating function
theory. Later, this isomorphism was reformulated by Abbondandolo–Schwarz [1] and Salamon–Weber [26]
using different approaches. The necessity of twisting by a local system was noticed by Seidel and worked out
in detail by Kragh [18]. The local coefficient system is trivial if and only if the second Stiefel–Whitney class
vanishes on every torus in the cotangent bundle. The reason for this phenomenon is that the orientability
of the moduli spaces being counted is determined by several characteristic classes; see [2, 3]. To avoid these
issues, we mainly use the Viterbo isomorphism with Z/2 coefficients.
Theorem 2.5 ([2, 3, 30]). There is a local coefficient system η on the free loop space such that
SH∗(T ∗Q; Z) ∼= Hn−∗(LQ; η).
Moreover, if Q is orientable and the second Stiefel–Whitney class vanishes on tori in T ∗Q, then η is trivial,
in which case
SH∗(T ∗Q; Z) ∼= Hn−∗(LQ; Z).
In all cases,
SH∗(T ∗Q; Z/2) ∼= Hn−∗(LQ; Z/2).
In particular, the isomorphisms above are isomorphisms of BV algebras.
