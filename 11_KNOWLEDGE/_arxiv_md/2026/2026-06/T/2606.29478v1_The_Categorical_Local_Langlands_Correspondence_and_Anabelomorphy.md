Anabelomorphy

Abstract
Let G/Qp be a connected, split, reductive group over Qp. In this paper I show that
if K and L are anabelomorphic p-adic fields i.e. K and L have topologically isomorphic
absolute Galois groups, then the stacks of Langlands parameters (for the fields K and L)
considered in [Fargues and Scholze, 2024], are also isomorphic (Theorem 2.2.1). This
leads to Conjecture 3.3.1 which provides a precise relationship between the main conjecture
of [Fargues and Scholze, 2024] and anabelomorphy of p-adic fields considered in [Joshi,
2020a]. I establish my conjecture for a split torus in Theorem 4.1.

Introduction
The Local Langlands Correspondence in all its avatars ([Henniart, 1988], [Fargues and Scholze,
2024], [Emerton, Gee, and Hellmann, 2025]) deals with representations of the absolute Galois
groups (or more precisely of the Weil or the Weil-Deligne groups, of p-adic fields) and (suitably
defined) automorphic representations. On the other hand, there exists non-isomorphic p-adic
fields K ,L with topologically isomorphic absolute Galois groups. This leads to the notion of
anabelomorphy, studied in detail in [Joshi, 2020a]. More precisely, two p-adic fields K ,L
with topologically isomorphic absolute Galois groups are said to be anabelomorphic p-adic
fields (examples of such fields are given by [Joshi, 2020a, Theorem 3.5.1 and Lemma 4.4]).
Especially, from [Joshi, 2020a, Proposition 7.1,2], one sees that anabelomorphic p-adic fields
have isomorphic Weil (and Weil-Deligne) groups. In particular, anabelomorphic p-adic fields
have naturally equivalent categories of finite dimensional Galois representations.
So one may ask if the automorphic sides of the Local Langlands correspondence for anabelo-
morphic fields are also directly and naturally related. This question was posed in [Joshi, 2020a,
§ 7], where partial, affirmative answers were obtained in the context of [Henniart, 1988] for all
principal series representations and all supercuspidal representations in the tame case (p ∤n) for
GLn (see [Joshi, 2020a, Theorem 7.3.3 and Theorem 7.4.1]). In [Joshi, 2020a, Theorem 7.6.1],
one obtains a complete result for GL2 and all odd residue characteristics. For my discussion of
the p-adic context of [Emerton et al., 2025], see [Joshi, 2020a, § 7.7, and also Question 15.17].
In the present paper, I formulate the precise expectation (Conjecture 3.3.1) for the categorical
avatar given by [Fargues and Scholze, 2024] for connected, split reductive groups over Qp
considered over anabelomorphic p-adic fields. In Theorem 2.2.1 (also see Corollary 2.2.3), I
establish the equivalence of the Galois side i.e. equivalence of the stacks of Langlands parameters
considered in [Fargues and Scholze, 2024]. In Proposition 2.2.12, I establish the isomorphism
of schemes of Langlands parameters defined by [Fargues and Scholze, 2024, Theorem VIII.1.3].
Equivalence of Whittaker data is established in Proposition 3.2.1. Finally in Theorem 4.1,
Conjecture 3.3.1 is proved for split torii.
The Asides § 3.4–§ 3.7 may be skipped on initial reading: § 3.4 is about groups with no
non-trivial pure inner forms; § 3.5 clarifies how anabelomorphy plays out on the G-bundle side
of [Fargues and Scholze, 2024] for extended inner forms of G = GLn; § 3.6 (resp. § 3.7) deals
with anabelomorphy and quadratic (resp. Hermitian) spaces.
