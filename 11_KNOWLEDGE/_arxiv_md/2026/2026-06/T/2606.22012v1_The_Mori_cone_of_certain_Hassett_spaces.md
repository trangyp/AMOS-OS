THE MORI CONE OF CERTAIN HASSETT SPACES
DAVIDE RICCI
Abstract. In this paper, we prove that the Mori cone of Hassett spaces whose
universal family is a P1-bundle is generated by 1-dimensional strata. This ex-
tends the case of the symmetric GIT quotient (P1)n // PGL2 established by
Bolognesi and Massarenti. Along the way, we review how these spaces are nat-
urally isomorphic to certain GIT quotients (P1)n // PGL2, characterize them as
the targets of the birational contractions of the blow-up of Pn−3 at n−1 general
points with Q-factorial image, and deduce that their effective cone is likewise
generated by strata.
1. Introduction
A central object in the Minimal Model Program is the study of the Mori cone
NE(X) of a variety X, that is, the closure of the cone spanned by the classes of
irreducible curves in the vector space of 1-cycles up to numerical equivalence. This
cone governs several geometric properties of X. For example, extremal rays having
negative intersection with the canonical divisor induce birational contractions to
spaces with mild singularities.
A long-standing problem is determining the Mori cone of the moduli space M g,n
of stable genus g curves with n markings. This space compactifies Mg,n, the mod-
uli space of smooth genus g curves with n markings, and is a stratified topologi-
cal space: a k-codimensional stratum is an irreducible component parameterizing
curves having at least k nodes. The F-conjecture, as stated in [GKM02], predicts
that NE(M g,n) is generated by 1-dimensional strata. In [GKM02], the authors
reduce the conjecture to proving the genus 0 case. In particular, the conjecture for
M g,n is equivalent to the conjecture on M 0,g+n/Sg, that is, the quotient of M 0,g+n
by the action of the symmetric group Sg that identifies the first g marked points.
The conjecture was proved for M 0,n for n ≤7 in [KM96]. For example, M 0,5 is a
del Pezzo surface of degree five and its Mori cone is well-known to be generated by
(-1)-curves. Recently, Fedorchuk and Mellit proved in [FM25] that the conjecture
on M 0,n for all n holds if and only if the conjecture on M 0,n/Sn holds for all n. They
also settled the case M 0,8. On the other hand, in [CT13],[GK16],[HKL16],[Mul25],
it was proved that M 0,n is not a Mori dream space for n ≥8. Mori dream spaces
are a particularly well-behaved class of spaces for the minimal model program and
include Fano varieties and, more generally, varieties of Fano type. Moreover, in
[KM96] it was proved that −KM0,n is not effective for n ≥8. These facts show

Definition 1.1. Given a weight tuple A = (a1, . . . , an), ai ∈Q, 0 < ai ≤1,
P ai > 2, a weighted pointed A-stable rational curve is an arithmetic genus zero
connected nodal curve with n smooth points (C, p1, . . . , pn) such that ωC(P aipi)
is ample and, every time some of the points coincide, the sum of their weights is
at most one. We call Hassett space the fine moduli space M A that parametrizes
A-stable curves. We say that M A is small if its universal family is a P1-bundle.
These spaces were introduced by Hassett in [Has03] and provide different com-
pactifications for M0,n. The spaces M A are generally easier to study, as they are
targets of blow-up morphisms M 0,n →M A, and carry an analogous stratification
of the boundary. Hence, it is natural to ask whether it is true that the Mori cone
of M A is generated by 1-dimensional strata. Moreover, the F-conjecture implies
the same statement for any of these spaces M A. In this paper, we focus on small
Hassett spaces, which are the simplest case. The main results are the following.
Theorem 1.2.
(1) The Mori cone of a small Hassett space NE(M A) is finitely
generated by 1-dimensional strata.
(2) The cone of effective divisors of a small Hassett space Eff(M A) is finitely
generated by 1-codimensional strata.
(3) Small Hassett spaces are Mori dream spaces.
(4) Over the complex numbers, small Hassett spaces are of Fano type.
(5) Small Hassett spaces can be identified with the GIT quotients (P1)n//PGL2
with no strictly semi-stable points.
(6) The targets of the birational contractions with Q-factorial image of the
blow-up of Pn−3 at n−1 points in general position Bln−1Pn−3 are precisely
the small Hassett spaces. In particular, they are all smooth.
This reproves and extends a result presented by Bolognesi and Massarenti in
[BM21]: the Mori cone of the GIT quotients (P1)n // PGL2 with the symmetric
linearizations is generated by 1-dimensional strata. In particular, when n is odd,
the space (P1)n // PGL2 is isomorphic to M A where A = (2/n + ε, . . . , 2/n + ε),
0 < ε ≪1. When n is even, (P1)n // PGL2 is singular, its Picard rank is one, and
M A is its Kirwan desingularization, but it is no longer small. In all cases, M A is an
instance of the M p,q spaces that appeared in [CT23], i.e. the Hassett spaces with
a1 = · · · = ap = a + ε, ap+1 = · · · = an = b, p + q = n, pa + qb = 2, 0 < b, ε ≪1.
These M p,q spaces are small as long as at least one among p and q is odd. When
the M p,q spaces are not small, they fit in the next easiest class of Hassett spaces
to study, that is, when the stable curves have at most two irreducible components.
We leave open the question of the generators for the Mori cones for these spaces.
In [BM21], the Mori chamber decomposition of Bln−1Pn−3 and its link with the
GIT chamber decomposition for the action of PGL2 on (P1)n were also studied.

3. Small Hassett spaces and GIT quotients of (P1)n
Small Hassett spaces can also be interpreted as GIT quotients of (P1)n by PGL2
acting diagonally, cf. Proposition 3.4. All the details about GIT quotients and this
particular action can be found in [Dol03, Chapter 11]. These GIT quotients depend

4. Small Hassett spaces as birational contractions of Bln−1Pn−3
4.1. Main results of the section. The purpose of this section is to characterize
small Hassett spaces as birational contractions of Bln−1Pn−3 associated with Mori
chambers. From this, we deduce that small Hassett spaces are Mori dream spaces
and, over the complex numbers, of Fano type. This highlights the simplicity of
these spaces in contrast with M 0,n. As a further consequence, our characterization
shows that the birational contractions of Bln−1Pn−3 with Q-factorial image are
smooth; this result was proved for the small Q-factorial modifications in [BM21,
Theorem 2.32]. The main results of the section are summarized in Proposition
4.5. The VGIT theory of (P1)n with the action of PGL2 and the Mori chamber
decomposition of Bln−1Pn−3 play a central role in the proof.

5. The Mori cone of small Hassett spaces
In this section, we prove that the effective and the Mori cone of small Hassett
spaces are generated by strata. The generation of the effective cone is induced
by Bln−1Pn−3 and its birational contractions. Moreover, this highlights another
difference between small Hassett spaces and M 0,n, whose effective cone is not
generated by 1-codimensional strata for n ≥6 (see [HT01]), and not even finitely
generated for n ≥8 (see [Mul25]).

erated by 1-codimensional strata. Moreover, 1-codimensional strata are precisely
the extremal rays if and only if there are no subsets I ⊂{1, . . . , n} with |I| = n−2

The next natural case of Hassett spaces to consider is when they parametrize stable
curves with at most two irreducible components. These Hassett spaces form an
inductive family. However, their effective cone is not generated by 1-codimensional
strata in general, as M 0,6 is of this kind. An inductive argument might still be
possible if the effective cone turns out to be finitely generated with generators of
