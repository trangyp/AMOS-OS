The Fractional Dunkl Laplacian: Extension Problem and
Fundamental Solution
Chaabane REJEB∗
Abstract
Consider the Dunkl Laplacian ∆k associated with a root system Φ in Rd and a nonnegative
multiplicity function k on Φ. In this paper, we establish a Caffarelli-Silvestre characteriza-
tion for the fractional Dunkl Laplacian through an extension problem. We also express the
corresponding fundamental solution in terms of the ∆k-Riesz kernel and prove a fractional
Nash-type inequality.
MSC (2020): 26A33, 47G20, 35R11, 35S05.
Key words: Dunkl Laplacian, Fractional Dunkl Laplacian, Extension problem, Riesz kernel and potential.

Introduction
The fractional Laplacian (−∆)α on Rd, 0 < α < 1, is one of the most prominent representatives
of the family of nonlocal operators.
It arises in many areas, including diffusion phenomena,
optimization, probability, finance and others. In their seminal work [3], Caffarelli and Silvestre gave
a characterization of the fractional Laplacian as the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map for an extension
problem to the half-space (0, +∞) × Rd. This point of view allows in somewhat to avoid the
nonlocal property of the fractional Laplacian by reducing it to a local operator acting on functions
defined on the cylinder (0, +∞) × Rd.
In addition, the Caffarelli-Silvestre extension technique turns out to be very fruitful for recovering
an elliptic PDE approach in a nonlocal framework and has since been applied in numerous works
(see, for instance, [4, 17, 35, 36] and the references therein).
The Dunkl Laplacian ∆k is a deformation of the usual Laplace operator with a differential-
difference additional terms that involve the action of a finite Coxeter group, i.e., a finite Euclidean
reflection group, (see (1.3) below). Extensive studies have been carried out on analysis associated
with the Dunkl Laplacian (see for instance, [9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 25, 27, 29, 32, 33]).
It generates a Markovian C0-semigroup

et∆k : t ≥0
	
which can be regarded as a generalization
of the usual multidimensional Gaussian semigroup (see [29, 30, 32] and Appendix A.3). This fact
together with the standard Bochner subordination principle lead to define the α-fractional Dunkl
heat semigroup, 0 < α < 1, whose infinitesimal generator is the operator −(−∆k)α.
The main purpose of this paper is to investigate results for the fractional Dunkl Laplacian.
More precisely, we provide a heat semigroup and a pseudodifferential representation formula,
derive a fundamental solution and a fractional heat kernel, establish a fractional Nash inequality
and obtain a Caffarelli-Silvestre extension problem characterization.
We now proceed to outline the main elements of our setting. Consider a Coxeter root system
Φ in Rd, i.e., Φ is a finite subset of Rd \ {0} such that for every υ ∈Φ, Φ ∩Rυ = {±υ} and
συ(Φ) = Φ, where συ is the Euclidean reflection with respect to the hyperplane Hυ orthogonal to
υ. Let W = W(Φ) be the finite Coxeter group generated by the reflections συ, υ ∈Φ. For more
detailed treatment on the topics of root systems and their reflection groups, we refer to [20, 22].
The root system Φ can be partitioned into disjoint orbits of the roots under the action of the
group W, and we may assign a parameter k, called a multiplicity, to each orbit. This defines a
multiplicity function k on Φ.
