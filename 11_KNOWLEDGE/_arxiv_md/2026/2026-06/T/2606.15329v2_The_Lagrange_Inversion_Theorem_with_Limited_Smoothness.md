The Lagrange Inversion Theorem with Limited Smoothness
J. Thomas Beale
Department of Mathematics, Duke University, Box 90320
Durham, North Carolina 27708, U.S.A.
Abstract
The Lagrange Inversion Theorem provides a formula for the power series of the
inverse of an analytic function. We present a straightforward extension to functions
with a finite number of derivatives.
The Lagrange Inversion Theorem provides a formula for the power series of the inverse of
an analytic function. The book [3] has a historical discussion and a proof using the Cauchy
integral formula. The Theorem is stated in various references, e.g. [1,5]. It is useful to be
able to apply the formula more generally to functions of limited smoothness. The purpose
of this note is to provide a straightforward extension to functions with a finite number of
derivatives, since we have not been able to find such a version available. Related results
extending a different version to nonanalytic functions are given in [2,4], and there are results
for formal power series.

with remainder O(|x|N). The same is true for ϕ(x) = x/f(x) and ψ(x) = x/p(x), according

in the product depends only on the coefficients of xm for m ≤n in the factors. Thus, since
the expansions for ϕ(x) and ψ(x) match to order N −1, the same is true for ϕ(x)n and ψ(x)n.
