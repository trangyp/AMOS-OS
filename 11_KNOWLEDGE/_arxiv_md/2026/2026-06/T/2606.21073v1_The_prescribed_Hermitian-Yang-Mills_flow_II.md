1. Introduction
This paper continues our previous work [XYY26+], where we introduced the
prescribed Hermitian–Yang–Mills flow and established its convergence under various
differential-geometric conditions, with applications to solving general prescribed
Hermitian–Yang–Mills tensor equations.
Here, building on these results, we prove
convergence of the flow under algebraic-geometric conditions and thereby obtain
solutions to the prescribed Hermitian–Yang–Mills tensor equations in this new setting.

Remark 1.8. The Griffiths conjecture asserts that every ample vector bundle over a
compact complex manifold admits a Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric. It is the vector
bundle analogue of Kodaira’s embedding theorem, which states that any ample line
bundle admits a smooth metric with positive curvature. The conjecture was settled for
compact Riemann surfaces through several independent approaches:
∙Campana and Flenner [CF90] proved it using base-change techniques for algebraic
curves (see also [Ume73]).
∙Li, Zhang, and Zhang [LZZ25] established the result by employing the refined
continuity method of Uhlenbeck-Yau [UY86]. They established that the existence
of a Hermitian metric with positive Hermitian-Yang-Mills tensor is equivalent to a
condition analogous to (1.7), which is a special case of Corollary 1.2.
∙Recently, Murakami [Mur26] provided an alternative proof utilizing the Demailly
system proposed in [Dem21]. See also the approaches in [Pin21].
∙Our proof provides a complete picture of the Griffiths-positive curvature tensor by
constructing a Hermitian metric with prescribed curvature.
The Griffiths conjecture has also been investigated in greater generality; see, e.g.,
Berndtsson [Ber09], as well as Liu, Sun, and the second-named author [LSY13]. More
recently, an RC-positivity analogue of the Griffiths conjecture was proposed in [Yang18]
and [Yang20], which also serves as the primary motivation for this series of works.
