THE MINIMUM GENUS OF GALOIS COVERS OF CURVES
MANISH KUMAR AND POULAMI MANDAL
Abstract. Let V −→X be a G-Galois connected cover of smooth projective connected
curves over an algebraically closed field k of positive characteristic p with the branch locus
contained in a finite subset of closed points SX in X, where G is a finite cyclic p-group.
Let l be a prime number other than p. Let Γ be an extension of an elementary abelian
l-group H by G. We find G-stable submodules of the l-torsion of the Picard group of V
and its generalisation. This is used to describe a method for finding the minimum genus
of Γ-covers of X, ´etale over X \ SX, dominating V ; and also the minimum of the genera
of Γ-covers of P1 ´etale over A1.
1. Introduction
Let X be a smooth connected projective curve over an algebraically closed field k of
characteristic p > 0, and SX be a finite subset of closed points in X. Since the ´etale
fundamental group π´et
1 (X \ SX) is a profinite group, understanding its finite quotients is
helpful in understanding its structure. Abhyankar’s conjecture on affine curves, proved
by Raynaud and Harbater ([21, Th´eor`eme 2.2.1 ], [6, Theorem 6.2] and [7, Corollary 4.7])
states that any finite group is a quotient of π´et
1 (X \ SX) if and only if the maximal prime-
to-p quotient of this group has a generating set of cardinality less than 2gX + rX −1,
where gX is the genus of X and rX > 0 is the cardinality of SX. However, this does not
completely describe the profinite group structure of π´et
1 (X \SX). Given two finite quotient
groups Γ and G of π´et
1 (X \ SX) where G is a quotient of Γ, we want to know how they fit
in the inverse system of finite quotients of π´et
1 (X \ SX). In other words, given a G-cover
ψ : V −→X ´etale away from SX, we are interested in Γ-covers W −→X ´etale away from
SX that dominate V −→X. Note that SX contains the branch locus of ψ, but it may
also contain some non-branch points.
The existence and non-existence of such Γ-covers have been studied in [18], [9], [12].
Also see survey papers [20] and [8] for more details.
A finite group is called quasi-p if it is generated by its Sylow-p subgroups. Let H be
the subgroup of Γ such that Γ/H ∼= G. When SX is non-empty and H is quasi-p, such
Γ-covers always exist (see [18, Theorem B], [7, Corollary 4.6]). If SX is empty and H
is a p-group, the existence of these Γ-covers was discussed by Pacheco, Stevenson ([17,
Theorem 1.3]) and also Borne ([1, Theorem 1.1]). Hence, we investigate the situation
when H is prime-to-p (abelian).

Another way to understand the ´etale fundamental group is to examine the ramification
groups at the branch points of Galois ´etale covers. One interesting question is to find

least genus is of interest. In [16, Theorem 4.6] and [2, Corollary 2.2], some Galois ´etale
covers of A1 with Galois group isomorphic to alternating groups were constructed, and it

actions of Z/pZ on (Z/lZ)n (with even n allowed to vary).
