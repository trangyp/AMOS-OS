Abstract
We prove that the differential of the log of the partition function for the 2-matrix model with quartic
interactions converges in a certain double-scaling regime to the differential of the τ-function for the (3, 4)
string equation. This confirms the convergence of the critical Ising model on random surfaces to the
(3, 4) topological minimal model, which was stated in the works of Douglas and Shenker, Br´ezin and
Kazakov, and Gross and Migdal. Our analysis is based on a steepest-descent analysis of a Riemann-
Hilbert problem associated to a family of biorthogonal polynomials.
New features in the matching
problem in the construction of local parametrices appear.

The relation between matrix integrals like (1.1) and Painlev´e equations (which we shall refer to as
critical phenomena) has been studied extensively for almost 40 years. The interest in this relation stemmed
from the connection of these critical phenomena to the minimal models of conformal field theory coupled
to topological gravity. In the mid-80s, Kazakov and Boulatov [Kaz86; BK86] demonstrated that matrix
models held promise in predicting what came to be known as the Kniznik Polyakov Zamolodchikov (KPZ)
scaling relations [KPZ88]. Several years later, the seminal works of Douglas and Shenker [DS90], Br´ezin
and Kazakov [Br´e+90], and Gross and Migdal [GM90a; GM90b], showed how in certain ‘double scaling’
limits, the partition function for a given matrix model will converge to a τ-function for the corresponding
Painlev´e equation, which could then be thought of as a partition function for the corresponding ‘topological
minimal model’.
These ideas were developed more analytically in the works of Fokas, Its, and Kitaev
[FIK91; FIK92], where the importance of the isomonodromy approach was elucidated in the case of the

work is essential for dealing with higher order criticalities in the 2-matrix model.
