the weaker factor s−1/2+β controls this corner and still leads to the explicit integral
estimate (5.41).

gral estimate (5.44). If s−1/2+β were used instead on S, the corresponding integrated
bound (5.44) would contain an extra factor ϕ−1/2 and therefore again would not be
