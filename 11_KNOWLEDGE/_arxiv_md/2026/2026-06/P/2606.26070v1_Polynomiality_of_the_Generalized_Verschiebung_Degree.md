Abstract. For a general curve in positive characteristic, taking the Frobenius pullback
induces a generically finite rational map V on the moduli space of rank 2 vector bundles
with trivial determinant. Recently, Kondo–Wakabayashi show that the generic degree of
V , considered as a function on the characteristic of the base field, is a quasi-polynomial.
In this paper, we show that this quasi-polynomial is indeed a polynomial, and we write
out this polynomial explicitly.

2.2. The set EdP,N,G.
For us, a finite graph G consists of finite sets V (G), E(G), and H(G) of vertices, edges,
and half-edges, together with maps ν : H(G) →V (G) and ϵ : H(G) →E(G), such that
every fiber of ϵ has cardinality 2. Multiple edges and loops are allowed. If the two half-
edges over an edge e have the same image under ν, then e is a loop. For a vertex v, the
set Hv = ν−1(v) is the set of branches incident to v, counted with multiplicity. A graph is
trivalent if |Hv| = 3 for every vertex v. In particular, a loop at v contributes two of the
three incident branches. Let G be a finite trivalent graph with edge set E(G).
Definition 8. [3, §5.1] Let EdP,N,G be the set of functions ℓ: E(G) →Z≥0 such that, at
every vertex v of G, the three incident branch labels form a triple in †CN(P).

As explained in [12, §1], one motivation for the introduction of opers is that, over C,

One is interested in studying how special the canonical oper is. For example, the as-

p-curvature. Among the opers with nilpotent p-curvatures, the ones with zero p-curvatures
have been particularly studied.
Dormant opers.
An oper (P, ∇) on X is called dormant if its p-curvature ψ(∇) vanishes. Equivalently,
by Cartier descent and the ´etale local triviality of PGL-torsors, this means that the pair
(P, ∇) is ´etale locally isomorphic to the constant P1-bundle together with the canonical
connection.
