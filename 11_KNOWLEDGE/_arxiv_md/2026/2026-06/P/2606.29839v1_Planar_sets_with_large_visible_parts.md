The visibility problem in fractal geometry is the quest for finding upper bounds on the
Hausdorff dimension of VisσpKq, typically when K is compact. The first positive re-
sults were published by Järvenpää, Järvenpää, MacManus, and O’Neil [7] in 2003. They
showed that if K Ă R2 is (i) a quasi-circle or (ii) a connected self-similar set without
rotations, then dimH VisσpKq ď 1 for all σ P r0, 1q. They also showed that the visible
parts of graphs Γ Ă R2 of continuous functions r0, 1s Ñ r0, 1s are at most 1-dimensional
in all directions, except possibly one: the (bi-)visible part of Γ in the vertical direction is
evidently dimH Γ-dimensional, and dimH Γ can take any value in r1, 2s.
While no "visibility conjecture" was formulated in [7], it soon turned into common
belief that if K Ă R2 is compact, then dimH VisσpKq ď 1 for almost all σ P r0, 1q. This has
been explicitly suggested (at least) in [12, Problem 11], [6, Conjecture 1.3], [5, Section 10],
and [4, Section 7]. The hypothesis has been confirmed for certain special families of sets
such as quasi-circles, fractal percolation, and some self-similar and self-affine sets (see
[1, 6, 7, 9, 18]). There is also a strong partial result for planar continua due to O’Neil [14].
In [8], it is shown that if s P p1, 2s, and K Ă R2 is a compact set with HspKq ă 8, then
HspVisσpKqq “ 0 for a.e. σ P r0, 1q.
For general compact sets, the first partial result appeared in [15], and this first a.e.
upper bound (1.99) was subsequently lowered by Matheus and D ˛abrowski [2, 11]. In
2026, we improved the bound further with A. Rutar [16] by establishing the following:
Theorem 1.4. Let K Ă R2 be compact. Then dimH VisσpKq ď 3
2 for a.e. σ P r0, 1q.
In this paper I prove that Theorem 1.4 is sharp:
Theorem 1.5. There exists a compact set K Ă R2 such that dimH Vis2
σpKq ě 3
2 for all σ P r0, 1q.
In particular dimH VisσpKq ě 3

While Theorems 1.4 and 1.5 resolve the visibility problem as commonly stated in the
literature, the problem remains open in the connected and Ahlfors regular cases:
Problem 1. Assume that K Ă R2 is compact and (a) connected, or (b) Ahlfors s-regular with
s P r0, 2s. Is it true that dimH VisσpKq ď mint1, su for a.e. σ P r1, 2s?
