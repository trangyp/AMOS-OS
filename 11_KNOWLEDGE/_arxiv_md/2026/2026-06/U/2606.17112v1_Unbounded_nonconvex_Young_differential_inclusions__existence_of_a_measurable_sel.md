Unbounded nonconvex Young differential inclusions:
existence of a measurable selection of solutions
Nathan Benichou
June 17, 2026
Abstract. We study the differential inclusion d𝑧𝑡∈𝐹(𝑧𝑡)d𝑥𝑡, with initial condition
𝑧0 = 𝜉, where 𝐹is a nonconvex-valued multifunction, and 𝑥a path of bounded 𝑞-
variation, for some 1 ⩽𝑞< 2, extending the work of Bailleul, Brault, and Coutin
(2020). We obtain existence of local and global solutions to this inclusion without
assuming 𝐹bounded. If 𝑧(𝜉, 𝑥) denotes such a solution, we obtain measurability of 𝑧
with respect to 𝑥and 𝜉. To establish this, we introduce a Skorokhod-type distance and
prove that Young integration is continuous with respect to it. By the way, we prove
that a compact-valued 𝛾-Hölder map 𝐹has, for any 𝑝> 1∕𝛾and 𝜉∈𝐹(0), a selection
𝑓(𝜉) of bounded 𝑝-variation, started at 𝜉, such that 𝑓is measurable in 𝜉.
