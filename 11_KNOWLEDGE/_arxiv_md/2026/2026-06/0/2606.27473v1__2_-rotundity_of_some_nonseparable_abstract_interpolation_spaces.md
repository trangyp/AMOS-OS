ABSTRACT INTERPOLATION SPACES
STEPHEN DLWORTH AND DENKA KUTZAROVA
Abstract. Generalizing a construction due to Argyros and Motakis [2],
we define a nonseparable abstract interpolation space associated to any
given reflexive space with an unconditional basis together with an in-
creasing sequence of compact families of finite subsets of an uncountable
set. Under a mild complexity assumption on the families, we prove that
the interpolation space admits a 2-rotund norm with an uncountable
1-unconditional basis.
1. Introduction
The notions of 2-rotund and weakly 2-rotund norms were introduced by
Milman [19] and are defined as follows.
Definition. Let X be a Banach space. We say that a norm ∥· ∥on X is
2-rotund (2R) (resp. weakly 2-rotund (W2R)) if for every (xn) ⊂X such
that ∥xn∥≤1 (n ≥1) and
lim
m,n→∞∥xm + xn∥= 2,
there exists x ∈X such that x = limn→∞xn strongly (resp. weakly).
It follows from a characterization of reflexivity due to James [15] that if
X admits an equivalent W2R norm then X is reflexive. H´ajek and Johanis
proved the converse: every reflexive Banach space admits an equivalent W2R
norm [13]. Odell and Schlumprecht [20] proved that every separable reflexive
Banach space X admits an equivalent 2R norm (cf. [12]). However, it is an
open question whether every reflexive Banach space admits an equivalent
2R norm.
In [9], motivated by Schreier hierarchy introduced by Alspach and Argyros
in [1] and a family of sets introduced by Benyamini and Starbird in [5], we
presented a general method for defining, for each countable ordinal α, a
family Fα for certain uncountable sets Γ. The construction is similar to that
of the transfinitely defined families introduced by Argyros and Motakis in [2].
We proved that the generalized Baernstein space B(Fα) has an equivalent
2R norm.
