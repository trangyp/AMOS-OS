1. Introduction
Let 𝑘be a perfect field and 𝑋be a quasiprojective subscheme of ℙ𝑛
𝑘.
For 𝑃∈𝑋, the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity 𝑒𝑃(𝑋) provides a coarse
measure of singularity of 𝑋at 𝑃. For example, if 𝑋is smooth at 𝑃, then
𝑒𝑃(𝑋) = 1; if 𝑋is reduced and equidimensional, the converse holds as
well ([Nag62, 40.6]).
One of the classical Bertini theorems says that if 𝑘is algebraically
closed and 𝑋is smooth, then a general hyperplane section of 𝑋is also
smooth. If 𝑘is infinite but not necessarily algebraically closed, then one
can still always find infinitely many smooth hyperplane sections of 𝑋.
When 𝑘is finite, the classical statement fails: one can construct smooth
𝑋so that no hyperplane sections, in fact no hypersurface sections up to a
fixed degree 𝑑, are smooth ([Kat99, Question 10], [Poo04, Theorem 3.1]).
