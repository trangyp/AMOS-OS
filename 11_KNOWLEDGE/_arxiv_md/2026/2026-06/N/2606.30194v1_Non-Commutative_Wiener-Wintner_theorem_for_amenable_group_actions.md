Abstract. Let G be a locally compact second countable amenable group acting on a finite von
Neumann algebra (M, τ) by trace-preserving automorphisms. In this article, we establish a Jacobs-
de Leeuw-Glicksberg decomposition for this action, obtaining a decomposition of M into its almost
periodic and weakly mixing components. As an application, we prove a noncommutative Wiener–
Wintner theorem for amenable group actions on finite von Neumann algebras.

2.1. Non-commutative Lp-spaces (1 ≤p ≤∞). Let M ⊆B(H) be a von Neumann algebra
equipped with a faithful, normal, semifinite (f.n.s.) trace τ. An (possibly unbounded) operator
x : D(x) ⊆H →H,
which is densely defined, closed, and affiliated with M, i.e, satisfying u′∗xu′ = x for every unitary
u′ ∈M′, will be denoted as x η M. Such an operator x is said to be τ-measurable if, for every ε > 0,
there exists a projection e ∈P(M) such that τ(1 −e) < ε and eH ⊆D(x). The collection of all
τ-measurable operators affiliated with M is then defined by
L0(M, τ) := { x η M : x is τ-measurable }.
The space L0(M, τ) forms a ∗-algebra under the adjoint operation, strong sum x+y := x + y, and
strong product x · y := xy, where x denotes the closure of x. Detailed descriptions may be found in
[SZ19, Hia21].
Several natural topologies arise on L0(M, τ). The first relevant one is the measure topology, whose
neighborhoods are of the form: x + N(ε, δ), ε, δ > 0, x ∈L0(M, τ), where
N(ε, δ) := { x ∈L0(M, τ) : there exist e ∈P(M) with τ(1 −e) < δ and ∥exe∥< ε }.
