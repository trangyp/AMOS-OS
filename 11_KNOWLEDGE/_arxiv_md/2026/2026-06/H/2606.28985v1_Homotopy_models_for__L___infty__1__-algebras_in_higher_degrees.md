the Whitehead theorem can be stated and proved following [2] (see Theorem 3.13):
Theorem 1.7 (Whitehead theorem). Over a field and for strict L∞[1]-algebras, a

The motivation for this work is to make use of the aforementioned structures
in developing a new, categorical definition of Kuranishi spaces [5], [6]. We remark
that Theorem 1.7 plays an essential role therein. It is through the notion of higher

objects compared to the strict and rigid existing definition in [3].
