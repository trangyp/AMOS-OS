VARIATION ON A THEME OF BEAUVILLE–VOISIN
ROBERT LATERVEER
ABSTRACT. The Beauville–Voisin conjecture is about the Chow ring of hyper-Kähler varieties.
We consider a variant version taking place in the ring of algebraic cycles modulo algebraic equiv-
alence. We prove this variant version is true in codimension at least 3 for Fano varieties of lines
on cubic fourfolds, and for double EPW sextics.
1. INTRODUCTION
Given a complex smooth projective variety 𝑋, let 𝐴∗(𝑋) = ⊕𝑖𝐴𝑖(𝑋) denote the Chow ring
with ℚ-coefficients [6], [18]. It is expected that for hyper-Kähler varieties, the Chow ring has
a particularly nice structure. Most famously, Beauville [1] and Voisin [17] have put forth the
following conjecture:
Conjecture 1.1 (Beauville–Voisin [1], [17]). Let 𝑋be a hyper-Kähler variety. The ℚ-algebra
⟨𝐴1(𝑋), 𝑐𝑗(𝑇𝑋)⟩⊂𝐴∗(𝑋)
generated by divisors and Chern classes of the tangent bundle injects into cohomology, under the
cycle class map.
Conjecture 1.1 is true in dimension 2 [3]. In dimension larger than 2, Conjecture 1.1 has been
verified for low-dimensional Hilbert schemes of K3 surfaces [17], for Fano varieties of lines
on cubic fourfolds [17], for generalized Kummer varieties [4] and for double EPW sextics [11].
Otherwise, this is wide open.
A few years ago, I proposed a variant version of Conjecture 1.1 [10]. This variant takes place
in the ring 𝐵∗(𝑋) of algebraic cycles with ℚ-coefficients modulo algebraic equivalence.
Conjecture 1.2 ([10]). Let 𝑋be a hyper-Kähler variety. The ℚ-algebra
⟨𝐵1(𝑋), 𝐵2(𝑋), 𝑐𝑗(𝑇𝑋)⟩⊂𝐵∗(𝑋)
generated by divisors, codimension 2 cycles and Chern classes of the tangent bundle injects into
cohomology, under the cycle class map.
Conjecture 1.2 does not follow from Conjecture 1.1 as such. However, as explained in [10,
Introduction], Conjecture 1.2 follows from the conjectural framework underlying Conjecture 1.1.

3. DOUBLE EPW SEXTICS
Notation 3.1. Double EPW sextics have been constructed by O’Grady [13], [14], [15] as double
covers 𝑋→𝑍of certain sextic hypersurfaces 𝑍⊂ℙ5. They form a locally complete family
of hyper-Kähler varieties of 𝐾3[2]-type. By construction, they come with a covering involution 𝜄
which is anti-symplectic.
