TUOMAS ORPONEN & ALEX RUTAR
ABSTRACT. Let K ⊂R2 be a compact set. For θ ∈S1, let Visθ(K) ⊂K be the
visible part of K in direction θ. We prove that dimH Visθ(K) ≤3
2 for H1 almost
every θ ∈S1. The previous record was dimH Visθ(K) ≤11/6 ≈1.833, due to
D. D ˛abrowski.
Our main tool is a variant of a recent incidence lower bound theorem due to
Cohen, Pohoata, and Zakharov where, roughly speaking, lines have been replaced
by rays, and δε-separated incidences are replaced by 1-separated incidences.

1.
INTRODUCTION
1.1. Visible parts and their dimensions. We start by introducing basic notation.
Notation 1.1. For z ∈R2 and σ ∈[−1, 1], let ℓ+
z,σ := {z + (r, σr) : r ≥0} be the ray with
slope σ starting from z. Similarly, let ℓz,σ := {z + (r, σr) : r ∈R} be the line with slope σ
passing through z. For ω = (z, σ) ∈R2 ×[−1, 1], we also write ℓ+
ω := ℓ+
z,σ and ℓω = ℓz,σ.
Definition 1.2 (Visσ(K)). Let K ⊂R2 be a compact set, and let σ ∈[−1, 1]. The visible
part of K in direction σ is the set
Visσ(K) := {z ∈K : K ∩ℓ+
z,σ = {z}}.
We are interested in the following conjecture.
Conjecture 1.3 (Visibility conjecture). Let K ⊂R2 be compact. Then dimH Visσ(K) ≤1
for H1-a.e. σ ∈[−1, 1].
More generally, the following estimate on the set of directions with exceptionally large
visible part seems plausible.
Conjecture 1.4 (Exceptional set estimate for visible parts). Let K ⊂R2 be compact and
α ∈[0, 1]. Then dimH Visσ(K) ≤2 −α for Hα-a.e. σ ∈[−1, 1].
Remark 1.5. Typically the visibility conjecture is stated in a form where directions are
parametrised by S1 in place of [−1, 1]; we also used this convention in the abstract.
It is easy to check that every upper bound for the variants in Conjecture 1.3 and
Conjecture 1.4 translates immediately to a corresponding upper bound for the S1-
variant.
Remark 1.6. Máthé has constructed1 for each α ∈[0, 1] a compact set N ⊂R2 such
that dimH N = 2 −α, and Visθ N = N in an α-dimensional set of directions θ ∈S1.
This shows that Conjecture 1.4 would be sharp if true. Mathé initially constructed his
example to show the sharpness of the main result in [DGO24+] concerning α-Nikodým
sets.
The visibility conjecture is a well-known open problem in geometric measure theory;
see, for instance, [JJMO03] [Mat04, Problem 11], [AJJ+12, (1.3)], [FF13, Conjecture 1.3]
or [Fal26+, Section 7]. The conjecture in R2 has been resolved for some special families
of sets such as quasi-circles and some classes of self-similar and self-affine sets (see
[AJJ+12; FF13; JJMO03; JJSW22; Ros21]). There is also a strong partial result for planar
continua due to O’Neil [ONe07]. In [JJN05], it is shown that if s ∈(1, 2], and K ⊂R2 is
a compact set with Hs(K) < ∞, then Hs(Visσ(K)) = 0 for a.e. σ ∈[−1, 1]. It however
remains open (under the same assumptions) whether dimH Visσ(K) < s for almost all
σ ∈[−1, 1].
The best previous result for general compact sets is due to D ˛abrowski [D ˛ab24], who
showed that dimH Visσ(K) ≤11/6 ≈1.833 for a.e. σ ∈[−1, 1]. Weaker bounds in this
generality were earlier obtained by Matheus and the first author; see [Mat21; Orp23].
1The details of Máthé’s construction are forthcoming, but a sketch can be found at https://www.
tu-chemnitz.de/mathematik/stochastik/fgs7/slides/Andras-Mathe.pdf.
