Rabinowitz Floer homology for Legendrian submanifolds in
prequantization bundles
Hanwool Bae, Jungsoo Kang, Sungho Kim
Abstract
Let Y be a prequantization bundle over an integral symplectic manifold (Σ, ω). Let L
be a closed monotone Lagrangian submanifold that admits a Legendrian lift L in Y . Under
the assumption that the minimal Maslov number NL of L is greater than 2, we define
the Rabinowitz Floer homology of L. We then establish an isomorphism between the
Zd-equivariant Rabinowitz Floer homology of L and the quantum homology of L, where
d is the degree of the covering map L →L. Under a more restrictive condition on NL,
we show that this map is a ring isomorphism. Using this isomorphism, we compute the
quantum homology ring of Lagrangian spheres in quadrics and two-step flag manifolds.
Furthermore, we investigate the implications of the quantum invertibility of ω for the
vanishing of the quantum homology of L and the obstructions to topologically simple
fillings of L. We also show that if (Σ, ω) admits a polarization and L is disjoint from the
Lagrangian trace, the quantum homology of L vanishes.

A similar vanishing property holds for Lagrangian submanifolds that are disjoint from the
Lagrangian traces in polarized symplectic manifolds. The notion of the Lagrangian trace was
introduced in [Bir06], where its role as an obstruction to displaceability was investigated. To
state our next result, which is along these lines, we recall the setting from [Bir06], see also
[Don96, Gir17]. Let (X, Ω, Σ) be a polarized symplectic manifold, meaning that (X, Ω) is a
closed symplectic manifold with an integral lift [Ω]Z ∈H2(X), (Σ, ω := Ω|Σ) is a symplectic
hypersurface such that the homology class [Σ] is Poincar´e dual to k[Ω]Z for some k ∈N,
and there is a Weinstein domain (W, λ, φ) such that the interior of W is symplectomorphic
to X \ Σ. Here λ is a Liouville 1-form and φ is a Morse function such that the Liouville
vector field Z is a gradient-like vector field of φ. The boundary Y := ∂W is a prequantization
bundle over (Σ, k[Ω]Z|Σ). The unstable manifold of Z at a critical point p of φ of Morse index
1
2 dim W is called a Lagrangian cocore disk, denoted by ∆(p). The boundary ∂∆(p) of ∆(p) is

2 dim W. Equivalently, the Lagrangian trace corresponds to the union of the
topological boundaries of int(∆(p)) ⊂X \ Σ within X. The Lagrangian spheres in (1.1) and
(1.2) are examples of Lagrangian traces.

exhibits a rigidity property. We refer to Section 6.3 for the proof.
