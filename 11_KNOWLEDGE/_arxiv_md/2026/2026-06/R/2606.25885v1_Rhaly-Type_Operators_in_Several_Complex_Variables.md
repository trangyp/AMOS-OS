Michael R. Pilla
Abstract. Recently, a generalization of the Ces`aro operator to several vari-
ables was introduced as a tuple acting on the Drury-Arveson space [14]. We
determine the norm of these Ces`aro-type operators and utilize it along with
this Ces`aro tuple definition to generalize the classical Rhaly operator and re-
cover some basic facts about this operator when applied to the generalization.
