MATRIX STABILITY AND MORITA INVARIANCE
EUGENIA ELLIS AND EMANUEL RODR´IGUEZ CIRONE
Abstract. Let G be a group. We prove that matrix stability for either G-
algebras or G-graded algebras guarantees Morita invariance. As a consequence,
bivariant algebraic K-theory (either G-equivariant or G-graded) is Morita in-
variant. In particular, we show that if G is a finite group acting freely on a
finite simplicial set X, then ℓX ⋊G and ℓX/G are kk-equivalent. Here, ℓY
denotes the ℓ-algebra of piecewise polynomial functions on Y with coefficients
in the ground ring ℓ.
1. Introduction
Let ℓbe a commutative ring with unit. Write Alg for the category of associative
and not necessarily unital ℓ-algebras and let Algu ⊆Alg be the subcategory whose
objects are the unital algebras and whose morphisms are unital algebra homomor-
phisms.
Two algebras R, S ∈Algu are Morita equivalent [14] if there exists a tuple
(R, S, P, Q, α, β) where P is an S-R-bimodule, Q is an R-S-bimodule, α : P ⊗R
Q →S is an S-bimodule isomorphism and β : Q ⊗S P →R is an R-bimodule
isomorphism. The morphisms α and β are moreover required to satisfy the mixed
associativity relations
α(p ⊗q) · p′ = p · β(q ⊗p′)
and
β(q ⊗p) · q′ = q · α(p ⊗q′)
for all p, p′ ∈P and q, q′ ∈Q.
It is well known that matrix algebras play an essential role in Morita theory. For
example, any unital algebra R is Morita equivalent to its algebra of n × n-matrices
MnR. Moreover, two unital algebras R and S are Morita equivalent if and only if
the algebras M∞R and M∞S are isomorphic; see, for example [1]. The notion of
Morita equivalence can be generalized both to algebras with the action of a group
and to graded algebras over a group. In the case of graded algebras over an abelian
group, the relation between Morita equivalences and matrix algebras we recently
studied in [1].

hand, F is called Morita invariant if its restriction to Algu is. On the other, F

2.3. G-Morita equivalence.
Definition 2.8. Two unital G-algebras R and S are G-Morita equivalent if there
exists a tuple (R, S, P, Q, α, β) satisfying the following conditions:
• The sets P and Q are G-modules equipped with an S-R-bimodule structure
and an R-S-bimodule structure, respectively. The actions of R and S on
P and Q are moreover compatible with the G-actions. That is, we have
g · (s · p · r) = (g · s) · (g · p) · (g · r)
g · (r · q · s) = (g · r) · (g · q) · (g · s)
for all r ∈R, s ∈S, p ∈P, q ∈Q, and g ∈G.
• The modules P ⊗RQ and Q⊗S P are equipped with the diagonal G-actions:
g · (p ⊗q) = (g · p) ⊗(g · q)
g · (q ⊗p) = (g · q) ⊗(g · p)
The maps α : P ⊗RQ →S and β : Q⊗S P →R are G-equivariant bimodule
isomorphisms satisfying the mixed associativity relations
α(p ⊗q) · p′ = p · β(q ⊗p′)
β(q ⊗p) · q′ = q · α(p ⊗q′)
for all p, p′ ∈P and q, q′ ∈Q.
We call such a tuple (R, S, P, Q, α, β) a G-Morita equivalence.
Remark 2.9. In the particular case where G = 1, Definition 2.8 recovers the usual
notion of Morita equivalence between unital algebras.
Remark 2.10. If (R, S, P, Q, α, β) is a G-Morita equivalence, we can consider
P ⊗R Q and Q ⊗S P as G-algebras with the following products:
(p ⊗q)(p′ ⊗q′) = p · β(q ⊗p′) ⊗q′
(q ⊗p)(q′ ⊗p′) = q · α(p ⊗q′) ⊗p′
With this structure, α and β are isomorphisms of G-algebras.

Corollary A.14. Let G be a finite group acting freely on a finite simplicial set X.
Then ℓX ⋊G and ℓX/G are kk-equivalent.
