1. Introduction
In p-adic Hodge theory, one of the most foundational results is that every weakly ad-
missible filtered (ϕ, N)-module is admissible. It implies that the Dst functor for semistable
Galois representations is fully faithful. In other words, the linear-algebraic data of filtered
(ϕ, N)-modules are enough to characterize crystalline and semistable Galois representations.
Several proofs of this foundational result exist in the literature (see [CF00, Col02, Ber08,
Kis06, FF18]). In their original approach, one of the key ingredients in the proof of Colmez
and Fontaine is the fundamental lemma in p-adic Hodge theory ([CF00, Proposition 2.1]).
Later, this fundamental lemma was elegantly refined by Colmez using the language of
Banach–Colmez spaces ([Col02, Proposition 7.11])1.
Recall that in [Col02] (where they are termed Espaces Vectoriels de dimension finie),
a Banach–Colmez space is a functor on the category of sympathetic Cp-algebras. Let V1
denote the Banach–Colmez space which evaluates to Λ on any sympathetic algebra Λ (see
[Col02, Example 7.1(ii)]). Colmez’s refined fundamental lemma can be stated as follows:
Theorem 1.1 ([Col02, Proposition 7.11]2). Let W be a Banach–Colmez space admitting a
presentation of the form
