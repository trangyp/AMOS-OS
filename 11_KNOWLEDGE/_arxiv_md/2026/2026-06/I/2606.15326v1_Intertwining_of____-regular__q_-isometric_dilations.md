ABSTRACT. A tuple T = (T1,...,Tk) of contractions on a Hilbert space H is said to be q-commuting
with ∥q∥= 1 if there exists a family of scalars q = {qij ∈C : |qij| = 1, qij = q−1
ji , 1 ≤i < j ≤k}
such that TiTj = qi jTjTi for 1 ≤i < j ≤k. In this article, we characterize q-commuting pairs of
contractions with ∥q∥= 1 that admit a minimal ∗-regular q-isometric dilation. We present sufficient
conditions for the commutant lifting theorem for such pairs. Moreover, sufficient conditions are
obtained for a q-commuting triple of contractions with ∥q∥= 1 to admit a q-isometric dilation.
1. INTRODUCTION
Throughout the paper, all operators are bounded linear maps on complex Hilbert spaces. We write
C, D and T for the complex plane, unit disc {z : |z| < 1} and unit circle {z : |z| = 1} respectively,
and B(H ) for the algebra of operators on a Hilbert space H . The symbols N, Z and Z+ denote
the sets of natural numbers, integers and non-negative integers respectively.
A contraction is an operator of norm at most 1. A classical theorem due to Sz.-Nagy [10] states
that every contraction dilates to a unitary. Ando [1] extended Nagy’s dilation theorem to pairs
of commuting contractions. However, Parrott [13] showed that a unitary dilation need not exist
for commuting triples of contractions. In this direction, Brehmer [4] introduced a stronger notion
of unitary dilation, called a regular unitary dilation, and characterized the commuting families
of contractions that admit such dilations. Another fundamental result in dilation theory is the
commutant lifting theorem, which states that if T and T ′ are contractions on Hilbert spaces H
and H ′, respectively, with minimal isometric dilations V on K and V ′ on K ′ respectively, and
if an operator A : H →H ′ intertwines T and T ′, i.e., AT = T ′A, then there exists an operator
B : K →K ′ with ∥B∥= ∥A∥such that BV = V ′B and APH = PH ′B. In contrast, M¨uller [9]
showed that the commutant lifting type theorem fails in general for two pairs of contractions even
if the pairs admit regular unitary dilations. Furthermore, the authors of [5, 6] present sufficient
conditions ensuring the existence of such a lifting, where the notion of ∗-regular isometric dilation
plays a crucial role. Recall from [5, 16] that an isometric dilation (V1,...,Vk) of a commuting tuple
(T1,...,Tk) of contractions on a Hilbert space H is said to be ∗-regular if for all m1,...,mk ∈Z,

A seminal result from [4] states that a commuting tuple T = (T1,...,Tk) of contractions has a
regular unitary dilation if and only if it satisfies Brehmer’s positivity condition, i.e.,
∆(u) =

The authors of [12] extended this result in the q-commutative setting, proving the following.
Theorem 1.2 ([12], Theorems 1.7 & 1.8). A q-commuting tuple of contractions with ∥q∥= 1 has
a regular q-unitary dilation if and only if it satisfies Brehmer’s positivity condition. In particular,
every doubly q-commuting tuple of contractions admits a regular q-unitary dilation.
It is evident from Theorem 1.2 that every doubly q-commuting tuple of contractions admits a
minimal q-isometric dilation. A natural question arises if such minimal q-isometric dilations are
also doubly q-commuting. Motivated by the works [5, 16], the author of [17] characterized such
q-commuting minimal isometric dilations.
Theorem 1.3 ([17], Theorems 2.15 & 2.16). Let T = (T1,...,Tk) be a q-commuting tuple of con-
tractions acting on a Hilbert space H . Then a minimal q-isometric dilation of T is doubly q-
commuting if and only if it is ∗-regular. Furthermore, T is doubly q-commuting if and only if T has
a minimal q-isometric dilation which is both regular and ∗-regular.
As shown in the above theorem, ∗-regular q-isometric dilations are essential in the study of
q-commuting contractions with ∥q∥= 1. Motivated by this, we provide in Theorem 2.1 a char-
acterization of q-commuting pairs of contractions with ∥q∥= 1 that admit a minimal ∗-regular
q-isometric dilation, leading to a structure theorem for such pairs. In particular, we show that for
any such pair (T1,T2), the operator T2 lifts to a contraction on the minimal isometric dilation space
of T1 that doubly q-commutes with the dilation of T1. Building on this, we obtain in Theorem 2.3 a
commutant lifting theorem for two q-commuting pairs of contractions with ∥q∥= 1, which is one
of the main results of the article.
As mentioned earlier, Parrott [13] constructed an example of a commuting triple of contrac-
tions which does not admit a commuting isometric dilation, and therefore an analogous conclusion
fails in the q-commutative setting. However, Theorem 1.3 guarantees that a doubly q-commuting
triple (T1,T2,T3) of contractions admits a q-isometric dilation. This naturally leads to the question
of whether the same conclusion holds for a strictly smaller subclass. As an application of The-
orem 2.3, we first present in Theorem 2.5 a sufficient condition for a q-isometric dilation for a
q-commuting triple of contractions with ∥q∥= 1. We then conclude this article by showing that
the doubly q-commutative condition on the entire triple (T1,T2,T3) may be relaxed and replaced
by requiring only that the pairs (T1,T2) and (T1,T3) be doubly q-commuting.
