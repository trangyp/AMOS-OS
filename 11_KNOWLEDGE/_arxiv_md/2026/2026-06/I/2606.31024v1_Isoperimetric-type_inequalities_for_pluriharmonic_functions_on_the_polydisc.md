theory, is that

argument. These ingredients allow us to transfer sharp holomorphic isoperimetric
inequalities to the pluriharmonic setting while keeping the loss entirely in the Riesz
constants.

Since holomorphic functions form a subclass of pluriharmonic functions, Marković’s
theorem gives the elementary lower bound Cp,m,n ≥1. For the holomorphic subclass
itself, the sharp constant is 1. Thus the open problem concerns whether the Riesz-
transfer constant ApBmp is genuinely forced by non-holomorphic pluriharmonic func-
tions. Natural test cases include normalized Szegő kernels and planar extremizing
sequences embedded into one coordinate.
Let us remark that this question is indeed quite difficult, even in the planar case.
For example, in the papers of Kalaj–Mestrović [8] and Kalaj [6], only the unit disk
was considered, yet the sharp constant remains unknown. In the planar case, the
constants obtained by the present transfer method coincide with the corresponding
constants obtained in [6, 8].
