The key point in the proof of [54, Theorem 1.2] is the handling of the boundary. After
obtaining local compactness in the whole space RN, Ponce established an Lp-estimate near
the boundary of Ω. Roughly speaking, this estimate shows that the Lp norm over the

compactness to compactness on the whole bounded Lipschitz domain. However, its proof
relies heavily on specific properties of Lp spaces, in particular polar decomposition and
translation invariance, which are not available in general function spaces.
Recall that the ball quasi-Banach function space was introduced by Sawano et al. [60]
in order to handle, in a unified way, many important function spaces arising in harmonic
analysis and partial differential equations, such as Morrey spaces, Orlicz spaces, and vari-
able Lebesgue spaces. It is worth pointing out that this framework is particularly flexible,
since it also contains several spaces which are not covered by the classical Banach function
spaces of Bennett and Sharpley [6].

carefully decompose the domain Ωinto finitely many interior cubes and boundary pieces
