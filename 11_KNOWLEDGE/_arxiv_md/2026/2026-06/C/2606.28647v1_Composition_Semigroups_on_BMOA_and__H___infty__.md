COMPOSITION SEMIGROUPS ON BMOA AND H∞
AUSTIN ANDERSON, MIRJANA JOVOVIC, AND WAYNE SMITH
Abstract. We study [φt, X], the maximal space of strong continuity for a
semigroup of composition operators induced by a semigroup {φt}t≥0 of ana-
lytic self-maps of the unit disk, when X is BMOA, H∞or the disk algebra. In
particular, we show that [φt, BMOA] ̸= BMOA for all nontrivial semigroups.
We also prove, for every semigroup {φt}t≥0, that limt→0+ φt(z) = z not just
pointwise, but in H∞norm. This provides a unified proof of known results
about [φt, X] when X ∈{Hp, Ap, B0, VMOA}.
1. Introduction
Let D denote the unit disk {z : |z| < 1} and H(D) the set of analytic functions
on D. A one-parameter semigroup {φt}t≥0 of analytic functions on D is a family
of analytic functions φt : D →D that satisfies the following three conditions:
(SG1) φ0 is the identity, i.e. φ0(z) = z, z ∈D;
(SG2) φs+t = φs ◦φt, for all t, s ≥0;
(SG3) the mapping (t, z) →φt(z) is continuous on [0, ∞) × D.
In the trivial case, φt(z) = z for all t ≥0.
Otherwise, we say that {φt} is
nontrivial. For {φt}, consider the set of linear operators {Ct}, where Ct(f) = f ◦φt
for f ∈H(D). Then C0 is the identity operator and Cs+t = CsCt, and so {Ct} is
called a composition semigroup. If Ct is a bounded operator on some Banach space
X ⊂H(D) for all t ≥0, we say that the semigroup {φt} acts on X. If in addition
the strong continuity condition
lim
t→0+ ∥f ◦φt −f∥X = 0
holds for all f ∈X, then it is said that {φt} generates {Ct}, and {Ct} is strongly
continuous on X.
The study of composition semigroups on spaces of analytic functions began in [3],
where Berkson and Porta investigated the basic structure of semigroups {φt} and
properties of {Ct} on the Hardy spaces of the disk. A survey of further developments
through 1998 of semigroups of composition operators acting on classical Banach
spaces can be found in [13]. More recent papers about composition semigroups
include [4], [5] and [6]. Banach spaces considered include the Hardy spaces Hp, the
Bergman spaces Ap, the disk algebra A, the Dirichlet space D, the Bloch space B,
BMOA, and their “little-oh” subspaces B0 and VMOA.
For a semigroup {φt} that acts on a Banach space X ⊂H(D), denote by [φt, X]
the maximal closed subspace of X on which {Ct} is strongly continuous. Thus

We note that Corollary 1.3 provides a unified proof of some of the known results
mentioned above. In particular, the corollary gives an alternate proof of the cases
that X ∈{Hp, Ap, B0, VMOA} in statement (i) above, since polynomials are dense
in these spaces, and also includes all of statement (iii). It also establishes that the
natural extension of statement (iii) to H∞is valid:
Corollary 1.4. For every semigroup {φt}, the disk algebra A satisfies
A ⊂[φt, H∞].
It is well known that a composition operator Cφ is bounded on the disk algebra
A if and only if φ ∈A. Hence a semigroup {φt} acts on A if and only if {φt} ⊂A.
Another consequence of Theorem 1.2 is the following:
Corollary 1.5. A semigroup {φt} satisfies {φt} ⊂A if and only if [φt, A] = A.
Corollary 1.5 should be compared to [6, Theorem 1.1], where additional condi-
tions are included with the statement that {φt} ⊂A. Theorem 1.2 allows removal
of these additional conditions.
