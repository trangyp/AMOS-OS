Definition 1. Let H be a complex Hilbert space. A map Υ : H →H is called a
conjugation if it satisfies the following properties:
(1) Conjugate-linearity: For all ξ1, ξ2 ∈H and λ1, λ2 ∈C, Υ(λ1ξ1 +λ2ξ2) =
λ1Υ(ξ1) + λ2Υ(ξ2).
(2) Involution: Υ2 = I, where I is the identity operator.
(3) Isometry: ∥Υξ∥= ∥ξ∥for all ξ ∈H.
Definition 2. Let L be a bounded linear operator on H, and let L∗denote its
adjoint. Then:
(1) L is called Υ-complex symmetric if L = ΥL∗Υ.
(2) L is self-adjoint if L = L∗.
(3) L is normal if LL∗= L∗L, or equivalently, ∥L∥= ∥L∗∥.
