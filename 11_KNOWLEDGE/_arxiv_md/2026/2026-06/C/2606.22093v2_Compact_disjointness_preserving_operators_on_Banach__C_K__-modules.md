COMPACT DISJOINTNESS PRESERVING
OPERATORS ON BANACH C(K)-MODULES.
ARKADY KITOVER AND MEHMET ORHON
Abstract. We show that some well known results concerning
compact disjointness preserving operators on Banach lattices can
be extended to the more general framework of finitely generated
Banach C(K)-modules. Specifically, we establish a series represen-
tation for such operators analogous to the de Pagter - Wickstead
and Arenson–Kitover theorems and provide some details about
their spectra.

For the reader’s convenience we briefly recall some basic facts about
Banach C(K)-modules (see [2])
A Banach space X is called a Banach C(K)-module if there is an
isometry m : C(K) →L(X), where L(X) is the space of all bounded
linear operators on X.
The center Z(X) of a Banach C(K)-module X is defined (see [2,
p.33]) as the closure of C(K) in L(X) in the strong operator topology.
In the current paper we will always assume that C(K) = Z(X).
For any x ∈X the subspace X(x) of X, X(x) = clX{fx : f ∈C(K)}
can be endowed with the structure of a Banach lattice with the cone
X(x)+ = clX{fx : f ∈C(K), f ≥0}. The space X(x) is called a cyclic
subspace of X.
By analogy with definition 1.1 we introduce the following definition
Definition 1.6. Let X be a Banach C(K)-module. An element x ∈X
is called an atom if the cyclic subspace X(x) is one-dimensional.

3. Some remarks about the spectrum of compact
disjointness preserving operators
We now turn to spectral properties of compact disjointness preserv-
ing operators.
In [5] H. Kamowitz proved the following
Theorem 3.1. Let K be a compact Hausdorff space and T be a compact
weighted composition operator on C(K), Tf = w(f ◦φ), where w ∈
C(K) and φ : K →K is a continuous map. The following statements
are equivalent
(1) λ ∈σ(T) \ {0}.
(2) There is a φ-periodic k ∈K of the smallest period p such that
λp = w(k)w(φ(k)) . . . w(φp−1(k)).
In the current section we will prove the following generalization of
Theorem 3.1.
Theorem 3.2. Let X be a Banach lattice.
Let T : X →X be a
continuous disjointness preserving operator.
Assume that λ ̸= 0, λ is an isolated point in σ(T) and moreover that
|λ| is isolated in the set |σ(T)| = {|α|, α ∈σ(T)}. Assume also that
dim ker (λI −T) < ∞.
Then there are an atom F ∈X′ and a p ∈N such that (T ′)pF = λpF
and the elements F, . . . , (T ′)p−1F are pairwise disjoint atoms.
Proof. Let X be a Banach lattice. From our assumptions follows that
there is an R, 0 < R < |λ|, such that σ(T) ∩RT = ∅, where T denotes
the unit circle. Let X1 and X2 be the spectral subspaces corresponding,

However, we can show that this condition is unnecessary
under the additional assumption that the adjoint operator T ′ preserves

compact disjointness preserving operator, and λ ∈σ(T) \ {0}. Then
there are an atom F ∈X′ and a p ∈N such that (T ′)pF = λpF and
the elements F, . . . , (T ′)p−1F are pairwise disjoint atoms.
