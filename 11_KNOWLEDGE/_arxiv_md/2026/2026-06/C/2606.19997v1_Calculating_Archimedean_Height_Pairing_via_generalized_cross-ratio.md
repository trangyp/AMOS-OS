Abstract
I answer a question which S. Bloch asked during the Regulators V conference in June 2024 in Pisa.
During the last several years, Spencer Bloch emphasized that Archimedean hight pairings between
two homologically trivial cycles of complimentary arithmetic dimensions, see (1) for the precise condition,
or the closely related classes in the biextension, should lead to interesting analogs of the classical cross
ratio. In this note I calculate such a generalized cross-ratio for planes in Pn.
