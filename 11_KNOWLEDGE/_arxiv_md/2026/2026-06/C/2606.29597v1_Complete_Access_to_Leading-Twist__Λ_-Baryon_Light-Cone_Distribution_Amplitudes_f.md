We report the first complete lattice-QCD determination of the leading-twist light-cone distribution
amplitudes (LCDAs) of the Λ baryon, obtained as full two-dimensional functions of the valence-
quark momentum fractions. The calculation employs large-momentum effective theory to relate the
light-cone amplitudes to equal-time nonlocal three-quark matrix elements of boosted Λ baryons.
Controlled physical extrapolations to the continuum, physical pion mass, and infinite momentum,
together with hybrid renormalization, large-λ extrapolation, and perturbative matching, yield the
three leading-twist LCDAs V , A, and T.
Using the lattice-determined LCDAs in place of the
asymptotic form, we find an O(10%) shift in the Λ electromagnetic form factor at perturbative
scales, demonstrating that the full two-dimensional LCDAs, rather than only their asymptotic shapes
or lowest moments, are required for precision baryonic phenomenology. This work, together with
the companion paper [1] detailing the baryon-LaMET framework, provides the first complete multi-
dimensional x-dependent baryon LCDAs from first principles and establishes a benchmark for lattice
access to multi-dimensional baryon structure.

This multi-dimensional structure is not only of intrinsic
interest, but also required for phenomenological applica-
tions. Baryon LCDAs enter QCD factorization formulas
for hard exclusive baryonic processes, including electro-
magnetic and transition form factors and weak decays of
heavy baryons. Baryon electromagnetic form factors pro-
vide a mature benchmark: they probe the internal struc-
ture of baryons and test hard-scattering factorization at
large momentum transfer. Recent next-to-leading-order
studies of nucleon Dirac form factors show that, once the
perturbative kernels are under control, the predictions
are sensitive to the shape of the leading-twist baryon
LCDAs [5, 6]. A first-principles determination of the full
x-dependent LCDA is therefore needed beyond asymp-
totic forms or a few low moments. This need is further
reinforced by recent progress in baryonic flavor and CP
physics, including CP violation in baryon decays [7–10]
and hyperon EDMs [11], where controlled baryonic non-
perturbative inputs are essential.
Existing information on baryon LCDAs has mainly
come from QCD sum rules and OPE-based lattice cal-
culations of local moments [12–16]. These studies have
provided important constraints on decay constants and
first moments, including continuum-extrapolated lattice
results for octet-baryon LCDAs [17, 18]. However, only
lowest-order moment-based information is far from de-
termining the full two-dimensional distribution required
in factorization formulas. This limitation is further ex-
acerbated for the Λ baryon, where certain antisymmetric
leading-twist structures have vanishing zeroth moments
and therefore no independent leading-twist decay con-
stants, even though their first moments can be accessed.
A direct determination of the full Λ LCDAs is therefore
needed to go beyond first-moment based information.
Large momentum effective theory (LaMET) [19, 20]
provides a first-principles formalism to access light-cone
physics in Euclidean lattice QCD. After extensive appli-
cations to parton distributions and meson distribution
amplitudes, LaMET has become a well established ap-
proach for extracting light-cone correlations from equal-
time spatial correlators of boosted hadrons. Extending
this framework to baryon LCDAs, however, introduces
qualitatively new challenges, including nonlocal three-
quark operators with two independent spatial separa-
tions, genuinely two-dimensional hybrid renormalization
and large-λ extrapolation, and a more involved perturba-
tive matching including hybrid counterterms and double
plus distributions. Recent theoretical developments have
established the corresponding factorization and renor-
malization structure [21–24], and our previous studies
have demonstrated the feasibility of applying LaMET to
baryon LCDAs, as well as implementing a systematic hy-
brid renormalization procedure [25, 26].

formed on seven 2 + 1-flavor lattice ensembles gener-

clover fermions coupled with the Symanzik-improved
gauge action [29, 30]. The ensembles cover pion masses
from mπ ≈317 to 136 MeV and lattice spacings from
a ≈0.105 to 0.052 fm, while the boosted Λ baryon

3 GeV, as summarized in Table I. This setup of ensem-

boosted baryon matrix elements, we employ momentum
smearing [31] for the quark propagators, one-step hyper-
cubic (HYP) smearing [32, 33] for the spatial gauge links
in the nonlocal operators, and a kinematically enhanced
source-side interpolator [34, 35] designed to increase the
overlap with the boosted ground state.

Following the conventions of
Refs. [36, 37], we project our results onto the correspond-

region and determine the expansion coefficients for the
Λ-baryon V , A, and T amplitudes. The recommended
second-order parameterization is summarized in Table II.
This truncation captures the main features resolved by
the lattice data while avoiding unnecessary higher-order
flexibility. Stability checks with first- and third-order fits
are given in the Supplemental Material [38]. Since the fit
is performed globally over the two-dimensional (x1, x2)
momentum-fraction plane, the finite set of coefficients is
constrained by the full lattice-determined shape rather
than by a small number of local moments, leading to rel-
atively small statistical uncertainties in the fitted results.
As a representative test of the phenomenological im-
pact from the lattice-determined LCDAs, we evalu-
ate the Λ electromagnetic form factor F1 following the
perturbative-QCD hard-scattering formula of Ref. [4],
supplemented by the corresponding NLO contribution for
Λ obtained from an extension of Ref. [5]. The compar-
ison with the commonly used asymptotic form is shown
in Table III. At Q2 = 20 GeV2, replacing the asymp-
totic form by our lattice-determined LCDAs changes F1
from 0.0043 to 0.0037 at LO, corresponding to a 14.1%
effect. At NLO, the corresponding change is from 0.0091
to 0.0079, giving a 13.0% effect. This visible O(10%) shift
supports the observation from recent NLO studies, once
the perturbative hard kernel is under control, the baryon
LCDAs input can become the dominant phenomenologi-
cal ambiguity in hard-scattering predictions. Our result
therefore gives a quantitative illustration of why the full
two-dimensional LCDAs, rather than only their asymp-
totic forms or lowest moments, are needed for precision
baryonic phenomenology.
Summary: We have presented the first complete first-
principles determination of the leading-twist Λ-baryon
LCDAs. Combining boosted nonlocal three-quark matrix
elements with hybrid renormalization, large-λ extrap-
olation, perturbative matching, and controlled extrap-

momentum limits, we obtain all V , A, and T ampli-
tudes as full two-dimensional functions of the valence-
quark momentum fractions. These results provide a di-
rect lattice-QCD access of the intrinsic multi-dimensional
light-cone structures to baryons, resolving both the dom-
inant symmetric amplitude and the antisymmetric struc-
tures beyond the local-moment constraints.

The coefficient ϕΛ,0 represents an overall normalization factor.

unity, which should be regarded as a residual systematic uncertainty in the normalization. For phenomenological

together with the parameterized LCDAs. The third-order parametrization is included mainly as a benchmark and
stability check, allowing one to estimate the possible impact of higher polynomial components.
