COMPACTNESS OF COMPOSITION OPERATOR ON WEIGHTED BERGMAN
SPACES OF THE POLYDISC
ANNE DORVAL
Abstract. We study composition operators induced by a smooth symbol between weighted Bergman
spaces of the polydisc. We first prove a compactness criterion that only requires knowing what hap-
pens on the distinguished boundary. Then we prove simple geometric characterizations of bounded-
ness and compactness on some A2
β(Dd), particularly for β > d −3.
1. Introduction
Let U be a domain in Cd, let X be a Banach space of holomorphic functions on U and let ϕ be a holo-
morphic self map of U (we will denote ϕ ∈O(U, U)). Then the composition operator with symbol ϕ is
defined by Cϕ(f) = f ◦ϕ, f ∈X. The first question to study is about boundedness: for which symbols ϕ
does Cϕ induced a bounded operator on X ? When X is the Hardy space or a weighted Bergman space
of the unit disc, Littlewood’s Subordination principle states that every analytic function on the disc
defines a bounded operator. However, as the dimension grows the problem gets trickier. On weighted
Bergman spaces of the bidisc a characterisation using only first order derivatives for smooth symbols
up to the boundary was given in [11]. It gets a lot harder to completely characterise boundedness on
Bergman spaces of the tridisc (see for example [2], [3] or [11]). Other authors have worked with affine
symbols (see [1],[14] or [13]) or rational inner functions (see [4] [5],[6] or [7]) for example. The next
question to study is the one about compactness. On the Hardy space of the unit disc, compactness of
composition operator was characterised by Shapiro in [12] using the Nevanlinna counting function of ϕ.
On the Hardy space of the bidisc or the tridisc, the question also gets trickier: in [2], Bayart only gives
a necessary and a sufficient condition for compactness for smooth symbols up to the boundary using
properties on the derivative of ϕ. In [8], Clos, ˇCuˇckovi´c and S¸ahuto˘glu give a geometric compactness
criterion on the unweighted Bergman space of the bidisc for smooth symbols that are Lipshitz on the
boundary, precisely: let ϕ ∈O(D2, D2) be Lipschitz on the closure, then Cϕ : A2(D2) →A2(D2) is
compact if and only if ϕ(D
2) ∩T2 = ∅and ϕ(D
2 \ T2) ∩bD2 = ∅, where bD2 denote the boundary of
the bidisc.
In this paper, we investigate compactness of composition operators on weighted Bergman spaces of
the polydisc of smooth symbol that are of class C1 up to the boundary. Our first result, Theorem 2.8,
is a technical result that characterise the compactness using only the values of ϕ on the polydisc. As
a consequence, we obtain a stability result for compatness on weighted Bergman spaces.
In the second part, we take a more geometric approach to characterise the boundedness (resp com-
pactness) of composition operators on A2
β(Dd) when β ≥d −3 (resp β > d −3).
