Abstract
This article examines the cross-connection between the normal normal category
F(H) and the normal dual F(H
′) of the finite-dimensional subspaces of a
Hilbert space H and it’s dual space H
′. Further, we describe the cross-connection
semigroup, which is a normed algebra isomorphic to the normed algebra of finite
rank bounded operators on H, then we characterize compact operators and their
spectrum by the normal cones in the normal category of proper subspaces of H.
Keywords: Regular semigroup, Normal category, Dual, Finite rank bounded
operators, Cross-connections, Compact operators

ordered sets of principal left and right ideals of the semigroup [5]. In 1974, Grillet
abstractly characterized these partially ordered sets as regular partially ordered sets
and established the relation between, which he referred to as a cross-connection of
regular partially ordered sets [2–4]. For each cross-connection, Grillet constructed a
fundamental regular semigroup, known as the cross-connection semigroup. However
a regular semigroup and its full regular subsemigroups have isomorphic cross-
connections, which reveals the limitation of the cross-connection of regular partially
ordered sets. Subsequently, K. S. S. Nambooripad substituted regular partially ordered

2 Normal categories and normal duals
Initially, we recall some of the terminology introduced by Nambooripad in the cross-
connection theory in [15]. Throughout this article, the order of the composition of
functions, morphisms and functors is from left to right. For a category C , we let vC
be the object class of C , C itself be the morphism class of C , and 1C be the identity
functor on C . For a, b ∈vC , let 1a be the identity morphism on a, and denote the
collection of all morphisms from a to b by the hom-set C (a, b).
A category P is called a preorder if each hom-set of P contains at most one
morphism. If P is a preorder, we can define a quasiorder ⊆on vP as p ⊆p′ if
and only if P(p, p′) ̸= ∅for p, p′ ∈vP. If the relation ⊆on vP is a partial order,
then the preorder P is a strict preorder. A small category C is said to be a category
with subobjects if there exists a subcategory P of C , which is a strict preorder with
vP = vC such that every morphism in P is a monomorphism in C and if f = hg
for f, g ∈P and h ∈C , then h ∈P. The morphisms in P are known as inclusions
in C , and if q ∈C is a right inverse of an inclusion in C , then q is a retraction. For
f ∈C , a factorization of the form f = quj with q a retraction, u an isomorphism,

Now, we proceed to investigate the cross-connections of F(H

recall cross-connection of normal categories as discussed in [15].
Let C be a normal category and c ∈vC . The principal ideal generated by c, denoted
by ⟨c⟩, is the full subcategory of C such that the objects of ⟨c⟩are subobjects of c in
C . For two normal categories C and D, an inclusion preserving functor F : C →D
is a local isomorphism if F is fully faithful and F|⟨c⟩is an isomorphism from ⟨c⟩onto
⟨F(c)⟩for every c ∈vC . The M-set MH(γ, −) of a normal cone γ in C is defined by
MH(γ, −) = {c ∈vC : γ(c) is an isomorphism}.
For two normal categories C and D, a triplet (D, C ; Γ) is a cross-connection from D to
C if Γ : D →N ∗C is a local isomorphism such that for every c ∈vC , there exists some
d ∈vD with c ∈MΓ(d). Also for each cross-connection (D, C ; Γ), there is a unique
dual cross-connection (C , D; ∆) such that there exists a natural isomorphism χΓ from
the bifunctor Γ(−, −) : C × D →Set to the bifunctor ∆(−, −) : C × D →Set, where
χΓ is referred to as the duality associated with Γ. We can link the normal cones in
C and D using the natural isomorphism χΓ in the following way: a cone γ ∈TC is
linked to a cone γ∗∈TD if there exists (c, d) ∈vC × vD such that γ ∈Γ(c, d) and
γ∗= χΓ(c, d)(γ). The set ˜SΓ of pairs of linked cones (γ, γ∗) is a regular subsemigroup
of TC × (TD)op, known as the cross-connection semigroup determined by Γ [15].
