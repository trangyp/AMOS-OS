CLOSED MINIMAL SURFACES OF INDEX ONE IN
RIEMANNIAN MANIFOLDS
FERNANDO C. MARQUES AND ANDR´E NEVES
Abstract. In this paper we prove that an (n+1)-manifold, compactly
n-enlargeable, where 3 ≤(n + 1) ≤7, has connected, immersed Morse
index one, closed minimal hypersurfaces with unbounded volumes for
bumpy metrics. We prove that in the three-dimensional case the hyper-
surfaces are geometrically distinct using cyclic coverings of manifolds
with boundary.
The proof extends to (n + 1)-fiberings.
We prove a
scalar curvature rigidity theorem for area-nonincreasing maps of three-
dimensional manifolds. The case of stable surfaces is also discussed by
using cohomology classes and incompressible surfaces.
1. Introduction
In this paper we are interested in the space of immersed, closed minimal
hypersurfaces with Morse index one in a closed Riemannian manifold.
Let (M n+1, g) be a closed Riemannian manifold, 3 ≤(n + 1) ≤7. By
the min-max theory for the area functional of Almgren and Pitts ([3], [40]),
there is a closed minimal hypersurface Σ in (Mn+1, g) which is smooth and
embedded. The hypersurface Σ is such that index(Σ) ≤1 ([33]).
A metric g is bumpy if any closed immersed minimal hypersurface is non-
degenerate. By [52], a smooth generic metric is bumpy. For these metrics,
there is a two-sided, connected component of Σ with index one by [34] and
[55].
We would like to count closed minimal hypersurfaces of index one using
topological conditions on the ambient space.
The Theorem 1.1 discusses compactly n-volume enlargeable manifolds.
Theorem 1.1. Let M n+1 be a compactly n-volume enlargeable closed man-
ifold, 3 ≤(n + 1) ≤7. Suppose g is a bumpy metric on M. Then, for any
λ > 0, there is a connected, smooth, two-sided, virtually embedded, closed
minimal hypersurface ψ : Σ →M, of Morse index one, with
volg(Σ) ≥λ.
Enlargeable manifolds were introduced by Gromov and Lawson to find
obstructions to positive scalar curvature ([18], [19]).

An immersed hypersurface ψ : Σ →M is virtually embedded if there is
a finite cover M′ of M such that ψ lifts to an embedding ψ′ : Σ →M′. We
denote by volg(Σ) the volume of Σ in the pull-back metric ψ∗(g). Hence
volg(Σ) = vol(ψ′(Σ)), where vol is the n-dimensional Hausdorff measure.
A closed Riemannian manifold with nonpositive sectional curvature such
that the fundamental group is residually finite is compactly enlargeable, and
therefore compactly n-volume enlargeable. This includes the nonpositively
curved closed locally symmetric spaces ([18]).
By the Theorem 1.1 there is a sequence of immersed closed minimal hy-
persurfaces ψi : Σi →M of Morse index one with unbounded volumes.
There is the question of whether these hypersurfaces are geometrically dis-
tinct: ψi(Σi) ̸= ψj(Σj) for i ̸= j. To prove that one would have to rule out
coverings. It could be that there is a covering map π : Σj →Σi such that
ψj = ψi ◦π.
The Theorem 1.2 answers this question in the three-dimensional case.
Theorem 1.2. Let (M, g) be a closed orientable three-dimensional manifold.
Suppose that M is not a connected sum of spherical quotients and copies of
S2 × S1. If g is bumpy, then there is a sequence of connected, smooth, two-
sided, virtually embedded, closed minimal surfaces ψi : Σi →M, of Morse
index one, such that
areag(ψi(Σi)) →∞.
A closed orientable three-manifold is compactly area enlargeable if and
only if it is not a connected sum of spherical quotients and copies of S2 ×S1.
By Theorem 1.2, since the area of the images of the index one minimal
immersions is unbounded there is {i′} ⊂{i} such that the surfaces ψi′(Σi′)
are geometrically distinct.
If M is diffeomorphic to a connected sum of spherical quotients and copies
of S2 × S1, there is a metric on M of positive scalar curvature Rg.
If
ψ : Σ →M is an immersed, two-sided, closed minimal surface of Morse
index one for a metric g such that Rg ≥6, then (e.g. appendix of [32])
areag(ψ(Σ)) ≤16π/3. Therefore for these manifolds there is an open set of
metrics Λ and a constant C > 0 such that
areag(ψ(Σ)) ≤C
for any g ∈Λ and ψ : Σ →M an immersed, two-sided, closed minimal
surface of Morse index one in the metric g. Since Λ is an open set there is
a bumpy metric g′ ∈Λ.
This proves that the topological hypothesis of Theorem 1.2 is sharp.
By [38], there are sequences of closed orientable three-manifolds (Mi, gi)
such that the Almgren-Pitts min-max widths are unbounded and Rgi ≥6. In
this case the min-max minimal surface is disconnected: there is a component
of Morse index one with area bounded by 16π/3 and ki stable components
that are diffeomorphic to spheres with areas bounded by 4π/3.

3. Minimal hypersurfaces of Morse index one
A smooth, generic metric is bumpy, in the sense that any immersed,
closed, minimal hypersurface is nondegenerate, by Theorem 2.1 of [52]. If
g is a bumpy metric, then there is no stable, degenerate, closed, two-sided,
immersed, minimal hypersurfaces or one-sided, closed, immersed, minimal
hypersurfaces with stable, degenerate, oriented two-cover.
Proposition 3.1. Let (Ω, g) be a compact Riemannian manifold, of dimen-
sion 3 ≤(n + 1) ≤7, with stable, minimal boundary ∂Ωif ∂Ω̸= ∅. Suppose
that g has no stable, degenerate, closed, two-sided, embedded minimal hyper-
surfaces or one-sided, closed, embedded minimal hypersurfaces with stable,
degenerate, oriented two-cover. Then there is a smooth, connected, embed-
ded, closed minimal hypersurface Σ in Ω, disjoint from ∂Ω, two-sided, with
Morse index one, such that volg(Σ) ≤Wg(Ω).
Proof. Let gi be a sequence of bumpy metrics converging smoothly to g
such that ∂Ωis a minimal hypersurface for gi. Consider a map Φ : [0, 1] →
Zn(Ω, Z2), continuous in the F-metric, such that Φ(t) = ∂Ω(t), with Ω:
[0, 1] →In+1(Ω, Z2) so that Ω(0) = Ω, Ω(1) = 0, continuous in the flat
topology. Let Π be the ([0, 1], {0, 1})-homotopy class of Φ in the notation
of [55]. Let Wgi(Π) be the width of Π for the area functional in the metric
gi. Then Wgi(Π) > Mgi(∂Ω), since ∂Ωis strictly stable. This follows for
instance by applying Theorem 6.1 of [34]. By applying the multiplicity one
theorem of Zhou (Theorem 4.1, [55]), combined with the index estimates of
[34], there is a smooth, connected, embedded, closed minimal hypersurface
Σi ⊂int(Ω) for the metric gi, two-sided, with Morse index one, such that
volgi(Σi) ≤Wgi(Ω).
