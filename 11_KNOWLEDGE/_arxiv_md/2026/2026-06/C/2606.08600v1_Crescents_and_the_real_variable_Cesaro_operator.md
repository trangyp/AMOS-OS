ABSTRACT. This paper explores a version of the classical Ces`aro inte-
gral operator for the Lebesgue space Lp(0, 1) where we discuss its norm,
spectral properties, cyclicity, and invariant subspaces. The spectrum of
the Ces`aro operator will be a crescent domain whose geometry depends
on p. An important tool will be semigroups of weighted composition
operators on Lp(0, 1).
