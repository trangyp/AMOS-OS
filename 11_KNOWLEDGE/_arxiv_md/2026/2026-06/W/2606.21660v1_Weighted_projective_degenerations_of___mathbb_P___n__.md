ABSTRACT. We study weighted projective klt Q-Gorenstein degenerations of projective
space Pn and construct infinitely many new degenerations of projective space in any di-
mension. We also study in detail the deformations of arbitrary weighted projective three-
folds. The rest of the paper provides several applications of the existence of degenerations
of Pn and our methods apply to find many degenerations of Pn beyond just weighted
projective spaces.

1. INTRODUCTION
The aim of this paper is to study mildly singular varieties that appear as degenerations
or limits of projective space Pn
C. We say that a Fano variety X is a Q-Gorenstein Fano
degeneration of Pn if there exists a flat family X →B, where 0 ∈B is a reduced pointed
scheme, such that KX/B is Q-Cartier, with fibers X0 ∼= X, and Xb ∼= Pn for general
b ∈B.
In what follows, a ‘degeneration’ of Pn will always refer to a Q-Gorenstein
Fano degeneration. We are particularly interested in the case when X has (at worst) klt
singularities. Such degenerations appear naturally in many areas of birational geometry
and moduli and have a particular importance in K-stability and moduli of Fano varieties.
In dimension one, klt is equivalent to smooth and therefore by flatness, the classification
of klt degenerations of P1 is equivalent to the classification of smooth genus 0 curves. In
particular, the only klt degeneration of P1 is P1 itself.
The situation in dimension two is also completely understood. In [Man91], Manetti
classified all normal degenerations of P2 that have quotient singularities, which are pre-
cisely the klt singularities in dimension 2.

(1) P3;

Beyond the canonical case for n = 3, and in general for n ≥4, there is very little known
about klt degenerations of Pn.

In this paper, we approach the problem of classifying degenerations of projective space
in two ways: first, by building degenerations of higher dimensional varieties out of de-
generations of lower dimensional varieties via a cone construction, and secondly, through
direct analysis of the deformation theory of weighted projective threefolds. Motivated by
the result of Hacking and Prokhorov which says every klt degeneration of P2 is a par-
tial smoothing of a weighted projective surface, we focus primarily on the classification
of weighted projective degenerations of Pn. Our main results in this direction are the
existence of infinitely many weighted projective spaces arising as degenerations of Pn.
Using known degenerations of P2 and P3, we construct two infinite families of new klt
Q-Gorenstein weighted projective degenerations of Pn for any integer n ≥3.
