SPECTRAL AND LOGARITHMIC ATIYAH CLASSES FOR HIGGS
BUNDLES
PRADIP KUMAR, SAI RASMI RANJAN MOHANTY, SAVITA RANI, AND RAHUL KUMAR SINGH
Abstract. For a regular semisimple Higgs bundle with a smooth spectral curve, we
prove that, over the étale locus, the Atiyah class of the underlying bundle is induced by
the Atiyah class of the spectral line bundle and takes values in the centralizer of the Higgs
field. Further, when the discriminant is reduced, we construct a logarithmic refinement
across the branch divisor: the Atiyah class extends as a class with logarithmic poles and
values in a natural regularized centralizer sheaf.
1. Introduction
The Atiyah class of a holomorphic vector bundle is a basic cohomological invariant of
its holomorphic structure. Introduced by Atiyah through the first jet sequence [Ati57], it
measures the obstruction to the existence of a holomorphic connection. Since then, Atiyah
classes have played an important role in deformation theory, Riemann–Roch formalisms,
and several related constructions in complex and algebraic geometry; see for instance
[Mar09, HT10, Kap99]. For a vector bundle F on a smooth complex projective curve C,
the class
At(F) ∈Ext1
C(F, Ω1
C ⊗F) ∼= H1(C, Ω1
C ⊗End(F))
is represented by the extension class of the first jet sequence
0 −→Ω1

A natural question is how this class behaves when a higher-rank bundle is obtained from a
simpler covering or spectral data.
Higgs bundles provide a natural setting for this question. Since the foundational work of
Hitchin and Simpson [Hit87, Sim92, Sim94], Higgs bundles on a smooth projective curve
have been studied via the Hitchin fibration, spectral curves, and non-abelian Hodge theory.
In the regular semisimple case, the Beauville–Narasimhan–Ramanan correspondence
identifies a Higgs bundle with line bundle data on its spectral curve [BNR89]; see also
Donagi’s broader spectral-cover viewpoint [Don96]. Concretely, if (E, θ) is a Higgs bundle
