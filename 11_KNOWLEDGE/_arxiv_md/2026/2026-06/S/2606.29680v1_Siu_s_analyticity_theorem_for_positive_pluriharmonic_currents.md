In [12], the first author and Lawrence extended Siu’s theorem to rectifiable positive
ddc-closed currents on any complex manifolds. In this work, we study the case of compact
manifolds, namely, the following long standing problem which is still open.
Problem 1. Let T be any positive ddc-closed current on a complex projective, or more gen-
