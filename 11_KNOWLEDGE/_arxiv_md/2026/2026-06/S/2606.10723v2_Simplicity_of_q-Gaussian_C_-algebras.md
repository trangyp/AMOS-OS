SIMPLICITY OF q-GAUSSIAN C∗-ALGEBRAS
TATTWAMASI AMRUTAM, DAVID JEKEL, AND MATEUSZ WASILEWSKI
Abstract. We show that q-Gaussian C∗-algebras for q ∈(−1, 1) have the Dixmier
averaging property, and hence are simple with a unique trace. We argue by com-
bining rapid decay and spectral gap estimates for the commutators with the gen-
erators, which are obtained from free probability.
1. Introduction
The q-commutation relations aia∗

in [FB70], are a natural generalization of the relations satisfied by bosonic (q = 1)
and fermionic (q = −1) creation and annihilation operators. Bożejko and Speicher
[BS91] constructed operators on a Hilbert space satisfying these relations, and in-
troduced the q-Gaussian algebras generated by the operators ai + a∗

a natural trace on the q-Gaussian algebra, it can be completed in two different ways:
to a C∗-algebra and to a tracial von Neumann algebra. These q-Gaussian operator
algebras were first seriously investigated in [BKS97], where the authors showed sim-
plicity of the C∗-algebra and factoriality of the von Neumann algebra in the case of
infinitely many generators. However, most of the recent results on q-Gaussians have
been about the von Neumann algebras, including important structural properties
such as factoriality [Ric05], non-injectivity [Nou04], complete metric approximation
property and strong solidity [Avs11], and maximal amenability of the generator
MASA for |q| < 1/9 [PSW18]. Several of these results have also been generalized
to the setting of q-Araki Woods factors, a natural non-tracial analog of q-Gaussian
algebras [KSW23, Yan24, MMP25].
Many of these results were first established for q = 0, in which the q-Gaussian
operators are a free semicircular family, and hence the von Neumann algebra is
isomorphic to a free group factor.
For this reason, the q-Gaussian algebras are
often viewed as deformations of free group factors. In fact, in the case of finitely
many generators, the q-Gaussian algebras are isomorphic to free group von Neumann
algebras for sufficiently small |q| (depending on the number of generators) by [GS14];
this was also generalized to the q-Araki–Woods and twisted Araki–Woods settings by
Nelson [Nel15] and Yang [Yan24]. On the other hand, for infinitely many generators
the q-Gaussian von Neumann algebra is not isomorphic to a free group factor as
soon as q ̸= 0 [Cas23]; the non-isomorphism of the C∗-algebras was obtained earlier
in [BCKW23]. The isomorphism problem for finitely many generators and large q
remains open.
Overall, compared with the litany of results for q-Gaussian von Neumann al-
gebras, surprisingly little attention has been given to the q-Gaussian C∗-algebras,
with only a few results known in the case of infinitely many generators. This paper
will establish several basic results about q-Gaussian C∗-algebras for finitely many
generators, namely, simplicity, uniqueness of the trace, and C∗-algebraic Dixmier av-
eraging. We use free probability techniques to establish the von Neumann algebraic

Theorem B (See Theorem 3.8). Let (M, τ) be a tracial von Neumann algebra gener-
ated by a self-adjoint tuple x = (x1, . . . , xd) with d ≥2. If x has Lipschitz conjugate

results of Dabrowski. Indeed, in the process of showing that the existence of conju-

proof of Lemma 9] with an identity of Mai [Mai17, Corollary V.5.6] (which was also
generalized to the operator-valued setting by Lee [Lee24, Lemma 3.2]).

Miyagawa and Speicher [MS23] that a finite q-Gaussian family has Lipschitz conju-
