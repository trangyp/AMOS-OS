Strong Evidence for Three-α Clustering in the Ground State of 12C
Kazuki Yoshida∗
Research Center for Nuclear Physics (RCNP), The University of Osaka, Ibaraki 567-0047, Japan and
RIKEN Center for Interdisciplinary Theoretical and Mathematical Sciences (iTHEMS), RIKEN, Wako 351-0198, Japan
Masaaki Kimura†
RIKEN Nishina Center, Wako, Saitama 351-0198, Japan
(Dated: July 1, 2026)
The ground state of 12C has often been approximated by a mean-field picture. This conventional
view has been challenged by recent nuclear theories suggesting non-negligible α-cluster formation,
but experimental evidence remains inconclusive. Here, we show that existing 12C(p, pα)8Be data
provide direct evidence for a pronounced α cluster formation in the ground state of 12C.
We
analyze the data with distorted-wave impulse approximation using α preformation amplitudes from
an unrestricted 3α cluster model and harmonic-oscillator-based models. The results show that the
former reproduces the measured cross sections, whereas the latter underestimate them by more than
an order of magnitude. Thus, contrary to conventional expectations, the data support a nearly fully
developed three-α cluster structure in the ground state of 12C.
The nucleus
12C has been a representative system
for studying the interplay between single-particle motion
and cluster correlations in finite nuclei.
The existence
of pronounced three-α clustering in excited states is well
established [1–3]. In particular, the Hoyle state and its
associated excitation modes have been extensively inves-
tigated [4–7].
In contrast, the ground state is often taken for granted
to be reasonably approximated by a p3/2 subshell-closed
system within a mean-field picture [8, 9].
This inter-
pretation has been partially supported by experimental
data such as single-particle spectroscopic factors of nu-
cleon knockout reactions [10, 11].
However, such data
do not uniquely determine the underlying many-nucleon
correlations, leaving the degree of cluster correlations un-
clear.
Recent theoretical studies, however, are challenging
this simple picture. Algebraic cluster models have de-
scribed the low-lying spectrum of 12C by assuming tri-
angular three-α configurations [12, 13]. This geometrical
picture has been supported by a model-independent anal-
ysis of electron-scattering data [14].
Modern ab-initio
calculations have also indicated non-negligible α-cluster
formation in the ground state [15, 16]. The possibility
of cluster formation has also been discussed in density-
functional approaches [17–19]. These developments de-
mand a clear discrimination between mean-field and α-
cluster descriptions of the ground state of 12C based on
observables directly linked to cluster correlations. A re-
cent example is α-cluster identification in 20Ne via rela-
tivistic heavy-ion collisions [20].
In this work, we exploit the (p, pα) reaction as a selec-
tive probe to discriminate between two extreme descrip-
tions of the ground state of 12C: the p3/2 subshell-closed

its suppression with increasing neutron number.

Figure 2 shows the α preformation amplitudes evalu-
ated with Eq. (3), where the α and 8Be wave functions
are given by Eqs. (4) and (6), respectively, while the
four models introduced above are employed for 12C. As
expected, the α-cluster models yield larger α preforma-
tion amplitudes than the HO-based descriptions. Within
the HO-based descriptions, the jj-coupling model yields
the smallest amplitude, because the nucleon spin is not
saturated and the overlap with the α + 8Be channel is
reduced.
The asymptotic behavior also shows strong model de-
pendence.
At large distances where the nuclear inter-
action and antisymmetrization are negligible, it should
approach the Whittaker function. The unrestricted 3α
model naturally reproduces this behavior, whereas the
triangular 3α model does not because the triangular
constraint prevents the α + 8Be asymptotic configura-
tion. In the HO-based models, the tail is governed by
the Gaussian form of the HO basis, resulting in rapid
damping. We also note that the cluster models exhibit
similar α preformation amplitudes in the interior region
(r ≲3 fm), indicating that the 3α system naturally fa-
vors a triangular configuration.

Keeping this in mind, we focus on the middle and lower
panels of Fig. 3, for which the DWIA description of quasi-
free knockout is expected to be more reliable owing to
the large momentum transfer to the α particle. In these
panels, the unrestricted 3α model clearly gives the closest
description of the data, reproducing the peak heights and
the local Tp and Q dependence around the peaks. By
contrast, the HO-based models underestimate the data
by more than an order of magnitude.
Thus, contrary
to conventional expectations, the data support a nearly
fully developed three-α-cluster structure in the ground
state of 12C as represented by the unrestricted 3α model,
and reject the conventional HO-based descriptions.
Although the unrestricted 3α model describes the peak
region well, notable discrepancies remain in the upper
panels and on the high-Tp side of the lower panels of
Fig. 3.
These discrepancies mainly reflect the limita-
tions of the present one-step DWIA description. In the
lower panels, the calculation tends to underestimate the
data on the high-Tp side, where the emitted α energy
is low, and final-state interactions between the α par-
ticle and the residual nucleus may become important.
Such effects, including possible channel coupling, are be-
yond the present DWIA framework. A similar discrep-
ancy at high Tp has been reported in previous 16O(p, pα)
and 40Ca(p, pα) analyses at around 100 MeV [40]. By
contrast, recent 40Ca(p, pα) data at 392 MeV [29] show
improved agreement with DWIA calculations, probably
because the higher incident energy makes the one-step
quasi-free knockout picture more appropriate. This sug-
gests that measurements at higher energies and over a
wider recoil-momentum range would be valuable.

3α-cluster limit is not affected by this uncertainty.
In summary, the ground state of 12C has long been
approximated by a mean-field picture, although the pres-
ence of α-cluster correlations remains an open question
due to the limited discriminating power of conventional
observables. In this work, we examine the 12C(p, pα)8Be
reaction as a direct probe of α-cluster formation by com-
bining DWIA calculations with α preformation ampli-
tudes derived from three-α-cluster models and HO-based
descriptions. The calculated cross sections show strong
model dependence: the unrestricted 3α-cluster model re-
produces the experimental data, whereas the HO-based
models underestimate the data by more than an order of
magnitude. Thus, contrary to conventional expectations,
