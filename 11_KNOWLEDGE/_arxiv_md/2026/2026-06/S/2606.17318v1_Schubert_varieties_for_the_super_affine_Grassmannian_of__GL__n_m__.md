coordinate descriptions, equations, and morphisms.

1. Introduction
The affine Grassmannian 𝐺𝑟𝐺of an algebraic group 𝐺is an infinite-dimensional analog of a partial flag variety. It
is related to the representation theory through the geometric Satake correspondence, which was proved in Mirković and
Vilonen (2007). The important component of this correspondence are the Schubert varieties Zhu (2016) ( Peerenboom
(2021) for more explicit description).
Research in the direction of Satake equivalence for supergroups is conducted in Braverman, Finkelberg, Ginzburg
and Travkin (2021). Their work operates on the high level of categories and sheaves and discusses questions of Gaiotto
cojectures and Langlands correspondence. We pursue more down to earth goals. We focus on the explicit coordinate
description of super Schubert varieties in the case of the supergroup 𝐺𝐿𝑛|𝑚.
This paper explores the super-analog of the affine Grassmannian. More specifically, we construct and study the
supervarieties that naturally emerge in this context. We start with a quick recollection of the basic definitions and then
formulate and proof our results in 3. In Theorem 1 we describe the general structure of a super Schubert variety in the
case of the super affine Grassmannian of the group 𝐺𝐿𝑛|𝑚. In Theorem 2 we give a coordinate description in case of
𝐺𝐿1|1, and Theorems 4 and 5 give a coordinate description of the Schubert varieties that correspond through Cartan
decomposition 1 to the typical and atypical cocharacters.
The explicit description of the Schubert varieties in dimension 2|1 reveals the structure, which can be interpreted as
the super-analog of the jet-scheme (see Mourtada (2023)), which we believe has never before appeared in the literature.
