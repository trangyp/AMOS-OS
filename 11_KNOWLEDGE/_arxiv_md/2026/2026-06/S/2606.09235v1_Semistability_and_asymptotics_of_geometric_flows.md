SEMISTABILITY AND ASYMPTOTICS OF GEOMETRIC FLOWS
SHING TAK LAM
Abstract. We prove that the asymptotics of the Hermitian-Yang-Mills flow on a slope semistable
holomorphic vector bundle over a compact K¨ahler manifold are determined algebro-geometrically,
via the iterated filtration defined by Haiden-Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pandit. This proves a con-
jecture of Haiden-Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pandit in this setting. Moreover, we prove a non-linear
analogue, relating the asymptotics of the Calabi flow near a cscK manifold to the iterated bal-
ancing filtration of the deformation space. In both settings, we reduce the infinite-dimensional
flow to a finite-dimensional flow, following the foundational work of Chen-Sun. In finite dimen-
sions, we prove that the asymptotics of the moment map flow are determined by the iterated
balancing filtration, proving a conjecture of Ib´a˜nez N´u˜nez.
1. Introduction
In complex geometry, a guiding principle is that geometric PDEs should arise as moment
maps.
Two prominent examples are the constant scalar curvature K¨ahler (cscK) equation,
where the moment map interpretation is due to Donaldson [Don97
Don97] and Fujiki [Fuj92
Fuj92], and
the Hermitian-Yang-Mills equation, where the moment map interpretation is due to Atiyah-
Bott [AB83
AB83] and Donaldson [Don85
Don85]. The moment map property of these equations motivates
the link between solutions to the PDEs and algebro-geometric stability conditions, namely
the Yau-Tian-Donaldson conjecture for the cscK equation [Yau93
Yau93; Tia97
Tia97; Don02
Don02], and the
Hitchin-Kobayashi correspondence, due to Donaldson-Uhlenbeck-Yau for the Hermitian-Yang-
Mills equation [Don85
Don85; Don87
Don87; UY86
UY86]. In this paper, we relate the asymptotics of the corres-
ponding gradient flow to algebro-geometric stability, providing a parabolic analogue of the above
results.
As part of their programme on categorical K¨ahler geometry [HKKP
HKKP], Haiden-Katzarkov-
Kontsevich-Pandit conjectured the following. Given a triangulated category C, for each object
E of C, we have a space Met(E) of metrics on E, and a convex function S : Met(E) →R.
Objects E such that S has a critical point correspond to polystable objects, with respect to a
Bridgeland stability condition [Bri07
Bri07]. Moreover, the flow under −∇S will decompose E into
its polystable components. However, the Harder-Narasimhan filtration only decomposes E into
semistable constituents. Thus, a main part of their programme is to understand the decompos-
ition induced by the flow of a semistable object into its polystable components.
Given an artinian abelian category A, and a homomorphism X : K0(A) →R which is positive
on each class of a non-zero object, for each object E ∈A, Haiden-Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pandit
[HKKP23
HKKP23] define a filtration of E, labelled by R∞= R⊕N with the lexicographic order, which we
will refer to as the iterated HKKP filtration in this paper. They conjecture that the asymptotics
of the flow are determined by the iterated HKKP filtration, and prove this conjecture for quiver
representations [HKKP23
HKKP23] and for holomorphic vector bundles over compact Riemann surfaces
[HKKP18

We develop a new technique to prove their conjecture in various cases. Our first result is
to prove their conjecture for holomorphic vector bundles over smooth projective varieties of
arbitrary dimension, assuming the graded object of the Jordan-H¨older filtration is locally free.

1.2. Outlook. Many geometric PDEs can be written as moment maps, and we expect the
general strategy of reducing to a finite-dimensional slice should apply, proving analogous results
for the asymptotics of the flow. We briefly discuss some examples, though the conjectures as
stated may be too na¨ıve.
The first example is the special Lagrangian equation. It is explained by Thomas [Tho01
Tho01]
that it arises as a moment map, and by Thomas-Yau [TY02
TY02] that for a specific choice of inner
product on the Lie algebra, the gradient flow is Lagrangian mean curvature flow. We also refer
to [Hor+03
Hor+03, Section 38.4]. Motivated by mirror symmetry, Solomon constructs a non-positively
curved space, along with a geodesically convex functional, whose critical points are the special
Lagrangian submanifolds [Sol13
Sol13; Sol14
Sol14]. The following is a variant of a conjecture by Haiden-
Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pandit.
Conjecture 1.8. Let (X, ω, Ω) be a compact Calabi-Yau manifold, L1, . . . , Lr special Lagrangian
submanifolds in X, with the same phase, and intersecting transversely. Let L = L1# · · · #Lr
denote the result of performing Lagrangian surgery near the intersection points. Then the asymp-
totics of the Lagrangian mean curvature flow with initial condition L are given by sums of iterated
logarithms.
Our conjecture is closely related to the Thomas-Yau conjecture, for which we refer to the
articles of Joyce [Joy15
Joy15] and Li [Li25
Li25], and references therein. The geometric setting in Conjec-
ture 1.8
1.8 is intended to be the semistable case, under an appropriate stability condition. We note
that when r = 1, Lagrangian mean curvature flow near a special Lagrangian converges exponen-
tially fast [Li12
Li12]. An example of the above set-up with r = 2 was constructed by Su-Tsai-Wood
[STW24
STW24], where they prove estimates on the asymptotic behaviour.
In [HKKP18
HKKP18, Conjecture 5.1], Haiden-Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pandit make a conjecture about
a modified curve shortening flow, motivated by stability in partially wrapped Fukaya categories,
as an A-side analogue of their result on the Yang-Mills flow on Riemann surfaces.
On the B-side, for the deformed Hermitian-Yang-Mills equation, Collins-Yau [CY21
CY21] show
that it can be interpreted as a moment map, as a mirror of Thomas and Solomon’s results
on the A-side. We also refer to the survey by Collins-Shi [CS22
CS22] and references therein. In
[Tak20
Tak20], Takahashi introduces the tangent Lagrangian Phase flow, which can be interpreted as
the gradient flow of the Kempf-Ness functional. We expect an analogue of Conjecture 1.8
1.8 should
hold in this setting. Many other flows have been introduced in this setting, such as the line bundle
mean curvature flow by Jacob-Yau [JY17
JY17], and the cotangent flow by Fu-Yau-Zhang [FYZ24
FYZ24].
It would be an interesting question to understand the asymptotics of these flows, and also in
the more general setting of the Z-critical equation, introduced by Dervan-McCarthy-Sektnan
[DMS24
DMS24], which is related to Bridgeland stability conditions on Db Coh(X).
Finally, the Calabi flow is a fourth-order fully non-linear PDE, which poses many analytic
difficulties. The K¨ahler-Ricci flow is a second-order PDE, and we conjecture that the asymptotics
of the K¨ahler-Ricci flow can also be computed from the iterated balancing filtration.
Conjecture 1.9. Let X be a K-semistable Fano manifold. Then an analogous result holds for
the K¨ahler-Ricci flow starting from ω.
One key obstruction is that the K¨ahler-Ricci flow is not given by the gradient flow of the
norm squared of a moment map, and it may be the case that the logarithm is replaced by a
different function. However, by a result of Donaldson [Don17
Don17], it is the gradient flow of a convex
function of a moment map. As a finite-dimensional analogue, we conjecture that a similar result
to Theorem 1.3
1.3 should hold for the gradient flow of a convex function of the moment map, as
studied by Lee-Sturm-Wang [LSW25
