SOHAM MONDAL
Abstract. Let X be a smooth irreducible projective variety of dimension n ≥3 over an alge-
braically closed field of characteristic zero, polarized by a very ample line bundle OX(1). Let E be
an Ulrich bundle on X. We prove that there exists an explicitly computable integer M ≫0 such
that for every m ≥M the global syzygy bundle SE(m) is slope semistable with respect to OX(1).
This confirms Conjecture 3.11 of Miró-Roig.
1. Introduction
The study of syzygy bundles—defined as the kernels of evaluation maps of globally generated
vector bundles—has been a central mechanism for understanding the projective geometry and co-
homological positivity of algebraic varieties.
Initiated in part by the foundational work of Ein
and Lazarsfeld [4], the stability of these bundles carries deep implications for the geometry of the
ambient space.
For curves, the stability of syzygy bundles is governed by the seminal theorem of Butler [2]:
if a globally generated bundle on a curve is semistable and has sufficiently large slope relative to
the genus, its syzygy bundle is semistable. Extending this to higher-dimensional varieties intro-
duces severe cohomological obstructions, primarily because moving curves constructed as complete
intersections within an n-dimensional variety produce restriction kernels whose dimension grows
polynomially in the twist.
In this paper we resolve the asymptotic semistability question for syzygy bundles associated to
Ulrich bundles. These bundles, characterized by maximal global sections and complete cohomolog-
ical vanishing [3, 1], possess a rigid algebraic structure that makes them natural candidates for this
problem. The key insight is a two-step reduction:
(1) Surface reduction: slice X with n −2 general hyperplanes to obtain a smooth complete
intersection surface Y on which E|Y remains Ulrich (and hence semistable).
(2) Decoupled moving curve: introduce a fixed geometric offset c ≥0 to decouple the curve
degree k from the bundle twist m by setting k = m −c, which forces the restriction kernel
to have constant dimension NK independent of m.
This decoupling, together with the arithmetically Cohen–Macaulay (aCM) property of Ulrich
bundles, enables a definitive contradiction against any destabilizing subsheaf.
Main result.
Theorem 1.1 (Main Theorem). Let X be a smooth irreducible projective variety of dimension
n ≥3 over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, polarized by a very ample line bundle
H = OX(1). Let E be an Ulrich bundle on X of rank r. Then there exists an explicitly computable
integer M ≫0 such that for every m ≥M the global syzygy bundle SE(m) is µH-semistable.
