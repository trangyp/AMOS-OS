Abstract. For an algebraically closed field K, let G be a finite abelian group of K-linear auto-
morphisms of a finite-dimensional algebra Λ and ¯Λ(= ΛG) is the associated skew group algebra.
The author with S. Trepode and A. G. Chaio introduced the notion of a Galois semi-covering
functor to study the irreducible morphisms over skew group algebras. In this paper, we establish
a Galois semi-covering functor between the morphism categories as well as the functor categories
over the algebras Λ and ¯Λ and prove that their Krull-Gabriel dimension are equal. This com-
putation confirms Prest’s conjecture on the finiteness of Krull-Gabriel dimension and Schr¨oer’s
conjecture on its connection with the stable rank (the least stabilized radical power) over skew
gentle algebras. Moreover, we determine all posible stable ranks for (skew) Brauer graph algebras.
1. Introduction
The Krull–Gabriel dimension, KG(Λ), of a ring was introduced as a modification of the Gabriel
dimension for the category of finitely presented modules by Geigle in his thesis [5]. This dimension
also turns out [8, Lemma B.9] to be the m-dimension of the lattice of pp formulas for Λ, equivalently
of the lattice of pointed finite-dimensional modules; that dimension is a variant of the elementary
Krull dimension, introduced by Garavaglia [3].
The Krull-Gabriel dimension is defined for any
locally bounded K-category. In fact, it is defined for any skeletally small abelian category.

Classifying a finite-dimensional associative algebra as finite, tame (domestic and non-domestic),
or wild is a challenging problem. The computation of some numerical invariants like KG dimension
and (stable) rank could make this task easier as we see in the last paragraph. Here, we are concerned
about the KG dimension of skew group algebras. These kind of algebras were first studied from the
point of view of representation theory in [15]. For a finite-dimensional algebra Λ over a field K and a
finite group G acting on Λ by automorphisms, the skew group algebra ¯Λ shares many representation-
theoretic properties with Λ often incarnated in properties of functors between mod-Λ and mod-¯Λ.
Definition 1.2 (Skew group algebras). Let G be a finite group acting on an algebra Λ by automor-
phisms. The skew group algebra ¯Λ is the algebra defined by:
●its underlying vector space is Λ ⊗K KG;
●multiplication is given by (λ ⊗g)(µ ⊗h) = ¯Λ(µ) ⊗gh for λ,µ ∈Λ and g,h ∈G, extended by
linearity and distributivity.

In [17], we established that the stable rank is preserved under skew group algebra construction.
The main contribution in this paper is that skewness also preserves the KG dimension.
Theorem 1.5. Suppose Λ is a finite-dimensional algebra, G is a finite abelian group of K-linear
automorphisms of Λ and ¯Λ is the associated skew group algebra. Then, the KG dimension KG(¯Λ)
of ¯Λ equals the KG dimension KG(Λ) of Λ.

of the field is different from two. We also verify the following well known conjectures regarding the
relation between the KG dimension and the nilpotent index of radical for skew-gentle algebras.

On the other hand, Brauer graph algebras, a special subclass of special biserial algebras that
consists of symmetric algebras [18, Section 2.3], originate in the modular representation theory
of finite groups in the form of Brauer tree algebras [7]. The interpretation of Brauer graphs as
ribbon graphs [10] links Brauer graph algebras with surface cluster theory and adds a geometric
perspective to the representation theory of Brauer graph algebras. Skew Brauer graph algebras are
generalization of Brauer graph algebras [16, Section 3.1]. It is shown in [16, Proposition 3.9] that for
a skew Brauer graph algebra ¯Λ one can construct a Brauer graph algebra Λ such that ¯Λ appears as a
skew group algebra of Λ with a certain action of the group of order two. As a consequence, their KG
dimensions are equal. Moreover, we demonstrate the following result regarding their stable ranks.
Theorem 1.7. For a skew Brauer graph algebra ¯Λ and its associated Brauer graph algebra Λ, we
have ω ≤st(Λ) = st(¯Λ) < ω2.

5. KG-dimension of the skew group algebras
The main contribution of this section is to demonstrate that KG dimension is preserved under
skewness. As a result, we verify Prest’s conjecture on the finiteness of KG dimension and Schr¨oer’s
conjecture on its connection with the stable rank over skew gentle algebras. Moreover, we determine
all posible stable ranks for (skew) Brauer graph algebras.
Here, we define KG(Λ) as the KG dimension KG(F(Λ)) of the category F(Λ) as follows.
Definition 5.1. Let F(Λ) be the category of all finitely presented contravariant K-linear functors
from mod-Λ to mod-K. A natural approach to study the structure of F(Λ) is via the associated
Krull-Gabriel filtration:
F(Λ)−1 ⊆F(Λ)0 ⊆F(Λ)1 ⊆⋯⊆F(Λ)α ⊆F(Λ)α+1 ⊆⋯
of F(Λ) by Serre subcategories, defined recursively as follows:
(1) F(Λ)−1 = 0;
(2) F(Λ)α+1 is the Serre subcategory of F(Λ) formed by all functors having a finite length in
the quotient category F(Λ)/F(Λ)α for any ordinal number α;
(3) F(Λ)β = ⋃α<β F(Λ)α for any limit ordinal β.
The Krull-Gabriel dimension KG(Λ) of Λ is the smallest ordinal number α such that F(Λ)α = F(Λ),
if such a number exists, and KG(Λ) = ∞if it is not the case. In the latter case, we call the smallest
ordinal number α such that F(Λ)α = F(Λ)α+1 as the stable Krull-Gabriel dimension KGst(Λ) of Λ.
The following lemma is essential to determine the KG dimension of a skew algebra.
Lemma 5.2. [8, Appendix B] Assume that C,D are abelian categories and F ∶C →D is an exact
functor. If F is faithful, then KG(C) ≤KG(D).
The next theorem concludes that the KG-dimension is preserved under skewness.
Theorem 5.3. Suppose Λ is a finite-dimensional algebra, G is a finite abelian group of K-linear
automorphisms of Λ and ¯Λ is the associated skew group algebra. Then, the KG dimension KG(¯Λ)
of ¯Λ equals the KG dimension KG(Λ) of Λ.
Proof. Propositions 4.2 and 4.6 ensure that ϕ is an exact faithful functor and hence KG(Λ) ≤
KG(¯Λ) by Lemma 5.2. Therefore, KG(Λ) ≤KG(¯Λ) ≤KG((¯Λ) ˆG) ≈KG(Λ) by Theorem 2.6.
