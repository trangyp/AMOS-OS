Abstract
We provide two representations of the Segal category X model-
ing natural phenomena, the first one being based on the concept of
micro-reversibility, producing a long sequence Σ of categories as a res-
olution of X, the second one providing graded categories cofibered in
groupoids over the categories of Σ, using the concept of consciousness
as impetus. We show those two representations are dual to each other.

Determinism vs free will
In the present work we have argued d1 can be immersed into Ω, the idea
being that one can categorically identify which events or close to one another,
which subsumes an underlying deterministic picture of d1, or equivalently

one adopts a representation by the Segal category δ∞X. On the other hand
in the KΦ, we discuss consciousness, which has a strong association with the
concept of free will. To be fair, the exact connection remains elusive and
is far from being settled. For our purposes, we take free will to designate
spontaneous generations from the perspective of the agent free will is in ref-
erence to, broadly speaking. No free will implies no possible consciousness
since we would just have a flow of information through the agent. By con-
trapositive, to have consciousness subsumes there is such a thing as free will.
If it was, and still is, difficult to define consciousness, not to have free will as
underlying assumption is an even more serious problem, notwithstanding the
fact that it seems K is defined within a deterministic ambient environment 1.
We argue there is actually no contradiction in having this picture in mind,
and that we can actually have both concepts coexisting. The argument goes
as follows. Consider a system endowed with free will, that we can repre-
sent by a category C. Its manifestations in the realm of natural phenomena
can be represented by a functor F : C →Cat∞. In particular free will is
a coherent manifestation in 1, so must be represented by such a functor.
However by the ∞-categorical Grothendieck construction ([Lu]), discussed
at length in [RG6], we have Fun(C, Cat∞) ≃coCartFib(C). Thus to any
functor F : C →Cat∞corresponds a co-cartesian fibration over C. This is
a functor into C, hence is purely deterministic. In this duality, C is a pivot,
and from that perspective the LHS of this duality presents Fun(C, Cat∞) as
providing something with no precedent prior to C, it being the point of focus,
thus we deal with something that can be interpreted as being free will-based.
As argued in [RG6], this duality provides an algebraic flow of information
through C, we have a conservation law in a sense, hence free will can be seen
as being deterministically produced. Additionally, what this duality says is
that it is perpetual, and simultaneous; information flowing into C generates
functors that correspond to manifestations of C in its ambient universe, which
in turn are fed back into co-cartesian fibrations over C, ad infinitum.
The individual decision making process also results from dynamics within
C, and this also is encoded in this Grothendieck duality; external events shape
C via co-cartesian fibrations, which generate functors F : C →Cat∞, some of
which produce internal ∞-categories, read self-characteristics, some of which
will be relevant for generating awareness. So far we are saying our inner self
is produced in a deterministic fashion. Those internal functors are then fed

will produce a basis for free will. Thus our own self can be argued to be
formed in a deterministic fashion, and those newly formed characteristics

mechanics of it all is deterministic but unnoticeable since it arises from myr-
