AMBRUS P´AL AND GEREON QUICK
Abstract. We study a weak form of formality for differential graded algebras,
called A3-formality, and show that the differential graded F2-algebras of con-
tinuous cochains of all pro-2 Demushkin groups are A3-formal. We prove this
result by an explicit computation of the Benson–Krause–Schwede canonical
class using the classification of pro-2 Demushkin groups by Demushkin, Serre,
and Labute. Compared to the case of odd primes, the new idea is to interpret
the data of the canonical class as defining systems of higher Massey products.
1. Introduction
Let F be a field and let GF denote its absolute Galois group. Let C‚pGF , F2q
denote the differential graded algebra of inhomogeneous continuous cochains of
GF with coefficients in the constant discrete GF -module F2. In [11], Hopkins and
Wickelgren showed that, for F a local or global field, all triple Massey products
of elements in H1pGF , F2q vanish whenever they are defined. Since triple Massey
products are the first obstruction to formality, Hopkins–Wickelgren therefore asked
in [11, Question 1.4] whether C‚pGF , F2q is A8-formal, i.e., the higher multiplication
maps mn on its minimal model are trivial for all n ě 3. However, Positselski showed
in [37, Section 9.11] and [38, §6] that C‚pGF , F2q is not A8-formal in general for
local fields.
Then Harpaz–Wittenberg in [9, Example A.15] and more recently
Merkurjev–Scavia in [23, Theorem 1.6] and [25, Theorem 1.3] provided examples
which show that the second obstruction to formality is not trivial in general, i.e.,
not all fourfold Massey products are defined when the neighbouring cup-products
vanish. For local fields, or more generally, for Demushkin groups, we may then ask
the weaker question whether the differential graded algebra C‚pG, F2q is A3-formal,
i.e., whether the first higher multiplication map m3 on its minimal model is trivial.
A3-formality is much stronger than the vanishing of triple Massey products.
1.1. Demushkin groups and the main result. The purpose of this paper is to
study A3-formality for C‚pG, F2q for pro-2 Demushkin groups.
Definition 1.1. Let p be a prime number and let G be a pro-p-group. Then G
is called a Demushkin group if dimFp H1pG, Fpq ă 8, dimFp H2pG, Fpq “ 1, and
the cup product H1pG, Fpq ˆ H1pG, Fpq Ñ H2pG, Fpq is a non-degenerate bilinear

1.3. Outline of the proof. We now give a brief outline of the proof of Theorem
1.2. While n-Massey vanishing for Demushkin groups is a direct consequence of the
non-degeneracy of the cup product, showing A3-formality is much more involved.
By the work of Kadeishvili, the differential graded algebra C‚pG, F2q is A3-formal
if and only if a certain canonical class γG in the Hochschild cohomology group
HH3,´1pH‚pG, F2qq is zero. The canonical class of a differential graded algebra has
also been studied by Benson–Krause–Schwede in [1] in the context of the realiz-
ability of modules over Tate cohomology. The strategy for proving Theorem 1.2
is then to show that γG vanishes in HH3,´1pH‚pG, F2qq for all pro-2 Demushkin
