Abstract
We construct new multiparticle models of N = 4 supersymmetric mechanics with spin de-
grees of freedom by employing nonlinear indecomposable supermultiplets (1, 4, 3)⊃
+(4, 4, 0).
These systems are proper deformations of those associated with the standard irreducible
d = 1, N = 4 multiplets.
In this way we find a new N = 4 supersymmetric gener-
alization of U(2)-spin rational Calogero system invariant under d = 1 superconformal
group OSp(4|2). One more deformed model reproduces N = 4 supersymmetric U(2)-spin
hyperbolic Calogero system, up to a shift of the Hamiltonian by some U(1) generators.

Introduction
An interest in supersymmetric Calogero models is caused by their close relationships with black
hole physics [1,2] and AdS/CFT correspondence [3]. Construction of the first supersymmetric
extension of the multiparticle Calogero mechanical system with d = 1, N = 2 superconformal
symmetry OSp(2|2) [4] initiated a lot of further studies on superconformal mechanics and
the associated super Calogero systems (see, e.g. [5–9] and [10] for a more complete list of
references). The Calogero-like models with internal spin degrees of freedom were pioneered
in [11] as gauged matrix models (see also a review [12]). As was shown in [13], N = 1, 2, 4
supersymmetrizations of such models can be accomplished by applying the superfield gauging
procedure [14,15]. An important feature of this approach is the use of the off-shell matrix d = 1
superfields carrying linear irreducible supermultiplets of the underlying supersymmetries. Its
one more crucial ingredient is the simultaneous consideration of both dynamical and the so
called semi-dynamical superfields (accommodating spin degrees of freedom).
Supersymmetric d = 1 models with dynamical and semi-dynamical (spin) degrees of freedom
can be also built on the indecomposable (not fully reducible or long) supermultiplets [16–20].
A recent study of nonlinear indecomposable (long) N = 8 supermultiplets [21] gave rise to the
off-shell formulation of N = 8 supersymmetric model with spin degrees of freedom described
earlier in [22] in terms of N = 4 superfields.1 In ref. [21], N = 8 superfield description of these
new indecomposable multiplets was given at the “kinematical” off-shell level (constraints and
component contents), while the problem of building the relevant manifestly N = 8 invariant
superfield Lagrangians remained unsolved. Only the component and N = 4 superfield actions
for these supermultiplets were partially worked out.
As a step towards constructing the complete N = 8 superfield description of such systems,
in the present paper we consider d = 1, N = 4 counterpart of the nonlinear indecomposable
N = 8 supermultiplet of ref. [21] and construct the related new models of N = 4 mechanics.
As a characteristic example, it is instructive to recall here the simpler linear indecomposable
(long) N = 4 multiplet introduced in [25].
Off-shell multiplets of d = 1, N = 4 supersymmetry can be conveniently presented in the
harmonic superspace formulation of N = 4 supersymmetric mechanics [26,27]. As was shown
in [25], the simplest example of indecomposable N = 4 multiplet is a system of the scalar real
superfield X and the analytic superfield W++, which are subject to the conditions 2

It makes sense to summarize the basic features of what we have achieved for the moment.
In the system described by the superfields X and Z+, ¯Z+, the component fields of Z+, ¯Z+
(multiplet (4, 4, 0)) transform through themselves under supersymmetry transformations,
whereas the component form of the superfield X (multiplet (1, 4, 3)) includes the fields of

(2.5) are nonlinear. That is, we are facing the nonlinear indecomposable d = 1 supermultiplet

Summary
In this paper, we demonstrated the effectiveness and novelty of using indecomposable
d = 1, N = 4 multiplets.
• For the first time, the nonlinear indecomposable d = 1, N = 4 off-shell supermultiplet
(1, 4, 3)⊃+(4, 4, 0) was defined. Its both component and superfield descriptions were given.
The one-particle dynamics described by this multiplet was studied. It was shown that,
with the appropriate choice of the Lagrangian, it yields N = 4 superconformal mechanics
with spin variables. The specific feature of the relevant superfield and component La-
grangians is that their (1, 4, 3) parts produce the WZ type actions for the (4, 4, 0) spin
parts, with the deformation parameter κ as the relevant constant WZ strength.
• A matrix nonlinear indecomposable supermultiplet (1, 4, 3)⊃+(4, 4, 0) was proposed. Ma-
trix models describing multiparticle systems with N = 4 supersymmetry were considered:
– The new superfield matrix model was found. It is a generalization of the n-particle
spin rational Calogero system and has the non-trivial traceless limit.
– The superfield matrix model which is N = 4 supersymmetric generalization of the
n-particle spin hyperbolic Calogero-Sutherland system was constructed.
• The generalization of the spin rational Calogero system was shown to be OSp(4|2) super-
conformal. The relevant generators of the N = 4 supersymmetry and N = 4 supercon-
formal symmetry for the matrix system were derived.
An important advantage of dealing with the nonlinear indecomposable N = 4 supermultiplet
(1, 4, 3)⊃+(4, 4, 0) studied here is the possibility of its straightforward generalization to higher
N > 4. In particular, we have two such examples in the N = 8 case [21], one of which was
earlier described in terms of N = 4 harmonic superfields in [22]. The main goal of [22] was to
construct N = 8 supersymmetric single-particle system with semi-dynamical supermultiplets.
Its generalization to multiparticle case and the subsequent problem of gauging remained open.
The methods considered here may hopefully allow us to resolve these problems.
Another important question is the integrability of the constructed multiparticle models. It
seems complicated to formulate the Lax representation for the rational case of Section 4, be-
cause the Hamiltonian (4.54) (or (4.70)) contains rather unusual potential terms. Deformation
of the hyperbolic Hamiltonian (5.4) has no dependence on the coordinates ya and contains only
the U(1) generators Jaii. These generators commute with all U(2) generators, so they must
commute with the rest of n−1 integrals of motion of the U(2)-spin Calogero-Sutherland system.
Hence, the new model in its bosonic sector is a trivial deformation of Calogero-Sutherland sys-
tem and integrable. However, we did not study its supersymmetric extension in full generality,
and such an analysis could be an interesting problem.
