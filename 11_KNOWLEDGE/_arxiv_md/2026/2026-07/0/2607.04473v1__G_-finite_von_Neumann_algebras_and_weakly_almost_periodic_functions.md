G-FINITE VON NEUMANN ALGEBRAS AND WEAKLY
ALMOST PERIODIC FUNCTIONS
PAUL JOLISSAINT
Abstract. Let M be a von Neumann algebra which acts in a standard way on
the Hilbert space H, let G be a subgroup of the group of all ˚-automorphisms
of M. We prove that M is G-finite (in the sense of I. Kov´acs and Sz¨ucs, i.e.
the set of all normal, G-invariant states on M is separating) if and only if, for
every x P M and all ξ, η P H, the coefficient function g ÞÑ xgpxqξ|ηy is weakly
almost periodic.
1. Introduction
Let M be a von Neumann algebra, let M˚ be its predual, let AutpMq be the
group of all ˚-automorphisms of M; we consider here exclusively the action of M
on its standard form pM, H, J, Pq as in [6] and in [11, Definition X.1.3]: H is a
Hilbert space with M Ă BpHq, J is a conjugate linear, isometric involution on H,
P is a selfdual cone in H (i.e. P “ tη P H : xη|ξy ě 0 @ξ P Pu) with the properties
(1) JMJ “ M 1, the commutant of M in BpHq;
(2) JcJ “ c˚ for every c P ZpMq, the center of M;
(3) Jξ “ ξ for every ξ P P;
(4) aJaJpPq Ă P for every a P M.

admits a unitary implementation, i.e.

Given a subgroup G of AutpMq, I. Kov´acs and J. Sz¨ucs introduced the notion

the more precise statement in the next theorem below. It is worth mentioning that
other characterizations were added, namely by E. Størmer in [9].

Here are the promised characterizations of G-finiteness.
Theorem 1.1. Let M be a von Neumann algebra and let G be a subgroup of
AutpMq. Then the following conditions are equivalent:
(1) M is G-finite: if x P M` is such that φpxq “ 0 for every φ P SG

(2) For every x P M, the set KGpxq X M G contains exactly one element de-

expectation which is G-invariant (i.e. EG ˝ g “ EG for every g P G).
(3) For every ψ P M˚, its orbit Gpψq is relatively σpM˚, Mq-compact.
(4) The w˚-closure of G in BpMq is contained in B˚pMq.
Moreover, if these conditions are satisfied, then EG is unique.
Equivalence between (1) and (2) is due to I. Kov´acs and J. Sz¨ucs [8], and equiv-
alences between (1), (3) and (4) are due to E. Størmer [9].
Observe that if G “ IntpMq is the group of all inner automorphisms of M, then
M is IntpMq-finite if and only if it is a finite von Neumann algebra. Apparently,
the latter case inspired the authors of [8]. E. Størmer was inspired by the article
of F.J. Yeadon [12] who proved the existence of the canonical center-valued trace
on a finite von Neumann algebra as a consequence of Ryll-Nardzewski fixed point
theorem.
It turns out that one of the main results of our article [7, Theorem 3.1] is a
characterization of finite von Neumann algebras in terms of suitable almost weakly
periodic functions on the unitary group UpMq. Thus, the aim of the present note is
to generalize the latter theorem to the more general case of arbitrary subgroups of
AutpMq. Here is our main result; the precise definition of weakly almost periodic
functions is recalled in the next section.
Theorem 1.2. Let M be a von Neumann algebra which acts standardly on H and
G be a subgroup of AutpMq. Then M is G-finite if and only if, for every x P M
and all ξ, η P H, the coefficient function g ÞÑ xgpxqξ|ηy is weakly almost periodic
on G.

The proof of Theorem 1.2 requires some preparation on weakly almost periodic
functions on topological groups and their properties.
