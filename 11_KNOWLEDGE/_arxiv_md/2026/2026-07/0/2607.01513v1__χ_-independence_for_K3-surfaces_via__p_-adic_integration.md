Abstract
This article provides a proof of a previously unknown case of Toda’s χ-independence conjecture by
reduction to non-archimedean local fields. Our strategy is based on a novel comparison of Frobenius-
traces for BPS sheaves on moduli spaces of objects in 2-Calabi-Yau categories and the integral of the
complex-exponentiated Hasse invariant of the obstruction gerbe. This result applies to many cases of
interest, including Nakajima quiver varieties, moduli of Higgs bundles and moduli of sheaves on K3
surfaces. Along the way, we describe the local structure of these moduli stacks and spaces over a base of
large mixed characteristic.

Introduction
The purpose of this note is to prove Toda’s χ-independence conjecture [Tod17, Conjecture 1.2] for the BPS
cohomology of moduli spaces of 1-dimensional sheaves on smooth projective surfaces X with trivial canonical
bundle, that is either a K3 or abelian surface.
Let β be an ample, base-point free curve class on X and χ ∈Z. We write Mβ,χ for the moduli stack
of pure 1-dimensional sheaves on X that are Gieseker-semistable with respect to a fixed polarization, have
support of class β and Euler-characteristic χ. Using dimensional reduction on the local Calabi-Yau 3-fold
X × A1 [Tod17] defines a perverse sheaf, in fact a mixed Hodge module, BPSMβ,χ on the good moduli
space Mβ,χ, which encodes the mathematical definition of Gopakumar-Vafa invariants in the presence of
strictly semistable objects. The invariants are essentially given as dimensions of graded pieces in the perverse
filtration on the cohomology H∗(Mβ,χ, BPSMβ,χ) induced by the proper Hilber-Chow morphism Mβ,χ →B =
PH0(X, OX(β)). Our first results is the following:
Theorem 1.1 (χ-independence for the perverse filtration). Let β be an ample and base-point free curve class
and χ, χ′ ∈Z. Then there exists an isomorphism of graded vector spaces
H∗(Mβ,χ, BPSMβ,χ) ∼= H∗(Mβ,χ′, BPSMβ,χ′),
respecting the perverse filtration.
We can also consider H∗(Mβ,χ, BPSMβ,χ) as a Hodge structure in which case we obtain:
Theorem 1.2 (χ-independence for Hodge numbers). Let β be an ample and base-point free curve class and
χ, χ′ ∈Z. Then, the BPS Hodge numbers satisfy for all p, q ≥0 the following relation
hp,q(Mβ,χ, BPSMβ,χ) = hp,q(Mβ,χ′, BPSMβ,χ′).
In fact, we prove relative versions of these results, see Theorem 5.1. These were also obtained by Davison–
Hennecart–Kinjo–Schiffmann–Vasserot simultaneously by means of a different argument [DHS+26].
Overview of previous results
Even though Toda’s χ-independence conjecture is expected to hold for a much wider class of moduli problems,
lately, the K3 case has received particular attention. The Euler characteristic version of Theorem 1.2 was
proven by Maulik–Thomas in [MT19, Theorem 1.2] and a K-theoretic refinement follows from [Tho24].
In [KK21], the χ-independence conjecture was established for the moduli problem of Higgs bundles on a
curve X. Due to the interpretation of Higgs bundles as coherent sheaves on the cotangent bundle T ∗X, this
case is often described as the local curve case.
Theorem 1.3 (Kinjo–Koseki). Let MDol(C; r, d) denote the moduli stack of semistable Higgs bundles of rank
r and degree d on a smooth proper curve C and MDol(C; r, d) its good moduli space. Then, for fixed r and
arbitrary integers d, d′ there exists an isomorphism
H∗(MDol(C; r, d), BPSMDol(C;r,d)) ∼= H∗(MDol(C; r, d′), BPSMDol(C;r,d′)).
When d and d′ are coprime to r, the resulting moduli spaces are smooth and BPS cohomology equals
singular cohomology. In the coprime situation, this degree-independence result was conjectured by Hausel
[Hau, Conjecture 3.2] and independently proven by Mellit in [Mel20] and the authors in [GWZ20].
Beyond the case of varieties with trivial canonical bundles, the Fano case has received attention. Coho-
mological χ-independence for moduli of sheaves on a del Pezzo surface with one-dimensional support, was
established by Maulik–Shen in [MS23]. In this case, the BPS-cohomology agrees with the classical intersec-
tion cohomology of the moduli space M and the stack M happens to be smooth. Both these properties fail
in the case of varieties with trivial canonical bundles.

The article is organised as follows: In Section 2 we establish several results regarding the the local
structure of moduli stacks, in particular with a symplectic structure, in mixed characteristics. This is applied
to moduli of 2-Calabi-Yau categories in Section 3, where we also introduce BPS sheaves. Section 4 establishes
the connection between BPS-invariants and p-adic integrals, Theorem 4.2, and finally Section 5 contains the
proof of the χ-independence statements.
