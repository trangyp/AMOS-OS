NILRADICAL
YANG CHEN, HAIJUN TAN, AND LU ZHANG
Abstract. In this paper, we classify the category of son(C)-modules whose restrictions to
the universal enveloping algebra of the abelian nilradical of a maximal parabolic subalgebra
are free of rank 1. We show that they generically are simple, and give an explicit description
of submodules in the non-simple case. This category embodies a certain connection between
weight modules and non-weight modules.
Keywords: Orthogonal Lie algebra, abelian nilradical, polynomial module, category O
2020 Math. Subj. Class.: 17B10, 17B20, 17B30, 17B35
1. Introduction
In Lie theory, the polynomial algebra serves as the fundamental space where Lie al-
gebras act as vector fields. If a Lie algebra is embedded in the n-th Weyl algebra, then it
has a polynomial module C[x1, · · · , xn]. Since the Cartan subalgebra h is abelian, the uni-
versal enveloping algebra U(h) is a polynomial algebra. In [7] and [8], Nilsson studied the
U(h)-free modules for finite-dimensional simple Lie algebras, and found that the U(h)-free
modules exist only for type A and type C using the weighting functor from this category to
the category of coherent families. In [11], Tan and Zhao determined the U(h)-free module
of rank one over the Witt algebra. Subsequently, in [6] and [2], simple U(h)-free modules
of arbitrary rank were constructed for sl2 and the Witt algebra respectively. In [3] and
[9], the authors independently constructed U(n)-free sll+1(C)-modules of rank 1, where n is
the abelian nilradical of the maximal parabolic subalgebra. Specifically, in [3] the authors
construct U(h)-free sll+1(C)-modules of finite rank using U(n)-free sll+1(C)-modules. In a
sense, the U(n)-free modules provide a class of polynomial modules. According to reference
[10], the parabolic subalgebra with abelian nilradical exists only in types A, B, C, D, E6, E7.
In [1], we study U(n)-free modules of rank 1 in type C, where the Krull dimension of U(n)
is much greater than the rank of sp2l(C). In the present paper, we classify the categories of
U(n)-free modules of rank 1 in type B and type D. The structure of U(n)-free modules in
type B is relatively simpler than that in type C. However, there exist two non-isomorphic
abelian nilradicals n1 and nl in type D, where U(n1)-free modules can be obtained from
U(n)-free modules in type B. It is worth mentioning that these modules are closely related
to lowest weight modules, which belong to the BGG category O, see [4]. By solving singular
vectors, we obtain a simplicity criterion and an algorithm to produce all composition series
for type B or type D, which also applies to other types. We will further explore U(n)-free
modules of arbitrary rank in a subsequent paper. Throughout this paper, we denote by Z,
Z+, N and C the sets of all integers, positive integers, nonnegative integers and complex
numbers, respectively. All algebras and vector spaces are assumed to be over C.
