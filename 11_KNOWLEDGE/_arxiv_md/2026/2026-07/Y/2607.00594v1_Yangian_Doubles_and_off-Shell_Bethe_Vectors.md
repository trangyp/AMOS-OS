Abstract
Off-shell Bethe vectors for a generic g invariant integrable model are constructed through
the currents of the Yangian doubles of the classical series. These off-shell Bethe vectors are
shown to satisfy the defining properties which were used in [28] to prove the rectangular
recurrence relations and verify the eigenvalue property of the on-shell Bethe vectors in g-
invariant integrable models.

Introduction
The algebraic Bethe ansatz for g-invariant integrable models provides a systematic framework
for constructing common eigenvectors of commuting integrals of motion. Originally developed
for low-rank algebras, in particular for g = gl2 [35], it allows not only the construction of on-shell
Bethe vectors but also the computation of scalar products, norms, and form factors.
Extending these results to higher-rank symmetries and to algebras different from gln requires
an explicit construction of off-shell Bethe vectors. Early approaches based on the nested Bethe
ansatz [23, 24, 33, 34] yield Bethe equations but are not efficient in constructing Bethe vectors
themselves. A combinatorial construction using the trace formula was developed for gln- and
Uq(gln)-invariant models in [36, 37], and later extended to the C and D series in [13]. To our
best knowledge no trace formula exist for the B series.
Since 2007, a new method based on Drinfeld’s ’new’ realization of quantum affine algebras
[8] has been proposed [11]. It relies on projections onto intersections of different types of Borel
subalgebras and expresses off-shell Bethe vectors as projections of ordered products of simple
root currents.
This approach was applied to supersymmetric Yangian doubles in [14], with
computational techniques developed in [20]. Using the Ding–Frenkel isomorphism [3], off-shell
Bethe vectors can be written as polynomials in the monodromy matrix entries Ti,j(u), whose
recurrence relations coincide with those obtained from the trace formula [37].
For the calculation of scalar products, explicit formulas are not as important as the re-
currence relations arising from the action of monodromy matrix elements on off-shell Bethe
vectors.
These relations determine recurrence relations for the highest coefficients of scalar

5
Conclusion
The main result of this paper is to establish that off-shell Bethe vectors in a generic g-invariant
integrable model can be defined within the framework of the projection method introduced
in [11] for quantum affine algebras and extended here to Yangian doubles. To prove this, it
is sufficient to verify that the off-shell Bethe vectors defined by (3.7) in the framework of the
projection method satisfy the defining relations for off-shell Bethe vectors formulated in [28]. In
the case of integrable models associated with the supersymmetric Yangian doubles DY (gl(m|n)),
the projection method was previously explored in [14].
This result, together with those obtained in the paper [28], shows that the structure of the
space of states in g-invariant quantum integrable models can be studied using two equivalent
approaches: either with the explicit construction of off-shell Bethe vectors via the projection
method, or with their implicit definition 3.1.
A natural next step is to investigate scalar products of off-shell Bethe vectors, their norms,
and form factors in generic g-invariant quantum integrable models using these complementary
approaches. We leave the exploration of this direction for future work.
