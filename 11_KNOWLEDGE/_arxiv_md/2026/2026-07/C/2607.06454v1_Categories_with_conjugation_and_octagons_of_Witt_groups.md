AND OCTAGONS OF WITT GROUPS
DAVID W. LEWIS† AND JEAN-PIERRE TIGNOL
Abstract. A categorical approach is proposed for the construction of 8-periodic chain com-
plexes of Witt groups of categories with duality.
Exactness of the resulting sequences is
proved under the hypothesis that the categories are abelian and artinian.
Introduction
In the algebraic theory of quadratic forms, the special rˆole played by quadratic field exten-
sions was noticed early. Among the first observations stands out the description by Pfister [16,
p. 123] of quadratic forms that become hyperbolic over a given quadratic extension, reformu-
lated in terms of hermitian forms by Milnor–Husemoller: see [15, App. 2], where it appears as
a 4-term exact sequence. Using Scharlau’s transfer, Elman–Lam extended the sequence into
a long 3-periodic exact sequence (an “exact triangle” or “symmetric exact hexagon”, see [3,
Th. 2.6]). Extending this early result in the framework of hermitian or skew-hermitian forms
over quaternion algebras, the first author uncovered a more complex behaviour, giving rise to
longer exact sequences relating forms over quaternion algebras and over quadratic subfields,
see [11], [12] (see also [18, Th. 10.3.2]). It was soon realised that exact sequences thus con-
structed are 8-periodic, and can therefore be folded into exact octagons, see [13]. Meanwhile,
Ranicki also constructed 8-periodic exact sequences of Witt groups for “twisted quadratic ex-
tensions” via L-theory: see [17] and [6, (0.2)] (and the comments after Corollary 4.3 for a precise
statement).
Building on the first author’s work, Parimala–Sridharan–Suresh [2, Appendix 2] extended
the scope of [12] beyond the case of quaternion algebras, obtaining exact sequences relating
Witt groups of hermitian or skew-hermitian forms over a central simple algebra and over the
centraliser of a quadratic subfield.
These exact sequences were further patched into exact
octagons of Witt groups by Grenier-Boley–Mahmoudi [5]. The latest development in this line
of investigation is due to First [4], who obtained an analogous exact octagon of Witt groups for
Azumaya algebras over semilocal rings. His paper also discusses several important applications.
The purpose of this work is to unveil the categorical mechanism that produces the octagons
referred to above. The basic tool is a notion of conjugation on an additive pseudo-abelian (=
idempotent-complete) category with 1

4. Witt groups
Throughout this section, C is, as in the preceding sections, a pseudo-abelian k-linear category
where k is a commutative ring in which 2 is invertible. We assume moreover that C is essentially
small, which means that the isomorphism classes of objects in C form a set.
If C carries
a conjugation (ι, λ) or a duality (D, δ), it follows that the categories C′ and S(C, D, δ) also
are essentially small. If (C, D, δ) is an exact category with duality in the sense of [1, §1.2.2],
then metabolic symmetric spaces are defined, and a classical construction due to Knebusch [1,
Def. 27] yields the Witt group W(C, D, δ): this group is the quotient of the monoid of isometry
classes of symmetric spaces in S(C, D, δ) by the submonoid of isometry classes of metabolic
symmetric spaces. Thus, the definition of W(C, D, δ) depends on some additional structure on
the category with duality (C, D, δ).
In the split exact structure, the exact sequences are split exact and, since 2 is invertible in k,
the metabolic spaces are the hyperbolic spaces, see [1, Example 21]. Therefore, the Witt group
W(C, D, δ) is the usual Witt group as defined in [18, Ch. 7, §2]. When C carries a conjugation
(ι, λ) and a commuting duality (D, δ), the functors in the octagon (3.2) induce Witt group
homomorphisms since they carry hyperbolic spaces to hyperbolic spaces, and Theorem 3.1
shows that they form an 8-periodic chain complex.
For the rest of this section, we assume that C is an abelian category, which we endow with its
usual exact structure. For every conjugation (ι, λ) on C, the condition that ιι is naturally equiv-
