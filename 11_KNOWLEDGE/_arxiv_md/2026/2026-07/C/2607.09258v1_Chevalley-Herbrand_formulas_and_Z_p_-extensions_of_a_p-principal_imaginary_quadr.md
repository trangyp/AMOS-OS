CHEVALLEY–HERBRAND FORMULAS AND Zp-EXTENSIONS
OF A p-PRINCIPAL IMAGINARY QUADRATIC FIELD
GEORGES GRAS
Abstract. Let k be an imaginary quadratic field and let p ̸= 2 be a prime number, split in
k into pp. We assume that the p-class group of k is trivial. Let δ ≥0 be the p-valuation of
the p-Fermat quotient of the fundamental p-unit x of k. Let K/k be any Zp-extension and
let pe be the degree of the inertia field of p, p being totally ramified. We prove that if e ≥δ,
then λ(K/k) = 1, µ(K/k) = 0; if e < δ a characterization is obtained from the Sp-Iwasawa
invariants (Theorems 6.1, 7.6). These results only use generalizations of Chevalley–Herbrand
formulas and the non-nullity of a p-adic regulator Rp
δ in incomplete p-ramification. They
provide effective and computable results that complement some aspects of Iwasawa theory.
A pari/gp program computes δ and Rp
δ, for p = 3, e = 1.

1. Introduction
Let k be an imaginary quadratic field and let p be an odd prime number split in k; we
denote by τ the complex conjugation and set (p) = pp, where p := pτ.
In [Oza2001, Theorem 1], Ozaki states:
Let k be an imaginary quadratic field and p ≥2 a prime number. Assume that the prime
p splits in k and the class number of k is prime to p. Then λ(K/k) = 1 and µ(K/k) = 0 for
all but finitely many Zp-extensions K over k.
We intend to analyze, in a practical and effective point of view, this result; so we assume
in this paper that the p-class group Hk of k is trivial. Let ek be the compositum of the Zp-
extensions of k. Let K/k be a Zp-extension in which p, p ramify, which excludes the conjugate
Zp-extensions L, L, defined as the inertia fields, in ek/k, of p, p. So, L/k is unramified at p
and p is totally ramified in L/k since non-trivial unramified extensions of k in ek do not exist;
hence, L ∩L = k and LL = ek.
Using the original Chevalley–Herbrand formula [Che1933, pp.
402–406] in Ln/k gives
λ(L/k) = µ(L/k) = ν(L/k) = 0 and similarly for L. But L, L play an important role.
Denote by Kn the layer of K of degree pn over k. Since the p-class group of k is trivial,
the Zp-extension K/k is totally ramified at one of the two primes p, p. If K/k is Galois, it is
totally ramified at p. If K/k is non-Galois, let’s say, for example, that p is totally ramified
and that p ramifies from Ke, e ≥0; since for the complex conjugate K, p is totally ramified
and p is ramified from Ke, one may choose the conjugate K such that e = 0 and e ≥0.
We have obtained the following result, for p ≥3 split in k and Hk = 1, without assuming
that the p-class group H ek of ek is a pseudo-null Λ-module (Minardi [Min1986]), nor using
any resoning in Iwasawa’s theory; that said, a complete use of p-adic class field theory is
obligatory in all the approaches, and we wanted to show that suitable Chevalley–Herbrand
formulas are sufficient in many cases:

addressed do indeed involve random invariants/objects, as the Fermat quotients (i.e. the

relative extensions). The rare partial proofs need very strong assumptions on the arithmetic
of the fields; don’t forget that in the present article (among others), all the fields under
consideration are p-rationals, which represents the zero level of arithmetic complexity. Last
but not least, one must accept classical Leopoldt and Gross-Kuz’min conjectures as is done,
without adverse consequences, in any arithmetic context.
