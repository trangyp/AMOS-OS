Chow classes of orbit closures of skew-symmetric matrix pencils
Ari Krishna
July 14, 2026
Abstract
Let 𝑉be a six-dimensional complex vector space and let
𝐺= Gr(2, Λ2𝑉∨)
parametrize pencils of skew-symmetric 6 × 6 matrices. The group PGL(𝑉) has 11 orbits on 𝐺, classified
by the Jordan–Kronecker canonical form. We compute the Chow class of every orbit closure in the
Schubert basis of CH∗(𝐺).
2020 Mathematics Subject Classification. Primary 14C17; Secondary 14L30, 14M15, 15A22.
Keywords. Skew-symmetric matrix pencil; Pfaffian cubic; orbit closure; Chow class; Schubert calculus;
Jordan–Kronecker canonical form; Gysin formula.

Introduction
Let 𝑉be a complex vector space of dimension six and put
𝑊= Λ2𝑉∨,
𝐺= Gr(2, 𝑊).
A point 𝐿∈𝐺is an unframed pencil of alternating forms on 𝑉. Choosing a basis 𝐴, 𝐵of 𝐿produces a
skew-symmetric matrix pencil
𝐴−𝜆𝐵,
well-defined up to simultaneous congruence by GL(𝑉) and change of basis in 𝐿. The congruence clas-
sification of skew-symmetric matrix pencils is classical and is expressed in terms of Jordan–Kronecker
blocks. The dimensions and closure relations of the corresponding affine congruence orbits have been
studied systematically by Dmytryshyn, Kågström, Sergeichuk, and Das [5, 4, 3]. Passing to unframed
pencils identifies eigenvalue configurations up to PGL2. In size six, there are at most three eigenvalues, and
consequently the induced action
PGL(𝑉) ↷𝐺
has finitely many orbits.
The projective geometry is governed by the Pfaffian cubic hypersurface
Pf(𝑉) = {[𝜔] ∈ℙ(𝑊) ∶rk(𝜔) ≤4}.
Its singular locus is the Plücker Grassmannian
𝑋= Gr(2, 𝑉∨) = {[𝜔] ∶rk(𝜔) ≤2} ⊂ℙ(𝑊).
