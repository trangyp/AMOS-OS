Abstract. We introduce categories of strings to give an explicit hom-object formula 
for colimits of enriched categories. As corollaries, we recover the necklace construction 
by Dugger and Spivak and describe colimits in Cat and of Lawvere metric spaces.

1. Introduction
Let a monoidal category 𝒱︀ be cocomplete and let the tensor product ⊗ be cocontinuous in each argument. 
This paper explicitly constructs the colimit of a diagram 𝐹: ℐ︀→Cat𝒱︀ in the category of 𝒱︀-enriched

enriched categories, Dugger and Spivak provide an explicit construction via necklaces in [DS11]. Section 6 
recovers this special case in Corollary 6.18 [DS11, Corollary 4.4].

colimits in CatsSet are notoriously difficult to understand.
