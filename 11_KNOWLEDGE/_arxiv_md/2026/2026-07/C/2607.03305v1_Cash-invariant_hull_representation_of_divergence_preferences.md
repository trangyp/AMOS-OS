CASH-INVARIANT HULL REPRESENTATION
OF DIVERGENCE PREFERENCES
ALEˇS ˇCERN´Ya,b, JOHANNES RUFb, AND MARTIN SCHWEIZERc
Abstract. Uniformly weighted divergence preferences (UWDP) introduced in Maccheroni
et al. [5] are an important class of risk-averse preferences that contain as a special case the
monotone mean–variance utility.
UWDP are characterised by the lowest expected value of
an act in L∞under an adversarially chosen probability measure combined with the diver-
gence of this measure.
Our main result provides an alternative, computationally friendlier
formula, which establishes in full generality that UWDP are the translation-invariant hull of
state-independent expected utility over L0. Some consequences of the new representation are
studied.
1. Introduction
This paper continues the study of divergence preferences introduced and axiomatized in
Maccheroni, Marinacci, and Rustichini [5] as part of the wider class of so-called variational
preferences.
We restrict our attention to uniformly weighted divergence preferences, which
are known to be probabilistically sophisticated [5, Theorem 16] and already cover the special
instance of the monotone mean–variance (MMV) preferences of Maccheroni, Marinacci, Rusti-
chini, and Taboga [6]. In this case, for a suitable non-negative convex function f with f(1) = 0,
the wealth distributions (acts) W are ranked by the divergence utility V : L∞→R, given by

Remark 3.3. By comparing Propositions 3.1 and 3.2, we observe that, for a given Q, finiteness

is finite but the divergence utility is infinite.
