Abstract
In this note we explore numerically the finite Bernoulli convolutions. We show that with a
suitable choice of parameter, it might serve as a toy model for intermittent energy cascade in fully
developed turbulence. We then show how the crossings of β-expansions distribute in β, and suggest
that it might highlight the parameters with enhanced overlap structure that are related to measures
that are singular continuous. We later introduce a notion of order to the β-expansions based on
the lexicographical order of the N-binary words, and observe that for most sampled adjacent pairs
when β = 2, the distance in their order increases exponentially when β decreases from 2 to 1.
This suggests ’chaotic’ behavior, with the ’Lyapunov exponents’ bunched into several clusters that
depend on N. We end the note with some ’order plots’ and an interesting connection between the
finite β-compactum with β = g (g being the golden ratio) and binary reflected Gray code.
