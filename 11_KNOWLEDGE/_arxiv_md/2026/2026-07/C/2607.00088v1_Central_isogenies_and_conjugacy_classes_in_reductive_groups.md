Abstract. Steinberg described the group of components of the centralizer of a semisimple element
of a connected semisimple algebraic group G as a subgroup of the fundamental group of G. We
show that this description can be generalized to explain the fact that centralizers of unipotent
elements can fail to be reduced when the universal cover of G is not ´etale. As applications, we
compute generic multiplicities in the special fibers of moduli spaces of L-parameters and universal
deformation rings, and we show there is no Springer isomorphism for PGLp in characteristic p.
1. Introduction
Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic p ≥0, let G be a simply connected
semisimple k-group, and let g ∈G(k). Steinberg showed [Ste68, Theorem 8.1] that if g is semisimple,
then the centralizer ZG(g) is connected. More generally, if π: G →G′ is a central isogeny, g is
semisimple, and g′ = π(g), then [Ste68, Theorem 9.1(a)] shows that π0(ZG′(g′)) is isomorphic
to a subgroup of (ker π)(k).
Indeed, G′ acts on G by conjugation, so there is a natural map
ZG′(g′) →ker π given by h 7→(hgh−1)g−1, and this induces a monic homomorphism
ig : ZG′(g′)/π(ZG(g)) →ker π.
By Steinberg’s connectedness result and the smoothness of ZG′(g′) [Bor91, Corollary to Theorem
9.2], we have ZG′(g′)/π(ZG(g)) ∼= π0(ZG′(g′)).
It is easy to check that if µ ⊂(ker π)(k) is a
subgroup, then the following are equivalent:
(1) the image of ig contains µ,
(2) the conjugacy class Cg of g is stable under multiplication by µ.
If g is instead unipotent, then the map ig still makes sense, but it does not appear to be very
interesting: indeed, the uniqueness of the Jordan decomposition shows that a unipotent orbit cannot
be stable under multiplication by a nontrivial central element of G(k). However, unlike in the case
treated above, it is no longer true that ZG′(g′) is smooth in general, even when Z(G′) is smooth.
This may be seen most easily in the following example.
