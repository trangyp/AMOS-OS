The vector sign has been measured. With both form factors positive, the IB-SD interference in K+ →µ+νµγ
is predicted to be net destructive, dominated by the INT−∝(FV −FA) term: Table 4 of Ref. [10] gives INT−=
−3.83 × 10−5 and INT+ = +1.44 × 10−5 over the full phase space. ISTRA+ [12] observed destructive interference in
the structure-dependent region of K+ →µ+νµγ and obtained FV −FA = 0.21 ± 0.04 ± 0.04 > 0, in agreement with
the prediction. Reversing the relative sign of the SD amplitude would reverse the observed interference.
Comparing with Eq. (7): the DF94 amplitude carries the correct sign in both sectors; the GR10 amplitude, and with
it the AHLRR analysis, carries the correct axial sign and the opposite of the correct vector sign; the DF93 amplitude
carried the opposite sign in both sectors. The sign reversal performed in the DF addendum [6], there motivated by the
chiral results of Ref. [10] and by SU(3) relations to the Kℓ4 anomalous form factor, is thereby confirmed. Reference [3]
(Sec. I) attributes that reversal to the absence of a Lagrangian approach in Refs. [5, 6]; the present analysis shows
the reversal was correct, and that it is the vector sector of the Lagrangian computation of Ref. [3] that disagrees with
the chiral anomaly.

and |Vus| analyses [16].
IV.
SUMMARY
(i) The convention dictionary in footnote 2 of Ref. [3] and footnote 1 of Ref. [2] has a sign error in its axial entry;
the correct relation is Eq. (6). No global factor relates the GR10 and DF conventions. (ii) Consequently the GR10
amplitude does not carry the pre-addendum DF93 relative sign: it agrees with DF94 in the axial sector, and footnote 6
of Ref. [2] does not hold. (iii) The relative sign adopted in the DF addendum [6] is the physical one in both sectors,
fixed by the chiral anomaly and L9 + L10 and anchored by the ISTRA+ measurement [12]; the vector sector of the
GR10 amplitude carries the opposite sign and should be corrected. (iv) The numerical impact on δrSD is small,
−0.004% (π) and ≈−0.02% (K); the dominant uncertainty in δrSD is instead the form factor shape dependence of
the axial interference, which is larger than the quoted errors.
