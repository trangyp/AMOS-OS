STABILITY OF SYZYGY BUNDLES ON CERTAIN PICARD RANK ONE
VARIETIES
SUPRAVAT SARKAR
Abstract. If X is a smooth projective variety of dimension at least 2 and Picard rank 1 on
which every ample line bundle is globally generated, we prove that syzygy bundle of any ample
line bundle is stable. This applies in particular to complete intersections in projective space.
This extends earlier results of Jiang-Ren, Coandă and others.
Keywords: syzygy bundle, stability
MSC Number: 14H60, 14C20
1. Introduction
We work throughout over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic 0. Given a nontrivial
globally generated line bundle L on a smooth projective variety X, one defines the syzygy bundle
ML to be the kernel of the evaluation map H0(X, L) ⊗OX →L. It has been an important area
of research to decide whether ML is stable with respect to a fixed polarization. In [7], it was
proved that ML is stable whenever L is sufficiently ample in some sense.
The problem is more interesting when X has Picard rank 1, as then the choice of the polarization
does not matter. Consider first the case where X is a curve of genus g. In this case many results
are known. For g = 0, clearly ML is never stable whenever deg L ≥2, as ML always splits. For
g ≥1, [6] proves that ML is stable whenever deg L ≥2g + 1. This bound was improved in [2],
in terms of Clifford index of X. [2] also shows that there are examples where ML is unstable.
On the other hand, when dim X ≥2 and X has Picard rank 1, there is no known example where
ML is not stable. So a natural question is the following:
Question 1.1. Let X be a smooth projective variety of Picard rank 1 and dimension ≥2, and
L a nontrivial globally generated line bundle on X. Is the syzygy bundle ML stable?
It appeared in a slightly different form in [8, Question 2]. In [7], Ein, Lazarsfield and Mustopa
conjectured an affirmative answer to this question when L is sufficiently ample, and this con-
jecture was proved by Rekuski in [14]. Affirmative answers to this question in several cases are
known by [3],[13], [4], [10] and other works.
The goal of this short note is to give an affirmative answer to Question 1.1 for a large class of
varieties X. Our result is the following.
Theorem A. Let X be a smooth projective variety of dimension at least 2 and Picard rank 1.
Suppose all ample line bundles on X are globally generated. Then for every ample line bundle L
on X, the syzygy bundle ML is stable.
The hypothesis of Theorem A is satisfied for all smooth complete intersections X of dimension
≥2 and Picard rank 1 in projective space, so we get stability of syzygy bundle on all such X
(Corollary 4.1). This was known before only for X Fano or Calabi-Yau, see [10].

Remark 4.2. Using Mehta-Ramanathan type restriction theorems, one can show that Question
1.1 always has an affirmative answer if and only if it has an affirmative answer whenever X
is a surface. By [3],[13] and [4], Question 1.1 has an affirmative answer for all Picard rank 1
surfaces not of general type. Corollary 4.1 answers the questions affirmatively for a large class
of surfaces of general type.
Remark 4.3. Question 1.1 is 1st part of [8, Question 2]. The latter question has two other
parts, but those are easier and one can show desired examples exist on P2 by elementary methods
or using [1, Theorem 7.4]. See also [12].
