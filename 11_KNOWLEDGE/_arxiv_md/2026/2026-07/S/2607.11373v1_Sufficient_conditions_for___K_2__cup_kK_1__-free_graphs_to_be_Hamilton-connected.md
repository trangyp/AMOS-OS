Motivated by these works, this paper studies sufficient conditions for the Hamilton-
connectedness of (K2 ∪kK1)-free graphs with k ≥1.
In 1972, a classical result of Chv´atal and Erd˝os [8] states that every k-connected
kP1-free graph is Hamilton-connected. It is well known that a Hamilton-connected graph
has toughness strictly greater than one. In 1978, for P4-free graphs, Jung [14] established
the equivalence between Hamilton-connectedness and toughness being greater than one.
Theorem 1.6 (Jung [14]). Let G be a P4-free graph. Then G is Hamilton-connected if
and only if τ(G) > 1.
Theorem 1.6 was later extended by Zheng, Broersma and Wang [27] to graphs for-
bidding an induced subgraph P3 ∪K1 or K2 ∪2K1.
Theorem 1.7 (Zheng, Broersma and Wang [27]). Let R be either P3 ∪K1 or K2 ∪2K1.
Then every R-free graph G with τ(G) > 1 on at least three vertices is Hamilton-connected.
Most recently, Liu [17] obtained the following theorem concerning the specific forbid-
den subgraph K2 ∪kK1.
Theorem 1.8 (Liu [17]). Let k be a positive integer. Then every 2k-connected (K2∪kK1)-
free graph G with τ(G) > 1 is Hamilton-connected.
In this paper, we demonstrate the connectivity condition in Theorem 1.8 can be
significantly reduced by imposing a minimum degree condition. Our main results are as
follows.

Conclusion
In this paper, we obtained two sufficient conditions for Hamilton-connectedness in
(K2 ∪kK1)-free graphs. The first result (Theorem 1.9) reduces the connectivity require-
ment in Liu’s result [17] from κ(G) ≥2k to κ(G) ≥k + 1 by adding the minimum degree
condition δ(G) ≥2k. In Proposition 3.3, we construct a (k−1)-connected graph M show-
ing that the connectivity condition in Theorem 1.9 cannot be reduced to κ(G) ≥k −1.
It would be natural to further consider the case of k-connected graphs; formally, we pose
the following problem.
Problem 1. For each integer k ≥1, is it true that every k-connected (K2 ∪kK1)-free
graph with τ(G) > 1 Hamilton-connected?
The second result (Theorem 1.11) shows that under an independence number condi-
tion 2k + 1 ≤α(G) < n
2, the connectivity can be further relaxed to k. However, we are
not sure whether the minimum degree condition is sharp in general, and we are interested
in whether it can be relaxed to k. Finally, we pose the following problem.
Problem 2. For each integer k ≥1, is it true that every k-connected (K2 ∪kK1)-free
graph with 2k + 1 ≤α(G) < n
2 Hamilton-connected?
