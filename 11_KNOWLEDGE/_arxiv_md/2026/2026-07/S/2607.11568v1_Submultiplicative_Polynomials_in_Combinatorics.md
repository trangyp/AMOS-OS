1. Introduction
We investigate submultiplicative sequences. They appear frequently in chem-
istry, statistical physics, and combinatorics. The Nobel laureate P. Flory [9] was
among the first to study self-avoiding walks in lattices to model the real-life behav-
ior of chain-like entities such as solvents and polymers. Let cn be the number of
self-avoiding walks in a lattice Λ visiting every vertex at most once and taking n
steps, started from some fixed vertex, e.g., the origin (see, [7]). Then

hexagonal lattice [8]. In many other cases the value of the connective constant is
unknown.
In the context of partitions this property (1.1) was detected by Bessenrodt
and Ono [3] for the partition function for n, m ≥5. Chern, Fu, and Tang [5] gen-

for all integers k ≥3 and n, m ≥1. Further, let P σ
