HARMONIC BERGMAN SPACES WITH APPLICATIONS TO MÖBIUS
INVARIANT SPACES
HIMADRI HALDER AND ROHIT KUMAR
Abstract. The aim of this paper is twofold.
First, we establish a Riesz conjugate
theorem for weighted harmonic Bergman spaces. More precisely, we prove that if f =
u + iv is a harmonic K-quasiregular mapping in D and the real part u belongs to the
weighted harmonic Bergman space ap
α, 0 < p < ∞, then the imaginary part v also belongs
to the same space, together with a quantitative norm estimate. Moreover, for 1 < p <
∞, the corresponding constant is shown to be independent of the weight parameter α.
Second, we establish Riesz–Fejér inequalities for weighted harmonic Bergman spaces for
1 < p < ∞. In the special case p = 2, we further improve the corresponding constant
by using the Hilbert space structure and orthogonality techniques. As applications of
our main results, we establish Riesz conjugate theorems and Riesz–Fejér inequalities for
the Möbius invariant spaces Q(n, p, α) introduced by Zhu [Illinois J. Math. 51 (2007),
pp. 977–1002] and their harmonic counterparts Qh(n, p, α) introduced by Sun, Liu, and
Wang [Potential Anal. 65 (2026), Article no. 12].

1.1. Riesz conjugate theorem for weighted harmonic Bergman space. One may
naturally ask whether Theorems A and B remain valid for complex-valued harmonic func-
tions. More precisely, let f = u + iv be a complex-valued harmonic function in D with
real part u ∈hp. Does it follow that v ∈hp for 1 < p < ∞? Likewise, if u ∈ap, does
one necessarily have v ∈ap for all 0 < p < ∞. In general, the answer to both questions
is negative (see [2, 6, 9, 25]). For the Hardy space setting, Liu and Zhu [25] showed that
the Riesz conjugate theorem holds provided that the harmonic mapping f is quasiregular.
This result was subsequently sharpened by Chen et al. [6]. For the Bergman space setting
ap, an analogous sufficient condition was recently obtained by Das and Rasila [9]. We next
recall the notions of sense-preserving harmonic mappings, quasiconformal mappings, and
quasiregular mappings.
Every complex-valued harmonic function in D admits a canonical, unique decomposition
of the form f = h+g, where h and g are analytic in D and are uniquely determined by the
normalization g(0) = 0. Such functions f in D is said to be sense-preserving if its Jacobian
Jf(z) := |h′(z)|2 −|g′(z)|2 is positive in D, or equivalently, |g′(z)| < |h′(z)| for all z ∈D.

quasiregular function. It says that if f = u + iv is a K-quasiregular harmonic function

Kolmogorov-type theorems for harmonic quasiregular mappings, and subsequently estab-
lished a quasiregular analogue of Zygmund’s theorem [23]. For recent advances on the
Riesz theorem and related results concerning quasiregular mappings, we refer the reader
to [19, 20, 21, 27].
