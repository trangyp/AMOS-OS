Revisiting wormhole-induced global symmetry breaking
Kiyoharu Kawana1, ∗
1School of Physics, Korea Institute for Advanced Study, Seoul 02455, Korea
(Dated: July 14, 2026)
It is widely believed that global symmetries cannot exist in a consistent theory of quantum gravity.
A prominent mechanism underlying this expectation is provided by gravitational instantons or
Euclidean wormholes, whose contributions to the Euclidean path integral generates local symmetry-
breaking operators in the effective action with couplings parametrized by the α-parameters −→
α =
(α1, α2, · · · ).
The appearance of these α-parameters is often taken as evidence that wormholes
explicitly break global symmetries. In this paper, we argue that this conclusion is premature. The
fate of the symmetry is determined by the ensemble average over these α-parameters. We show
that, under broad situations, this average can be sharply dominated by the symmetric point −→
α = 0,
while contributions from other symmetry-breaking critical points −→
α ̸= 0 are typically suppressed by
a doubly exponential factor exp(−e2Sins), where Sins is the instanton action. In particular, standard
U(1) Peccei–Quinn models fall into this class, implying that the conventional axion quality problem
does not arise in the wormhole-induced effective theory. Our analysis is formulated for general U(1)
p-form global symmetries.
I.
INTRODUCTION
It has long been argued that global symmetries can-
not exist in a consistent theory of quantum gravity [1–
11].
A concrete realization of this expectation is pro-
vided by Euclidean wormholes or gravitational instan-
tons. The Giddings–Strominger wormhole [12] is an Eu-
clidean solution connecting two asymptotically flat space-
times through an S3 throat supported by the H3 = dB2
flux, where B2 is the dual field of the axion.
In the
dual picture,
R
S3 H3 corresponds to the U(1) Peccei–
Quinn (PQ) charge, and the wormhole is transporting
the charge from one Universe to another, leading to an
explicit breaking of the U(1) global symmetry.
Summing over such wormholes in the Euclidean path
integral generates bilocal terms in the effective action [2,
13–17], and they can be converted into local symmetry-
breaking terms by introducing auxiliary α-parameters.
These terms correspond to wormhole-induced potentials,
whose coupling constants are suppressed by the instanton
factor e−Sins. This factor serves as a qualitative indicator
of the strength of symmetry breaking induced by worm-
holes.
In this paper, we revisit this wormhole-induced mecha-
nism of global-symmetry breaking by carefully analyzing
the ensemble average over the α-parameters. We focus on
a general matter theory with a U(1) p-form global sym-
metry and study its effective theory in the presence of
the α-parameters generated by gravitational instantons.
Throughout this paper, we denote the U(1) p-form global
symmetry simply as U(1)[p] and the set of α-parameters
by −→
α = (α1, α2, · · · ).
The key observation is that the mere existence of α-
parameters does not immediately imply explicit breaking
of U(1)[p]; rather, the ensemble average can dynamically

Of course, this ensemble perspective was
