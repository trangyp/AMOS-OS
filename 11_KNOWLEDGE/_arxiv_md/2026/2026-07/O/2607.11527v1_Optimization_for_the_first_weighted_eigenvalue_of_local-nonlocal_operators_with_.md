OPTIMIZATION FOR THE FIRST WEIGHTED EIGENVALUE OF
LOCAL-NONLOCAL OPERATORS WITH A POTENTIAL
R. LAKSHMI, SEKHAR GHOSH AND RATAN KR. GIRI
Abstract. In this work, we study the optimization problem for the first eigenvalue of mixed
local nonlocal operators plus a potential V . We begin by investigating the existence and fun-
damental properties of the eigenvalues, with special emphasis on the first eigenvalue. Finally,
we discuss the dependence of the first eigenvalue on the potential function and establish the
existence of optimal potentials within certain admissible classes. In particular, we show the
existence of a unique maximizer and a minimizer of the first eigenvalue on any bounded,
closed, and convex subset of Lq(Ω). Moreover, these results enable us to characterize the
maximizers and minimizers in the closed unit ball of Lq(Ω), as well as in the class of rear-
rangements of any V ∈Lq(Ω).
1.
Introduction
The analysis of eigenvalue problems for elliptic operators plays a fundamental role in the study
of partial differential equations, owing to their strong influence on the existence and qualitative
properties of solutions to elliptic PDEs, as well as their wide applications in mathematical
physics [5, 24, 34].
Over the years, the existence and properties of eigenvalues for various
local, nonlocal, and mixed operators have been extensively investigated; see, for example,
[12,15,20,21,30,31] and the references therein.
The optimization theory of eigenvalues of elliptic operators is a significant problem in the
theory of eigenvalues due to their importance in fields such as scattering theory, quantum
mechanics, etc., (see [22, 34]). One of the earlier works in the optimization of eigenvalues to
elliptic operators is by Harrell [25], who studied the existence and properties of a potential V
which maximizes the first eigenvalue for the operator −∆+ V on a set of potentials with a
restriction on the Lp norm of V . Later, for the same operator −∆+V with some restrictions on
V , the existence and uniqueness of both maximizers and minimizers to the first eigenvalue were
studied by Ashbaugh and Harrell [3], and the optimizers were characterized. Subsequently,
Bonder and Pezzo [19] extended the results in [3] to the first eigenvalue for the operator
−∆p + V , where ∆p is the p-Laplacian defined by
∆pu = div(|∇u|p−2∇u).
