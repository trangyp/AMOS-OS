ON THE SELF-ADJOINT PROPERTIES OF THE STANDARD
WHITTAKER (g, K)-MODULES
KENJI TANIGUCHI
Abstract. The structure of the standard Whittaker (g, K)-module is exam-
ined in the case when the group in question is a real split reductive linear Lie
group. This module is an injective object in the category of Harish-Chandra
(g, K)-modules which admit a fixed infinitesimal character. The global char-
acter of this module is determined. The main theorem of this paper is that it
has a self-adjoint structure. Also obtained are the explicit socle filtrations of
the standard Whittaker (g, K)-modules for the rank two split groups SL(3, R),
Sp(2, R) and G2(split).
1. Introduction
Let G be a real reductive linear Lie group in the sense of [21] and G = KAN be
an Iwasawa decomposition of it. Let η : N −→C× be a unitary character of N. We
assume η is non-degenerate, i.e. it is non-trivial on every root space corresponding
to a simple root of Σ(n0, a0). Define

As for the subrepresentations of this space, many deep and interesting results

In the group split G2, there are four conjugacy classes of Cartan subgroups. The
fundamental Cartan subgroup Hf is a compact Cartan subgroup. Between Hf and
the split Cartan subgroup Hs, there are two Cartan subgroups. One is the Cayley
transform of the compact Cartan subgroup through a short non-compact imaginary
root. We call it H1 = T1A1. The other intermediate Cartan subgroup is the Cayley
transform of the compact Cartan subgroup through a long non-compact imaginary
root. We call it H2 = T2A2.
There are three K-conjugacy classes of regular characters of Hf, which we call γ0,
γ1 and γ2. There are three K-conjugacy classes of regular characters of H1, which
we call γ3, γ6 and γ7. There are three K-conjugacy classes of regular characters
of H2, which we call γ4, γ5 and γ8. There are four K-conjugacy classes of regular
characters of Hs, which we call γ9, γ10, γ11 and γ0′. γ0′ is contained in the block
G2(compact) and other regular characters are all contained in the block G2(split).
