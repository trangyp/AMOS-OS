Laboratoire de Math´ematiques, Universit´e Marie et Louis Pasteur , 25030 Besan¸con Cedex,

Abstract. We show that the range of a contractively decomposable projection on a non-
commutative Haagerup Lp-space, Lp(M, φ), for 1 < p < ∞, is completely isometrically
isomorphic to a corner of a noncommutative Lp-space, that is eLp(N, ψ)(1 −e), with e ∈N
a projection. In the setting of Schatten spaces, we obtain a more precise description: the
range of a contractively decomposable projection on Sp(K, H) is isometric to an ℓp direct
sum of subspaces of the form Sp(K′, H′). Furthermore, we show that contractively 1-pseudo
decomposable projections on Schatten spaces are automatically contractively decomposable,
establishing the equivalence between these two notions in this setting.
Keywords: Projections, complemented subspaces, Haagerup Lp-spaces, Schatten spaces,
decomposable maps.

1. Introduction
The study of contractive projections and 1-complemented subspaces of Banach spaces is
a major theme in the geometry of Banach spaces. Recall that a subspace Y of a space X is
1-complemented if it is the range of a linear contractive projection P : X →X. For more
information on this topic, we refer to the survey [23]. In classical (i.e. commutative) Lp-
spaces, a description of 1-complemented subspaces of Lp(Ω, F, µ), for (Ω, F, µ) a probability
space, is given by Douglas for p = 1, and by Ando for 1 < p ̸= 2 < ∞, in 1965 and 1966
respectively, see [1], and [11]. These results were generalized by Bernau and Lacey for an
Lp-space on a general measured space (see [8]). Note that in a smooth Banach space X,
if Y is a 1-complemented subspace of X, then there exists a unique contractive projection
P : X →X onto Y , see [10, Theorem 6]. Hence, in Lp-spaces, for 1 < p < ∞, the study of
contractive projections is equivalent to the study of 1-complemented subspaces.
In 1978 and in 1992, Arazy and Friedman described all 1-complemented subspaces of
Sp(K, H) in [2] and [3], for 1 ≤p ̸= 2 < ∞and for H, K some separable Hilbert spaces.
Recall that for H and K some Hilbert spaces, and for 1 ≤p < ∞, Sp(K, H) denotes the
Schatten space, that is the space of all bounded operators x : K →H such that Tr(|x|p) < ∞,
