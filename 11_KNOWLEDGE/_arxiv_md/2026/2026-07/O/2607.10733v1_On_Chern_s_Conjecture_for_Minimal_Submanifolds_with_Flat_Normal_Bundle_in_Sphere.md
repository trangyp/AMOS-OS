Abstract. Let M n (n ⩾3) be a closed minimal submanifold in the unit sphere
Sn+m (m ⩾2) with flat normal bundle, and let S denote the squared norm of its
second fundamental form. We prove an explicit second-gap rigidity theorem for S.
More precisely, if S is constant and
