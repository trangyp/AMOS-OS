1. Introduction
The first studies of low degree cohomology groups of the moduli
space of curves M g,n go back to the early nineties. A few years later
Arbarello and Cornalba developed an algebro-geometric approach in
their work [1], by exploiting the geometric description of the boundary
in terms of moduli spaces of curves with smaller invariant g.
This
method is nowadays known as “Arbarello and Cornalba’s induction”
and it still stands as a very effective tool to this kind of investigation.
For instance, their vanishing result in odd degree has been extended
from degree 5 up to degree 9 and their approach has been fruitfully
applied also in higher degree (see, e.g. [5, 9, 8]), by combining their
original ideas with new techniques.
A natural new direction to investigate is how to adapt their method
to other moduli spaces of curves: this has been done, for instance, in
degree ≤3 for the moduli space of spin curves in [6] and in degree 1
for the Hurwitz space with marked ramifications in [12].
In this note, we follow this line of thought and address the question
for the moduli space of twisted Prym curves. A smooth twisted prym
