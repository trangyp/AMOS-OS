ZYGMUND TYPE SPACES OF HARMONIC FUNCTIONS ON
THE REAL UNIT BALL
XI FU, ANTTI RASILA, AND XIAOQIANG XIE
Abstract. In this paper, we obtain several characterizations of harmonic Zyg-
mund type spaces on the real unit ball B of Rn. First, we characterize the harmonic
Zygmund space HZα (0 < α ≤2) in terms of Zygmund type conditions. Our re-
sult extends [3, Theorem 3.4] to the setting of the real unit ball B. Second, we
establish an analogue of the Holland-Walsh characterization of the harmonic Zyg-
mund space HZ. Finally, an integral characterization of HZα is also discussed.
All obtained results can be viewed as counterparts of the known results for Bloch
spaces.

1.2. Holland-Walsh criteria for harmonic function spaces. For µ, ν ≥0 and
f ∈C(B), if there exists a constant C such that
(1.7)
(1 −|x|2)µ(1 −|y|2)ν|f(x) −f(y)| ≤C|x −y|,
for all x, y ∈B, then we say that f satisfies a weighted Lipschitz condition of indices
(µ, ν) (cf. [27, 28]).
In 1986, a celebrated criterion of Holland and Walsh [12] states that an analytic
function f on D belongs to the Bloch space B if and only if it satisfies the weighted
Lipschitz condition with indices (1/2, 1/2), namely, (1.7) with µ = ν = 1/2. This
result can be regarded as a derivative-free characterization of Bloch spaces. Since
then, the question of obtaining derivative-free characterizations of Bloch, α-Bloch,
and Besov spaces of analytic and harmonic functions has attracted much attention.
In [29], Ren and Tu extended the Holland-Walsh characterization to the several
variables case on the unit ball of Cn. By adding the restriction “w belongs to a small
neighborhood of z”, Li and Wulan [17] characterized the α-Bloch space in terms of a
weighted Lipschitz condition of indices (β, α−β). See e.g. [19, 25, 27, 28, 30] for more
discussion on characterizations of Bloch, Besov and Zygmund spaces. This work also
continues our previous study [11] where problems of this type in the half-space case
were considered.
