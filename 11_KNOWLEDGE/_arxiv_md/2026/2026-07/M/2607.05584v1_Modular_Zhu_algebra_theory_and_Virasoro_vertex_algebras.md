Abstract. We develop the representation theory of vertex algebras over arbitrary rings
using higher Zhu algebras and mode transition algebras. Among our results, we give
several equivalent conditions for rationality of M¨obius vertex algebras over a field of
positive characteristic, generalizing the work of Damiolini, Gibney, and Krashen. As an
application of these results, we prove that, for any field F of characteristic 0 and coprime
integers r, s > 1, the discrete series Virasoro vertex operator algebra LVir(cr,s, 0)F is
rational. The proof involves using a certain integral form for LVir(cr,s, 0)F to calculate
the Zhu algebra over F. If char F = p > 2, then we show that for p = 3, 5 the simple
quotient LVir(c, 0)F is holomorphic for all c ∈Fp. We show the same result for p = 7
and c ∈Fp \ {3, 6}.
1. Introduction
Vertex (operator) algebras (VOAs) are often studied over C due to their many appli-
cations in geometric representation theory and modular tensor categories. However, it
is possible to study vertex algebras with C replaced by any commutative ring R and in
particular any field. These objects similarly possess a theory of intertwining operators
[DR14], and they likely have similar applications in the study of the birational geometry
of Mg,n [DGT23]. To these ends, we develop the representation theory of Z-graded ver-
tex algebras over R via the associated universal enveloping algebra, following the work
of [DGK25]. Many important constructions, such as the higher Zhu algebras Ad(V ) and
mode transition algebras A(V ), may be naturally defined using the universal enveloping
algebra. We show in Proposition 4.13 that the higher Zhu algebras may be equivalently
defined as a quotient of the vertex algebra V over any ring, extending the work of [He17].
Our first main result is a characterization of rationality for M¨obius vertex algebras (or
H-module vertex algebras, from [LM18]) over an arbitrary field:
Theorem 4.31. Let V be a M¨obius vertex algebra over a field R. Assume that:
• The Zhu algebra A is a finite product of matrix algebras over R (hence semisimple).
• Given any admissible V -module M with M0 ̸= 0, we have that Mn ̸= 0 for n ≫0.
Then the following statements are equivalent:
(a) For every simple A-module M, the induced Verma module ΦL(M) is simple and
quasi-rigid (has finite-dimensional graded parts).
(b) For every simple A-module M, the map ψ∨
M = ΦL(θM∨) →(ΦL(M))′ is an isomor-
phism.
(c) Ad is strongly unital for all d ∈N.
(d) Ad is semisimple for all d ∈N.
(e) V is rational.

1.1. Motivation. There are many natural reasons to study vertex algebras over fields F
of positive characteristic. For us, the primary motivation to study vertex algebras is the
construction of sheaves of coinvariants and conformal blocks on the moduli space Mg,n of
stable, n-pointed curves of genus g [DGT21], [DGT23], [DGK25]. The birational geometry
of Mg,n over an algebraically closed field F of positive characteristic can vary dramatically
compared to the case over C, so it is natural to ask if this difference is reflected in the
representation theory of VOAs in positive characteristic. Given a C2-cofinite and rational
VOA V , the sheaves of coinvariants associated to V give rise to finite rank vector bundles
on Mg,n. It is a major goal of ours to reproduce this result in positive characteristic. This
result also suggests that C2-cofinite, rational VOAs are incredibly valuable in the study of
Mg,n over C, so it is reasonable to assume the same is true over any algebraically closed
field. A related problem in this area is to give a geometric interpretation of intertwining
operators over positive characteristic fields from [DR14]. Intertwining operators may be
understood in terms of spaces of meromorphic functions on P1
C, but over any field we
cannot use analytic tools (unless the field was non-archimedean, for example). It is an
interesting problem to consider what the viable alternatives are in positive characteristic.
Aside from geometric considerations, there are genuine representation theoretic reasons
to consider VOAs over positive characteristic fields. Many standard examples of VOAs
seem to behave very differently over positive characteristic fields, such as the Heisenberg,
affine, and Virasoro VOAs [LM15], [JLM19], [LM23].
The simple quotients are often
holomorphic, and many of these admit interesting intermediate quotients that are C2-
cofinite but not rational. From a coinvariants perspective, these intermediate quotients are
also valuable because they give rise to coherent sheaves on Mg,n [DGT23]. Understanding
rational VOAs over C is a famously difficult problem, and it is reasonable to expect that
the problem is similarly interesting and complicated in positive characteristic. That said,
we are unaware of many examples of rational VOAs in positive characteristic. Another
interesting direction to consider is the relationship between VOAs and modular tensor
categories over C [Hua05].
One could use non-archimedean analogues of VOAs, like
those considered in [FM22], to give an analytic interpretation of intertwining operators
in positive characteristic.
The last remark we give about the problem of positive characteristic VOAs is on the
existence of rational VOAs. The only proposed example of a simple, rational VOA with
more than one distinct simple module (that we are aware of) is the Virasoro VOA L(1
2, 0)F
from [DR16]. As we show, their result as stated is incorrect in characteristics 3, 5, and 7.
This is because the simple quotient has a different structure than the one stated, and there
is only one simple module up to isomorphism. From our perspective, it is unclear if there
are any simple, rational VOAs in positive characteristic that have a simple module that
is not isomorphic to the adjoint module. At the very least, it appears that the structure
of such examples is heavily restricted or differs dramatically from those defined over C.
This alone is a problem worth examining.
