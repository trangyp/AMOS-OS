Abstract
Symmetric Poisson CGL extensions form a particular class of polynomial Poisson algebras
that are shown by K. Goodearl and M. Yakimov to admit compatible cluster structures. In
this paper, we give explicit formulas for a family of mutation matrices in the Goodearl-
Yakimov theory via matrix products as well as by entry-wise description.

Introduction and statements of main results
1.1
Introduction
Among the systematical examples of commutative and associative algebras admitting (meaning-
ful) cluster structures [FZ02] are a class of polynomial Poisson algebras studied by K. Goodearl
and M. Yakimov in [GY14, GY23].
Let k be a field of characteristic 0. A length n iterated Poisson-Ore extension (of k) is a
polynomial Poisson algebra (k[x1, . . . , xn], { , }) such that
{k[x1, . . . , xj−1], xj} ⊂xjk[x1, . . . , xj−1] + k[x1, . . . , xj−1],
∀2 ≤j ≤n.
(1.1)
Such an iterated Poisson-Ore extension is said to be symmetric if it also satisfies
{xj, k[xj+1, . . . , xn]} ⊂xjk[xj+1, . . . , xn] + k[xj+1, . . . , xn],
∀1 ≤j ≤n −1.
(1.2)
Given a split k-torus T, a T-Poisson CGL extension (reps. symmetric T-Poisson CGL exten-
sion), as defined in [GY14, GY23] and named after G. Cauchon, K. Goodearl, and E. Letzter,
is an iterated Poisson-Ore extension (resp. symmetric iterated Poisson-Ore extension) together
with a compatible T-action and certain nilpotency condition. See §1.2 for details.
In a remarkable theory developed in [GY14, GY23], K. Goodearl and M. Yakimov showed
that a presentation of a Poisson algebra (R, { , }) as a symmetric T-Poisson CGL extension gives

In the theory of cluster algebras, it is often desirable to have a good and easily workable
description of the mutation matrices, as each one of them completely determines the cluster
structure up to isomorphisms. For a symmetric T-Poisson CGL extension (R, { , }) of length n,
Goodearl and Yakimov constructed in [GY23] (again under some mild conditions on scalars and
Poisson CGL generators) a family of seeds (yτ, Mτ) in ΣGY, where τ lies in a certain subset Ξn
of the symmetric group Sn. The mutation matrix Mτ for each τ ∈Ξn is characterized in [GY23,
Theorem 11.1] as the unique solution to a certain system of linear equations, which we refer to
as GSV Equations (after M. Gekhtman, M. Shapiro, and A. Vainstein), that are defined by the
Poisson coefficient matrix of yτ and the T-characters of the elements in yτ. The existence and
uniqueness of Mτ are proved in [GY23, Theorem 11.1] by a rather involved induction procedure.
In this paper, for any length n symmetric Poisson CGL extension R and for each τ ∈Ξn, we
solve the GSV Equations on Mτ explicitly, and we do so by first proving some general results
on Poisson CGL extensions that are not necessarily symmetric.
More precisely, for an arbitrary T-Poisson CGL extension (R, { , }) of length n, not neces-
sarily symmetric, we use the sequence y = (y1, . . . , yn) of homogeneous Poisson prime elements
of R defined in [GY23] to formulate the GSV Equations on a matrix M (see (1.9)). Then
1) by some elementary linear algebra arguments, we show that a solution M to the GSV
Equations, if exists, must be given by a certain explicit matrix product, implying in particular
the uniqueness of such an M;
2) by a computation on the Poisson bracket { , }, we describe an explicit integer solution M
to the GSV Equations via the expansion of certain elements of R as Laurent polynomials of y;
3) under some mild conditions on scalars (to ensure that M is skew-symmetrizable) and
normality of R (Definition 3.5.1), we show that the upper cluster algebra defined by (y, M)
(with no frozen variables inverted) coincides with the polynomial ring R.
When the T-Poisson CGL R is symmetric, it is shown in [GY23] that the same Poisson
algebra (R, { , }) becomes a (in general no longer symmetric) T-Poisson CGL extension Rτ in
the coordinates (xτ(1), . . . , xτ(n)) for each τ ∈Ξn. Applying our aforementioned results on (the
not necessarily symmetric) T-Poisson CGL extension Rτ, we arrive at not only the existence
and uniqueness but also explicit formulas of Mτ as matrix products.
A classification of symmetric T-Poisson CGL extensions is recently given in [ML25] in terms
of their log-canonical terms and the second T-invariant Poisson cohomology of the log-canonical
terms. In particular, it is shown in [ML25] that there is a collection of non-positive integers,
called Cartan integers, associated to the log-canonical term of any symmetric T-Poisson CGL
extension R. As an application of our explicit formulas, we show that the non-zero entries of
Mτ, for every τ ∈Ξn, are either ±1 or ±a, where a is a negative Cartan integer associated to
the log-canonical term of R.

The results in this paper are in particular applicable to examples of symmetric Poisson
