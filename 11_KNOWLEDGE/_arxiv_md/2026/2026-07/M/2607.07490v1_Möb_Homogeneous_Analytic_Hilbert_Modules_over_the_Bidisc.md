M¨OB-HOMOGENEOUS ANALYTIC HILBERT MODULES OVER THE
BIDISC
JYOTIRMAY DAS AND SOMNATH HAZRA
Abstract. An analytic Hilbert module H over the polynomial ring, consisting of holomor-
phic functions over the bidisc, is said to be M¨ob-homogeneous if the corresponding pair of
multiplication operators is homogeneous with respect to the diagonal action of the group
{(φ, φ) : φ ∈M¨ob} ∼= M¨ob. In this article, we construct three families of mutually uni-
tarily inequivalent M¨ob-homogeneous analytic Hilbert modules, distinct from the family of
weighted Bergman modules over the bidisc. We further show that none of the reproducing
kernels in one of these families induces a K¨ahler–Einstein metric on the bidisc.
1. Introduction
Let D denote the open unit disc in the complex plane C and M¨ob be the group of all the
biholomorphic automorphisms of D. Suppose M¨ob × M¨ob is the direct product of two copies
of the group M¨ob. Every (φ1, φ2) ∈M¨ob × M¨ob gives rise to a biholomorphism of D2 via
the map
(z1, z2) →(φ1(z1), φ2(z2)), (z1, z2) ∈D2.
Note that the biholomorphic automorphism group Aut(D2) of D2 is the semi-direct product
of the group M¨ob × M¨ob and the permutation group S2 of two elements. The diagonal
subgroup {(φ, φ) : φ ∈M¨ob} of M¨ob × M¨ob is naturally isomorphic to M¨ob. Therefore,
every φ ∈M¨ob gives rise to a biholomorphic map ˜φ of D2 defined by
˜φ(z1, z2) = (φ(z1), φ(z2)), (z1, z2) ∈D2.
Throughout this article, given a φ ∈M¨ob, ˜φ represents the biholomorphic map on D2 defined
in the equation above.
