Abstract
We show that replacing the Preliminary Reference Earth Model (PREM) den-
sity profile with a single path-averaged value generates a joint degeneracy in the
(δCP , ∆CPT , θCPT ) parameter space indistinguishable from genuine new physics at
baselines L ≥5000 km. Unlike our prior work [1,2], which established individual chan-
nel biases separately, the present paper provides – for the first time – analytic proof
that these biases form an extended two-dimensional valley in the full three-parameter
space, a rigorous demonstration that multi-channel combination alone cannot resolve
this valley, and a Poisson log-likelihood χ2 analysis confirming that DUNE-like ex-
posures would report the artifact as a ±3σ BSM discovery.
At L = 7000 km the
constant-density approximation shifts the recovered δCP by 17.8◦and fabricates spuri-
ous CPT violation at ∆CPT = 4.1 × 10−4 eV2. Full spatially resolved PREM propaga-
tion is therefore a mathematical requirement, not an optional refinement, for DUNE,
Hyper-Kamiokande, and P2O.

Introduction
Pinning down the leptonic Dirac CP phase δCP and testing CPT invariance are two of the
most consequential goals in neutrino physics. Both rest on the same comparison—neutrino
versus antineutrino oscillation probabilities—and both are exposed to the same contaminant:
the MSW matter potential A(x) =
√
2GFNe(x) [4,5].
This potential flips sign between
neutrinos and antineutrinos, injecting an extrinsic CP-like asymmetry that can masquerade
as genuine δCP or, if the density profile used in the analysis is wrong, as fundamental CPT
violation.
Two earlier papers from our group examined separate pieces of this puzzle. Reference
[1] showed that replacing the realistic PREM profile with a single average density produces

Results and Discussion
Synthetic oscillation spectra are generated using the full PREM spatial propagator at the
physical truth values: δCP = −90◦, ∆CPT = 0, θCPT = 0. Fitting these exact spectra with a
single path-averaged density shortcut forces the minimizer to compensate for the localized
profile mismatch by systematically shifting the parameters along the degeneracy valley of
Eq. (15).

(∆δCP = 17.8◦, consistent with Eq. (14)) and a fabricated ∆CPT = 4.1 × 10−4 eV2. Table I

In conclusion, long-baseline neutrino experiments face a severe CP–CPT parameter de-
generacy originating from simplification of the Earth’s matter density profile.
We have
derived analytically, with fully evaluated partial derivatives (Eqs. 6–14), that this degener-
acy forms a two-dimensional valley in the full (δCP, ∆CPT, θCPT) parameter space; confirmed
it numerically across three baselines (Tables I–II); quantified its statistical severity using
a realistic Poisson log-likelihood χ2 framework (Section IV); and demonstrated that even a
5-shell intermediate approximation leaves a residual bias exceeding DUNE’s 1σ precision (Ta-
ble III). Fully spatially resolved PREM propagation is a strict mathematical requirement for
reliably separating intrinsic CP violation from CPT-violating new physics in next-generation
long-baseline experiments.
