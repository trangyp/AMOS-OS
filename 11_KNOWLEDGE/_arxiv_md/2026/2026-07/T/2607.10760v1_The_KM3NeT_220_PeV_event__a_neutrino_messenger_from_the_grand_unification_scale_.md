The KM3NeT 220 PeV event: a neutrino messenger from the grand unification scale?
Vernon Barger1
1Department of Physics, University of Wisconsin–Madison, Madison, WI 53706, USA
(Dated: July 12, 2026)
The 220 PeV neutrino KM3-230213A, in tension with IceCube and Auger limits unless it is a
narrow transient feature, admits a two-body reading: the decay X →ν¯ν of a relic with mX =
4.4 × 108 GeV. The flux fixes τ/f, lifetime over dark-matter fraction, and the lifetime fixes an
effective coupling Geff ∼10−46 GeV−2; for a flavored onium decaying through one electroweak-Higgs
insertion the mediator lands near 1016 GeV, so energy and rate together read out the grand-unified
scale. We develop the construction and its tests against the astrophysical alternative.
The KM3NeT/ARCA detection of the muon event
KM3-230213A, with reconstructed neutrino energy near
220 PeV and a 90% interval from 72 PeV to 2.6 EeV, is
the highest-energy neutrino yet reported [1]. Its interpre-
tation is set as much by non-detections as by the event.
IceCube reports no comparable event [2], the Pierre
Auger Observatory none in the EeV band [3–5], and
Baikal-GVD sets consistent multi-PeV constraints [6]; a
joint analysis finds a mild to moderate 1.6σ–2.9σ ten-
sion for a diffuse isotropic flux, mildest for a transient
point source [7], and a cosmogenic origin is disfavored by
the same EeV-band limits [8]. A narrow spectral feature
at the event energy removes the cross-energy constraints
(Fig. 1): with no power-law tail below it the IceCube
TeV–PeV limits never engage, and with nothing above
it the EeV-band limits from Auger and the in-ice radio
arrays [9] are untouched, as is the reach of the ongoing
array-wide radio search [10]. What no spectral shape re-
moves is the residual ∼2σ at the peak energy itself, an
exposure effect carried by transience or by rarity, not by
shape [7, 11].
Two mechanisms make a narrow feature, and they are
not equally narrow.
An astrophysical accelerator can
produce a resonant peak, but pion-decay kinematics floor
its width at a factor of 2–3. A line, narrow to detector res-
olution, has a particularly economical origin, two-body
decay X →ν¯ν of a relic with mX = 2Eν ≈4.4×108 GeV,
a reading with an active literature [12, 13]. This Letter
develops the line reading into a quantitative claim: un-
der minimal assumptions the event is a low-energy image
of grand-unified physics, with the neutrino energy mea-
suring the relic mass, the flux measuring the lifetime,
and the lifetime measuring the scale of the symmetry
breaking that lets the relic decay. We state the claim, its
assumptions, the astrophysical alternative it must beat,
and the tests that decide.
The messenger chain.
Three measured or inferred
numbers map onto three theory scales. (i) The energy
fixes the mass: mX = 2Eν ≈4.4×108 GeV, with the 90%
interval spanning 1.4×108 to 5×109 GeV; no astrophys-
ical parameter enters. (ii) The flux fixes the combination
f/τ, with f the fraction of the dark matter the relic con-
stitutes and τ its lifetime; explicit fits to the event yield
τ/f ≈1029–1030 s while respecting the IceCube, Auger,
and γ-ray bounds [12, 13].
(iii) The lifetime, through
the bound-state decay rate below, fixes an effective four-
fermion coupling Geff, and the operator that generates
Geff converts it into a mediator mass once the symmetry-
breaking insertions are identified. The chain terminates,
in its minimal form, near 1016 GeV. The flux alone leaves
f and τ degenerate; the degeneracy breaks observation-
ally, since the rate normalizes f/τ while the sky gradient
and the redshift structure of the line (below) are shape
observables independent of it, and it breaks theoretically
once a production mechanism fixes f, after which τ is a
measurement.
Why an onium. Spin statistics select the state. A two-
fermion final state ν¯ν requires a bosonic parent, so the
relic is a bound state of superheavy fermions, the flavored
1S0 ground state of a ¯Q′Q system, rather than a single
fermion, whose two-body channel would be νh or νZ and
whose line would sit differently in every flavor observ-
able. A same-flavor onium annihilates promptly through
its own gauge interactions (Γ ∼α5
effmX) and cannot sur-
vive cosmologically; the relic must be the flavored combi-
nation, stable under an accidental flavor symmetry of the
dark sector and decaying only through the operator that
breaks it. Stability places a sharp requirement on that
symmetry. Quantum gravity is expected to violate global
symmetries, and a Planck-suppressed dimension-6 oper-

The unification decade.
The landing point matters,
because the 1016 GeV decade is not unoccupied. It is
where the gauge couplings of the minimal supersymmet-
ric extension meet, and where proton-decay bounds re-
quire the heavy states of a unified theory to live; among
scales above 1015 GeV it is the one decade with an in-
dependent reason to be populated, with the seesaw win-
dow of the two-insertion reading its counterpart below.
A dimension-7 coupling generated by integrating out a
field of mass M therefore has a natural host in the uni-
fied sector, a heavy singlet or triplet exchanged between
the dark bilinear and ¯L ˜HνR, and the singlet constituents

hence the meeting point itself, untouched at one loop.

operator opens on a GUT-scale Standard-Model-singlet

Production, binding, survival. The required abundance
is minute, which makes production easy. For the bench-
mark lifetimes, ΩXh2 = 0.12 f corresponds to a comov-
ing yield nX/s ≈10−18f. Freeze-in from the reheating
bath reaches this for a reheating temperature a factor of
20–30 below the constituent mass mQ ≈mX/2, where
the pair-production yield scales as e−2mQ/TRH and is di-
aled by TRH [23]; gravitational particle production sup-
plies a floor and can itself reach the required yield for
suitable inflationary scales at mQ ∼108 GeV [24]. The
estimate assumes instantaneous reheating; a prolonged
reheating era shifts the relevant temperature toward the
maximum attained and moves the required hierarchy by
an O(1) factor in the exponent [23]. Keeping TRH below
mQ also avoids a thermalized heavy-quark population,
which at mQ ≈2 × 108 GeV would violate the unitarity
bound on thermal relics [25]. Binding follows if the con-
stituents confine under a dark color group with confine-
ment scale well below the Bohr momentum αeff mX/4: at
the dark phase transition every surviving quark is swept
into a hadron, same-flavor onia annihilate promptly, and
at 10−18 per unit entropy the flavored onia are colli-
sionless thereafter, so rearrangement annihilation never
engages.
The binding and survival fractions are O(1)
model-dependent factors absorbed into f. Dark confine-
ment also disarms the constraint that kills the colored
version: constituents with Standard Model color would
leave free-quark relics bound into anomalously heavy iso-
topes, excluded far below any plausible leftover fraction,
so the constituents are singlets, vector-like under the dark
group.
Line shape and flavor at Earth. The spike at mX/2 is
broadened only by detector resolution and by the elec-
troweak final-state radiation that necessarily dresses a
ν¯ν final state at mX ∼4 × 108 GeV, a soft contin-
uum treated in the relic literature and constrained by
γ-ray observations [12, 13]. The full spectral shape car-
ries a signature of its own: the Galactic component is a
sharp edge at mX/2, while the extragalactic component
of the same decay arrives redshifted, filling a tail below
the edge with weight concentrated within a factor of ∼2
(z ≲1); the predicted feature is an edge with a redshifted
shoulder, not a symmetric bump (Fig. 2, End Matter),
and the shoulder is far too short to engage the IceCube
TeV–PeV limits. Flavor is observed after propagation:
over the halo and extragalactic baselines the oscillation
phases fully decohere, matter effects are negligible along
halo and intergalactic paths even at 220 PeV, and de-
cohered vacuum mixing maps any flavor structure of the
decay operator onto an Earth flux with every flavor above
≈15%; a nonzero ντ component at 220 PeV is therefore
guaranteed. For a charge-symmetric X– ¯X relic popula-
tion the line also delivers neutrinos and antineutrinos in
equal measure, unlike the ¯νe-poor photohadronic alter-
native below, though at these energies the ν/¯ν discrimi-
nation is statistical at best.
Sky morphology and rate.
The source is the dark-
matter halo, so the flux traces the line-of-sight column,
diffuse with no counterpart, no time clustering, and no
preferred distance.
Because decay integrates the den-
sity rather than its square, the anisotropy is gentle; the
Galactic-center-to-anticenter ratio is a factor of a few
for standard halo profiles, the extragalactic component
is isotropic, so the morphology test needs several events.
The event direction (RA 94.3◦, Dec −7.8◦) lies toward
the Galactic anticenter, near the halo minimum, where
the column falls below the all-sky average by a factor
of order two for standard profiles; this is a mild prior
against the halo origin for this particular event, which
only statistics can overturn. The same f/τ that yields
one event in the partial-array exposure predicts a rate
scaling directly with the completed ARCA exposure and
with IceCube-Gen2, with arrival directions accumulating
toward the Galactic center. The prediction is bounded
rather than open-ended: IceCube’s larger integrated ex-
posure has not accumulated events at these energies, so
the line hypothesis requires the KM3NeT detection to be
an upward fluctuation at the level quantified by the joint-
exposure analysis [7], which also caps the rate expected
at the completed arrays; the accompanying electroweak-
radiation photons populate the γ-ray sky at a level al-
ready probed by the existing bounds [12, 13].
The astrophysical alternative.
The reading the line
must beat is a resonant peak from a flaring accelera-
tor, and it is a serious competitor because candidate
counterparts exist. A quasi-monoenergetic proton beam
from electrostatic gap acceleration in a black-hole mag-
netosphere [26–28], at Ep ≈20 Eν ≈4.4 EeV for fidu-
cial parameters [29], interacting at the ∆(1232) reso-
nance [30, 31] with the thermal infrared field of warm
dust, produces a peak whose energy measures the target
through ε0Ep ≈0.16 GeV2, selecting dust at T ≈150–
300 K; an accretion-flare interpretation with a dust-echo
target has been developed for the candidate MRC 0614-
083 [32], and the counterpart study finds a radio flare of
PMN J0606-0724 coincident with the arrival time (pre-
trial 0.26%) and a γ-ray flare of PKS 0605-085 peak-
ing before it [33, 34].

Relation to other new-physics readings.
The event
has drawn a range of beyond-Standard-Model interpre-
tations, from modified neutrino propagation and dark-
matter-induced effects along the flight path [36] to gen-
eral assessments of what new physics the single event
can support [37]. Most such readings modify how a neu-
trino of astrophysical origin travels or interacts; the relic
line is different in kind, because it replaces the source,
and that is what makes it spectroscopic: propagation
effects inherit the broad spectrum of whatever source
they start from, while two-body decay manufactures a
feature whose width, position, flavor content, and sky
distribution are Lagrangian parameters. The line read-
ing is accordingly the most falsifiable of the family. The
EeV-scale ANITA anomalies [38–40], which have drawn
subsurface-reflection and other instrumental readings [41]
alongside new-physics ones, lie well above mX/2 and are
unconnected to the line, whose flux ends at 220 PeV.
Spectroscopy of the dark sector. If events accumulate
at the line, the feature becomes a laboratory. The edge
position measures mX to detector resolution; the normal-
ization measures f/τ; the ratio of the resolution-narrow
edge to the redshifted shoulder measures the Galactic-
to-extragalactic split and hence the halo column toward
each event; and the flavor ratios measure the chirality
structure of the decay operator, since averaged mixing
maps distinct structures onto distinguishable Earth com-
positions, e.g., (1 : 0 : 0)S →(0.55 : 0.17 : 0.28)⊕against
(0 : 1 : 0)S →(0.17 : 0.45 : 0.38)⊕(End Matter). Galac-
tic and extragalactic columns are comparable for stan-
dard profiles, so edge and shoulder share events roughly
evenly, and the split is measured to ∼N −1/2: ten line
events give a first decomposition, a few tens begin to con-
strain the Galactic-to-extragalactic split quantitatively,
and of order one hundred approach ten-percent statisti-
cal precision. A line is richer than a peak: each of its
properties is a Lagrangian parameter, not a source envi-
ronment.
What a unified completion must deliver. The construc-
tion is a checklist rather than a model: (i) a vector-
like pair Q, Q′ of Standard Model singlets charged un-
der a dark color group confining below the Bohr mo-
mentum; (ii) a gauged discrete flavor symmetry distin-
guishing them, forbidding the dimension-6 operator; (iii)
a mediator near 1016 GeV coupling the dark bilinear
to ¯L ˜HνR, or the double-insertion Weinberg analogue
at the seesaw scale; (iv) a reheating temperature near
mQ/20 ≈107 GeV. Each ingredient is standard on its
own; embedding the checklist in a specific unified group
is follow-up work on which the Letter’s claim does not
depend.
Tests. The reading is falsifiable on a short horizon. (i)
Morphology. Subsequent events at 100–300 PeV tracing
the halo column, with a soft gradient toward the Galac-
tic center and no repeating position, support the line;
clustering at RA 94.3◦, Dec −7.8◦, or a second neutrino
coincident with a flare of PMN J0606-0724 or PKS 0605-
085, decides for the astrophysical peak. (ii) Width. With
several events, a spread consistent with detector resolu-
tion supports the line, with the redshifted shoulder sup-
plying an internal consistency check once a few events
exist; a factor 2–3 spread supports the peak. Any con-
firmed event at, say, 10 EeV falsifies both single-feature
hypotheses. (iii) Timing. An IceCube time-dependent
search at the event position restricted to a ≲2 yr win-
dow around February 2023 tests the transient alterna-
tive directly [11]; a null result there shifts weight to-
ward the time-independent line. (iv) Companions. The
electroweak-radiation photon shoulder is a diffuse γ-ray
prediction at a level adjacent to current bounds [12, 13],
and a nonzero ντ flux component at 220 PeV is guaran-
teed by the averaged mixing. (v) Theory-side. The con-
struction requires a gauged discrete flavor symmetry and
TRH ≈mQ/20; neither is directly observable, but both
are falsifiable within a specified unified model, which is

A single event cannot carry a discovery, and this Letter
does not claim one. It claims a chain: if the 220 PeV fea-
ture is a line, it is two-body relic decay; if it is two-body
relic decay of a flavored onium protected by a gauged
symmetry, the lifetime measures Geff ∼10−46 GeV−2
up to an order-unity rate normalization; and if the
symmetry-breaking insertion is the electroweak Higgs,
the mediator sits near 1016 GeV, with the two-insertion
alternative landing at the seesaw scale instead. Each link
is stated with its assumption, each assumption has an
observable consequence, and the first discriminants, mor-
phology and width, arrive with the completed ARCA ar-
ray. If they break toward the line, the most energetic
neutrino ever detected is a messenger from the grand-
unified scale; if they break toward the peak, the same
event is a probe of black-hole gap acceleration on warm
dust. Either resolution repays the exposure.
