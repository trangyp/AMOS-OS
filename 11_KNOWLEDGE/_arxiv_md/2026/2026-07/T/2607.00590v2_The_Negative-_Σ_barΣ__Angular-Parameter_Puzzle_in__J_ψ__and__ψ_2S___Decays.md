Chao-Qiang Geng Xiang-Nan Jin Chia-Wei Liu
School of Fundamental Physics and Mathematical Sciences, Hangzhou Institute for Advanced

E-mail: cqgeng@ucas.ac.cn, xnjin@ucas.ac.cn, chiaweiliu@ucas.ac.cn
Abstract: Experimental measurements of J/ψ and ψ(2S) decays to octet baryon pairs
reveal a negative-Σ¯Σ puzzle in the angular distributions: αJ/ψ
Σ
< 0, whereas the other
measured J/ψ octet channels and the corresponding ψ(2S) channels have positive angular
parameters. We show that an SU(3)F -exact singlet description is strongly disfavored in a
χ2 fit, giving χ2 ≃105. By contrast, a reduced scenario in which a common final-state
interaction mixes the S- and D-wave amplitudes, with an antisymmetric direct octet and a
full diagonal S–D rescattering block, gives χ2 = 4.21 for two nominal degrees of freedom.
The negative Σ¯Σ angular parameter is traced to the pattern of the rescattered S- and
D-wave amplitudes.The apparent large SU(3)F -breaking effect is decomposed into three
ingredients: the fitted D-wave source is much larger than the S-wave source, the breaking
remains modest when normalized to the D wave, and D →S rescattering transfers the
SU(3)F -breaking effect from the D wave into the small S-wave baseline, where it appears
large. The largest remaining pull comes from the ψ(2S) →N ¯N angular input, motivating
a renewed experimental check of ψ(2S) →n¯n.
Keywords: Charmonium, flavor symmetry, final-state interactions, baryon-antibaryon
production

Introduction
The experimental angular distributions for baryonic J/ψ and ψ(2S) decays show an anoma-
lous sign pattern in the Σ¯Σ channel. Here and below N = p, n, Σ = Σ+,0,−, and Ξ = Ξ0,−.
In J/ψ decays the data find αJ/ψ
Σ
< 0, whereas the measured N ¯N, Λ¯Λ, and Ξ¯Ξ chan-
nels, as well as the baryonic decays of ψ(2S), have positive angular parameters [1–12].
The relevant data are collected in Table 1. The phase measurements sharpen this obser-
vation.
In J/ψ decay, ∆ΦΣ is negative, whereas the measured Λ¯Λ and Ξ¯Ξ phases are
positive. Thus the Σ¯Σ channel is singled out both by the sign of αJ/ψ
Σ
and by a distinct
strong-phase pattern.
This makes final-state interaction (FSI) a natural place to look:
it can modify phases after the short-distance production step and thereby turn a mod-
erate SU(3)F -breaking source into a larger channel-dependent effect. A useful precedent
is provided by two-body hadronic D-meson decays, where long-distance rescattering and
exchange topologies are known to be important and can generate sizable effective SU(3)F
breaking [13–15]. We therefore ask whether a common baryon–antibaryon FSI, combined
with the SU(3)F -breaking production, can account for the data. The left panel of Fig. 1
shows the corresponding short-distance three-gluon production topology, while the right
panel illustrates, for instance, the rescattering of N ¯N to Σ¯Σ.

We have studied the negative-Σ¯Σ puzzle in J/ψ, ψ(2S) →BB angular distributions. The

In a singlet plus octet basis with a
common FSI, the SU(3)F -exact singlet description is strongly disfavored, giving χ2 ≃105.

direct octet and a full diagonal kSD block, gives χ2 = 4.21 for two nominal degrees of
freedom. The fit values and predictions are shown in Tables 2 and 3. The negative Σ¯Σ
angular parameter is traced to the different rescattered S- and D-wave pattern in J/ψ →
Σ¯Σ.
