within the dihadron fragmentation framework

Abstract
We calculate energy-energy correlations for two hadrons produced inside a jet in transversely polarized proton-proton
collisions. We make numerical predictions based on a simple model that utilizes a previous global QCD analysis of
dihadron fragmentation and transversity parton distribution functions. The results show remarkable agreement with
a very recent STAR measurement. We also find the data at large jet transverse momentum have a slight preference
for extractions of transversity that are consistent with lattice QCD computations of the nucleon tensor charges. Over-
all, this work provides further evidence for the underlying non-perturbative mechanism of near-side energy-energy
correlators as well as highlights the potential for these observables to probe transverse-spin effects inside the nucleon.
1. Introduction
Transverse single-spin asymmetries (TSSAs) and energy-energy correlators (ECCs) represent two long-standing
phenomena in the study of perturbative QCD and hadronic structure, both dating back to the 1970s [1–4]. The former
allow one to explore 3-dimensional imaging of hadrons and multi-parton correlations inside of them (see the review
in Ref. [5]), while the latter provide a platform to investigate asymptotic freedom and confinement in QCD (see the
review in Ref. [6]). Over the last few years, theoretical connections have been made between these two areas [7–
13], with a very recent measurement from the STAR Collaboration at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC)
producing the first experimental data in this domain [14]. STAR measured both one-point (OPEC) and two-point
(EEC) energy correlators inside a jet in high-energy collisions of transversely polarized and unpolarized protons, i.e.,
p↑p →(jet, h) X and p↑p →(jet, h1h2) X, respectively. Both observables are sensitive to the transversity parton
distribution function (PDF) [15]. This function has received particular attention since it can be used to compute the
up and down quark tensor charges, δu and δd, of the nucleon, which bridge different areas of nuclear physics, like
low-energy beyond the Standard Model (BSM) studies (see, e.g., Refs. [16–24]), lattice QCD (LQCD) [25–40], and
model calculations [41–53].
The theoretical framework for the OPEC in p↑p →(jet, h) X was established in Ref. [10] and predictions were
provided in the STAR paper to compare with their experimental data. However, the same was not available for the
EEC in p↑p →(jet, h1h2) X. In Ref. [13], which had been finalized just as the STAR paper [14] became available, we
developed the theoretical underpinnings, based on dihadron fragmentation [54–60], for EEC-type TSSA observables
in semi-inclusive deep-inelastic scattering (SIDIS) and e+e−annihilation for the case where the angle χ between
the two hadrons ≈0 (near side). In this Letter, we now extend our work in Ref. [13] to p↑p →(jet, h1h2) X and
generate predictions to compare with the STAR data. Within a simple model that utilizes a previous global QCD
analysis [61, 62] of dihadron fragmentation functions (DiFFs) and transversity PDFs, we find remarkable agreement
with the STAR data.

Overall, our analysis can account for the dominant features of the STAR proton-proton measurement, which is
nontrivial because the PJT and χ dependencies are predicted simultaneously with no refit to the STAR data. This
indicates that dihadron fragmentation can account for the underlying mechanism of near-side EECs, and the same
transverse-spin effects studied previously (e.g., the transversity PDF) using “standard” TSSA observables can also be
probed using EECs.
4. Summary and Outlook
We have studied near-side EECs in transversely polarized proton-proton collisions. In particular, we considered
the process p↑p →(jet, h1h2) X, where the EEC is measured between two hadrons inside the identified jet. Working
within the dihadron fragmentation framework, we derived the corresponding factorized expression and made numer-
ical predictions using a simple model for the relevant EEC-DiFFs. Utilizing input from the JAMDiFF QCD global
analysis [61, 62], we find remarkable agreement with the very recent STAR measurement of this observable [14]. In
addition, the STAR data shows a preference for the predictions obtained with transversity PDFs that are consistent
with computations of the nucleon tensor charges from lattice QCD. This further confirms dihadron fragmentation as
the underlying non-perturbative mechanism of near-side EECs and highlights the potential for these observables to
probe transverse-spin effects inside the nucleon. We look forward to future measurements/re-analyses of SIDIS and
e+e−annihilation data on EEC TSSAs that can be combined together in a simultaneous analysis with the proton-proton
data from STAR [14] to offer a simplified extraction of the transversity PDFs [13].
