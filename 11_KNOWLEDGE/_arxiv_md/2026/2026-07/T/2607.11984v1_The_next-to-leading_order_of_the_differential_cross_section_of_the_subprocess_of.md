ABSTRACT 
Next-to-the leading order of the differential cross-section of the subprocess of 
annihilation of quark-antiquark pair   ̄     of prompt photon production in 
nonpolarized and longitudinally polarized proton-proton collisions at NICA 
energies has been investigated. 
Shown, that contribution of the next-to-leading order to the differential cross-
section of annihilation of quark-antiquark pair   ̄     is significant at the high 
energies of colliding protons and consist around 15% of leading order calculation.  
The influence of longitudinal polarization of colliding protons on the 
contributions of the next-to-leading order is more significant than, on the 
contributions of the leading order and it is significant at high energies of colliding 
protons.  
PYTHIA 8.316 + POWHEG BOX Monte Carlo (MC) event generator has

of quark-antiquark pair in NLO approximation for determination of the 
dependences of cross section on the sum of energies of colliding protons √ , 
transverse momentum   , cosine of scattering angle    ( ), rapidity y of photon 
and   . The NLO+PS simulation accurately reproduces the fundamental signatures 
of pQCD.

take into account the effects of one-loop virtual diagrams and real production of 
additional particles [6-12].
