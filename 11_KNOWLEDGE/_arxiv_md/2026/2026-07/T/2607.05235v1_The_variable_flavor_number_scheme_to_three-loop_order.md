We describe the variable flavor number scheme to three-loop order, which modifies the massless
parton densities by single- and two-mass effects and introduces heavy-quark parton distribution
functions for charm and bottom. A renormalization group analysis shows the validity of this
picture at large scales 𝑄2, where it resembles the non-power-suppressed heavy-flavor corrections
completely. We also provide numerical implementations of a series of charged and neutral current
Wilson coefficients.

1.
Introduction
The variable flavor number schemes (VFNS) account for the universal heavy-flavor contributions
of the deep-inelastic (DIS) structure functions in the form of modified parton distribution functions.
They are important for the matching at flavor thresholds. In the region of large virtualities 𝑄2 ≫𝑚2
𝑄,
where 𝑚𝑄denotes the heavy-quark mass, full account is given of all heavy-quark corrections,
neglecting power corrections of 𝑂((𝑚2/𝑄2)𝑘), 𝑘≥1. This is implemented by the renormalization
group equation [1, 2]. Since the ratio of the charm and bottom quark masses is not small, one
may consider the decoupling of two quarks at the same time. The single-mass VFNS has been
introduced in Ref. [3] to two-loop order and two-loop two-mass effects were described in Refs. [4, 5].
The single-mass VFNS at three-loop order has been presented in Ref. [6, 7] in the unpolarized and
polarized cases. The structure of the two-mass VFNS to three-loop orders was described in Ref. [8].
Using the VFNS, massless Wilson coefficients are paired with massive operator matrix elements
(OMEs). This does not allow the description of power corrections arising in observables, which
will become important in the kinematic region of virtualities 𝑄2 below a certain scale 𝑄2
0. For the
structure function 𝐹2(𝑥, 𝑄2) one has 𝑄2
0 = 10 𝑚2
𝑄, cf. [9].
Some modifications of the single-mass VFNS have been discussed in the literature in Refs. [10–
14], to second order. Also these approaches have to be shown to obey the renormalization group
equation to construct observables using the respective modified parton distribution functions.
This paper is organized as follows. Basic relations of the VFNS are discussed in Section 2.
In Section 3 the heavy-quark corrections to structure functions at large virtualities are described.
Quantitative results on the single-mass heavy-quark effects on parton distribution functions are
described in Section 4. The two-mass operator matrix elements are illustrated in Section 5 and
Section 6 contains the conclusions.

and polarized cases. They consist of factorizable and, from three-loop order, also non-factorizable
contributions. The emergence of two masses complicates the respective integrals and functional
representation of the final results.4 The respective contributions at three-loop order have color
factors of 𝑇2
