TILES AND WEAK TILES IN Zpq
MAMATELI KADIR AND KAIBO FAN
Abstract. This paper investigates the relationship between tiles and weak tiles
in the context of finite cyclic group Zpq. We prove that weak tiles and translational
tiles are equivalent in this group. Our proof employs Fourier analysis, Delsarte
parameters, and the Coven-Meyerowitz conditions.
1. Introduction
1.1. Translational tiling and spectral set. The study of tiling, the covering of
space by congruent copies of a given shape, stretches back to the dawn of civilization.

set of ”shapes” called prototiles and using congruent copies of these prototiles to
cover the whole Euclidean space without overlapping. Translational tiling is one
such example, in which only the translations of the prototiles are used to tile the
space.

This conjecture has spurred extensive research, revealing deep connections to

While progress was made in special cases, including convex planar domains [5],

1.2. Weak tiling and pd-tiling. A pivotal concept in the proof for convex bodies
[12] was that of weak tiling, which relaxes the stringent conditions of classical
translational tiling. A set A ⊆Rd weakly tiles another measurable set B ⊆Rd,
if there exists a positive, locally finite measure ν on Rd such that ν({0}) = 1 and
1A ∗ν = 1B a.e.. This analytic formulation provided the crucial bridge to connect
spectrality to a geometric property.
Subsequently, Kiss, Matolcsi, Matolcsi, and Somlai [6] adapted this notion to
finite abelian groups G.
Definition 1.1. A set A weakly tiles G, if there exists a nonnegative function
f : G →R+ such that f(0) = 1 and 1A ∗f = 1G.

concept of a positive-definite weak tile (or pd-tile), where the function f is
additionally required to be positive definite and normalized f(0) = 1.

3.3. Open problems. We have proved that for distinct primes p, q, every positive-

(1) Can the equivalence of pd tiling and tiling be extended to more general finite

and the C-M conditions, thereby completely characterizing when a pd-tile
is a tile?
