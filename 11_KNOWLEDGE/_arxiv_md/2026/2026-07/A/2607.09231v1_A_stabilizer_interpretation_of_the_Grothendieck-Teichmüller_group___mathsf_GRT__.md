A STABILIZER INTERPRETATION OF THE
GROTHENDIECK-TEICHM ¨ULLER GROUP GRT1(k)
BENJAMIN ENRIQUEZ AND HIDEKAZU FURUSHO
Abstract. If u and v are Lie algebras, then the product Out(u) × Out(v) of their outer au-
tomorphism groups naturally acts on the set of outer Lie algebra morphisms from u to v; the
stabilizer of the outer class of a given such morphism is then a subgroup of Out(u) × Out(v).
We show that this leads to two related interpretation of the Grothendieck-Teichm¨uller group
GRT1(k), where u, v are the Lie algebra of infinitesimal braids on the plane (resp. framed infin-
itesimal braids on the sphere) with 3 and 4 (resp. 4 and 5) strands: namely, it can be expressed
as the joint intersection of the stabilizer groups of the outer classes of certain strand doubling
morphisms ϕ and ψ with Out∗(u) × Out(v), where Out∗(u) is a subgroup of Out(u) of outer
classes of inertia-preserving automorphisms of u.

0.1. Introduction. In [D], Drinfeld introduced the related notions of associators and the Gro-
thendieck-Teichm¨uller (GT) Q-group scheme. These notions were later used in many areas of
mathematics (see a survey in the Introduction of [W]).
The GT group functor takes a commutative Q-algebra with unit k to GRT1(k), defined in
[D] as an automorphism group of the collection of all symmetric monoidal categories over k
which are infinitesimally braided (i.e. equipped with an infinitesimal braiding). As GRT1 is a
pro-unipotent group scheme, it can uniquely be recovered from its Lie algebra grt1. This Lie
algebra is presented in [D] by an explicit system of linear conditions, called 2-cycle, hexagon and
pentagon relations.

Equivalent linear systems for the definition of grt1 have been given in several works. In [F1],
grt1 was shown to be equal to the a priori larger set of solutions of the pentagon relation. In
[FHK], it was shown to be equal to an a priori even larger set of solutions of a linear system.
On a different note, an interpretation of grt1 in terms of confluence relations was obtained in
[HS, F2].
Identifications of grt1 with explicit Lie algebras of derivations were also proposed. In [BN], grt1
is interpreted as the Lie algebra of derivations of a ‘universal’ infinitesimally braided symmetric
monoidal category, denoted PaCD (parenthesized chord diagrams), compatible with the cabling
operations of this category. The underlying operadic structure was later clarified in [Fr], which
enabled the identification of GRT1(k) with an operadic automorphism group.
An interpretation of grt1 in terms of infinitesimal braid Lie algebras was obtained in [I2].
There, a form over Z of this Lie algebra is identified with the ‘stable derivation algebra’ of the
tower of infinitesimal braid Lie algebras on the sphere (Pn)n≥3. For n ≥3, Pn = grπ1Cfn(S2),
where gr is the Lie algebra associated with the lower central series filtration of a group, and
Cfn(X) is the configuration space of n distinct points on a space X; it contains elements Xij
where i ̸= j ∈[1, n] which correspond to the winding of braids i and j. One defines der∗(Pn)
as the set of all derivations of Pn which take each Xij to an element of [Xij, Pn]; this is a Lie
subalgebra of der(Pn), which also contains the Lie subalgebra inn(Pn) of inner derivations as an
ideal, and one defines out∗(Pn) as the quotient der∗(Pn)/inn(Pn), and finally Dn := out∗(Pn)Sn,
the action being induced by that of the symmetric group Sn on Pn by permutation of indices.
There is a natural map Dn+1 →Dn, which is constructed as follows. Let i ∈[1, n + 1].
The erasing of strand i induces a Lie algebra morphism pri : Pn+1 →Pn.
There is a Lie
algebra morphism pi : der∗(Pn+1) →der∗(Pn), such that pri(D(x)) = pi(D)(pri(x)) for any
x ∈Pn+1, D ∈der∗(Pn+1). The morphism pi induces a morphism pi : out∗(Pn+1) →out∗(Pn).
The restriction of pi to Dn+1 is independent of i and its image is contained in Dn; this is the
map Dn+1 →Dn. The results of [I2] can then be summarized as follows:
Theorem 1. (see [I2]) The map Dn+1 →Dn is an isomorphism if n ≥5 and is injective if
n = 4; moreover, there is an isomorphism D5 ⊗Q ≃grt1.
From the geometric viewpoint, the key role in this result is therefore played by the tower of
Lie algebras (Pn)n≥4 together with the compatible actions of the symmetric groups, and with
the ‘erasing of strands’ morphisms pri : Pn+1 →Pn, which is part of the operadic structure
involved in [Fr].
The purpose of the present work is to exhibit two related alternative interpretations of the Lie
algebra grt1 in terms of infinitesimal braid Lie algebras. One of these interpretations is based on
particular morphisms of doubling of strands from the tower of Lie algebra (tn)n≥0, which turns
out to be also part of the operadic structure involved in [Fr].
