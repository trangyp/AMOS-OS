KATE PONTO
INNA ZAKHAREVICH
ABSTRACT. This paper gives some technical results about cofibrant symmetric spectra of simplicial sets,
and uses them to produce a model of the K -theory of a Waldhausen category that lands in the subcategory
of (projective) cofibrant spectra.
INTRODUCTION
When working with algebraic K -theory there is often a tension of approaches. On one hand, working
with ∞-categories and abstract approaches produces satisfying and beautiful results, such as Blumberg–
Gepner–Tabuada’s result about the universal property of K -theory [BGT13]. In this approach it is not im-
portant to construct models, and this often strengthens and streamlines results. Unfortunately, some-
times (such as in cases where there is not a stable ∞-categorical structure, such as combinatorial K -
theory [Zak17, BGM+24]) it is necessary to work directly with explicit constructions of K -theory, such
as Waldhausen’s S•-construction. Unfortunately, with such direct constructions we often run into prob-
lems: we must work with model structures, and thus we often need to know that the spectra that we are
working with are cofibrant.
Waldhausen’s S•-construction naturally produces a symmetric spectrum of simplicial sets. Symmetric
spectra of simplicial sets have three main model structures on them: the level model structure, the flat
model structure, and the projective model structure. These have decreasing collections of cofibrations,
with the projective model structure being the one most useful for many applications. It is thus desirable
to know when the S•-construction produces projectively cofibrant spectra.
Theorem A (Proposition 2.4, Corollary 2.5). For a Waldhausen category C , the S•-construction produces
