We investigate the Standard Model (SM) description of the singly Cabibbo-suppressed charm
decays D0 →π−π+, D0 →K−K+ and D0 →K0
SK0
S.
Using factorisation together with
isospin symmetry, we constrain non-factorisable effects and the associated U-spin breaking
required by the measured branching fractions.
We find that corrections of order 50% are
sufficient to describe all modes, which although sizeable, are not unexpected for hadronic
charm decays. Using the constraints from the branching fractions, we derive SM predictions
for the direct CP asymmetry in D0 →K0
SK0
S, finding it to be at most at the per-mille level
in our benchmark scenario, providing motivation for future precision measurements.

Introduction
Charm decays provide a unique probe of the interplay between weak and strong interactions in
the SM. However, obtaining robust theoretical predictions remains challenging 1.
Among hadronic charm decays, the singly Cabibbo-suppressed modes D0 →π−π+, D0 →
K−K+ and D0 →K0
SK0
S are of particular interest. Although they proceed through the same
underlying quark-level transitions, they probe different hadronic dynamics and U-spin-breaking
effects. The D0 →π−π+ and D0 →K−K+ modes are related by U-spin symmetry and admit
a factorisation description, as they receive contributions from colour-allowed tree amplitudes.
Their branching fractions are precisely measured and exhibit sizeable U-spin breaking2. By con-
trast, D0 →K0
SK0
S proceeds only via non-factorisable contributions, dominated by the exchange
topology, and essentially vanishes in the U-spin limit. Nevertheless, its branching fraction is only
about an order of magnitude smaller 2 than those of D0 →π−π+ and D0 →K−K+. These
complementary dynamics are also reflected in the pattern of direct CP violation.
Experimentally, CP violation in charm has been established at the per-mille level by the
LHCb collaboration through the measurement of the difference of CP asymmetries, ∆ACP,
between D0 →K−K+ and D0 →π−π+ 3. More recently, LHCb reported the first evidence for
CP violation in D0 →π−π+ 4. On the other hand, CP violation in D0 →K0
SK0
S remains less
constrained, with current measurements consistent with zero at the percent level 5.
In this contribution, we discuss how the measured branching fractions can be used to con-
strain non-factorisable contributions and associated U-spin breaking effects in the SM, and to
derive bounds on the direct CP asymmetry in D0 →K0
SK0
S.
Our analysis is based on the
factorisation framework and supplemented by minimal additional assumptions 6.

Conclusions
We have shown that the measured branching fractions of D0 →π−π+, D0 →K−K+ and
D0 →K0
SK0
S can be consistently described within the SM with non-factorisable effects and
U-spin breaking of order 50%.
The mode D0 →K0
SK0
S provides a sensitive probe of these
effects. The corresponding direct CP asymmetry is predicted to be at most at the per-mille
level, providing a benchmark for future high-precision measurements.
