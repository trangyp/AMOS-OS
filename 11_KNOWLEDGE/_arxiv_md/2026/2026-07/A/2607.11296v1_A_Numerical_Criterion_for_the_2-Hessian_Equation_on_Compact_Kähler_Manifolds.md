A NUMERICAL CRITERION FOR THE 2-HESSIAN EQUATION ON
COMPACT KÄHLER MANIFOLDS
JIXIANG FU, DEKAI ZHANG, AND ZIYI ZHANG
Abstract. We show that a Nakai–Moishezon-type criterion associated with the complex
2-Hessian equation produces a Gauduchon class.
In complex dimension three, this
numerical criterion is equivalent to the existence of a smooth 2-admissible representative
and hence to the solvability of the 2-Hessian equation. As consequences of these results,
we prove the corresponding conjectures of Murakami for the complex Hessian equation
and of Székelyhidi for the Hessian quotient equation in dimension three. We also establish
a boundary version of the above results.
