A LINEAR LOWER BOUND ON THE ULRICH COMPLEXITY OF
HYPERSURFACES
ANGELO FELICE LOPEZ, DEBADITYA RAYCHAUDHURY AND YUTA TAKAHASHI
Abstract. We give a lower bound on the Ulrich complexity of hypersurfaces in terms of their dimension.
1. Introduction
Let X ⊂PN be a smooth irreducible variety of dimension n. In order to study the geometry of X,
in recent years, an interesting point of view has emerged, the one of Ulrich bundles. An Ulrich bundle
is a vector bundle E on X such that Hi(E(−p)) = 0 for all i ≥0 and 1 ≤p ≤n. In the presence of
such bundles, several geometrical features can be discovered on X, see for example the seminal paper
[ES], the survey [B] and the book [CMRPL]. The main open problem about Ulrich bundles is their
existence, conjectured to happen in all cases. Aside for curves and several surfaces, there are a few
families of varieties that are known to carry an Ulrich bundle, among which complete intersections, by
[HUB]. Once the existence is established, one defines the Ulrich complexity as
uc(X, OX(1)) = min{r ≥1 : there exists a rank r Ulrich bundle on X}.
We simply write uc(X) when OX(1) is naturally given.
In the case of a smooth hypersurface X ⊂Pn+1 of degree d ≥2 (when d = 1, uc(X) = 1), Ulrich
complexity of X falls within the Buchweitz, Greuel and Schreyer’s conjecture [BGS], a stronger conjec-
ture regarding aCM bundles, implying that uc(X) ≥2⌊n−1
