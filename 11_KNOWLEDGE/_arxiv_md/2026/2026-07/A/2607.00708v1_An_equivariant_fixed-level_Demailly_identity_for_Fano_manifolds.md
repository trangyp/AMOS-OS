Abstract. For a Fano manifold X, a compact group G ⊂Aut(X), and a fixed level k with

stein’s question in full generality. The main result of this paper was obtained by Chatgpt 5.5
pro, and the Danus system based on the Rethlas system.

The proof uses only the G-linearization of −kKX and the basepoint-freeness of the linear
system. It makes no use of a toric structure, of monomial bases, of finite group orbits, or of the
classification of Fano manifolds.
The structure of the argument is as follows.
We first parametrize the invariant Bergman
potentials in (1.1) by positive Hermitian frame operators, and observe that, after Haar averaging,
an invariant Bergman potential is represented by a frame operator commuting with the unitary
representation on E (Section 2).
The upper bound αk,G ≤glctk,G is then a degeneration:
approaching the orthogonal projection onto a worst invariant subspace through positive definite
operators produces invariant potentials whose integrability collapses exactly like the subspace
linear system (Section 3). The lower bound αk,G ≥glctk,G is the crux. A trace-normalized
commuting frame operator has a top eigenspace which is automatically G-invariant and carries
at least a 1/N fraction of the pointwise squared norm; the desired uniform integrability over
all invariant Bergman potentials is then reduced to a single uniform integrability bound over
the compact family of invariant subspaces, which we obtain from the effective semicontinuity of
complex singularity exponents of Demailly and Kollár [DK01, Main Theorem 0.2(2)] (Sections 4
and 5).

Proof of Theorem 1.3. Let X be a Fano manifold, G ⊂Aut(X) a compact subgroup, and k a
positive integer with −kKX basepoint-free. Then X is compact, and since G is compact we may
choose a smooth G-invariant Hermitian metric h on L = −kKX, a G-invariant Hermitian inner
product on E = H0(X, −kKX) inducing the unitary representation ρ, and a smooth positive
volume measure µ. With this data the hypotheses of Theorem 1.2 hold, and the definitions
(1.2) and (1.4) are those of [JR25, Definition 4.1, Lemma 4.3, and Definition 4.4]. Theorem 1.2
therefore gives αk,G = glctk,G, which is (1.5). As L = −kKX is the k-th tensor power of −KX,
this is the fixed-level anticanonical equality of Question 1.1, answered affirmatively for every
Fano manifold X and every compact subgroup G ⊂Aut(X).
□
Remark 6.1. The identity is genuinely a fixed-level statement: the proof compares αk,G and
glctk,G at one and the same k and does not pass to the limit k →∞. The only external analytic
input is the Demailly–Kollár effective semicontinuity theorem [DK01, Main Theorem 0.2(2)],
used in Proposition 4.2; the remaining ingredients are the operator-averaging parametrization
of invariant Bergman potentials and the top-eigenspace domination (5.3). None of these uses a
toric structure, so the toric hypothesis in [JR25, Proposition 1.3] is removed.
