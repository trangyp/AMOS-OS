Abstract
Let k be a field, and let X be a smooth projective surface over k. Fix a Lef-
schetz pencil on X, and let C be its fibre at the generic point of P1. The closed
immersion of C in to Xk(t) induces the Gysin homomorphism from the Jacobian
A of the curve C to the Chow group A0(Xk(t)) of 0-cycles of degree 0 on Xk(t).
Embedding k(t) in to an uncountable universal domain k, we obtain the corre-
sponding homomorphism from A(k) to A0(Xk), whose kernel is either countable
or the union of translates of a certain abelian subvariety inside Ak, due to the
Deligne-Katz irreducibility of monodromy action on vanishing cycles. We prove
three dichotomy theorems on the structure of the kernel of Gysin homomorphism
on 0-cycles, in terms of ´etale monodromy action, and working over a field of arbi-
trary characteristic.

The Mumford-Ro˘ıtman lemma
In [26], Mumford proved a certain lemma saying that rational equivalence of 0-cycles on
a variety X is encoded by a countable collection of closed subschemes in the symmetric
powers of X, when the ground field is C. Later on, Ro˘ıtman gave a detailed proof of
Mumford’s lemma in [31], also working over C. Our first aim in the present paper
is to convince the reader that the Mumford-Ro˘ıtman theorem holds true in positive
characteristic too.
