Abstract. We develop and study an obstruction for lifting schemes over the
integers to spectral schemes over the sphere spectrum. This extends a result
of Nikolaus for rings, which states that a necessary condition for liftability
is existence of a ˆδ-structure. We prove descent properties for ˆδ-rings, define
ˆδ-schemes, and prove an analogous statement. We then apply it to concrete
examples such as number rings, closed subschemes of Pn, and various group
schemes.

1. Introduction
We are interested in the question of liftability of schemes (over Z) to spectral
schemes over the sphere spectrum S. In the affine case, this is equivalent to searching
for E∞-ring structures on Moore spectra such as S[i] (of Z[i]), S/n (of Z/n), SE (of
an ´etale algebra E), S[x] (of Z[x]), and so forth.
The case of S[i], and adjoining roots of unity to E∞-ring spectra in general, was
first considered in [SVW99]. Using base change formulas for topological Hochschild

monoid ring S[N] = Σ∞

In each of the aforementioned examples very different techniques were employed
to obstruct multiplicative structures on spherical lifts. We will content ourselves
with an obstruction for E∞-lifts only, but which will be applicable to all commuta-
tive rings at once. It was observed by T. Nikolaus1 and comes from the Tate-valued
Frobenius on E∞-rings. More precisely, if R admits an E∞-lift SR, then the base
change of the Tate-valued Frobenius on SR endows the p-completion of R with a
derived Frobenius lift, that is a δp-structure. This leads to the following definition.
Definition 1 (Definition 2.1.1). A ˆδ-ring (read: delta-hat ring) is a ring R together
with a δp-structure on R∧
p for all primes p.
This gives a relatively simple algebraic obstruction for lift existence. After estab-
lishing the obstruction (the proof of which uses some non-trivial topological input),
non-existence of E∞-structures on S/n becomes a quick calculation (see Corollary
4.3.4). We look at rings of integers and ´etale algebras as well. For example, we
show that no ring of integers of a number field lifts to S as an E∞-algebra and prove
the same for orders of the form Z[ni] (see Chapter 4).
The obstruction is furthermore functorial and can be generalized to schemes,
which is one of the main purposes of the paper.
Theorem 2 (Theorem 3.2.3). Let X be a scheme. If X admits a spectral lift to S,
then there exist compatible ˆδ-structures on the global sections of affine opens of X.
Moreover, the association of a lift to X with its ˆδ-structure is functorial.
Using this we show that neither the additive group scheme Ga (see also [Lur18a,
Proposition 1.6.20]), nor the general linear group GLn, for n ≥2, admit group
scheme lifts to Spec S. We also prove non-liftability for certain closed subschemes
of the projective space (see Chapter 4).
We would also like to bring attention to Section C of the Appendix where we
present a strategy for studying δ-rings using the cotangent complex formalism.

2. ˆδ-structures
Our main goal in this chapter is to define the notion of a ˆδ-scheme. We begin
in Section 2.1 by introducing animated ˆδ-rings, the affine building blocks for the
theory, and establishing some of their formal properties. Certain aspects, like limits
and truncations, do not behave as well as one would wish and require special care.
In Section 2.2 we establish descent for ˆδ-structures and define ˆδ-schemes.
Our
definition is of the form “a ringed space locally looking like Spec of a ˆδ-ring”.
We work with animated rings ([Lur18b, Section 25.1]) because of their good
formal behaviour, but an unacquainted reader may suppose all rings to be dis-
crete. The obstruction will only deal with discrete rings afterwards. Nonetheless,
it is crucial, even with discrete rings, that we take derived quotients and derived
completions in the definitions!
Before we get to the main content, we do a quick revision of δp-rings. This notion
was introduced by Joyal in [Joy85].
