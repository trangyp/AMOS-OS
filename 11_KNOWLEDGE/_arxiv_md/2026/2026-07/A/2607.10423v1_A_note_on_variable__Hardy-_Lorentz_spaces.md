A note on variable (Hardy-)Lorentz spaces
Pablo Rocha
July 14, 2026
Abstract
The purpose of this note is to establish further properties of the variable Lorentz
spaces Lp(·),q(·)(Rn) introduced by L. Ephremidze, V. Kokilashvili and S. Samko,
which will allow us to apply the theory of Hardy spaces associated with ball quasi-
Banach function spaces, and so define the variable Hardy-Lorentz spaces associated
with Lp(·),q(·)(Rn). Then, the finite and infinite atomic decompositions for these
spaces will be deduced immediately. We also obtain the boundedness of singular
integrals and fractional type operators in variable Hardy-Lorentz spaces, and provide
a Fefferman-Stein vector-valued inequality for the fractional maximal operator on
the r-convexification of variable Lorentz spaces.
1
Introduction
L. Ephremidze, V. Kokilashvili and S. Samko in [5] introduced the variable Lorentz
spaces Lp(·),q(·)(Ω), where Ω⊆Rn is an open set and p(·),q(·) : (0,|Ω|) →(0,∞) are
certain variable exponents.
When, p(·) = p and q(·) = q are constants, the spaces
Lp(·),q(·)(Ω) coincide with the classical Lorentz spaces Lp,q(Ω). However, the spaces
Lp(·),p(·)(Ω) might not coincide with the variable Lebesgue spaces Lp(·)(Ω), since in the
definition of these spaces the exponent p(·) is defined on whole Ω. In [5] the authors, as-
suming decay conditions of log-type on the exponents p(t) and q(t) as t →0+ and t →∞,
obtained the boundedness of singular integrals and fractional type operators, and corre-
sponding ergodic operators in the spaces Lp(·),q(·)(Ω) (see also [4]). On the other hand,
H. Kempka and J. Vyb´ıral in [8] introduced another variant of variable Lorentz spaces
Lp(·),q(·)(Rn), where p(·),q(·) : Rn →(0,∞) are certain variable exponents. They inves-
tigated their basic properties, among them, proved the identity Lp(·),p(·)(Rn) = Lp(·)(Rn).
Keywords: variable Lorentz spaces, variable Hardy-Lorentz spaces, quasi-Banach function spaces, sin-
gular integrals, fractional type operators, fractional maximal.

developed a theory of real interpolation and formulate the dual space of the variable
Hardy–Lorentz space for 0 < p−≤p+ ≤1 and 0 < q < ∞. They also investigated the
boundedness of singular operators on H p(·),q(Rn) for 0 < p−≤p+ < ∞and 0 < q < ∞.
The purpose of this work is to incorporate to the spaces Lp(·),q(·)(Rn) within the
framework developed by Y. Sawano, K.-P. Ho, D. Yang and S. Yang in [15], and so
define and study the variable Hardy-Lorentz spaces associated with Lp(·),q(·)(Rn) from
this broader perspective.
The paper is organized as follows.
In Section 2, we present the variable Lorentz spaces Lp(·),q(·)(Rn) following to [5],
and provide some further properties of these spaces. For instance, we will show that
the quasi-norm of Lp(·),q(·)(Rn) is absolutely continuous when q(0) ≤p(0) and p(∞) ≤
q(∞), a H¨older’s inequality will be established for these spaces and will also prove that
the K¨othe dual (Lp(·),q(·)(Rn))′ = Lp′(·),q′(·)(Rn). This identity was established without
proof in [5, Lemma 2.7], and subsequently used in [1] and [20]. Assuming q(0) ≤p(0)
and p(∞) ≤q(∞), we will show that (Lp(·),q(·)(Rn))′ is isomorphic to the dual space
(Lp(·),q(·)(Rn))∗. Section 2 concludes with a Fefferman-Stein valued-vector inequality
for the Hardy-Littlewood maximal operator.
In Section 3, we define the variable Hardy-Lorentz spaces H p(·),q(·)(Rn) associated
with Lp(·),q(·)(Rn). By means of the properties described in Section 2 and the theories
developed by X. Yan, D. Yang and W. Yuan in [19], and by Y. Sawano et al. in [15], we
obtain the finite and infinite atomic decompositions for the spaces H p(·),q(·)(Rn) respec-
tively.
Finally, in Section 4 we obtain the boundedness of singular integrals and fractional
type operators in H p(·),q(·)(Rn), as well as a Fefferman-Stein vector-valued inequality for
the fractional maximal operator on the r-convexification of Lp(·),q(·)(Rn). These results
are based on two previous works of the present author, which were realized within the
frame of the ball quasi-Banach function spaces (see [13] and [14]).
