A VARIATION ON THE P´OLYA-SEG˝O PRINCIPLE IN ONE
DIMENSION
MARTIN LIND AND SORINA BARZA
Abstract. We prove a P´olya–Szeg˝o principle for the Riesz (p, α)-variation, a
scale of fractional smoothness interpolating between bounded p-variation and
the Sobolev space W 1,p. In contrast to the classical P´olya–Szeg˝o inequality,
our result also holds for certain nowhere differentiable functions possessing
fractional smoothness, including Takagi–van der Waerden-type functions, and
Riemann’s ”nondifferentiable” function.
1. Introduction
For a measurable function f : I →R (where I = [a, b] ⊂R) we set
µf(y) = |{x ∈I : |f(x)| > y}|,
where |·| is the Lebesgue measure. The non-increasing rearrangement of f is defined
by
f ∗(t) = inf{y > 0 : µf(y) < t}.
As f ∗(t) = 0 for t > b −a, we view f ∗as a function defined on I∗:= [0, b −a].

(1.1)
|{x ∈I : |f(x)| > s}| = |{t ∈I∗: f ∗(t) > s}|.
