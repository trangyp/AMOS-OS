Abstract
We provide an elementary proof of a symmetric property of the
top dimensional components of the quiver subvariety An . . . A1 = 0.
1
Introduction
Given a dimension vector ⃗d = (d0, d1, . . . , dn) one can consider a type A
quiver V0 →V1 →· · · →Vn with complex vector spaces Vj of dimension
dim Vj = dj, 0 ≤j ≤n, equipped with the natural action of G = G⃗d =
GL(V0) × · · · × GL(Vn) on H = H⃗d = Hom(V0, V1) × · · · × Hom(Vn−1, Vn),
and define the product-zero variety
Σ = Σ⃗d = {(A1, . . . , An) ∈H⃗d | An . . . A1 = 0}.
This variety is studied in detail in [1] and [2] and one surprising result is that
the number and dimension of the top dimensional components of Σ⃗d are the
same for those ⃗d whose entries form permutations of one another. The proofs
involve highly abstract tools, and the main purpose of this note is to provide
an elementary proof.
In Section 2 we reformulate the problem in a purely combinatorial way
and reduce it to a technical statement that is proved in Section 3.
