Our proof follows the analytic route used in [13, 26], but introduces a new energy estimate. As
pointed out by Eskenazis, Giannopoulos and Tziotziou [13, Remark 3.8], a set of large measure

Our

before integrating the equation over the low-gradient set. This leads to a new energy estimate
(Lemma 3.1), which bypasses the convexity obstruction and improves the exponent obtained in
[13] by a factor of n.
