Abstract. We prove a criterion for a finitely presented surjection of
algebraic spaces to be descendable in a topological weave. We apply this
to show that rational motivic sheaves satisfy v-descent, and the same
for étale motivic spectra on noetherian finite-dimensional schemes with
residue fields of uniformly bounded étale cohomological dimension. We
also construct the “forgetting supports” isomorphism f! ≃f∗for a proper
DM morphism of Artin stacks, in rational motivic sheaves.

descent theorem of [SGA4, Exp. Vbis, Prop. 4.3.2], which says that the

descent for the h-topology of Voevodsky [Voe]; this amounts to descent for

both étale surjections and finitely presented proper surjections. Moreover, this
can be upgraded to h-descent for the presheaf of ∞-categories S ↦D´et(S;Λ),
where the transition functors are ∗-pullbacks.
In this paper we study the question of h-descent for abstract topologically
flavoured six-functor formalisms, i.e., for the topological weaves of [Kha]. We
provide a general criterion for h-descent, which we apply in particular to
various flavours of motivic sheaves. Consider first Morel’s model for rational
motivic sheaves: over an arbitrary base S, SHQ,+(S) denotes the plus part
of the ∞-category of rational motivic spectra over S (see [CD, §16]).
Theorem A. SHQ,+(−) satisfies v-descent (and hence a fortiori h-descent)
on all algebraic spaces.
Next consider SH´et(S), the ∞-category of motivic spectra over S satisfying
étale hyperdescent, its Λ-linearization DA´et(S;Λ) for a commutative ring Λ,
and (for S noetherian of finite dimension) the version with transfers, étale
motivic sheaves DM´et(S;Λ) as defined in [CD2, §2].
Theorem B. SH´et(−) satisfies h-descent on noetherian finite-dimensional
algebraic spaces whose residue fields have uniformly bounded étale cohomo-
logical dimension. The same holds for DA´et(−;Λ) and DM´et(−;Λ) for any
commutative ring Λ.
On global sections, Theorem A recovers h-descent for motivic cohomology
with rational coefficients, which was proven on quasi-excellent noetherian
schemes by Cisinski–Déglise (see [CD, Thms. 14.3.4, 16.2.13]). Theorem B
recovers h-descent for étale motivic cohomology, which was proven for noe-
therian schemes, assuming 2 is invertible in the coefficient ring, by Ayoub
and Cisinski–Déglise (see [Ayo, Thm. 3.9] and [CD2, Cor. 5.5.7]). Ruimy
and Tubach have shown that DM´et,gm(−;Λ), the sub-presheaf of geometric
objects, satisfies h-descent on all qcqs schemes of finite Krull dimension, when
Λ is ind-regular (see [RT, Thm. 5.2]).
In fact, in both contexts we show the stronger assertion that any finitely
presented surjection f ∶Y ↠X of qcqs algebraic spaces is descendable in
the sense of Mathew [Mat]: that is, the unit object 1X ∈D(X) can be built
out of f∗(1Y ) as follows: it lies in the smallest full subcategory containing
f∗(1Y ) and closed under cofibres, fibres, direct summands, and (−) ⊗F for
every F ∈D(X). When f is proper, this in particular implies Čech descent
along f (see Proposition 2.6).

Theorem D. Let f ∶X →Y be a proper 1-Artin morphism with proper

5. Lisse extensions and forgetting supports
We fix a presentable weave D on algebraic spaces and consider its lisse
extension to Artin stacks. For every 1-Artin morphism f ∶X →Y with proper
diagonal, there exists a natural transformation αf ∶f! →f∗, called forgetting
supports (see [Kha2, §2.2]). In this section we provide sufficient conditions
for this map to be invertible when f is proper. In particular, this will imply
that f admits ∗-direct image in D, in the sense of [Kha2, §1.4].
5.1. Descendability. We begin with the following stacky generalization of
Theorem 1.21. Note that this does not require D to be lisse-extended from
algebraic spaces.
Theorem 5.1. Let D be a presentable weave on Artin stacks satisfying
localization, and suppose every finite surjection which is either étale or radicial
is descendable. Let Y be a qcqs Deligne–Mumford stack and f ∶X ↠Y a
representable finitely presented surjection. Then f is descendable.
Proof. Let C ⊆Art/Y be the full subcategory of Artin stacks which are étale
and finitely presented over Y, and denote by C0 ⊆C the full subcategory
spanned by Y′ →Y such that fY′ ∶= f ×Y Y′ is descendable. We will show that
C0 = C, so in particular f is descendable, by applying the dévissage theorem
of [Ry, Thm. D].

a representable étale surjection y ∶Y ↠Y. Then Y ∈C and fY is a finitely
presented surjection of algebraic spaces, so Y ∈C0 by Theorem 1.21.

●For any morphism Y′′ →Y′ in C with Y′ ∈C0, we have Y′′ ∈C0. Indeed,

●For any finite étale surjection g ∶Y′′ →Y′ in C with Y′′ ∈C0, we
