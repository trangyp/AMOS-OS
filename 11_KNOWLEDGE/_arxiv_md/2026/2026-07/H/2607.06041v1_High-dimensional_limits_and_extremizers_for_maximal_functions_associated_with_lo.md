1.1.4. Variance and thin-shell type bounds in high-dimensional convex geometry. We briefly provide
some context for the variance type bound (1.20). In the case of isotropic random vectors with a
radial log-concave density, the variance type bound (1.20) can be proven more directly by an
application of the Laplace method. However, the case of general isotropic log-concave random
vectors is significantly more challenging and has profound implications. A first highly non-trivial
bound in the general case was proved in [21], showing that (1.19) holds with δd ď C
?
d{ log d.
Such a bound was sufficient to establish Klartag’s central limit theorem for convex sets [21]. The
importance of (1.20) and the related thin-shell conjecture also stems from the fact that the latter
implies a positive resolution of the Bourgain slicing problem. This problem was solved by Klartag
and Lehec in [22]. Their proof relies on a bound of Guan [19], which was obtained in the course of
proving that δd ď C log log d. Indeed, a remarkable sequence of developments followed the seminal
work [21], leading both to successive improvements in the bound for δd and to the introduction of
novel tools. For a detailed account of these developments, we refer the reader to the introduction
of [23] and references therein. This line of work culminated in the recent resolution of the thin-shell
conjecture by Klartag and Lehec in [23], which establishes (1.20).
Somewhat surprisingly, these geometric developments have remained largely separate from the
study of high dimensional maximal functions. We view our paper as a first attempt to bridge this
gap.
