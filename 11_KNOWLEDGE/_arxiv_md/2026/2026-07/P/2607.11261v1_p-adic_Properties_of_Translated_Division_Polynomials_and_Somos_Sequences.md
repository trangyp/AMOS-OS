Abstract. In this paper we consider the sequences (Ψn(P))n≥0, (Φn(P))n≥0
and (Ωn(P))n≥0 of values of the translated division polynomials of an elliptic
curve E/K evaluated at a point P ∈E(K)2. We prove that these sequences are
purely periodic when K is a finite field. Then we use the periodicity properties
of these sequences to prove that certain subsequences of these sequences are
Zp-Cauchy. Finally, we use this result to prove analogous results for Somos 4
and Somos 5 sequences.

In [30], Swart used explicit formulas to prove Somos 4 sequences are periodic
modulo prime powers pl. The proofs of Theorems 1.1 and 3.1 for the sequence
(Ψn(P))n≥0 would follow from Theorems 9.3.2 and 9.4.1 of [30].
Alternatively,
these theorems could follow from Theorem 1.13 of [1], which can be considered as

Our proof of Theorem 1.1 for the sequence (Ψn(P))n≥0 is inspired by Silver-
