GRADED FAMILIES OF IDEALS AND CONVEX REGIONS
HAOXI HU
Abstract. We study the interplay between graded families of ideals in k-domains and
their associated convex regions. These regions, called Newton-Okounkov regions, arise nat-
urally from graded families of ideals associated to a valuation with one-dimensional leaves.
Our main focus is to compute asymptotic resurgence number of a pair of graded families of
ideals. By combining techniques from Attouch–Wets topology and convex-geometric proper-
ties of Newton-Okounkov regions, we characterize the asymptotic resurgence number through
containment relations between the pair of corresponding Newton-Okounkov regions.

A key feature of our paper is the new methodology developed in the proof of the main
results, whereby we establish essential tools bridging algebraic properties of family of ideals
with convex geometry. We begin by studying the convex-geometric properties of Newton-
Okounkov regions. In particular, when R is Noetherian, each member of a graded family of
ideals corresponds to a polyhedron (called valued polyhedron), and its recession cone is the
convex hull of the semigroup of R. Then we work with Attouch-Wets topology, and construct
a sequence of valued polyhedra that is Attouch-Wets convergent to the Newton-Okounkov
region (see Theorem 2.13). This sequence of valued polyhedra, along with convex-geometric
properties of valued polyhedron (see Theorem 2.9), enable us to control the distance between
boundaries of the pair of associated Newton-Okounkov regions and detect ideal containment
simultaneously.

Definition 2.1. Let us recall that a valuation on a domain R is a function ν : K →G where
G is an ordered abelian group and K is the field of fraction of R, it satisfies the following
axioms:
(1) ν(0) = +∞.
(2) ν(xy) = ν(x) + ν(y) for x, y ∈K.
(3) ν(x + y) ≥min{ν(x), ν(y)} for x, y ∈K.
In particular, our R is a k-domain, we restrict the valuation with more conditions:
(4) G is totally ordered.
(5) ν(k) = 0 for all k ∈k.

3. Asymptotic resurgence number of a pair of graded families of ideals
In this section we show main results that share the same goal: the asymptotic resurgence
number of a pair of families of ideals equals to the supremum of noncontainment of the
corresponding pair of Newton-Okounkov regions.
We derive properties of valued regions and Newton-Okounkov regions in the previous
section, which are Theorem 2.9 and 2.13, they are the main tools for Theorem 3.5. However,
instead of focusing the general case, we want R to be Noetherian, then valued regions are
just valued polyhedra. We also show Theorem 3.6, leading to a cleaner and slightly restricted
version of Theorem 3.5, which is the Theorem 3.7. Moreover, we show Theorem 3.11, and
this allows us to prove Theorem 3.12 that generalize [HJKN26, Theorem 2.7].

Example 3.13. Given a filtration a• = {an}n∈Z≥0 in a Noetherian ring, recall the definition
of a I-stable filtration (or I-good filtration) if a• satisfies the following:
(1) Ian ⊆an+1 for all n ≥0.
(2) Ian = an+1 for all n ≫0.
Let R(a•) be the Rees algebra associated to α•, and R(I) be the Rees algebra associated
to the I-adic filtration (i.e. filtration of ordinary powers of I). By [HKU11, Remark 2.2],
If α• is an I-stable filtration, then R(a•) is finitely generated as R(I)-module. It is a well-
known fact that R(I) is Noetherian when I is an ideal in a Noetherian ring, so R(a•) is also

is Noetherian.
Example 3.14. Let R be analytically unramified Noetherian local ring, i.e. The completion
of R with respect to the maximal ideal is reduced. Let I be an ideal of R, and let R(¯I) be the
Rees algebra associated to the integral closure filtration of I (i.e. filtration of integral closure
