Universal minimal flows of the homeomorphism groups of
pseudo-solenoids are non-metrizable
Jan Boro´nski∗
Aleksandra Kwiatkowska†
July 3, 2026
Abstract
We note that homeomorphism groups of all pseudo-solenoids, including the pseudo-circle, have
non-metrizable universal minimal flows.
A pseudo-solenoid is a circle-like hereditarily indecomposable continuum1 with nontrivial 1-st ˇCech
cohomology group2. The pseudo-circle is the unique planar pseudo-solenoid. Iyer [6] showed that the
group of homeomorphisms of the universal pseudo-solenoid has non-metrizable universal minimal flow.
We note that a much more general result holds true.
Theorem 1. Homeomorphism groups of all pseudo-solenoids have non-metrizable universal minimal
flows.
Proof. By [3, Theorem 9.2] all pseudo-solenoids admit a minimal homeomorphism 3 and by [4, Theorem
4] all pseudo-solenoids are almost-chainable.
By [7, Theorem 5] the homeomorphism group of an
almost-chainable continuum with Kelley property, that admits a fixed-point-free homeomorphism,
does not have a dense Gδ-orbit. This applies to pseudo-solenoids as all hereditarily indecomposable
continua have the property of Kelley [5, 8.5 Theorem]. Therefore the homeomorphism group of every
pseudo-solenoid admits a minimal flow with all orbits meager. By [2, Theorem 1.2] and [1, Proposition
14.1] our proof is complete.
