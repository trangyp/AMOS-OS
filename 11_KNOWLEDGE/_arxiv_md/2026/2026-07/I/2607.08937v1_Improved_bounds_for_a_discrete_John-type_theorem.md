IMPROVED BOUNDS FOR A DISCRETE JOHN-TYPE THEOREM
DANILA SOLUNOV
Abstract. Tao and Vu introduced a discrete analogue of John’s theorem in which convex
progressions are approximated by generalized arithmetic progressions. In the covering version
of this problem, one asks for a small GAP containing all lattice points of a given origin-
symmetric convex body. We prove that every such convex progression in dimension n admits
an infinitely proper GAP cover whose size is within a factor O(n)2n of the cardinality of the
original set, improving the previously known factor O(n)3n. We also show that a loss of order
Ω(n)n is unavoidable for infinitely proper GAP covers.
1. Introduction
John’s theorem is one of the basic approximation results in convex geometry. It says that
every centrally symmetric convex body can be replaced, up to a dimension-dependent loss, by
a much simpler object — an ellipsoid. More precisely, if K ⊂Rn is an origin-symmetric convex
body, then there exists an ellipsoid E ⊂Rn such that
E ⊆K ⊆√n E.
The discrete counterpart of this problem was introduced by Tao and Vu [TV06, TV08]. In
the discrete setting the goal is to approximate a convex progression, that is, the set of lattice
points inside a convex body. The role of the approximating objects is played by symmetric
generalized arithmetic progressions (GAPs), namely sets of the form

much simpler and more explicit structure, which makes them easier to control and compute.
The discrete John problem asks how efficiently a convex progression can be replaced by such
an object.
For a GAP P defined as above and a number t > 0, let Pt denote the GAP obtained
by keeping the same generator matrix A and replacing each parameter bi by tbi. With this
notation, Tao and Vu proved the following discrete analogue of John’s theorem.
Theorem 1.1 ([TV08, Theorem 1.6]). Let K ⊂Rn be an origin-symmetric convex body and
Γ ⊂Rn be a full-rank lattice. Then there exists an infinitely proper GAP P in Γ such that
(1) P ⊆K ∩Γ ⊆PO(n)3n/2,
(2) |K ∩Γ| ⩽O(n)7n/2|P|.
Berg and Henk substantially improved the bounds of Tao and Vu. They showed that the
two conclusions above can be significantly improved separately.

Theorem 1.2 ([BH19, Theorem 1.1]). Let K ⊂Rn be an origin-symmetric convex body and
Γ ⊂Rn be a full-rank lattice. Then the following statements hold.
(1) There exists an infinitely proper GAP P in Γ such that
P ⊆K ∩Γ ⊆PnO(log n).
(2) There exists an infinitely proper GAP P in Γ such that P ⊆K ∩Γ and
|K ∩Γ| ⩽O(n)n|P|.
In general, the two GAPs in Theorem 1.2 need not be the same. Berg and Henk also proved
a simultaneous version, which slightly improves the result of Theorem 1.1.
Instead of seeking a proportional GAP approximation of a convex progression, one can
consider two weaker one-sided problems. The first problem asks for the smallest dimension-
dependent constant Cin(n) such that, for every origin-symmetric convex body K ⊂Rn and
every full-rank lattice Γ ⊂Rn, there exists an infinitely proper GAP Pin ⊆K ∩Γ such that
|K ∩Γ| ⩽Cin(n) |Pin|.
The second part of Theorem 1.2 gives the upper bound Cin(n) ⩽O(n)n. Berg and Henk also
proved a lower bound of the form Cin(n) ⩾(2n + 1)/3, showing that an exponential loss is
unavoidable in this problem.
The second one-sided problem is the covering problem. Here one asks for the smallest
dimension-dependent constant Cout(n) such that, for every origin-symmetric convex body
K ⊂Rn and every full-rank lattice Γ ⊂Rn, there exists an infinitely proper GAP Pout ⊇K ∩Γ
such that
|Pout| ⩽Cout(n) |K ∩Γ|.
This is the problem studied in the present paper.
The proportional inclusion results presented in Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 give immediate upper
bounds for Cout(n). Indeed, if P ⊆K ∩Γ ⊆Pt for some t ⩾1, then Pt is a GAP containing
K ∩Γ. Moreover, since P has rank at most n, we have
|Pt| ⩽O(t)n|P| ⩽O(t)n|K ∩Γ|.
Therefore, Theorem 1.1 gives Cout(n) ⩽nO(n2), while the first part of Theorem 1.2 gives the
improved bound Cout(n) ⩽nO(n log n).
The covering problem was recently studied directly by van Hintum and Keevash, who
obtained the following result.
Theorem 1.3 ([vHK24, Theorem 1.1]). Let K ⊂Rn be an origin-symmetric convex body and
Γ ⊂Rn be a full-rank lattice. Then there exists an infinitely proper GAP P in Γ such that
(1) K ∩Γ ⊆P,
(2) |P| ⩽O(n)3n|K ∩Γ|.
Equivalently, this gives Cout(n) ⩽O(n)3n. The main result of the present paper improves
the bound of van Hintum and Keevash from O(n)3n to O(n)2n.
Theorem 1.4. Let K ⊂Rn be an origin-symmetric convex body and Γ ⊂Rn be a full-rank
lattice. Then there exists an infinitely proper GAP P in Γ such that
(1) K ∩Γ ⊆P,
(2) |P| ⩽O(n)2n|K ∩Γ|.
We also prove a lower bound, showing that Cout(n) ⩾Ω(n)n.
