Abstract
The paper deals with the Riemann and Lebesgue integrability of functions. Sufficient
conditions and criteria are stated for the integrability of a composition of functions. Relevant
counterexamples are constructed based on Cantor’s perfect sets and the Cantor staircase function.
The main tool of research is Lebesgue’s criterion for Riemann integrability.
Keywords
Riemann integral, Lebesgue integral, absolutely continuous function, Cantor’s
set, Cantor staircase function

Introduction
Let 𝑓: [𝑎, 𝑏] →[𝑐, 𝑑], 𝑓be a Riemann integrable function on [𝑎, 𝑏] and 𝐺be a
continuous function on [𝑐, 𝑑], then the composition 𝐺◦𝑓is Riemann integrable;

Riemann integrability [1], stated in Theorem 2.1 below.
On the other hand, a composition of Riemann integrable functions need not be
such. A counterexample is constructed in Gelbaum and Olmsted (2003), Chap. 4, Sect.
9, where 𝑓is the Riemann popcorn function equal to 1/𝑞if 𝑥∈[0, 1] is rational and

𝐺is the indicator of (0, 1]. Both functions are Riemann integrable in [0, 1], but 𝐺◦𝑓
is not.

Conclusion
We found conditions which ensure that a composition of functions is Riemann- or
Lebesgue integrable. In particular, Theorems 2.3 and 2.5 provided nontrivial criteria
for the Riemann integrability. Various examples and counterexamples were presented.
The authors intend to expand the results to multiple integrals.
