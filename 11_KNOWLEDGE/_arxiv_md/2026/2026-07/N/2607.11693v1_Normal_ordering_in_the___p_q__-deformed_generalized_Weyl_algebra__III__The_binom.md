4. Conclusion
Following our introduction of normal ordering in the pp, qq-deformed generalized Weyl Algebra in
[29, 30] – focusing first on its algebraic framework and subsequently on its interpretation via rook
placements – the present work establishes further explicit normal ordering expansions within this
setting. Specifically, the binomial formula pX ` Y qn was derived within this deformed algebra. Upon
expanding the power one has to be careful due to the noncommutativity of the letters X, Y and Zp.
To fix the order of the letters on the right-hand side, we chose to use the normal ordered form (as is
usual). Thus, to derive the binomial formula, we had to perform two steps: 1) Write pX ` Y qn as a
sum over all possible words of length 2n containing n letters of X and n letters of Y . This is standard
and quite generic. 2) Normal order each of these words in the sum. This depends on the commutation
relations assumed and involved in our case the pp, qq-deformed s-rook numbers introduced before. In
the special case of the conventional Weyl algebra (i.e., s “ 0 and p “ 1 “ q), Varvak [52] gave a
combinatorial interpretation of the normal ordering coeﬀicients of pX `Y qn in terms of a sum of rook
numbers over all Ferrers boards fitting into particular rectangles. We derived for small values of s
(s “ 0, 1, 2, 3) and for special values of the parameters p, q, h analogous results, thereby recovering
known results in the literature as well as deriving new ones. However, for some cases (in particular
when p ‰ 1 ‰ q, but sometimes also when p “ 1, q ‰ 1) we were not able to give explicit expressions
– and these remain open for future study.
