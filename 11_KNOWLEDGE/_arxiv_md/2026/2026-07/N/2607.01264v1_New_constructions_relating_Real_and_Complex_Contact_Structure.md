NEW CONSTRUCTIONS RELATING REAL AND COMPLEX
CONTACT STRUCTURES
ALI M. ELGINDI
Abstract. We establish new connections between real and complex contact
geometry via embeddings of 3-manifolds into C3. We introduce a new contact
wedge construction combining two transverse real contact structures into a
complex one, subject to obstructions measured by the Nijenhuis tensor and
Dolbeault cohomology. Dually, we form a hedge construction which extracts
real contact structures from complex ones. Applying these tools, we prove that
C3 admits uncountably many complex contact structures, using constructions
over the standard contact S3 with Legendrian non-isotopic knots as complex
tangents.
MSC: 32V40, 57K33
Key words: contact structure, holomorphic
1. Introduction
We will make a relationship between real and complex contact structures in
the sense that we can construct new complex contact structures using a pair of
transverse real contact structures, in a process we call the ”contact wedge”. This
construction will be subject to the vanishing of two topological invariants.
Theorem 1. Let (ξ1, ξ2) be two transverse contact structures on a real 3-manifold
M. Then their wedge δ = ξ1 ∧ξ2 defines an almost complex structure on a com-
plex 2-plane bundle, which extends to a complex contact structure in a neighbor-
hood of M provided the Nijenhuis tensor vanishes and the Dolbeault obstruction in
H1(M, O(ξ1 ⊗C)) is trivial.
We will also formulate a new construction which we call the ”hedge” from which
we can construct a real contact structure ξ over a real 3-manifold M which is itself a
submanifold of a complex manifold N admitting a given complex contact structure.

3. The Contact Wedge
In this section we determine the obstructions to the existence of a contact wedge
given a pair of transverse real contact structures on a real 3-manifold M.
Theorem 2. Let (M, ξ1, ξ2) be a 3-manifold with two transverse contact structures.
Let γ ⊂M be a knot.
There exists an embedding F : M ,→C3 such that γ
forms exactly the set of complex tangencies to the embedding F. This embedding,
considered with the Fubini-Study metric, defines:
(1) A holomorphic line bundle η on F(γ),
(2) A real vector field ρ (the Reeb field of ξ2), normal to ξ1.
Given two transverse real contact structures ξ1, ξ2 on M ⊂C3, we need to ensure
their wedge δ = ξ1∧ξ2 is a complex contact structure on a neighborhood of M ⊂C3.
We first define a C-linear operator J on δ = ξ1 ⊕ξ2 such that J2 = −I. By
transversality, at each point x ∈M we may write TxM = ξ1 ⊕L(x) where L(x)
is a real line field, and ξ2 projects to contain L isomorphically to ξ1. Stipulating
Jδ(v) = w for v ∈ξ1 as the unique vector in ξ2 corresponding to v makes δ into an
almost complex structure.
The obstruction to integrability is given by the Newlander-Nirenberg theorem:
Jδ is integrable iff the Nijenhuis tensor N(Jδ) vanishes.
Theorem 3 (Obstruction for existence of the Wedge). The wedge δ = ξ1 ∧ξ2 can
be given the structure of a complex contact structure on a neighborhood of M in C3
only if the almost complex structure Jδ is integrable. The primary obstruction is
the vanishing of the Nijenhuis tensor N(Jδ) on M.
We assume the extension of holomorphic line fields over γ extends naturally to
all of M and C3, which requires the vanishing of another obstruction given by sheaf
cohomology.

4. The Hedge
The hedge is the dual construction: extracting a real contact structure from a
complex one.
Definition 8 (Hedge). Let (N, ψ) be a complex contact manifold of dimension
2n + 1, and let ξ ⊂ψ be a holomorphic line subbundle. Let M ⊂N be a totally real
submanifold of real dimension 2n + 1 that is Legendrian with respect to Im(ψ). We
say ξ is a hedge of ψ over M if ξ|M is a real contact structure on M.
Theorem 9 (Hedge Induces Contact Structure). Let (N, ψ) be a complex contact
manifold of dimension 3, and ξ ⊂ψ a holomorphic line subbundle. Let M ⊂N be
a totally real 3-manifold that is Legendrian with respect to Im(ψ). Then ξ|M is a
real contact structure on M.
Proof. Let θ be a holomorphic 1-form defining ψ, so θ∧dθ ̸= 0. Since ξ ⊂ψ = ker θ,
the form θ vanishes on ξ. Restrict θ to M. By the totally real condition, TpM ∩
J(TpM) = {0}, so the real and imaginary parts of θ|M are linearly independent.
Define α = Re(θ|M). The Legendrian condition ensures α ∧dα ̸= 0. Since ker α =
ξ|M by construction, ξ|M is a contact structure.
□
Definition 10 (Anti-hedge). Given a hedge ξ ⊂ψ, the anti-hedge is the holomor-
phic line bundle τ defined by the exact sequence:
0 →ξ →ψ →τ →0.

hedge over M, then the anti-hedge τ = ψ/ξ restricts to a real contact structure on
M, and ξ|M ⊕τ|M = ψ|M.
Proof. Let θ be a holomorphic 1-form defining ψ, so θ∧dθ ̸= 0. Since ξ ⊂ψ = ker θ,
the form θ vanishes on ξ. Restrict θ to M. By the totally real condition, TpM ∩
J(TpM) = {0}, so the real and imaginary parts of θ|M are linearly independent.
Define α = Re(θ|M). Then ker α = ξ|M, and since M is Legendrian, α ∧dα ̸= 0.
Thus ξ|M is a contact structure.

5. Abundance of Complex Contact Structures
Theorem 12 (Uncountability of Complex Contact Structures). The set of complex
contact structures on C3 up to isotopy is uncountable.
Proof. We construct an uncountable family {ψα}α∈I of complex contact structures
on C3 that are pairwise non-isotopic.
Let (S3, ξstd) be the standard contact 3-sphere. There exists an uncountable
family of Legendrian knots {Lα}α∈I in (S3, ξstd) that are smoothly isotopic but
Legendrian non-isotopic. For instance, one may take a family of torus knots whose
rotation numbers α ∈R/Z are distinct. This is a standard result in Legendrian
knot theory [6].
Choose an overtwisted contact structure ξot on S3 that is transverse to ξstd. Such
a pair exists because overtwisted structures are flexible and can be chosen to be

For each Legendrian knot Lα, we apply the corresponding explicit embedding as

Fα : S3 ,→C3
such that the complex tangents of Fα are exactly Lα and the holomorphic tangent
planes along Lα are ξstd|Lα.
The pair (ξstd, ξot) on the abstract S3, together with the embedding Fα, defines
a formal complex contact structure δα along Fα(S3) in C3. Concretely, the wedge
construction δα = ξstd ⊕Jξot gives an almost complex structure on a complex 2-
plane bundle over Fα(S3). considering this bundle together with the almost complex
structure, will constitute a formal solution of the complex contact relation in the
sense of Forstneriˇc [9]. The this formal construction varies continuously with α
because the embeddings Fα and the contact structures can be chosen continuously.
We now apply Forstneriˇc’s parametric h-principle as given in Forstneriˇc [9] which
provided for a parametric h-principle of complex contact structures on Stein man-
ifolds. Specifically, a continuous family of formal complex contact structures along
a closed totally real submanifold M ⊂X (where X is Stein) can be deformed into a
family of genuine holomorphic complex contact structures on a Stein neighborhood
of M, keeping the deformation fixed on a prescribed closed subset.
Here X = C3 (Stein) and M = Fα(S3) (totally real except along Lα). Applying
the parametric h-principle to the family {δα} yields a family {ψα} of genuine holo-
morphic complex contact structures defined on a Stein neighborhood of Fα(S3) in
C3. Because C3 is Stein and contractible, each ψα extends uniquely to all of C3.
Suppose ψα and ψβ are isotopic as complex contact structures for α ̸= β. Then
their formal data would be homotopic (by the h-principle), which would imply that
the Legendrian knots Lα and Lβ are Legendrian isotopic in (S3, ξstd). However,
by construction Lα and Lβ have distinct rotation numbers and are therefore not
Legendrian isotopic.
This contradiction shows that {ψα}α∈I is an uncountable
family of pairwise non-isotopic complex contact structures on C3.
□
6. Conclusion
We have introduced two constructions relating real and complex contact struc-
tures. The contact wedge combines two transverse real contact structures into a
complex contact structure on a neighborhood in C3, subject to vanishing of the
Nijenhuis tensor and a Dolbeault obstruction. The hedge extracts a real contact
structure from a complex one by restriction to a totally real Legendrian submani-
fold. As an application, we showed that C3 admits uncountably many non-isotopic
complex contact structures.
