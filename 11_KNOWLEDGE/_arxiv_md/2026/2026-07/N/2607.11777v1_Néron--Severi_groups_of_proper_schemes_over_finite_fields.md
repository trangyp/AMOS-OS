Abstract. Let X be a proper reduced scheme over a finite field k, let ℓbe a prime different
from char k, and write X = X ×k k for its base change to an algebraic closure k of k. Call
a class in H2
´et(X, Zℓ(1)) Zariski-locally trivial if it vanishes on a Zariski-open cover of X. We

In the finite-field setting neither seminormality nor irreducibility is needed.

1. Introduction
Let X be a smooth projective complex variety. The first Chern class embeds the Néron–
Severi group NS(X) = Pic(X)/ Pic0(X) into H2(X, Z) and the resulting subgroup admits
two (equivalent) descriptions:
(a) By the Lefschetz theorem on (1, 1)-classes it is F 1 ∩H2(X, Z), the integral classes in
the first level of the Hodge filtration.
(b) It is also the subgroup H2
ZL(X, Z) ⊆H2(X, Z) of Zariski-locally trivial classes, those
vanishing on some Zariski-open cover.
For singular X both descriptions fail. One has only the inclusions NS(X) ⊆F 1 ∩H2(X, Z)
and NS(X) ⊆H2
ZL(X, Z), and each can be strict [BVS94]. What was shown to hold instead
in many situations is their intersection, that is NS(X) = F 1 ∩H2
ZL(X, Z). This was proved for
normal projective varieties by Biswas–Srinivas [BS00] and for proper seminormal varieties
by Barbieri-Viale, Rosenschon and Srinivas [BVRS09]. Over C seminormality cannot be
dropped [BS00, Section 5].
This article proves the corresponding statement over a finite field. Let X be a proper reduced
scheme over k = Fq, let ℓbe a prime different from p = char k, and put X = X ×k k. Call a
class in H2

Definition 2.7 (Frobenius module). A Frobenius module is a finite-dimensional Qℓ-vector
space V together with a Qℓ-linear automorphism F all of whose eigenvalues are q-Weil
numbers5. A morphism of Frobenius modules is a Qℓ-linear map which respects the F-action.
Call V pure of weight w if all eigenvalues are q-Weil numbers of weight w. The zero module is
pure of every weight. Write V w=w0 for the largest F-stable subspace all of whose eigenvalues
have weight w0.
