EXISTENCE AND UNIQUENESS OF A TRANSPORT EQUATION
COUPLED WITH A BALANCE LAW
SHUVAM BANERJEE AND MANAS R. SAHOO
Abstract. In this article, we study the existence and uniqueness of a transport equation
with discontinuous coefficients that comes from the solution of a balance law with a time-
dependent source term. We also give a simplified proof providing an explicit formula for the
solution of the balance law using a variational formulation. This system can be considered as a
generalized version of the one-dimensional model for the large-scale structure of the formation
of the universe.
