LINDEL¨OF SCATTERED W-SPACES ARE σ-COMPACT
MIKO LAJ KRUPSKI
Abstract. We show that every Lindel¨of scattered W-space is σ-compact.
This result generalizes a theorem proved recently by Avil´es and the author in
[Topology Appl. 363 (2025), Paper No. 109234] and answers a question posed
by Tkachuk.
1. Introduction
All spaces under consideration are assumed to be regular. In this paper, we study
the problem, considered in [7] and subsequently in [1], of when a Lindel¨of scattered
space X is σ-compact. The main theorem of [1], which improves an earlier result
established by Tkachuk in [7], reads as follows:
Theorem 1.1. [1, Theorem 1.2] If X is a Lindel¨of scattered subspace of a Σ-product
Σ(Q
γ∈Γ Yγ, a), of first-countable spaces Yγ, then X is σ-compact.
The assumptions on X in the above theorem involves an external structure,
namely, the Σ-product. One may wonder if Theorem 1.1 can be generalized in such
a way that σ-compactness of a Lindel¨of scattered space X follows from an internal
topological property of X.
The notion of a W-space was introduced by Gruenhage in [3] as a generalization
of first-countability. It is defined in terms of a two-player topological game, called
the W-game. Let us recall that given a space X and a point x ∈X, the W-game at
x is a game with ω-many innings, played by two players: player I and player II. In
the n-th round of the game (n = 1, 2, . . .), player I picks on open neighborhood Un
of x in X and player II responds by choosing a point xn ∈Un. Player I wins the
game if the sequence (xn)∞
n=1 converges to x. Otherwise, player II wins. We say
that a point x ∈X is a W-point if player I has a winning strategy in the W-game
at x. A space X is a W-space if all of its points are W-points.
It is easy to see that any first countable space is a W-space and any subspace
of a W-space is a W-space itself. It is also known that any Σ-product of a family
of W-spaces is a W-space (see [4]). This motivated Tkachuk to ask the following
natural question (see [6]):
Question 1.2. (Tkachuk) Let X be a Lindel¨of scattered W-space. Is X σ-compact?
The purpose of this note is to answer the above question in the affirmative. We
will prove the following:
Theorem 1.3. If X is a Lindel¨of scattered W-space, then X is σ-compact.

and player II responds by choosing a point xn ∈X ∩Vn (this can be done because
X is dense in K). It is readily seen that player I has a winning strategy in this new
game if and only if player I has a winning strategy in the W-game at p ∈X. Thus,
in the situation described above we will always assume that the moves of player I,
given by a strategy in the W-game, are open in K.
