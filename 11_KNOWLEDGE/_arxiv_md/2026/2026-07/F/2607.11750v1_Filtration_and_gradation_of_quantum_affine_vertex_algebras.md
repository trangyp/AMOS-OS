Abstract
In this paper, we present a uniﬁed construction of quantum aﬃne vertex alge-
bras associated to double Yangians, untwisted quantum aﬃnization algebras, and
twisted quantum aﬃnization algebras. We show that these ℏ-adic quantum vertex
algebras contain a dense quantum vertex subalgebra equipped with a increasing
ﬁltration. Moreover, we prove that the associated graded algebras with respect to
these ﬁltrations are isomorphic to a Z-graded dense quantum vertex subalgebra
of the quantum aﬃne vertex algebras associated to double Yangians.
Keywords: Quantum vertex algebras, quantum aﬃne vertex algebras, gradation,
increasing ﬁltration, twisted quantum aﬃnizations

1 Introduction
It is well known that vertex algebras are naturally associated with untwisted aﬃne
Lie algebras, while twisted aﬃne Lie algebras are linked to the same vertex alge-
bras through the theory of twisted modules (or quasi-modules). In the general vertex
algebra theory, an important subject arguably is establishing and exploring natural
associations of quantum vertex algebras to double Yangians and (untwisted or twisted)
quantum aﬃne algebras.
As one of the fundamental works, Etingof and Kazhdan ([5]) developed a theory of
quantum vertex operator algebras in sense of formal deformations of vertex algebras,

and constructed a family of examples as formal deformations of the (universal) aﬃne
vertex algebras of type A by using the R-matrix type relations given in [28]. The
most visible diﬀerence between quantum vertex operator algebras and vertex algebras
is that the usual locality is replaced by the S-locality. This S-locality is governed by
an operator called the quantum Yang-Baxter operator, which satisﬁes the rational
quantum Yang-Baxter equation.
In [20, 21], the notions of nonlocal vertex algebra and (weak) quantum vertex
algebra were introduced and systematically studied; nonlocal vertex algebras serve as
vertex algebra analogues of noncommutative associative algebras. A nonlocal vertex
algebra is a weak quantum vertex algebra [20] if it satisﬁes the S-locality. In addition,
it becomes a quantum vertex algebra [20] if the S-locality is controlled by a rational
quantum Yang-Baxter operator. As slight generalizations of quantum vertex operator
algebras in the sense of Etingof-Kazhdan, ℏ-adic (weak) quantum vertex algebras,
which are formal deformations of (weak) quantum vertex algebras, were studied in
[23]. In particular, a conceptual construction of ℏ-adic weak quantum vertex algebras
and their modules was established from “S-local” sets of ﬁelds (vertex operators).
As an application, ℏ-adic quantum vertex algebras were associated to the centrally
extended double Yangian of sl2. Mainly in order to associate quantum vertex algebras
to quantum aﬃne algebras, a theory of φ-coordinated quasi-modules was developed
in [24, 25].
In [1], Butorac, Jing and Kožić extended Etingof-Kazhdan’s construction to type
B, C and D rational R-matrices. The modules of these quantum vertex operator
algebras are in one-to-one correspondence with restricted modules for the correspond-
ing double Yangians (see [15]). For any symmetrizable generalized Cartan matrix A,
we introduced an algebra d
DY(A) in [14], which is essentially the centrally extended
double Yangian when A is of ﬁnite type, and we gave a Drinfeld-type presentation
of d

for each ℓ∈C, and established an isomorphism between the category of restricted

Based on the R-matrix presentation of quantum aﬃne algebras (see [3, 11, 12]),
Kožić constructed the quantum vertex operator algebras associated with trigono-
metric R-matrices of types A, B, C and D ([15, 16]), and established a one-to-one
correspondence between φ-coordinated modules and restricted modules for quantum
aﬃne algebras. In [10], we developed a method for constructing quantum vertex
operator algebras by using vertex bialgebras. Applying this method together with
the Drinfeld’s quantum aﬃnization construction ([4, 8, 27]), we constructed in [18]
the quantum aﬃne vertex algebras Vˆg,ℏ(ℓ, 0) and Lˆg,ℏ(ℓ, 0) for all symmetric Kac-
