FACTORING MAPS TO BIG COHEN-MACAULAY ALGEBRAS THROUGH
BLOWUPS
KARL SCHWEDE
Abstract. We show that for a reduced universally catenary equidimensional Noetherian local ring
R, any sufficiently functorial big Cohen-Macaulay algebra assignment R 7→BR, and any proper
birational map Y −→Spec R, there exists a factorization R −→RΓ(Y, OY ) −→BR in the derived
category of R-modules.
1. Introduction
Big Cohen-Macaulay modules and algebras were identified by Hochster as a tool for proving
deep results in commutative algebra [Hoc73, Hoc75], cf. [Sha81]. Big Cohen-Macaulay algebras
associated to an excellent Noetherian local domain R were shown to exist in characteristic p > 0
in [HH92] where Hochster and Huneke showed that R+, the integral closure of R in an algebraic
closure of its fraction field, is big Cohen-Macaulay. From this the existence of big Cohen-Macaulay
algebras in characteristic zero can also be deduced.
Also see [HH95, HL07, Smi94].
In mixed
characteristic, big Cohen-Macaulay algebras were shown to exist by Andr´e in [And18, And20], see
also [Gab18, HM18]. More recently, Bhatt showed that the p-adic completion c
R+ of R+ is big
Cohen-Macaulay in mixed characteristic [Bha20], also see [Sch04] in characteristic zero.
An interesting feature of c
R+ in positive and mixed characteristic is that if π : Y −→Spec R is
any proper birational map, one has a factorization
R −→RΓ(Y, OY ) −→c
R+.
In characteristic p > 0, this was shown in [Bha12] and in mixed characteristic it follows quickly
from [Bha20].
In this note we show that such a factorization is a formal consequence of any sufficiently functorial
assignment of big Cohen-Macaulay algebras. Our main result is the following.

Definition 2.1 (Balanced big Cohen-Macaulay modules). Suppose (R, m) is a Noetherian local
ring. An R-module M is called weakly balanced big Cohen-Macaulay if every system of parameters
of R is a weakly regular sequence2 on M. We say it is balanced big Cohen-Macaulay if it is weakly
balanced big Cohen-Macaulay and mM ̸= M.
We recall that both weakly balanced and balanced big Cohen-Macaulay R-modules behave well
under localization.
Lemma 2.2 ([Tak84], [Zar87, Corollary 2.5], [Sha81]). Suppose (R, m) is a Noetherian universally
catenary and equidimensional local ring. If M is a (weakly) balanced big Cohen-Macaulay R-module,
then MQ is a (weakly) balanced big Cohen-Macaulay RQ-module for every Q ∈Spec R such that
MQ ̸= 0.
Proof. We sketch the case that M is weakly balanced big Cohen-Macaulay as that case is not
stated in the literature (although of course, the proof is easier). For any system of parameters
(x1, . . . , xr) for RQ, we can find (y1, . . . , yr), part of a system of parameters for R, such that
(y1, . . . , yi)RQ = (x1, . . . , xi)RQ for all i, see [Zar83, Lemma 2.2].
By hypothesis yi+1 is not a zero divisor on M/(y1, . . . , yi)M and hence also not a zero divisor
on the localization. By assumption yi+1RQ/(y1, . . . , yi)RQ = xi+1RQ/(x1, . . . , xi)RQ and so mul-
tiplication by xi+1 and yi+1 agree up to a unit of RQ on the localized quotient, and so the result
follows.
