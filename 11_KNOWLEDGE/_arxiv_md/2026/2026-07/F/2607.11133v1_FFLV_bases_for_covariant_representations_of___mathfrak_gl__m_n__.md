FFLV BASES FOR COVARIANT REPRESENTATIONS OF gl(m|n)
IBRAHIM AHMAD AND PRANAV ENUGANDLA
Abstract. We study the PBW filtration on covariant representations for the Lie superalgebra
gl(m|n).
We prove for all covariant weights of the form (λ|µ, 0n−1), that there exists a lattice
polytope such that the lattice points of this polytope parametrize a basis of the corresponding
associated graded space. As a consequence, we obtain degenerations of partial flag supervarieties
for the supergroup GL(m|n) into toric supervarieties.
1. Introduction
Let g = n−⊕h⊕n+ be a finite-dimensional simple Lie algebra with a fixed triangular decomposi-
tion. By the PBW-theorem, the associated graded space of the universal enveloping algebra of n−,
which we denote by U(n−), is the symmetric algebra Sym(n−). This filtration induces an abelianzed
action of Sym(n−) on the associated graded module gr V (λ) =: V (λ)a. In [FFL11a] and [FFL11b],
Feigin, Fourier and Littelmann studied the PBW filtration of the finite-dimensional irreducible
g-modules V (λ) in types An and Cn, and constructed a monomial basis of V (λ)a parametrized by
the lattice points of a normal polytope, which is known as the Feigin-Fourier-Littelmann-Vinberg
polytope. Similar results on polytopes parameterizing monomial bases of the associated graded
modules are known in type G2 [Gi15] and Bn [BK19; Mak19]. The FFLV polytopes are fascinating
in a variety of aspects.
From a combinatorial standpoint, these polytopes are very closely linked to the Gelfand-Tsetlin
polytopes [GC50]: Ardila, Bliem and Salazar proved in [ABS11] that both polytopes can be realized
as marked versions of Stanley’s order and chain polytopes [Sta86] and provide piecewise-affine
bijection between them.
Geometrically, Feigin introduced the PBW-degnerate flag variety in [Fei12] by considering the
closure of the highest weight orbit of the abelianized Lie group N−,a · [vλ] ⊆PV (λ)a and showed that
there is a flat degeneration of the flag variety into the PBW-degenerate flag variety. The normality
of the FFLV polytopes allowed Feigin, Fourier and Littelmann to conclude in [FFL17b] that the
representations V (λ) are favourable. This condition provides a way to degenerate the embedded
partial flag variety G/P ,→PV (λ) to the PBW-degenerate flag variety and also degenerate the
PBW-degenerate flag variety to the toric variety given by the FFLV polytope. Later on, Fang,
Fourier and Littelmann gave in [FFL17a] a more uniform construction of toric degenerations of flag
varieties via birational sequences.
Previously, Fourier and Kus constructed in [FK21] lattice points of a polytope yielding monomial
bases for typical representations of various Lie superalgebras, including gl(m|n). In this article,
we provide lattice polytopes parametrising monomial bases compatible with the PBW-filtration
for certain covariant gl(m|n)-representations.
First introduced by Berele-Regev in [BR87] and
Sergeev in [Ser84], covariant representations form the irreducible components of the semisimple
representation (Cm|n)⊗k for k ≥0. Their highest weights are parametrized by pairs of partitions
(λ|µ) = (λ1, . . . , λm|µ1, . . . , µn) such that λm ≥|{i | µi ̸= 0}|. This can be translated combinato-
rially as saying that the composition (λ, µ′), where µ′ is the transpose of µ, is a partition whose
Young diagram sits inside the (m, n)-hook, i.e., does not contain the (n+1)th box in row m+1. For
covariant representations, there are already Gelfand-Tsetlin bases available constructed in [Mol11]
and [SJ10], but an FFLV counterpart, even in spirit, remained missing.
