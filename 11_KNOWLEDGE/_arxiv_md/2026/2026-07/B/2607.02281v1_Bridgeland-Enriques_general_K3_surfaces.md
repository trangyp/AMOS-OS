BRIDGELAND–ENRIQUES GENERAL K3 SURFACES
ZIQI LIU
Abstract. This article introduces a notion of Bridgeland–Enriques general
K3 surfaces motivated by the study of Enriques categories over K3 surfaces
and the invariant Bridgeland stability conditions. The family of Bridgeland–
Enriques general K3 surfaces of degree 10 detects a categorical degeneration
of special Gushel–Mukai threefolds. Also, the families of Bridgeland–Enriques
general K3 surfaces with higher degrees are closely related to Hodge-special
Gushel–Mukai fourfolds and double EPW sextics.
