Tensor Hinted Mv Conjectures
Zhao Song∗
Abstract
Brand, Nanongkai, and Saranurak [BNS19] introduced a conjecture known as the Hinted Mv
Conjecture. Although it was originally formulated for the matrix case, we generalize it here to
the tensor setting.

Conjecture 2. For any algorithm solving Tensor Hinted Mv (Definition 1, Type I), given polyno-
mial preprocessing in Phase 1, the following lower bounds apply to at least one phase:
• Phase 2 requires Ω(nω(1,1,τ)−δ + kn1+τ−δ).
• Phase 3 requires Ω(n1+τ−δ + knτ−δ).
For every δ > 0.
