PAVEL ČOUPEK, EVANGELIA GAZAKI, AND ADRIANO MARMORA
Abstract. Let K be a p-adic field and T a lattice in a semistable representation
of Gal(K/K) with Hodge-Tate weights in [0, r]. Assuming 0 ≤r < p −1, we prove
that for a semistable extension of Zp by T, the corresponding sequence of strongly
divisible modules is exact. The same statement is proved for Breuil–Kisin modules
for all r ≥0. In the crystalline case, we deduce that the integral Bloch-Kato Selmer
group H1
f(K, T) is computed by Ext1 in the category of crystalline strongly divisible
modules.
Using further exactness results, we define a tensor product of strongly
divisible modules, which commutes with the functors to Galois representations. As
an application, we show that for abelian varieties A1, A2 over K with good reduction,
the cup product map δ1 ∪δ2 : A1(K)⊗A2(K) →H2(K, Tp(A1)⊗Tp(A2)) induced by
the Kummer sequences of A1, A2 factors through an Ext2 group of strongly divisible
modules.
1. Introduction
Let K be a p-adic field. One of the prevalent themes in p-adic Hodge Theory is
the construction of equivalences connecting various categories of representations of
GK = Gal(K/K) with suitable categories of “linear-algebraic data”. Integral p-adic
Hodge theory strives to realize this goal specifically for categories of lattices in GK-
representations, i.e., finite free Zp-modules T endowed with Galois action.
In the integral context, a complicating factor is that such categories of lattices are
not abelian, but only exact categories (in the sense of Quillen [Qui73]). Since the datum
of exact category is an additional structure rather than property of the category, an
equivalence between such categories often fails to be exact in one or both directions.
This can be an obstacle to computations. For example, one might hope to establish an
isomorphism of Ext groups on both sides of the equivalence in order to compute Galois
cohomology. Exactness issues can often hinder such efforts.
In this paper, we investigate these (non-)exactness phenomena in the context of two
classical theories: the theory of strongly divisible modules of Breuil [Bre97] and the
theory of Breuil–Kisin modules [Kis06]. In both cases, the respective category comes
equipped with an exact, fully faithful functor to Galois lattices; however, the quasi-
inverse on their essential image is not exact in general. After documenting some of
these pathologies, we prove several positive results, showing that short exact sequences
needed for Galois cohomology often retain exactness. Our main result, formulated in
terms of the category Mod(S)φ,N
r,log of strongly divisible modules, is the following.
