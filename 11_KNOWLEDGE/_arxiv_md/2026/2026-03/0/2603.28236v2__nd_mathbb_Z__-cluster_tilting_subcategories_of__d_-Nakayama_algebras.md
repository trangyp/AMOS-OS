ABSTRACT. Jasso and K¨ulshammer introduced the class of d-Nakayama algebras as a
higher dimensional analogue of Nakayama algebras.
In particular, they are endowed
with a distinguished dZ-cluster tilting subcategory. In this paper, we investigate which
d-Nakayama algebras admit an ndZ-cluster tilting subcategory for n > 1. The radical
square zero case is already covered by results on classical Nakayama algebras due to Her-
schend–Kvamme–Vaso. For each remaining non-self-injective d-Nakayama algebra, we
provide a complete classification of its ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories. In fact, there ex-
ists at most one for a suitable integer n. A self-injective d-Nakayama algebra is determined
by two positive integers m and ℓ. We show that an ndZ-cluster tilting subcategory is only
possible if n|m and n|(ℓ−2). In case n = ℓ−2, we show that such subcategory does indeed
exist by constructing an explicit example.

1. INTRODUCTION
Auslander-Reiten theory is fundamental in the study of representation theory of finite
dimensional algebras from the perspective of homological algebra. A higher dimensional
analogue was introduced in [Iya07a,Iya07b] and has since found connections to algebraic
geometry [IW11,IW13,IW14,HIMO23], combinatorics [OT12] and symplectic geometry
[DJL21]. It also plays a key role in the proof of the Donovan-Weymss conjecture [JKM22].
In higher Auslander-Reiten theory, the object of study is a category A, typically the module
category of a finite dimensional algebra or its bounded derived category, equipped with a d-
cluster tilting subcategory M ⊆A, possibly with some additional properties. Depending on
different settings, d-cluster tilting subcategories give rise to higher notions in homological

Let A be a finite dimensional algebra and denote by modA the category of finitely gen-
erated right A-modules. The algebra A is called d-representation finite if gldimA = d and
there exists a d-cluster tilting subcategory M ⊆modA. In this case, M is unique and
canonically induces a d-cluster tilting subcategory U in Db(A). More precisely,
U = add{M[di] | M ∈M and i ∈Z}.
This case was intensively studied by many authors, see for example [Iya11, IO11, IO13,
HI11a, HI11b]. For A with gldimA > d, much fewer results are known. Certain families
of such algebras which are not self-injective were found by Vaso [Vas19, Vas20, Vas21,
Vas23]. Large classes of self-injective algebras with a d-cluster tilting subcategory were
constructed in [DI20], including many instances of n-fold trivial extension algebras and
higher preprojective algebras. Other notable families of such algebras include Nakayama
algebras and higher Nakayama algebras [JK19].
The notion of dZ-cluster tilting property was introduced by Iyama and Jasso [IJ17].
This property is significantly stronger than the d-cluster tilting property. In particular, if an
algebra A with gldimA < ∞is such that modA admits a dZ-cluster tilting subcategory, then
d must divide gldimA. In the special case where gldimA = d, any d-cluster tilting subcate-
gory of modA is trivially dZ-cluster tilting. A key benefit of the dZ-cluster tilting property,
as observed by Kvamme [Kva21], is that a dZ-cluster tilting subcategory in the module cat-
egory canonically induces such a subcategory in its singularity category. Furthermore, the
Auslander-Iyama correspondence introduced by Jasso-Muro [JKM22] established a one-
to-one correspondence between triangulated categories with a dZ-cluster tilting object and
twisted periodic self-injective algebras up to equivalence.
For the reasons given above, we are motivated to search for algebras that admit dZ-
cluster tilting subcategories. A complete classification of such subcategories for Nakayama
algebras was provided in [HKV25]. As a natural continuation, we restrict our attention
in this paper to d-Nakayama algebras, since they are homologically complex yet com-
putationally accessible. By construction, a d-Nakayama algebra A has a distinguished
dZ-cluster tilting subcategory in its module category [JK19], and this property forces
gldimA ∈dZ. We further restrict our search to ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories for a
positive integer n > 1. More precisely, we give a classification of ndZ-cluster tilting sub-
categories for non-self-injective d-Nakayama algebras, and we construct explicit examples
of such subcategories for self-injective d-Nakayama algebras satisfying an additional con-
dition. A necessary condition for s-representation finite d-Nakayama algebras was given
in [Sen23] for some positive integer s. The same paper posed the question that whether
this necessary condition is also sufficient. As a consequence of our classification result, we
provide an affirmative answer to this question.
Certain methods used in this paper are of independent interest, in particular the sink-
source gluing of two algebras. Such gluing constructions have been considered in [IPTZ87,
L´ev08, BCW15, Vas20]. We show that the algebra obtained by the sink-source gluing of
algebras admitting a d-cluster tilting subcategory (respectively, a dZ-cluster tilting subcat-
egory) itself possesses a canonical d-cluster tilting subcategory (respectively, dZ-cluster
tilting subcategory) in its module category, which is obtained by gluing the corresponding
d-cluster tilting subcategories (respectively, dZ-cluster tilting subcategories) of each piece.

Our classification result for higher Nakayama algebras generalizes the classification

algebra A with rad 2A = 0, we may view it as a classical Nakayama algebra and apply

cluster tilting subcategories and dZ-cluster tilting subcategories for radical square zero

injective d-Nakayama algebra, we give a classification of ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories
for n > 1. In this case, such an integer n is unique and the ndZ-cluster tilting subcategory
is also unique. We subdivide the classification result into three cases – depending on the
Kupisch series – namely, acyclic homogeneous, acyclic non-homogeneous and cyclic non-
homogeneous.
The classification for acyclic homogeneous d-Nakayama algebras is given in Theo-
rem 3.11. In this case, all such algebras with ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories are 2-
subhomogeneous (see [Xin25, Definition 3.1]).
Equivalently, the indecomposable ob-
jects in such an ndZ-cluster tilting subcategory consist of projective or injective objects.
The classification result for acyclic non-homogeneous d-Nakayama algebras is given in
Theorem 3.15. All such algebras are obtained by sink-source gluing of acyclic homoge-
neous ones with ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories. The classification result for cyclic non-
homogeneous d-Nakayama algebras is given in Theorem 3.24. Such algebras are obtained
by self-gluing acyclic non-homogeneous ones with ndZ-cluster tilting subcategories.
For a self-injective d-Nakayama algebra, such a classification result seems to be in-
feasible. So instead we construct an explicit example for each such algebra with extra
conditions.
