TEPPEI TAKAMATSU AND SHOU YOSHIKAWA
Abstract. We study the perfectoid pure threshold with respect to p, an invariant
of singularities in mixed characteristic (0, p) arising from perfectoid purity. In this
paper, we compute perfectoid pure thresholds for lifts of rational double points. We
show that the set of such thresholds is contained in Q and satisfies the ascending
chain condition. In characteristic 2, all reciprocals of positive integers occur, and 0
is the unique accumulation point.
1. Introduction
The theory of singularities in mixed characteristic has recently undergone remark-
able development, as demonstrated in works such as [MST+22], [BMP+24b], and
[HLS24]. This line of research has led to profound applications in both algebraic ge-
ometry and commutative algebra, including the mixed characteristic minimal model
program [BMP+23], [TY23], as well as Skoda-type theorems [HLS24].
In this paper, we study an invariant of rings in characteristic (0, p) called the per-
fectoid pure threshold. The notion of perfectoid purity was introduced in [BMP+24a]
in the study of singularities in mixed characteristic, and the perfectoid pure threshold
can be viewed as an analogue of the F-pure threshold in positive characteristic, intro-
duced in [TW04], and of the log canonical threshold in characteristic zero. Note that
closely related mixed characteristic thresholds have also been investigated recently
(see [RV25], [CPQG+25], and [BJP+25]).
The second author introduced methods for computing perfectoid pure thresholds
with respect to p in [Yos25a] and [Yos25b]. Using these methods, the second author
proved the following result.
Theorem 1.1 ([Yos25b, Theorem C]). Let d be a non-negative integer. Then the
following two sets coincide:
• the set Pd,p of ppt(R, p) such that (R, m) is a regular local ring of dimension
d with p ∈m and R/pR is F-finite, and
• the set Td,p of fpt(R, f) such that (R, m) is a regular F-finite local ring of
dimension d of characteristic p and 0 ̸= f ∈m.
Since the set Td,p is known to satisfy several discreteness properties—namely rational-
ity, the ascending chain condition, and restrictions on accumulation points [BMS08],
[Sat21], [Sat23]—the same properties also hold for Pd,p.
