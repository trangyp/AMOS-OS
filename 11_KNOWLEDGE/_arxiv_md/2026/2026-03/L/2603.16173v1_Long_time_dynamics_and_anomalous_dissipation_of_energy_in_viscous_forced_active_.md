LONG TIME DYNAMICS AND ANOMALOUS DISSIPATION OF
ENERGY IN VISCOUS FORCED ACTIVE SCALAR EQUATIONS
SUSAN FRIEDLANDER AND ANTHONY SUEN
Abstract. We study an abstract family of advection-diﬀusion equations within
the framework of the fractional Laplacian. The system involves two indepen-
dent diﬀusion parameters: one introduced via a damping operator acting on
the scalar unknown and the other as the coeﬃcient of the fractional Laplacian.
We establish existence and convergence results in speciﬁc parameter regimes
and limits. In particular, we demonstrate the absence of anomalous energy dis-
sipation for long-time averaged solutions. Moreover, we investigate the long
time dynamics and prove the existence of a unique global attractor.
These
results are then applied to two speciﬁc classes of active scalar equations in
geophysical ﬂuid dynamics, namely the surface quasigeostrophic equation and
the magnetogeostrophic equation.

Another model with related, but distinct, features is the three-dimensional mag-

equation have been determined in various settings of the diﬀusive parameters via an
analysis of the Fourier multiplier symbol relating the velocity u and the scalar ﬁeld
θ. In a series of papers [FS15, FS18, FS19], the authors proved global existence of
classical solutions to the forced MG equations and obtained convergences of solu-
tions as various diﬀusive parameters vanish. Moreover, it was shown in [FS21] that
the MG equations possess global attractors with various interesting properties; also
refer to the survey articles [FRV14, FS23] for more details.
The purpose of our current work is to investigate properties of the abstract sys-
tem (1.1) under assumptions A1–A4, with emphasis on the long time dynamics
and convergence results in the context of the fractional Laplacian κΛγ and the
damping parameter λ, and then we apply these results to physical models as men-
tioned above. Speciﬁcally, we examine the limiting behaviour of solutions as κ or
λ vanishes, and two main studying directions are illustrated as follows:
1. With the presence of forcing, the long time behaviour of (1.1) can be related to
the study of global attractors or dynamical systems. There is a considerably rich
literature on the topic of global attractors; see for example [CCP12, CD18, CF85,
HOR15, Rau02, Rob13, Rob01] and the references therein. For the dissipative SQG
equation, the global attractor has been addressed previously for the subcritical
regime [Ju05, WT13, CZ18] as well as the critical case [CTV15, CCZV16]. In the
context of MG equation, the existence of compact global attractor in L2 was proved
in [FS18], the convergence and upper upper semicontinuity of the global attractors
were later obtained in [FS21]. With the presence of damping term λu, recently the
authors in [GST26] showed the existence of a robust family of exponential attractors
for the scalar-valued convective Cahn–Hilliard/Allen–Cahn (CH-AC) equation, and
yet there is no known result on global attractors for active scalar equations with
the damping operator λD. In this current study, we aim to ﬁll this research gap by
investigating the existence and convergence properties of global attractors for the
abstract active scalar equations (1.1) under the inﬂuence of λD.

The rest of the paper is organised as follows. In Section 2, we state our main
results on the forced active scalar equation (1.1), while in Section 3 we introduce
some notations and useful formulae for later analysis. In Section 4 the parameter
λ is taken to be positive and we investigate the anomalous dissipation for (1.1).
Following the similar idea in the proof in [CTV14] for critical SQG, we show that
there is no anomalous dissipation by introducing stationary statistical solution for
the viscous (κ > 0) and inviscid (κ = 0) cases. We note that the results in Section 4
are valid in the examples of the fractional SQG equation and MG equation. In
contrast, in Section 5, when the viscosity κ is kept to be positive but λ is allowed to
vary, we obtain global-in-time existence and convergence results as λ →0 under an
extra smoothing assumption on Tij that is consistent with the case of MG equations.
In Section 6 we further study the long time behaviour of solutions to the abstract
system when λ ≥0 and κ > 0. We prove that the solution map associated with
(1.1) possesses a unique global attractor Gλ in H1. With the restriction that λ > 0,
we prove that Gλ always has ﬁnite fractal dimension. In Section 7 we apply the
results proved in Section 6 to the speciﬁc example of the MG equation. We prove
convergence of Gλ as λ goes to zero to the global attractor G0 in H1 for the MG
equation whose existence was demonstrated in [FS21].
