1. Introduction
Introduced in the 1960s as a tool for studying sheaves and their cohomology in algebraic geom-
etry, local cohomology has since found extensive applications in commutative algebra. Over the
years, it has developed into a fundamental tool and a vibrant area of research in its own right.
Local cohomology modules are not finitely generated in general. The lack of finite generation
has turned attention of researchers to study other type of finiteness properties; see, for instance,
[3, 4, 5, 6]. Nevertheless, for the purposes of this article, we restrict our attention to the graded
components of local cohomology modules. We now review some related literature and also state
a result of the second author, joint with a coauthor, from [14], which motivates the present work.

local cohomology with respect to S+ is particularly well behaved [1, Theorem 15.1.5]. It is well-
