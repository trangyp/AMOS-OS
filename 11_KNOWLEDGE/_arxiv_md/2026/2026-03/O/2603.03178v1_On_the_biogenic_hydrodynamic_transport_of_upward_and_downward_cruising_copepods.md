INTRODUCTION
Mesozoplankton are abundant and widely distributed acros diverse marine environ-
ments, from coastal shelves to the open ocean. Many species are wel known for undergoing

Important insights have ben gathered from controled laboratory studies [5{7] and numeri-

establish an acurate evaluation of the net eect of BHT in real marine ecosystems, where

complex ocean environments.

teristics of ecologicaly relevant organisms aect net transport and, consequently, ireversible
mixing. As one of the most abundant multicelular taxa in the ocean, copepods are invari-
ably a primary driver of BHT. Extensive research has characterized their behavioral traits
