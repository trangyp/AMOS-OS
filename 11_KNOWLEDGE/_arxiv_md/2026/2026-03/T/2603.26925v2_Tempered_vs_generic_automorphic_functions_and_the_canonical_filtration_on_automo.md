TEMPERED VS GENERIC AUTOMORPHIC FUNCTIONS AND
THE CANONICAL FILTRATION ON AUTOMORPHIC FUNCTIONS
DENNIS GAITSGORY, VINCENT LAFFORGUE AND SAM RASKIN
Abstract. We introduce and study the filtration on the space of automorphic functions (in the
everywhere unramified situation for the function field case) obtained by transferring the filtration
on the spectral side of the classical Langlands conjecture, induced by coherent singular support.
We propose a number of conjectures that tie this filtration (which, by design, arises from the
notion of cohomological support) to a filtration on the space of C-valued automorphic functions
that arises by considering the analytic spectrum of Hecke operators.

0.5. Conventions and notation. The conventions and notations in this paper follow those adopted
in [GR].
0.5.1.
We fix X a smooth and complete curve and G a reductive group over Fq, but both assumed
to be defined over Fq, so that they carry an action of the geometric Frobenius. For simplicity, we will
assume that the Fq-form of G is split.
We let ˇG be the Langlands dual of G, which we regard as a reductive group over the field of
coefficients Qℓ.
0.5.2.
We will deal with two kinds of algebraic geometry. One is taking place over the ground field,
which is Fq, and we will be interested in the category of constructible sheaves on schemes or algebraic
stacks over this Qℓ. As this category is not sensitive to infinitesimals, we can stay within the realm of
classical (as opposed to derived) algebraic geometry.
The second kind of algebraic geometry is taking place over the field of coefficients, which is Qℓ.
We will be dealing with coherent (or ind-coherent) sheaves on prestacks over Qℓ. As these categories
are sensitive to the derived structure, we have to place ourselves in the context of derived algebraic
geometry (but in the simplified context4, since Qℓhas characteristic 0).
0.5.3.
We will be dealing with higher algebra over Qℓ, in which the basic object is the ∞-category of
Qℓ-linear DG-categories, denoted DGCatQℓ(see, e.g., [GaRo1, Chapter 1, Sect. 10]). This category
carries a symmetric monoidal structure, given by the Lurie tensor product, for which the unit is the
category VectQℓof chain complexes of Qℓ-vector spaces.
In particular, for a dualizable object C ∈DGCatQℓequipped with an endofunctor F, we have a
well-defined object
Tr(F, C) ∈VectQℓ.
