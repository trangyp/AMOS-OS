THE STRUCTURE OF ZnG AND ITS UNIT GROUP
JYOTI GARG, SUGANDHA MAHESHWARY, AND HIMANSHU SETIA
Abstract. This article determines the structure of the group ring ZnG, where G is a finite
group and Zn is the ring of integers modulo n, such that n is relatively prime to the order
of G. The decomposition of ZnG is given as a direct sum of matrix rings over Galois rings,
thereby extending the structural theory of group rings beyond the classical field setting. We
also provide a method to compute a generating set of the unit group U(ZnG), in terms of
elementary matrices, using Shoda pair theory. The results are illustrated with examples.
1. Introduction

where |G| denotes the order of G.

Lemma 4.1. Let u be a unit of order l in FpG. Then u is a unit of order lpr−k in ZprG, where

nil ideal, we have that uv is unipotent unit in ZprG and hence u is invertible in ZprG.
