Abstract. For a simplicial poset P, Stanley assigned the face ring
AP , which is the quotient of the polynomial ring S := K[tx | x ∈
P \{b0}] by the ideal IP . This is a generalization of Stanley-Reisner
rings, but S and AP are not standard graded in this case, and IP
is not a monomial ideal. To establish the foundation of the theory
on local cohomology Hi
Ip(S) and its injective resolution, we give
an explicit description of the graded injective envelope ∗ES(S/px),
where px is the prime ideal associated with x ∈P, and analyze
their behavior in the graded dualizing complex.
1. Introduction
Throughout the paper, let P be a simplicial poset, that is, P is a finite
poset with the smallest element ˆ0, and the subposet {y ∈P | y ≤x} is
isomorphic to a boolean algebra for all x ∈P. If we regard the set of
faces of a simplicial complex as a poset by inclusions, it is simplicial.
Moreover, any simplicial poset is isomorphic to the face poset of a
particular class of regular cell complexes. Moreover, simplicial posets
and their face rings play roles in toric topology (c.f. [7, 8]).
