Abstract
Fix a knot K0 in R3 and consider a Lagrangian submanifold L of T ∗R3 that is isotopic
to the conormal bundle of K0 by a compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy and
intersects the zero section R3 cleanly along a knot. In this paper, using microlocal
sheaf theory and some results in 3-manifold theory, we prove that the knot type of
K1 := L ∩R3 in R3 is strictly constrained from the knot type of K0. Specifically, we
deduce the existence of a surjective group homomorphism π1(R3 \ K0) →π1(R3 \ K1)
preserving the longitude and meridian with respect to the Seifert framing. Moreover,
combining with a previous work by the second author, we obtain a rigidity result
which was only known for the unknot: If K0 is the (2, q)-torus knot or the figure-eight
knot, K1 must have the same knot type as K0.

Introduction
1.1
Background
Let L1 and L2 be Lagrangian submanifolds of a symplectic manifold (X, ω) and consider
their intersection L1 ∩L2. Here, we suppose that L1 and L2 have a clean intersection (for
the definition, see Definition 2.1). In this case, the intersection L1 ∩L2 is a submanifold
of L1 (and L2) and may have a positive dimension. When dim(L1 ∩L2) > 0, unlike the
case of transversal intersections, it is a non-trivial question how L1 ∩L2 is embedded in
L1 (and L2) as a submanifold.
With a perspective of low dimensional topology, an interesting case would be when
dim X = 6 and L1 ∩L2 is an embedded circle, that is, a knot in the 3-manifold Li for
i = 1, 2.
For a pair of Lagrangian 3-spheres, Smith made a question concerning the
‘persistence (or rigidity) of unknottedness of Lagrangian intersections’ under Hamiltonian
diffeomorphisms [GP21, Question 1.5].
When X is a plumbing of two copies of T ∗S3
along the unknots in S3, there are results related to this question by Ganatra–Pomerleano
[GP21, Proposition 6.29], Smith–Wemyss [SW23, Proposition 4.10] and Asplund–Li [AL25,
Theorem 1.3]. In particular, [AL25, Theorem 1.1] shows the persistence of unknottedness
of clean Lagrangian intersections under a ‘nearby Hamiltonian diffeomorphism’.
In the present paper, we consider the case where X = T ∗R3 with a standard symplectic
structure. For any smooth knot K in R3, let T ∗
KR3 denote its conormal bundle. Note that
T ∗
KR3 and the zero section 0R3 intersect cleanly along K ⊂0R3 = R3. Let Hamc(T ∗R3)
denote the group of compactly supported Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms on T ∗R3. Given
a knot K in R3, we ask the following:
Question 1.1. Is there φ ∈Hamc(T ∗R3) such that φ(T ∗
KR3) and 0R3 have a clean inter-
section along a knot whose knot type is different from K?
Here, the knot type means the isotopy class of a knot in R3.

1.2.2
On enhanced A-polynomials
For a knot K, there is a polynomial AK ∈Z[λ, µ] associated to the character variety of
SL2(C)-representations of π1(R3\K), so called the A-polynomial of K. It is a classical knot
invariant introduced by [CCGLS94]. Ni-Zang [NZ17] considered a slight enhancement of
A-polynomial, which we denote by ˜AK. It is a polynomial in Z[λ, µ] defined by omitting
curves from reducible SL2(C)-representations of π1(R3 \ K).
Theorem 1.9 (Theorem 4.14). ˜AK0 is divisible by ˜AK1.
For any knot K, AK is the least common multiple of ˜AK and λ −1, so this theorem
implies that AK0 is divisible by AK1, which is already known by [SW06, Proposition 4.2].
For the proof of [SW06, Proposition 4.2], the surjectivity of a homomorphism between knot
groups is not important, while the surjectivity of k is essential for Theorem 1.9. We utilize
the fact that the pullback by k preserves the irreducibility of (SL2(C)-)representations.

1.5
Organization of paper
In Section 2, we give a geometric setting about clean Lagrangian intersections. We intro-
duce some geometric objects, such as the 3-manifold Mφ, and study their basic properties.
In Section 3, we apply microlocal sheaf theory to this setting. In Section 3.3, a cobordism
functor from [Li23] is introduced. From this functor, we construct in Section 3.4 a group
homomorphism h from π1(R3 \K0) to π1(Mφ). In Section 3.5, we prove the surjectivity of
h (Proposition 3.25), together with Proposition 3.26 about representations of π1(R3 \ K0)
and π1(Mφ). In Section 4, we see that the results in Section 3.5 give constraints on the
knot types of clean Lagrangian intersections in terms of knot groups, A-polynomials and
KCH-representations. In particular, combining with [Oka25b, Theorem 1.1], Theorem 1.4
is proved. In Section 5, we upgrade Proposition 3.26 to Theorem 1.3 by using 3-manifold
theory.
