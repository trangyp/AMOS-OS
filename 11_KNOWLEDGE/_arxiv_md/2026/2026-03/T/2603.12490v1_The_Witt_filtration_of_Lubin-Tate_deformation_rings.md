THE WITT FILTRATION OF LUBIN-TATE DEFORMATION RINGS
CHARLES REZK
Abstract. This note is a meditation on a generalization WE of the classical p-typical Witt vectors
Wp, which arises (geometrically) from isogenies of deformations of formal groups, or (topologically)
from the theory of power operations on Morava E-theory. For formal groups of height 1 we have
WE = Wp, but the WE are richer when height is ≥2. We show that Wp splits naturally from WE.
A key property of WE is the isomorphism π0E ≈WE(π0E/m), the “cofreeness of the Morava
E-theory” proved by Burklund, Schlank, and Yuan. This isomorphism determines a natural “Witt
filtration” on π0E. We describe how this Witt filtration interpolates between the p-adic filtration
and a geometric filtration on π0E/(p). We use this to give a new proof of cofreeness.
1. Introduction
In [LT66] Lubin and Tate identified the space of deformations of 1-dimensional commutative
formal groups of finite height h ≥1: given such a formal group Γ over a perfect field κ, its universal
deformation is defined over a ring O isomorphic to a power series ring Wpκ[[u1, . . . , uh−1]]. Morava
observed [Mor85] that there is a complex orientable cohomology theory associated to any such
universal deformation, now called Morava E-theory or Lubin-Tate theory, with the property
that π∗E ≈O[µ±] with |µ| = 2. Goerss, Hopkins, and Miller [GH04] proved that the representing
spectrum of any Morava E-theory admits an essentially unique commutative ring structure, and
that it functorial in formal groups. In particular, E admits an action by the automorphism group
of Γ/κ.
This commutative ring structure on E endows it (and its cohomology theory) with additional
structure, namely power operations, whose structure has been explored in depth [Str97], [Str98],
[AHS04], [Rez09]. In particular, as shown in [Rez09], if R ∈CAlgE(SpK(n)), then π0R ∈AlgT,
where the latter denotes the category of algebras over a certain monad T on the category CAlgO of
(ordinary) commutative O-algebras. As noted in [Rez09, 4.23], the forgetful functor AlgT →CAlgO
is both monadic and comonadic.
The goal of this note is to explore the underlying functor of this comonad, which we denote
WE : CAlgO →CAlgO and call the Lubin-Tate-Witt vectors, or more colloquially the E-typical
Witt vectors, to indicate its dependence on a choice of Morava E-theory. The name is chosen to
reflect a connection to the classical p-typical Witt vectors. It was introduced in [BSY22], where it is
denoted WT.
1.1. Example (Lubin-Tate-Witt vectors at height 1). Let Γ be a height 1 formal group law over κ,
and E its associated Morava E-theory spectrum. Then O = Wpκ, and there is an isomorphism of
functors WE ≈Wp, where Wp denotes the classical p-typical Witt vectors (restricted to CAlgWpκ).
Furthermore, the comonad structure on WE recovers the theory of δ-rings (O-algebras equipped
with a p-derivation) [Joy85].
A striking feature of the E-typical Witt vectors is the following result.
1.2. Theorem (Cofreeness of the Lubin-Tate ring [BSY22, Thm. 3.4]). The O-algebra structure
map O →WE(κ) is an isomorphism.

“Cofreeness” has interesting implications for the Lubin-Tate ring and Morava E-theory. The
E-typical Witt vector functor admits a canonical descending filtration WE ⊇I1 ⊇I2 ⊇· · · by

emphasize that the Witt filtration is functorial in the formal group: in particular it is compatible
with the action by Aut(Γ/κ) on O.
For formal groups of height h = 1, this is just p-adic filtration of the p-typical Witt vectors of a
perfect field: Id = pdWpκ. However, when h > 1 the Witt filtration on O is more subtle.
In fact, we will prove the following (as (12.2), (7.1) and (8.5), and using cofreeness).
Theorem. The Witt filtration {Id} of the Lubin-Tate ring O satisfies
• Id ∩Wpκ = pdWpκ,
• pId = Id+1 ∩pO,
