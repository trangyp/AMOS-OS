Abstract. We investigate translation actions of countable dense subgroups of non-unimodular locally com-
pact second countable (lcsc) groups. Using left-right actions, we show that the left translation action Γ ↷G
given by a countable dense subgroup Γ of a locally compact second countable group G can only be strongly
ergodic if G is almost unimodular. We show that the strong ergodicity of the action Γ ↷G for an almost
unimodular lcsc group G is equivalent to the strong ergodicity of Γ ∩ker(∆G) ↷ker(∆G), where ∆G is
the modular function. We demonstrate the absence of rigidity, by showing that non-isomorphic lcsc almost
unimodular groups can admit orbit equivalent translation actions.
Introduction
Defined first for actions of countable groups on a measure space [Sch80], a non-singular countable measurable
equivalence relation R on a standard probability space (X, µ) is called strongly ergodic if the relation does not
contain any non-trivial almost invariant sequences. An almost invariant sequence is a sequence of measurable
subsets An such that for all g ∈[R] (the full group of the equivalence relation), µ(An∆gAn) →0. A sequence
An is called trivial if µ(An)(1 −µ(An)) →0. Although not a complete match, strong ergodicity is closely
tied to fullness of von Neumann algebras. Houdayer-Marrakchi-Verraedt have shown that Connes’ structure
theorem for almost periodic weights on full von Neumann algebras [Con74, Lemma 4.8] has an analogue for
almost periodic measures on strongly ergodic equivalence relations [HMV19, Theorem D]. In Section 1, we
use their theorem to prove that if the left translation action of a countable dense subgroup Γ of a lcsc group
G is strongly ergodic, then G is almost unimodular. Following [GGN25], we call a locally compact group G
with modular function ∆G : G →R+ almost unimodular if ker ∆G is open. In the second countable case,
this is equivalent to ∆G(G) ̸= R+. We prove a slightly generalized version of the following in Theorem 1.2:
Theorem A. Suppose G is a lcsc group such that ∆G(G) = R+. Then, no left translation action of a
countable dense subgroup on G equipped with its left Haar measure is strongly ergodic.
For a non-unimodular, almost unimodular group G, given a countable dense subgroup Γ, it turns out that
the strong ergodicity of Γ ↷G is equivalent to the strong ergodicity of Γ ∩ker ∆G ↷ker(∆G). This reduces
the question of strong ergodicity for left actions to the unimodular case.
For strongly ergodic left translation actions Γ ↷G, rigidity phenomena have been observed (see [Fur11,
Ioa17]). The action Γ ↷G may “remember” the inclusion Γ < G in the sense that orbit equivalent actions
Γ ↷G and Λ ↷H imply the existence of a topological isomorphism δ : G →H satisfying δ(Γ) = Λ. The
current most general rigidity statement for non-compact groups is due to Ioana [Ioa17, Theorem A], which
asserts that when G is simply connected, a strongly ergodic action Γ ↷G remembers the inclusion Γ < G
in this way. Notice that G being simply connected and admitting a strongly ergodic left translation action
forces G to be unimodular. Given a non-unimodular almost unimodular lcsc group G, we show in Section 2
that for any strongly ergodic action by a countable dense subgroup Γ ↷G, there exists an orbit equivalent
action Λ ↷H such that ∆G(G) ̸= ∆H(H). However, in the case where the unimodular part G1 of G is

1. Strong Ergodicity, the Non-Unimodular Case
We recall certain notions of orbit equivalence. All actions are assumed to be non-singular.
Given two actions Γ ↷(G, µ) and Λ ↷(H, λ), the actions are called orbit equivalent (OE) if there exists
a non-singular Borel isomorphism of measure spaces θ : G →H such that for almost every x ∈G, “θ sends
orbits to orbits”:
θ(Γx) = Λθ(x).
The two actions are called stably orbit equivalent (SOE) if there are E ⊂G, F ⊂H two non-null sets such
that for almost every x ∈G, there exists a Borel isomorphism of measure spaces θ : E →F such that
θ(Γx ∩E) = Λθ(x) ∩F
To make our statements more concise, we define the notion of a countable dense inclusion.
Definition 1.1. For a lcsc group G, denote by mG its left Haar measure and µG a probability measure on
G equivalent to mG. Let Γ be a countable dense subgroup of G. The inclusion Γ < G is called a countable
dense inclusion. The left translation action of Γ on the measure space (G, µG) is called a countable dense
action.

countable dense actions are ergodic. For the convenience of the online researcher, we provide an argument.

Example 3.2. Consider G = GLn(R)+ ⋊Rn for some n ≥2, the group of orientation preserving affine
transformations on Rn. G is connected. Let Γ be a countable dense subgroup of G containing SLn(Z). Since
SL2(Z) (and hence Γ) contains the free group on two generators, Γ is not amenable. By the previous lemma
and Theorem 1.2, the countable dense action Γ ↷G is neither amenable nor strongly ergodic. Moreover,
mG indudces an infinite trace. Hence, the von Neumann algebra L(Γ ↷G) is a non-injective non-full factor
of type II∞. Given t ∈R+, there exists g ∈G with ∆G(g) = t. Let U : L2(R) →L2(R) be defined as

Example 3.3. Using the setup of the previous example, let Λ be any countable subgroup of G. Consider the
left-right equivalence relation R given by x ∼γxλ for γ ∈Γ and λ ∈Λ. Since Γ ↷Λ is a countable dense
action, ker(δmG) is ergodic. Therefore by the proof of [HMV19, Lemma 5.6], the asymptotic ratio set of R, mG

Λ, one can obtain a von Neumann algebra M of all subtypes IIIλ for λ ∈(0, 1]. M contains L(Γ ↷G) with
expectation and hence is non-injective. Moreover by Theorem 1.2, the von Neumann algebra is not full.
