THE IMAGE OF FUNCTOR MORPHING
EHUD MEIR
ABSTRACT. Functor morphing provides a method to translate complex representations of automorphism groups
of finite modules over finite rings to representations of automorphism groups of functors in some abelian category.
In this paper we give an explicit criterion for a representation to be in the image of functor morphing using the
action of parabolic subgroups. We then demonstrate this criterion on Borel groups of finite fields.
