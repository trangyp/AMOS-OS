1.3. Series context. This paper is the first in the series Persistent Heuristics. The Lorentzian

genuine invariants: the spacelike property holds for any weight satisfying mild decay condi-
tions (Section 13.4), and the density bounds depend on the weight only through computable
constants.

4.2. The Grand Simplicity Hypothesis.

sub-case.
