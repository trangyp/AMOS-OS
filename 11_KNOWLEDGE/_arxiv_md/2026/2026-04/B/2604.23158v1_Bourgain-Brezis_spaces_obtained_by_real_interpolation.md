Bourgain-Brezis spaces obtained by real
interpolation
Eduard Curc˘a ∗
Abstract
In 2002, Bourgain and Brezis proved that for the space X = W 1,d (on Td, with d ≥2) we
have the equality of images
div(L∞∩X) = div X,
(∗)
i.e., given a vector field v ∈X there exists a vector field u ∈L∞∩X such that div u = div v.
In this paper we show that if X is a function space satisfying (∗) then, any real
interpolation space Xθ,q = (L∞,X)θ,q (where θ ∈(0,1) and q ∈[1,∞)) also satisfies (∗). The
proof is based on a general method that allows us to interpolate solutions of linear equations.

Introduction
Let X be a Banach function space on Td, where d ≥2. We say that X is a Bourgain-Brezis space
(BB space, for short) if for any vector field v ∈X there exists another vector field u ∈L∞∩X
such that we have
div u = div v,
(1)
in the sense of distributions on Td. In short, we can express this as the equality of images
div(L∞∩X) = div X.
(2)
In other words, if X is a BB space, the divergence operator does not make distinction between
X and the usually smaller space L∞∩X.
By using an involved construction Bourgain and Brezis ([3], 2002) proved that the Sobolev
space X = W 1,d is a BB space. They also provided a much simpler argument (based on duality;
see [3, Section 4]) for the fact that X = H1 = W 1,2 is a BB space, when d = 2. Later, using a
simple duality argument, Mazya ([15], 2007) has shown that the Sobolev space X = Hd/2 is a BB
space, for any d ≥2. These simple arguments1 are, however, based on the spectral structure of
the spaces Hd/2 and do not seem to be appropriate in the case of other types of spaces.
On the other hand, the constructive arguments of Bourgain and Brezis (see [3] and [4]) have
been generalized to other critical function spaces like the Triebel-Lizorkin scale F d/p,p
q
(see [5] and
[6]).

In this paper we use a similar approach that is adapted to the real interpolation method
instead of the complex one. Our main result is the following:
Theorem 2. Let X be a BB space on Td such that (L∞,X) is an interpolation couple, L∞∩X
has a separable predual and X ↪H−m, for some m > d/2. Then, for any θ ∈(0,1), q ∈[1,∞) the
real interpolation space Xθ,q ∶= (L∞,X)θ,q is a BB space.
Theorem 2 gives us a method for constructing new BB spaces from old. In particular, com-
bining Theorem 2 with the result of Mazya that Hd/2 is a BB space, we get the following result.
Corollary 3. For any θ ∈(0,1), q ∈[1,∞) the real interpolation space Xθ,q ∶= (L∞,Hd/2)θ,q is a
BB space.

solution belongs to the smaller space L∞∩(L∞,Hd/2)θ,q. The result is also non-trivial in the sense
that (see Section 3.1)
(L∞,Hd/2)θ,q /↪L∞.
