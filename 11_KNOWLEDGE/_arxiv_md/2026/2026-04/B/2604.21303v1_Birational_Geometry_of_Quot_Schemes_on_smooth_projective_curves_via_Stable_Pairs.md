PROJECTIVE CURVES VIA STABLE PAIRS
CHANDRANANDAN GANGOPADHYAY AND ATSUSHI ITO
Abstract. Let C be a smooth projective curve of genus g ≥2 over C, and let E0 be a vector
bundle on C. We investigate the birational geometry of the Quot scheme QuotC(E0, k, n),
which parametrizes quotients of E0 of rank k and degree n, and its fiber QL over Picn(C)
for n ≫0. Our main tool is the moduli space of stable pairs, which yields small Q-factorial
modifications (SQMs) of QuotC(E0, k, n) and QL. We explicitly describe the nef, movable,
and effective cones of each SQM. Consequently, we prove that QL is a Mori dream space and
that the determinant morphism QuotC(E0, k, n) →Picn(C) is a Mori dream morphism.

1. Introduction
Let C be a smooth projective curve over the field of complex numbers C. Let E0 be a vector
bundle on C of rank r and degree e. For integers 0 ≤k ≤r−1 and n, let QuotC(E0, k, n) be
the Quot scheme parametrizing quotients of E0 of rank k and degree n. The Quot scheme has
played a fundamental role in the construction and study of moduli spaces of vector bundles
(see e.g. [DN89], [PR03], [BGL94], [MO07a]). Since the Quot scheme for E0 = Or
C can be
regarded as a compactification of the space of morphisms from C to the Grassmannian, it
has been extremely useful in Gromov–Witten theory (see e.g. [BDW96], [Ber97], [MO07b],
[Opr06]).
The geometry of these Quot schemes has been studied extensively, in particular the case of
QP1 := QuotP1(Or
P1, k, n). If k = r−1 or n = 0, QP1 is a projective space or a Grassmannian,
respectively. If 0 ≤k ≤r −2 and n > 0, Strømme in [Str87] showed that QP1 is a smooth
projective variety with Pic(QP1) = Z2 and the nef cone Nef(QP1) is generated by two globally
generated line bundles. In [Jow12] and [Ven11], the effective cone Eff(QP1) and the movable
cone Mov(QP1) were computed respectively. In [Ito17], the birational geometry of QP1 was
studied and it was shown that QP1 is a Mori dream space. Recall that a normal projective
variety X is called a Mori dream space if
1) X is Q-factorial and Pic(X) ⊗Q = N 1(X)Q,
2) Nef(X) is spanned by finitely many semiample line bundles,
3) there exist finitely many small Q-factorial modifications (SQMs) fi : X 99K Xi such
that each Xi satisfies 2) and Mov(X) = S
i f ∗
i (Nef(Xi)).
Mori dream spaces were introduced by Hu and Keel in [HK00] and they have nice properties
from the perspective of the minimal model program. In [Ito17], it was further shown that
all the small Q-factorial modifications of QP1 have moduli-theoretic descriptions.
For C with genus g ≥2, the Quot scheme Q := QuotC(E0, k, n) can be reducible and
non-reduced. However, for n ≫0, Q is known to be irreducible and generically smooth for
E0 = Or
C by [BDW96], and for arbitrary E0 by [PR03]. Furthermore, Q is locally factorial
and its Picard group was computed for n ≫0 by [GS24].
