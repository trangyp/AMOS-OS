BRANE QUANTIZATION AND SYZ MIRROR SYMMETRY
KWOKWAI CHAN, NAICHUNG CONAN LEUNG, QIN LI, YAT-HIN SUEN AND YUTUNG YAU
Abstract. Coisotropic A-branes were introduced by Kapustin–Orlov to enlarge the Fukaya
category of a symplectic manifold in a way that aligns with predictions from homological
mirror symmetry. From a mathematical perspective, however, the categorical framework
governing such branes remains largely undeveloped. On the other hand, Gukov–Witten’s
brane quantization suggests that a holomorphic deformation quantization of a holomorphic
symplectic manifold X arises from the endomorphism algebra HomA(Bcc, Bcc) of a canonical
coisotropic A-brane Bcc, which naturally acts on the morphism space HomA(B, Bcc) with a
Lagrangian A-brane B that in turn gives precisely the geometric quantization of B.
In this paper, we consider a holomorphic symplectic manifold X which admits an SYZ
fibration and apply SYZ mirror symmetry to study its brane quantization. Given any semi-
affine, space-filling coisotropic A-brane Bcc on X, we construct the mirror B-brane ˇBcc on
the mirror manifold ˇ
X by an SYZ transform. We then present a mathematical definition
of the endomorphism algebra HomA(Bcc, Bcc) by constructing a distinguished non-formal
holomorphic deformation quantization of X. Using a twisted family Toeplitz construction, we
transform HomA(Bcc, Bcc) to the mirror B-side and prove that this induces an isomorphism
HomA(Bcc, Bcc) ∼= HomB( ˇBcc, ˇBcc) between the endomorphism algebras. Furthermore, taking
any torus fiber of X as the Lagrangian A-brane B, we fully realize Gukov–Witten’s proposal,
namely, there is a natural action of HomA(Bcc, Bcc) on HomA(B, Bcc) which is precisely mirror
to the natural action on the mirror B-side. This provides a mathematical framework which
is compatible with Gukov–Witten’s brane quantization proposal, SYZ mirror symmetry as
well as family Floer theory.
1. Introduction
1.1. Background and the main result.
Kontsevich’s homological mirror symmetry [27] asserts that the Fukaya category Fuk(X, ω)
of a symplectic manifold (X, ω) is quasi-equivalent to the derived category Db( ˇX, ˇJ) of co-
herent sheaves of its mirror complex manifold ( ˇX, ˇJ). However, Kapustin–Orlov [25] observed
that for some special symplectic structure, the Fukaya category of (X, ω) could be smaller
than Db( ˇX, ˇJ). An algebraic way to resolve this asymmetry is to add more objects by taking
the split closure of Fuk(X, ω), as was done in e.g., [2, 22, 42]. A more geometric approach
was proposed by Kapustin–Orlov [25], who suggested that coisotropic submanifolds, not just
Lagrangian submanifolds, should be considered as objects in the Fukaya category. These new
objects are called coisotropic A-branes. Nevertheless, a definition of morphism spaces between
coisotropic A-branes remains elusive, despite much effort [3, 8, 18, 24, 33, 36, 37, 38, 45].
On the other hand, Gukov–Witten [21] introduced the notion of a canonical coisotropic
A-brane of a symplectic manifold (X, ω). This is a space-filling brane, meaning that it is a
coisotropic A-brane Bcc supported on the whole X, and it is equipped with a U(1)-line bundle
(L, ∇L) over X such that I := ω−1F∇L defines a complex structure on X; here −2π√−1F∇L
is the curvature 2-form of the U(1)-connection ∇L. We thus obtain a structure sheaf OX and
a Dolbeault operator ¯∂I on X. Also, the 2-form ΩX := F∇L + √−1ω defines a holomorphic
symplectic structure on (X, I). Gukov and Witten proposed that

In Section 2, we review the notion of coisotropic A-branes and in particular the semi-affine
condition introduced in [17]. In Section 3, we construct a non-formal Moyal-type holomorphic
star product on OX[[ℏ]]. We also present three concrete examples (including a non-K¨ahler
