Abstract
We study the minimality of nˆn Hermitian matrices A respect to
a C˚-subalgebra B of MnpCq in the spectral norm, that is
}A} ď }A ` B},
for every B P B.
We generalize the notion of the moment of a subspace and relate it
to the joint numerical range and the subdifferentials of the maximum
eigenvalue. We extend results previously known for the subalgebra of
diagonal operators and describe the subdifferential of the maximum
eigenvalue in terms of the moment of the corresponding eigenspace.
We also characterize B-minimality via moments and subdifferentials,
and provide examples.
