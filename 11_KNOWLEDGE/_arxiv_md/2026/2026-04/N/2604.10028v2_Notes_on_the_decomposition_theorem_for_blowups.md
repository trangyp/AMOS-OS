In this section, we prove that the decomposition isomorphism (1) is equivariant
with respect to the (universal) Hodge group, as suggested by Katzarkov-Kontsevich-

First, we recall the Hodge group (also known as the special Mumford-Tate group)
associated with a Q-Hodge structure (see e.g. [2]). Let H be a Q-Hodge structure;
that is, a finite-dimensional rational vector space H equipped with a decomposition
