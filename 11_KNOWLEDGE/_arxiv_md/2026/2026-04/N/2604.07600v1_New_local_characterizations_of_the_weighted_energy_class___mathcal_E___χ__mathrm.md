New local characterizations of the weighted energy class Eχ,loc(Ω)
Hoang Nhat Quy
1. abstract
Let Ω⊂Cn be a hyperconvex domain and let χ : R−→R+ be a de-
creasing function. This note studies the local weighted energy class Eχ,loc(Ω)
introduced in [16].
We establish two main results on local membership in this class. First, we
prove a new local boundedness property for the weighted Monge–Amp`ere
energy: if u ∈PSH−(Ω) admits suitable local majorants in Eχ,loc near the
boundary of every relatively compact hyperconvex subdomain D ⋐Ω, then
the weighted energy
R
K χ(u)(ddcu)n remains locally finite for every compact
set K ⊂D. This gives the first explicit local control of the energy functional
and is new even in the unweighted setting.
Second, we obtain a substantial improvement concerning the local control
of the Monge–Amp`ere measure. We show that if, in addition to the boundary
condition, (ddcu)n is locally dominated by (ddcw)n for some w ∈Eχ,loc(D)
inside D, then u ∈Eχ,loc(D). This domination condition is strictly weaker
than the previous requirement of local finiteness of the weighted energy,
thereby significantly enlarging the class of admissible functions.
Our results extend and refine the local theory developed in [22, 23] and
provide a more flexible framework for plurisubharmonic functions with pos-
sible singularities on compact subsets.
Keywords: plurisubharmonic functions, Cegrell classes, weighted energy
classes, local classes, complex Monge–Amp`ere operator, local energy bound-
edness, measure domination.
2. Introduction
Let Ωbe a hyperconvex domain in Cn. Denote by PSH(Ω) the set of

plurisubharmonic functions. Bedford and Taylor, in their fundamental works

defined on the class of locally bounded plurisubharmonic functions. In [9],
Cegrell introduced the classes E0(Ω), Fp(Ω) and Ep(Ω) on which the complex
Monge–Amp`ere operator is well defined. Later, in [10], Cegrell introduced
the classes F(Ω) and E(Ω) consisting of plurisubharmonic functions for
which the operator (ddc·)n is well defined and continuous under decreasing

that a class K(Ω) ⊂PSH−(Ω) is said to be local if φ ∈K(Ω) implies

The proofs of Theorems 1.4 and 1.5 in [1] show that every function φ ∈
Eχ(Ω) satisfies limz→ξ φ(z) = 0 for all ξ ∈∂Ω. Consequently, if φ ∈Eχ(Ω)
and D ⋐Ωis a relatively compact hyperconvex subdomain, then φ /∈Eχ(D).
Thus Eχ(Ω) is not a local class. The main result of [16] establishes that
Eχ,loc(Ω) is indeed a local class. In [22], the notions of local classes of type
1 and type 2 were introduced, and it was shown that E0(Ω), F(Ω) and
Eχ(Ω) are local classes of type 2. Moreover, Theorem 3.8 in [22] provides
an explicit construction of Eχ,loc(Ω). In [23], some local characterizations of
the existence of solutions to the complex Monge–Amp`ere equation in the
classes Eχ(Ω) and Eχ,loc(Ω) were obtained (Theorem 3, 4).
The main purpose of this note is to establish several local conditions on
a function u ∈PSH−(Ω) ensuring that u belongs to the class Eχ,loc(Ω).
