ABSTRACT
We establish isomorphism ranges for the comparison maps between algebraic and
topological K-groups, extending classical Quillen-Lichtenbaum conjecture to separated
complex schemes of finite type after refinement. Additionally, we generalizes the
conjecture through the lens of noncommutative geometry.

K-Theory, Category theory

INTRODUCTION
The Quillen-Lichtenbaum conjecture [PW01, Theorem 4.1] compares the algebraic
K-theory and topological K-theory of smooth separated complex schemes of finite type.
Theorem 0.1 (Quillen-Lichtenbaum conjecture).
Let 𝑋is a smooth separated scheme over C of finite type and 𝑚≥1 be an integer. Then
the canonical map
𝐾𝑛(𝑋,Z/𝑚Z) →𝐾𝑈−𝑛(𝑋,Z/𝑚Z)
is an isomorphism for 𝑛≥dim 𝑋−1 and a monomorphism for 𝑛= dim 𝑋−2.
The Quillen-Lichtenbaum Conjecture is now a consequence of the Bloch-Kato
conjecture (Readers can see [FHW04] for details), which has been proved by Voevodsky
[Voe08]. Hence, it is already a theorem.
