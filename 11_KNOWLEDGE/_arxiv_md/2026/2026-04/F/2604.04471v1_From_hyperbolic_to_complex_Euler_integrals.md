FROM HYPERBOLIC TO COMPLEX EULER INTEGRALS
N. M. BELOUSOV, G. A. SARKISSIAN, AND V. P. SPIRIDONOV
Abstract. Hyperbolic hypergeometric integrals are defined as Barnes-type integrals of
products of hyperbolic gamma functions. Their reduction to ordinary hypergeometric func-
tions is well known.
We study in detail their degeneration to complex hypergeometric
functions. Namely, using uniform bounds on the integrands, we prove that the univariate
hyperbolic beta integral and the conical function degenerate to two-dimensional integrals
over the complex plane.
