ABSTRACT. We define a Fr´echet topology on the space C∞(X)[[¯h]] of formal smooth func-
tions on a symplectic manifold X, by constructing a sequence of semi-norms on it. For any
star product ⋆on C∞(X)[[¯h]] making it a formal deformation quantization of X, we will
show that the quantum product ⋆is jointly continuous, and making it a Fr´echet algebra.

cally dense in all formal smooth functions. We will also show that the canonical trace of
any formal deformation quantization is continuous under this Fr´echet topology.

1. INTRODUCTION
A symplectic manifold X is the mathematical description of the phase space of a clas-
sical mechanical system, whose classical observable algebra is modeled by smooth func-
tions C∞(X). Deformation quantization of C∞(X) models the quantum observable algebra
of the corresponding quantum mechanical system. There are two types of deformation
quantization of a symplectic manifold: strict and formal deformation.
A formal deformation quantization of a symplectic (K¨ahler) manifold denotes a star
product ⋆on the space C∞(X)[[¯h]] which deforms the classical commutative product on
smooth functions as
(1.1)
f ⋆g = f g + ∑
i≥1
¯hi · Ci( f, g).
It is required that C1( f, g) −C1(g, f ) = { f, g}, which implies that the first order non-
commutativity of ⋆is the Poisson bracket. For general Ci(−, −)’s, they are usually re-
quired to be bi-differential operators, as we do in this paper. The formal variable ¯h corre-
sponds to Planck’s constant in physics. From a physical point view, such a star product
only works when convergence of the series in equation (1.1) is satisfied for the evaluation
of ¯h at the Planck’s constant. On the other hand, the strict deformation quantization intro-
duced by Rieffel in [12] uses the framework of C∗algebras. Instead of a formal variable,
here ¯h belongs to an additional R parametrizing star products ⋆¯h which are required to
depend continuously on ¯h.
It is believed that there are close relations between these two types of deformation
quantizations. For instance, the formal star product should be the asymptotic expansion
of the product of C∗algebras in strict quantization. Conversely, subalgebras of C∞(X) for
which the power series (1.1) converges for small values ¯h > 0 can recover (part of) the in-
formation of strict quantization. To understand this relation, Waldmann et al. introduced
in [1, 2] a Fr´echet algebraic deformation quantization which is intermediate between the
formal and strict quantization, and considered a Fr´echet topology on certain subclass of
functions on which the formal star product is convergent for small ¯h > 0. See also [11] for
the study of convergence of formal deformation quantization.
Although the convergence issue for formal deformation quantization of smooth func-
tions and strict quantization are important questions, it is also essential to consider quan-
tization of formal functions C∞(X)[[¯h]]. Here we describe an example in geometric quan-
tization. Recall that the Hilbert spaces in the Kostant-Souriau geometric quantization
of K¨ahler manifolds are given by holomorphic sections of the (tensor powers of) pre-
quantum line bundles L:
Hk := H0

For a subclass of functions with a formal variable ¯h, we can quantize them to holomorphic
differential operators on the Hilbert space Hk where ¯h takes the evaluation ¯h = 1/k. An

formal function f + ¯h · ∆f acts on Hk for all k > 0 via the associated Kostant-Souriau
operator. Thus it is necessary to keep this formal variable ¯h. In the K¨ahler geometry

are dense in all formal smooth functions with respect to the Fr´echet topology.

invariant is the trace of these formal functions which is uniquely defined (see Definition
4.1). To justify that this Fr´echet topology on C∞(X)[[¯h]] is appropriate, we will show the
continuity of the (renormalized) trace with respect to the Fr´echet topology by applying
the Batalin-Vilkovisky quantization method developed in [9].

function of a one-dimensional Chern-Simons model, and the proof is based on explicit
computation via Feynman graph weights and the integral of a differential form, which at
each point x0 ∈X depend on the jets of f and the curvature (and its covariant derivatives)
of X.

2. DEFORMATION QUANTIZATION AS A FR´ECHET ALGEBRA
There have been tremendous studies on deformation quantization of symplectic mani-
folds, most of which focus on its geometric construction ([5]), algebraic properties includ-
ing the trace of these quantum algebras ([6–8,10]), or its description as certain operators
on Hilbert spaces, for instance deformation quantization on K¨ahler (and also symplec-
tic) manifolds via Berezin-Toeplitz operators. There are also studies on the covergence of
these quantum algebras for small enough evaluation of ¯h ([11]). Here we still regard ¯h as
a formal variable, and define a Fr´echet topology on C∞(X)[[¯h]] which is compatible with
any star product ⋆making (C∞(X)[[¯h]], ⋆) a formal deformation quantization.
This Fr´echet topology on C∞(X)[[¯h]] is defined via a sequence of semi-norms {|| −
||¯h,k : k ≥0} which are roughly given by a sum of the suprema of derivatives of a fixed
order. It turns out that for any star product ⋆on C∞(X)[[¯h]], the multiplication operation
( f, g) 7→f ⋆g is jointly continuous thus making (C∞(X)[[¯h]], ⋆) a Fr´echet algebra.
2.1. Fr´echet spaces.
There are several equivalent definition of Fr´echet spaces, we will adopt the approach
via a countable family of semi-norms which is convenient for the space of formal smooth
functions. We first recall the notion of semi-norms:
Definition 2.1. A non-negative function p on a vector space V satisfying the following
conditions is called a semi-norm:
(1) For any scalar λ and v ∈V, there is p(λ · v) = |λ| · p(v);
(2) For all v1, v2 ∈V, there is p(v1 + v2) ≤p(v1) + p(v2);
(3) For any v ∈V, there is p(v) ≥0.
A simple observation is that suppose p1, p2 are two semi-norms, there sum p1 + p2 is
also a semi-norm.
Definition 2.2. Suppose on a vector space V, there are a countable family of seminorms
{|| · ||k}k∈Z≥0 satisfying the following two properties:
• Suppose x ∈V, and ||x||k = 0 for all k ≥0, then x = 0;
• If a sequence {xn} in V which is Cauchy with respect to each semi-norm || · ||k,
then there exists x ∈V, such that {xn} converges to x with respect to each semi-
norm || · ||k.
