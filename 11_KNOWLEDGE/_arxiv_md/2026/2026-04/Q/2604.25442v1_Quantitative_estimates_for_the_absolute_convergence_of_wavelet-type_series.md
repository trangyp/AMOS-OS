The study of RC and UC multipliers for classical orthonormal systems is a longstanding
topic in the theory of orthogonal series. It is well known that the trigonometric, Walsh,
Haar and Franklin systems are convergence system, i.e. the sequence w(n) ≡1 is a
Weyl multiplier for those systems. However, w(n) ≡1 fails to be an RC-multiplier for
these systems. A. N. Kolmogorov [17] was the first to observe this phenomenon for the
trigonometric system, although he did not publish a proof of this fact. A proof was later
provided by Z. Zahorski [41]. Afterward, developing A. Zahorski’s argument, P. L. Ul′yanov
[34,35] established analogous results for Haar and Walsh systems. Using techniques based
on the Haar system, A. M. Olevskii [25] proved that this phenomenon is, in fact, typical
for arbitrary complete orthonormal systems. Later, P. L. Ul′yanov [33,36,37] determined
the optimal growth of RC and UC multipliers for the Haar system.
Theorem A (Ul′yanov, [36, 37]). The sequence log n is an RC-multiplier for the Haar
system. Conversely, any nondecreasing sequence w(n) satisfying w(n) = o(log n) is not
an RC-multiplier for the Haar system.
