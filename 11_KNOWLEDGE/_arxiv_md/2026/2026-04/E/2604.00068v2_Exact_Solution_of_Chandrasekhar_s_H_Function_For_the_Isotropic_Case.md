Abstract: In this work, we obtained the exact solution of Chandrasekhar’s H function with 
isotropic scattering. The H function is a nonlinear integral equation that has long kept 
scientists busy in the field of radiative transfer theory. In this study, we first obtained the 
differential form of the integral equation because the solution would be easy. We used the 
classical integral technique to construct the differential form. By solving the differential 
equation we obtained exact solution of the H function. We compared the numerical results of 
exact solution with the numerical results obtained by Chandrasekhar in his book. We 
introduced them in the table to see the differences. 
Keywords: Radiative Transfer; Integral Equations; Chandrasekhar H Function; Moments of 
H Function; Light Scattering in the Atmosphere of Planets 
1. Introduction 
       As will be explained in detail in the next section, the H Function is an important function 
for radiative transfer in the atmosphere of planets (Chandrasekhar 1960) and its solution is a 
necessity. The fact that the equation is a nonlinear integral equation makes the solution very 
difficult. So far, no exact analytical solution of the H function has been found.  In cases where 
an exact analytical solution cannot be found, numerical solutions must be sought. What kind 
of approach will be used and what kind of method will be applied in solving this type of 
nonlinear equations is also an important issue.  
 
     Approximate solutions of the H function are realized by proposing an approximate 
analytical function and defining an algorithm that performs fast solution of the integral 
equation representing the H function (Jablonski 2015). Seeking an analytical solution was 
carried out using the mean value theorem for the integral in the integral equation and 
reasonable results have been obtained (Vukanic 2007). The H function and its moments are 
calculated with the help of a computer algorithm that gives compact, fast, reliable and high-
digit numerical results (Kawabata 2022). A new approach using shifted Legendre polynomials 
is applied for the numerical solution of the nonlinear integral equation for the Chandrasekhar 
H-function for isotropic scattering and the results are in good agreement with the literature 
and the first derivative of the H-function is also given numerically (Anlı and Öztürk 2021). 
By using an exponential approach to the integral equation, a new integral equation similar to 
the H function was obtained and both the solution of the H function and its moments were

tabulated to describe light reflected from the surface of celestial bodies and to understand the 
radiation transport in spheres (Hapke 1981; Hulst 1980). In conclusion, thousands of articles 
have been written on this subject, but none have been found to have a complete solution.  
      In the second part, we obtained the differential equation of the H function by a very 
comprehensive analysis on the integral equation of the H function. In the third part, we 
presented the solution of the differential equation by two different methods to verify the 
accuracy of the processes performed. In the last section, we presented the results we obtained 
and made some explanations.
