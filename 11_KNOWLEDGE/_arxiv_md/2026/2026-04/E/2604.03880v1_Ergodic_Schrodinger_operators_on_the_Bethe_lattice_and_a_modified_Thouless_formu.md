ERGODIC SCHR¨ODINGER OPERATORS ON THE BETHE LATTICE
AND A MODIFIED THOULESS FORMULA
PETER D. HISLOP AND CHRISTOPH A. MARX
Abstract. The main result of this paper is a modiﬁed Thouless formula relating the
density of states for ergodic Schr¨odinger operators on the Bethe lattice to the Lyapunov
exponent. The modiﬁed Thouless formula consists of a Thouless-like term, involving
the density of states, and a remainder term. The remainder term vanishes when the
connectivity κ equals one, yielding the usual Thouless formula for ergodic Schr¨odinger
operators on Z. We prove the remainder term is nontrivial for κ ě 2. We also discuss
the automorphism group of the Bethe lattice and its relation to ergodic Schr¨odinger
operators. In particular, we clarify the use of the multiparameter noncommutative
ergodic theorem in evaluating the limit of Green’s functions along certain paths.

1. Introduction
We study ergodic Schr¨odinger operators on the Bethe lattice B “ pV, Eq with
vertices V, edges E, and connectivity κ ě 2. The Bethe lattice (also known as the
inﬁnite Cayley tree) is a regular tree in which each each vertex is connected to κ ` 1
neighboring vertices, where κ ě 2 is called the connectivity. The constant degree κ`1 of
each vertex is also known as the coordination number of the Bethe lattice in recognition
of the radial structure (coordination spheres or levels) that arises when designating one
vertex as the root of the tree; see Figure 1. For the sake of consistency, we mention
that we used the coordination number in our previous papers [15, 16] but decided to
use the connectivity here because κ is more directly related to the spectrum of the free
Laplacian on B, given by the closed interval r´2?κ, 2?κs. Since the main result of this
paper generalizes results for Schr¨odinger operators on Z to Schr¨odinger operators on B,
we note that Z can be viewed as a trivial case of the Bethe lattice if one admits κ “ 1.
The Bethe lattice B is a regular tree graph so that each pair of distinct vertices is
connected by a unique path; or equivalently, so that there are no closed loops. The tree
structure leads to a recursive nature which is a feature shared with Schr¨odinger operators
on Z. In stark contrast to Z, the Bethe lattice, however, exhibits hyperbolic geometry:
ﬁnite graph approximations, obtained by restricting to a ﬁnite number of levels, are

For the case of random Schr¨odinger operators on B with identically distributed
(iid) random potentials, absolutely continuous spectrum is known to persist for non-
zero potentials (disorder) [17, 18] (see also [14, 3]). This is in stark contrast to the
spectral behavior for random Schr¨odinger operators on Z. Any non-zero disorder de-
stroys the absolutely continuous spectrum and leads to spectral and dynamical localiza-
tion throughout the spectrum [4, Chapter 10]. Concerning the weak disorder behavior of
random Schr¨odinger operators on B with iid potentials, we mention the results by Acosta
and Klein [7] on the analyticity of the density of states for probability measures close to
Cauchy, and results on the nature of the spectrum by Klein [18], with generalizations
in [19], which established that for weak disorder, absolutely continuous spectrum per-
sists in the interior of the spectrum of the discrete Laplacian E P r´2?κ, 2?κs. In [5],
Aizenman and Warzel developed techniques (”resonant delocalization criterion”) which,
contrary to earlier expectations, provided strong evidence for purely absolutely contin-
uous spectrum across the entire spectrum for suﬃciently weak disorder and bounded
potentials. For unbounded potentials, their criterion for resonant delocalization implies
the persistence of some absolutely continuous spectrum for 2?κ ă |E| ă κ ` 1 in the
weak disorder regime.
