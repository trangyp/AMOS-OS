Abstract
We study deformations of the mixed associator which is part of the data of a module
category M over a k-linear monoidal category C for a field k. We set up a cohomology
theory H•
mix(M) whose coefficients are C-module endofunctors.
The underlying com-
plexes control deformations of mixed associators, C-module functors and their obstruc-
tions. We show that they are isomorphic to the Davydov–Yetter (DY) complex of the
representation functor ρ : C →End(M). Using our previous results on DY cohomology
[arXiv:2411.19111], we prove for any finite tensor category C and any finite C-module cat-
egory M that the cohomology groups H•
mix(M) are isomorphic to the relative Ext groups
Ext•
Z(C),C(1, AM) for the usual adjunction of the forgetful functor from the Drinfeld center
Z(C) to C, where AM is the so-called adjoint algebra of the module category M. This
allows us to give a dimension formula for Hn
mix(M) in terms of certain Hom spaces in
Z(C), and also to show that every mixed associator deformation of the regular module
category M = C is trivial. We also show that the algebra AM is the “full center” of an
algebra in C realizing M. We furthermore prove a generalized version of Ocneanu rigidity
for monoidal functors with coefficients, and provide its application to general (non-exact
and non-finite) C-module categories over a fusion category C such that dim(C) ̸= 0. As an
illustration, we spell out these results for module categories defined by finite-dimensional
comodule algebras over finite-dimensional Hopf algebras. Examples based on comodule
algebras over Sweedler’s Hopf algebra are worked out in detail; in particular we obtain
new continuous families of inequivalent non-exact module categories.

Introduction
A module category M over a monoidal category C is the categorified version of the notion of a
module M over an algebra A. The latter is defined by an “action map” · : A × M →M such
that a·(a′·v) = (aa′)·v and 1A·v = v. A standard procedure categorifies maps by functors while
equalities are categorified by natural isomorphisms which have to satisfy further compatibility
conditions. A module category is thus defined by an “action functor” ▷: C×M →M together
with a natural isomorphism m having components of the form
mX,Y,M : X ▷(Y ▷M) →(X ⊗Y ) ▷M

and called mixed associator, which is required to satisfy the obvious coherence diagram (see
§2.1.1). We also require that IdM = 1 ▷−, and m behaves well with this condition. The goal
of this paper is to introduce the deformation theory of such mixed associators and to provide
a computational tool for it which is based on relative homological algebra. For simplicity we
assume everywhere that C is strict monoidal.
There are several reasons to study such deformations. In the same way the study of modules
is necessary to understand the structure of rings and algebras, module categories lead to a deeper
understanding of monoidal categories. The class of exact module categories is particularly well
understood [EGNO]; it leads, for example, to the notion of Morita equivalent finite tensor
categories. It was moreover shown [CMZ] that exact module categories over a finite tensor
category C is the same as semi-simple algebras in C, and in many interesting cases there are
even classification results [EO, Mom, NSS]. In contrast, non-exact finite module categories
are poorly understood due to absence of appropriate techniques, despite their importance in
studying extensions of exact module categories.
In this context, it is remarkable that our
methods provide new deformation techniques applicable to any finite module category, not
necessarily exact, notably Thm. 3.10 together with the continuous family of examples of non-
exact module categories constructed thanks to Prop. 5.9.
Module categories also naturally appear in applications in mathematical physics. A subclass
of exact module categories, known as pivotal, describes boundary conditions for 3d topological
field theories of Turaev-Viro type [FSV]. It has been known for a long time [FRS] that objects
in exact module categories over modular fusion categories describe boundary conditions in 2d
rational conformal field theories. There is a strong indication that this structure generalizes
to non-semisimple or logarithmic theories: an object in a pivotal module category M over
a modular finite tensor category should still describe a boundary condition, while inner Hom
objects describe boundary fields, and the mixed associator of M is responsible for the boundary
OPEs. We refer to [FS, §2.2] for a more detailed discussion. Our results are thus also first
steps towards an algebraic deformation theory of 2d conformal field theories via their boundary
OPEs.
Let k be a field. In all the sequel we assume that C and M are k-linear and that the
monoidal product ⊗: C × C →C and the action ▷: C × M →M are k-bilinear bifunctors.
Given an integer N ≥1 and a formal variable h, it is natural to define a deformation of
the mixed associator m of order N as an expression m = m + PN
i=1 fihi where each fi is a
natural transformation of the form (1), such that m satisfies the defining properties of a mixed
associator over k[h]/⟨hN+1⟩; see §2.3.2 for the precise definition. The important case N = 1

categories C, M and the action ▷are not deformed; we only extend their scalars to k[h]/⟨hN+1⟩

(DY) cohomology, and to further use the adjunction theorem for DY cohomology [FGS2, §2].

Let ρ : C →Endk(M) be the representation functor, defined by ρ(X) = X ▷−. The
category Endk(M) is monoidal, with monoidal product being the composition of endofunctors.
It is easily seen that the mixed associator m of M gives rise to a monoidal structure bm for ρ,
i.e. a natural isomorphism of the form bmX,Y : ρ(X)ρ(Y ) ⇒ρ(X ⊗Y ), see §2.2.2. Moreover,
the centralizer Z(ρ) can be identified with EndC(M), see §2.2.3. Within such an identification:
Proposition 1. (Prop. 2.11) For all F, G ∈EndC(M) = Z(ρ), the cochain complexes C•

Building upon results of [GHS] it has been shown in [FGS] that, under certain finite-
ness assumptions, the Davydov–Yetter cohomology groups H•
DY(Γ; V, W) of a monoidal functor
Γ : C →D are isomorphic to the relative Ext groups Ext•
Z(Γ),D(V, W). In [FGS2, §3.2] we gener-
alized this isomorphism without finiteness assumption on the target category D. In particular,
it is not necessary to require that D is rigid or has an exact tensor product. It suffices to require
that the tensor product is right exact, which holds e.g. in the category of bimodules Rexk(M).
This generalization is crucial for applications in this paper to module categories.
We recall that relative Ext groups can be defined for any resolvent pair between abelian
categories, i.e. an adjunction satisfying certain assumptions, and these groups are computed
through relative projective resolutions, a generalization of usual projective resolutions satisfying
a “splitting” condition [ML, Chap. IX], see also [FGS, §2.1] for a short and self-contained review.

As its name indicates, the object AM has a natural structure of an algebra in Z(C), which is
recalled in §3.4.1. Since the C-module category M is finite we can assume that M = ModC(A)
for some algebra object A ∈C, see [EGNO, Cor. 7.10.5]. In Thm. 3.25, we prove that AM
is isomorphic as an algebra in Z(C) to the full center of the algebra A, which is a categorical
definition of the center of an algebra introduced in [Sch, Def. 4.2.1] and [Dav2, §4]. Based on the
description of bulk fields in 2d rational conformal field theories [FRS], the name “full center”
was introduced in [FFRS, Def. 4.9] for a commutative algebra in C ⊠Crev for C modular fusion
category, in which case C⊠Crev is braided equivalent to Z(C). This has led to a characterization
of the full center as a commutative algebra in Z(C) in terms of a universal property [Dav2, §4].
It is thus an intriguing problem to explore the consequences of the appearance of the full center
in an algebraic deformation theory of 2d conformal field theories.

3.3
Rigidity results
Here, we obtain two rigidity results: the first is that the deformation cohomology for the
regular C-module category vanishes, as a non-trivial consequence of Theorem 3.10, and the
second is a generalized Ocneanu rigidity stating that every k-linear monoidal functor out of
a fusion category (with non-zero categorical dimension) to a fairly general target category
(k-linear abelian and with right exact monoidal product) has trivial DY cohomologies with
any coefficients, and as a consequence, we get that every module category (not necessarily
semisimple or even finite) over a fusion category has vanishing mixed associator cohomologies,
and even every module endofunctor has vanishing cohomologies in this case.
