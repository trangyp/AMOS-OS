Abstract
We complete Maksimova’s classification of the normal extensions of S4 with inter-
polation. In particular, we prove Craig interpolation for the six extensions of S4
for which Craig interpolation was still open. The proof strategy builds upon the
ideas of Smoryński, but employs a novel approach using Fine’s frame formulas for
splitting clusters.
1
Introduction
Maksimova [8] proved the celebrated result that exactly eight superintuitionistic logics
have the Craig interpolation property. Building on this work, in [9, 10, 11] she con-
sidered the Craig interpolation property and the deductive interpolation property for
normal extensions of S4. Maksimova showed that between 31 and 37 normal extension
of S4 have the Craig interpolation property and between 43 and 49 have the deductive
interpolation property, leaving the status of the interpolation properties open for six
logics.
A logic Λ is said to have the Craig interpolation property (or CIP for short) if for
all formulas 𝜑, 𝜓with 𝜑→𝜓∈Λ, there exists a formula 𝜒whose atomic propositions
appear in both 𝜑and 𝜓, such that 𝜑→𝜒, 𝜒→𝜓∈Λ. In this case the formula 𝜒is
called an interpolant for the implication 𝜑→𝜓.
Maksimova [9] showed that if a normal extension of S4 has the CIP, then its intu-
itionistic fragment also has the CIP. Moreover, Maksimova [8] characterized the eight
superintuitionistic logics with the CIP, see Table 1.
For this reason Maksimova introduced the following notation: for 𝑚, 𝑛∈ℕ∪{𝜔}
and a superintuitionistic logic Λ, Γ(Λ, 𝑚, 𝑛) denotes the normal extension of S4 that is
complete with respect to the finite 𝜏(Λ)-frames for which the final clusters have size at
most 𝑚and the non-final clusters have size at most 𝑛, where 𝜏(Λ) denotes the smallest
modal companion of Λ. For example, we have Γ(Int, 𝜔, 𝜔) = S4, Γ(Int, 1, 1) = Grz,
Γ(KC, 𝜔, 𝜔) = S4.2, and Γ(Cl, 𝜔, 0) = S5.

Method.
Our approach is based on the one that Smoryński [13] used to show the CIP
for GL. This approach was also used by Boolos [3] to prove the CIP for Grz and by
Maksimova [11] for two further normal extensions of S4.
Smoryński’s method relies on syntactically constructing finite models. First it is as-
sumed that an implication does not have an interpolant. Using this, a finite countermodel
for the implication is constructed (see Section 3). For logics that impose restrictions on
the clusters in their frames, it is usually necessary to tweak the definition of the accessib-
ility relation so that the constructed model is a model of the logic, cf. [3, 11, 13]. Thus,
every implication lacking an interpolant is invalid in the logic, so the CIP is obtained.
The key difference of our approach compared to the previous ones is that we employ
a more flexible and semantic approach towards abiding by the restrictions on clusters.
We first construct a standard Smoryński model 𝔐, which is only a model for S4 (or
S4.2). The cluster restrictions are then expressed using Fine’s frame formulas [5], and
it follows from the Truth Lemma for Smoryński models that 𝔐validates (sufficiently
large) substitutions of these frame formulas. Using these, we show that every cluster
contains a small enough adequate subset of points, which together do not rely on other
points in the cluster to witnesses the satisfaction of formulas. This then finally allows
us to modify the accessibility relation into one that does not contain clusters that are
too large, while preserving the Truth Lemma; an operation which we will call refining
the clusters.
Outline.
The paper is structured as follows.
Section 2 contains the preliminaries
needed for the rest of the paper. In particular, we recall the definition and fundamental
properties of subframe formulas and the definition of the relevant normal extensions of
S4 for which we show the CIP. In Section 3, we present Smoryński’s construction of
finite models, which we call Smoryński models, and consider the fundamental properties
of these models.
In Section 4, we establish a way to reduce the size of clusters in suitable finite models.
In Section 5, we combine the methods of the two previous sections to prove the main
theorem of this paper. We show that the six normal extensions of S4 for which the
status of the CIP is open have the CIP.
