5. Further directions
In [3], Jung et al. conjectured that the sequence of iterated Aluthge transforms
of a bounded linear operator on a separable, complex Hilbert space converges in
the norm topology to a quasinormal operator. Later, Antezana et al. [2] con-
firmed this conjecture in the finite dimensional setting. However, Thompson (as
communicated in Example 5.5 of [4]) constructed an operator whose sequence of
iterated Aluthge transforms converges in the strong operator topology but fails to
converge in the norm topology, thereby disproving the conjecture in general. Con-
sequently, Jung et al. [4] revised the conjecture and proposed that, for operators
on infinite dimensional Hilbert spaces, the sequence of the Aluthge transforms
converges in the strong operator topology. This revised conjecture was further
challenged when Cho et al. in [5], exhibited a unilateral weighted shift for which
the sequence of iterates of the Aluthge transforms does not converge even in the
weak operator topology.

similar results for certain composition operators on weighted Bergman space and
