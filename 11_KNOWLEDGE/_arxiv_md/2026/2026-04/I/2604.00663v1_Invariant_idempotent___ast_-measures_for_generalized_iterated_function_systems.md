INVARIANT IDEMPOTENT ∗-MEASURES FOR
GENERALIZED ITERATED FUNCTION SYSTEMS
NATALIA MAZURENKO AND MYKHAILO ZARICHNYI
Abstract. The notion of ∗-measure on a compact Hausdorff space
can be defined for arbitrary continuous triangular norm ∗. The
well-known Hutchinson-Barnsley theory deals with the iterated
function systems (IFSs) of probability measures and establishes
existence and uniqueness of invariant measures.
In the previous paper, IFSs of ∗-measures were considered. In
the present paper we deal with generalized invariant function sys-
tems (GIFSs) of ∗-measures, which are counterparts of GIFSs in
the sense of Mihail and Miculescu.
The notion of invariant ∗-
measure is introduced for such GIFSs and we prove existence and
uniqueness of such elements.
1. Introduction
The notion of ∗-measure, for any triangular norm ∗(see the defi-
nition below), is introduced in [13]. The functor M ∗of ∗-measures
acting in the category Comp of compact Hausdorff spaces and contin-
uous mappings turns out to be isomorphic to the functor of idempotent
measures defined in [20] (see [6]). Idempotent measures are analogues
of probability measures in idempotent mathematics (this is the name
of the branch of mathematics where the role of the field of real numbers
is played by idempotent semi-rings; see, e.g., [4]). For idempotent mea-
sures, one can establish analogues of various results valid for probability
measures. In particular, one can prove the existence of invariant idem-
potent measures for iterated function systems (IFSs) in full accordance
with Hutchinson’s results for probability measures; see [5].
It is also proved in [13] that the functor M ∗is the functorial part
of a monad in the category Comp. The monad structure is a natural
framework for defining the notion of invariant ∗-measure for IFSs in
metric spaces. The existence and uniqueness of invariant ∗-measures
for some IFSs is proved in [6].

4. Remarks and open questions
Thus, the class of invariant elements of G-GIFS of order m is con-
tained in the class of invariant elements of H-GIFS of order m. We
conjecture that, in general, these classes do not coincide.
The considerations of G-GIFSs can be extended in two directions.
First, instead of ∗-measures one can consider compact sets, probabil-
ity measures etc.
Second, one can replace the G-symmetric powers
by another constructions known as functors of finite degree. As an
example, we consider the m-th hypersymmetric powers expm X, i.e.
the spaces of nonempty subsets of X of cardinality ≤m endowed
with the Vietoris topology. An expm-GIFS is then a family of maps
gi : expm X →X, i = 1, . . . , n, and a compact subset A of X is called
invariant if A = ∪n
i=1 expm A. Many examples of such constructions
can be found in the general theory of functors of finite degree (see, e.g.,
[19]).
The notion of place-dependent idempotent iterated function systems
and its invariant idempotent measure considered in [7] has its natu-
ral analog in the class of ∗-measures. We return to these IFSs in a
subsequent publication.
