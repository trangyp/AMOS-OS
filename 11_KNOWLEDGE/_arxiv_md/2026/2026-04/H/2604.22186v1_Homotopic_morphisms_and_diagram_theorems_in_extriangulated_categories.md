Abstract.
Homotopic morphisms of E-triangles in extriangulated categories
are introduced. Any morphism of E-triangles is a composition of homotopic
morphisms. Any morphism (α1, α2, α3) of E-triangles can be modified to be
homotopic, by changing one of αi; moreover, all the 15 cases where αi is an
E-inflation (E-deflation) are analyzed. Some diagram theorems, especially 4×4
Lemma and its 14 variants, including 3×3 diagram and Horseshoe Lemma, are
investigated. A relation between homotopic morphisms and (middling) good
morphisms in triangulated categories are given. Weakly idempotent complete
extriangulated categories are characterized.
Keywords and phrases. extriangulated category, homotopic morphism, 6-
term exact sequence, 4 × 4 Lemma, weakly idempotent completeness

1. Introduction
The notion of extriangulated category, introduced by H. Nakaoka and Y. Palu
in [14], is a common generalization of exact categories and triangulated categories.
There are many extriangulated categories which are neither exact nor triangulated
([14, Remark 2.18, Proposition 3.30], [18, Remark 4.13]; also [17], [19]).
Diagram theorems are widely used and have interests in various categories. Since
E-triangles cannot rotate, a diagram theorem in extriangulated categories has vari-
ants. For example, 4 × 4 Lemma in an extriangulated category has 14 variants.
In this paper, homotopic morphisms of E-triangles in an extriangulated category
are introduced, and various diagram theorems are studied.
1.1. Homotopic morphisms. A homotopic morphism of E-triangles is defined in
Definition 3.4. This notion seems to be new even for triangulated categories. Com-
paring with morphisms of E-triangles, homotopic morphisms enjoy pleasant proper-
ties, and play an important role. They induce new E-triangles, and any morphism
of E-triangles is a composition of two homotopic morphisms (Proposition 3.8).
Given a morphism (α, β, γ) of E-triangles, it is a question that whether it can
be modified to be homotopic; and furthermore, if two of α, β, γ are in
{E-inflation and E-deflation, E-inflation, E-deflation}
whether the third one is still in the set, such that (α, β, γ) is a homotopic mor-

2.1. Extriangulated categories. We recall the definition of an extriangulated
category from H. Nakaoka and Y. Palu [14].
