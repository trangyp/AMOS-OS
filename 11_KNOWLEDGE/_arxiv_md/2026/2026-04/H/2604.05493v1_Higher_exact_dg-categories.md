HIGHER EXACT DG-CATEGORIES
NAO MOCHIZUKI AND HIROYUKI NAKAOKA
Abstract. We introduce the notion of an n-exact dg-category. This notion provides a
higher analogue of Chen’s exact dg-category, in the sense that the case where n equals 1
recovers exact dg-categories.
We prove that, under a suitable vanishing condition on the cohomologies of Hom-
complexes of an n-exact dg-category A , its homotopy category admits a natural n-
exangulated structure. Thus n-exact dg-categories provide dg-enhancements of n-exangulated
categories. At the same time, our framework can be regarded as a dg-categorical general-
ization of n-exangulated categories applicable even without the vanishing condition.
In the latter part of the article, we show that an n-cluster tilting subcategory of an
exact dg-category naturally carries the structure of an n-exact dg-category. This result
indicates that n-exact dg-structures provide an intrinsic dg-categorical axiomatization of
n-cluster tilting subcategories, highlighting the advantages of studying dg-generalizations
of n-exangulated categories.

1. Introduction
The notion of an n-exangulated category was introduced in [HLN1] as a common gener-
alization of (n + 2)-angulated categories, n-exact categories, and n-abelian categories. Here,
(n + 2)-angulated categories and n-exact categories were introduced in [GKO] and [J], re-
spectively, in order to formalize n-cluster tilting subcategories inside triangulated and abelian
categories, which play a central role in higher dimensional Auslander–Reiten theory [I]. When
n = 1, the notions of 1-exangulated category, 3-angulated category, 1-exact category, and 1-
abelian category coincide, respectively, with extriangulated categories [NP1], exact categories,
and abelian categories.
For the case n = 1, namely extriangulated categories, it is remarkable that, besides unify-
ing exact and triangulated categories, they admit various natural operations such as taking
extension-closed subcategories, relative theories, and localization [NOS]. Moreover, extrian-
gulated categories possess natural enhancement notions: Barwick’s exact ∞-categories [Ba]
and Chen’s exact dg-categories [C1] provide ∞-categorical and dg enhancements, respectively.
(The relation with exact dg-categories is established in [C1], while the relation with exact
∞-categories can be found in [NP2] or in the embedding into stable ∞-categories obtained
in [Kle].) The existence of such natural enhancement notions indicates that the definition of
extriangulated categories captures a mathematically natural structure.
The framework of n-exangulated categories not only likewise includes (n + 2)-angulated
categories and n-exact categories (see also Remark 6.19), but also it is known that sev-
eral operations analogous to those in the case n = 1 are available, such as taking relative
theories [HLN1], and n-extension-closed subcategories [Kla]. Furthermore, it was shown in
[HLN2] that an n-cluster tilting subcategory of an extriangulated category naturally carries
an n-exangulated structure under some conditions, which is an n-exangulated categorical
unification of the results by [GKO] and [J].
In this article, we propose a dg-enhancement notion for n-exangulated categories. More
precisely, we consider n-exact sequences in a connective additive dg-category A , and use
them to formulate the notion of an n-exact dg-category. When n = 1, our definition recovers
Chen’s exact dg-category, while when A is an ordinary category, it coincides with Jasso’s
notion of an n-exact category.
Our main result (Theorem 6.13) shows that if A satisfies the vanishing condition Hi(A ) =
0 for all −n < i < 0 for the cohomologies of Hom-complexes, then the additive category
H0(A ) admits a natural n-exangulated structure.
Theorem A. (= Theorem 6.13) Let (A , S) be an n-exact dg-category, with skeletally small
H0(A ). Suppose that A satisfies HiA (A, B) = 0 for all A, B ∈ob(A ) and for all −n < i < 0.
Then, H0(A ) has a structure of an n-exangulated category induced by S.
In other words, this theorem asserts that n-exact dg-categories satisfying an appropriate co-
homological vanishing condition provide dg-enhancements of n-exangulated categories. From
this viewpoint, the result can be regarded as supporting the naturalness of the definition of
n-exangulated categories. In the other direction, our notion of an n-exact dg-category can be
interpreted as a dg-categorical generalization of n-exangulated categories to situations where
the above vanishing condition is not imposed. Thus, by working with dg-categories rather
than ordinary additive categories, we propose n-exact dg-categories as a framework capable
of treating such more general situations.
At the end of the article, we establish another main result (Theorem 7.22), showing that
an n-cluster tilting subcategory of an exact dg-category naturally carries the structure of
an n-exact dg-category.
This theorem can be viewed as a dg-categorical analogue of the
preceding result for ordinary n-exangulated categories. We remark that in previous works,
in order to equip an n-cluster tilting subcategory T of an extriangulated category C with
an n-exangulated structure, additional assumptions were required: in [GKO], when C is

triangulated, T was assumed to be closed under n-shifts, while in [HLN2] a corresponding

Theorem B. (= Theorem 7.22) Let E be an exact dg-category, and let T ⊂E be an n-cluster

differentials are, respectively, an inflation and a deflation in E . Then (T , S) becomes an
n-exact dg-category.
This result shows that our notion of an n-exact dg-category provides an intrinsic and dg-
categorical axiomatization of the concept of an n-cluster tilting subcategory. For H0(T ) to
become an n-exangulated category, the cohomological vanishing condition required in Theo-
rem 6.13 is equivalent, to the condition that the subcategory T is closed under the n-shift,
or equivalently that it is an nZ-cluster tilting subcategory.
The above theorem suggests that even in the absence of such conditions, a natural struc-
ture still exists at the dg-level. In general situations of this kind, the homotopy category
H0(T ) need not admit a structure describable in terms of ordinary n-exangulated categories.
Hence the structure captured by our approach appears to be accessible only through the
dg-categorical framework developed in this article.
2. Preliminaries
Throughout this article, let k be a commutative ring, and let A denote a dg-category over
k. Also, let n be a positive integer. We are mainly interested in the case of n ≥2.
Definition 2.1. A is said to be connective if positive cohomologies of the Hom-complexes
vanish; that is, if HiA (A, B) = 0 holds for any A, B ∈ob(A ) and all i > 0. If A satisfies
A (A, B)i = 0 for all A, B ∈ob(A ) and i > 0, then A is said to be strictly connective.
Definition 2.2. A is said to be additive if H0(A ) is an additive category. If Z0(A ) is an
additive category, then A is said to be strictly additive.
In our main theorem (Theorem 6.13), we will show that H0(A ) of any n-exact dg-category
A can be endowed with a natural n-exangulated structure under some condition (Condi-
tion 6.1). We note that if A is connective and additive, then there exists a strictly connective
and strictly additive dg-category that is quasi-equivalent to A (see [G, Section 1]). We also
remark that Bennett–Tennenhaus and Shah have shown in [B-TS] that n-exangulated struc-
tures can be transported along equivalences of categories. Thus, in view of [B-TS, Theorem
3.9], we may replace the dg-category A with a quasi-equivalent dg-category for the pur-
pose of proving our main theorem. For this reason, we henceforth assume that A is strictly
connective and strictly additive.

7.3. More on nZ-cluster tilting subcategories. To explain the relationship between nZ-
cluster tilting subcategories and (n + 2)-angulated categories, we recall the following from

Definition 7.25. ([LZ, Definition 3.2]) Let (F, F, sF) be an n-exangulated category. We
call (F, F, sF) a Frobenius n-exangulated category if F has enough projectives and enough
injectives, and projective objects coincide with injective objects.
Fact 7.26. ([LZ, Theorem 3.13]) Let (F, F, sF) be a Frobenius n-exangulated category. Then,
the ideal quotient of F by projective-injectives has a natural structure of an (n+2)-angulated
category.
As a special case, if projective objects are only zero objects in a Frobenius n-exangulated
category F, then F itself has a structure of (n + 2)-angulated categories. The following
corollary is an immediate consequence of this; we record it here for the reader’s convenience.

complete. Then the following are equivalent.
(1) (F, F, sF) is a Frobenius n-exangulated category and projective objects are only zero
objects.
(2) Any morphism f : X →Y in F is inflation and deflation.
