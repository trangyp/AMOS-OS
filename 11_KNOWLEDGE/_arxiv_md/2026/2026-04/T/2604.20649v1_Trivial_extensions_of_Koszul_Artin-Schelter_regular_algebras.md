Abstract. Let S be an N-graded Koszul Artin-Schelter regular algebra and let σ be a graded
algebra automorphism of S.
We study the stable category of graded maximal Cohen-Macaulay
modules over the trivial extension algebra S ⋉Sσ(−1).
We show that this category is triangle
equivalent to the bounded derived category of finitely generated (ungraded) modules over the Koszul
dual algebra of the Zhang twist Sσ−1. In the connected graded case, we also obtain a criterion for
when two such stable categories are triangle equivalent, and show that such an equivalence induces
an equivalence between the categories of graded modules over the original algebras.
1. Introduction
Trivial extension algebras play an essential role in both commutative algebra and the represen-
tation theory of finite-dimensional algebras, since they form a rich source of important examples
in these areas (see e.g. [2, 9, 34]). In particular, graded trivial extensions arise naturally in the
representation theory of finite-dimensional Gorenstein algebras (see e.g. [8, 12, 24]).
In recent years, the stable categories of graded maximal Cohen-Macaulay modules over graded
Gorenstein algebras have been widely studied as triangulated categories related to various areas
including representation theory, commutative algebra, and noncommutative algebraic geometry
(see e.g. [1, 7, 11, 14, 15, 16, 28, 29, 36]).
Motivated by these developments, in this paper we study graded Gorenstein algebras arising
from trivial extensions that are not necessarily commutative or finite-dimensional. More precisely,
we investigate the stable category CMZ(S ⋉L) of graded right maximal Cohen-Macaulay modules
over the trivial extension algebra S ⋉L, where S is a N-graded Koszul Artin-Schelter (AS) regular
algebra and L is a graded invertible S-bimodule generated in degree 1. Our main result describes
this category in terms of the Koszul dual of a twisted algebra of S.
Theorem 1.1 (Theorem 5.5). Let S be an N-graded Koszul AS-regular algebra of dimension d
and let σ ∈GrAut(S) be a graded algebra automorphism of S. Consider the graded S-bimodule
L := Sσ(−1) in GrMod Se. Then there are equivalences of triangulated categories

2.3. AS-regular algebras and Koszul algebras. Artin-Schelter (AS) regular algebras, defined

Corollary 5.11. Let S and R be Koszul AS-regular algebras of dimensions d and d′, respectively.

6. Examples
We present several examples illustrating the main results.
In this section, we work over an
algebraically closed field k of characteristic 0.
To illustrate Corollary 5.11, we first compute the Zhang twist Sν−1 of a connected graded Koszul
AS-regular algebra S by the inverse of its Nakayama automorphism ν.
By [37, Theorem 9.2], the Nakayama automorphism ν of a connected graded Koszul AS-regular
algebra S is determined by that of its Koszul dual S!, which is a Koszul self-injective algebra. More
precisely, ν(a) = (−1)(d+1)iη′(a) for a ∈Si, where η′ denotes the automorphism of S induced by
the Nakayama automorphism of S!.
Moreover, the Nakayama automorphism η of a connected graded Koszul self-injective algebra A
can be computed using the Frobenius structure of A. Namely, if n denotes the socle degree, then
for a ∈A1 and b ∈An−1 one has ab = bη(a), which determines η; see [35, Lemma 3.3].
We begin with the case where S is a 2-dimensional connected graded Koszul AS-regular algebra.
It is well-known that every such algebra is isomorphic to k⟨x, y⟩/(xy −αyx) (0 ̸= α ∈k) or
k⟨x, y⟩/(xy −yx −x2).
Example 6.1. (1) We first treat the case S = k⟨x, y⟩/(xy −αyx) (0 ̸= α ∈k). It is easy to see that
S! ∼= k⟨x, y⟩/(x2, αxy + yx, y2). Since xy = −α−1yx in S!, the Nakayama automorphism η of S! is
given by η(x) = −α−1x and η(y) = −αy. Thus the Nakayama automorphism ν of S is determined
by ν(x) = α−1x and ν(y) = αy, and its inverse is given by
ν−1(x) = αx
and
ν−1(y) = α−1y.

(2) We next treat the case S = k⟨x, y⟩/(xy −yx −x2). It is easy to see that S! ∼= k⟨x, y⟩/(xy +
