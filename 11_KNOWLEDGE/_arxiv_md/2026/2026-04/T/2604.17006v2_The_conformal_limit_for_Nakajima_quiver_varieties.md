THE CONFORMAL LIMIT FOR NAKAJIMA QUIVER VARIETIES
SOTIRIA CHATZIMARKOU AND PANAGIOTIS DIMAKIS
Abstract. Inspired by Gaiotto’s conformal limit construction for Higgs bundles we intro-
duce and study a conformal limit construction for Nakajima quiver varieties. We prove that
the conformal limit is indeed a limit of a one-parameter family of points inside a specified
quiver variety and that it gives a biholomorphic map between holomorphic Lagrangian
submanifolds foliating two related quiver varieties. Finally, we introduce the analog of
Simpson’s conjecture on the completeness of these holomorphic Lagrangian submanifolds
and provide a complete proof under a genericity assumption. Central to both proofs is the
existence of a Bia lynicki-Birula slice associated to fixed points of a natural circle action.
