1. Introduction
The objective of this paper is to further study the pointed virtual curves deﬁned in [14], and to introduce
the notion of an inclusion of CAVC-type (cf. Lemma 3.1(ii); here, “CAVC” stands for “cuspidal-admissible
virtual curve”) and the notion of a virtual decuspidaloid (cf. Lemma 4.5). Roughly speaking, the notion of an
inclusion of CAVC-type is a group-theoretic abstraction of the inclusion of the geometric virtual fundamental
group ∆C of a pointed virtual curve C into the virtual fundamental group ΠC (cf. § 2 the discussion entitled
“Virtual Curves”), while the notion of a virtual decuspidaloid is a categorical-theoretic abstraction of the
decuspidalization operation on virtual fundamental groups of pointed virtual curves developed in [[14], §5].
Our initial theorem extends [[14], Theorem A] from the genus-zero situation to curves of arbitrary genus.
Whereas [[14], Theorem A] concerns hyperbolic curves of genus 0 under suitable hypotheses, we now distin-
guish the following three mutually exclusive cases, where g denotes the genus and r denotes the number of
cusps (cf. § 2, the discussion entitled “Fields, Schemes and Curves”):
Case 0: g = 0 and r ≥3 (cf. Theorem A);
Case 1: g = 1 and r ≥1 (cf. Theorem B);
Case 2: g ≥2 (cf. Theorem C).
