1. Introduction

with pointwise weak* topology. We denote by M ⋊α G the crossed product
of M by the action α of G on M. If, in addition, G is Abelian, then there
exists a dual action ˆα of ˆG on M ⋊α G. The Takesaki duality theorem ([32,

In 2014, N. C. Phillips asked a problem if there is an Lp-version of the
Takai duality theorem (see [26, Problem 8.7]). S. Zhu and I proved that
the Takai duality theorem can not be generalized to crossed products of
Lp-operator algebras when p ̸= 2 (see [36]).
For p ∈[1, ∞), we say that a Banach algebra A is an Lp-operator algebra
if it is isometrically isomorphic to a norm closed subalgebra of the algebra
B(E) of all bounded linear operators on some Lp-space E. The study of
Lp-operator algebras traces back to C. Herz’s influential work on harmonic
analysis of group algebras in the 1970’s [16, 17, 18]. Recently, people renew
interest in Lp-operator algebras due to the work of N. C. Phillips. In the
passing ten years, N. C. Phillips introduced and studied Lp-operator alge-
bras [22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28]. These studies encourage many authors to
participate in the research of Lp-operator algebras. This includes the work
on group Lp-operator algebras [12, 38]; groupoid Lp-operator algebras [13];
Lp-Roe type algebras [6]; Lp-operator crossed products [14, 36, 37] and the
lp-Toeplitz algebra [35].
Since von-Neumann algebras are weak* closed L2-operator algebras, it is
natural to ask if there is a Takesaki duality theorem for crossed products
of weak* closed Lp-operator algebras. The aim of this paper is study this
problem.

Definition 4.1 ([21, Definition 2.6.1]). Let A be a unital Banach algebra in

of all numbers w(a) ∈C with w being a continuous linear functional on A
