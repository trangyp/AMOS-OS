THE INTERVAL NEIGHBORHOODS IN THE REAL GROTHENDIECK GROUPS
SOTA ASAI
Abstract. For a finite dimensional algebra A, the TF equivalence on the real Grothendieck group
K0(proj A)R can be regarded as a completion of the g-fan. For example, the silting cones C◦(U) of
2-term presilting complexes U give the most fundamental family of TF equivalence classes. The next
step is studying the TF equivalence classes around each silting cone C◦(U). Thus, in this paper, we
investigate the closed interval neighborhood D(U) of C◦(U). As our main result, we give a 2|U| : 1
correspondence between the TF equivalence classes in D(U) and those in K0(proj B)R, where B is the
algebra appearing in the τ-tilting reduction at U. For this purpose, we give an explicit description
of defining inequalities and the faces of D(U) as a polyhedral cone, by using 2-term simple-minded
collections and M-TF equivalences.

1. Introduction
1.1. Background: the g-fan and the TF equivalence. Tilting complexes in the perfect derived
category Kb(proj A) for a ring A is a fundamental notion characterizing derived equivalences [Ri]. In
order to get categorical relationships between rings, it is important to study many tilting complexes.
For this purpose, silting complexes [KV], which generalize tilting complexes, play an important role.
Aihara-Iyama [AiI] established mutations of silting complexes, which allow us to construct silting
complexes in a systematic way.
Within the range of 2-term silting complexes for a finite dimensional algebra A over a field K, we
have many more nice properties. A 2-term silting complex S = Ln
i=1 Si with Si indecomposable has a
unique mutation at each Sj [AIR, DF]. Thus mutations of 2-term silting complexes are strongly related
to cluster theory [FZ, KeY]. Moreover, 2-term silting complexes have bijections with other important
notions in the module category mod A and its bounded derived category D(A) := Db(mod A); see
[AIR, As1, BY, IT, KoY, MŠ]. There have been various types of studies of (2-term) silting complexes

As in [AHIKM], the set of all closed silting cones C(U) is a non-singular fan in K0(proj A)R, called
the g-fan of A. In general, the g-fan is not necessarily complete. In fact, the g-fan is complete if and
only if there are only finitely many isoclasses of basic 2-term silting complexes [As2].
In this context, the TF equivalence on K0(proj A)R [As2] is a useful notion which can be regarded
as a completion of the g-fan. Each θ ∈K0(proj A)R induces an R-linear form θ: K0(mod A)R →R via
the Euler bilinear form, and give two semistable torsion pairs (T θ, Fθ) and (Tθ, Fθ) in mod A [BKT].
We say that θ, η ∈K0(proj A)R are TF equivalent if (T θ, Fθ) = (T η, Fη) and (Tθ, Fθ) = (Tη, Fη).
Then each silting cone C◦(U) is a typical TF equivalence class [As2, BST, Y].
Namely, θ ∈
K0(proj A)R belongs to C◦(U) if and only if
(T θ, Fθ) = (⊥H−1(νU), Sub H−1(νU)) =: (T U, FU),
(Tθ, Fθ) = (Fac H0(U), H0(U)⊥) =: (TU, FU).
The association U 7→C◦(U) gives an injection from the set 2-psilt A of basic 2-term presilting complexes
to the set of TF equivalence class in K0(proj A)R.
This map is restricted to a bijection from the
set 2-silt A of basic 2-term silting complexes to the set of full-dimensional TF equivalence classes in
K0(proj A)R [As2]. Thus we can recover the g-fan from the TF equivalence.
These notions are strongly related also to the wall-chamber structure on K0(proj A)R introduced by
[B, BST] by using the θ-semistable subcategory Wθ := T θ ∩Fθ of King [K]. As examples of relevant
studies, we refer to [BCDMTY, BPPW, HIKT, M, PYK, STTV].
