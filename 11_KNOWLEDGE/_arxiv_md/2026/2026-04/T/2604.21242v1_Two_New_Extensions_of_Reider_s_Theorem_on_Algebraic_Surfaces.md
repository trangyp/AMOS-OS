Two New Extensions of Reider’s Theorem on Algebraic Surfaces
Aaron Bertram1, Jonathon Fleck, Liebo Pan, Joseph Sullivan
1. Introduction
Walls and chambers for the moduli of semi-stable objects in the derived category
of a complex projective manifold have been used to prove “classical” theorems about
the Brill-Noether theory of curves in surfaces [7, 8, 9, 10, 21] and about the degree
and genus of curves in projective three-space [26]. Here we use them to prove two
extensions of Reider’s theorem on the positivity of adjoint linear series on a surface.
We begin by recalling Reider’s Theorem [29].
Let S be a smooth complex projective surface.
Reider. Let D be an ample (not necessarily effective) divisor on S. If
D2 > 4 and D · C > C2 + 1
for all curves C ⊂S satisfying C2 ≤0, then |KS + D| is base point free, and if
D2 > 9 and D · C > C2 + 2
for all C ⊂S with C2 ≤0, then |KS + D| is very ample.
One extension due to Beltrametti, Francia and Sommese [13] is the following. If
D2 > (k + 1)2 and D · C > C2 + k
for all C ⊂S with C2 ≤0, then H1(S, ωS(D) ⊗IZ) = 0 for Z ⊂S of length ≤k.
This is a natural generalization of Reider’s Theorem to the separation of k-jets.
Our first theorem is about the nef cone of the blow-up of |KS + D|∨along S.
Theorem 7.1. In the context of Reider’s Theorem, suppose that
D2 > 9 and D · C > C2 + 2 when C2 ≤0
so that S ⊂|KS + D|∨and let Y := blS(|KS + D|∨) with exceptional divisor E.
If in addition:
(a1) D2 ≥16 then 3H −E is nef on Y .
(a2) D2 > 16 and D · C > C2 + 3 when C2 = −1, 0 then 3H −E is ample.
(b1) D2 ≥25 and D · C > C2 + 3 when C2 = −1, 0, then 2H −E is nef.
Note that 2H −E cannot be ample on Y due to the presence of secant lines.
However, if
(b2) D2 > 25 and D · C > C2 + 4 when C2 = −1, 0, then |2H −E| contracts:
(Y, Σ1(S)) →(Y0, S[2])
where Σ1(S) is the bundle of secant lines contracting onto the Hilbert scheme.
Remark. The necessity of the additional inequality in (b2) is seen with the Veronese:
P2 ⊂P5 associated to D = 5l
in which 2H −E on Y is nef, but the secant variety is “defective.” Instead, in this
case the contraction is a P2-bundle over the dual projective plane (P2)∨.

There are many computations in the literature of the nef cones of Hilbert schemes
for special surfaces [5, 30, 15, 9, 15], as well as some more recent, much more intricate
computations of the larger movable cones [1, 18, 22]. The most general results on
the nef cones of Hilbert schemes we are aware of are in [20], which also employs the
Bayer-Macr`ı strategy. To our knowledge, the results in this paper are the first to
apply to all surfaces and to condition ampleness on Reider-type inequalities.
