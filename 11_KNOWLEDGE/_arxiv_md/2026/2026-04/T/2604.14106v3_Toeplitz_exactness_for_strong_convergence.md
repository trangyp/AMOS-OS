used to aid in proving the existence of strongly converging unitary represen-

Remarks to the reader. The proof of the main result, Theorem 1.1,
is quite succinct using the gauge-invariant uniqueness theorem of Fowler-
Muhly-Raeburn [FMR03], once the relevant setup and notations have been
constructed precisely. One of the more notationally challenging aspects of
these results is to set up the ambient weak and strong convergence hypothe-
ses appropriately in various concrete settings (see Examples 2.5). Once this
is set up with care in amalgamated free products, Corollary 1.2 is proved
by fitting in [BO08, Theorem 4.8.2] which concretely links general reduced
amalgamated free products to Toeplitz-Pimsner algebras.
As far as HNN extensions are concerned, we exploit the recent insight from
[GKPT26] expressing the HNN extension as a concrete unital subalgebra of
an amalgamated free product. Therefore, Corollary 1.3 can be proved by
using Corollary 1.2 appropriately. The proof of Corollary 1.4 on the other
hand is quite straightforward from the way the A-valued semicirculars are
constructed, and is a direct application of Theorem 1.1 in that setting.
