Definition 3.1. Let G be a vector bundle and A be an ample line
bundle on a projective variety X. The bundle G is said to be V-big, if
for some positive integers m and r0 the bundle
Sr(Sm(G)) ⊗A−r
is generated by its global sections on a non-empty open set U of X for

Question: Let L be a big and nef line bundle on a projective variety

Example: Let C be a non singular projective curve of genus g ≥1
over C and L be a degree zero non torsion line bundle on C. For P ∈C
consider the vector bundle E = L ⊕OC(P) and set X = P(E). On X
the line bundle OP(E)(1) is big and nef (see, Lemma 2.3.2. [4]). But
p∗(OC(−P)) ⊗OP(E)(n) is not nef for any n, where p : X →C is the
natural projection.
