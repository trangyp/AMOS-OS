Abstract
Pagani and Tommasi have introduced a class of smoothable fine compactified Jacobians

Compactified universal Jacobians
A classical problem in algebraic geometry is to extend the family

Jacobian extending J d

2.1
Rigidity
We will use the following Rigidity Theorem for variations of Hodge structure proven by Schmid
[53, (7.24)], extending previous results of Grothendieck, Griffiths, and Deligne. All schemes and
stacks here are separated and of finite type over C.
Theorem 2.1 (Rigidity Theorem). Let B be a smooth connected algebraic space, x ∈B a
point. Suppose V and W are polarizable variations of Hodge structure over B. Suppose given
an isomorphism ϕ : Vx →Wx of Hodge structures with an action of π1(B, x). Then there is a
unique isomorphism V →W of variations of Hodge structure extending ϕ.
