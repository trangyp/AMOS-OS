Unirational algebraic groups and tame ramification
Otto Overkamp and Ismaele Vanni
Abstract
Let OK be a complete discrete valuation ring with field of fractions K and algebraically closed
residue field k. Let G be a smooth connected commutative algebraic group over K which does not
contain a copy of Ga . For each d prime to p :“ char k, let Kpdq be the unique extension of K of
degree d. We investigate how the Néron lft-model of G behaves under base change to the ring of
integers OKpdq . Information about this behaviour is encoded in the jumps of Edixhoven’s filtration
on the special fibre of the Néron lft-model of G, as well as in Halle-Nicaise’s motivic zeta function of
G. If G is unirational (e. g. an algebraic torus), we show that the jumps of G are rational numbers
and that the motivic zeta function of G is a rational function. We also deduce analogous results
for Abelian varieties with potentially totally multiplicative reduction. This answers a question of
Halle-Nicaise and partially one of Edixhoven. Along the way, we answer a question of Oesterlé about
the structure of unipotent algebraic groups over function fields in positive characteristic. Under
stronger conditions on G, we obtain rationality of jumps even for separably closed but imperfect k.

Introduction
Let OK be a complete discrete valuation ring with field of fractions K and separably
closed residue field k of characteristic p ą 0. A smooth algebraic group G over K will be
called wound if HomKpGa, Gq “ 0 (all group schemes appearing on this article will be
commutative). All semiabelian varieties over K are wound, but there also exist unipotent
examples. A fundamental result about these groups is that they admit Néron lft-models
over OK, i. e. for each smooth connected wound algebraic group G over K, there exists
a smooth separated group scheme G
Ñ Spec OK with generic fibre G such that, for each
smooth morphism S Ñ Spec OK and each map ϕK : S ˆOK Spec K Ñ G, there exists a
unique OK-morphism ϕ: S Ñ G extending ϕK [6, Chapter 10.1, Theorem 2 (b’)]. Such a
model of G is unique up to unique isomorphism. Now let F be a finite separable extension of
K and let OF be the integral closure of OK in F; this is another complete discrete valuation
ring which is finite and flat over OK . Suppose G is a smooth connected wound algebraic
group over K with Néron lft-model G Ñ Spec OK . It is well-known that the base change

However, the canonical morphism G ˆOK Spec OF Ñ GF is usually not an isomorphism,
and it is an interesting (and rather delicate) problem to predict this morphism’s behaviour.
One approach to this problem in the case of tame extensions, which we shall now briefly
describe informally, is due to Edixhoven [15]; it will be recalled more precisely in the next
section.
