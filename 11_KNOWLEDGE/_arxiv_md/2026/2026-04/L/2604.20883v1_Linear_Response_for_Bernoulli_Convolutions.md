Most papers above discuss the smoothness of averages such as (1.1) for smooth ob-
servables ϕ. On the other hand in applications one often has to deal dynamically de-
fined observables such as the directions of the invariant splitting, and those observ-
ables are only Hölder in general, [32, 29, 28, 52]. This motivates the study of regularity
of (1.1) for Hölder functions.
Another reason for us to study Hölder observables is that if ϕ is smooth, trivially (1.1)
is smooth. Indeed, X(λ) is an analytic function in λ and the image of X(λ) is uniformly
bounded on any compact subset of [0, 1). So, if ϕ is differentiable, then h(λ) is automat-
ically differentiable. We will discuss this in more detail in §3.3. It is therefore natural
to consider observables with worse regularity like Hölder functions, which turns out to
be more subtle. In this paper we present several results demonstrating both regularity
of (1.1) for some values of the parameters λ and the lack thereof for other parameters.
In particular, our results, shows, the phase transition: as λ increases the averages
seem to become more regular, compare Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 below.
