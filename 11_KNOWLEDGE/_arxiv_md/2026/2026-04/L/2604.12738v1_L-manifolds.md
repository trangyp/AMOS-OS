Introduction
The notion of a Frobenius manifold appears in relation to various topics in algebraic and
analytic geometry, such and quantum cohomology, deformation of meromorphic connections,
unfolding of singularities and others. Of a particular interest for us here will be the formal
Frobenius manifolds, that is a Frobenius structure on the formal completion of a manifold
at a point. In this situation there are two equivalent descriptions, either as an algebra over a
certain cyclic operad Com∞, or alternatively as a genus 0 cohomological field theory. Let us
briefly recall these correspondences, for a detailed discussion of it, as well as general theory
of Frobenius manifolds we refer to [Du] and [Ma].

Frobenius structure on X is, roughly speaking, a commutative and associative binary OX-
linear operation ◦: S2(TX) →TX, which in flat coordinates is described by an even potential

of a cyclic Com∞-algebra and a cohomological field theory.
0.3
In his book on Frobenius manifolds ([Ma]) Yuri Manin asks what would be the analog
of this picture if one starts with the Lie∞-operad instead of the Com∞. In this paper we
outline the first steps towards answering this question. First, in section 1 we give a definition
of an L-manifold, which is the global geometric notion extending Lie∞-algebras, similar to
how Frobenius manifold is the global version of the Com∞-algebra. Here we have to work
with the curved cyclic Lie∞algebras in order to have the same pattern as in the Frobenius
setting. Then in section 2 we give several basic examples of L-manifolds, including explicit
description in the cases of low odd dimension. Then in sections 3 and 4 we define spaces H•
S
which we use to define a version of cohomological field theory equivalent to the notion of a
curved cyclic Lie∞-algebra.
Of a particular interest here is that combinatorially all the constructions go parallel to the
case of Frobenius manifolds and moduli spaces of stable curves. This leads us to formulate
two questions.
Conjecture 0.4 There exist a family of spaces (or rather Artin stacks) L0,n forming a cyclic
operad, and equipped with the “forgetful” maps p: L0,n →L0,m, for m ⩽n, as well as the
“section” maps xi : L0,n →L0,n+1 for 1 ⩽i ⩽n + 1, satisfying the obvious relations (as for
M0,n), such that the spaces H•
n ≃H•(L0,n).
Question 0.5 If such spaces L0,n exist, are they moduli spaces of something? And if the
answer is positive, is there a natural higher genus version of these space Lg,n, and what is
the corresponding higher genus notion of Lie∞-algebra?

Examples
We begin with two extreme examples. First, if X is a purely even manifold, then the ring
of functions OX is purely even and the potential Φ, being an odd function, has to be zero.
In other words a purely even manifold doesn’t admit a non-trivial L-manifold structure.
On the other hand, if X is a purely odd manifold, then it is formal, and its ring of
function is a finite dimensional exterior algebra in odd variables. Hence, by proposition 1.6,
it is given by a purely even curved cyclic Lie∞-algebra g with only even operations ℓn, n ⩾0.
