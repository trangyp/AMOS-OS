REGULARITY OF ROOFTOP ENVELOPES
ELEONORA DI NEZZA AND ALEXANDER RASHKOVSKII
Abstract. We study continuity, H¨older regularity, and C1,1-regularity
of geodesics between continuous plurisubharmonic functions on bounded
domains of Cn. We then derive regularity properties of rooftop envelopes.
1. Introduction
The subject of geodesics in the space of K¨ahler potentials on a compact
K¨ahler manifold (X, ω) originates from the pioneering work by Mabuchi [39]
and had since become a central topic in K¨ahler geometry. It is well known
that such geodesics can be characterized as solutions to a homogeneous
complex Monge-Ampère equation [53]. In recent years, substantial progress
has been achieved through the contributions of Guedj, Zeriahi, Berndtsson,
Darvas, Di Nezza, Lu, and others.
A particularly effective approach to connecting quasi-plurisubharmonic
functions by geodesics is based on the use of rooftop envelopes. In this
framework the geodesic joining two bounded ω-plurisubharmonic functions
φ0, φ1 is φt, t ∈(0, 1) which can be described as an envelope on M = X × A,
A = {0 < log |ζ| < 1} ⊂C,
φlog |z|(x) := Φ(x, z) = sup{U ∈PSH(M, ˜ω) : U|∂M ≤φ0, φ1},
where ˜ω is the pullback of ω to M. This viewpoint has proved to be very
fruitful and has been further developed in several recent works ([52], [16],
[18]–[22]). An overview of the subject can be found in [24].
Similar questions in the local setting, namely for plurisubharmonic func-
tions defined on bounded domains in Cn, have been studied in [1], [2],
[7],[47]–[50] and other works, and this remains an active area of research. We
refer to [51] for a recent survey.
In this context, the geodesic between two plurisubharmonic (psh) functions
u0 and u1 arises as the upper envelope of plurisubharmonic functions on a
product domain and can be described as the supremum of all subgeodesics.
More precisely, denote Aj = {log |ζ| = j}, j = 0, 1 and consider the class
W(u0, u1) of functions v ∈PSH(Ω× A) such that lim sup v(·, ζ) ≤uj(·) as

3. Continuity of envelopes
In this section, we will review continuity properties of the rooftop envelopes
P(u, v) and, more generally, of the envelopes P(h), assuming u, v, h to be
continuous in Ω. For a class of domains containing the B-regular ones, conti-
nuity can be established using the notion of Jensen measures and Edwards’
theorem, a technique whose application to plurisubharmonic functions was
initiated in [54] and later further developed in subsequent works [44], [45],
[38], [57], [31], [27], [42], [43]. After summarizing those results, we introduce
a notion of Jensen pairs for ω-psh functions on compact K¨ahler manifolds
and establish an analog of Edwards’ theorem for such functions, which gives
an elementary proof of continuity of envelopes in this setting.

2]; furthermore, if Ωis B-regular, then P(h) ∈PSHc(Ω) [56, Theorem 3].
