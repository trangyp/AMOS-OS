Abstract. We study the moduli spaces of rational curves on cu-
bic hypersurfaces in characteristic ̸= 2, 3. As a result, we prove
that for every integer d ≥1 the Kontsevich moduli space of stable
maps on a smooth cubic hypersurface X of degree d is irreducible
if the dimension of X is greater than or equal to 4.

1. Introduction
Study of rational curves on Fano varieties is an interesting subject.
Rational curves play an important role in classiﬁcation theory, such as
the study of rational connectedness or the minimal model program. In
this paper, we are interested in the irreducibility of the moduli spaces of
rational curves on Fano varieties in positive characteristic. We study
cubic hypersurfaces of dimension ≥3, deﬁned over an algebraically
closed ﬁeld of characteristic ̸= 2, 3. In the case that the dimension is
greater than or equal to 4, we obtain the following theorem:
Theorem 1.1. Let X be a smooth cubic hypersurface of dimension
n ≥4 deﬁned over an algebraically closed ﬁeld of characteristic ̸= 2, 3.
Then for every integer d ≥1, the Kontsevich moduli space M 0,0(X, d)
is irreducible and generically parametrizes free curves.
This result extends [CS09, Theorem 1.1] which treats the case of
characteristic 0.
Generalizing the theorem by Coskun and Starr to
positive characteristic presents another diﬃculty: the existence of in-
separable maps. Because of this, we cannot deduce the existence of
free rational curves solely from the dominance of the evaluation maps.
To overcome this diﬃculty, we use the dimension counting argument

In characteristic 0, Geometric Manin’s Conjecture, proposed in [LT19],
predicts the structure of moduli spaces of rational curves on Fano vari-
eties; namely, the number of irreducible components of a given degree,
the dimension of the irreducible components, and so on.
The moduli spaces of rational curves on del Pezzo surfaces in positive
characteristic are studied in [BLRT23] from the viewpoint of Geometric
Manin’s Conjecture.
Let S be a del Pezzo surface, and Rat(S) be
the union of the irreducible components of M 0,0(S) which generically
parametrize stable maps from irreducible domains. For a smooth cubic
surface S, [BLRT23, Theorem 1.1] states that, if S is not the Fermat
cubic surface deﬁned over a ﬁeld of characteristic 2, every dominant
component of Rat(S) is separable and generically parametrizes free
rational curves. Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2 partially generalize this
result to higher dimensional cases, and give new examples supporting
Geometric Manin’s Conjecture in positive characteristic.
Remark 1.3. In [BLRT23, Remark 1.4] it is pointed out that the excep-
tions in [BLRT23, Theorem 1.1] coincide with non-globally F-regular
del Pezzo surfaces if the degree of del Pezzo surface is ≥2 (See also
[LT26, Section 3]). We remark on globally F-regularity of smooth cubic
hypersurfaces and freeness of lines on non-globally F-regular ones.
By [KT25, Theorem B], a smooth cubic hypersurface is not globally
F-regular only if the characteristic of the ground ﬁeld is 2. Combining
Fedder’s criterion and the argument in the proof of [FFK10, Theorem
4.4], we see that a smooth cubic hypersurface which is not globally
F-regular is projectively equivalent to the Fermat cubic hypersurface.
Let X be the Fermat cubic hypersurface of dimension n ≥3 deﬁned
over an algebraically closed ﬁeld of characteristic 2. Then any line on
X is non-free (See [Fur12, Proposition 1.2] for n = 3 and [FFK10, Proof
of Theorem 0.4] for n ≥4). In particular, Theorem 1.1 and Theorem
1.2 do not hold for the Fermat cubic hypersurfaces in characteristic 2.

assumptions.
Regarding Manin’s conjecture and Geometric Manin’s conjecture,
[Tan25] is a good reference. Also, [LT26] provides a nice description
of formulations and developments of the conjectures in positive char-
acteristic.

Lemma 2.3. Let X be a smooth cubic hypersurface of dimension n ≥
4. Then a general ﬁbre of the evaluation map ev : M 0,1(X, 1) →X is
irreducible.
Proof. For a general point x ∈X, the proof of [Gla25, Proposition 4.2]
implies that ev−1(x) is a complete intersection. Hence it is connected
if n ≥4. By Lemma 2.1, the union of non-free lines on X is a proper
closed subset. Therefore by [Kol96, 3.5.4 Corollary], ev−1(x) is smooth.
Therefore the general ﬁbre is irreducible.

4. Free curves on cubic threefolds
Let X be a smooth cubic threefold deﬁned over an algebraically
closed ﬁeld k of characteristic ̸= 2, 3. By the same argument as in
Lemma 3.3, we can prove that M0,0(X, d) generically parametrizes free
rational curves for any d ≥1. However, by a dimension reason, we
have to check the case of the space of conics M0,0(X, 2) directly.
Lemma 4.1. The space M 0,0(X, 2) consists of two irreducible compo-
nents R2, N2, where R2 generically parametrizes birational stable maps
