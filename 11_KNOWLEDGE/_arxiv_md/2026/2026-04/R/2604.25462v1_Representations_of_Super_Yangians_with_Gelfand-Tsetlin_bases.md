REPRESENTATIONS OF SUPER YANGIANS WITH GELFAND-TSETLIN BASES
VYACHESLAV FUTORNY, ZHENG LI, AND JIAN ZHANG
ABSTRACT. The evaluation homomorphisms from the super Yangian Y(glm|n) to the univer-
sal enveloping algebra U(glm|n) allows one to regard the covariant tensor module of glm|n as
Y(glm|n) modules. We study simple quotients of the submodules generated by a tensor product
of highest weight vectors inside the tensor products of covariant evaluation modules. In the case
n = 0, this recover all ﬁnite-dimensional simple modules of Y(glm). We give a necessary and
sufﬁcient condition for such modules to be tame, which generalizes the earlier work of Nazarov
and Tarasov for Y(glm) to the super case.
1. INTRODUCTION
Consider the following chain of subalgebras of Yangian Y(glm) ,
Y(gl1) ⊂Y(gl2) ⊂· · · ⊂Y(glm).
The Gelfand-Tsetlin subalgebra A(glm) of Y(glm) is generated by the centers of subalgebras in
the chain, which is maximal commutative (see [2], [14]). The generators of this subalgebra can
be described by quantum determinants [11, 9]. A ﬁnite-dimensional module with a semisimple
action of the subalgebra A(glm) is called tame. A module V is tame if and only if the module V
is obtained by pulling back through some automorphism ωf from the tensor product
Vz1(λ1/µ1) ⊗Vz2(λ2/µ2) ⊗· · · ⊗Vzk(λk/µk)
of the elementary Y(gln)-modules, for some skew Young diagrams λ1/µ1, . . . , λk/µk and some
complex numbers z1, . . . , zk such that zi −zj /∈Z when i ̸= j. This was conjectured by I.
Cherednik and proved in [2] under certain extra conditions on the module V . In full generality
this fact was proved in [15]. It turns out that the spectrum of the action of the subalgebra A(gln)
in every irreducible tame module is simple. The eigenbases of A(gln) in the tame modules are
called Gelfand-Tsetlin bases. Such bases were described in [8, 14, 15].
Super Yangian Y(glm|n) was introduced by Nazarov in [12]. Its simple ﬁnite-dimensional
modules were characterized by Drinfeld polynomials [20, 21]. Consider the following chain of
subalgebras of Yangian Y(glm|n),
Y(gl1) ⊂· · · ⊂Y(glm) ⊂Y(glm|1) ⊂Y(glm|n).
The Gelfand-Tsetlin subalgebra A(glm|n) of Y(glm|n) is generated by the centers of subalgebras
in the chain. A ﬁnite-dimensional module with a semisimple action of the subalgebra A(glm|n)
is called tame.
Similar to Y(gln), the evaluation homomorphism from the super Yangian Y(glm|n) to the
universal enveloping algebra U(glm|n) allows one to view glm|n-modules as Y(glm|n)-modules.

In contrast to the case of Y(gln), not every ﬁnite-dimensional simple module L(λ) of glm|n is
tame. There are two known types of tame modules: covariant tensor modules and essentially
typical modules; see [19, 10, 16, 17]. A covariant tensor module L(λ) of glm|n can be realized
as a submodule of a tensor product of natural representations; see [1, 18]. The Gelfand-Tsetlin
bases for covariant tensor modules were described in [19] and [10]. When regarded as modules
over the super Yangian Y(glm|n), covariant tensor modules will be called covariant evaluation
modules.
Let λ(i) be a covariant glm+r|n-weight, µ(i) be a glr-weight, r be a non-negative integer, N be
a positive interger and h(i) ∈C for 1 ≤i ≤N. Let us consider the product of skew modules
W = Lh(1)(λ(1)/µ(1)) ⊗· · · ⊗Lh(N)(λ(N)/µ(N)).
Via the Hopf algebra structure of Y(glm|n) together with the evaluation homomorphism, W can
be regarded as a Y(glm|n)-module. In [7], Lu and Mukhin proved that W is a simple Y(glm|n)-
module if h(s) −h(t) /∈Z by using the Drinfeld functor. In [6], Lu constructed a Gelfand-Tsetlin
type basis for Lh(λ/µ) and proved that W is tame and simple in the special case N = 1.
In this paper, we construct a Gelfand-Tsetlin type basis of W for any positive integer N if
h(s) −h(t) /∈Z. As a consequence, we show that W is simple in this case. Moreover, our proof
also shows that the module W is tame under this condition. Such module can be regarded as an
example of our following study. Consider the tensor product of covariant evaluation modules,
i.e.,
Lz1(λ(1)) ⊗Lz2(λ(2)) ⊗· · · ⊗Lzk(λ(k)),
such that every λ(i) (1 ≤i ≤k) is a covariant glm|n-weight. Let ξi be a highest weight vector
of Lzi(λ(i)). Let V be the simple quotient of the submodule generated by ξ1 ⊗ξ2 ⊗· · · ⊗ξk.
In the case n = 0, this recovers all ﬁnite-dimensional simple modules of Y(glm). We study the
necessary and sufﬁcient conditions for V to be tame. As a result, we get the following theorem.
Theorem 1.1 (Theorem 5.6 and Theorem 5.7). Let V be the simple ﬁnite-dimensional highest
weight Y(glm|n)-module described above. Then V is tame if and only if it is isomorphic to
Vh(1)(Θ(1)) ⊗Vh(2)(Θ(2)) ⊗· · · ⊗Vh(N)(Θ(N))
up to an automorphism ωf of Y(glm|n), where h(a) −h(b) /∈Z, Θ(a) satisﬁes the strong non-
crossing condition and 1 ≤a ̸= b ≤N.
Furthermore, we establish the connection between the factor Vh(Θ) in V and the skew module.
One can check that when n = 0, Vh(Θ) is indeed the skew module from [15]. Thus, our results
extend those of [15] to the super setting.
This paper is organized as follows, in Section 2, we review some known facts for super Yangian
Y(glm|n). We omit the proofs of some well-known results, while providing proofs for those
results that may not be widely known. In Section 3, we recall the Gelfand-Tsetlin basis of a
covariant evaluation Y(glm|n)-module L(λ) and the skew module of Y(glm|n). After that we
give a Gelfand-Tsetlin type basis of the tensor product of skew modules in Section 4. It implies
that such modules are tame and simple. In Section 5, we study the necessary and sufﬁcient
