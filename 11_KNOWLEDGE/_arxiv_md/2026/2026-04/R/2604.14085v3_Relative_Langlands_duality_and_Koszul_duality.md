Abstract. Consider a pair of S-dual hyperspherical varieties G ⟳X and

the Z/2-graded B-equivariant category (Dψ(Y )-modB)Z/2 and the Z/2-graded
unipotent B∨-monodromic category (Q(X∨)-modB∨,mon)Z/2.

1. Introduction
1.1. Relative Langlands duality and equivalence of spherical categories.
Let G be a connected reductive group over C and let Y be a smooth affine
spherical G-variety – i.e. a smooth affine G-variety with an open dense B-orbit,
where B is a Borel subgroup of G. We shall make an additional assumption: the
stabilizer of a general point of Y in B is connected.
Remark 1.1.1. In fact the setup of [BZSV] is more general. In loc.cit. the authors
start with a hyper-spherical affine Poisson G-variety X such that the stabilizer
of a general point in G is connected. If one starts with a spherical variety Y as
above then one can attach to it a hyper-spherical variety X = T ∗Y. Moreover, it
is shown in [BZSV] that the stabilizer of a general point of X in G is connected if
and only if the stabilizer of a general point of Y in B is connected. This language
makes what follows much more symmetric, but the main results of this paper will
be concerned with the cotangent case, i.e. the case when X = T ∗Y as above, so
we shall restrict ourselves to this setup from the very beginning.
One of the main conjectures of [BZSV] is that there should exist a dual hyper-
spherical affine Poisson G∨-variety X∨endowed with the following structures:
1) A C×-action so that the Poisson structure has degree 2.

with 0 differential.

1.2. Koszul duality conjecture. In addition to Conjecture 1.1.2 the following
conjecture was formulated in [FGT]. First let R be a (non-commutative) ring
endowed with a strong action of an algebraic group B; by definition this means
that B acts on R and there is a homomorphism of Lie algebras ι: b →R so that
the derivative of the B-action is equal to the adjoint action of ι. Then one can talk
about the category of monodromic R-modules with unipotent monodromy. By
definition this is the category of modules M over R endowed with a compatible
action of B so that difference between the derivative of this action and the action
of b on M coming from ι is equal to the derivative of some fixed character λ: B →
Gm (it is easy to see that this definition does not depend on the choice of λ).
