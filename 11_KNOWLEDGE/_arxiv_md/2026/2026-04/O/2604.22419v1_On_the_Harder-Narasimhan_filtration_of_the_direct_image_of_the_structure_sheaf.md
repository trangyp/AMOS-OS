IMAGE OF THE STRUCTURE SHEAF
INDRANIL BISWAS, MANISH KUMAR, AND A. J. PARAMESWARAN
Abstract. We compute the Harder-Narasimhan filtration of vector bundles f∗OY for
certain finite morphisms f : Y −→X and in some other cases.
1. Introduction
The Harder-Narasimhan filtration of a vector bundle E on a smooth projective curve
X is a canonical filtration which is useful to study the vector bundles which may not be
semistable. While the filtration is unique and has nice properties, it is very difficult to
compute in general. In this note we compute explicitly the Harder-Narasimhan filtration
in the following instances.
(1) Let E be a rank two vector bundle on X and Y an ample divisor on PX(E). Let
f be the finite morphism obtained by the restriction of the structure morphism
PX(E) −→X to Y . Lemma 2.1 computes the Harder-Narasimhan filtration of
f∗OY in terms of the Harder-Narasimhan filtration of E.
(2) Let Y be a complete intersection of codimension r in Pn, and let f : Y −→Pn−r
be the projection morphism from a linear subspace of dimension r. Then Theorem
4.2 does the computation for f∗OY .
Let ϕ : X −→P1 be a finite morphism with X a smooth irreducible curve and E a
vector bundle on X with vanishing first and zeroth cohomology. Then we observe that
the direct image ϕ∗E is the direct sum of copies of OP1(−1) (Lemma 5.1).
