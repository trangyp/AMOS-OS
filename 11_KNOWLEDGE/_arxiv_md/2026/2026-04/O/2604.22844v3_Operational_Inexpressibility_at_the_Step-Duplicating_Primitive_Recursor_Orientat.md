Abstract
We identify a structural property of term-rewriting proof systems called operational in-
expressibility:
for a fixed input and a fixed dimension of that input, no derivation in the
proof language both depends on that dimension and constrains the target question.
The
canonical instance is direct aggregation on the primitive recursion duplicator F(x, y, Z) →x,
F(x, y, S(n)) →G(y, F(x, y, n)), where the step argument y is duplicated on the right-hand
side.
The companion paper [29] delineates the schema-level non-representability frontier:
a
twelve-class direct-measure barrier package (its theorems on additive, transparent-compositional,
affine, restricted-quadratic, bounded cross-term, bounded multilinear, generalized polynomial,
max-plus, tracked-vector, lex, mixed-coordinate, and projected matrix barriers), the symbolic
Knuth-Bendix-order (KBO) variable-condition corollary, the dependency-pair (DP) projection
escape, and the transparency-essentiality witness. The present paper develops the schema-level
diagnostic layer above that frontier: confession dominance, proof-entropy monotonicity, norm
mismatch, seed-carrier factorization, the construction-versus-confession asymmetry, operational
incompleteness at the payload dimension, and the static projection-transaction account of the
boundary. We prove here that this frontier is an instance of operational inexpressibility at the
step-argument dimension. Under any direct whole-term measure the recursor’s mass profile coin-
cides with that of a true circular reference, and the boundary operator’s channel-preservation
axiom together with the dependency-pair soundness license is what separates them, with a
non-derivability theorem and the lift to formal term-rewriting-system (TRS) isomorphism and
information-equivalence mechanized in the upstream Lean stack (§4, §5).
Sound responses split into construction methods (polynomial interpretations, path orderings)
that extend the proof language, and confession methods (dependency pairs, counter-projection,
size-change termination (SCT), argument filtering) that project away the unincorporable dimen-
sion under an external soundness license; all four confession methods share the same projec-
tion rank and certified-forgetting witness interface. The Arts-Giesl soundness theorem is a Π0

Introduction
Consider the first-order rewrite rule
F(x, y, S(n)) →G(y, F(x, y, n)),
paired with the base case F(x, y, Z) →x. The counter decreases from S(n) to n, the recursive call
persists, and the step argument y is simultaneously preserved in the surrounding wrapper G(y, ·). This
is the base/step/counter core of primitive recursion on natural numbers. In first-order rewriting terms,
it is a standard base/step schema with a decreasing counter and a persistent recursive call [4, 31].
Closely related recursor normalization appears in G¨odel’s 1958 Dialectica system [16].
The companion paper [29] establishes the orientation boundary for the step-duplicating schema
in two stratified layers. The barrier section (theorems on the additive and transparent-compositional
schema barrier, the affine barrier under unbounded pump, the restricted-quadratic, bounded cross-
term, bounded multilinear, generalized degree-bounded polynomial, and max-plus barriers, the
tracked componentwise, lexicographic, mixed-coordinate, and weighted scalar-projection matrix bar-
riers, the projected-primary dominance and scalar-projection meta-theorems, and the symbolic
variable-condition / KBO barrier) rules out the entire formalized direct universe.
The escape
side (the dependency-pair projection escape, the transparency-essentiality witness on the Rec∆-
core, the nonlinear polynomial full-step witness, the specialized multiset path order (MPO), and
the escape trichotomy) characterizes which structural assumptions any successful response must im-
port.
[29] also keeps the norm-mismatch and seed-carrier factorization fragments of the schema-
level diagnostic layer as structural companions to its barriers.
The remaining diagnostic con-
tent (confession-dominance product form, proof-entropy monotonicity, schema-level operational in-
completeness, construction-versus-confession asymmetry, projection-transaction, and the witness-
language hierarchy with orientation-boundary predicate OB) is developed in this paper. The present
paper asks a different question about the same boundary:
What kind of inability does the duplicating step expose in the direct proof language, and
what does a sound response to that inability look like?
The answer we develop is operational inexpressibility: the proof system’s operational repertoire
cannot incorporate the step-argument dimension into any derivation whose conclusion bears on termi-
nation. The dimension is present, its value is determinate, and yet no derivation in the base language
both depends on it and constrains the termination verdict. This is not G¨odel incompleteness (which
is an expression-to-proof gap at the statement level), not Turing undecidability (which is the absolute
non-existence of a decision algorithm for a problem class), and not abstraction or lossy compression
(which are active engineering choices).
It is a structural mismatch between the proof language’s
operations and a specific dimension of its input.
Sound responses to operational inexpressibility split into two structurally distinct kinds. Construc-
tion methods (polynomial interpretations, path orderings) extend the operational repertoire with new
mathematical content, such as a polynomial or a symbol precedence, and verify the extended system
directly. Confession methods (dependency pairs) drop the unincorporable dimension under an exter-
nal meta-theoretic license and prove termination of the smaller residual problem. The asymmetry is
structural. Constructions add objects to the proof data; confessions subtract a dimension from the
input. The licensing theorems have different quantifier structures, different storage requirements at
the machine level, and different relationships to the base proof language’s expressiveness. We prove
that the confession response on the primitive recursion duplicator has the six-step structural shape of
G¨odel’s 1931 incompleteness move, and that within the analyzed family the duplicator is the unique
structurally complete member for which the confession step is required.
A quantitative analysis sharpens the boundary. Along the canonical trace of F(a, b, Sk(0)), each
firing of the recursive rule converts one unit of proof-certifiable counter structure into one unit of proof-
discarded payload structure. The confessed structural burden grows quadratically across the trace
(as the triangular number (k+1)(k+2)

Information access, sequentiality, and hidden progress
The meta-object separation can first be stated in information-theoretic terms and then in proof-
theoretic ones. A meta-level system asks the object level for a verdict only when that verdict is not
already directly retrievable from the meta-level’s accessible information. In that sense, the act of
querying is itself an information-seeking act. The object layer then matters only if it contributes
genuine uncertainty reduction through a sequential trace rather than by immediate retrieval.
Definition 2.1 (Meta-access model). Fix a finite verdict alphabet V and let X be a random variable
ranging over the object-level possibilities compatible with the meta-level information state. Let R(X) ∈
V be the target verdict, and let IM denote the sigma-algebra generated by the meta-level’s accessible
information. An object-level computation is represented by a trace of state-valued random variables
T0(X), T1(X), . . . , TK(X).
All entropies below are Shannon entropies computed relative to this epistemic distribution.
Definition 2.2 (Non-vacuous meta-query). A query from the meta level to the object level about the
target verdict R(X) is non-vacuous if
H(R(X) | IM) > 0.
Equivalently, the verdict is not already determined by the meta-level’s accessible information.
Proposition 2.3 (Information-seeking character of non-vacuous query). If a meta-level query is
non-vacuous, then there is verdict-relevant information not yet accessible at the meta level. If instead
H(R(X) | IM) = 0,
then the verdict is directly retrievable from the meta-level state and the query is informationally
vacuous.
Proof. By definition, H(R(X) | IM) > 0 means that the target verdict is not measurable with respect
to the meta-level information state alone. So some verdict-relevant information is still missing from
what the meta-level state makes available. Conversely, if H(R(X) | IM) = 0, then R(X) is measurable
with respect to IM, hence directly retrievable from the meta-level state. In that case no object-level
uncertainty reduction is required.
Definition 2.4 (Hypothesis form of a meta-query). Let A ⊆V be an acceptance set for the target
verdict, and define the binary hypothesis variable
χA(X) := 1R(X)∈A.
A meta-query about R(X) is in hypothesis form when its task is to decide whether χA(X) = 1. It is
non-vacuous in this form when
H(χA(X) | IM) > 0.
Thus the meta layer is not retrieving a stored verdict. It is asking the object layer, or a supervisor
over the object layer, to decide a binary obligation whose value is not already fixed by the accessible
state.
Proposition 2.5 (Non-vacuous hypothesis tests require new access). Suppose a hypothesis-form
meta-query is non-vacuous and is resolved after observing a trace state TK, so that
H(χA(X) | IM) > 0
and
H(χA(X) | IM, TK) = 0.
Then the trace carries positive conditional mutual information about the hypothesis:
I(χA(X); TK | IM) > 0.
Proof. By the definition of conditional mutual information,
I(χA(X); TK | IM) = H(χA(X) | IM) −H(χA(X) | IM, TK).
The first term is positive by non-vacuity, and the second term is zero by the assumed resolution at
TK.

Remark 3.2 (The opposed growth law and the information content of the confession). The obstruc-

3.3
Architectural necessity of the duplication
A structural prior fact stands behind the quantitative results above: the duplication is not an acciden-
tal feature of the particular rule studied here. It is the unique atomic move available to a first-order
base/step/counter schema that emits a per-step record frame while preserving its generator. Any rule
shape that attempts to emit the record without the duplication either erases the generator or breaks
the base/step/counter pattern.
The formal result used here is the minimal many-sorted positional theorem: a right-hand side with
both a new record frame and a recursive active site must contain two distinct generator occurrences.
The stronger semantic and information-theoretic remarks below are interpretations of that core, not
separate stronger theorem claims.

4.2
The boundary operator’s channel-preservation axiom
What distinguishes the recursor from a circular reference is not in the rewrite signature. It is the
existence, on the recursor side, of a strictly descending channel coordinate that an external observer
can identify and project onto. The abstract operation that performs the projection is the boundary
operator; its operational signature is recorded below for self-contained reference.
Definition 4.2 (Boundary operator). The boundary operator B is a partial map from a pre-commit
state space (the rule’s left-hand side together with its surrounding context) to a post-commit state space
paired with a record alphabet L of classical entries. Three axioms among the operator’s signature are
used essentially in the proofs of this section:
• Channel preservation. The channel coordinate is strictly descending and uniquely invertible
under B. Given the post-commit channel state, the pre-commit channel state is uniquely recoverable
up to channel gauge.
• Gauge covariance. For every permutation σ of payload positions at a given step, B ◦σ = B.
The record entry produced by B is invariant under payload permutation.
• Payload discarding. Payload multiplicity is not in the image of B. For any two pre-commit
states differing only in payload multiplicity, B produces the same record entry up to channel gauge.
The recursor’s counter coordinate Sk−i(0) →Sk−i−1(0) at step i satisfies the channel-preservation
axiom directly. A circular reference admits no such coordinate; its rule shape carries no slot whose
value descends along the rewrite, so it lies outside the domain of B. The boundary that separates the
two systems is therefore the membership predicate dom(B), gated by the channel-preservation axiom
of Definition 4.2.
Remark 4.3 (The boundary is the rule). Proposition 4.1 together with the channel-preservation
axiom answers the question of which rule distinguishes the recursor from a circular reference. No
internal rewrite rule in either system distinguishes them. The rule responsible is the boundary operator
B together with the licensing axioms that fix its domain. Inside dom(B) the recursor admits an external
projection onto its channel coordinate, which is what dependency pairs perform; outside dom(B), no
projection is available because no descending coordinate exists.

Definition 7.7 (Construction method). A termination method is a construction method if it extends
the proof language with a specific mathematical object (for example, a polynomial interpretation or
symbol precedence) and verifies the instance directly in the extended language.

Proposition 7.9 (Construction/confession asymmetry). Construction methods add an object to the
proof data and verify it. Confession methods do not add an object of the same kind; they project

two classes therefore use different quantifier structures and different proof objects even when both are
sound.
Proof. Polynomial interpretations and path orders quantify over witness objects such as polynomi-

Remark 7.10 (The confession is a declaration of internal inexpressibility at the dimension). Defini-

exclusive and structurally distinct. This remark records a further reading of the confession side. Call-
ing the wrapper dimension inert under the Arts and Giesl license is not a neutral bookkeeping move.
It is a formal declaration, ratified by an external metatheorem, that the base proof language cannot
internally resolve this dimension. In the vocabulary of operational inexpressibility (Definition 7.1):
the dimension is present, its value is determinate, no derivable statement of the base language both
depends on it and constrains the target verdict, and the external license then ratifies the admission that
the language is operationally inexpressive there. The structural parallel to G¨odel is exact at this level.
G¨odel’s system admits, under an external reflection principle, that it cannot internally derive its own
consistency; the dependency-pair framework admits, under the Arts and Giesl license, that the base
direct-aggregation language cannot internally resolve the step-argument dimension. Both admissions
take the form of an external license to cross a boundary that the internal language cannot cross on
its own. This is what gives “confession” in “confession method” its precise proof-theoretic content: a
formal dimension-level admission of internal inexpressibility, externally ratified.
Remark 7.11 (Why the projection cannot be rewritten as an ordinary axiom extension). A natural
follow-up question is whether the confession could be recast as simply adding an axiom to the base
language. It cannot, in a specific formal sense. Adding an axiom of the form “the step-argument
dimension is irrelevant to termination” would be internally indexable: the base language would contain
a derivation that depends on the dimension (namely the derivation of the new axiom applied to a
specific input) and constrains the target verdict, contradicting the operational-inexpressibility diagnosis
at πy. The dimension-level admission discharged by the Arts and Giesl license is not a statement about
the dimension. It is a projection of the dimension out of the proof obligation itself, after which the
residual problem no longer depends on the dimension at all. Construction methods import objects
that the base language can reason about internally; confession methods import a license to re-type the
proof obligation so that an entire dimension drops out of the base language’s scope. The two moves
differ in quantifier structure (Proposition 7.9) precisely because one adds to the proof data and the
other subtracts a dimension from the problem shape. This is also why the confession is not reducible

Remark 7.26 (Wrapper-accumulation irreversibility is not fixed-point-freeness of an endomap). The

them figures in the Lawvere and Yanofsky argument. The LCEL substrate separates them by placing
the G-wrapper’s accumulated content inside πirr, a projection-theoretic datum on the rewrite relation,
rather than inside any self-applicative internal-evaluation structure. Theorem 7.22 therefore does not
require, and does not claim, a Lawvere-style fixed-point argument on the dependency-pair side; the
reflection-family placement of Proposition 7.24 is consistent at exactly this point.

in the reflection family captures exactly the “internal inadequacy plus external license plus licensed
reimport” shape that Theorem 7.22 identifies.

distinct, and only the confession route discharges the operational-inexpressibility diagnosis. Hence a

Remark 7.28 (Diagonal component versus reflection component: terminology and scope). By “diag-

Definition 7.36 (Orientation boundary as projection-transaction). For the step-duplicating recursor

Proof. The retained dimension, external license, and forgetting witness are fixed data of a single

• The construction/confession asymmetry (Proposition 7.9) is the distinction between enriching the

These are three views of the same boundary structure. The projection-transaction description records

Remark 7.38 (Projection is licensed multiplicity collapse). The transaction does not assert that y

Distinctions from neighboring concepts
Operational inexpressibility is not the same as G¨odel incompleteness, Turing undecidability, abstrac-
tion, parametricity, or lossy compression.
Distinction from G¨odel incompleteness.
G¨odel incompleteness is an expression-to-proof gap
at the statement level. Operational inexpressibility is a query-level failure: the system cannot incor-
porate a specific input dimension into a verdict-bearing derivation.
Distinction from Turing undecidability.
Turing undecidability is a global impossibility result
about problem classes.
Operational inexpressibility is local to a proof system, an input, and a
dimension.
Distinction from abstraction.
Abstraction is a chosen simplification. Confession in the present
sense is a forced structural response to a proof-language mismatch.
Distinction from parametricity.
Parametricity is a strength claim about uniform behavior. Op-
erational inexpressibility is a weakness claim about failure to incorporate a dimension at all.
Distinction from lossy compression.
Lossy compression trades fidelity for compactness under
an application-level tolerance. Confession here is not a fidelity trade-off; it is a theorem-licensed
projection whose soundness is all-or-nothing.

rately. Its role here is applicative: it tests whether candidate outputs cross the formal boundary,
while this paper supplies the operational-inexpressibility, witness-order, and projection-transaction
theory behind it.

Conclusion
The paper’s central result is that the orientation boundary admits a precise structural diagnosis. In
the language of the trilogy, that boundary is the failure floor: the condition under which no proof
system can produce a sound termination verdict without either a W1 construction that imports global
comparison structure or a W2 confession that projects away the duplicating dimension under external
license. Below the floor, the admissible outputs in this taxonomy are typed abstentions. Above it,
the escape routes are theorem-backed. The boundary established in the companion development is
a theorem-backed schema-level frontier for the step-duplicating recursor rather than only a finite
KO7-local list of blocked methods: direct whole-term families and duplication-sensitive comparators
fail under reusable pump, transparency, and projection barriers; construction-style witness extractors
and confession-style escape theorems make the frontier explicit; and KO7 is the canonical certified
specialization.
In the vocabulary of this paper, that boundary is the event OBPRC(x) = 1, i.e.

Between the boundary and the successful witness lies the structural diagnosis introduced here:
operational inexpressibility.
The duplicator is an instance on which direct aggregation fails, and
more specifically an instance on which direct aggregation is operationally inexpressible at a spe-
cific dimension, the step argument, in the precise sense that no derivable statement of the base
proof language both depends on that dimension and bears on termination.
This is a query-level
failure, not an expression-to-proof failure in the G¨odelian sense. Yet the confession response to op-
erational inexpressibility has the six-step structural shape of G¨odel’s 1931 confession move, with the
dependency-pair soundness theorem playing the role of the meta-theoretic resolution step. Within
the analyzed primitive-recursion family, the primitive recursion duplicator is the unique structurally
complete member at which this confession shape and the construction/confession asymmetry are
required for producing a working termination proof.
The quantitative analysis sharpens the same point from two angles at once. Structurally, the
confessed burden grows quadratically while the residual proof work grows linearly, so the confession
becomes the dominant event in the proof. Information-theoretically, the direct whole-term carrier
accumulates a redundancy-heavy representation whose Shannon orbit entropy grows only logarithmi-
cally in the number of payload positions. The resulting inefficiency coefficient diverges on the direct
carrier, while the seed-carrier factorization criterion shows exactly what the dependency-pair projec-
tion preserves: not multiplicity of carriers, but the seed relevant to the residual termination question.
In this sense Shannon theory does not replace the proof-theoretic result; it validates it from a second

The constructive side of the argument is equally precise. The successful methods differ in proof-

verify the richer object. Confession methods project away a structurally unincorporable dimension

The witness hierarchy records exactly where this first shift

Three sharper results of [29] support the same point: the barrier appears on the smaller
Rec∆-core; the additive and affine barriers survive typed and many-sorted pumping settings; the
extracted dependency-pair problem admits a simple linear base order. What changes at the boundary
is therefore not that the residual problem becomes exotic, but that the original witness language
cannot express the correct control-focused presentation.
Two proof-theoretic refinements then place the entire diagnosis on its correct taxonomic ground.
The dependency-pair ascent is not a Lawvere and Yanofsky diagonal instance; it belongs to the
Feferman and Beklemishev reflection family, and the Arts and Giesl soundness license operates at the

below the ε0-scale ascent required by classical G¨odelian reflection over PA. The six-step structural
identity with G¨odel 1931 holds at the level of shape; the metatheoretic weight is sharply lighter. The
orientation boundary is thereby described as a static projection-transaction: generative trace, step-

The lesson of the primitive self-duplicating recursor is therefore narrow and strong.
