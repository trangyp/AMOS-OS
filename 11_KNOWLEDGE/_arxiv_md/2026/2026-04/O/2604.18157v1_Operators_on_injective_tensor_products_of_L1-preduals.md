1. Introduction
If 𝐸, 𝐹are Banach spaces and 𝐾a compact Hausdorff topological space, it
is well known that a bounded linear operator 𝑇: 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) →𝐹from the space
of all continuous 𝐸-valued functions on 𝐾to 𝐹admits a representing measure
𝐺: Borel(𝐾) →𝐿(𝐸, 𝐹∗∗) with finite semivariation ∥𝐺∥, see Section 2.3. We refer
the reader to [6], [23], and [5] for a detailed study of this representation. A recent
treatment of this representation can be found in [61].
The representing measure 𝐺serves as a tool for proving many results on 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸)-
spaces. The main feature of these proofs is the fact that an unconditionally con-
verging (see Section 2.4) operator 𝑇: 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) →𝐹is strongly bounded, i.e., the
semivariation ∥𝐺∥of the measure 𝐺is continuous at ∅, see [23]. Then it also holds
that 𝐺has values in 𝐿(𝐸, 𝐹). This leads to a question what is the relation between
properties of 𝑇and 𝐺. This problem was extensively studied by many authors, see
e.g. [71], [2], [1], [12], [9], [8], [11], [13], [53], [33], [32], [14], [31], [52].
There is a long series of papers devoted to the question what properties of a
Banach space 𝐸are transferred to the space 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸).
We mention a result in
[10] stating that if 𝐸is a Banach space with the separable dual, 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) has the
Dieudonn´e property.
This result was improved in [42] where they showed that
𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) possesses the Dieudonn´e property whenever 𝐸does not contain a copy
of ℓ1 (isomorphically). Further, [16] proved a result stating that 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) has the
Pelczynski property (V) provided 𝐸does not contain a copy of ℓ1 and has the
property (u). A different proof was provided in [75] and [26]. The paper [59] gives
the result that 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) has the Pelczynski property (V) whenever 𝐸is a separable
space with the property (V). It was shown in [41] that 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) has the property
semi-(V) provided 𝐸has the property (u). An interested reader can consult [67]
for a survey of related results. A number of results on 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces and strongly
bounded operators can be found in [32].
The space 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸) can be considered as the space of all affine continuous 𝐸-
valued functions defined on the convex (weak∗) compact set 𝑀1(𝐾) of Borel proba-
bility regular measures on 𝐾. Since this set is a Bauer simplex, one might consider
the problem of extending results on 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces to the context of spaces of
affine continuous 𝐸-valued functions defined on a compact convex set 𝐾(denoted
as 𝐴(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces). The first task is to establish the existence of a representing
measure 𝐺for an operator 𝑇: 𝐴(𝐾, 𝐸) →𝐹. This was done in [63], where such
a measure is constructed for 𝐾being a simplex. Moreover, the representing mea-
sure is constructed in such a way that it is boundary. Thus we may ask whether
analogous results for operators on 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces remain valid for 𝐴(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces
with 𝐾being a simplex. Since 𝐴(𝐾, 𝐸) can be identified with the injective tensor
product 𝐴(𝐾)b⊗𝜀𝐸of 𝐴(𝐾) with 𝐸, we may try to extend the theory even for these
spaces. For a simplex 𝐾, 𝐴(𝐾) is an example of an 𝐿1-predual, see [29, Proposition
3.23] and [45, Chapter 7, §23, Theorem 6]. A close relation between simplices and
𝐿1-preduals shows a paper [49] proving that a real 𝐿1-predual is 1-complemented
in 𝐴(𝐾, R) for a suitable simplex 𝐾. A development of a representation theory for
operators on injective tensor products 𝑋b⊗𝜀𝐸, where 𝑋is an 𝐿1-predual, can be
found in [68]. In particular, the following question is crucial:
Is the representing measure for an unconditionally converging operator on 𝑋b⊗𝜀𝐸
strongly bounded?

The aim of our paper is to further develop ideas from [68]. We extend an operator

known theorems for 𝐶(𝐾, 𝐸)-spaces.
The paper is organized as follows.
We present the necessary definitions and
notions in Section 2. Then we recall the notion of the representing measure for an
operator on 𝑋b⊗𝜀𝐸and present the known result that an unconditionally converging
operator on 𝑋b⊗𝜀𝐸is strongly bounded. The study of strongly bounded operators is

admits an extension to 𝐶(𝐵𝑋∗, 𝐸) with preservation of properties (Section 6). Then
we provide a survey of results accessible by our extension method for the 𝑋b⊗𝜀𝐸-
spaces. The last Section 8 is devoted to a particular case of 𝐿1-preduals 𝑋with
ext 𝐵𝑋∗scattered.
