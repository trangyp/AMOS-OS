ON THE JACOBIAN ALGEBRAS OF ZIEGLER PAIRS OF PLANE
ARRANGEMENTS
ALEXANDRU DIMCA1 AND PIOTR POKORA2
Abstract. We consider a Ziegler pair of plane arrangements, that is two plane
arrangements A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 in the projective space P3, such that
the intersection lattices L(A) and L(A′) are isomorphic, but the Betti numbers of
the minimal resolutions of their Jacobian algebras are not the same. We introduce
several properties for such pairs and relate them to cones over Ziegler pairs of line
arrangements in P2.

Definition 1.1. Two hyperplane arrangements A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 in Pn
are said to form a Ziegler pair if they have isomorphic intersection lattices L(A) and
L(A′), obtained from the corresponding central arrangements in Cn+1, and distinct
sets of graded Betti numbers coming from the minimal resolutions of their Jacobian
algebras M(f) and M(f ′).
A Ziegler pair A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 may have one or several of the following
additional properties.
(1) We say that the Ziegler pair A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 satisfies the condi-
tion (HP) if the Jacobian algebras M(f) and M(f ′) have the same Hilbert
polynomial, that is
dim M(f)k = dim M(f ′)k for any integer k ≥k0
for some k0 ∈Z.
(2) We say that the Ziegler pair A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 satisfies the condi-
tion (HF) if the Jacobian algebras M(f) and M(f ′) have the same Hilbert
function, that is
dim M(f)k = dim M(f ′)k for any integer k.
(3) We say that the Ziegler pair A : f = 0 and A′ : f ′ = 0 satisfies condition
(MDR) if the Jacobian algebras M(f) and M(f ′) have the same minimal

the realization space for R(Tn, C) with n ≥10 is isomorphic to the modular curve
X1(n) – for details please consult [3].
