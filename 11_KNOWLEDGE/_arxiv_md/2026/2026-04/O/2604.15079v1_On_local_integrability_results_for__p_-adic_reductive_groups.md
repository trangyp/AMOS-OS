ON LOCAL INTEGRABILITY RESULTS FOR p-ADIC REDUCTIVE
GROUPS
CHENG-CHIANG TSAI
Abstract. We present a short proof, based on local character expansions, of the
celebrated theorem of Harish-Chandra about local integrability of complex char-
acters of p-adic reductive groups. The proof gives an algebraic incarnation of the
local integrability that works for some coefficients different from C, verifies local
integrability in cases that appear not covered in the literature, and shows that a
character is locally-Lα for some specified α > 1 as in [GGH23].
1. Introduction
Let F be a non-archimedean local field. We fix a reductive group G over F (possibly
disconnected). Fix also a non-trivial additive character ψ : (F, +) →C×; for ease of
reading we will write our proof in the traditional language of complex coefficients, but
explain in Remark 3.2 that we can work with fields such as Qℓ, and, for exponentially
large ℓ, with Fℓ. Let π be a finitely generated admissible C-representation of G :=
G(F). For G and other p-adic reductive groups we will fix a Haar measure on each
of them, as well as an translation-invariant measure on each vector space over F; the
choices will never matter.
We assume Hypotheses (A), (B), (C) and (D) in §4, which states roughly that local
character expansions hold, and either char(F) = 0 or p does not divide |WG|. Our
goal is to prove the celebrated theorem [HC99, Theorem 16.3] of Harish-Chandra
about its character Θπ [HC99, Preface, p. x] that
(I) The distribution Θπ|Grs is represented [HC99, p. 1, (†)] by a locally constant
function Fπ ∈C∞(Grs)G.
(II) The function Fπ is locally-L1 on G.
(III) The distribution Θπ is represented by Fπ.

4. Hypotheses
Let F and G be as before. The hypotheses are (A), (B), (C) and (D) as follows:
(A) Either char(F) = 0, or char(F) = p does not divide the order of the absolute
Weyl group of G.
By [McN04, Proposition 48] this1 implies that Jordan
decompositions always exist in H = ZG(s) for any s ∈Gss. In particular we
do not treat the subtle case of GLp(Fp((t))) as in [AGK+26]. We also have
(1) Separability of adjoint actions so that orbital integrals can be defined
[McN04, Lemma 44, Theorem 45].
(2) The number of G-conjugacy classes of maximal F-tori in G is finite.
(3) Weyl integration formula holds.
(4) Regular semisimple elements are Zariski open dense [Kno, Moh03, Propo-
sition 3.8] for G as well as Lie H.
(5) h ∼= h∗as H-representations.
(6) h ∼= Z(h) × hder.
(7) For any X ∈hss, there exists sX ∈Gss such that ZH(X) = ZG(sX).
(B) For any subgroup H := ZG(s) for some s ∈Gss, we fix an H-equivariant map
expH : uH
∼−→UH where uH ⊂h (resp. UH ⊂H) is an H-invariant open
subset containing 0 (resp. 1), and such that (d expH)0 = idh.
