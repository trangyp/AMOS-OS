ON THE CLASSIFICATION OF PRODUCTS OF HILBERT SCHEMES OF
POINTS OVER A SURFACE
ARIJIT DEY, ARIJIT MUKHERJEE, AND ANUBHAB PAHARI
Abstract. Let S be a smooth projective surface over C and S[n] be the Hilbert scheme of n
points over S, for any positive integer n. Let a = (n1, . . . , nr) and b = (m1, . . . , ms) be two
distinct partitions of any positive integer n. We prove that, under certain conditions, the 2n-
dimensional schemes S[a] = S[n1] ×· · ·×S[nr] and S[b] = S[m1] ×· · ·×S[ms] are not isomorphic,
using invariants like Betti numbers, Hodge numbers and Euler characteristics of the individual
factors. We provide a complete classification of such product spaces for K3 surfaces using its
inherent symplectic structure. Consequently, we obtain a complete classification for products
of generalised Kummer varieties over any abelian surface.

1. Introduction
Let S be a smooth projective surface over C. We denote the Hilbert scheme of n points over
a surface S by S[n]. It is well known that S[n] is a smooth projective variety over C of dimension
2n and is a (crepant) resolution of the singularity of the nth symmetric product S(n) (cf. [7]). In
the last few decades, the study of these varieties has been a central topic in algebraic geometry,
differential geometry, number theory, and related areas.
In this paper, we deal with the classification of products of Hilbert schemes of points over
surfaces. We introduce the notation of these product schemes as we are going to use it repeatedly.
Let a := (n1, · · · , nr) be any r-tuple of positive integers. By S[a], we mean the following product:
S[a] := S[n1] × S[n2] × · · · × S[nr].
We call the product S[a] as the Hilbert scheme of points over S associated to a.
The spaces S[a] appeared several times in the literature. Oberdieck, Song and Voisin (cf. [16,
Theorem 0.1]) studied the spaces S[a] in the context of the cobordism class of a given compact
complex manifold. They showed that the complex cobordism class of any compact complex
manifold with trivial odd Chern classes is a unique combination with rational coefficients of
cobordism classes of S[a], where S is a K3-surface. In fact, one can replace the K3 surface with
any surface S whose first and second Chern numbers satisfies c1(S)2 = 0 and c2(S) ̸= 0, as the
cobordism class of S[a] depends only on these two Chern classes (cf. [6]). The same result holds if
one replaces S[a] by the varieties Kum[a](A)(:= Kumn1+1(A)×Kumn2+1(A)×· · ·×Kumnr+1(A)),
where Kumn+1(A) is the generalised Kummer variety (cf. Definition 2.6) of dimension 2n over
an abelian surface A (cf. [16]).

2. Classification of products of Hilbert scheme of points over a K3 surface
In this section, we completely classify the product of the Hilbert scheme of points S[a] of a
K3 surface S associated to a partition a of any positive integer, using its inherent symplectic
structure. The key idea is to note that S[a] is a simply connected compact Kähler manifold with
a Ricci-flat Kähler metric (cf. Lemma 2.4). Then we invoke the Beauville decomposition (cf.
Theorem 2.2) in the Kähler category to recover the uniqueness of the factors.
We first recall the following result by Yau (cf. [19, Theorem 2, p. 364]).
Lemma 2.1. Let X be a compact Kähler manifold with first Chern class c1(X) = 0. Then
for every Kähler class [ω], there is a unique Kähler metric gω, associated with [ω], with Ricci
curvature Ric(gω) = 0, i.e., the metric gω is Ricci-flat.
The following theorem is known as Beauville decomposition (cf. [1, Theorem 1, p.759]).
Theorem 2.2. Let X be a compact Kähler manifold with flat Ricci curvature. Then, the uni-
