Abstract. We establish new strong factorization properties for the smooth vectors
of representations of exponential solvable Lie groups on Fr´echet spaces. In particular,
our results improve upon the Dixmier-Malliavin factorization theorem for simply
connected nilpotent Lie groups.

The aim of this article is to extend and improve Theorem 1.1. Our main result is the
following strong bounded factorization theorem for exponential solvable Lie group rep-
resentations. We refer to Subsection 2.1 below for the precise definition of the algebra
of exponentially rapidly decreasing smooth functions K(G) on G, and to Subsection
2.2 for that of representations of exponential type on Fr´echet spaces.
Theorem 1.2. Let π be a representation of exponential type of an exponential solvable
Lie group G on a Fr´echet space E. Then, given a bounded subset B ⊂E∞, one can
always find χ ∈K(G) and another bounded subset B′ ⊂E∞such that B = Π(χ)(B′).
In particular, E∞= Π(K(G))E∞.
Besides holding for broader classes of representations on more general Lie groups
and yielding simultaneous strong factorization for elements in bounded sets, Theorem
1.2 also significantly improves upon Theorem 1.1 in the particular case of simply con-
nected nilpotent Lie groups G by employing the much finer convolution algebra K(G),
rather than the larger Schwartz space S(G) occurring in the Dixmier-Malliavin strong
factorization theorem.
We will divide the proof of Theorem 1.2 into several intermediate steps. In Section
3, we show the result for the special case of the real line G = R. The general case
of exponential solvable Lie groups will then be reduced to that of the real line in
Section 5 by combining an iteration method due to Dixmier and Malliavin with results
connecting K(G) and the convolution algebra K(g) on the Lie algebra g via special
exponential coordinates of the second kind. The latter tools shall be established in
Section 4.
We end this introduction by mentioning that it would be interesting to show Theorem
1.2 for other classes of Lie groups. In fact, we actually conjecture that the strong
bounded factorization property holds true for any connected Lie group:
Conjecture 1.3. The space of smooth vectors E∞associated with a representation of
exponential type of a connected Lie group G on a Fr´echet space has the strong bounded
factorization property with respect to K(G).
