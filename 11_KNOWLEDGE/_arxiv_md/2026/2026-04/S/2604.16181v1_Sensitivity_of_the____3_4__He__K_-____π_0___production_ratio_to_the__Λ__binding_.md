Abstract
We study the production of 3
ΛH and 4
ΛH in the 3,4He(K−,π0) reactions at pK−= 1.0 GeV/c
within the distorted-wave impulse approximation, using the optimal Fermi-averaged K−p →π0Λ
amplitude.
Because the 3
ΛH ground state is extremely weakly bound, the d–Λ wave function
becomes spatially extended. We calculate the integrated cross sections σlab and their ratio R34 =
σlab(3
ΛH)/σlab(4
ΛH) for forward angles θlab = 0◦–20◦. The production strength of 3
ΛH and the ratio
R34 are strongly sensitive to the Λ binding energy BΛ, which is constrained to be approximately
0.05–0.15 MeV by comparison with experimental data from the J-PARC E73 experiment. This
indicates that the 3He(K−,π0) reaction provides a sensitive probe of the weak binding of 3
ΛH.

I.
INTRODUCTION
The hypertriton 3
ΛH is the lightest Λ hypernucleus and also one of the most delicate
bound states in nuclear physics [1]. Its Λ binding energy BΛ = M(d) + mΛ −M(3
ΛH) is
extraordinarily small on the nuclear scale, so that the Λ wave function inevitably develops
a long tail.
This feature makes 3
ΛH a stringent benchmark for the ΛN interaction near
threshold and amplifies the impact of BΛ on observables that probe the spatial overlap with
a compact nuclear core.
Recently, the 3
ΛH ground state (g.s.) has drawn renewed attention, partly because its
weak binding is intertwined with the mesonic weak-decay and lifetime issue [2–5], and partly
because ab initio calculations based on modern NN and Y N interactions have reached a
level where sensitivity to long-range dynamics can be quantified in a controlled manner [6–8].
However, the precise value of the Λ binding energy BΛ in 3
ΛH has not yet been established
conclusively.
Several determinations of BΛ(3
ΛH) show a certain spread, including weak-
binding values from reaction analyses [5, 9], as well as larger values reported by the STAR
Collaboration [3] and by decay-pion spectroscopy at MAMI [10].
Production reactions provide such an opportunity to probe this feature. In particular, the
3He(K−,π0) reaction at forward angles is driven predominantly by the non-spin-flip ∆S = 0
elementary process K−p →π0Λ. Under these kinematics the reaction directly probes the
spatial overlap between the initial proton distribution in 3He and the final Λ distribution
in 3
ΛH. Consequently, if 3
ΛH is as loosely bound as suggested by emulsion [1, 5] and modern
analyses [2–4, 9, 10], its extended wave function should leave a visible imprint on the absolute
yield and, more clearly, on suitable cross-section ratios.
In this paper, we investigate the sensitivity of the 3
ΛH production strength to the Λ binding
energy BΛ within the distorted-wave impulse approximation (DWIA), employing the optimal
Fermi-averaged K−p →π0Λ amplitude. To reduce common systematic uncertainties, we
focus on the ratio of integrated cross sections
R34 = σlab(3
ΛH)/σlab(4
ΛH),
(1)
where 4
ΛH is produced under closely related conditions within the same theoretical frame-
work. We calculate the integrated cross sections σlab and the ratio R34 for forward angles,
and examine how the experimental value Rexp

B.
Cross-section ratio R34 and implications for BΛ
To minimize common normalization uncertainties we consider the ratio R34 defined in
Eq. (1). Figure 3 shows the calculated values R34 as a function of BΛ. Because the numer-
ator and denominator are evaluated within the same DWIA framework and share similar
elementary amplitudes and meson distortion, the dominant normalization effects largely
cancel in the ratio. Consequently, R34 provides a more robust observable than the absolute
cross sections while preserving sensitivity to the spatial extension of the d–Λ wave function.

The difference between the DWIA and PWIA results mainly reflects the effect of meson
distortion and recoil kinematics. We therefore regard it only as a rough indicator of the the-
oretical uncertainty associated with the reaction mechanism. Within this limitation, values
of BΛ significantly larger than 0.3 MeV appear to be disfavored in the present framework.
Using the DWIA curve as the baseline and the PWIA curve as an indicator of model
dependence, the data favor a loosely bound Λ hypernucleus with an extended Λ distribu-
tion. A sharper constraint will require improved few-body wave functions and dedicated
measurements of the ratio under closely matched kinematics. Nevertheless, the present re-
sult already demonstrates that the 3He(K−,π0) reaction provides a useful probe of BΛ. In
this sense, the present result is broadly consistent with weak-binding values of BΛ extracted
from reaction analyses of the same system. The STAR Collaboration [24] and recent pion
spectroscopy at MAMI [10], however, have reported substantially larger values. Within the
present DWIA framework, such a larger BΛ would imply a more compact Λ wave function

C.
Effect of the 4
ΛH binding energy
The extraction of the Λ binding energy of 3
ΛH in the present analysis relies on the theoret-
ical calculation of R34 = σlab(3
ΛH)/σlab(4
ΛH), as discussed in Sec. III B. In those calculations,
the Λ binding energy of 4
ΛH was taken to be 2.16 MeV, following the A1 Collaboration
result [21].
More recently, the STAR Collaboration [24] reported BΛ(4
ΛH) = 2.22 ± 0.06 (stat.) ±
0.14 (syst.) MeV, while the J-PARC E07 Collaboration [5] reported BΛ(4
ΛH) = 2.25 ±
0.10 (stat.) ± 0.06 (syst.) MeV. To estimate the sensitivity of the present analysis to this
input parameter, we have repeated the DWIA calculation of the 4
ΛH production cross section
in the forward angles θlab = 0◦–20◦using BΛ(4
ΛH) = 2.16 MeV and 2.25 MeV, respectively.
The resulting integrated cross sections are 63.1 µb for BΛ(4
ΛH) = 2.16 MeV and 63.9 µb for
BΛ(4
ΛH) = 2.25 MeV, with the corresponding ⟨r2
t-Λ⟩1/2 = 3.68 fm and 3.64 fm. As expected,
a slightly larger binding energy BΛ(4
ΛH) leads to a more compact wave function and hence
to a marginal increase of the production cross section σlab(4
ΛH). The change amounts to only
about 1.3%. Consequently, the theoretical value of the ratio R34 is reduced by a factor of
0.987 when the larger binding energy is adopted. This confirms that the present extraction
of BΛ(3
ΛH) is primarily governed by the spatial extension of the d–Λ wave function, while
the uncertainty associated with the input 4
ΛH binding energy plays only a minor role.
IV.
SUMMARY AND CONCLUSION
We have studied the production of 3
ΛH and 4
ΛH in the 3,4He(K−,π0) reactions at pK−=
1.0 GeV/c within DWIA using the optimal Fermi-averaged K−p →π0Λ amplitude. We have
emphasized the usefulness of the integrated cross-section ratio R34 = σlab(3
ΛH)/σlab(4
ΛH),
which is expected to be stable against common normalization uncertainties.
In conclusion, the production strength of 3
ΛH and the ratio R34 exhibit strong sensitivity to
BΛ, driven by the spatial extension of the d–Λ wave function. Comparison of the calculated
ratio R34 with the experimental data from the J-PARC E73 experiment indicates that the

ΛH is constrained to be approximately 0.05–0.15 MeV in the present
DWIA framework, supporting the picture of an extremely loosely bound Λ hypernucleus.
