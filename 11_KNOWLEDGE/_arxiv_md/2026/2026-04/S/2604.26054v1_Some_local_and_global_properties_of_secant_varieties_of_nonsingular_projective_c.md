SOME LOCAL AND GLOBAL PROPERTIES OF SECANT VARIETIES OF
NONSINGULAR PROJECTIVE CURVES
LAWRENCE EIN, WENBO NIU, AND JINHYUNG PARK
Abstract. The main goal of this paper is to study some local and global properties of secant
varieties of algebraic curves. These results complement our previous work [8] by addressing
issues given therein and providing solutions to problems raised subsequently. Specifically, we
show a description of tangent cones of secant varieties of curves, and compute the cohomology
groups of secant sheaves on symmetric products of curves, which answers a question posed in
[8] and leads to a recursive formula for Hilbert polynomials of secant varieties of curves. In
the appendix, we present a cohomological approach to arithmetical Cohen–Macaulayness of
secant varieties of curves, completing the proof in [8].
1. Introduction
Throughout the paper, we work over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic zero. Let
C be a nonsingular projective curve of genus g ≥0, and let L be a very ample line bundle
determining an embedding
C ,−→Pr := P(H0(L)), where r := r(L) = h0(L) −1.
Assume that deg L ≥2g + 2k + 1 for an integer k ≥0, and consider the k-th secant variety
Σk = Σk(C, L) ⊆Pr
of C ⊆Pr defined as the union of (k +1)-secant k-planes to C. Note that dim Σk = 2k + 1 and
the singular locus of Σk is Σk−1 unless (C, L) = (P1, OP1(2k + 1)) in which case Σk = P2k+1.
It has attracted considerable attention in last two decades on understanding the geometry
of secant varieties (see e.g., [3, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13, 14]).
In our previous work [8], we studied
singularities and syzygies of secant varieties of curves by settling several conjectures in this
area. In particular, we proved that Σk has normal Du Bois singularities, Σk ⊆Pr is projectively
normal, and Hi(Σk, OΣk(ℓ)) = 0 for i > 0 and ℓ> 0. The necessary basic known facts on
secant varieties of curves are reviewed in Section 2. In the sequel, we look at further local and
global properties of secant varieties of curves, which are complementary results to [8].
The first result that we establish in this paper is the description of tangent cones of secant
varieties of curves. The classical Terracini’s lemma (see e.g., [5, Theorem 1.1]) determines the
tangent spaces of secant varieties at general points. In our situation, if x ∈Σk \ Σk−1, then
there exists a unique effective divisor ξ ∈Ck+1 of degree k+1 such that x ∈⟨ξ⟩. Then Terracini
lemma says that the projective tangent space to Σk at x is ⟨2ξ⟩. If x ∈Σm \ Σm−1 in Σk, then
there exists a unique effective divisor ξ ∈Cm+1 such that x ∈⟨ξ⟩. We actually prove that the
“projective” tangent cone to Σk at x is a cone over the secant variety Σk−m−1(C, L(−2ξ)) with
the vertex ⟨2ξ⟩(see Theorem 3.2, in which the “projectivized” tangent cone is described). Our

formula for the Hilbert polynomials of secant varieties of curves (see Theorem 4.4). For the
