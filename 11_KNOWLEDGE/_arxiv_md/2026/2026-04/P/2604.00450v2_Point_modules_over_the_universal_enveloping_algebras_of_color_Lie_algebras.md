Abstract. Let k be an algebraically closed field with characteristic 0. In this paper,
we define the notion of a q′-Heisenberg normal element of a Z-graded k-algebra. This
q′-Heisenberg normal element gives the structure of some sets of modules related to
point modules. Also, we determine the set of point modules over an Artin–Schelter
regular algebra obtained as the universal enveloping algebra of a color Lie algebra.
Moreover, we give a concrete integer such that the inverse system of truncated point
schemes of it is constant.

A module called a point module over a connected N-graded finitely presented k-algebra
A generated in degree 1 is important from the following fact ([3]): The set of closed points
of the point scheme of A corresponds one-to-one to the set of isomorphism classes of point
modules over A. In this paper, in order to study a point module, we define the notion of
a q′-Heisenberg normal element of a Z-graded k-algebra.
Definition (Definition 3.1). Let A be a Z-graded k-algebra and g a regular homoge-
neous normal element of A with degree n ≥1. A regular homogeneous normal element g
is called a q′-Heisenberg normal element of A, if there exist u ∈k×, x ∈A1, and y ∈An−1
satisfies the following equations;
(i) g = xy −uyx;
(ii) 0 = xg −ugx;
(iii) 0 = gy −uyg.
The relations in the above definition of a q′-Heisenberg normal element are similar to
defining relations of another q-analog of the universal enveloping algebra of the Heisenberg
algebra (cf. Kirkman–Small [13]). To emphasize a correspondence to another case, we
denote q′ instead of simply denoting q. This q′-Heisenberg normal element gives us the
structure of some sets of modules having a relationship to a point module. Assume that
A is a connected N-graded finitely presented k-algebra generated in degree 1 and g is
a q′-Heisenberg normal element of A. Proposition 3.7 shows that the set of base-point
modules over A completely overlaps one of A/⟨g⟩. Also, in Theorem 4.3, we obtain a
criterion for the nonexistence of a truncated point module. Therefore, we obtain the fact
that some set of truncated point modules over A completely overlaps one of A/⟨g⟩. In
Theorem 4.5, using this result, we determine the structure of the set of closed points of
the point scheme of the universal enveloping algebra of color Lie algebras.
For a connected N-graded finitely presented k-algebra A, Artin, Tate, and Van den
Bergh raised the question [3, Question 3.16] as follows: When A is noetherian, is the
inverse system {Γi(A)} constant for large i? A partial answer to this question was given
by Artin and Zhang [5]: When A is strongly noetherian, {Γi(A)} is constant for some
i > 0. In Corollary 4.6, for the universal enveloping algebra U(L) of a color Lie algebra
L, we give a concrete integer i such that {Γi(U(L))} is constant. Note that this integer i
is obtained by a property of the color Lie algebra.
This paper is organized as follows: In Section 2, we recall some definitions and prop-
erties of the quasi-Veronese algebra defined by Mori [18], the twisted algebra defined by
Zhang [31], the dehomogenization, point modules, and color Lie algebras. In Section 3,
we define the notion of a q′-Heisenberg normal element and prove basic properties. In
Proposition 3.7, we prove that the non-existence of a g-torsionfree base-point module for
a q′-Heisenberg normal element g. In Section 4, we prove Theorem 4.3, which is a theorem
of the nonexistence of a g-torsionfree module with some conditions. By Theorem 4.3, we
determine the set of closed points of the point schemes of the universal enveloping algebra
of color Lie algebras in Theorem 4.5. At the end of this section, we show that the inverse
system of the truncated point schemes of it is constant in Corollary 4.6.
