Plücker degrees of Quot schemes
SAMUEL STARK
Abstract
We study the Plücker degree of the main component of the Quot scheme of
length l quotients of a locally free sheaf on a smooth projective scheme S of di-
mension d ⩾1. This degree is determined by classes in the Chow ring of the sym-
metric product S(l), which are given by the pushforward of the powers of c1(O[l])
with respect to the canonical morphism from the Quot scheme to S(l). We describe
a decomposition of these classes, allowing us to compute the (in a certain sense)
leading term of the Plücker degree. We also obtain a higher-dimensional analogue
of a classical result of Schubert.

1.1
Notations
The ideas and results presented in this section come from Grothendieck’s paper [15].
Our notations are essentially the same as in [31], which we briefly recall for the benefit
of the reader. We denote by E a locally free sheaf of rank r on a smooth projective
irreducible d-dimensional scheme S over C.
The Quot scheme Quotl
S(E) is the fine moduli scheme of length l coherent sheaf
quotients of E; a point q of Quotl
S(E) thus corresponds to a quotient E →Qq of
coherent sheaves, where Qq satisfies dim(Qq) = 0 and dim H0(Qq) = l. As we have
indicated in the introduction, we are primarily interested in the main component of
Quotl

i=1 Osi. It

to as the principal or geometric component) is better behaved [16, 8, 3]; for us, it is
important that is has the right dimension, i.e. lp with p = r −1 + d. (We refer to [19]
