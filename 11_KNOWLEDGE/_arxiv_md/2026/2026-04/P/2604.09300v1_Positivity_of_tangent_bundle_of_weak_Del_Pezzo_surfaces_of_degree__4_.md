Positivity of tangent bundle of weak Del Pezzo
surfaces of degree 4
Qimin Zhang
Abstract. In this paper, we prove that for any weak Del Pezzo surface S of
degree at least 4, the tangent bundle TS is almost nef. For the proof, we use
total dual VMRTs induced by conic bundle structures.

Introduction
The positivity properties of the tangent bundle play an important role in the
birational and differential–geometric classification of projective varieties. For
example, a famous result of Mori [Mor79] states that a smooth projective variety
with ample tangent bundle is isomorphic to the projective space Pn.
Motivated by this theorem, weaker positivity conditions on the tangent bun-
dle have been widely studied. In particular, for projective manifolds with nef
tangent bundle, Campana and Peternell obtained a classification of smooth sur-
faces with nef tangent bundle and conjectured that any projective manifold with
nef tangent bundle should be homogeneous [CP91, DPS94].
More recently, attention has turned to even weaker positivity conditions,
such as pseudo-effectivity and almost nefness of the tangent bundle. Recall that
for a projective manifold X, a vector bundle E is said to be pseudo-effective if
the tautological line bundle OP(E)(1) is pseudo-effective. We also recall that:
Definition 1.1. Let X be a projective manifold. A vector bundle E is said to be
almost nef if there exists a countable family of proper closed subsets {Zi ⊊X}
such that for every curve C ⊂X with C ̸⊂S
i Zi, the restriction E|C is nef.
These two notions of positivity are closely related. On a projective manifold,
a line bundle is pseudo-effective if and only if it is almost nef. In contrast, for
vector bundles, almost nefness implies pseudo-effectivity, whereas the converse
generally fails [DPS94, DPS01, BDPP13].
There are several classification results for projective manifolds with almost
nef tangent bundle, although the general picture is still incomplete.
In the
surface case, Iwai obtained a classification of smooth projective surfaces without
(−1)-curves whose tangent bundle is almost nef [Iwa22, Proposition 4.11]. More
precisely, if S is such a surface, then TS is almost nef if and only if S is one
of the following: up to a finite ´etale cover, an abelian surface; a minimal ruled
surface over either P1 or an elliptic curve; P2.

For surfaces containing (−1)-curves the situation is more subtle, even for
weak Del Pezzo surfaces the situation is not clear. For a (strong) Del Pezzo
surface S of degree d [HLS22, Theorem 1.2] shows that
TS is pseudo-effective ⇐⇒d ≥4.
Moreover, combining the techniques of [HLS22, KKL24], we have the following:
for a Del Pezzo surface S of degree d,
TS is almost nef ⇐⇒d ≥4.
The case of degree 4 is thus a boundary case for this theory, it is also interesting
for the existence for the Lagrangian fibration of ΩX [KL26, BEH+24]. In this
paper, we prove the following result.
Theorem 1.2 (Main Theorem). Let S be a weak Del Pezzo surface of degree 4.
Then the tangent bundle TS is almost nef.
As an immediate corollary of Theorem 1.2, if S is a weak Del Pezzo surface
of degree at least 4, then TS is almost nef.
For the strict Del Pezzo surfaces the result is based on a decomposition of
elements in |OP(TS)(2)|. There are five pairs of total dual VMRTs, such that the
sum of each pair is |OP(TS)(2)|. However, for weak Del Pezzo surfaces of degree 4,
we do not have this good property. Thus, understanding the almost nefness of
the tangent bundle therefore requires a more refined geometric analysis.
Strategy of the proof. In the strategy, we work on a weak Del Pezzo surface
S of degree 4. Our approach is inspired by [HLS22, KKL24]. Let
π : P(TS) →S
be the natural projection. The basic idea is to construct an effective divisor
D ∈|kζ −π∗E|,
where k ∈Z>0 and E is an effective divisor on S, such that ζ|D is pseudo-
effective. We refer to Proposition 2.2 for a precise formulation.
By [HLS22, Corollary 2.13], one can construct effective divisors
Di ∈|ζ −π∗Tfi|
associated with conic bundle structures fi : S →P1. These divisors can be
interpreted as total dual VMRTs. The strategy is therefore to find several such
divisors Di satisfying:
• ζ|Di is pseudo-effective for each i;
• the sum P Di belongs to a linear system of the form |kζ −π∗E| with E
effective.

To implement this strategy, we proceed as follows. In Section 2, we establish
a criterion to ensure that ζ|D is pseudo-effective (Proposition 2.7 (3)).
The
main part of our effort is then to describe all the conic bundle structures on S:
let f : S →P1 be a conic bundle structure, and let F be a general fibre. If
µ : S →P2 is the blow-up map and H denotes the hyperplane class on P2, then
F · µ∗H ∈{1, 2}
by Proposition 4.3. Accordingly, we say that the conic bundle structure is of
degree 1 or 2.
Conic bundle structures of degree 1 always exist on S.
In contrast, the
existence of conic bundle structures of degree 2 is equivalent to the existence of
four points in good position, i.e. no three of them are colinear in the generalized
sense (see Definition 3.14).
We introduce the notation of good position to distinguish different types of
weak Del Pezzo surfaces (Definition 4.6), it might be interesting for weak Del
Pezzo surfaces of other degree.
In Section 5, we show that for every divisor Di arising from a conic bundle
structure, the restriction ζ|Di is pseudo-effective.
Finally, in Section 6, we prove the main theorem. If there exist four points in
good position, we construct divisors D1 and D2 corresponding to conic bundle
structures of degree 1 and 2, respectively, such that
D1 + D2 ∈|2ζ −π∗(Tf1 + Tf2)|
and Tf1 + Tf2 is effective. If no such four points exist, then we show that there
exists a degree 1 conic bundle structure f such that
D1 ∈|ζ −π∗Tf|
and Tf is effective. In both cases, we conclude that TS is almost nef.
