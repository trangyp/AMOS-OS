Koszul Duality for Quadratic Monomial Algebras
M. Bouhada
Abstract
This paper provides a new class of examples for the Koszul dualities established
in [5]. We study quadratic monomial algebras from the perspective of Koszul duality,
with particular emphasis on finitely presented and finitely copresented graded modules
over the Koszul dual algebra.
For a finite-dimensional quadratic monomial algebra Λ, we prove that the Koszul
dual Λ! is both left coherent and left co-coherent, and that finitely presented (resp.
finitely copresented) modules coincide with perfect (resp. coperfect) modules. As a
consequence, the associated tails and cotails categories are abelian and hereditary, and
admit explicit structural descriptions.
We further show that quadratic monomial algebras are absolutely Koszul and have
global linearity defect at most one. In particular, every finitely presented module has
rational Poincar´e and Hilbert series.
Building on these results, we refine the graded derived and singular Koszul dualities,
as well as the graded BGG correspondence, by giving explicit realizations of the asso-
ciated triangulated equivalences. These equivalences induce nonstandard t-structures
whose hearts admit concrete descriptions in terms of linear complexes and moduless.
We also obtain corresponding refinements in the ungraded setting.

Keywords: Koszul duality, quadratic monomial algebras, derived categories, singularity
categories, t-structures, BGG correspondence.

Introduction
Quadratic monomial algebras form a distinguished class of graded algebras that arise natu-
rally in several areas of mathematics, including the representation theory of finite-dimensional
algebras, the theory of semisimple Lie algebras, and homological mirror symmetry. In the
representation theory of associative algebras, they provide a rich source of examples and
counterexamples for testing conjectures and investigating homological phenomena. For in-
stance, it is well known that, for monomial algebras, the big finitistic dimension need not
coincide with the small finitistic dimension, while the latter is always finite; see [12, 29].
In the context of semisimple Lie algebras, quadratic monomial algebras already appear in
classical situations. For example, the principal block O0 of the Bernstein–Gelfand–Gelfand
category O associated with sl2(C) is equivalent to the category of finite-dimensional modules
over the algebra given by the quiver

see [23, Theorem 5.3.1].
Moreover, in the framework of homological mirror symmetry, quadratic monomial alge-
bras are closely related to Fukaya categories; see [4, 14, 19]. In both the commutative [11]
and noncommutative [13] settings (see also Theorem 2.3), these algebras are known to be
Koszul: every simple module generated in degree 0 admits a linear projective resolution, and
their Koszul duals are again quadratic monomial algebras. This self-duality property makes
them particularly well suited for the study of Koszul duality phenomena.
Despite their importance, comparatively little is known about the bounded derived cat-
egories of quadratic monomial algebras. Apart from the general Koszul duality framework
developed in [5], no explicit description of these categories is currently available in this level
of generality. Much of the existing literature has therefore focused on special subclasses,
such as gentle algebras, motivated in part by their connections with Fukaya categories; see,
for example, [14, 19].
The aim of this paper is to provide a systematic study of quadratic monomial algebras
from the perspective of Koszul duality. More precisely, this work continues the program initi-
ated in [5], where several classes of algebras were shown to admit refined forms of derived and
singular Koszul dualities. We show that quadratic monomial algebras exhibit particularly
favorable duality properties within this framework.
Our approach is based on a detailed analysis of the categories of finitely presented and
finitely copresented graded modules over the Koszul dual algebra Λ!. We prove that, for
a finite-dimensional quadratic monomial algebra Λ, the algebra Λ! is both coherent and
co-coherent, and that finitely presented (resp. finitely copresented) modules coincide with
perfect (resp. coperfect) modules. As a consequence, the associated tails and cotails cate-
gories are abelian and hereditary. Furthermore, we show that quadratic monomial algebras
are absolutely Koszul, have global linearity defect at most one, and that every finitely pre-
sented module has a rational Poincar´e series.
We also show that finitely presented (resp. finitely copresented) modules are precisely

The aim of this section is to review the basic definitions and properties that will be used

Hom-sets are k-vector spaces and whose composition maps are bilinear over k. Throughout,
Λ denotes a quadratic monomial algebra, which may be either locally finite-dimensional
or finite-dimensional, and is regarded as a k-linear category. Unless stated otherwise, all
Λ-modules are assumed to be left modules, viewed as covariant k-linear functors.
