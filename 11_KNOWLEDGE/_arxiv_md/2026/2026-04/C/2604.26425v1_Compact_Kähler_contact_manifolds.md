projective setting. Roughly speaking, the arguments in [KPSW00, Dem02] rest
on three core ingredients: Demailly’s Frobenius integrality theorem [Dem02], the
Ionescu–Wi´sniewski inequality [Kol96, IV, § 2], and the contraction theorem in
Mori theory.
In the K¨ahler setting, Demailly’s integrality theorem holds true, and the
Ionescu–Wi’sniewski inequality can be derived using the same arguments as for
projective manifolds, with suitable adaptations via Douady/Barlet spaces (see § 2
for details). However, the contraction theorem for general K¨ahler spaces remains
open, except in dimensions ď 3.
On the other hand, combining results from
[Dem02] and [Ou25] implies that every compact K¨ahler contact manifold X is
uniruled. We therefore consider the maximally rationally connected quotient of
X, instead of appealing to the contraction theorem. This allows us to apply a
result of C. Araujo [Ara06, ADK08] to construct a dominant family of projective
spaces contained in X, which we then show induces a projective bundle structure
of X.
Throughout this paper, for a compact complex space X, we will denote by BpXq
and DpXq its Barlet space and Douady space, respectively. For a vector bundle
E, we denote by PE its projectivization in the sense of Grothendieck.
