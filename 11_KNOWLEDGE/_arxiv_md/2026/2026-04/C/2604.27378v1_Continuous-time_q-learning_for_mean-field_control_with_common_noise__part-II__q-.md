Abstract
This paper is a continuation work of Ren et al. (2026) aiming to further devise q-learning
algorithms for mean-eld control (MFC) with controled comon noise. Based on the re-
laxed control formulation, we rst establish the martingale condition of the value function
and the Iq-function by evaluating along the conditional state distributions generated by al
test policies. As the data in the relaxed control formulation are not observable in practice, we
quantify the eror incured when they are replaced by the observable ones in the exploratory
formulation under discretely sampled actions. This, together with a two-layer xed point
characterization of an optimal policy in Ren et al. (2026), alows us to propose several al-
gorithms including the Actor-Critic q-learning algorithm, in which the policy is updated in
the Actor-step based on the iteration rule induced by the improved Iq-function, and the
value function and Iq-function are updated in the Critic-step based on the martingale or-
thogonality condition using the data from the exploratory formulation. We also establish the
convergence of the iner iterations in the Actor-step in an innite-horizon linear quadratic
(LQ) framework. In two examples, within and beyond LQ framework, our q-learning algo-
rithms are implemented with satisfactory performance.
Keywords:

learning algorithm, Actor-Critic q-learning algorithm

vehicles on the road and the central bank that sets economic policy such as tax rates to maximize
a social welfare function.

beyond the LQ framework. In both examples, we are able to derive the explicit form of
the optimal policy and the resulting optimal value function and the optimal Iq-function,
alowing us to obtain the exact parameterizations of these target functions.
