Abstract. Let χ be a complex irreducible character of a finite group G. The
conductor of χ, denoted cpχq, is the smallest positive integer n such that χpxq P
Qpexpp2πi{nqq for all x P G. We show that for certain rank 1 finite groups of Lie
type, the conductor cpχq is realized at a single group element; that is, there exists
g P G such that cpχq “ cpχpgqq. In some quasisimple cases, we further prove that
the field of values Qpχq is generated by a single value. This phenomenon, which is
related to a well-known conjecture of W. Feit, was recently observed by Boltje et
al. in their reduction of the conjecture to finite simple groups. Our approach uses
techniques from algebraic number theory together with the known character tables
of these groups.

irrational values, and much of the paper therefore draws on algebraic number theory.
