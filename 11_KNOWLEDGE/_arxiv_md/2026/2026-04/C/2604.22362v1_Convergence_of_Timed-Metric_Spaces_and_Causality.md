CONVERGENCE OF TIMED-METRIC SPACES AND CAUSALITY
MAURICIO CHE AND RAQUEL PERALES
Abstract. We introduce the notion of timed Gromov–Hausdorff distance for timed-metric
spaces. We prove that this distance is bilipschitz equivalent to the intrinsic timed-Hausdorff
distance of Sakovich–Sormani, and therefore induces the same notion of convergence. We
establish a compactness theorem for the timed Gromov–Hausdorff distance, obtained as a
straightforward consequence of Gromov’s classical compactness theorem.
We then investigate the causal structure of timed-metric spaces and the stability of
causality under intrinsic timed-Hausdorff convergence. We further analyze causally-null
timed-metric spaces and develop several of their basic properties. As a curiosity, we
include in an appendix Gromov’s original proof of his compactness theorem, as presented
in his paper on groups of polynomial growth.

Our paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review Gromov–Hausdorff distance,
Fréchet maps, timed-metric spaces, timed-Fréchet maps, intrinsic timed-Hausdorff distance
and Gromov’s precompactness for intrinsic timed-Hausdorff distance. In Section 3, we
prove Theorem 3.2, which asserts that Definition 1.3 is equivalent to the notion previously
introduced by Sakovich–Sormani in [SS25, Definition 4.22]. We also show that the triangle
inequality holds for this new notion. Further, we prove Theorem 1.4.
In Section 4 we show that the causal structure induced by a timed-metric space defines
a partial order, give the proof of Corollary 1.7, and reformulate both second items of
Corollary 1.5 and Corollary 1.7, for the intrinsic timed-Hausdorff distance using addresses
in the sense of Definition 2.12. Moreover, in Propositions 4.5 and 4.6, we establish two
results that are analogous to [SV16, Corollary 3.19] and to part of [SV16, Lemma 3.20],
concerning curves that are distance realizers in causally-null timed-metric spaces.
In Section 5 we study the null distance and causally-null timed-metric spaces. In
particular, we give the proof of Proposition 1.9. Then we see that [KS22, Lemma 3.5]
can be adapted to provide a sufficient condition for obtaining a causally-null timed-metric
space. We conclude the paper proving Gromov’s Compactness and Embedding Theorem
[Gro81], which we use to prove Theorem 1.4.
