Contractive analytic self-mappings of the disc
Artur Nicolau
Abstract
Analytic self-maps of the unit disc whose hyperbolic derivative is
uniformly bounded by a constant smaller than one, are called con-
tractive. We describe these maps in terms of their Aleksandrov-Clark
measures and in terms of their inner-outer factorization. In addition,
we show that contractive inner functions can be described in terms
of a certain mixing property of its boundary values. We also present
other results on the boundary behavior of contractive inner functions.

Finally, we present some natural examples of contractive analytic self-
mappings.
A domain Ω⊂D will be called a contractive domain if any
analytic mapping f : D →Ωsatisfies sup{Dh(f)(z) : z ∈D} < 1. We will
show that a domain Ω⊂D is contractive if and only if there exists a constant
R = R(Ω) > 0 such that Ωdoes not contain any hyperbolic disc of hyper-
bolic radius R. This is analogous to the well known description of Bloch
domains from [ACP]. Contractive analytic mappings also appear naturally
when studying composition operators in the classical Bloch space. We will
also give other natural examples of inner functions and discuss when they are
contractive and of bounded compression, which were considered in [IN25].
This paper is organized as follows. Theorem 1.1 and the corresponding
remarks are discussed in Section 2. Section 3 is devoted to the proofs of
Theorems 1.2 and 1.3. Theorems 1.4 and 1.5 are proved in Section 4. Fi-
nally Section 5 contains the description of contractive domains, composition
operators with contractive symbols and other natural examples.
