0. Introduction
Let π : X →C be a P1-bundle over a smooth projective curve C of genus g. Let C0 be a minimal section
of π with (C2
0) = −e and f a fiber of π. Then KX ≡−2C0 −(2 −2g + e)f. If ((KX + f) · H) < 0. then
Ext2(E, E(−f)) = 0 for all µ-semi-stable sheaves. Thus E is a prioritary sheaf in the sense of Walter. Then
Walter [7] proved the irreducibility by using deformation theory of prioritary sheaves.
In this note, we shall slightly generalize the irreducibility of the moduli spaces. Thus we shall prove the
irreducibility of the moduli spaces under the assumption (KX · H) < 0. We note that (KX · H) < 0 for any
ample divisor H, if g = 0, 1. Indeed −KX is numerically equivalent to an effective divisor. If g = 0, then
−KX −f is also effective, and hence the irreducibility is a consequence of [7]. On the other hand if g = 1,
then −KX −f is numerically equivalent to an effective divisor if and only if e ≥1. So we are interested in
the case where e = 0, −1. We also study the existence condition of the µ-semistable sheaves in these cases.
For a coherent sheaf on X, rk E denotes the rank of E and we set τ(E) := (rk E, c1(E), χ(E)). Let
MH(e)µss be the moduli stack of µ-semi-stable sheaves E with τ(E) = e. Then we have the following
result.
