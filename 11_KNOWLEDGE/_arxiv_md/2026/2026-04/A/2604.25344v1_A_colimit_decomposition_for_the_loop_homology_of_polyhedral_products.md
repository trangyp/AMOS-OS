A COLIMIT DECOMPOSITION FOR THE LOOP HOMOLOGY OF
POLYHEDRAL PRODUCTS
LEWIS STANTON AND FEDOR VYLEGZHANIN
Abstract. We show that the loop homology algebras of polyhedral products of the form (X, ∗)K
can be written as a colimit over the flagification of K, and obtain a similar result for the
Poincar´e series.
This effectively reduces the study of the algebras H∗(Ω(X, ∗)K) to the case
of 1-neighbourly simplicial complexes. We give presentations of the loop homology of Davis–
Januszkiewicz spaces (i.e. Yoneda algebras of Stanley–Reisner rings) and calculate the Poincar´e
series of looped polyhedral products associated to various families of simplicial complexes, in-
cluding HMF-presented complexes and skeleta of flag complexes.
1. Introduction
Polyhedral products are a family of spaces which have been the subject of intense study recently
due to their many applications across mathematics. One particular object of study has been their
pointed loop space, and much work has been done to study them from various perspectives [BBC20].
These include aspects of their homotopy theory [Th17], and their connections to commutative
algebra [Vy22] and geometric group theory [PRah24].
In this paper, we focus on commutative algebra by studying the loop homology of a family of
polyhedral products. To proceed further, we state the definition of a polyhedral product. Let K be
a simplicial complex on the vertex set [m] = {1, . . . , m}, and let (X1, A1), · · · , (Xm, Am) be pairs
of pointed CW-complexes. For a face I ∈K, define (X, A)I = Qm

In this paper, the main object of study is Ω(X, ∗)K. When K is a flag complex, the homotopy

the loop homology was given in [Ca24]. For arbitrary K, information about the torsion appearing

In this paper, we expand out understanding of the algebra structure of H∗(Ω(X, ∗)K) by giving a
general colimit decomposition of H∗(Ω(X, ∗)K) for any simplicial complex K, generalising a result
of [Dob09, Ca24]. For a simplicial complex K, let Kf be the flag complex on the 1-skeleton of
K. It was shown in [St25] that the homotopy type of Ω(X, ∗)K is essentially determined by the
homotopy type of Ω(X, ∗)KI, for all I ∈Kf. We prove an algebraic analogue of this result, which
gives a colimit decomposition of H∗(Ω(X, ∗)K) over all faces of Kf.

is a free k-module. Then the natural map of Hopf algebras

In the case of the Davis–Januskiewicz space and a field k, the Stanley–Reisner ring associated

Theorem 1.1 gives a colimit decomposition of such Ext algebras (Corollary 5.3), and
Theorem 1.2 can be used to prove a more explicit formula for the Poincar´e series in certain cases.
We also show that the algebra Extk[K](k, k) is multiplicatively generated by Ext1 and Ext2 for a
wide class of complexes (Theorem 5.4), extending a result of Conner and Shelton [CoS12].
In addition, we give explicit presentations of H∗(ΩDJK; k) as a graded Hopf algebra when K is
a HMF-presented simplicial complex (see Definition 4.3). The class of HMF-presented complexes
are closely connected to homology missing face complexes introduced by Beben and Grbi´c [BG21]
and totally homology fillable complexes introduced by Iriye and Kishimoto [IK19]. Theorem 1.1
allows us to obtain a presentation of H∗(ΩDJK; k) under a weaker assumption that KI is HMF-
presented for each I ∈Kf (see Theorem 5.6). This assumption holds for k-skeleta of a flag simplicial
complexes, so we upgrade the module information of H∗(ΩDJK; k) from [St24] to a presentation
of H∗(ΩDJK; k) as an algebra in this case.
Finally we extend the aforementioned results to weighted polyhedral products, a generalisation
of polyhedral products defined by So, Stanley and Theriault [SST25]. In this context, we give a
presentation of certain weighted polyhedral products associated to graphs, and obtain a loop space
decomposition in the case of a tree.
In Section 2, we state some algebraic and categorical results we will require, as well as defining
the weighted polyhedral product. In Section 3, we prove the main technical result behind Theorem
1.1, namely that the loop homology functor preserves pushouts of maps admitting left homotopy
inverses. In Section 4, we define HMF-presentable complexes and prove some general properties
of the associated H∗(ΩDJK). Section 5 contains the proof of Theorem 1.1, and this theorem is
applied to give presentations of H∗(ΩDJK) in certain cases. Applications to the Poincar´e series
are given in Section 6 and generalisations to weighted polyhedral products associated to graphs
are considered in Section 7.

Another result, which is important in the proof of Theorem 3.6, is the description of the loop

Theorem 2.3. Let X1, X2 be simply connected CW-complexes such that each H∗(ΩXi; k) is
a free k-module. Then the natural map H∗(ΩX1; k) ∗H∗(ΩX2; k) →H∗(Ω(X1 ∨X2); k) is an
isomorphism.
Proof. By [AH, Theorem 2.1], each simply connected CW-complex X admits an Adams–Hilton
model AH(X) – a dg-algebra such that the underlying algebra is a free tensor algebra with
generators corresponding to cells of X, and H(AH(X)) ∼= H∗(ΩX; k). By [AH, Corollary 2.2],

free products of dg algebras with torsion-free homology.
