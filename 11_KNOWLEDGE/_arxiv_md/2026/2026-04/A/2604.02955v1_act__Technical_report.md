from the set {U, T}, denoting whether the reference appears in an untimed or timed judgement. An
untimed state is a single state 𝑠U = 𝑠and a timed state is a pair of pre- and post-states
𝑠T = (𝑠pre,𝑠post). We will omit the tag 𝑡from the rest of the judgements, assuming they just
propagate the same tag on the states, as given. The tag 𝑡𝑝∈{U, pre, post} denotes the timing of
the evaluated reference.
