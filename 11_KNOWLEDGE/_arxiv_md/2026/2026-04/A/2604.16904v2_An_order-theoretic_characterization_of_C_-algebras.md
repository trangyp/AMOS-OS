Abstract
We give an order-theoretic characterization of the essential image of the forgetful functor from
the category of real/complex unital C*-algebras to the category of real/complex unital operator
systems. It is based on the characterization of JB-algebras among the order unit spaces in
terms of the existence of gauge-reversing bijections obtained by M. Roelands and the author in
[RT25]. To this end, we show that a unital operator system is completely order isomorphic to
a C*-algebra if and only if each of its matrix spaces admits a compatible JB-algebra structure.
As an application, we prove that for n ≥4 the range of a unital n-positive projection on a
unital real C*-algebra is unitally n-order isomorphic to a unital real C*-algebra, which is the
analogue of a result proven for complex C*-algebras by Choi–Effros.
Keywords: operator systems, JB-algebras, real C*-algebras, gauge-reversing bijections, order unit spaces,
positive projections on real C*-algebras, C*-algebras

Introduction
C*-algebras form a natural framework for the mathematical formulation of quantum mechanics.
For this reason, there has been a substantial interest in characterizations of C*-algebras in terms
of the underlying order or Banach space structure [She56], [Sak56].
The commutative case of
this problem is classical, see e.g. [Kad51] and the references therein. Impetus to the study of the
noncommutative case was given by Connes’ characterization of the ordered vector spaces underlying
σ-finite von Neumann algebras [Con74]. Alfsen–Shultz gave a convex-geometric characterization of
the state spaces of unital C*-algebras [AS78]. Characterizations of C*-algebras and related objects
in terms of the existence of holomorphic (or analytic) symmetries have been given in various
categories [Upm84, NR03, EMR01, Chu23]. The main result of this article is the following order-
theoretic characterization of unital C*-algebras among the operator systems (pertinent definitions
are given later in this introduction).
Theorem 1.1. Let E be a real (resp. complex) complete unital operator system.
Then E is
unitally completely order isomorphic to a unital real (resp. complex) C*-algebra if and only if for
arbitrarily large integers n ≥1 there exists an order-anti-isomorphism Φn : Mn(E)◦
+ →Mn(E)◦
+
which is homogeneous of degree −1 of the interior of the positive cone of Mn(E).
We remark that no smoothness assumptions are made on the maps Φn, which are known as gauge-
reversing bijections. The key ingredient in the proof is a characterization of JB-algebras among
the order unit spaces obtain by M. Roelands and the author in [RT25]. We shall first discuss this
result for JB-algebras, and then make the connection with operator systems.

Operator systems
We briefly introduce operator systems. We refer to the book [Pau02] for results on complex oper-
ator systems and to [BR26, Sect. 3] for a systematic discussion which of those results remain valid
for real operator systems.
Given a vector space F over a field K and integers m, n ≥1, we write Mmn(F) for the F-vector

2.3
Real C*-algebras
We assume the reader to be familiar with the basic theory of complex C*-algebras, but will give a
brief discussion of real C*-algebras based on [Sch93] and [Ros16]. For a more in-depth treatment
of real C*-algebras, the reader may consult [Goo82].
Definition 2.4. A real C∗-algebra is a real associative ∗-algebra A which is complete for a norm
∥·∥satisfying for all a, b ∈A:
(i) ∥ab∥≤∥a∥∥b∥,
(ii) ∥a∗a∥= ∥a∥2, and,
(iii) ∥a∗a∥≤∥a∗a + b∗b∥.
Conditions (i)-(iii) may be replaced by the requirement that A be isometrically isomorphic to a
norm closed *-algebra of bounded operators on a real Hilbert space. If A is a real C*-algebra,
then its complexification AC := A ⊗R C becomes a complex C*-algebra. On the other hand, if a
complex C*-algebra B admits an involutive complex-linear ∗-antiautomorphism θ, then B is the
complexification AC of the real C*-algebra A := {x ∈B : θ(x) = x∗}. Moreover, each complex
C*-algebra B becomes a real C*-algebra by restriction of scalars.
The category of commutative real C*-algebras is equivalent to the category of pairs (X, τ)
where X is a locally compact Hausdorff space and τ is an involutive autohomeomorphism of X.
This equivalence is implemented by the functor sending the pair (X, τ) to the real C*-algebra
C0(X, τ) := {f ∈C0(X) : f(τ(x)) = f(x) for all x ∈X}.
The following definition should be clear.
Definition 2.5. A (real or complex) normed *-algebra A is called a pre-C*-algebra if it satisfies
all conditions to be a C*-algebra except that its norm need not be complete.
It is straightforward to verify that the norm completion of a real (resp. complex) pre-C*-algebra
is a real (resp. complex) C*-algebra.

Example 4.3. The following example demonstrates that, in contrast to the complex case, the real
case of part (1) of Theorem 4.2 does not hold if one merely assumes m ≥2.
Let k be a positive integer with k ̸∈{1, 2, 4}. There exist pairwise anti-commuting skew-adjoint
unitaries s1, . . . , sk−1 ∈B(H) on a complex Hilbert space H of sufficiently large dimension. Let
E ⊂B(H) be the unital real operator system given by the real linear span of IdH and s1, . . . , sk−1.
Then M2(E)sa is a JB-subalgebra of B(H)sa, hence M2(E)◦
+ admits a gauge-reversing bijection.
We claim that E cannot be unitally 2-order isomorphic to a real C*-algebra A. In fact, if E and
A were *-isomorphic, then Asa ∼= Esa = R. For y ∈A we have yy∗= ∥y∥2 = y∗y, from where A is
a normed associative division algebra over R. But then by Mazur’s theorem [Maz38] this implies
that A is isomorphic to R, C or H, hence E has dimension 1, 2 or 4. Since dim E = k ̸∈{1, 2, 4},
we arrive at a contradiction.
The author does not know of a counterexample with m = 3.
Theorem 4.1 may provide a new perspective on the Choi–Effros theorem [CE77, Thm. 3.1] stating
that the range of a unital completely positive projection on a unital complex C*-algebra is unitally
completely order isomorphic to a unital complex C*-algebra. The analogous result for real unital
C*-algebras was established by Ruan in [Rua03, Section 4] for real B(H) and in the general case by
Blecher in [Ble24, p. 5]. Neal–Russo proved in [NR03, Cor. 5.6] that the range of a unital 2-positive
projection on a unital complex C*-algebra is unitally 2-order isomorphic to a unital complex C*-
algebra.
All their results may be deduced in one fell swoop from the corresponding result for
JB-algebras due to Effros–Størmer (cited as Theorem 2.13) by an application of Theorem 4.1.
This approach also allows to treat the case of a unital n-positive projection on a unital real C*-
algebra for n ≥4.
Theorem 4.4. Let A be a real/complex unital C*-algebra. Let n be an integer such that n ≥4 if
A is real and n ≥2 if A is complex. Suppose P : A →A is a unital n-positive (resp. completely
