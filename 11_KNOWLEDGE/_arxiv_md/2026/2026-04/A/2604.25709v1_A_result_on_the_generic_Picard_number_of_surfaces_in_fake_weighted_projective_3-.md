Abstract
We give a criterion for certain generic nondegenerate surfaces in a
fake weighted projective 3-space to have Picard number > 1. These
algebraic surfaces are of general type. We do this by considering de-
generations (along an edge), keeping track of the geometric genus,
and using vanishing cohomology classes to construct a rational Picard
class on the surface not proportional to the canonical divisor.
