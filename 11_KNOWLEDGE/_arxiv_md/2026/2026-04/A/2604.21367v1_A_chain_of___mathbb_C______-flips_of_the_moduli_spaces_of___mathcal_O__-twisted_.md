A CHAIN OF C∗-FLIPS OF THE MODULI SPACES OF O-TWISTED RANK 2
CONSTRAINED FRAMED HITCHIN PAIRS ON A SMOOTH CURVE
YONGJOO SHIN AND SANG-BUM YOO
ABSTRACT. Let X be a smooth complex projective curve. We prove that there exists a surjective
commutative forgetful diagram from the chain of C∗-flips of the moduli spaces of OX-twisted rank 2
constrained framed Hitchin pairs on X to the chain of C∗-flips of the moduli spaces of rank 2 framed
modules on X.
1. INTRODUCTION
Birational geometries of the moduli spaces of Hitchin pairs were studied in various ways, using
the symplectic cut [Haus01], the flips of moduli of parabolic Higgs bundles [Tha02] and the flips
of moduli of framed Hitchin pairs [Sch00]. Especially, the second and third ways can be recovered
by using GIT flips of [Tha96]. We focus on the third.
Let X be a smooth complex projective curve of genus g throughout this paper. Let L and
H be vector bundles on X. Fix a positive σ ∈Q . Then we consider the moduli space of σ-
semistable (resp. σ-stable) framed modules (E, ψ: E →H) of rank r and degree d on X denoted
by FMσ−ss(r, d, H) (resp. FMσ−s(r, d, H)), and the moduli space of σ-semistable (resp. σ-stable)
L-twisted framed Hitchin pairs (E, ε ∈C, ϕ: E →E ⊗L, ψ: E →H) of rank r and degree d
on X denoted by FHσ−ss(r, d, L, H) (resp. FHσ−s(r, d, L, H)). Further, FHσ−ss

FHσ−s

(E, ε, ϕ, ψ) such that ϕ ∈End ((E, ψ)), where End ((E, ψ)) is the space of endomorphisms of (E, ψ)
(see Section 2). The object parametrized by FHσ−ss

A chain of C∗-flips connecting to the moduli space of vector bundles on X was constructed
via a variation of FMσ−ss(r, d, H) in [OST99]. A chain of C∗-flips connecting to the moduli space
of L-twisted Hitchin pairs on X was constructed via a variation of FHσ−ss(r, d, L, H) in [Sch00].
Here a C∗-flip is a variation of GIT under a C∗-action in the sense of [Tha96, Theorem 3.3]. The
purpose of this work is to show the following main result.
Theorem 1.1 (i.e. Theorem 5.4). There exists a surjective commutative forgetful diagram from the chain
of C∗-flips of FHσ−ss

Theorem 1.2 (i.e. Theorem 4.1, and cf. Theorem 4.6 for an oriented case). A constrained OX-twisted
framed Hitchin pair is σ-semistable if and only if its underlying framed module is σ-semistable.

2. FRAMED MODULES, FRAMED HITCHIN PAIRS AND THEIR ORIENTED OBJECTS
In this section we give definitions of (oriented) framed modules, (orinted) framed Hitchin pairs
and their stabilities. See [HL95b, OST99, Sch00] for more details.
2.1. Framed modules. Let H be a vector bundle on X. A framed module of type (r, d, H) on X is
a pair (E, ψ) which consists of the following ingredients:
• a vector bundle E of rank r and degree d on X;
• a homomorphism ψ: E →H, called the framing of E.
Fix a positive σ ∈Q. For a vector bundle E, PE denotes the Hilbert polynomial of E. The Hilbert
polynomial of a framed module (E, ψ) is defined by P(E,ψ) := PE −δ(ψ) · σ, where δ(ψ) = 1 if
ψ ̸= 0 and δ(ψ) = 0 otherwise.
If F is a subbundle of E with the quotient G = E/F, then a framing ψ: E →H induces
framings ψF = ψ|F on F and ψG on G: if ψF ̸= 0, then ψG = 0, and otherwise it is the induced
homomorphism of ψ on G. With this convention we have
P(E,ψ) = P(F,ψF ) + P(G,ψG).
A framed module (E, ψ) is σ-(semi)stable, if for any proper, nonzero subbundle F of E,

rank E .
Let FMσ−ss(r, d, H) be the moduli space of σ-semistable framed modules of type (r, d, H) with
nonzero framings on X. FMσ−ss(r, d, H) was constructed in [HL95b]. FMσ−s(r, d, H) denotes the
stable locus of FMσ−ss(r, d, H).
Remark 2.1. The construction of FMσ−ss(r, d, H) in [HL95b] has a generality. The moduli space parametrizes
objects with underlying coherent sheaves on a smooth complex projective variety, where σ is a rational poly-
nomial with a positive leading coefficient.

Hom((E, ψ), (E′, ψ′)) of homomorphisms of framed modules is a linear subspace of Hom(E, E′).

2.2. Framed Hitchin pairs. Let L and H be vector bundles on X.
A framed Hitchin pair of type (r, d, L, H) on X is a quadruple (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) which consists of the
following ingredients
• a vector bundle E of rank r and degree d on X;
• a complex number ε;
• a homomorphism ϕ : E →E ⊗L, called the L-twisted Higgs field of E;
• a framing ψ : E →H
such that ϕ is not nilpotent, i.e., (ϕ ⊗idL⊗i−1) ◦· · · ◦ϕ ̸= 0 for all i ∈N, when ε = 0 (see [Sch00,
Section 1]). A constrained framed Hitchin pair on X is a framed Hitchin pair (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) with
ϕ ∈End ((E, ψ)).
Let (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) and (E′, ε′, ϕ′, ψ′) be framed Hitchin pairs of type (r, d, L, H) and (r′, d′, L, H′)
respectively. A homomorphism h : (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) →(E′, ε′, ϕ′, ψ′) of framed Hitchin pairs is a
homomorphism of the underlying bundles h : E →E′ for which there are elements z, w ∈C such
that ε′ = zε, ϕ′ ◦h = z((h ⊗idL) ◦ϕ), and ψ′ ◦h = wψ. The set Hom((E, ε, ϕ, ψ), (E′, ε, ϕ′, ψ′)) of
homomorphisms of framed Hitchin pairs is a linear subspace of Hom(E, E′). If h : (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) →
(E′, ε, ϕ′, ψ′) is an isomorphism, then it is an isomorphism of the underlying bundles h : E →E′
and the factors z, w are taken in C∗.
Fix a positive σ ∈Q. A framed Hitchin pair (E, ε, ϕ, ψ) is σ-(semi)stable, if for any proper,
nonzero subbundle F of E which is ϕ-invariant,
P(F,ψF )
rank F < (≤) P(E,ψ)
rank E .

with nonzero framings on X. FHσ−ss(r, d, L, H) was constructed in [Sch00]. FHσ−s(r, d, L, H) de-
