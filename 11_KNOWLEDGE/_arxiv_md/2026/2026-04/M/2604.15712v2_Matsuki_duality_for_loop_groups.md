TSAO-HSIEN CHEN AND LINGFEI YI
To George Lusztig with deepest admiration
Abstract. We establish versions of Matsuki duality for loop groups. The main result is a
bijection between symmetric loop group orbits and real polynomial loop group orbits on the
affine Grassmannians or affine flag varieties. Along the way we obtain orbit parametrizations
and make connections with vector bundles on real and twistor-P1 and Kottwitz sets .

1. Introduction
Let G be a complex connected reductive group. Let GR be a real from of G and let K be
a symmetric subgroup of G such that KR = GR ∩K is a maximal compact subgroup of both
GR and K. The celebrated Matsuki duality for G is a bijection
K\B ←→GR\B
between K-orbits and GR-orbits on the flag variety B of G with remarkable properties
[Mat91]. In this paper we establish a version of Matsuki duality for loop groups. The main
result is a bijection
K((t))\Fℓ←→LGR\Fℓ
between K((t))-orbits and LGR-orbits on the affine flag variety Fℓwith several remarkable
properties. Here K((t)) is the loop group of K and LGR is the polynomial loop group of GR
consisting of maps that take the unit circle S1 ⊂C to GR ⊂G. We also establish a version
of Matsuki duality for twisted loop groups orbits and make connections with vector bundles
on the twistor-P1 and Kottwitz sets B(GR) for GR.
Our approach is entirely combinatorial and is inspired by the work [Nad04] of Nadler
on Matsuki duality for affine Grassmannians and our previous work [CY] on singularities of
K((t))-orbit closures on Fℓ. The method of proof treats simultaneously all the real forms that
are in the same pure inner class. Along the way, we obtain parametrizations for both K((t))
and LGR-orbits and the equivariant local systems on them. In a forthcoming work [CNY], we
will provide a Morse-theoretic interpretation and refinement of the Matuski duality and use it
to upgrade the Matuski duality to an equivalence of equivariant derived categories, extending
the works [MUV92] and [CN] in the setting of flag varieties and affine Grassmannians. Such
an equivalence is a key ingredient to relate geometric Langlands for real groups and relative
Langlands duality, see [Che,CN].
We now describe the paper in more details.

1.2. Derived equivalences. In a forthcoming work [CNY], we will provide a Morse-theoretic
interpretation and refinement of the Matsuki dualities and use it to obtain a lift of the main
results to an equivalence of derived categories of sheaves, extending the previous work in
[MUV92] and [CN] in the settings of flag varieties B and affine Grassmannians Gr with

another construction of the Matsuki duality and its lifting to derived equivalences without
the Morse-theoretic interpretation. Such a connection to real affine Grassmannians is not
available in the setting of affine flag varieites Fℓand the twsited setting ϵ = −1.

and establish Matsuki duality for Iwahori orbits. In Section 5, we study connections with
