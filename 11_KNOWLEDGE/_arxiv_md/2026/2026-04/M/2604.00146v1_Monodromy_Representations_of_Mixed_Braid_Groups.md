Abstract. We explicitly describe unitary representations of mixed braid groups on the cohomology
of Abelian branched covers of CP1. We show that the image of the representation is generated by
complex reflections and relate it to the multivariate Burau representation.

1. Introduction
The action of the braid group on the cohomology of covering spaces of the punctured plane is a
classical subject. Note that a tuple p = (p1, . . . , pn) in Cn of n distinct points determines a compact
Riemann surface Xp with affine part
{(x, y) ∈C2 | yd = (x −p1)(x −p2) · · · (x −pn)}.
The map Xp →CP1 extending (x, y) 7→x is a Galois (branched) covering with cyclic Galois group
Z/dZ. Now if we vary p over the unordered configuration space
Confn(C) = {(z1, . . . , zn) ∈Cn | zi ̸= zj}
Sn
we have a (smooth) fiber bundle π : X →Confn(C) with fibers π−1(p) = Xp. Let CX be the
(locally) constant sheaf on X. Taking the first higher direct image sheaf:
V = R1π∗CX
we obtain a local system V over Confn(C) with fibers
Vp = H1(Xp, C).
Consequently we have a monodromy representation of the Braid group
Bn = π1(Confn(C)) →GL(H1(Xp, C)).
