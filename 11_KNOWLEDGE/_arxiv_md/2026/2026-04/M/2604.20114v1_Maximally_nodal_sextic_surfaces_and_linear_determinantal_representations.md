It is interesting and widely open question to ask whether every (nonsingular) projective variety admits
an Ulrich sheaf. On the other hand, the existence of Ulrich line bundles is often unexpected. For
instance, if Pic Y is generated by OY (1), then Y does not admit an Ulrich line bundles unless Y ⊂P

Proposition 2.1], Y is not linearly determinantal. Thus, a general sextic surface in P3 is not linearly
determinantal, hence cannot admit Ulrich line bundles. Theorem 1.1 says that, unlike the other general
sextics, maximally nodal sextic surfaces always admit Ulrich line bundles.
