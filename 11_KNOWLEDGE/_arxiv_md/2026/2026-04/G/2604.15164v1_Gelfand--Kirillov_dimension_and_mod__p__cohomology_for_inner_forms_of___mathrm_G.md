ANDREA DOTTO AND BAO V. LE HUNG
Abstract. Under standard assumptions, we compute the GK-dimension of Hecke eigenspaces in
the mod p cohomology of an inner form D× of GL2 over a totally real field unramified at p,
allowing D to be a division algebra at p. Our arguments also apply when D is a matrix algebra
at p, in which case they give a simplified proof of a theorem of Breuil–Herzig–Hu–Morra–Schraen.
