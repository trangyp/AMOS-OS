GEOMETRY OF BOUNDED GENERIC DOMAINS WITH
PIECEWISE SMOOTH BOUNDARY
XINGSI PU1 & LANG WANG2
Abstract. In this paper, we study the geometry of bounded domains with
piecewise smooth boundary. Specifically, we obtain the relationship between
the squeezing function corresponding to polydisk and Levi flatness on bounded
generic convex domains. As an application, we prove that a two dimensional
bounded generic convex domain with piecewise C2-smooth boundary that ad-
mits a finite volume quotient is biholomorphic to bidisk. Moreover, we show
that any Teichm¨uller space Tg with g ≥2 can not be biholomorphic to a
bounded generic domain with piecewise C2-smooth boundary.
1. Introduction
In complex space Cn, it is a fundamental fact that every bounded domain with
C2-smooth boundary admits a strongly pseudoconvex boundary point. Strong pseu-
doconvexity yields numerous characterizations of the unit ball. A landmark result
states that if a strongly pseudoconvex domain has a non-compact automorphism
group, then the domain is biholomorphic to the unit ball (one can see [20, 21] for
more details).
On the other hand, Pinchuk [18] introduced domains with generic piecewise C2-
smooth boundary and generalized this characterization to products of balls under
the assumption of homogeneity. Moreover, Kodama [14] improved this result to the
domains that have a compact quotient. For generic convex domains with piecewise
Levi flat boundary, Kim [12] characterized those possessing a non-compact auto-
morphism group, and Fu-Wong [8] later extended this to two-dimensional simply
connected generic domains with piecewise smooth Levi flat boundary.
This paper is devoted to study the geometry of bounded domains with generic
piecewise smooth boundary, a natural class that interpolates between smooth do-
mains and product domains. For a smooth boundary point, strong pseudoconvexity
forces the squeezing function to approach 1. By duality, Levi flatness is expected
to play an analogous role for the polydisk: it should force the squeezing function
corresponding to polydisk to converge to 1 at boundary points of Levi flat hyper-
surfaces. Let Ω⊂Cn be a bounded generic convex domain with piecewise Levi flat
boundary, and TΩbe the squeezing function corresponding to polydisk. Our first
main result confirms this expected boundary behavior in the two-dimensional case.
Theorem 1.1. For a two-dimensional bounded generic convex domain Ωwith piece-
wise Levi flat boundary, if p ∈∂Ωis a boundary point, then we have
lim
z→p TΩ(z) = 1.

For a bounded domain Ω⊂Cn with C2-smooth boundary, Zimmer [22] proved
that: if Ωhas a finite volume quotient with respect to either the Bergman volume,
the K¨ahler-Einstein volume, or the Kobayashi-Eisenman volume, then it is biholo-
morphic to the unit ball. In the setting of bounded convex domains, a folklore
conjecture asserts that a bounded convex domain that admits a finite volume quo-
tient is biholomorphic to a bounded symmetric space(see [16] for more details).
Motivated by the main theorem of [19], we can pose the following problem.
Problem 1.8. Let Ω⊂Cn be a bounded generic convex domain with piecewise C2-
smooth boundary and Γ ≤Aut(Ω) be a discrete group acting freely on Ω. Suppose
Γ \ Ωhas a finite volume, is Ωbiholomorphic to Bn1 × Bn2 × · · · × Bni for some
positive integers n1, · · · , ni satisfying n1 + · · · ni = n?
After applying Theorem 1.1, we prove the following two-dimensional case of this
problem.
Theorem 1.9. Let Ω⊂C2 be a bounded generic convex domain with piecewise
C2-smooth boundary, and Γ ≤Aut(Ω) be a discrete group acting freely on Ω. If
Γ \ Ωhas finite volume with respect to either Bergman volume, the K¨ahler-Einstein
volume, or the Kobayashi-Eisenman volume, then Ωis biholomorphic to the bidisk.
We now turn to the characterization of Teichm¨uller space within the class of
bounded domains.
Let S be a compact surface with genus g ≥2, and Tg be
the Teichm¨uller space associated to S. It’s well known that any Tg can not be
biholormophic to a bounded domain in Cn with C2-smooth boundary. One can
refer [11, 22] for more details.
In the setting of bounded generic domains with
piecewise C2-smooth boundary, we can prove the following theorem.
Theorem 1.10. Any Teichm¨uller space Tg with g ≥2 can not be biholomorphic to
a bounded generic domain with piecewise C2-smooth boundary.
For non-smooth bounded domain, the main theorem in [17] states that any
Teichm¨uller space Tg with g ≥2 can not be biholomorphic to a bounded convex
domain. Later, Gupta and Seshadri [11] extended the result to the case of locally
strictly convex domains.
Moreover, they conjectured that local strict convexity
can be relaxed to local convexity. Recently, Liu and Wang [15] proved that such
a domain can not even be locally log-type convex near a boundary point. In this
paper, by relaxing locally strict convexity to C2-smoothness and local convexity at
a boundary point, we obtain the following theorem.
Theorem 1.11. Any Teichm¨uller space Tg with g ≥2 can not be biholomorphic to
a bounded domain which is C2-smooth and convex at some boundary point p ∈∂Ω.
Definition 1.12. Let Ωbe a domain in Cn. A boundary point p is said local peak
if there exists a neighborhood U of p and a holomorphic map f : U →C such that
|f(z)| < 1 whenever z ∈U ∩Ω\ {p} and f(p) = 1.
If we relax the C2-smoothness assumption in Theorem 1.11 to Alexandrov smooth-
ness (see Section 5), we obtain the following result.
Theorem 1.13. Any Teichm¨uller space Tg with g ≥2 can not be biholomorphic to
a bounded domain that possesses a local peak point p which is an Alexandrov smooth
point.
