MODULO A SUBTORUS
ARKADEV GHOSH AND S. S. KANNAN
Abstract. Let G = PSL(n, C). Let T be a maximal torus of G. Let ωr denote the
rth fundamental weight. Let L(nωr) denote the line bundle on the Grassmannian Gr,n
associated to the character nωr of T. In [13], it is proved that there is a unique minimal
dimensional Schubert variety X(wr,n) in Gr,n admitting semistable points for the T-
linearized ample line bundle L(nωr).
Assume that n = rq + 1, where r, q ∈N and
q ≥2. In this paper, we study the GIT quotient of X(wr,n) modulo a subtorus TJr of
T generated by the one parameter subgroups of T corresponding to the peaks of wr,n
(see [18, Definition 4.6]). We prove that the GIT quotient of X(wr,n) modulo TJr is
isomorphic to the total space of the rth stage of an iterated projective space bundle over
Pq−1.
1. introduction
Let r, q ∈N with q ≥2 and n = rq + 1. Let G = PSL(n, C). Let T be a maximal
torus of G and B be a Borel subgroup of G containing T. Let W = NG(T)/T denote
the Weyl group of G with respect to T. The set of roots of G with respect to T will
be denoted by R. Let R+ ⊆R be the set of positive roots with respect to (B, T). Let
S = {α1, . . . , αn−1} ⊆R+ be the set of simple roots of G with respect to B. The simple
reflection in W corresponding to αi is denoted by si. Let {ωr : 1 ≤r ≤n −1} denote the
fundamental weights associated to S. Let {λ1, . . . , λn−1} denote the set of one parameter
subgroups of T dual to S. Let PS\{αr} be the standard maximal parabolic subgroup of G
corresponding to S \ {αr}. Then the Grassmannian Gr,n is isomorphic to G/PS\{αr}.
Consider the action of T on the Grassmannain Gr,n.
In [5], Hausmann and Knutson
identified the GIT quotient of Grassmannian G2,n by T with the moduli space of polygons
in R3. They showed that GIT quotient of G2,n by T can be realized as the GIT quotient of
the n-fold product of projective lines by the diagonal action of PSL(2, C). More generally,
using Gel’fand-Macpherson correspondence GIT quotient of Gr,n by T can be identified
with the GIT quotient of (Pr−1)n by the diagonal action of PSL(r, C).
In [21], Skorobogatov gave a combinatorial description of the set of semistable points
in Gr,n for the T-linearized line bundle L(nωr). As a corollary, he showed that when
r and n are coprime, semistability is same as stability. In [9] and [10], second named
author characterized the parabolic subgroups Q of a simple algebraic group H containing
a fixed Borel subgroup, for which there exists an ample line bundle L on H/Q such that
(H/Q)ss
T (L) = (H/Q)s
T(L). In particular, when H = PSL(n, C) and Q = PS\{αr}, he
showed that (Gr,n)ss
T (L(nωr)) = (Gr,n)s
T(L(nωr)) if and only if r and n are coprime.
