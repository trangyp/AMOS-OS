GRUENBERG-KEGEL GRAPHS WITH FOUR EDGES
OF FINITE SOLVABLE CUT GROUPS
SARA C. DEB´ON, DIEGO GARC´IA-LUCAS, AND ´ANGEL DEL R´IO
Abstract. We prove that there is a unique graph with four edges which is the Gruenberg-Kegel
graph of a solvable cut group. This contributes to the classification of the Gruenberg-Kegel graphs
of solvable cut groups initiated in [4].
The Gruenberg-Kegel graph (also known as prime graph) of a group G is the one having as vertices
the primes ocurring as the order of some element of G and edges p −q whenever G has an element
of order pq. The Gruenberg-Kegel graph of G is denoted ΓGK(G) and in the remainder of the paper
we call it GK-graph, for brevity. This graph was introduced by Karl Gruenberg and Otto Kegel who
proved that if G is solvable then ΓGK(G) is disconnected if and only if G is Frobenius or 2-Frobenius
(see [33], which quotes an unpublished manuscript of Gruenberg and Kegel). Since the publication
of this paper, the GK-graph has received a lot of attention and in particular, the properties of the
GK-graph of a group in a class C of groups, or the realizability of a graph as the GK-graph of an object
of C, have been object of study [1, 6, 7, 14, 16, 17, 25, 34].
A group is said to be cut if the central units of its integral group ring are trivial. It is well known that
a finite group G is cut if and only if it is inverse semi-rational, i.e. if and only if for every g ∈G, all the
generators of the group generated by g are conjugated to g or g−1 (see [28] or [21, Corollary 7.1.15]). In
particular every rational group is cut. The study of rational groups is a classical topic in representation
theory [12, 15, 18, 22, 31]. Besides the connections of cut groups with units of integral group rings,
they have a role in representation theory because they can also be characterized as the groups for
which the field of character values of every irreducible character is an imaginary quadratic extension of
the rationals. So the interest for cut groups and other generalizations of rational groups have received
some attention during the last decades [2, 3, 5, 8, 23, 26, 27, 29, 30, 32].
The classification of the GK-graphs of solvable rational groups and of solvable cut groups was almost
achieved in [4]. For rational solvable groups the classification was completed in [10, 11].
The aim of this paper is to contribute to the classification of the GK-graphs of solvable cut groups.
In [4] the graphs with at most three vertices which occur as the GK-graph of a solvable cut group were
classified. It was also proved that a graph with more than three vertices realizable as the GK-graph
of a solvable cut group is one of a list of seven graphs, of which three are indeed the GK-graph of a
solvable cut group. Of these three, one has four edges, another has five and the last one has six edges.
However, the question was not settled for the remaining four graphs and the authors posed this in the
form of the following question:
