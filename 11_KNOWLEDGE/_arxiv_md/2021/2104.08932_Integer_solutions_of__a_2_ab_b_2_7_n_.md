The Second Proof- by Le Tien Nam
This proof is elementary, easily inderstood by high school students.
We restate the proposition and prove it by induction.
Claim 1. The equation a2 + b2 + ab = 7n has at least one positive integer
solution (a, b) such that gcd(a, 7) = 1
Proof. It is easy to see that for n = 1, 2, (1, 2) and (3, 5) solve the equation.
Assume that the claim is true for n, that is there are relatively prime positive
integers a, b satisfying the claim. We shall prove that the claim is also true
for n + 1.

three pairs of numbers:
