Abstract
This study examines the efficacy of two SOTA
large language models (LLMs), namely Chat-
GPT and Microsoft Bing Chat (BingChat),
in catering to the needs of Vietnamese stu-
dents. Although ChatGPT exhibits proficiency
in multiple disciplines, Bing Chat emerges
as the more advantageous option. We con-
duct a comparative analysis of their academic
achievements in various disciplines, encom-
passing mathematics, literature, English lan-
guage, physics, chemistry, biology, history, ge-
ography, and civic education. The results of our
study suggest that BingChat demonstrates supe-
rior performance compared to ChatGPT across
a wide range of subjects, with the exception of
literature, where ChatGPT exhibits better per-
formance. Additionally, BingChat utilizes the
more advanced GPT-4 technology in contrast
to ChatGPT, which is built upon GPT-3.5. This
allows BingChat to improve to comprehension,
reasoning and generation of creative and infor-
mative text. Moreover, the fact that BingChat
is accessible in Vietnam and its integration of
hyperlinks and citations within responses serve
to reinforce its superiority. In our analysis, it is
evident that while ChatGPT exhibits praisewor-
thy qualities, BingChat presents a more adapted
solutions for Vietnamese students.

Introduction
Recent advancements in LLMs have enabled AI to
understand and communicate with humans, creat-
ing opportunities for its use in education. LLMs
have shown great potential in education, content
development, and language translation. The two
primary architectures of LLMs are BERT (Bidi-
rectional Encoder Representations from Trans-
formers) and GPT (Generative Pre-trained Trans-
former). In 2018, Google introduced BERT (De-
vlin et al., 2018), which excelled in various natural
language processing (NLP) tasks. OpenAI devel-
oped the GPT algorithm (Alec et al., 2018), which
was trained on extensive unlabeled text datasets.
Facebook’s RoBERTa (Liu et al., 2019) built on
Google’s research, and Google released T5 (Raffel
et al., 2020) in 2019. In 2020, OpenAI created
GPT-3 (Brown et al., 2020), which demonstrated
exceptional performance in various NLP tasks. Re-
cently, OpenAI developed GPT-4 (OpenAI, 2023),
a text-to-text machine learning system capable of
processing both text and image inputs. GPT-4 has
shown human-level performance in many profes-
sional and academic criteria, although it may not
perform as well as humans in other contexts.
LLMs need to provide citations and hyperlinks
in generated answers in order to become useful
tool in education. Liu et al. (Liu et al., 2023) pro-
posed ALCE, a benchmark for evaluating the ci-
tation quality of text generated by LLMs. Their
experiments reveal room for improvement, as even
the best LLM model lacks complete citation sup-
port in 49% of its generations on the ELI5 dataset.
Gao et al. (Gao et al., 2023) discussed the impor-
tance of verifiability in generative search engines
and find that existing engines often provide fluent
but unsupported and inaccurately cited responses.
They highlight the low rates of fully supported sen-
tences (51.5%) and supporting citations (74.5%),
emphasizing the need for trustworthy information
sources.
In (Dao et al., 2023a), ChatGPT can pass
the Vietnamese National High School Gradua-
tion Examination (VNHSGE). Nevertheless, Chat-
GPT is currently unavailable in Vietnam, whereas
BingChat is accessible. The objective of this study
is to conduct a comparative analysis of the effi-
cacy of ChatGPT and BingChat in providing aca-
demic support to Vietnamese students across mul-
tiple academic fields. To comprehensively evaluate
the capabilities of both models, it is important to
acknowledge that ChatGPT utilizes GPT-3.5 tech-
nology, while BingChat utilizes the more advanced
GPT-4 technology. This distinction presents poten-
tial benefits in terms of LLMs’ ability to understand

istry, GRE Quantitative section, and AP Physics 2.
In contrast, the performance of GPT-3.5 on these
benchmarks is notably inferior compared to that of

2.2
Evaluation of LLMs on Vietnamese
education
In their study, Quy et al. conducted an evaluation of
the performance of LLMs on the VNHSGE exami-
nation (Dao et al., 2023e; Dao and Le, 2023; Dao
et al., 2023d,c,b,a). In order to assess the efficacy
of LLMs in the context of the VNHSGE exami-
nation, Quy et al. constructed a dataset known as
VNHSGE dataset (Dao et al., 2023e). This dataset
encompasses nine distinct subjects and was derived
from the VNHSGE examination as well as other
comparable examinations. The dataset comprises a
total of 300 literary essays and 19,000 multiple-
choice questions pertaining to various subjects.
The dataset possesses the potential to assess the
performance of LLMs across various multitask
scenarios, including but not limited to question an-
swering, text generation, reading comprehension,
visual question answering, and other related tasks.
The recent research conducted a thorough eval-
uation of ChatGPT’s mathematical proficiency by
assessing its performance in answering multiple-
choice questions from the VNHSGE examina-
tion, covering various subjects and levels of diffi-
culty (Dao and Le, 2023). The findings indicated
that ChatGPT exhibited varying levels of profi-
ciency in mathematics, which were contingent upon
the complexity of the tasks and the specific subject
matter. The system demonstrated superior perfor-
mance on knowledge-level questions, achieving an
accuracy rate of 83%. Nevertheless, as the com-
plexity of the task increased, there was a corre-
sponding decline in the accuracy rate. Specifically,
the scores for comprehension level, application
level, and high application level were recorded at
62%, 27%, and 10% respectively. In a related study,
the authors explored the performance of ChatGPT
and BingChat in various domains, as documented
in references (Dao et al., 2023d,c,b,e).
According to Dao et al. (Dao et al., 2023a), Chat-
GPT achieved a mean score ranging from 6 to 7
on the VNHSGE, thereby showcasing the transfor-
mative capabilities of this technology in reshaping
the educational domain. The evaluation of Chat-
GPT’s performance unveiled its competence across
various disciplines such as mathematics, English,
physics, chemistry, biology, history, geography,
civic education, and literature. This implies that
it has the capability to offer efficient assistance to
learners.
2.3
Hyperlink Feature in Answers
2.3.1
Importance of Citations and Hyperlinks
in LLMs-Generated Answers
The utilization of LLMs is experiencing a growing
trend in various domains, encompassing education,
research, and entertainment. Nevertheless, it is cru-
cial to ascertain the accuracy and reliability of the
information presented by LLMs. Specifically, cita-
tions and hyperlinks play a crucial role in academic
discourse.
Citations: Citations play a crucial role in guar-
anteeing the veracity of the responses provided in
LLMs. By citing authoritative references, LLMs
can substantiate their assertions with empirical
data, thereby guaranteeing the credibility and de-
pendability of the information presented. Citations


## Page 4


additionally foster transparency and accountabil-
ity by affording users the opportunity to scrutinize
the sources of information utilized by LLMs. This
feature facilitates users in making informed assess-
ments regarding the veracity and dependability of
the content that is generated.
Hyperlinks: Hyperlinks are an additional essen-
tial element for guaranteeing the quality of LLMs’
responses. By supplying hyperlinks to the original
sources, LLMs allow users to directly access the
information and validate the model’s claims. This
encourages critical thinking and a culture of fact-
checking. In addition, hyperlinks enable users to
investigate related information beyond the gener-
ated response, which can be beneficial for further
study and learning.
2.3.2
Benefits of Citations and Hyperlinks
The incorporation of citations and hyperlinks
within LLMs responses can yield several advan-
tages, such as:
• Factual accuracy: Citations play a crucial
role in guaranteeing the accuracy and reliabil-
ity of information provided by LLMs.
• Transparency: Citations play a crucial role in
fostering transparency by disclosing the ori-
gins of information utilized by LLMs.
• Verifiability:
Hyperlinks can serve as a
means to enable autonomous verification of
the information provided by LLMs.
• Further exploration: Hyperlinks can facil-
itate users in accessing supplementary infor-
mation that is pertinent to the generated re-
sponse.
• Learning and research: Citations and hyper-
links are of significant value to students, re-
searchers, and individuals who aim to enhance
their comprehension of a particular subject.
Overall, citations and hyperlinks in LLMs an-
swers contribute to factual accuracy, transparency,
verifiability, and facilitate further learning and re-
search. By incorporating these features, LLMs can
enhance the quality and reliability of the informa-
tion they provide.
3
Objectives and Methods
The objective of this study is to assess the effi-
cacy of LLMs in successfully completing the Viet-
namese National High School Graduation Exami-
nation (VNHSGE) and offer a comprehensive anal-
ysis of the obtained outcomes. In this study, we
conduct a comparative analysis of the performance
exhibited by ChatGPT and BingChat. Addition-
ally, we examined the performance of LLMs in
relation to Vietnamese students’ performance on
the VNHSGE exanimation. This study assessed
the performance of LLMs utilizing an evaluation
set derived from the VNHSGE dataset (Dao et al.,
2023e). The evaluation set consists of questions
from the VNHSGE examination spanning the years
2019 to 2023, covering a range of subjects includ-
ing mathematics, English, physics, chemistry, bi-
ology, history, geography, civic education, and lit-
erature. The evaluation set consists of a total of
30 essay questions specifically designed for the
literature subject, while the remaining 1700 ques-
tions are in the form of multiple-choice for other
subjects. The primary objective of this study is to
make a valuable contribution to the advancement
of AI tools designed to provide educational sup-
port. Additionally, this research seeks to provide
insights into the potential future applications of AI
in reshaping the educational environment.

Conclusion
This study presents an analysis indicating that
Microsoft Bing Chat exhibits superior perfor-
mance and capability compared to OpenAI
ChatGPT specifically for Vietnamese students.
BingChat, leveraging the advanced GPT-4 technol-
ogy, demonstrates enhanced comprehension and
agility in addressing intricate inquiries, resulting
in the production of text that is both more precise
and informative. While ChatGPT demonstrates
exceptional performance in the field of literature,
BingChat surpasses it in various other academic
disciplines including mathematics, physics, chem-
istry, biology, history, geography, and civic educa-
tion. The presence of this technology in Vietnam
facilitates direct utilization of its functionalities, in
contrast to the unavailability of ChatGPT within the
country at present. Furthermore, the integration of
hyperlinks within BingChat’s responses enhances
the educational process by offering supplementary
materials and facilitating deeper investigation. In
general, BingChat is identified as the superior op-
tion for Vietnamese students due to its advanced
functionalities and improved accessibility.
