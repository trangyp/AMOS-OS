In this paper, we study existence and compactness of entire solutions of the equation

Equation (1) is the prescribed Gaussian curvature

The study of equation (1) started with the work of Liouville [25] who considered the case

The ﬁrst existence result for solutions to equation (1) on the entire plane, when the function
K is nonpositive and satisﬁes further growth conditions at inﬁnity, was given by Ni [33] and it
was then reﬁned by McOwen [30] using a weighted Sobolev space approach. Later, a complete
classiﬁcation of all possible solutions to (1) in some important cases when K is nonpositive was
obtained in [14, 15, 16, 13].
