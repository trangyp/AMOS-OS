A ﬁrst integral of the associated vector ﬁeld of a diﬀerential system reduces the dimension
of the system by one, which makes the analysis of the system easier. Therefore, ﬁnding
ﬁrst integrals is extremely important in the qualitative theory of diﬀerential equations.

in general.If the vector ﬁeld is Hamiltonian, then one easily gets at least one ﬁrst integral.
In addition, if there is another independent ﬁrst integral of a Hamiltonian system then it is
called integrabile in Liouville sense. A Liouville integrable system satisfy the assumptions
of the famous Liouville theorem, see [1]. So, the corresponding system can be solved by
quadratures, i.e., by solving a ﬁnite number of algebraic equations and computing a ﬁnite
number of deﬁnite integrals. Also, Darboux theory of integrability [7, Chapter 8] is very
useful to compute ﬁrst integrals.

Proposition 2.1. Let χ be a polynomial vector ﬁeld in Rn and W a ﬁnite dimensional
vector subspace of R[x1, x2, · · · , xn] with dim(W) > 1. Then, an invariant algebraic hy-
