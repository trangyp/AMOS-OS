Abstract. We establish an optimal (topological) irreducibility criterion for p-adic Banach principal
series of GLn(F ), where F/Qp is ﬁnite and n ≤3. This is new for n = 3 as well as for n = 2, F ̸= Qp
and establishes a reﬁned version of Schneider’s conjecture [Sch06, Conjecture 2.5] for these groups.
1. Introduction
Suppose that F/Qp is a ﬁnite extension with normalized absolute value |·|F and residue ﬁeld of
cardinality q. This paper concerns the continuous representations of G = GLn(F) on p-adic Banach
spaces over a coeﬃcient ﬁeld C that is a (suﬃciently large) ﬁnite extension of Qp.
Such Banach
representations were introduced in the work of Schneider–Teitelbaum [ST02] and play a fundamental role
in the p-adic Langlands program (see for example [Bre04], [BS07], [Col10], [Eme11], [Paˇs13], [CEG+16]).
Little has been known about Banach representations outside the group GL2(Qp) so far. The main goal
of this paper is to determine an optimal (topological) irreducibility criterion for Banach principal series
of GLn(F) when n ≤3. This goes further than Schneider’s conjecture [Sch06, Conjecture 2.5] for these
groups.
