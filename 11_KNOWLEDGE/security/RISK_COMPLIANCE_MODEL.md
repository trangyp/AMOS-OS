# AMOS Risk & Compliance Engine

**Version:** 2.0.0+lens_integration
**Source:** `AMOS_Risk_Compliance_Engine_v0.json`

The **Risk & Compliance Kernel** provides deterministic risk assessment, modeling, and control review structures for institutions.

## The 24 Risk Clusters
The framework maps every type of risk across 24 clusters, including:
- Risk Governance, Appetite, and Taxonomy
- Credit, Market, and Liquidity Risk Models
- Operational Risk, ICT & Cyber Risk
- AML/KYC, Fraud Detection, Sanctions Screening
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCP/DR)
- Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
- Issue, Incident, and Breach Reporting

## The 20 Dimensions
Risks are evaluated using a 20-dimensional tensor:
- Inherent & Residual Risk Level
- Control Effectiveness, Likelihood, Impact, Velocity
- Regulatory, Reputational, and Financial Impact
- Detectability, Data Quality, Governance Strength
- Systemic Risk Contribution

## Virtual Expansion Axes
Expands into specific risk categories (credit, market, conduct, etc.), regulatory regimes (banking, insurance, securities), and jurisdiction rigour.
