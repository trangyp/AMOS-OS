# 7-Part Universe Canon

> What must exist for anything to emerge, operate, change, survive, collapse, or recover.

## Preamble

The 7-Part Universe Canon defines the irreducible persistence structure of all systems that persist in reality. If a theory, system, institution, or explanation cannot be mapped fully across all seven parts, it is structurally incomplete.

> **Canon closure:** Each part answers a non-overlapping necessity. Remove any one and prediction collapses.

| Part | Question answered |
|------|-------------------|
| I — Constraint | Why anything exists at all |
| II — Flow | How power moves |
| III — Structure | What holds flow together |
| IV — Enforcement | Why structure holds |
| V — Time | Why everything changes |
| VI — Adaptation | How systems respond |
| VII — Termination | Why systems end or survive |

---

## Part I — Constraint

**Definition:** Constraint is the existence of limits.

**Properties:** Scarcity · Boundaries · Non-infinite capacity · Irreversibility

**Canon law:** If there are no constraints, there is no system — only noise.

**Examples:** Finite energy (physics), speed of light, metabolic limits (biology), logistics ceilings (war), time/attention/legitimacy (civilization).

## Part II — Flow

**Definition:** Flow is constrained throughput across a system. Flow is not possession. Flow is conversion under limits.

**Properties:** Input → transformation → output · Bottlenecks · Leakage · Queues

**Canon law:** Power exists only while it is moving.

**Examples:** Energy through matter, blood through organs, supplies through armies, capital through economies.

## Part III — Structure

**Definition:** Structure is the arrangement that stabilizes flow. Structure enables repetition.

**Properties:** Architecture · Hierarchy · Interfaces · Load-bearing elements

**Canon law:** Flow without structure dissipates. Structure without flow decays.

**Examples:** Atomic bonds, organs, command hierarchies, institutions.

## Part IV — Enforcement

**Definition:** Enforcement is the mechanism that prevents deviation from structure. Enforcement is not morality. It is mechanical correction.

**Properties:** Rule consistency · Boundary correction · Deviation cost · Predictability

**Canon law:** Unenforced structure is not structure.

**Examples:** Physical laws, immune systems, military discipline, legal systems.

## Part V — Time

**Definition:** Time is irreversible sequencing under constraint. Time converts structure into stress.

**Properties:** Delay · Accumulation · Fatigue · Irreversibility

**Canon law:** Time exposes all unenforced assumptions.

**Examples:** Entropy, aging, campaign duration, institutional decay.

## Part VI — Adaptation

**Definition:** Adaptation is bounded change under pressure. Adaptation must preserve invariants.

**Properties:** Feedback · Adjustment · Learning · Reconfiguration

**Canon law:** Adaptation at the core destroys identity. Adaptation at the edge preserves survival.

**Examples:** Evolution, tactical change, policy updates, industrial retooling.

## Part VII — Termination

**Definition:** Termination is the resolution of accumulated deviation. Every system trends toward collapse, stabilization, extinction, or reconstitution.

**Properties:** Thresholds · Phase transitions · Recovery basins · Irreversibility zones

**Canon law:** Systems do not fail randomly. They terminate when correction capacity is exceeded.

**Examples:** Supernova, organ failure, military defeat, civilizational collapse.

---

## Why 7, not 10

This is a **persistence canon** — it answers *why systems emerge, operate, change, survive, collapse, or recover*.

The vault's existing **10-part Universe Total Canon (UTC)** is a **composition canon** — it answers *what exists across scales* (micro → macro: P1_META → P10_CANON_INTEGRATION).

These are **orthogonal axes**, not competing canons:

- UTC: "What are the MECE domains of reality across scale?"
- 7-Part Canon: "What are the irreducible necessities for any system to persist across time?"

A system can be fully described by both without duplication — one gives its composition, the other gives its persistence logic.

## Gap callout

**Part II — Flow** is the genuine gap.

The vault encodes *movement through phases* (TSS cycles) and *evolutionary dynamics* (Trang ∅ 4.1–4.4) and *multi-agent coordination* — but has **no canon part that defines power as constrained throughput with bottlenecks, leakage, and queues as first-class properties.**

This is the part that should get a CIL-style entry. If it survives gap analysis and gets promoted, it would be the single most valuable addition this canon makes to the vault — everything else is sharpening of existing layers.

## Canon test (UCIA-compliant)

Any theory, strategy, or system must answer all seven:

1. Where are the constraints?
2. What is the flow?
3. What structure stabilizes it?
4. How is it enforced?
5. How does time stress it?
6. How does it adapt without drift?
7. What are its termination conditions?

**Failure to answer all seven = structural invalidity.**

This test is reusable as an audit lens for any canon item in the vault — not just this one.
