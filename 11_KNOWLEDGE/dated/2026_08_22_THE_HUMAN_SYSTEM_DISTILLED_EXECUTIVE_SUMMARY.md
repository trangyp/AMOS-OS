## VIII. The Schizophrenia Structural Model (Most Specific Claim)

This is the most falsifiable claim in the book.

```text
Schizophrenia = Boundary_Coherence failure

Mechanism (convergent evidence, structural interpretation):

1. NMDA receptor hypofunction (especially on PV interneurons)
2. → AMPA receptor hyperfunction (compensatory up-regulation)
3. → Parvalbumin (PV) interneuron loss / dysfunction
4. → Disinhibition of pyramidal neurons
5. → Mesolimbic dopamine excess (positive symptoms: hallucinations, delusions)
6. → Mesocortical dopamine deficit (negative symptoms: avolition, flat affect)
7. → DMN hyperconnectivity with failed deactivation during task
8. → Thalamic gating failure
9. → If Boundary_Coherence ↓:
     Internal_Signal ≈ External_Signal
     Self ≈ Other, Internal ≈ External, Signal ≈ Noise
```

**Epistemic status:** MODEL — structural interpretation of convergent evidence, not independently confirmed as the complete etiology. Most specific claim in the book. Falsifiable by: showing that NMDA hypofunction is NOT a causal factor; showing that PV interneuron loss is NOT present; showing that DMN hyperconnectivity is NOT associated with symptoms.

6. **Platform_Model(User) > User_Model(Self)** — true for engagement-optimized platforms, not generally.
