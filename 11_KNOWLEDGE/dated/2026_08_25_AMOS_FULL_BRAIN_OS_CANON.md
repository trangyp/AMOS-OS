(CognitiveState) but does NOT ground them in actual UBI engines (BRAIN_CORE gap). It has a gap registry

routing, no planning layer, no OS kernel runtime, and no infrastructure control plane.

The pipeline is a governance-aware demonstration, not a complete brain. The 3 CRITICAL gaps
(GAP-BRAIN-001, GAP-OMNI-001, GAP-OMNIVERSE-001) define the minimum path to closer alignment with the canon.
