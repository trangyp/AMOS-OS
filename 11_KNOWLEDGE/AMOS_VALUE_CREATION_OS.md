## SC-005 — “100% structural fit”

**Source claim:** tested cases fit C1–C7 and R/T/A/Sg without exception.

**Class:** `SOURCE_CLAIM / UNVERIFIED`

Possible alternative explanations:

1. categories are broad enough to absorb most outcomes;
2. cases were selected after taxonomy creation;
3. labels were assigned retrospectively;
4. transitions are narratively rather than quantitatively defined;
5. non-fitting cases are under-sampled;
6. “fit” measures classification compatibility, not predictive validity.

**Discriminating test:** blind held-out classification with frozen definitions.

> Can independent raters assign historical/system cases to C1–C7 and R/T/A/Sg with high agreement using frozen definitions?

```text
CohenKappa
KrippendorffAlpha
InterRaterAgreement
UnclassifiedRate
```

Question:

> Can TPE predict future outcomes better than strong baselines using only information available at prediction time?

Required design:

```text
Training cutoff t0
Validation window t1
Test window t2
No future leakage
Frozen feature mapping
Frozen thresholds
Recorded abstentions
```

- persistence;
