## Purpose

The RSCF (Reasoning, Source, Claim, Freshness) sub-plane is the canonical registry of all RSCF epistemic classification indices in the AMOS OS. RSCF is the foundational epistemic discipline that preserves the distinction between source claims, observations, derived conclusions, models, decisions, competing hypotheses, and unknown/gaps. This sub-plane aggregates per-framework RSCF indices so that any artifact in the vault can be traced to its epistemic class, provenance, and freshness state.

> **Epistemic boundary:** RSCF indices are `SOURCE_CLAIM` — they describe the classification system itself, not the classified content.
