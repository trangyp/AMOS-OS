Introduction</strong></h2></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80e1-9bfb-e160af1abfa8" class="">Over the past decade, Vietnam’s economy has expanded at an average rate of <strong>6–7% annually</strong>, driven largely by export-oriented manufacturing and integration into regional supply chains. This model—anchored in cost efficiency and labour-intensive production—has delivered sustained growth but now faces diminishing returns as global competition shifts toward automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and knowledge-based value creation.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8090-8504-de74d1065791" class="">To sustain competitiveness in a <strong>post-pandemic, AI-driven economy</strong>, Vietnam must transition from <strong>cost-based advantage</strong> to <strong>innovation-based resilience</strong>. The private sector is central to this transition: it contributes <strong>42% of GDP</strong>, generates <strong>85% of employment</strong>, and accounts for nearly <strong>98% of registered enterprises</strong> (MPI, 2024). 
However, productivity within the domestic private sector remains only <strong>60% of the ASEAN-6 average</strong>, constrained by limited access to innovation finance, fragmented R&amp;D infrastructure, and weak integration with global value chains (GVCs).</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8004-991a-ecc84b2d822f" class="">Current structural challenges include:</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-8036-9cd9-dc8b743df882" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><strong>Innovation financing gaps</strong>—private R&amp;D expenditure remains below 0.5% of GDP, compared with 2–3% in regional innovation leaders.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-8023-834b-dbca06714277" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><strong>Dispersed research and technology transfer mechanisms</strong>—leading to duplication and low commercialisation of public R&amp;D.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b1-aa78-e7f9967b5bd0" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><strong>Limited international collaboration</strong>—restricting exposure to frontier technologies and cross-border innovation networks.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-809a-b1fe-eaaa73f03aa4" class="">This policy briefing proposes a coherent reform framework to reposition Vietnam’s private sector as an <strong>innovation partner</strong> rather than merely a production actor. 
It integrates fiscal, institutional, and digital policy levers to embed domestic enterprises within <strong>regional and global innovation ecosystems</strong>, ensuring that growth in the 2026–2030 period is both <strong>resilient and value-creating</strong>.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b4-96cd-f59d09e56ddf"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h2 id="289c5e6f-95bd-808e-9cff-f038cb55c3ae" class=""><strong>4. Analytical Framework (MECE Model)</strong></h2></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80d5-9a30-c59e77efc64c" class="">Vietnam’s transition from cost-based production to innovation-led growth requires a <strong>systemic and interdependent reform model</strong>.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-806f-a6f3-f0eb2874a4ba" class="">To ensure full coverage without overlap, the challenge is decomposed into <strong>five mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive (MECE) domains</strong>, each representing a policy lever that directly enhances enterprise competitiveness, national innovation capacity, and international integration.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-8000-8271-c2200e4d84df"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-802f-81d5-fb924daac805" class=""><strong>1. 
Innovation and Digital Ecosystems</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80c8-924b-e11aab41fe4f" class=""><strong>Definition:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8087-a292-cc116642d4a4" class="">Institutional, technological, and collaborative structures enabling the creation, diffusion, and commercialisation of new ideas.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8036-9337-d790b2b45f5a" class=""><strong>Rationale:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8031-abe6-cd7ec90ec90b" class="">Innovation ecosystems—comprising research centres, startups, corporates, and universities—form the backbone of competitiveness. Vietnam’s innovation density remains low, with limited R&amp;D commercialisation (public R&amp;D contribution to patents &lt;15%, WIPO 2024).</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8018-a856-da8661d981b9" class=""><strong>Policy focus:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80f4-a09c-d1a5dda5bfd6" class="">Develop regional <strong>innovation clusters</strong>, strengthen <strong>university–industry partnerships</strong>, and scale <strong>digital platforms</strong> that enable data-driven collaboration.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ea-a897-f38bbcc1e5cb"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b6-93f3-e6380b6e99c9" class=""><strong>2. 
Financial and Fiscal Mechanisms</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-803e-9b7f-fb7ebf5f70e1" class=""><strong>Definition:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-805a-8b69-ecde2f4dc34e" class="">Funding, incentives, and policy instruments that lower the cost of innovation and stimulate private R&amp;D.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8053-b76e-e7ee7b58f404" class=""><strong>Rationale:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8082-80b2-e596742709e9" class="">Private R&amp;D expenditure in Vietnam accounts for <strong>0.45% of GDP</strong>, compared with <strong>2.1% in South Korea</strong> and <strong>1.8% in Singapore</strong> (OECD, 2025). Financing constraints prevent firms from upgrading technologies or pursuing frontier research.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8022-930c-cc0d6729d3d8" class=""><strong>Policy focus:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ad-859a-ef5e94fb0b69" class="">Expand <strong>innovation financing</strong> through venture capital incentives, R&amp;D tax credits, and <strong>co-financed innovation funds</strong> that derisk private investment in emerging technologies.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b5-b356-e8ba4ae16895"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-8053-9eed-cc79202fcc82" class=""><strong>3. 
Corporate Governance and Ethical Standards</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8025-81f3-fcbc4d277428" class=""><strong>Definition:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8025-b444-edc5ab84e3f5" class="">Institutional norms and governance structures that ensure transparency, accountability, and ethical innovation within enterprises.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80a5-adf2-d69700fca2a2" class=""><strong>Rationale:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-804f-854d-ff9824cd3d44" class="">Strong governance promotes investor confidence and sustainable growth. Weak IP enforcement and inconsistent ESG adoption deter high-value foreign partnerships.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-804c-b026-f43ce58d2fd7" class=""><strong>Policy focus:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-800d-b856-c75e2412ebb7" class="">Embed <strong>innovation ethics</strong> (data integrity, IP rights, AI safety) into corporate governance codes; align ESG and reporting frameworks with <strong>OECD Responsible Business Conduct</strong> and <strong>UN Global Compact</strong> standards.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80e9-8239-ee7f51f4c9e9"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-809d-b82a-e7c6a0d0a765" class=""><strong>4. 
International Integration and Value-Chain Positioning</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-802b-902f-d13b7a5b74f8" class=""><strong>Definition:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8007-8ed4-d4ccb49ff890" class="">Policies that strengthen Vietnam’s participation in regional and global innovation networks and GVCs.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ba-9464-ff876d4ad27d" class=""><strong>Rationale:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8043-a0b7-e9bc2aa8a153" class="">Vietnam’s integration into global supply chains is deep but primarily at the <strong>assembly and low-R&amp;D stages</strong>. Enhancing participation in <strong>design, technology, and services</strong> segments is critical for value capture.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80f0-9864-c47206967878" class=""><strong>Policy focus:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ee-9506-ccd77e3c2761" class="">Negotiate <strong>technology transfer compacts</strong>, join <strong>OECD and ASEAN innovation platforms</strong>, and attract <strong>FDI in R&amp;D-intensive sectors</strong> through targeted incentives and IP protection.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b4-832c-d26f26d47c88"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-8075-ab0a-f4169179a652" class=""><strong>5. 
Human-Capital and Skill Systems</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-808b-b484-ef528b06c47d" class=""><strong>Definition:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8076-8c89-ce1fce495143" class="">The education, training, and talent-development mechanisms that sustain innovation-driven productivity.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-809f-a1ce-d17279457b6b" class=""><strong>Rationale:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8042-8b86-f3e2d9d4aaa1" class="">Only <strong>9% of Vietnam’s workforce</strong> is engaged in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) occupations—below the <strong>ASEAN average of 15%</strong> (World Bank, 2025). 
Skill deficits limit absorptive capacity and innovation diffusion.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80a6-b068-c6f1df260a6a" class=""><strong>Policy focus:</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8043-8aa3-e45c1c1f7b31" class="">Modernise <strong>STEM and digital curricula</strong>, expand <strong>industry-linked vocational training</strong>, 
and promote <strong>return migration programs</strong> for overseas researchers and entrepreneurs.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-8090-8af7-e45a283486c3"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-80d4-b7a1-efd4827c4a6c" class=""><strong>Integrated Logic</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80b3-b282-d1fd53de4666" class="">These five domains operate as a <strong>closed innovation system</strong>:</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-808a-89dd-f15f01e898ff" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><em>Ecosystems</em> generate ideas.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-8068-841a-e60f734cf231" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><em>Finance</em> enables implementation.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-809e-9c92-d96e7fb6c5c4" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><em>Governance</em> ensures credibility.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-806c-8b6b-d3a55433e105" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><em>Integration</em> scales impact globally.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><ul id="289c5e6f-95bd-80f5-b3c5-f22505f8d4e3" class="bulleted-list"><li style="list-style-type:disc"><em>Human capital</em> sustains the cycle.</li></ul></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ce-b444-c3b9287ea44f" class="">Together, they form the <strong>structural blueprint for Vietnam’s innovation-driven private-sector transformation</strong>.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80c7-8afa-e42a396a7317"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h2 id="289c5e6f-95bd-8090-95fc-d66c11e81623" class=""><strong>5.

Innovation Concentration</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ff-a49d-f8e75e9b4811" class="">Innovation remains concentrated among <strong>state-owned enterprises (SOEs)</strong> and <strong>foreign direct investment (FDI) firms</strong>, which account for over <strong>70% of formal R&amp;D spending</strong>.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80d7-b0c9-db8acbaef4b6" class="">Domestic SMEs, representing <strong>97% of enterprises</strong>, engage primarily in incremental process improvements rather than core technology development.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-804c-ad74-c4439310cf07" class=""><em>Impact:</em> Limited domestic spillovers and low innovation diffusion across supply chains.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8031-bed5-d27622ec8a69" class=""><em>Relevant Domain:</em> <strong>Innovation and digital ecosystems</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-805c-85a1-e2f2007f9aeb"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-80f5-ad21-fa083b8641b6" class=""><strong>2. Weak Protection of Intellectual Property (IP)</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8061-a79f-dfc90d8cb3fd" class="">While Vietnam has strengthened its IP legal framework, <strong>enforcement capacity and judicial consistency remain weak</strong>. 
Patent registration procedures are slow, and dispute resolution mechanisms are under-resourced.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80f7-a06f-f875912479b6" class=""><em>Impact:</em> Reduces incentives for private R&amp;D, deters technology investors, and limits participation in cross-border innovation agreements.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8074-80c8-dac826fa5879" class=""><em>Relevant Domain:</em> <strong>Corporate governance and ethical standards</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-80e3-ab5b-ce68a50057bf"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-80e7-b3ad-db21486f9104" class=""><strong>3. Restricted Access to Innovation Finance</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-8088-8125-d9f4799a9141" class="">Only <strong>one in five SMEs</strong> can access innovation-oriented loans or venture capital. Risk-averse banking practices and a lack of credit guarantees for intangible assets constrain R&amp;D investment.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80ad-bb66-f18809616314" class=""><em>Impact:</em> Capital allocation favours short-term working capital over technology upgrading, reinforcing the productivity gap.</p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-80fa-85b7-c8fd33734c6f" class=""><em>Relevant Domain:</em> <strong>Financial and fiscal mechanisms</strong></p></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><hr id="289c5e6f-95bd-8002-9e8c-fa94cc7d8669"/></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><h3 id="289c5e6f-95bd-8054-8a6d-cd2bf40ea86a" class=""><strong>4. Limited GVC Upgrading and Technology Transfer</strong></h3></div><div style="display:contents" dir="auto"><p id="289c5e6f-95bd-807c-aab9-d6d829cafa70" class="">Vietnam remains primarily an <strong>assembly and processing hub</strong>.
