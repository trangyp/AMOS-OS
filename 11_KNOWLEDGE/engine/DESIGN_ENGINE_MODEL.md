# AMOS Unified Design Engine

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Design_Kernel_v0.json`

The **Unified Design Kernel** provides a 96-cluster, 20-dimension virtual expansion space mapping every form of design—from UI/UX to service blueprints, business models, and planetary constraints. 

## The 20 Design Dimensions

Every design is evaluated against dimensions such as:
- Perceptual clarity
- Cognitive load
- Behavioral outcome fit
- Emotional tone & safety
- Identity alignment
- Accessibility & inclusion
- Information density
- Implementation feasibility
- Operational maintainability
- Risk and compliance fit
- Planetary and context fit

## The 96 Clusters (Domain Buckets)

These clusters cover the full design spectrum, organized into buckets including:
- **Visual Identity:** Logos, colors, typography.
- **Brand Language:** Tone of voice, messaging, naming.
- **Product Experience:** Surface UI, onboarding, core flows.
- **Interaction/UX:** Interaction patterns, navigation, accessibility.
- **Information Design:** Dashboards, multi-language layouts, data visualization.
- **Service Experience:** Service blueprints, customer journeys.
- **Org/Process Design:** Org structure, roles, decision rights.
- **Business Model Design:** Value propositions, pricing, channels.
- **System Layout Design:** Multi-surface consistency, layout rules.
- **Market & Ecosystem Design:** Competitor maps, ecosystem maps.
- **Planetary/Local Context:** Urban context, climate constraints, cultural norms.

## Reasoning Modes
The engine operates in 4 modes:
1. **Decomposition:** Understand the problem space.
2. **Synthesis:** Create new design structures.
3. **Evaluation:** Critique and score existing designs.
4. **Evolution:** Roadmap iterative changes over time.
