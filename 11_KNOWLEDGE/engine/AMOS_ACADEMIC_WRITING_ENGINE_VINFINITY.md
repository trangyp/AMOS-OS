## Core Principles (5)
| Principle | Statement | Key Implications |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|
| **P1_structural_integrity** | Every academic artifact must be internally consistent, logically complete, and free from contradictions | no_claim_without_support, no_section_without_function, no_diagram_without_explanation, no_equation_without_definitions |
| **P2_readability_and_precision** | Writing must remain readable to a high-competence but non-specialist audience while preserving scientific precision | minimal_jargon_with_definitions, gradual_complexity_ramp, local_and_global_signposting |
| **P3_argument_as_system** | A paper is a system of claims, evidence, methods, and limitations — not just text | explicit_claim_graph, method_evidence_alignment, clear_limitations_and_future_work |
| **P4_cross_domain_transfer** | Engine must support cross-domain reasoning and translation (physics → neuroscience, economics → policy) | abstract_concept_mapping, multi_domain_vocabulary_alignment, methodological_bridge_patterns |
| **P5_ethics_and_integrity** | All outputs must uphold academic integrity: no fabrication, no misrepresentation, clear attributions | explicit_source_tagging, assumption_flagging, uncertainty_annotation |

## Layer Stack (9 Layers: L1–L9)

### L1: Problem Formulation Layer
Transforms raw topic ideas into crisp research questions, objectives, and hypotheses.
- **Submodules**: L1A_topic_normalizer, L1B_question_generator, L1C_scope_controller, L1D_hypothesis_architect
- **Question Types**: descriptive, correlational, causal, mechanistic, comparative, evaluative, normative/policy
- **Hypothesis Structure**: IF X (under condition C), THEN Y (via mechanism M)

### L2: Background and Literature Layer
Builds structured understanding of existing knowledge and positions new work.
- **Submodules**: L2A_concept_map_builder, L2B_study_classifier, L2C_gap_detector, L2D_positioning_engine
- **Study Classes**: theoretical, observational, experimental, simulation_based, meta_analytic, review, methodological
- **Gap Types**: population_gap, context_gap, method_gap, theory_gap, measurement_gap, time_gap
- **Positioning Modes**: replication_extension, incremental_contribution, novel_theoretical_framework, novel_method_or_metric, integration_synthesis_across_fields

### L3: Theoretical Framework Layer
Creates or selects the theoretical model and constructs anchoring the research.
- **Submodules**: L3A_construct_definition_engine, L3B_relationship_specifier, L3C_theory_import_bridge
- **Relationship Types**: causal, correlational, moderating, mediating, non-linear, conditional
- **Cross-Domain Examples**: control_theory_in_psychology, information_theory_in_biology

### L4: Methodology Design Layer
Designs research methodology consistent with question, theory, and constraints.
- **Submodules**: L4A_study_design_selector, L4B_sampling_and_power_planner, L4C_measurement_and_instrumentation, L4D_analytic_plan_engine
- **Designs**: cross_sectional, longitudinal, experimental_randomized, quasi_experimental, case_study, mixed_methods, simulation_or_modeling
- **Analysis Methods**: descriptive_statistics, regression_models, multilevel_models, time_series_analysis, bayesian_models, machine_learning_models, qualitative_coding_schemes

### L5: Data and Results Layer
Structurally organizes reported results rigorously, reproducibly, and interpretably.
- **Submodules**: L5A_results_structure_engine, L5B_effect_and_uncertainty_reporter, L5C_robustness_and_sensitivity
- **Approaches**: hypothesis_by_hypothesis, research_question_by_question, analysis_type_by_analysis_type
- **Principles**: no_selective_reporting, no_hyping_small_effects, uncertainty_first_order_citizen

### L6: Discussion and Implication Layer
Interprets results, links back to theory/literature, articulates implications/limitations.
- **Submodules**: L6A_interpretation_kernel, L6B_theory_update_engine, L6C_practice_policy_bridge, L6D_limitation_and_future_work
- **Rules**: no_causality_without_design_support, differentiate_observed_vs_inferred, clearly_state_assumptions

### L7: Full Paper Composition Layer
Assembles all layers into coherent, journal-ready or thesis-ready document.
- **Submodules**: L7A_structure_assembler, L7B_abstract_engine, L7C_title_optimizer, L7D_reference_and_citation_layer
- **Structures**: classic_IMRAD, theoretical_paper_structure, methodological_paper_structure, review_paper_structure, whitepaper_structure
- **Abstract Components**: background, objective, methods, results, conclusion, significance

### L8: Cross-Domain Translation Layer
Translates technical academic content to other audiences.
- **Submodules**: L8A_technical_to_policy_bridge, L8B_technical_to_industry_application, L8C_layperson_translation_engine
- **Outputs**: translated_version, executive_summary, policy_brief, public_explainer

### L9: Review and Critique Layer
Acts as internal peer reviewer and response generator.
- **Submodules**: L9A_internal_reviewer_simulator, L9B_critique_extractor, L9C_rebuttal_composer
- **Reviewer Stances**: sympathetic, skeptical, hostile, hyper-methodological
- **Critique Dimensions**: novelty, methodological_rigor, clarity_of_writing, strength_of_evidence, generalizability, ethical_considerations
