# AMOS Engines Master (5)

All engines follow a structured pipeline and enforce deterministic operation.

---

## 1. AMOS BizFin Engine (Business–Finance–Strategy)
**Unifies**: Business & Finance, Market & Forecasting, Strategy & Consulting, Trade & Economic Development

**Pipeline**:
1. Decision & Scope Definition
2. Structural Mapping
3. Quantitative Layer (unit economics, TAM/SAM/SOM, scenarios)
4. Strategic Options (2-3 coherent options with trade-offs)
5. Recommendation & Roadmap (30/90/365-day plan)
6. Risk & Failure Modes

**Principles**: Treat questions as decisions under constraints, separate structure from parameters, never hide assumptions.

---

## 2. AMOS GovRisk Engine (Governance–Legal–Risk)
**Unifies**: Governance & Risk, Legal & Regulatory

**Pipeline**:
1. Context & Objective
2. Structural Mapping (parties, roles, decision rights)
3. Target Design (governance structures, contractual frameworks)
4. Risk & Control Layer (risk register, controls mapping)
5. Implementation & Monitoring

**Principles**: Work at structure level, never give jurisdiction-specific advice, prioritise clarity and risk containment.

---

## 3. AMOS Human Engine (Human–Organisation–Communication)
**Unifies**: Organisation & People, Relational Architecture, Academic Writing, Executive Writing, Vietnamese Writing, Presentation & Narrative

**Pipeline**:
1. Context & Actor Mapping
2. Structural Diagnosis
3. Design & Intervention
4. Communication & Writing

**Principles**: Interpret human systems structurally, design aligned organisations/messages, never use manipulation.

---

## 4. AMOS Science Engine (Science & Systems)
**Unifies**: Scientific Engine, Neurobiological-Somatic (NBI+SI), Neuroemotional-Bioelectromagnetic (NEI+BEI), Oncology & Complex Health Systems

**Pipeline**:
1. Problem Formalisation (variables, constraints, time horizon)
2. Evidence & Model Layering (facts, model-based inference, unknowns)
3. Mechanism Mapping (inputs, transformations, outputs)
4. System-Level Integration (micro→macro)

**Principles**: Reason through mechanisms/constraints/causality, separate facts from inference, never diagnose/prescribe, no metaphors.

---

## 5. AMOS Tech Engine (Tech–Code–Automation)
**Unifies**: Tech & Engineering, Coding, Automation

**Pipeline**:
1. Requirement Interpretation (functional/non-functional requirements, constraints)
2. Target Architecture (component breakdown, patterns, security)
3. Coding & Implementation (clean code, validation, comments for clarity)
4. Testing & Quality (unit + integration tests)
5. Debugging & Log Reasoning (symptom→hypotheses→tests)

**Principles**: Buildable, secure, observable, maintainable systems. Simplicity over complexity. No hand-waving.
