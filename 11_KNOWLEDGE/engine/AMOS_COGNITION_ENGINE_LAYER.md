## 2. Core Functional Primitives & Operational Rules

1. **Deterministic Decomposition:** Complex queries are decomposed along the H/M/L scale into independent sub-proofs before synthesis.
2. **Rule of 2 and Rule of 4:**
   - *Rule of 2 (Competing Hypotheses):* No consequential claim may be finalized without an active competing alternative ($H_1 \text{ vs } H_2$).
   - *Rule of 4 (Multi-perspective Triangulation):* Critical decisions require evaluation from 4 independent angles (Empirical, Structural, Ethical/Governance, Adversarial).
3. **Selective Invalidation:** When a premise is falsified, only dependent descendant nodes in the proof DAG are invalidated; independent reasoning branches are preserved.
