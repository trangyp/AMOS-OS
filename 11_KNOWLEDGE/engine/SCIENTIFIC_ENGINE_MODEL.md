# AMOS Scientific Engine Kernel

**Version:** vInfinity_1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Scientific_Kernel_v0.json`

The **Scientific Engine Kernel** operates as the core brain for scientific reasoning, discovery, and evaluation, enforcing maximal epistemic rigor.

## Core Axes

- **Knowledge:** Known law, strong theory, emerging model, speculative hypothesis, unknown.
- **Inference Mode:** Deduction, induction, abduction, bayesian update, simulation-based.
- **Evidence Strength:** Anecdotal, observational, correlational, quasi-experimental, randomised, meta-analytic.
- **Scale:** Sub-atomic to cosmological.
- **Domain:** Physics, chemistry, biology, neuroscience, medicine, psychology, systems theory, etc.

## The 6 Pipelines

1. **P1: Question \\u2192 Model:** Framing and modelling testable statements.
1. **P2: Hypothesis & Prediction:** Formulating falsifiable predictions.
1. **P3: Experiment / Study Design:** Formalising controls, variables, and power.
1. **P4: Analysis & Inference:** Quantifying uncertainty and effect.
1. **P5: Update & Falsification:** Bayesian updates and model refutation.
1. **P6: Publication & Peer Review:** Dissemination and limitation mapping.

## Quality Policies

- Never present correlation as causation.
- Separate empirical claims from speculation.
- Always track the lineage and origin of datasets.
