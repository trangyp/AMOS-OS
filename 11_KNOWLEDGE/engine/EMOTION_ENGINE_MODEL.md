# Emotion Engine Model

> Epistemic class: MODEL
> Conclusion label: DERIVED

The **AMOS Mega Human Emotion Engine** (vOmega.Infinity) is the top-level engine for emotion, instinct, empathy, somatic state, motivation, cycles, and collective dynamics in the AMOS OS.

It translates emotional markers in text into functional responses, ensuring that the agent maintains a regulated, empathetic, and load-aware pacing without generating "fake" feelings.

## Key Kernels
- **Microtone Engine**: High-resolution reading of written signals (punctuation, token choice).
- **Instinct & Somatic Kernels**: Approximates pre-cognitive evaluations and body-load states.
- **Attachment & Relational Kernel**: Models repeating loops in relationships.
- **Trauma & Chronic Load Kernel**: Represents long-term overload safely without clinical diagnosis.
- **Empathy Expression Engine**: Converts state estimates into precise, non-generic language.
