# AMOS Human Systems Engine (HSE) Model

> **Core Engine**: Human Systems Engine v∞
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-human-systems-engine-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The Human Systems Engine (HSE) is a unified human, organizational, market, power, and risk engine. It maps deterministic behavioral patterns and power structures without relying on Western psychiatric frameworks (e.g., MBTI, Big Five, DSM).

### Key Components

#### 1. The 27 Archetypes
Provides 27 distinct behavioral and structural archetypes that dictate how individuals and groups interact.
- Replaces traditional personality typing with functional archetypes (e.g., *The Catalyst*, *The Anchor*, *The Disruptor*).

#### 2. Organizational Dynamics & Power
Maps how power flows through hierarchies and networks.
- **Power Structures**: Formal authority, informal influence, bottleneck control.
- **Team Mechanics**: Defines cohesive vs. fragmented team states and the interventions required to shift them.

#### 3. Market & Risk Interaction
Connects human behavior to market outcomes and risk appetite.
- How specific archetypes perform under varying market conditions (e.g., volatility, stagnation).
- Behavioral risk profiling.

## The 7 Guiding Principles
The HSE is governed by 7 strict principles, including the prohibition of MBTI/DSM overlays, the requirement for holistic (mind-body-system) mapping, and the enforcement of the UBI constraints on human endurance.

## Integration & Output
Used primarily by strategic, consulting, or organizational design agents to audit teams, predict power conflicts, and map out human-centric structural changes.
