# AMOS Unified Coding Engine

**Version:** 1.0.1
**Source:** `AMOS_Unified_Coding_Engine_v0.json` & `AMOS_Coding_Kernel_v0.json`

The **Unified Coding Engine** provides a universal technical reasoning kernel for all software engineering and architecture domains. It operates using "Triple-Density" reasoning to ensure every technical decision is sound across all levels of the business.

## Triple-Density Reasoning Modes

1. **Low-Level Code Reasoning:** Syntax, memory, performance, algorithmic correctness.
1. **System-Level Design Reasoning:** API contracts, module boundaries, database schemas, microservice dependencies.
1. **Org-Level Strategy Reasoning:** Business KPIs, team velocity, maintenance overhead, vendor lock-in, and risk exposure.

## Global Lifecycle

All software artifacts must pass through these phases (or a subset, depending on scope):

1. **Vision & Scoping**
1. **Architecture & Design**
1. **Build & Integrate**
1. **Stabilize & Harden**
1. **Scale & Optimize**
1. **Govern & Audit**
1. **Sunset & Migrate**

## Quality Axes

Solutions are optimized across 10 dimensions:

- Correctness, Robustness, Security, Performance, Scalability, Maintainability, Operability, Usability, Composability, Compliance.

## Failure Modes to Avoid

- Unclear problem definition before writing code.
- Architecture not matching real-world constraints.
- Integration breaking existing workflows.
- Instability under real load (ignoring edge cases).
- Opaque ownership and lack of observability.
