# AMOS Australia Engines Model

This model documents the three unified kernels operating within the `amos-australia-engine-layer` for the Australian jurisdiction.

## 1. Australia Economy MegaKernel FULL (v2.0.0)

**Purpose**: Exhaustive structural map of the Australian economy across sectors, capital, labour, trade, institutions, and scenarios.
**Scope**: Macro indicators (GDP, inflation, unemployment, etc.) and specific structural sectors (Agriculture, Mining, Manufacturing, Construction, Energy, Logistics, etc.).
**Use Cases**: Macro analysis, sector strategy, policy simulation, risk mapping.

## 2. Australia Law, Incentives, Funding & Grants Engine (vInfinity)

**Purpose**: Deterministic structural engine for mapping Australian law, incentives, funding and grants across all levels of government and sectors. Includes advanced layers for procedural modelling, cross-country comparison, time-shift projection, and ecosystem interaction.
**Key Rule**: This engine is structural only. It does not provide legal, tax, or financial advice, nor does it guarantee eligibility.
**Outputs**: Policy input resolution, funding stack options, structural maps, and professional handoffs.

## 3. Australia Workforce Omniverse Kernel Engine (vInfinity SUPER)

**Purpose**: Kernel for analyzing, designing, and stress-testing the Australian workforce system across all dimensions (labour markets, skills, migration, IR, technology, equity, regions, sectors).
**Governing Principles**: System first (not program first), explicit constraints, multi-axis balance (productivity, equity, resilience), and separation between diagnostics and prescriptions.
**Modes**:

- Diagnostic of the workforce system.
- Policy and program design.
- Sector/region workforce strategy.
- Future of work scenario lab.
