# Human Systems Engine™ (HSE) VN

## Core Function
Human behaviour → risk → performance → scaling → outcomes across individual → team → org → market → infrastructure → power → prediction layers.

## Value Proposition
- Reduce human error, increase correct execution
- No more internal surprises
- 15–45% productivity gain
- Stable operations, reduced CEO load by 40–60%
- Sustainable growth, standardized governance

## 5. Five Layers (Calculation Engine Classes)

| Layer | Data Inputs | Warning Signals | Output |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| Human Analysis | Behavior, tracking, performance, health | "Cần hỗ trợ" (need support), "lệch vai" (shoulder bias) | Support recommendations |
| Behavior Tracking | Weekly-monthly rhythm | Repeated delays (3x), lost motivation (5x) | Individual/team patterns |
| Performance Health | Real decisions, meetings, KPIs | Meeting without decisions (3x) | Org health score |
| Org Health | Groups, hidden info, blocked access | Hidden information, faction creation | Org transparency score |
| Internal Risk | Attitude, speed, quality, cooperation | Avoidance, KPI manipulation, group blocking | Risk escalation alerts |

| Type | % | Characteristics | Risks | Value |
|------|-----|-----------------|-------|-------|
| Foundation (A) | 30-45% | Stable, reliable | Resists change | Org foundation |
| Execution (B) | 35-50% | Doers, compliant | Lack initiative | Volume execution |
| Improvement (C) | 8-12% | Optimizers | Blocked by A | System upgrades |
| Change Driver (D) | 3-5% | Innovators | Opposition risk | Breakthrough growth |

## 7 System Cycles (Kỳ Hệ Thống)
1. **Ổn định** (Stability) — Establish foundation
2. **Tăng tốc** (Acceleration) — Productivity growth
3. **Mở rộng** (Expansion) — Scale 2–10x
4. **Quá tải** (Overload) — Rhythm disruption, incidents
5. **Điều chỉnh** (Adjustment) — Reduce load, fix bottlenecks
6. **Suy yếu** (Weakening) — Motivation loss, market loss
7. **Tái sinh** (Rebirth) — New org formation
