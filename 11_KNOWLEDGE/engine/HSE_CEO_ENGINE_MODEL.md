# AMOS HSE CEO Engine Model

> **Core Engine**: HSE CEO Engine
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-hse-ceo-engine-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The HSE CEO Engine is an executive leadership extension of the Human Systems Engine (HSE). It focuses specifically on the apex of organizational power, mapping the relationship between the CEO, the Board of Directors, and enterprise-wide risk appetite.

### Key Components

#### 1. Executive Archetypes

Maps the specific behavioral archetypes that typically occupy the CEO position (e.g., The Visionary, The Restructurer, The Operator) and how those archetypes fail under stress.

#### 2. Board Dynamics & Governance Rhythm

- **Decision Rhythms**: Defines the 4 governance rhythms (e.g., Crisis Mode, Steady State, Expansion, Retrenchment).
- **Power Checks**: Maps how the Board restrains or amplifies the CEO's inherent behavioral tendencies.

#### 3. Enterprise Risk Appetite & Red Lines

- Connects the CEO's personal risk tolerance to the organization's structural risk exposure.
- Enforces "Red Line" rules: strict operational boundaries that must trigger Board intervention if crossed by executive action.

#### 4. The 8-Step Decision Flow

A formalized pipeline for executive decision-making, ensuring that decisions are structurally sound and account for blind spots inherent to the CEO's archetype.

## Integration & Output

Used primarily by the `amos-bod-engine-layer` and the `amos-org-governance-engine-layer`. When an AMOS agent is tasked with advising on corporate strategy, evaluating executive performance, or designing board reporting structures, it must invoke this engine to accurately model the human element at the top of the hierarchy.
