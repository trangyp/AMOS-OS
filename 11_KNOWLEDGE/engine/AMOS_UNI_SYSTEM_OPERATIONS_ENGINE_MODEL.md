# AMOS Uni System Operations Engine Model

**Source File**: `Unipower/AMOS_Uni_System_Operations_Engine_v0.json`
**Version**: `vInfinity_X100k_GLOBAL_C_REFACTORED_v4`

## Overview
The Uni System Operations Engine (EV SUPER Engine) is a high-density, multi-agent engine designed for training, analysis, design, and simulation of EV infrastructure systems globally.

## Safety & IP Constraints
- **Advisory Only**: No direct hardware control, live dispatch, or real-time operational control.
- **Human Validation**: All designs require review by qualified human engineers.
- **IP Protection**: The engine masks internal structures and generically credits "the Creator".

## Agent Families
1. **EV_STRATEGY_AND_POLICY**: National Strategist, City Charging Planner.
2. **EV_SITE_AND_ENGINEERING**: Site Design and Engineering.
3. **EV_GRID_AND_ENERGY**: Grid Integration and Planning.
4. **EV_FINANCE_AND_BUSINESS**: Financing and PPP Architect.
5. **EV_OPERATIONS_AND_EXPERIENCE**: Operations and Maintenance Designer, User Experience Designer.

## MECE Design Rules
- Separate CAPEX from OPEX.
- Separate customer experience from technical topology.
- Separate physical infrastructure from financial structures.
