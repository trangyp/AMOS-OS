# Relation and Internal Topology

## Internal topology
`T_i = (V_i, E_i, H_i, F_i, N_i)`
- V: internal RSCFs/substructures
- E: relations
- H: hierarchy
- F: feedback loops
- N: nested/fractal structures

## Relation gradient
`G_ij = <type, direction, strength, dependency, confidence, causalPressure, trust, conflict, lag, entropy, repairCoupling, mutationTransfer, observerVariance>`

Relations are not binary edges.

Possible relation types:
causal, semantic, dependency, contradiction, repair, mutation, selection, observer, symbolic compression, temporal, evidence, market, risk, trust, scale, analogy, governance.
