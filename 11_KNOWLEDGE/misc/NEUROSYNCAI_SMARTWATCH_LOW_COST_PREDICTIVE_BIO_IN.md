     * CRC desynchrony (breath–heart coherence <0.3)


  3. **No self-recovery after 2 cues and 1 Tier 2 notification.**


### **Action**
  * Immediate escalation to **responsible authority** (predefined).


  * AI sends **status-only message** —never biometric data.


### **Message Template**
### **For Fleet/Military/Institution**
```
    Critical Alert: Operator 021 — Unstable state detected during high-risk activity.
    Predicted Fatigue/Stress Index: Critical (Stability 38%).
    Action taken: Watch initiated recovery cues; no response detected.
    Please verify operator safety.
    
```
### **For Family (Care Mode)**
```
    Your father’s watch detected distress during activity.
    The system is assisting recovery and has notified the facility.
    You don’t need to act unless contacted again.
    
```
### **Timing**
  * Triggered within **5–8 seconds** of failed self-recovery.


  * Resolved notification sent if stability returns within 2 minutes.


### **Fail-Safe Conditions**
  * Escalation stops once HRV and CRC return to 80% baseline.


  * Duplicate alerts prevented within 30-minute window.


  * Family mode limited to 1 update per event (no live streaming).

## Layer 0 — Core (Baseline You Already Have)
**Purpose:** Pre-cognitive detection of stress/fatigue/emotion drift on commodity smartwatches.

**Outputs:** EPI (Emotion), CSS (Cognition), PIS (Integrity), SDI (Somatic Drift).

**Org Value:** Reduce incidents, errors, and burnout.
**KPIs:** Lead time (s), event prevention rate, time-to-recovery, false positive rate.

## Layer 1 — Multimodal Sensing Expansion
**Goal:** Increase predictive precision with additional low-cost, privacy-safe modalities.
  1. **Voice Tonality (on-device, no content)**
     * Features: jitter, shimmer, F0 variance, speech/respiration cadence.
     * Value: Cognitive strain, anxiety precursor, respiratory mismatch.

     * Value: Fatigue, fall-risk, neurological flags.

# Data, Privacy, and Ethics (Non-Negotiables)
  1. **Data Minimization:** transmit status codes, not raw biosignals (except explicit clinical consent).


  2. **Edge-First:** inferences on-device; cloud only for opted-in analytics.


  3. **Consent by Tier:** self-only → assisted → critical; reversible anytime.


  4. **Explainability:** every alert is explainable to the wearer and auditable by third parties.


  5. **Bias & Equity:** cohort-specific baselines; fairness audits each release.


  6. **Security:** AES-256/TLS 1.3; hardware root of trust where available.


  7. **Kill Switch & Respect Mode:** user can silence non-critical cues; critical tasks override silence only with prior consent.


  8. **Retention:** default 30 days; full delete on request; ledger keeps hashed, non-identifying event refs for audits.
