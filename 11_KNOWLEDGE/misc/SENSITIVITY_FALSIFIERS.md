# Sensitivity and Falsifiers

## Sensitivity target
Find the smallest premise, observation, threshold, assumption, or dependency whose change flips the conclusion.

## Falsifier
A valid falsifier can be:
- contradictory observation
- source update/revocation
- failed premise
- scope/regime mismatch
- counterexample
- benchmark reproduction failure
- hard invariant violation
- environment/dependency drift

Fragile conclusions become CONDITIONAL.
