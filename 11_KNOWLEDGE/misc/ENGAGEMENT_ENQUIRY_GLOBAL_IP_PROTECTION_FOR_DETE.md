Specifically, I would like advice and support on the following workstreams:

  * Formal trade-secret framework for the scientific canon, core algorithms, kernels and methods.


  * Policies and procedures (access control, documentation, disclosure rules) suitable for both research collaborations and commercial engagements.

    * Australia;
    * United States;
    * European Union (and any other recommended jurisdictions).


  * Strategy for evidencing authorship, dates of creation and chain of title.


### **4.3 Patents**
  * Assessment of which components are **patentable** without compromising trade-secret value, including:
    * deterministic reasoning engine and drift-prevention mechanisms;
    * audit and causal reconstruction engine;
    * biological–computational programming method;
    * multi-domain routing / entanglement graph design;
    * other implementation-level inventions.


  * Development of a filing strategy (priority jurisdiction, PCT, timing relative to potential publications and commercial launches).


### **4.4 Trademarks and Brands**
  * Registration strategy for marks including (non-exhaustive):
    * AMOS – Absolute Meta Operating System;
    * Quantum Logic Systems (QLS);
    * Bio-Logical Intelligence Systems;
    * Unified Biological Intelligence;

  * Advice on the most appropriate jurisdiction and entity structure for an **IP-holding company** (for example, Australia, Singapore or a US entity such as Delaware), with:
