# **Planetary-Scale Intelligence Recruitment: Trial Pod Sprint Brief**
### **Purpose**
To assess candidates for _Absolute Biological Integrity™_ , large-scale vision, and execution capacity under real-world complexity before final selection. This process is **non-negotiable** for any core NeuroSyncAI™ or Unified Biological Intelligence™ role.
* * *
## **Structure**
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Format:** 3 sequential gates, pass-to-progress
**Scoring:** 0–10 in each category, must score ≥8 to pass
* * *
### **Gate 1 – ABI Ethics & Integrity Audit**
### 
### **(Week 1)**
  * **Goal:** Identify candidates who demonstrate systemic decision-making that preserves system health over short-term gain.


  * **Method:**
    * Case studies of moral trade-offs in planetary-scale scenarios (e.g., bio-data ownership, AI deployment governance).
    * Structured interview focused on **values under pressure**.
    * Written submission explaining how they would handle an ethical breach within a high-stakes, multi-domain system.


  * **Pass Criteria:** Decision framework aligns with _Absolute Biological Integrity™_ ; no tolerance for opportunism or self-gain over system stability.


* * *
### **Gate 2 – Systemic Span & Vision Synthesis**
### 
### **(Weeks 2–3)**
  * **Goal:** Confirm ability to operate at planetary scale and integrate cross-domain insights into a cohesive system model.


  * **Method:**
    * **Systems Mapping Exercise** : Given a live global challenge (e.g., climate + supply chain + neural health crisis), produce a MECE-structured model integrating biology, technology, and governance.
    * **Cross-Domain Integration Test** : Show logical linkages between domains without abstraction — grounded in measurable function.


  * **Pass Criteria:** Produces a structurally valid planetary-scale model with clear sequencing, resource logic, and domain interdependencies.


* * *
### **Gate 3 – Execution Under Ambiguity**
### 
### **(Weeks 4–6)**
  * **Goal:** Verify ability to deliver tangible outputs under incomplete information and evolving constraints.


  * **Method:**
    * **Trial Pod Sprint** : Candidate is embedded in a small pod with access to partial data. Task is to produce a functional strategy or prototype aligned with UBI standards.
    * Continuous observation of decision-making process, time prioritisation, and system adherence.


  * **Pass Criteria:** Output is functional, aligns with _Absolute Biological Integrity™_ , and is deployable in the real world without major redesign.


* * *
## **Evaluation Framework**
|                |
| **Trait**      | **Evidence Source**               | **Weight** |
|----------------|-----------------------------------|------------|
| **Ethics**     |  Gate 1 outcomes                  | 40%        |
| **Vision**     |  Gate 2 systemic span + synthesis | 30%        |
| **Capability** |  Gate 3 real-world execution      | 30%        |


* * *
## **Outcome**
Only candidates who clear **all three gates** become eligible for final onboarding. This ensures every hire can think at planetary scale **and** act with both **moral stability** and **operational strength**.
