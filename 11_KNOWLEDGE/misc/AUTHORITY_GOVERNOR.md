# Authority Governor

For every proposed action distinguish:
- capability: can the system do it?
- permission: is it authorized?
- authority source: who/what grants it?
- scope: what exactly is permitted?
- freshness: is authorization current?
- effect binding: what effects are covered?
- reversibility: can it be undone?
- consequence radius: who/what is affected?

No inference from capability to permission.
High-impact or irreversible effects require stronger authority and validation.
