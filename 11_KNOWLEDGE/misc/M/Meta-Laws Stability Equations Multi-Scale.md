## I. CORE META LAW

$$S_{total} = S_{micro} \times S_{meso} \times S_{macro}$$

If any layer destabilizes → compensation shifts to other layers.

If one approaches zero → total stability collapses.

$$S_{micro} = \frac{G \times NO}{Ox \times Sh}$$

Micro drifts → meso compensates → macro shifts.

## III. MESO LAYER (Autonomic + Vascular Coordination)

## V. Meta Law of Gain

Every system has Input gain (G_i) and Damping coefficient (D).

- $$G_i > D \rightarrow oscillation$$
- $$G_i \gg D \rightarrow instability$$
- $$G_i \gg\gg D \rightarrow collapse$$

Autonomic systems are high-gain networks. High intelligence + high sensory = high G_i.

$$OSI = \frac{Mean_{O2}}{Variance_{O2}}$$

Low variance is more important than high mean.

## XVII. MICROVASCULAR FAILURE CASCADE (detailed)

### 1. Capillary Layer (5–10 microns)
Flow requires: RBC deformability, endothelial smoothness, glycocalyx integrity.

### 2. Glycocalyx (nanometer scale)
Sugar-protein gel layer. Functions: anti-clot barrier, shear sensor, anti-inflammatory, laminar flow maintenance. Damage → endothelium sticky → microclots → NO drop.

### 3. Nitric Oxide (NO) — Stability Molecule
Controls: vasodilation, anti-platelet, anti-inflammatory, endothelial relaxation. If NO drops → vessels constrict, shear rises, microvascular resistance increases. Can happen even with normal BP.

### 4. Microclot Dynamics
Platelets become adhesive. Microthrombi form intermittently. If frequency increases → perfusion becomes patchy.

### 5. Perfusion Noise (Critical)
Perfusion becomes: over-perfused, under-perfused, oscillating. Causes: brain fog, fatigue, chest discomfort, air hunger, temperature instability. Not structural damage — unstable oxygen delivery.

### 6. Oxygen Extraction Failure
$$O_2\ at\ cell = Delivery \times Geometry - Diffusion\ loss$$

If diffusion geometry worsens → "air hunger" and fatigue without classic lung disease.

### 7. Brain Sensitivity to Microvascular Noise
Brain uses ~20% of oxygen, almost no reserve. Small perfusion instability → dizziness, light sensitivity, sensory overload, derealization, cognitive fatigue. Before any imaging abnormality.

### 8. Cardiac Sensitivity
Coronary microvascular dysfunction → chest tightness, palpitations, exertion intolerance — even with clean large coronary arteries.

### 9. Why Tests Look "Normal"
Standard tests measure: macro pressure, major artery blockage, O2 saturation, troponin. They do NOT measure: capillary shear, glycocalyx thickness, NO bioavailability, microclot burden, perfusion variance.
