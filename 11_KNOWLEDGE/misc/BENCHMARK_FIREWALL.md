# Benchmark Firewall

Benchmark claims require comparability:
hardware, dataset, configuration, version, measurement_methodology, warmup, load, latency_percentiles

If missing: `INCOMPARABLE`

## Rules
- 100% on a benchmark is not universal validity.
- Distributed/Byzantine tests are not general formal proofs unless a formal proof exists.
- Latency is environment/hardware/configuration dependent.
- Preserve failures and known gaps from earlier versions.
