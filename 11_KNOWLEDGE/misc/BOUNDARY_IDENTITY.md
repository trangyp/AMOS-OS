# Boundary and Identity

## Distinction
`Distinct(R_i,R_j)=1` only when a structurally relevant property differs.

Identity is defined by positive identity conditions, negative/exclusion conditions, and invariants.

## Boundary
`B_i = <Inside, Outside, Ingress, Egress, Permissions, Permeability>`

Boundary health model:
`BoundaryHealth = Integrity × Selectivity × AdaptivePermeability`

Failure regimes:
- permeability → 1: identity leakage
- permeability → 0: adaptive rigidity

Identity drift above tolerance triggers clarification, split, ontology revision, merge, quarantine, or retirement.
