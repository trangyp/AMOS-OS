# Invariants

Hard gate: `Admit(x)=AND_i I_i(x)`.

- mediation is not inferred from sequence alone
- confounders remain separate from mediators
- cross-scale mediator claims require bridge evidence
- direct and indirect effects are not double counted
- intervention-effect language requires intervention-appropriate evidence
