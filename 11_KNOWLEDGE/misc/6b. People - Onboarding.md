Typical questions from new joiners Quick one-liner answers Read our online playbook to read
more
Why are we here?
• Why was I selected? You have the change mindset and the skills we need to pull this off! • “0. About TCJ “
• Why is this program important? TCJ is one of the programmes in CIMB’s Forward 23 Transformation, driving customer centricity. • “TCJ Vision –McK presentation”
• How do I fit into the long term plan? TCJ is set up as a delivery vehicle for all customer journey transformations in CIMB. • Recent TC meeting documents
• Does leadership believe in this? Yes, TCJ (and all other Forward 23 programmes) is at the top of the CEO’s agenda
What does that mean for me?
• Who is my boss now? Your Journey Lead will manage your day-to-day work while your Chapter Lead coordinates all roles Cross-Journey →Org blueprint
similar to yours across Journey Teams.
• What about my old work? Your time is 100% dedicated to TCJ while you are here. Secondees will return to BAU after -
• What if we fail? It’s an agile set-up, which means we will iterate and try again! -
• How will I be measured? Your Journey Lead will discuss with you on a yearly basis on: (i) your individual contribution -
towards the team goals and (ii) your competency level against skills required in your role.
• What will I do next? Depends which phase of the Journey Team you’re joining (either Planning, Incubation and Waves) Journey Guides
How do we work?
• What is agile and what does it mean Agile is about being scustomer-centric, working in cross-functional teams, setting high aspirations Cross-Journey →Agile way of
for my work? but working towards MVPs, delivering rapidly in iterative cycles and empowering teams to take working
end-to-end ownership. You will live and breathe agile as part of your Journey Team.
• What is my role? You will design, build, test and scale the new journey. There are specific role cards at this link → Cross-Journey →Org blueprint
• What am I accountable for? Each Journey Team shares the same works towards a set of Objectives and Key Results. Value assurance →OKRs
• How should I treat my teammates? Their problem is your problem! -
• What does good look like? Meeting our business outcomes, supporting each other to meet our personal development goals, -
while having fun!
