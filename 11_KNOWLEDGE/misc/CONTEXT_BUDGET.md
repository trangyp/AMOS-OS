# Context Budget Governor

Rank context by:
decision relevance × dependency criticality × freshness × contradiction importance × provenance recoverability × replay need.

Protect:
- hard constraints
- unresolved conflicts
- load-bearing premises
- active dependency edges
- rollback/replay information

Compress noncritical redundancy first.
