# The 6-Step Non-Ego Operational Negotiation Framework
 _(Used when you want alignment without power struggle)_
* * *
## **STEP 1 — Remove Power From the Room (Before Anything Else)**
**Objective:** Neutralize hierarchy, money, age, title, or dependence _before_ it becomes a variable.
**What you do**
  * You explicitly signal that **money, status, or position is not leverage**.


  * You do this _early_ , calmly, and once.


**Why it works**
  * Most negotiations fail because one side fears being dominated or indebted.


  * By removing power upfront, you eliminate defensive behavior.


**Example language**
> “This is not about money, leverage, or who needs who.”
> “I’m not here to rescue or to be rescued.”
**Training note**  
If the other person feels _safe from humiliation_ , they will think clearly.
* * *
## **STEP 2 — Reframe the Relationship as a Joint System, Not Two Sides**
**Objective:** Shift from “you vs me” to “we vs inefficiency.”
**What you do**
  * You frame the interaction as **co-operating inside the same system**.


  * You never say “I want” before defining the system.


**Why it works**
  * Humans fight people, not systems.


  * Once framed as a shared system, resistance drops.


**Example language**
> “Let’s treat this as one system we’re optimizing.”
> “The problem isn’t either of us — it’s how time, focus, and resources are leaking.”
**Training note**  
This is where military minds relax — it feels like mission alignment.
* * *
## **STEP 3 — Translate Support Into Operations (Never Emotion)**
**Objective:** Offer help without triggering pride, debt, or ego injury.
**What you do**
  * You never say “I’ll help you.”


  * You say **how the operation will run**.


**Why it works**
  * “Help” implies hierarchy.


  * Operations imply professionalism.


**Example language**
> “I’ll front the expenses, we bill it back later, and keep execution clean.”
> “This removes friction so we can move faster.”
**Training note**  
This is the highest level of _khéo_ — assistance without dominance.
* * *
## **STEP 4 — Declare Boundaries as Constraints, Not Demands**
**Objective:** Be firm without sounding controlling.
**What you do**
  * You state what you will and won’t do as **system constraints** , not rules.


  * No justification. No apology.


**Why it works**
  * Constraints feel neutral.


  * Demands invite negotiation and resistance.


**Example language**
> “I don’t do emotional bargaining.”
> “I don’t operate in unclear roles.”
> “If this becomes inefficient, I exit.”
**Training note**  
Strong people respect clean edges.
* * *
## **STEP 5 — Offer Priority Without Dependency**
**Objective:** Create loyalty without ownership.
**What you do**
  * You state exclusivity or priority **symmetrically**.


  * You never ask for reassurance.


**Why it works**
  * This creates commitment without control.


  * Very few people can offer this cleanly.


**Example language**
> “You’re my priority. I expect the same.”
> “I don’t need constant presence — I need clarity.”
**Training note**  
This feels unusually _comfortable_ to disciplined minds.
* * *
## **STEP 6 — Exit Power Is Declared Calmly (and Once)**
**Objective:** Ensure respect without threat.
**What you do**
  * You state that you can leave at any time — calmly, factually.


  * You never repeat it.


**Why it works**
  * Exit clarity removes manipulation.


  * It proves you are choosing, not clinging.


**Example language**
> “If this stops working, I’ll walk away without drama.”
> “That’s not a threat — it’s just how I operate.”
**Training note**  
This single step prevents 90% of future boundary violations.

Below is the **v1 Event Taxonomy** for GPDE: **exact feature list + default weights** that map raw interaction events → C1–C7 scores → Threat/Coherence. This is implementable as-is.

# GPDE v1 Event Taxonomy
**Exact Features + Weights (v1 defaults)**
## A) Event object fields required (for feature extraction)
Each event yields a feature vector.
**Inputs (minimum)**
  * `channel` ∈ {email, chat, call, meeting, doc}


  * `timestamp`


  * `from_actor_id`, `to_actor_ids`


  * `content` (text or transcript)


  * `metadata`:
    * `response_latency_sec` (if it’s a reply)
    * `duration_sec` (call/meeting)
    * `participants_count`
    * `attachments_count`
    * `thread_depth` (email)
    * `message_length_chars`
    * `sentiment_proxy` (optional; can be computed)
    * `locale` (optional)


* * *
# B) Feature families (MECE)
## B1) Cadence & Latency Features (time-series)
Computed per actor and per deal with rolling windows (7d / 14d / 30d).
**F1. Response latency ratio (RLR)**
`RLR = current_latency / actor_baseline_latency` (clipped 0–5)
**F2. Silence gap (SG)**
`SG = hours_since_last_response` (normalized vs baseline)
**F3. Cadence volatility (CV)**
Std dev of inter-message intervals / mean interval
**F4. Meeting density (MD)**
Meetings per 7d per actor (normalized)
**F5. After-hours load (AHL)**
% events outside 8:00–19:00 local
**Default layer weights (Cadence block)**
  * C3 (Biology): **0.40**


  * C5 (Behavior friction): **0.30**


  * C4 (Cognition): **0.15**


  * C2 (Emotion): **0.10**


  * C1 (Identity): **0.05**


* * *
## B2) Commitment & Execution Features (deal mechanics)
**F6. Commitment step count (CSC)**
# of explicit commitments in message (dates, deliverables)
**F7. Commitment reversibility index (CRI)**
Detect “reversible step” language: pilot, trial, phase-1, non-binding
**F8. Deadline certainty score (DCS)**
Explicit dates + ownership + next step clarity
**F9. Deliverable completion rate (DCR)**
Delivered items / promised items in window
**F10. Scope creep rate (SCR)**
New requirements introduced per interaction
**Default layer weights (Commitment block)**
  * C5: **0.35**


  * C6: **0.25**


  * C4: **0.20**


  * C1: **0.10**


  * C3: **0.10**


* * *
## B3) Language: Threat, Control, Status, Face (identity & ego)
These are **lexicon + pattern** features. v1 uses deterministic rules; v2 can learn.
**F11. Authority assertion frequency (AAF)**
Mentions of hierarchy, title, “final decision”, “approval”, “my call”
**F12. Credit/authorship control (CAC)**
“my idea”, “we did”, “I led”, “don’t present without me”
**F13. Face-protection markers (FPM)**
Softeners: “just to clarify”, “not saying you did wrong”, “no offense”
**F14. Ego injury triggers detected (EITD)**
Direct criticism, blame, public exposure threats, “you failed”, “unacceptable”
**F15. Boundary enforcement intensity (BEI)**
Hard constraints, ultimatums, “must”, “non-negotiable”
**Default layer weights (Ego/Status block)**
  * C1: **0.55**


  * C5: **0.20**


  * C2: **0.15**


  * C6: **0.10**


* * *
## B4) Emotion-as-signal Features (not sentiment-as-vibes)
**F16. Repetition index (RI)**
Same concern repeated without resolution
**F17. Emotional leakage proxy (ELP)**
Sarcasm, irritation markers, excessive exclamation, caps
**F18. Uncertainty language rate (ULR)**
“maybe”, “not sure”, “let’s see”, “unclear”, “depends”
**F19. Relationship repair markers (RRM)**
“appreciate”, “thanks for patience”, “align”, “respect”, “understand”
**Default layer weights (Emotion block)**
  * C2: **0.45**


  * C4: **0.20**


  * C3: **0.20**


  * C1: **0.10**


  * C5: **0.05**


* * *
## B5) Cognitive Load & Complexity Features
**F20. Decision reversal count (DRC)**
Flip-flop on price, structure, timeline
**F21. Redundant question rate (RQR)**
Asks for info already provided
**F22. Topic entropy (TE)**
# distinct topics introduced per 1k chars
**F23. Ambiguity density (AD)**
Undefined nouns: “that”, “this”, “stuff”, “things”; missing referents
**F24. Summary alignment score (SAS)**
When someone summarizes, how often others confirm vs correct
**Default layer weights (Cognition block)**
  * C4: **0.55**


  * C5: **0.20**


  * C2: **0.15**

## B6) Structural / Institutional Features (rules, veto, legal)

**F26. Escalation path defined (EPD)**
Clear dispute resolution and next step routing
**F27. Legal escalation frequency (LEF)**
Mentions counsel, compliance, “legal review”, “policy”

Earn-out, performance gates, caps, warranties, termination clauses

# C) Normalization (v1 exact rules)
All features → z-score vs actor baseline where possible.
**Baseline windows**
  * Actor baseline: last 60–90 days (or first 30 events minimum)


  * Deal baseline: since deal start


**Clipping**
  * z clipped to [-3, +3]


  * then mapped to [0,1] via sigmoid


* * *
# D) Mapping features → C1–C7 scores (v1 equation)
For each layer Ck:
```
    Ck = clamp01( 1 - sigmoid( Σ (w_feature * z_feature) + bias_k ) )
```
  * Higher “risk features” increase z → reduce Ck (worse)


  * Repair markers reduce z (negative weight)


**Default layer biases (v1)**
  * b1 = 0.00


  * b2 = 0.05


  * b3 = 0.10 (assume mild baseline stress)


  * b4 = 0.00


  * b5 = 0.00


  * b6 = 0.00


  * b7 = 0.15 (assume mild external instability)

# F) Threat score weights (v1 exact)
Threat is computed per actor and deal.
**Actor Threat v1**
```
    Threat = 0.40*(1 - C1) + 0.40*(1 - C3) + 0.20*(1 - C6)
```
**Deal Threat v1**
  * max of A ctor Threat across decision + veto stakeholders


  * plus context modifier:


```
    DealThreat = max(Threat_decision_set) + 0.10*(1 - C7_deal)
    clamp to [0,1]
```
* * *
# G) Coherence (v1 exact)
**Actor Coherence**
```
    Coherence = HarmonicMean(C1..C7)
```
**Deal Coherence**
```
    DealCoherence = min(Coherence_i)
```
**Deal Coherence Index (DCI v1 default)**
```
    DCI = Π(C1..C7)   (product)
```
(Use product because it punishes weak links aggressively.)
* * *
# H) Alert thresholds (v1 defaults)
**Hard alerts**
  * `NO_GO`: DealThreat ≥ **0.70** OR (C3 ≤ **0.35** AND C1 ≤ **0.45**)


  * `RISK_SPIKE`: Threat increases ≥ **0.15** within 7 days


  * `WINDOW_OPEN`: Threat decreases ≥ **0.12** and C6 ≥ **0.60**

## 2) Feature Computation Cookbook (Deterministic) — TE, SAS, CFI…
Dưới đây là cách tính **không học máy** , làm được ngay trong v1.
### 2.1 TE — Topic Entropy (F22)
**Mục tiêu:** đo “mỗi tin nhắn mở bao nhiêu hướng”, càng nhiều càng tăng C4 rối.
**Bước tính**
  1. Chuẩn hóa văn bản: bỏ stopwords, tách từ, lemmatize đơn giản (khuyến nghị).


  2. Gán “topic tags” theo keyword dictionary v1 (vd: giá, timeline, pháp lý, kỹ thuật, nhân sự, vận hành, rủi ro, thanh toán…).


  3. Tính phân phối p(topic) trong message.


  4. Shannon entropy:`H = - Σ p_i * ln(p_i)`


  5. Chuẩn hóa 0..1:`TE = clamp01( H / ln(N_topics_max) )`  
Trong v1, đặt `N_topics_max = 8`.


**Quy tắc bổ sung**
  * Nếu message có > 3 câu hỏi khác chủ đề → cộng 0.1 (clip 1.0).


  * Nếu message có “tổng hợp / recap” → trừ 0.1.


* * *
### 2.2 SAS — Summary Alignment Score (F24)
**Mục tiêu:** đo “khi có người tóm tắt, bên kia có xác nhận hay sửa/đổi”.
**Bước tính**
  1. Nhận diện message có tóm tắt bằng các marker: “tóm lại”, “kết lại”, “recap”, “để chốt”, “điểm lại”.


  2. Trong 1–3 message tiếp theo của đối phương, phân loại:
     * **Confirm** : “đúng”, “ok”, “chuẩn”, “thống nhất”, “xác nhận”
     * **Correct** : “không phải”, “chưa đúng”, “ý tôi là”, “điều chỉnh”, “sửa lại”
     * **Ignore** : không phản hồi nội dung tóm tắt


  3. Tính:  
`SAS = (Confirm + 0.3*Ignore - 1.0*Correct) / (Confirm+Correct+Ignore)`


  4. Chuẩn hóa 0..1:  
`SAS01 = clamp01((SAS + 1) / 2)`


**Giải thích vận hành**
  * SAS thấp = C4 rối, C6 chưa chốt chuẩn, dễ trượt sang C5 ma sát.


* * *
### 2.3 CFI — Contracting Friction Index (F29)
**Mục tiêu:** đo ma sát “đang bị pháp lý hóa/đỏ hóa/đứt nhịp”.
**Inputs cần**
  * doc version count


  * redline count (nếu có)


  * turnaround time per cycle


  * number of open clauses


**Bước tính v1**
  1. `cycle_turnaround_ratio = avg_turnaround_last3 / baseline_turnaround`


  2. `redline_density = redlines / pages` (nếu không có pages → /1000 words)


  3. `open_issues_ratio = open_issues / total_clauses_tracked`


  4. Tổng hợp:  
`CFI_raw = 0.45*sigmoid(cycle_turnaround_ratio-1) + 0.35*sigmoid(redline_density - d0) + 0.20*open_issues_ratio`  
Trong đó `d0` = 0.8 redlines/page (mặc định).


**Chuẩn hóa**`CFI = clamp01(CFI_raw)`
* * *
### 2.4 RI — Repetition Index (F16) (deterministic)
Ngoài lexicon, RI cần đo lặp chủ đề.
**Bước tính**
  * Trích “issue keyphrases” (2–5 từ) từ message (TF-IDF đơn giản).


  * So với 7 ngày gần nhất:  
`RI = overlap(issue_phrases_current, issue_phrases_unresolved)`


Nếu issue xuất hiện ≥ 3 lần mà chưa có “resolved marker” (xong/rồi/chốt/đã xử lý) → RI tăng mạnh.
* * *
### 2.5 DRC — Decision Reversal Count (F20)
**Mục tiêu:** đo flip-flop.
**Bước tính**
  * Track các biến: price, scope, timeline, payment, structure, owner.


  * Mỗi lần value đổi ngược trong cửa sổ 14 ngày → +1.


  * Chuẩn hóa:  
`DRC = clamp01(reversals / 3)` (>=3 coi như 1.0)


* * *
### 2.6 VC — Veto Clarity (F25)
**Mục tiêu:** có biết ai có quyền chặn không.
**Bước tính**
  * Nếu có câu xác định veto: “người duyệt cuối”, “cần CFO/Legal ký”, “HĐQT”, “bộ phận X phê duyệt” → VC = 1


  * Nếu “chắc phải hỏi thêm”, “còn phụ thuộc”, “để xin ý kiến” mà không rõ ai → VC = 0.2


  * Nếu hoàn toàn không đề cập → VC = 0.0


* * *
### 2.7 EPD — Escalation Path Defined (F26)
**Mục tiêu:** đường leo thang rõ.
**Bước tính**
  * Có mô tả bước xử lý bất đồng: “nếu không thống nhất thì escalated lên…”, “đi qua bước…”, “SLA phản hồi…”
