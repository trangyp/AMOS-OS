# Falsifiers

- mediator manipulation does not change target under valid conditions
- cause-target relation persists unchanged after mediator removal
- claimed mediator is actually a confounder under stronger evidence

If a falsifier succeeds, downgrade, condition, preserve COMPETING, or return UNKNOWN/GAP.
