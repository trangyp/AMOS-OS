# Entropy, Lacunarity, Repair

These quantities are domain-sensitive. Do not confuse AMOS structural proxies with thermodynamic entropy or formal mathematical lacunarity unless definitions match.

## Source equations
- `E_X = -(1/ln N) Σ_i p_i ln p_i`
- `E_total = w_L E_L + w_M E_M + w_H E_H`
- `Λ_X = Var(Mass)/Mean(Mass)^2`
- `Λ_X = (1/N Σ_i (Z_i-Zbar)^2)/Zbar^2`
- Framework approximation: `Λ_X ≈ 1/(1+e^{-k(E_X-0.5)})`

## Structural persistence
`PV = (BoundaryIntegrity × MemoryContinuity × RepairCapacity × RelationCoherence) / (EntropyLoad × ContradictionDensity × FragmentationPressure × ObserverVariance)`

## Repair rule
Sustained viability requires repair capacity/rate to exceed degradation/entropy accumulation in the chosen model.
