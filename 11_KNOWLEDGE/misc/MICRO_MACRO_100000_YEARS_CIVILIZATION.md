# MICRO↔MACRO MAP: 100,000 Years of Civilization

Structural mapping of civilization across ~100,000 years using the 19×19 system model. Same invariants at micro (biology/individual), meso (firms/cities), and macro (states/empires) scales. Only coupling density (C), time constants (τ), and buffer form (B) change.

---

## 1. Core Claim

"Civilizational change" = same phenomenon repeated at different scales:

- **Micro**: cells, nervous systems, families, teams
- **Meso**: firms, cities, supply chains
- **Macro**: states, empires, global orders

**Invariant**: stable system = clean resource flow + bounded leakage + buffers replenishing faster than shocks.

## 3. The 100,000-Year Arc

### Phase 1 — Forager Bands (pre-agrarian)
- C: low, N: low-medium, D: social cohesion + mobility, B: low stored buffer
- **Failure mode**: localized mortality, not systemic collapse
- **Invariant**: mobility is damping

### Phase 2 — Early Agriculture & Sedentism
- C↑, B↑ (stored grain), but N↑ (disease, raids) and Cor↑ (rent extraction begins)
- **Critical shift**: stored buffer enables hierarchy; hierarchy introduces leakage
- **Failure mode**: famine + extraction → legitimacy collapse

### Phase 3 — Empire Logistics Era (bronze/iron classical empires)
- Key variables: L (logistics friction), E (energy of food systems)
- Stabilize when: Adm↑, Jud/Enf↑, Sk↑
- Destabilize when: H↑, Cor↑, Buf↓, Fx↑
- **Invariant**: empires die when shocks hit depleted buffers + high leakage

### Phase 4 — Commercial/Industrial Acceleration
- E↑↑, Pr↑↑, C↑↑, N↑ (financial cycles, geopolitical entanglement)
- **New fragility**: productivity hides rising systemic risk because C × G grows faster than D
- **Invariant**: when connectivity increases faster than damping, small errors cascade

### Phase 5 — Late Modern / Digital-Global System (today)
- Digitalization: G↑, Inf↑, C↑, often without proportional D↑ or Buf↑
- Result: fast narrative swings, fast capital flow, rapid polarization, brittle trust
- **Invariant**: high gain + high noise requires stronger damping or oscillation/fragmentation

## 4. Civilization Stability Index (CSI)

```latex
CSI = \frac{D + B}{G \cdot N}
```

- CSI > 1: stable growth / manageable volatility
- CSI ≈ 1: fragile; politics becomes "noise management"
- CSI < 1: cascade-prone

**Collapse formula (universal)**:

```latex
CSI < 1 \Rightarrow collapse
```

Typically caused by: B exhausted, D eroded, Cor rising, Inf/Pol rising, C too high for existing D.

## 6. Where "Stage 7" Sits

Stage 7 = re-stabilization via parameter shift:

- Enf/Jud/Adm rebuilt (predictability)
- Cor structurally reduced
- Buf replenished (household and fiscal)
- Inf/Pol damped by architecture (not slogans)
- Connectivity becomes selective (C managed, not maximized)

Historically rare at scale because it requires: admitting hidden leakage, sacrificing short-term extraction, rebuilding trust through delivery.

## 7. The Practical Bridge

Civilizations don't "think." They oscillate under these parameters.
