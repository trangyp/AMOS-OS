# Competing Hypothesis Field

Maintain incompatible claims as COMPETING when evidence is:
equal, incomparable, correlated, insufficient, or regime-dependent.

## Hypothesis record
`H = [id, claim, assumptions, support, contradiction, provenance, scope, regime, falsifiers, decision_relevance]`

## Dominance
Do not collapse on:
authority alone, source count, stylistic certainty, popularity, or repeated descendants.

Collapse only when discriminating evidence establishes epistemic dominance for the required scope.

## Test selection
Choose the cheapest high-information test capable of changing the decision.
