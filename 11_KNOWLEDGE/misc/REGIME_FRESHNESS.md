# Epistemic Regime and Freshness

## Applicability envelope
`A = [system/population, environment, scale, time, regime, measurement_method, assumptions]`

## Freshness tensor
`F = T[claim_id, source_time, observation_time, valid_from, valid_until, environment_epoch, dependency_epoch, last_revalidated, revalidate_after]`

A source may remain historically accurate while becoming decision-invalid under a regime shift.

Regime changes selectively invalidate claims whose applicability envelope no longer matches.
