# Benchmark Firewall

Any performance, superiority, scaling, latency, throughput, repair, or compression claim requires:
`benchmark_id, version, baseline, workload, harness, environment, raw_outputs, statistic, result, scope, limitations, provenance`.

Never:
- generalize beyond the tested scope;
- treat structural properties as measured performance;
- combine incomparable benchmark statistics;
- claim universal superiority from one component-level result.
