# Causal Firewall

Classify each relation as:
association | correlation | enabling condition | mediator | confounder | feedback |
necessary condition | sufficient condition | mechanism | intervention effect.

## Rule
A stronger causal class requires stronger evidence.

Sequence, co-occurrence, analogy, structural resemblance, and predictive success do not by themselves establish mechanism or intervention effect.

## Counterfactual gate
For causal counterfactuals:
- identify intervention variable
- hold relevant background conditions explicit
- separate observed from hypothetical states
- mark unidentifiable counterfactuals UNKNOWN
