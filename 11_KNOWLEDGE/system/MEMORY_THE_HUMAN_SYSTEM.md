## Improved Equations (Precision-Tightened)

**Original → Improved:**

```
BAD:  Humans are not primarily rational creatures
GOOD: The majority of human cognitive processing is automatic, affective, and regulatory
      rather than deliberative and propositional.

BAD:  80-90% of vagal fibers travel Body → Brain
GOOD: Approximately 80% of the ~100,000 vagal fibers are afferent (Body → Brain)
      [Berthoud & Neuhuber, 2000 — anatomical reference; percentage varies by source]

BAD:  Social_Pain = Physical_Pain (same ACC/insula network)
GOOD: Social pain activates overlapping neural substrates in dorsal anterior cingulate
      cortex and anterior insula, as demonstrated by Eisenberger et al. (2003) and
      replicated in subsequent fMRI studies. (Physical pain additionally recruits
      somatosensory and nociceptive pathways that social pain does not.)

BAD:  Engagement ≈ Reward_Uncertainty × Reward_Frequency
GOOD: Engagement increases with the variance of reward prediction error, not its magnitude
      — consistent with variable-ratio reinforcement schedules and dopaminergic phasic
      firing patterns. (Specific functional form unproven.)

BAD:  Manipulation = Control of perception through regulation pathways below conscious awareness
GOOD: Much contemporary digital manipulation operates primarily through regulation pathways
      (threat, reward, status, belonging, arousal) that influence perception and behavior
      below the threshold of conscious deliberation. Beliefs often form afterward as
      post-hoc rationalizations. (Conscious manipulation — propaganda, coercion — exists
      and is not covered by this claim.)

BAD:  Depression = Distinction_Collapse across Future_Space
GOOD: Depression can be structurally understood as a collapse in the capacity to construct
      and commit to a compressed future — consistent with observed deficits in hippocampal
      pattern completion, reduced dopaminergic reward prediction, and vmPFC hyperactivity
      associated with rumination. (Structural interpretation, not DSM/ICD diagnostic model.)
```
