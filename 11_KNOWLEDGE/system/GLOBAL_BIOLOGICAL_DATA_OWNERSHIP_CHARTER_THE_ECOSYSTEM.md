# **Global Biological Data Ownership Charter**
* * *
 _(Applies to all data generated in the Proof-of-Signal Network)_
* * *
## **1 — Ownership Principle**
  * **Data Controller** : [Your Entity Name] retains exclusive ownership of all raw and processed biological data collected via the network.


  * **Master Signal IP** and the **Global Biological Dataset** are proprietary assets of [Your Entity Name], protected under copyright, database rights, and trade secret law.


  * All contributors and participants acknowledge this ownership as a condition of network participation.


* * *
## **2 — Consent & Participation**
  * Data is collected **only with explicit, informed, and revocable user consent**.


  * Consent records are cryptographically bound to each Proof-of-Signal Block (PSB) via blind signature tokens.


  * Participants may opt out of any modality (audio, haptic, visual) or data field without penalty to network access.


* * *
## **3 — Data Classification**
  1. **Consensus Data** — minimum dataset required to validate Proof-of-Signal and reward nodes.


  2. **Extended Reward Data** — optional fields that increase node rewards and network value.


  3. **Vault Data** — rich biometric and contextual data stored exclusively in the Sovereign Data Vault; never shared raw.


* * *
## **4 — Data Processing & Control**
  * **Raw data** remains on participant devices unless explicitly exported.


  * **Anonymized aggregates** may be transmitted to the Sovereign Data Vault for validation, rewards, and research.


  * **No partner** may copy, resell, or use data outside the licensed scope defined in signed agreements.


* * *
## **5 — Access & Licensing**
  * Partners access only **aggregated, anonymized data** under time-bound, field-of-use restricted licenses.


  * Every license includes:
    * **Non-transferability** — no resale or sublicensing without approval.
    * **Usage reporting** — partners must log all queries and provide usage audits.
    * **BTC micropayment integration** for ongoing access.


  * All licenses are recorded on-chain (Bitcoin or compatible sidechain) for transparency.


* * *
## **6 — Provenance & Audit**
  * Every dataset hash and master signal hash is **timestamped on Bitcoin** at creation.


  * This ensures immutable provenance and protects against false claims of origin.


  * Independent validators may audit datasets against on-chain hashes at any time.


* * *
## **7 — Enforcement & Termination**
  * Violation of the Charter or license terms results in:
    * Immediate termination of access.
    * Public revocation notice on-chain.
    * Legal enforcement under governing jurisdiction.


  * Disputes resolved in [Jurisdiction of Choice], with Bitcoin transaction history admissible as evidence.


* * *
## **8 — Perpetuity Clause**
  * This Charter is **irrevocable** unless modified by on-chain governance vote of node operators and data contributors.


  * Modifications require:
    * ≥ 75% approval of active governance keys.
    * 90-day public review period.
    * On-chain publication of new hash.
