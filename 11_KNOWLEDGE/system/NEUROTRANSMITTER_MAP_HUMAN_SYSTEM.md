## Complete Grounding Table

| Neurotransmitter | Synthesis Site | Release Site | Receptor Types | Function |
|------------------|----------------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| **Glutamate** | All neurons | Axon terminals | AMPA, NMDA, Kainate, mGluR | Excitation, learning |
| **GABA** | Interneurons | Axon terminals | GABA-A, GABA-B | Inhibition, regulation |
| **Dopamine** | VTA, SNc, hypothalamus | Mesolimbic, mesocortical, nigrostriatal | D1–D5 | Reward, motivation, movement |
| **Norepinephrine** | Locus coeruleus | Ascending projections | Alpha-1, Alpha-2, Beta-1, Beta-2 | Arousal, attention, vigilance |
| **Serotonin** | Raphe nuclei | Ascending projections | 5-HT1–7 | Mood, appetite, sleep, impulse |
| **Acetylcholine** | Basal forebrain, brainstem | Cholinergic projections | Nicotinic, Muscarinic M1–M5 | Learning, memory, attention |
| **Endorphins** | Hypothalamus, pituitary | Opioidergic projections | Mu, Delta, Kappa | Pain, pleasure, reward |
| **Oxytocin** | Hypothalamus (PVN, SON) | Neurohypophysis, CNS | OXTR | Bonding, trust, social |
| **Vasopressin** | Hypothalamus (PVN, SON) | Neurohypophysis, CNS | V1a, V1b, V2 | Stress, social behavior |
| **Cortisol** | Adrenal cortex (HPA axis) | Systemic circulation | GR, MR | Stress, metabolism, immune |
| **Melatonin** | Pineal gland | Systemic circulation | MT1, MT2 | Sleep, circadian rhythm |

## Key Structural Facts

**Dopamine pathways (three):**

| Pathway | Origin | Target | Function |
|---------|--------|--------|----------|
| Mesolimbic | VTA | Nucleus accumbens | Reward, motivation |
| Mesocortical | VTA | Prefrontal cortex | Motivation, executive function |
| Nigrostriatal | SNc | Striatum | Movement |

**Serotonin receptor families (7):**

```
5-HT1 through 5-HT7
```

Different subtypes have different distributions, signaling pathways, and functions. Not interchangeable.

**GABA receptor types:**

| Receptor | Type | Function |
|----------|------|----------|
| GABA-A | Ionotropic (Cl⁻ channel) | Fast inhibition |
| GABA-B | Metabotropic (G-protein) | Slow, sustained inhibition |

**Glutamate receptor types:**

| Receptor | Type | Function |
|----------|------|----------|
| AMPA | Ionotropic (Na⁺/K⁺) | Fast excitation, synaptic plasticity |
| NMDA | Ionotropic (Ca²⁺/Na⁺) — voltage-dependent | Learning, memory, coincidence detection |
| Kainate | Ionotropic | Modulatory excitation |
| mGluR | Metabotropic (G-protein) | Modulatory, slower glutamate signaling |

**Acetylcholine receptor types:**

| Receptor | Type | Subtypes |
|----------|------|----------|
| Nicotinic | Ionotropic (Na⁺/K⁺/Ca²⁺) | Various subunits — muscle, autonomic, CNS |
| Muscarinic | Metabotropic (G-protein) | M1–M5 — different G-protein coupling |

**Opioid receptor types:**

| Receptor | Endogenous ligand | Primary function |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Mu | β-endorphin, enkephalins | Pain relief, euphoria, reward |
| Delta | Enkephalins | Modulatory, mood |
| Kappa | Dynorphins | Stress, dysphoria, diuresis |

**Stress hormone receptors:**

| Hormone | Receptors | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Cortisol | GR (glucocorticoid receptor), MR (mineralocorticoid receptor) | GR = high-capacity, lower affinity; MR = low-capacity, higher affinity; different genomic effects |
| Vasopressin | V1a, V1b, V2 | V1a = vascular smooth muscle, CNS; V1b = pituitary ACTH release; V2 = kidney water reabsorption |

**Social neuropeptide receptors:**

| Neuropeptide | Receptor | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Oxytocin | OXTR (single receptor type, GPCR) | Distributed in amygdala, hypothalamus, NAc, PFC — social bonding, trust, stress buffering |
| Vasopressin | V1a (CNS) | Social behavior, aggression, pair bonding — species-specific distribution patterns |

**Circadian:**

| Hormone | Receptor | Function |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Melatonin | MT1, MT2 | MT1 = sleep onset (inhibits SCN); MT2 = circadian phase shifting |
