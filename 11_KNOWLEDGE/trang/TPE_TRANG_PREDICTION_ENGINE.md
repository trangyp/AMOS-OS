# The Trang Prediction Engine™ (TPE) — Official Manual

## Purpose

Structural forecasting — anticipate the evolution of human-linked systems. Not statistical, not domain-specific, not historical-precedent dependent. Built on universal forces governing human systems under pressure.

Never predicts: specific individuals | exact dates | assassinations | sudden disasters | individual-level outcomes

---

## Three Output Categories

1. **Class Prediction** — Type of transition (fragmentation events, governance crises, economic downturns, etc.)
2. **Window Prediction** — Time horizon for transition probability (orgs: 1-3y, states: 5-15y, civilizations: 25-80y)
3. **Cascade Prediction** — First-order/second-order consequences across subsystems (e.g., financial crisis → political instability → institutional reform/collapse)

---

## Six Core Inputs (from TSS)

| Input | Meaning | Captures |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Ω | Overload | Stress from complexity, obligations, resource constraints |
| H | Cohesion | Trust, legitimacy, coordination, identity |
| F | Fragmentation | Power divides, silos, factions |
| S | Shocks | Sudden/slow disturbances |
| C | Cycle | Current position in 7-cycle trajectory |
| O | Outcome trajectory | Early indication of likely path |

## Seven Analytical Layers

1. **Load Architecture** — Type of overload (fiscal strain, bottlenecks)
2. **Cohesion Layers** — Subdomains (institutional trust, cultural unity)
3. **Fragmentation Typology** — Form of division (elite splits, identity divides)
4. **Shock Typology** — Classifies shocks (climatic, financial, political, technological)
5. **Structural Velocity** — How fast variables change
6. **System Entanglement** — Cross-system interactions
7. **Resilience Buffers** — Hidden strengths (savings, redundancy, alliances)

---

## Seven-Step Predictive Logic

1. **System State Identification** — Current TSS cycle (C1–C7)
2. **Variable Mapping** — Measure Ω, H, F, S
3. **Structural Drift Detection** — Rising/falling/stable trends (drift > magnitude)
4. **Transition Rule Application**:
   - C3 cannot stabilize indefinitely → must reform, fragment, or crisis
   - High Ω + high F → crisis risk (even without shocks)
   - High H buffers shocks but not extreme overload
   - Fragmentation reversal requires intentional action (no self-correction)
   - Crisis → renewal OR collapse (fork cannot be avoided)
5. **Outcome Boundary Identification** — Which long-term outcomes remain open
6. **Cascade Simulation** — Variable propagation chains
7. **Intervention Sensitivity Analysis**:
   - Reduce overload → simplification, capacity investment, reduce demand
   - Increase cohesion → inclusive governance, legitimacy restoration
   - Reduce fragmentation → conflict resolution, incentive realignment
   - Manage shocks → resilience systems, diversification

---

## Mathematics of Structural Pressure (Qualitative)

- **Overload Pressure**: Ω rising faster than capacity → C3/C4
- **Cohesion Buffer**: H slows crisis transition
- **Fragmentation Multiplier**: F amplifies Ω and S
- **Shock Conversion**: S reveals weaknesses; doesn't cause collapse alone

Civilizational: resource-limits/demographics/competing-states/climate/trans-tech
