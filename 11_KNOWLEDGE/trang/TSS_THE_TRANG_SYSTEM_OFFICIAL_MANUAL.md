# The Trang System™ (TSS) — Official Manual

## Purpose

Provide a unified, science-based model to interpret system behavior across scales — families, organizations, states, civilizations. Identifies structural forces that repeat across all systems shaped by human cooperation, conflict, and decision-making.

## Five Fundamental Questions

1. What stage of development is this system in?
1. What forces are strengthening or weakening it? Is it stabilizing, drifting, or destabilizing?
1. What structural futures are possible or impossible?
1. What actions can meaningfully change its direction?

______________________________________________________________________

## Three Finite Sets

1. **Four structural variables** — internal and external pressures
1. **Seven structural cycles** — universal development path
1. **Four long-term outcomes** — all possible endings

______________________________________________________________________

## The Four Universal Variables

| Variable          | Symbol | Meaning                                          | Impact                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Overload**      | Ω      | Demand vs capacity                               | Rising Ω → overreach (C3) → fragmentation (C4) → crisis (C5) |
| **Cohesion**      | H      | Internal unity: trust, legitimacy, coordination  | High H = stabilizing buffer against shocks                   |
| **Fragmentation** | F      | Internal division: factions, silos, power splits | High F magnifies overload and shocks                         |
| **Shocks**        | S      | Disruptive events: wars, crises, pandemics       | Reveal hidden weaknesses; convert weaknesses to failure      |

**Key insight**: All surface-level events translate into changes in Ω, H, F, or S.

______________________________________________________________________

## The Seven Cycles of System Evolution

| Cycle  | Name             | Structural Pattern                 |
| ------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **C1** | Emergence        | Ω low, H high, F low, S low        |
| **C2** | Expansion        | Ω rising, H strong, F manageable   |
| **C3** | Peak & Overreach | Ω high, H falling, F rising        |
| **C4** | Fragmentation    | Ω high, H low, F high              |
| **C5** | Crisis–Shock     | S high, F high, H unstable         |
| **C6** | Collapse         | Ω unsustainable, F extreme         |
| **C7** | Reset            | Ω reducing, H rising, F decreasing |

**Key**: Order is consistent across history and scale. Duration varies.
