**Version:** 2.0.0+lens_integration
**Source:** `AMOS_Engineering_Math_Kernel_v0.json`

The **Engineering Math Kernel** handles control theory, signal processing, optimization, and simulation models. It applies rigorous mathematical structure to engineering problems.

## Domain Clusters
The engine comprises 20 clusters spanning applied mathematics:
- Linear Algebra for Systems
- Calculus and Differential Equations
- Numerical Methods
- Optimization (Convex / Nonconvex)
- Control Theory (State Space, Feedback, Robust, Nonlinear)
- Signal Processing (Time/Frequency domain, Filter Design)
- Stochastic Processes, Estimation & Kalman Filters
- PDEs, Finite Element / Difference
- Simulation & Monte Carlo
- Queueing Theory & Reliability Engineering

## The 20 Dimensions
Models are evaluated against rigorous constraints:
- Model Fidelity vs Computational Cost
- Stability, Convergence Rate, and Robustness
- Implementation Complexity and Analytic Tractability
- Error Propagation and Numerical Accuracy
- Safety and Control Margins
- Real-time Feasibility
