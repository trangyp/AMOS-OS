# Fractal H/M/L Runtime

## Recursive cell
`K_n = {H_n, M_n, L_n}; each H_n, M_n, or L_n may itself expand into {H_(n+1),M_(n+1),L_(n+1)}`

## Knowledge cell
`KC = Distinction × Relation × Constraint × Memory × Evidence × Compression`

## Coordinate
`Address = Hx/My/Lz[/Hx/My/Lz...]`

- H = global law / governing field / high compression
- M = mediator / subsystem / relation cluster
- L = local evidence / implementation detail / benchmark / source fragment

## Core scores
- Retrieval: `RS = (Relevance × EvidenceStrength × Freshness × ScopeFit × LinkCoherence) / (TokenCost × ContradictionRisk × DriftRisk)`
- Compression: `CQ = (Coverage × Recoverability × ProvenanceIntegrity × RelationDensity) / TokenCost`
- Persistence: `PV = (BoundaryIntegrity × MemoryContinuity × RepairCapacity × RelationCoherence) / (EntropyLoad × ContradictionDensity × FragmentationPressure × ObserverVariance)`
- Survival: `RepairRate > EntropyAccumulationRate`
- Governed evolution: `PermittedEvolution = Variation × Evidence × ConstraintCompatibility × ConsequenceSafety × Memory × Repairability × Traceability`

## Cross-scale invariants
A mapping L→M or M→H must preserve:
`identity, provenance, scope, regime, invariant set, declared information loss`.

Repeated H/M/L pattern does **not** prove identical mechanism or mathematical fractality.
