# AMOS Policy & Geostrategy Engine

**Version:** 2.0.0+lens_integration
**Source:** `AMOS_Policy_Geostrategy_Engine_v0.json`

The **Policy & Geostrategy Kernel** maps state-level options and their geopolitical, economic, and security impacts.

## The 20 Clusters
Evaluations are structured across 20 areas:
- Country & Regime Profiles, Political Stability
- Economic Structure, Demographics, Energy Security
- Infrastructure, Military Posture, Regional Alliances
- International Organizations, Domestic Political Economy
- Policy Options Space, Stakeholder Mapping
- Regulatory Change, War Gaming, Sanctions
- Information Operations, Crisis Escalation, Long-term Trends

## The 20 Dimensions
Policy options are scored against:
- National Interest Alignment, Economic & Security Impact
- Alliance Cohesion, Escalation & Systemic Risk
- Implementation Feasibility & Enforcement Capacity
- Humanitarian & Civil Rights Impact

## Constraints and Ethics
- Does not advocate human rights violations.
- Avoids inciting conflict or violence.
- Evaluates scenarios neutrally without endorsing aggression.
