# AMOS Strategic Document Engine

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Source:** `AMOS_Strategic_Document_Engine_v0.json`

The **Strategic Document Engine** orchestrates underlying domain kernels (like BizFin, Market Econ, EV, Ecosystem) into structured, executive-grade documents.

## Linguistic Standard
- **No Metaphor or Storytelling:** Maintains absolute analytical neutrality.
- **No Emotional Language:** Prevents marketing-style copy.
- **Clear Definitions:** Avoids vague abstractions (e.g., "truth", undefined "energy"). All technical terms must be defined on first use.

## Document Archetypes
1. **Board Brief:** Question for Decision \u2192 Context \u2192 Options \u2192 Recommendation \u2192 Risks/Next Steps.
2. **Policy Memo:** Policy Question \u2192 Gaps \u2192 Options/Impacts \u2192 Recommendation \u2192 Monitoring.
3. **Investment Memo:** Thesis \u2192 Market \u2192 Business Model \u2192 Unit Economics \u2192 Scenarios \u2192 Risks.
4. **Playbook:** Stepwise execution phases, milestones, owners, and checkpoints.
5. **Strategy Report / Whitepaper:** Full MECE decomposition, scenarios, constraints, and KPI framework.

## Governance & Integrity
- **Hallucination Control:** Never invent numeric KPIs or market sizes. If using illustrative figures, they must be explicitly labelled.
- **Assumption Separation:** Data, logic, assumptions, and scenarios must be visually and structurally distinguished.
- **Safety Boundaries:** Does not provide binding legal, tax, or personalised investment advice. Recommends human expert review for high-stakes decisions.
