# Neuromorphic Spiking Transformer (Spikformer) & Direct Spike Attention Ledger

## 1. Mathematical Architecture & Spike-Driven Self-Attention (SDSA)

The Spikformer architecture eliminates energy-expensive floating-point multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations in standard Transformers by replacing softmax self-attention with Spike-Driven Self-Attention (SDSA).

### Binary Spike Thresholding & Dynamics
Membrane potentials $U(t)$ follow Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) dynamics with surrogate gradient $\sigma'(U)$:
$$S[t] = \Theta(U[t] - V_{\text{th}}), \quad U[t] = \tau U[t-1] + X[t] - V_{\text{th}} S[t-1]$$

### Floating-Point-Free Attention Kernel
With discrete spike tensors $\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V} \in \{0, 1\}^{N \times d}$:
$$\text{SDSA}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}) = \text{SN}\left( \frac{\mathbf{Q} \mathbf{K}^\top}{\sqrt{d}} \right) \mathbf{V}$$
where $\mathbf{Q} \mathbf{K}^\top$ and the projection are computed entirely through sparse integer additions (ACs), bypassing power-hungry matrix multiplier hardware.

## Spikformer Spike Attention Dynamics

The Spikformer replaces continuous-valued attention with Spike-Driven Self-Attention (SDSA), where queries, keys, and values are binary spike tensors $\{0, 1\}^{N \times d}$ generated by Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neurons. The LIF membrane potential integrates input current with exponential decay governed by time constant $\tau$, firing when it exceeds threshold $V_{\text{th}}$. The surrogate gradient $\sigma'(U)$ approximates the non-differentiable Heaviside step function $\Theta$ during backward propagation, typically using the atan or fast-sigmoid surrogate.

The attention matrix $\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top$ is computed entirely through integer additions (accumulators) rather than floating-point multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations. Since $\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K} \in \{0, 1\}$, each entry of $\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top$ is simply a count of co-active spike positions — a popcount-like operation implementable on neuromorphic hardware (Loihi, SpiNNaker) with orders-of-magnitude lower energy. The spike normalization (SN) operation replaces softmax with a spike-based divisive normalization, preserving the relative scaling without floating-point division.

The 46x energy reduction stems from eliminating MAC operations in both the attention computation and the value projection. However, the binary representation limits information per token-channel to 1 bit per timestep, requiring temporal coding (multiple timesteps) to achieve accuracy comparable to dense ANN transformers. This introduces a latency-accuracy tradeoff: more timesteps improve expressivity but increase inference time and total energy.

## Epistemic Boundary

- `MODEL != OBSERVATION` — the 46x energy gain is measured on CMOS 45nm simulation; real neuromorphic hardware (Loihi 2, SpiNNaker 2) may show different ratios due to routing overhead and memory access patterns.
- `DOCUMENTED != IMPLEMENTED` — surrogate gradients enable training but introduce bias relative to true gradients; the approximation quality depends on the surrogate function and membrane potential distribution.
- Binary spike coding compresses information to 1 bit per channel per timestep; accuracy parity with ANN transformers requires multi-timestep temporal coding whose optimal duration is task-dependent and not theoretically bounded.
- Synaptic sparsity (71.2%) indicates most spike intervals are silent; this benefits energy but may indicate underutilization of the attention mechanism for certain input distributions.
