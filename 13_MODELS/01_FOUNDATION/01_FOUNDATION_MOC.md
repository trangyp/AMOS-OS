# 01 Foundation Models — MOC

## Purpose

The Foundation Models sub-plane of `13_MODELS` governs the canonical AMOS foundation model artifacts: reality architecture models, biological computing models, omniverse brain specifications, and universal field architectures. These models define the structural and semantic substrate upon which AMOS cognitive, reasoning, and execution layers are built.

> **Epistemic boundary:** `AMOS_MODEL` / `DOCUMENTED != IMPLEMENTED`. These models are architectural specifications. Their executable closure is `UNKNOWN/GAP` unless independently validated.
