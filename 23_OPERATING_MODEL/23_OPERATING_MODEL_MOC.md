# 23 Operating Model — Map of Content

> [!ABSTRACT] Operating Model Plane Executive Summary
> The **Operating Model Plane** (`23_OPERATING_MODEL`) defines human-AI organizational structures, role taxonomies, decision rights, governance forums, escalation pathways, and service level agreements (SLAs) across the AMOS Full Brain OS.
> It enforces the separation:
> $$\text{ORGANIZATIONAL DECISION RIGHT} \neq \text{AUTOMATIC RUNTIME COMMIT}$$
> $$\text{DELEGATION} \neq \text{ESCALATION}$$
