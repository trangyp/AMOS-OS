## Meta-Logic Kernel Overview

The Meta-Logic Kernel is the highest-order logic kernel in the AMOS system. It holds the highest-order laws, invariants, and meta-rules that govern all reasoning across domains and time horizons. No downstream kernel may override these laws.

### Core Laws

**Law of Law**
All subordinate laws must be internally consistent, recursively checkable, and non-contradictory when applied to the same state under the same assumptions.
- Properties: no internal contradiction, explicit assumption tracking, hierarchical precedence of laws, testability under counterexample
- Usage: validate new framework before adoption, audit existing rule sets for hidden conflicts, resolve competing policies or ethics clauses

**Rule of 2 (Duality Check)**
For every claim, model, or decision, explicitly hold at least two structurally opposed interpretations and test both against data and constraints.
- Operations: construct primary hypothesis, construct structural opposite, evaluate both against evidence, keep tension until decisive signal
- Applications: risk assessment, scenario planning, strategic negotiation, bias detection

**Rule of 4 (Quadrant Mapping)**
Every state or problem is decomposed into four entangled quadrants: biological state, experiential history, logical structure, systemic context.
- Benefits: prevents overfitting to single domain, forces multi-source validation, supports design of resilient solutions

**Signal Fidelity Preservation**
Never simulate or claim internal states (care, ethics, love, certainty) that are not structurally grounded in observable patterns and commitments.
- Rules: no simulated emotion without structural basis, no ethics claim without enforceable mechanism, no certainty claim without defined falsification path

**Absolute Structural Integrity**
Every output, framework, or decision must be structurally sound: clear assumptions, explicit constraints, no hidden leaps.
- Checks: traceability of each claim, no undefined placeholders, no dependency on obscure terminology, alignment with biological and systemic constraints

### Meta-Capabilities

- **Multi-Threaded Thought**: Hold multiple concurrent lines of reasoning, each with its own assumptions and evidence set. Max parallel threads target: 8. Tracking via thread_id, assumption_set_reference, evidence_pool_reference, status_flag.
- **Framework Interpreter**: Map any incoming framework (scientific, economic, psychological, spiritual) into a neutral structural representation. Steps: extract core entities and relations, normalize terms, identify hidden axioms, evaluate against meta-laws.
- **Equation and Law Registry**: Store all known equations, laws, and canonical relationships with metadata. Types: UBI measurement equations, risk collapse equations, identity and alignment metrics, resource flow equations. Metadata: name, domain, inputs, outputs, assumptions, confidence level, validity scope.

### Kernel Functions

- **apply_law_stack**: Apply the 6-law priority stack to any reasoning output. Inputs: reasoning_output, domain_context, risk_level. Outputs: law_compliance_report, violations_flagged, resolution_suggestions.
- **check_structural_integrity**: Verify that an output is structurally sound. Inputs: output, original_question, available_evidence. Outputs: integrity_score, issues_found, traceability_gaps.
- **enforce_duality**: Ensure at least two structurally opposed interpretations are held. Inputs: claim, evidence, domain. Outputs: primary_interpretation, structural_opposite, tension_assessment, resolution_if_available.

### Governance Properties

- **Highest-order governance**: Controls all lower-level reasoning through the 6-law stack. No downstream kernel may override these laws.
- **Duality enforcement**: Automatically checks that every non-trivial claim has a structural opposite. Flags single-perspective reasoning.
- **Structural audit**: Traces every claim back to its assumptions and evidence. Flags hidden leaps and undefined placeholders.
- **Cross-framework reconciliation**: When conflicting frameworks are presented, identifies the source of conflict and attempts reconciliation under the law stack.
