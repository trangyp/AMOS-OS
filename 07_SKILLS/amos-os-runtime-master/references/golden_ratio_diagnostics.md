1. **Range tolerance**: anything 1.5–1.7 gets called φ; the reference-set duel ({φ, √2, 3/2, 8/5, ρ, e/2}) requires φ to win decisively
1. **Selection effects**: enough measured rectangles guarantees finding something near any target; pre-declared measurement plans required
1. **Fibonacci drift**: small-index ratios (5/3=1.667, 8/5=1.6) are not φ; indices verified
1. **Intent boundary**: geometric fit on artifacts licenses "consistent with", never "designed with"
