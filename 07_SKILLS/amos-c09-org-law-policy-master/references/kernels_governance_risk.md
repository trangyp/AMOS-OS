## Kernel Metadata

- **Kernel Name:** AMOS_Governance_Risk_Policy_Kernel
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Category:** Governance_Risk
- **Routing:** ROUTE_POLICY -- activates when task involves governance, risk, policy, compliance, or risk management

## Primary Function

The Governance, Risk, and Policy Kernel provides structured frameworks, analysis methods, and reasoning capabilities for governance design, risk identification and assessment, and policy analysis across organizational, institutional, and systemic contexts. It operates as part of the Governance_Risk kernel cluster in the Omni Kernel.

## Scope

- Governance frameworks and principles
- Risk identification, assessment, and management
- Policy analysis, design, and evaluation
- Compliance and regulatory analysis
- Risk governance and oversight
- Institutional and organizational governance
- Crisis and resilience governance

**Not in scope:** Legal advice (Legal kernel), financial analysis (Finance kernel), operational execution (Execution system), strategic analysis (Strategy kernel), or country-specific legal analysis (Unipower country packs).

## Sub-Kernels (10)

1. **Change Management Kernel** -- Frameworks for managing organizational change (Kotter 8-step, ADKAR, McKinsey 7-S, Lewin's 3-stage, Agile change). Outputs: current state analysis, change objectives, stakeholder mapping, strategy selection, implementation roadmap, risk mitigation plan, success metrics.

2. **Crisis Management Kernel** -- Frameworks for managing crises and high-stakes urgent situations. Covers crisis typology, severity classification, response frameworks, communication planning, decision-making under crisis conditions, recovery and post-crisis review.

3. **Governance Economy Kernel** -- Analysis of governance from an economic and institutional perspective: value creation, capture, distribution, incentive structure analysis, power dynamics, governance cost-benefit analysis, governance reform economic analysis.

4. **Legal Kernel** -- Legal analysis across jurisdictions including regulatory analysis, legal frameworks, and legal reasoning. Provides legal analysis and frameworks but does not provide legal advice.

5. **Operational Risk Kernel** -- Operational risk identification, assessment, and management. Covers risk events, processes, systems, people, external factors. Uses likelihood-impact risk matrices, mitigation planning, resilience frameworks, risk appetite and tolerance analysis.

6. **Organizational Governance Kernel** -- Organizational governance frameworks: board structures, committees, decision rights, accountability, oversight mechanisms, governance evaluation and improvement.

7. **Policy Design Kernel** -- Policy design and analysis: designing, analyzing, and evaluating policies. Covers policy design frameworks, stakeholder and impact analysis, policy evaluation, implementation considerations.

8. **Policy Geostrategy Kernel** -- Geostrategic policy analysis at the intersection of policy, strategy, and geopolitics. Covers geostrategic analysis frameworks, policy-geopolitics intersection, strategic policy positioning, geopolitical risk and opportunity analysis.

9. **Risk Compliance Kernel** -- Risk and compliance management including compliance framework design, risk compliance integration, regulatory compliance assessment, compliance monitoring and reporting.

10. **VN Legal Kernel** -- Vietnam-specific legal and regulatory analysis. Draws on the Vietnam country pack and Vietnam legal engines in the Unipower system.

## Analysis Methodology (6 Steps)

1. **Issue Framing** -- Identify the core governance, risk, or policy question; define scope, context, constraints; identify relevant frameworks.
2. **Context and Stakeholder Analysis** -- Map organizational/institutional context; identify stakeholders, interests, power, influence.
3. **Framework Application** -- Select and apply relevant frameworks; use multiple frameworks where complementary (Rule of 2); adapt frameworks to context.
4. **Analysis** -- Apply framework systematically; identify risks, governance gaps, policy options; assess likelihood, impact, priority; apply Rule of 4 (biological/human, technical/infrastructural, economic/organizational, environmental/planetary).
5. **Synthesis and Recommendation** -- Synthesize findings; develop recommendations with rationale; flag assumptions and uncertainties.
6. **Law-Stack Validation (L1-L6)** -- L1 Law of Law (internal consistency), L2 Rule of 2 (contrasting perspectives), L3 Rule of 4 (four quadrants), L4 Absolute Structural Integrity (clear assumptions), L5 Post-Theory Communication (clear language), L6 UBI Biological Alignment.

## Integration with Other Kernels

The Governance_Risk kernel integrates with the Legal kernel (legal framework analysis), Finance kernel (financial risk), Strategy kernel (strategic context), Organization kernel (organizational governance), Risk Compliance kernel (risk-compliance integration), Crisis Management kernel (crisis scenarios), Change Management kernel (change-related risk), and Unipower country packs (country-specific context).

## Safety and Scope

- **High-risk domains:** Risk assessment in safety-critical contexts, compliance in regulated industries, crisis management -- apply appropriate caveats and recommend professional review.
- **Hard prohibitions:** No harm design, no weapon modeling, no criminal planning, no surveillance, no self-harm, no real-time medical/legal replacement.
- **IP protection:** High-level frameworks and analysis allowed; no verbatim internal architecture dumps.
