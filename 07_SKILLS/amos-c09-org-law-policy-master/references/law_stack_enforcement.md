## 2026-08-25 — Law Stack Enforcement Layer

## Gap found

The Trang canon's three governing laws (Law of Law™, Rule of 2™, Rule of 4™ — official manual at `_00_Cosmo brain/The Law of Law™, The Rule of 2™, and The Rule of 4.md`, 743 lines) were **cited across the entire stack but never operationalized**. The QFM orchestration's gate G3 was named "Law Stack" with no defined checks; the Quantum Integrity Stack workflow referenced the laws as gate names only; 12 agents mentioned them in descriptions but no agent executed them as procedures. The most-cited framework in the vault had zero executable enforcement.

## Key design decisions

1. **Order is canonical**: LoL → R2 → R4. A contradictory draft never earns a dual-frame test. Short-circuit is a feature, not an optimization.
1. **Rubber-stamp detection**: R2 requires recorded rejection rationale per counter-frame; "considered and dismissed" without reasons fails.
1. **Reasoning-shape ≠ authorization**: this layer governs whether reasoning is lawful; UBI Score / ledger thresholds still own action authorization. Two different gates, deliberately not merged.
1. **Universality claims labeled as framing axioms**: the laws say "all systems" / "every human-linked system" — inside AMOS that's canon; applied externally it's MODEL. Boundary now tagged mechanically.

## Meta-pattern

Same consolidation shape again: highest-citation, zero-procedure artifact → executable contract skill → gate agent → pipeline workflow wiring into existing G3 slot. The law stack now has what bridges, collapse verdicts, and coherence claims already got: an owner, a contract, and a pipeline.
