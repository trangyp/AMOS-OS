> **Core Engine**: Tech Quantum Engine
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-tech-quantum-engine-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The Tech Quantum Engine extends technical reasoning into the domain of quantum logic, superposition, and probability matrices. It provides the architectural primitives for systems that must handle extreme parallel complexity or non-binary states.

### Key Components

#### 1. Quantum State Management

- Defines protocols for handling superposition states (where variables hold multiple potential values simultaneously until observed/collapsed).
- Maps the entanglement of subsystems where the state of one component instantaneously dictates the state of another.

#### 2. Probability Matrices & Collapse Rules

- Replaces standard deterministic boolean logic with probability distributions.
- Defines the exact conditions under which a quantum state "collapses" into a definitive outcome.

#### 3. Quantum Architecture Primitives

- Modifies the standard 18 technical primitives to account for non-locality and wave-function interference.

## Integration & Output

This model is typically invoked as a specialized extension of the `amos-total-technical-engine-layer`. It is used by advanced technical agents designing systems that emulate quantum behavior (e.g., highly complex AI reasoning pathways, non-deterministic routing algorithms) or actual quantum computing interfaces.
