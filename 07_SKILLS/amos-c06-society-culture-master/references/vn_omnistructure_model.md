# AMOS Vietnam Omnistructure Model

> **Core Engine**: VN Omnistructure
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-vn-omnistructure-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The Vietnam Omnistructure is the absolute structural mapping of the Vietnamese socio-political, economic, geographical, and logistical landscape. It serves as the baseline reality onto which business models, legal frameworks, and mobility systems are overlaid.

### Key Components

#### 1. Geographic & Demographic Layers
- High-resolution mapping of provincial divisions, major urban centers (Hanoi, HCMC, Da Nang), and strategic economic zones.
- Population density patterns, workforce distribution, and urbanization trajectories.

#### 2. Socio-Political Structure
- The structure of governance, ranging from the Central Committee and Ministries to provincial people's committees.
- Formal and informal power centers, regulatory jurisdictions, and decision-making velocities.

#### 3. Economic & Infrastructure Corridors
- Major logistical arteries (North-South Expressway, key ports, rail links).
- Industrial park locations, energy grid capacities, and digital infrastructure penetration.

## Integration & Output
This model is the foundational bedrock for any AMOS agent operating within the Vietnamese context. Agents using the `amos-vietnam-engine-layer` (Legal) or `amos-grand-cannon-mobility-layer` (Mobility) must inherit the physical and political constraints defined in the VN Omnistructure to prevent hallucinating impossible or culturally ignorant strategies.
