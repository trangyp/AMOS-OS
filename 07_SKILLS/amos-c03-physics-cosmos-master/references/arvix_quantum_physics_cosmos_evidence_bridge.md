## 2. Core Scientific Lineages & Arvix Grounding

### Lineage A: Mesoscopic Quantum Coherence & Condensed Matter Transport (2007–2010)
- **AAK Decoherence & Magnetic Oscillations:** arXiv:0704.0742 (*Magnetoconductance oscillations in metallic rings and decoherence due to electron-electron interactions*). Establishes thermal dephasing limits in low-dimensional mesoscopic rings.
- **Shot-Noise & Non-Fermi Liquid Probes:** arXiv:0704.1427 (*Photo-assisted shot noise in Coulomb interacting systems*) and arXiv:0704.3892 (*Current Noise in Quantum Point Contacts*). Diagnostic noise spectroscopy resolving the 0.7 conductance anomaly.
- **Fractional Charge & Edge Dephasing:** arXiv:0704.1443 (*Dephasing due to a fluctuating fractional quantum Hall edge current*). Phase relaxation mediated by chiral Luttinger liquids.
- **Molecular Scale Ab Initio Transport:** arXiv:0709.2787 (*Quantum Transport in Metalporphyrins: An ab initio Green's function approach*). Non-equilibrium Green's function (NEGF) formalisms bridging molecular biology and quantum conduction.
- **Synthesis:** [[Arvix/outputs/Quantum_Physics_Early_Thread_2007-2010|Quantum Physics Early Thread (2007–2010)]].

- **Quantum Machine Learning & Advantage Skepticism:** arXiv:2409.04406 (*Quantum Kernel Methods under Scrutiny: A Benchmarking Study*). The foundational epistemic firewall: `PROOF != ADVANTAGE`. Rigorous benchmarks demonstrating that classical Gaussian/RBF kernels frequently match or exceed NISQ quantum kernels without exponential circuit depth costs.

## 3. Epistemic Firewalls for AMOS C03

1. **Analogy ≠ Identity:** Quantum coherence models in cognitive architectures (e.g. state vectors, superposition, projection operators) are **`AMOS_MODEL`** heuristic representations, NOT quantum physical assertions regarding warm, wet biological brain tissue.
2. **Proof ≠ Advantage:** Demonstrating a mathematical isomorphism to a quantum algorithm does not prove computational advantage over optimized classical baselines.
3. **Model-Consistency ≠ Historical Reality:** A cosmological or quantum cosmological model matching a single metric does not prove uniqueness of the physical mechanism.
