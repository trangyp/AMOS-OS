## Purpose

This redirect anchors the AMOS C09 Org, Law and Policy master skill, the root authority for governance design, authority structures, compliance, regulatory frameworks, constitutional governance, legal reasoning, and policy analysis. It routes all org-law-policy queries to the canonical SKILL.md and its 29 consolidated sub-skills.

## Domain Coverage

1. Organization structure design: legal entities, reporting structure, operating model, decision rights
2. Decision rights framework: five questions for every recurring decision area
3. Controls, risk, and compliance: preventive/detective/corrective across strategic/tactical/operational
4. Legal reasoning primitives: PERSON, ORGANISATION, STATE, ASSET, CONTRACT, OBLIGATION, RIGHT
5. Jurisdiction mapping: connecting factors, mandatory overrides, unresolved overlaps
6. Compliance reasoning: activity-based scope, policy gap analysis, paper vs effective program
7. Succession architecture: structural property evaluation, successor type classification
