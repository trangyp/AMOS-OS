# Making Planetary Consent Infrastructure Relatable

## 4 Relatable Anecdotes

### 1. The Smart Home That Actually Listens
Sarah's devices collect family data → currently shared with dozens of companies without asking → solution: every device asks "Is it okay if we use this to help optimize energy use?" → you say yes/no, choice remembered across systems

### 2. The Coffee That Knows Its Story
Maria wants ethically-sourced coffee → supply chains too complex for "fair trade" labels → solution: coffee cup tells the story — farmer consented to fair wages, transport confirmed environmental standards, roaster verified transparent practices → every step has community consent

### 3. The City That Asks Before It Decides
City wants traffic sensors → usually installed without asking residents → solution: "Your city wants to install smart traffic systems. Are you okay with anonymous traffic data? Yes/No/Yes-but-only-for-traffic." → system built only if neighbors consent

### 4. The Forest That Has a Voice
Logging company wants timber → no ecosystem input → solution: forest speaks through environmental sensors + community reps → "Does this forest consent to this harvesting?" → based on biodiversity, soil health, community input → logging only happens with ecosystem + community consent

## Common Q&A

| Q | A |
|---|----|
| "Won't constant permission be annoying?" | Opposite — one system learns your preferences, applies automatically. No 50 pop-ups. |
