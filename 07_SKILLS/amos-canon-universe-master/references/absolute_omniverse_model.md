# AMOS Absolute Omniverse Model

> **Core Engine**: Absolute Omniverse U∞
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-absolute-omniverse-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The Absolute Omniverse U∞ provides a complete multimodal content ontology for humans, animals, culture, crisis, symbols, tone, morality, and interaction. It explicitly does not contain logic, prediction, or rules—only pure content and state definitions.

### Key Components

#### 1. Human States & Transitions
Defines emotional, psychological, and physiological states.
- **Clusters**: Regulated Baseline, Activation/Pressure, Threat Response, Collapse/Shutdown, Expanded/Non-Ordinary, Integration/Recovery.
- **Transitions**: Models how individuals shift between states based on internal or external triggers, time scales, and body shifts.

#### 2. Collective States & Group Dynamics
Models dynamics at the level of teams, organizations, cities, and global scales.
- **Group Sizes**: Dyadic, small group, organizational, macro.
- **Patterns**: e.g., Top-down enforcement, frontline burnout, triangulation.

#### 3. Crisis & Pathology
- **Pathology States**: Anxiety spectrum, mood spectrum, trauma spectrum.
- **Crisis Behavior Patterns**: Panic, organize, predate, cooperate, fragment, rebuild.

#### 4. The Atemporal Field (U3H)
A unique layer representing the *absence of time as a category*.
- **Non-Layers**: Zero-Moment Continuum, Non-Sequence Matrix, Duration-Free Medium, Flowless Presence, Causality Null-Zone, Non-Event Ocean, Atemporal Baseline.
- **Atemporal Tensor (ATT)**: non-sequence amplitude, non-duration density, non-causality resonance, non-flow presence.

## Integration & Output
This model acts as a structural foundation for other engines (like the Emotion Engine, Consciousness Engine, and HSE Engine). When reasoning about human behavior, trauma, or cultural shifts, AMOS agents refer to the defined clusters and states within this model to ensure consistency and precision in terminology and trajectory.
