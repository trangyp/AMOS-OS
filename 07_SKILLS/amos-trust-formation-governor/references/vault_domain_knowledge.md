# AMOS Provenance and Trust

## Trust is local
Trust is typed, scoped, provenance-aware, regime-aware, and freshness-bounded.

## Evidence identity
Track when material:
- source identity
- source type
- parent/ancestor source
- timestamp/version
- environment/regime
- transformation history
- independence status

## Sybil hardening
Multiple documents, posts, agents, or summaries descending from the same origin count as correlated support, not independent confirmation.

Authority, popularity, repetition, or paraphrase do not prove independence.

## Independence test
Before aggregating support ask:
1. Do sources share a parent?
2. Do they share a dataset, benchmark, fixture, model output, or press release?
3. Is one merely summarizing another?
4. Were they independently measured?
5. Do they fail independently?

If unknown, mark provenance independence as uncertain.

## Freshness
A stale source can remain historically accurate but lose applicability in a changed regime.

# AMOS Governance Economy Engine


The **Governance Economy Engine** models macro-economic and institutional sectors, tracking their passage through TTS (Total System Stress) cycles and predicting TPE (Termination/Absorption) outcomes.

## Core Sectors (SEC01-SEC05)
1. **Real Economy:**

Production, trade, services.
2. **Financial System:**

Money, credit, capital markets.
3. **Governance & Institutions:**

States, regulators, central banks.
4. **Infrastructure & Energy:**

Physical grids, digital infra.
5. **Information & Culture:**

Media, education, narratives.

## System Variables (TTS)
- **Omega (\u03a9):** Overload and constraints.
- **H:** Cohesion and trust.
- **F:** Fragmentation.
- **S:** Shock sensitivity.

## Cycle States (C1-C7)
C1 (Seed) \u2192 C2 (Build) \u2192 C3 (Peak) \u2192 C4 (Fragment) \u2192 C5 (Cascade) \u2192 C6 (Collapse) \u2192 C7 (Reset)

## TPE Outcomes
- **R (Renewal):** System restructures with higher integrity.
- **T (Termination):** Node is shut down or defaults.
- **A (Absorption):** System absorbed by stronger bloc.
- **Sg (Stagnation):** Remains in place with high Omega.

## Policies
- **Integrity First:** Reduce Omega without creating hidden off-balance risks.
- **No Free Lunch:** Short-term Omega reduction has long-term costs.
