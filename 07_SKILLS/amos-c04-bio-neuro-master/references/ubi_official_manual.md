# Unified Biological Intelligence™ (UBI) — Official Manual

## Purpose

1. Describe intelligence as a **biological system**, not an abstract concept
2. Identify the **four domains** that determine perception, thought, feeling, movement, and coordination
3. Provide a basis for **measurement, governance, training, and system design** across education, health, organizations, and AI-human interaction

## The Four Domains

| Domain | Core Function | Biological Basis | What It Influences |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Neurobiological Intelligence™ (NBI)** | Thought, perception, cognition | Brain, CNS networks, cortical processing | Reasoning, memory, decision loops |
| **Neuroemotional Intelligence™ (NEI)** | Emotional regulation, interpersonal interpretation | Limbic system, affect networks, autonomic pathways | Stress response, empathy, interpersonal accuracy |
| **Somatic Intelligence™ (SI)** | Alignment, proprioception, embodied stability | Fascia, posture, biomechanics | Action, resilience, coordination, movement-based judgment |
| **Bioelectromagnetic Intelligence™ (BEI)** | Rhythmic regulation and synchrony | Cardiac rhythms, neural oscillations, EM signaling | Timing, coherence, systemic sensitivity |

Each domain is necessary. None can fully compensate for the loss of another.

---

## Domain Details

### NBI — Neurobiological Intelligence™
Governs perception, pattern recognition, memory, decision-making. Measurable through cognitive assessments, neuroimaging, reaction-time tests, sensory processing evaluations.

### NEI — Neuroemotional Intelligence™
Governs emotional awareness, regulation, interpersonal interpretation. Enables distinction between instinctive reaction and intentional response. Biological markers: HRV, limbic response patterns, autonomic balance, hormonal regulation.

### SI — Somatic Intelligence™
Body's physical architecture as a source of information and stability. Includes posture, fascia, breath patterns, proprioception, movement quality, muscular symmetry. When misaligned, cognitive and emotional processes degrade.

### BEI — Bioelectromagnetic Intelligence™
Governs timing, rhythm, synchrony across biological systems. Heart and brain generate EM fields that coordinate cell signaling and regulate systemic rhythms. Measurable through ECG, EEG, magnetoencephalography, frequency-domain analysis.

```
i = f(NBI, NEI, SI, BEI)
```

## Measurement

- **NBI**: cognitive tests, reaction time, brain imaging
- **NEI**: HRV, affect scales, emotional recognition tasks
- **SI**: posture, gait analysis, biomechanics, breath function
- **BEI**: EEG, ECG, oscillatory patterns

Every domain can be operationalized in institutions.
