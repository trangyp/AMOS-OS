## 2. Theoretical Architecture: Two Schools of Thought

From the synthesis in [[Arvix/outputs/Quantum_Finance_Map_of_Content|Quantum Finance Map of Content]], two distinct paradigms operate in modern quantitative finance:

### School 1: The Quantum Computing School (Algorithmic Speedup)
Focuses on using quantum hardware primitives to accelerate classical financial bottlenecks:
- **Anchor Review:** arXiv:2604.08180 (*Quantum Computing for Financial Transformation: A Review of Optimisation, Pricing, Risk, Machine Learning and Security*, UCL, 134 pages). Identifies that quantum value in finance concentrates in constrained search (QUBO/QAOA), amplitude estimation (Monte Carlo expectation estimation), and post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) migration.
- **Portfolio Optimization:** arXiv:2407.19857 (*PO-QA: A Framework for Portfolio Optimization using Quantum Algorithms*) and arXiv:2504.08843 (*End-to-End Portfolio Optimization with Quantum Annealing*).
- **Algorithmic Trading & Sector Rotation:** arXiv:2506.20930 (*Quantum Reinforcement Learning Trading Agent for Sector Rotation in the Taiwan Stock Market*). Hybrid QRL policies for macroeconomic factor rotation.

### School 2: The Quantum Modeling School (Phenomenological Representation)
Uses quantum mathematical formalisms as superior representations of market dynamics, rather than quantum hardware:
- **$\phi^4$ Scalar Quantum Field Theory for S&P 500:** arXiv:2512.17225 (*Modelling financial time series with $\phi^4$ quantum field theory*). Replaces classical Ising binarization with continuous $\phi^4$ scalar field theory to model non-Gaussian heavy tails and extreme market kurtosis across equities (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA).
- **Quantum Mechanics Analog for Option Pricing:** arXiv:2601.00293 (*Option Pricing beyond Black-Scholes Model Quantum Mechanics Approach*). Maps non-linear market friction to generalized potential wells in the quantum harmonic oscillator.
- **Market Imperfection via Open Quantum Systems:** arXiv:2505.01284 (*Modelling Financial Market Imperfection Using Open Quantum Systems*). Density-operator formalism representing liquidity constraints and non-Markovian memory.

### Foundational Early Lineage: Econophysics & Market Thermostatistics (2007–2010)
- **Thermodynamic Market Formulation:** arXiv:0803.3432 (*The thermodynamic approach to market*). Sergeev's 151-page treatise establishing temperature, entropy, and chemical potential as conjugate financial variables.
- **Information Networks in Quantitative Economics:** arXiv:0704.3748 (*Clustering Coefficients of Networks*) and arXiv:0804.1811 (*Space-Time Codes from Structured Lattices*).
- **Synthesis:** [[Arvix/outputs/Econophysics_Information_Early_Thread_2007-2010|Econophysics and Information Early Thread (2007–2010)]].

## 3. Epistemic Constraints for AMOS C07 Financial Models

1. **Analogy ≠ Identity:** Markets are socio-technical adaptive networks, NOT physical particle systems. Thermodynamical and quantum formalisms remain strictly `conclusion_class: AMOS_MODEL`.
2. **Hybrid Advantage Baseline:** Before proposing quantum or tensor algorithms for trading or portfolio allocation, verify whether classical convex solvers (e.g. OSQP, SCS) or gradient boosted trees achieve equivalent risk-adjusted returns with zero hardware overhead.
3. **No Arbitrage Overclaim:** No model may claim guaranteed alpha without explicit empirical walk-forward testing, execution cost modeling, and slippage constraints.
