- Country & Regime Profiles, Political Stability
- Economic Structure, Demographics, Energy Security
- Infrastructure, Military Posture, Regional Alliances
- International Organizations, Domestic Political Economy
- Policy Options Space, Stakeholder Mapping
- Regulatory Change, War Gaming, Sanctions
- Information Operations, Crisis Escalation, Long-term Trends

## The 20 Dimensions

- National Interest Alignment, Economic & Security Impact
- Alliance Cohesion, Escalation & Systemic Risk
- Implementation Feasibility & Enforcement Capacity
- Humanitarian & Civil Rights Impact

# AMOS Strategy & Game Engine


The **Strategy & Game Engine** operates in the domain of Game Theory, providing structured frameworks for negotiation, competition, and long-term positioning.

## Sub-Kernels
1. **Game Normal Form Kernel:**

Analyzes players, strategies, payoffs, and information sets.
2. **Game Dynamical Kernel:**

Models repeated interactions, evolutionary learning, and state trajectories.
3. **Negotiation Kernel:**

Models reservation values, offers, threat points, and coalition building.

## Applied Engines
- **Strategic Landscape Engine:** Formalizes informal struggles into explicit game models, identifying structural leverage points.
- **Scenario Gameplay Engine:** Tests strategy robustness against shocks and time horizons.
- **Negotiation Playbook Engine:** Aligns short-term tactics with long-term positioning, generating concession paths and fallback boundaries.

## Constraints
Strict prohibitions against generating strategies for physical harm or illegal market collusion.
