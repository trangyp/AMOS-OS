## 2. Core Mathematical Lineages from Arvix

### Lineage A: Commutative Algebra & Singularities (Hanoi School & Worldwide Collaborations)
- **Vanishing Conjecture on Differential Operators:** arXiv:0704.1691 (*A Vanishing Conjecture on Differential Operators with Constant Coefficients*). Algebraic analysis of polynomial differential systems.
- **Stability of Associated Primes:** arXiv:0705.2309 (*Stability of associated primes of monomial ideals*). Asymptotic behavior of graded modules and primary decomposition.
- **Adelic Resolutions for Homology Sheaves:** arXiv:0705.2597 (*Adelic resolution for homology sheaves*). Global algebraic geometry via local-global adelic machinery.
- **Non-Proper Value Sets & Jacobian Conjectures:** arXiv:0710.5212 (*A note on singularity and non-proper value set of polynomial maps of $\mathbb{C}^2$*). Testing invertibility and non-proper bifurcations.
- **Conical Singularities & Differential Topology:** arXiv:1006.5707 (*Smooth structures on pseudomanifolds with isolated conical singularities*). Extending calculus and index theorems to singular spaces.
- **Division Rings & Locally Solvable Groups:** arXiv:1007.1288 (*On locally solvable maximal subgroups of the multiplicative group of a division ring*). Non-commutative algebra structures.

### Lineage B: Discrete Mathematics, Graph Theory & Combinatorics
- **Combinatorics on Words (Iterated Hairpins):** arXiv:1010.3640 (*On the Iterated Hairpin Completion*). Formal language theory modeling DNA secondary structure self-assembly through language completions.
- **Fatgraphs & Finite-Type Invariants:** arXiv:0907.2827 (*Finite type invariants and fatgraphs*). Vassiliev knot invariants and ribbon graph topological formalisms.
- **Bounded Integer Programming:** arXiv:0808.1364 (*On Bounded Integer Programming*). Complexity bounds in discrete combinatorial optimization.
- **Local Cohomology of Curves:** arXiv:0910.0919 (*On local cohomology of a tetrahedral curve*). Hilbert functions and projective space ideals.
- **Foliations & Hilbert's 12th Problem:** arXiv:0804.0057 (*Measured foliations and Hilbert 12th problem*). Complex multiplication and abelian extensions of real quadratic fields.
