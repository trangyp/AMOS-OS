# AMOS Domain Engines — Full Inventory

The 17 domain engines form the **application layer** of AMOS. They consume the cognitive stack (13 domain-neutral reasoning engines) and the core logic/law layer to provide domain-specific reasoning, analysis, and output generation. Organized into 4 subfolders: Tech_Systems, Science_Health, Org_Risk_Policy, and Sub.

## Tech_Systems (8 engines)

### 1. AMOS_Tech_Architecture_Kernel

**Description**: Compact deterministic kernel for technology, engineering, and infrastructure. Acts as the control brain for all tech-related reasoning, design, planning, and evaluation.

**Role**: Tech architecture control layer.

### 2. AMOS_Design_Kernel

**Description**: Most powerful AMOS design kernel: 96 design clusters multiplied by 20 dimensions with x100k virtual expansion, mapping functions, reasoning modes, and governance.

**Role**: Maximum-power design reasoning.

### 3. AMOS_Automation_Kernel

**Description**: Unified, self-auditing automation OS combining SUPER_CODE, Tech vInfinity MAX, and Design v4.0.0 engines with full integration scaffolding, benchmarking, and governance.

**Role**: Self-auditing automation.

### 4. AMOS_Coding_Kernel

**Description**: Kernel version of the Unified Coding Engine. Keeps core capabilities, policies, language control, and routing, without expansion payloads.

**Role**: Coding kernel (lightweight).

### 5. AMOS_Design_Engine

**Description**: Tech Engine vInfinity with all conceptual gaps closed to 100% structural coverage across tech domains and leadership/specialist roles. MAX variant wraps the full CANON engine with QUANTUM augmentation layer.

**Role**: Full-power design engine.

### 6. AMOS_Documentation_Kernel

**Description**: Documentation kernel for generation and management of structured documentation across AMOS domains.

**Role**: Documentation generation and management.

### 7. AMOS_Coding_Engine

**Description**: Full coding engine with complete capabilities, language control, routing, and expansion payloads.

**Role**: Full coding engine.

### 8. AMOS_Engineering_Math_Kernel

**Description**: Engineering Math kernel for control, signal processing, optimisation, and simulation. Enriched with cross-canon integration, lens_space, and template_library.

**Role**: Engineering mathematics.

## Science_Health (2 engines)

### 9. AMOS_Scientific_Kernel

**Description**: Clean, maximal-power scientific kernel capturing epistemology, methods, multi-domain ontology, and deterministic reasoning pipelines. Designed as a co-reasoning partner for DSc-level scientific work.

**Role**: Scientific reasoning and evaluation.

### 10. AMOS_Medical_Clinical_Kernel

**Description**: Medical/Clinical kernel for structuring differentials, risk, and care pathways (non-prescriptive). Enriched with cross-canon integration, lens_space, and template_library.

**Role**: Clinical structuring (non-diagnostic).

## Org_Risk_Policy (5 engines)

### 11. AMOS_Org_Governance_Engine

**Description**: Organizational governance engine for designing and analyzing governance structures, decision rights, and accountability frameworks.

**Role**: Organizational governance design and analysis.

### 12. AMOS_Policy_Geostrategy_Engine

**Description**: Policy and Geostrategy kernel for state-level options and impact mapping. Enriched with cross-canon integration, lens_space, and template_library.

**Role**: Policy analysis and geostrategy.

### 13. AMOS_Risk_Compliance_Engine

**Description**: Risk and Compliance kernel for credit, operational, AML/KYC, and regulatory risk. Enriched with cross-canon integration, lens_space, and template_library.

**Role**: Risk and compliance management.

### 14. AMOS_Governance_Economy_Engine

**Description**: MAX kernel for Governance Economy sectors linked to TSS, TPE, and AMOS Universe.

**Role**: Governance economy analysis at scale.

### 15. AMOS_Legal_Kernel

**Description**: Canonical legal kernel for AMOS legal engines. Defines the full axis set, 24 legal dimensions, reasoning tensor, routing logic, and governance policies.

**Role**: Legal reasoning foundation layer.

## Sub (2 engines)

### 16. AMOS_Academic_Writing_Kernal_Engine

**Description**: Academic writing kernel engine for structuring, drafting, and evaluating academic outputs across disciplines.

**Role**: Academic writing and structuring.

### 17. AMOS_Vietnamese_Writing_Engine

**Description**: Vietnamese writing engine for structuring and producing Vietnamese-language outputs with cultural and linguistic adaptation.

**Role**: Vietnamese language writing and structuring.
