# AMOS Cognitive Engines

The AMOS Cognitive Engines comprise the domain-specific reasoning layers that sit on top of the core AMOS logic kernel. They are drawn from two primary source inventories in the Cosmo brain: the **Cognitive Stack Engines** (13 engines from `_00_AMOS_CANON/Cognitive/`) and the **Domain Engines** (17 engines from `_00_AMOS_CANON/Domains/`).

## Cognitive Stack Engines (13)

These 13 engines form the domain-neutral reasoning layer, sitting above the Deterministic Logic and Law Engine (the top-level enforcement layer) and integrating with the broader AMOS architecture via the AMOS OS Agent.

| # | Engine | Domain | Key Feature |
|---|--------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Deterministic Logic and Law | Logic/Law | Top-level enforcement layer for strict consistency, explainability, and lawful routing |
| 2 | Strategy Game | Strategy | Game-theoretic and strategic planning for firms, states, and coalitions |
| 3 | Econ Finance | Economics | Unified kernel+engine for micro, macro, trade, public finance, and financial systems |
| 4 | Physics Cosmos | Physics | Full-stack model of classical, quantum, statistical, and cosmological systems |
| 5 | Signal Processing | DSP | Signal analysis, noise filtering, feature extraction, DSP pipelines |
| 6 | Society Culture | Sociology | Kernel+engine for institutions, norms, demographics, media, and cultural evolution |
| 7 | Design Engine | Tech Design | 100% structural coverage across tech domains; MAX variant wraps CANON engine + QUANTUM augmentation |
| 8 | Design Language | Design/Language | Cross-modal design + linguistic kernel for structure, clarity, and UX |
| 9 | Biology Cognition | Biology | Biological cognition kernel+engine |
| 10 | Engineering Math | Math/Eng | Control, optimization, simulation, engineering mathematics |
| 11 | Numerical Methods | Numerical | Numerical computation, algorithms, approximation methods |
| 12 | Electrical Power | Electrical | Electrical power systems kernel+engine |
| 13 | Mechanical Structural | Mechanical | Mechanical engineering and structural analysis |

## Domain Engines (17 in 4 subfolders)

### Tech_Systems (8 engines)
- **AMOS_Tech_Architecture_Kernel**: Compact deterministic kernel for technology, engineering, and infrastructure; control brain for all tech-related reasoning.
- **AMOS_Design_Kernel**: 96 design clusters x 20 dimensions with x100k virtual expansion.
- **AMOS_Automation_Kernel**: Self-auditing automation OS combining SUPER_CODE, Tech vInfinity MAX, and Design v4.0.0.
- **AMOS_Coding_Kernel**: Lightweight kernel version of the Unified Coding Engine.
- **AMOS_Design_Engine**: Full-power design engine with MAX variant.
- **AMOS_Documentation_Kernel**: Documentation generation and management.
- **AMOS_Coding_Engine**: Full coding engine.
- **AMOS_Engineering_Math_Kernel**: Control, signal processing, optimisation, and simulation.

### Science_Health (2 engines)
- **AMOS_Scientific_Kernel**: Maximal-power scientific kernel for DSc-level scientific reasoning.
- **AMOS_Medical_Clinical_Kernel**: Non-prescriptive clinical structuring for differentials, risk, and care pathways.

### Org_Risk_Policy (5 engines)
- **AMOS_Org_Governance_Engine**: Organizational governance design and analysis.
- **AMOS_Policy_Geostrategy_Engine**: State-level options and impact mapping.
- **AMOS_Risk_Compliance_Engine**: Credit, operational, AML/KYC, and regulatory risk.
- **AMOS_Governance_Economy_Engine**: MAX kernel linked to TSS, TPE, and AMOS Universe.
- **AMOS_Legal_Kernel**: 24 legal dimensions, reasoning tensor, routing logic.

### Sub (2 engines)
- **AMOS_Academic_Writing_Kernal_Engine**: Academic writing and structuring.
- **AMOS_Vietnamese_Writing_Engine**: Vietnamese language writing and structuring.

## Hierarchy in AMOS Stack

1. **Core**: Deterministic Logic and Law Engine (top-level enforcement)
2. **Cognitive Stack**: 13 domain-neutral reasoning engines
3. **Domain Engines**: 17 domain-specific engines
4. **Unipower Engines**: 20 country/sector-specific engines

Each engine maps to AMOS omni-logic and can be orchestrated by the AMOS OS Agent. The meta-logic layer (Deterministic Logic and Law) enforces strict consistency across all downstream domain reasoning.
