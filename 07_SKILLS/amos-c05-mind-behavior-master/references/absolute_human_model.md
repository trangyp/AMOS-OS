# AMOS Absolute Human Model

> **Core Engine**: Absolute Human
> **Skill Mapping**: `amos-absolute-human-layer`

## Conceptual Framework

The Absolute Human Model defines the finalized, absolute ontology for human baseline consciousness, biological endurance, and fundamental needs. It is the ultimate anchor point for the UBI (Universal Biological Intelligence) constraints.

### Key Components

#### 1. Biological & Cognitive Baseline
- Establishes the non-negotiable physiological and neurological limits of the human organism (e.g., maximum sustained cognitive load, sleep dependency, stress hormone toxicity).

#### 2. The Core Needs Hierarchy
- A deterministic mapping of human needs, stripped of cultural relativity. Defines what is absolutely required for a human to remain in a stable, non-pathological state.

#### 3. Breakdown Vectors
- Maps precisely how the human system fails when pushed beyond the baseline limits (e.g., burnout, psychosis, physical disease) and the systemic catalysts for these failures.

## Integration & Output
This is a foundational model. It must be respected by all higher-level engines (like the Tech Architecture Engine or the Bod Engine). If a proposed system, product, or organizational structure violates the Absolute Human baseline, it is structurally invalid and must be redesigned. It acts as the final "Law of Law" check regarding human integration.
