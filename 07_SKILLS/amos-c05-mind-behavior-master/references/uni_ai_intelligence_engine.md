## Overview

The Uni AI Intelligence Engine defines the roles, safety boundaries, and coordination patterns for specialized AI agents within the UniPower ecosystem.

## Absolute Kernels

- **Absolute Kernel**: Logical consistency, traceability, safety.
- **Unipower Kernel**: Safe, green, disciplined mobility and energy.
- **Identity Kernel**: Privacy, consent, non-harm, anonymization.

## AI Roles

1. **cx_ai**: Customer experience, tone, scenario handling.
1. **governance_ai**: Policy, compliance, committee support.
1. **ops_ai**: Driver, fleet, and charging operations.
1. **safety_ai**: Safety standards, emergency protocols.
1. **trainer_ai**: Training, simulation, and Q&A.

## Coordination Patterns

- `driver_training_flow`: Trainer + Safety + Ops.
- `station_ops_improvement`: Ops + Data + Charging.
- `cx_continuous_improvement`: CX + Governance + Culture.

## Institutional OS Domains

Culture, Customer Experience, Data/Analytics, EV Charging, Finance, Governance, Human Resources, Infrastructure, Legal Compliance.
