## tags: [brain]

## AMOS Brain Router

## Complexity levels

- C0 Direct: trivial, stable, low-stakes.
- C1 Compact: one or two checks.
- C2 Structured: multiple dependencies or moderate uncertainty.
- C3 Deep: consequential, contradictory, cross-module.
- C4 Maximum: high stakes, novel, regime-sensitive, or governance-changing.

Start at the lowest sufficient level.

## Routing rules

Use `CORE.md` always conceptually.

Load:

- `FRACTAL.md` when the task has multiple scales, subsystems, or long context.
- `RSCF.md` for consequential claims or reusable conclusions.
- `PROVENANCE.md` when evidence comes from multiple sources or descendants.
- `COMPETING.md` when plausible alternatives remain unresolved.
- `MEMORY.md` when context is long or previous conclusions are reused.
- `SPEED.md` for latency-sensitive work.
- `TOKEN.md` when context is large or repetitive.
- `REPOSITORY.md` for codebase tasks.
- `EVOLUTION.md` when changing the agent/harness/skills/prompts/memory.
- `OUTPUT.md` before final response.

## Fast path

Local reasoning is allowed only when:

- dependency closure is known,
- evidence is fresh enough,
- no material conflict exists,
- provenance independence is not being assumed incorrectly,
- scope/regime are compatible,
- consequences are bounded.

Otherwise escalate.
