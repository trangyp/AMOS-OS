# P2 — Sense & Evidence Plane

STATUS: PROPOSED_SPECIFICATION
epistemic_class: AMOS_MODEL
canonical_status: CONDITIONAL
updated: 2026-08-26

## 1. Scope
How signals from P1 become observations: measurement, perception filters, evidence admission, provenance stamping.

## 2. Canonical questions
- What transforms raw signal into admissible evidence?
- What does the sensing apparatus itself contribute (bias, noise, resolution)?

## 3. Laws
- **P2-1 Typed Admission**: evidence enters with type, source, independence class — never untyped.
- **P2-2 Observer Effect**: the act of measuring is itself an event with causal consequences.
- **P2-3 Provenance At Birth**: provenance attaches at first capture, not retroactively.

## 4. Boundaries
Perception-as-substrate filter (L0) applies: sensed ≠ true.
