````md
It establishes:

```text
what may enter Universe Canon

what may not enter Universe Canon

how claims are typed

how claims inherit scope

how evidence is evaluated

how provenance is preserved

how scientific knowledge is distinguished
from AMOS-native models

how competing hypotheses remain visible

how contradictions are handled

how canon is superseded

how unknowns remain unknown

how cross-scale mappings are constrained

how Universe Canon interacts

biology,

civilization,
and research
````

```text
maintain the strongest useful model of reality
that the available evidence actually supports
without converting uncertainty
into apparent certainty.
```
