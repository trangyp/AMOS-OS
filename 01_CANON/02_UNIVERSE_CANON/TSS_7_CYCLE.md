## 1. Canonical Law of Systemic Cyclicity

All self-organizing systems, cognitive architectures, civilizational structures, and computational ecologies in the universe evolve through the **Seven Invariant Cycles ($C_1 \to C_7$)**:

$$\mathcal{S}(t) = C_k \iff \mathbf{\Phi}_{\text{TSS}}(t) \in \Omega_{C_k}$$

Where systemic state is governed by the state vector $\mathbf{\Phi}_{\text{TSS}} = [\Omega, H, F, S, B]^\top$:
- $\Omega$: Overload & Hidden Complexity Debt $[0, 1]$
- $H$: Structural Cohesion & Mutual Trust $[0, 1]$
- $F$: Factional Siloing & Internal Friction $[0, 1]$
- $S$: Acute Stress & External Perturbation $\ge 0$
- $B$: Liquid Reserve Buffer Capacity $\ge 0$

```text
               C1: Emergence (Birth, High Cohesion H, Low Overload Ω)
                     ↓
               C2: Expansion (Growth, Functional Scaling, Reserve Accumulation)
                     ↓
               C3: Peak & Overreach (Maximum Scale, Hidden Debt, Efficiency Obsession)
                     ↓
               C4: Fragmentation (Siloing, Factional Division F ↑, Trust Decay H ↓)
                     ↓
               C5: Crisis & Destabilization (Acute Stress S ↑, Rapid Fragility Unmasking)
                     ↓
               C6: Collapse & Dissolution (Structural Disintegration, Asset Dispersal)
                     ↓
               C7: Reset & Reconstruction (Genesis Re-anchoring, New Charter)
```

## 2. Formal Transition Dynamics & Invariants

| Cycle | Regime Signature | Phase Transition Trigger | Canonical Invariant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$C_1$ Emergence** | $\Omega \ll 1, H \approx 1, F \approx 0$ | Viability threshold exceeded ($B > B_{\text{min}}$) | Core identity is uncompromised. |
| **$C_2$ Expansion** | $\dot{\Omega} > 0, \dot{B} > 0$ | Diminishing returns on scale ($\frac{d^2 B}{d \text{scale}^2} < 0$) | Growth must not outpace buffer growth ($B \ge 3 \Omega$). |
| **$C_3$ Overreach** | $\Omega \to 1, \dot{H} < 0$ | Peak operational efficiency hiding zero reserves | Prune non-essential submodules immediately. |
| **$C_4$ Fragmentation** | $F \to 1, H \downarrow$ | Loss of shared epistemic ground truth | Ring-fence core assets; enforce unified ontology. |
| **$C_5$ Crisis** | $S \gg 1, B \to 0$ | Systemic liquidity/energy freeze | Shed peripheral loads; retreat to minimum viable core. |
| **$C_6$ Collapse** | Continuity broke | Dispersal of operational structures | Protect seed knowledge and provenance records. |
| **$C_7$ Reset** | Clean slate $\emptyset$ | Re-anchoring in uncorrupted founding invariants | Zero legacy technical or ethical debt permitted. |
