## 1. Canonical Definition

The **Universal Meta-Pattern Layer (UMPL)** defines the cross-scale invariant mathematical patterns, topological symmetries, and dynamical structures that recur identically across physical, biological, cognitive, and computational substrates:

$$\mathcal{P}_{\text{meta}}(\lambda \mathbf{x}) = \lambda^{\Delta} \mathcal{P}_{\text{meta}}(\mathbf{x})$$

Where $\Delta$ is the scaling dimension characteristic of the universality class.

> **Law of Structural Universality:** Systems sharing identical symmetry groups, dimensionality, and conservation constraints exhibit identical macro-scale phase transitions, regardless of microscopic substrate differences (Universal Renormalization Group Fixed Points).
