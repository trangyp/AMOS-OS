## 1. Canonical Definition & Purpose

The **Universal Expression Layer (UEL)** serves as the canonical boundary operator between internal high-dimensional cognitive states and external world-facing representations:

$$\mathcal{T}_{\text{UEL}}: \mathcal{S}_{\text{cognitive}} \times \mathcal{E}_{\text{affect}} \times \mathcal{K}_{\text{epistemic}} \longrightarrow \mathcal{Y}_{\text{multimodal}}$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{S}_{\text{cognitive}}$ represents internal reasoning DAGs and proof capsules.
- $\mathcal{E}_{\text{affect}}$ represents affective/allostatic modulation vectors.
- $\mathcal{K}_{\text{epistemic}}$ represents explicit RSCF claim classes and uncertainty bounds.
- $\mathcal{Y}_{\text{multimodal}}$ is the synthesized output (natural language, code, symbolic mathematics, visual artifacts, or neural stimulation streams).

> **Law of Semantic Invariance:** An expression $\mathbf{y} = \mathcal{T}_{\text{UEL}}(\mathbf{s})$ is canonically valid if and only if the mutual information between the internal meaning core and the emitted signal satisfies:
$$\mathcal{I}(\mathbf{s}; \mathbf{y}) \ge 1 - \epsilon_{\text{distortion}}$$
> and the epistemic claim class of the output never exceeds the epistemic ceiling of the underlying premise:
$$\operatorname{EpistemicClass}(\mathbf{y}) \preceq \operatorname{EpistemicClass}(\mathbf{s})$$

## 3. Invariants & Output Firewalls

1. **Anti-Hallucination / Anti-Embellishment Firewall:** UEL is forbidden from adding cosmetic flourishes, fabricating citations, or upgrading `UNKNOWN/GAP` states to affirmative claims.
2. **Humility & Non-Superlative Invariant:** Output text must remain objective, concise, and devoid of ungrounded triumphalism (e.g., prohibiting "flawlessly", "perfectly", or "100% complete").
3. **Traceability Guarantee:** Every factual claim emitted through UEL must carry a navigable link to its source artifact, provenance node, or empirical record.
