# P7 — Evolution & Learning Plane

STATUS: PROPOSED_SPECIFICATION
epistemic_class: AMOS_MODEL
canonical_status: CONDITIONAL
updated: 2026-08-26

## 1. Scope
Bounded self-improvement: cycles, adaptation under invariants, anti-regression.

## 2. Canonical questions
- How does the system improve without corrupting its own integrity stack?
- What bounds mutation?

## 3. Laws
- **P7-1 Cycle Discipline**: additive learning cycles with verification gates (quantum library pattern: 10 cycles, stable contract).
- **P7-2 Refine After Build**: components drift within a session; alternate build/refine passes.
- **P7-3 Repair ≥ Entropy**: repair rate must match or exceed entropy injection rate for viability (PV law).

## 4. Boundaries
Self-modification of kernel laws excluded (K-2); evolution bounded by L9 Evolution Laws.
