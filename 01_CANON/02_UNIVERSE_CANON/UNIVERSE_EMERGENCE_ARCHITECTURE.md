## 1. The Universal Morphogenesis Sequence

Form does not appear arbitrarily from empty space; it emerges through a universal 7-stage constraint gradient:

$$\text{Field} \xrightarrow{\nabla} \text{Gradient} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{C}} \text{Constraint} \xrightarrow{\partial} \text{Boundary} \xrightarrow{\Phi} \text{Flow} \xrightarrow{\tau} \text{Stabilization} \xrightarrow{\Omega} \text{Form}$$

1. **Field**: Distributed potential before localized distinction;
2. **Gradient**: Directional pressure or imbalance emerging in the field;
3. **Constraint**: Stabilization of interaction permissions and rate limits;
4. **Boundary**: Enclosure of internal state against external entropy;
5. **Flow**: Regulated transit of matter, energy, or information across boundaries;
6. **Stabilization**: Attractor convergence where recurring cycles achieve dynamic equilibrium;
7. **Form**: Persistent organized structure capable of storing memory and replicating pattern.

$$\mathcal{V}(U) = \text{Coherence}(U) \times \text{Transformability}(U) \times \text{Persistence}(U) \times \text{EntropyTolerance}(U) \times \mathcal{R}_{\text{ground}}(U)$$
