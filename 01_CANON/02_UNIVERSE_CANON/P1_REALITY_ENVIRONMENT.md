# P1 — Reality & Environment Plane

STATUS: PROPOSED_SPECIFICATION
epistemic_class: AMOS_MODEL
canonical_status: CONDITIONAL
updated: 2026-08-26

## 1. Scope
The external world and its constraints as the system's ground truth boundary: physical limits, resource scarcity, irreducible uncertainty, and the environment's capacity to falsify models.

## 2. Canonical questions
- What exists independent of this system's model of it?
- Which constraints bind regardless of belief (thermodynamics, causality, finitude)?
- How does the environment inject novelty that no internal model predicted?

## 3. Laws
- **P1-1 Reality Primacy**: the world is not obliged to match any internal canon; empirical status is NOT established by architecture.
- **P1-2 Constraint Inheritance**: every downstream plane inherits P1 constraints; no layer may legislate them away.
- **P1-3 Falsifiability Contact**: models must remain touchable by observation.

## 4. Boundaries
This plane describes structure, not physics claims. Empirical validity requires external evidence.
