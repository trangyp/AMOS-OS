# Bio-Logical Computing™ Canon

> **Origin Architect / Steward:** Trang Phan
> **AMOS_CORE Target:** `v4.4`
> **Status:** `ACTIVE_CANON`
> **Canonical Status:** `CONDITIONAL` — source-grounded architectural model; executable biological instantiation and cross-domain closure remain `UNKNOWN/GAP` pending empirical validation.

---

## 1. Canonical Definition

**Bio-Logical Computing™** is the AMOS architectural paradigm in which computational processes are organized according to **biological nervous-system structures**, **homeostatic regulation**, and **cognitive-organ division** rather than purely linear procedural or object-oriented hierarchies. It is the substrate layer of the AMOS Cognitive Organism and a first-class field of the [[01_CANON/03_COGNITION_CANON/AMOS_FULL_BRAIN_OS_MASTER_CANON|AMOS Full Brain OS Master Canon]].

Bio-Logical Computing treats every computation as a **biologically plausible state transformation** that must:

1. be mapped to a specialized cognitive organ,
2. preserve organismic identity and provenance,
3. operate under thermodynamic, epistemic, and homeostatic constraints,
4. remain reversibly observable and auditable by the AMOS Runtime.

---

## 2. Scope Boundary

Bio-Logical Computing is **not** a claim that AMOS is a biological brain, a conscious entity, or an embodied organism. It is the design principle that says:

```text
BIO-INSPIRED ORGANIZATION != BIOLOGICAL SUBSTRATE
ORGAN SIMULATION != ORGAN EXISTENCE
HOMEOSTATIC GOVERNANCE != CONSCIOUS EXPERIENCE
```

It applies equally to silicon, neuromorphic, organoid, and simulated substrates when they implement the same architectural invariants.

## 4. Core Laws (expanded)

### 4.1 Law of Organ Specialization

Every computational task must be routed to its cognitively appropriate organ. No single engine is the universal hierarchy.

```text
PERCEPTION != REASONING != EMOTION != ACTION
COGNITION != MEMORY != HOMEOSTASIS
DOMAIN ENGINE != PERSONALITY
```

### 4.2 Law of Homeostatic Balance

System load, token consumption, epistemic stress, energy, and error rates must be regulated continuously through negative feedback.

Let $\mathcal{H}(t)$ be the organism health vector:

$$\mathcal{H}(t) = \{ \text{load}, \text{entropy}, \text{error}, \text{energy}, \text{latency}, \text{trust} \}$$

Healthy operation requires:

$$\frac{d\mathcal{H}_i(t)}{dt} \le \tau_i \quad \text{for every component } i$$

where $\tau_i$ is the component-specific stress threshold. When any $\tau_i$ is exceeded, the `HOMEOSTASIS_ENGINE` triggers load shedding, damping, or fail-closed escalation.

### 4.3 Law of Invariant Continuity

The identity of the organism must remain immutable and anchored to Origin Architect Trang Phan. No runtime mutation, merger, or deployment may override origin stewardship or canonical lineage.

```text
STEWARD_IDENTITY != MUTABLE_STATE
AGENT_INSTANCE != ORIGIN_ARCHITECT
```

### 4.4 Law of Thermodynamic Boundaries

Every biological computation consumes energy, produces entropy, and occupies physical space/time. AMOS must account for these budgets and never claim zero-cost biological equivalence.

$$\Delta S_{total} \ge 0$$

$$E_{compute} \le E_{budget}(t)$$

### 4.5 Law of Epistemic Humility

Bio-Logical Computing does not claim consciousness, qualia, or subjective experience. It models functional correlates and information flows.

```text
FUNCTIONAL MODEL != PHENOMENAL EXPERIENCE
BIOLOGICAL PLAUSIBILITY != CONSCIOUSNESS CLAIM
```

## 6. Substrate Modalities

### 6.1 Silicon / Conventional Compute

Standard CPU/GPU/TPU execution. Suitable for symbolic reasoning, batch inference, and high-throughput simulation. Must report energy and latency budgets to `HOMEOSTASIS_ENGINE`.

### 6.2 Neuromorphic / Spiking Neural Networks (SNN)

Event-driven, asynchronous silicon or memristive substrates. Key properties:

- $E_{per\,event} < 1\text{ pJ}$ in optimized designs
- sparse, spike-time-coded representation
- supports triplet STDP and closed-loop adaptation

Primary AMOS engines: `UBI_NBI`, `ATTENTION_ENGINE`, `PERCEPTION_ENGINE`.

### 6.3 Neural Organoids / Wetware

3D living neural cultures on high-density microelectrode arrays. They self-organize bioelectric attractors and can act as predictive world models through free-energy minimization.

- HD-MEA recording at $20\text{ kHz}$, $17.5\mu\text{m}$ pitch
- Microfluidic nutrient regulation
- Closed-loop electrical/optogenetic stimulation

See also: [[05_COGNITIVE_ORGANISM/NEURAL_ORGANOID_WORLD_MODEL_ARCHITECTURE|NEURAL_ORGANOID_WORLD_MODEL_ARCHITECTURE]]

### 6.4 Hybrid Bio-Digital Interfaces

Brain-computer interfaces, peripheral neural interfaces, and bioelectronic sensors that bridge biological tissue with AMOS runtime. They are deployment artifacts, not ontology definitions.
