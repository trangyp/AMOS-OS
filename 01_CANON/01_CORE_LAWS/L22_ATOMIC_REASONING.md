## 0. Status

## 2. Laws
- **AR-1 Atomic Steps**: reasoning steps are individually checkable; composite claims decompose into atoms.
- **AR-2 Local Validity ≠ Global Validity**: each valid step does not certify the chain; chain validity needs its own check.
- **AR-3 Replayable Chains**: reasoning chains can be re-executed deterministically against pinned inputs.
- **AR-4 Loop Detection**: cycles in reasoning graphs are defects, not depth.

## 4. Falsifiers
F1: authoritative reasoning canon defines different atomicity contract.
