## 1. Formal Mutation Model

$$\mu(P, T) = rac{K}{M_{	ext{total}} - E}$$

Where:
- $K = |\{m \in M \mid \exists t \in T : t(m) 
eq t(P)\}|$ is the number of killed mutants.
- $M_{	ext{total}} = |M|$ is the total number of mutants generated.
- $E$ is the number of provably equivalent mutants ($orall t, t(m) = t(P)$).
